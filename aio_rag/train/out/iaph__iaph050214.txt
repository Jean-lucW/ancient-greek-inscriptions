men exists for good repute,

of good will to the most

towards which many and exceptional

claims to a return of affection

are its due; and because of these things

Aur(elius) Achilleus, who took up training

of the body, and in athletic competition

the most noble, and in life and

principle the most venerable, so that in

him has been blended all the excellence that

belongs to soul and to body, (the city) having welcomed (him)

often also in the

previous contests, which he adorned

conspicuously, having competed with all

manliness, but especially

in the contest of the Olympia, because, when

the city as his fatherland had urged him

to enter the most consummate of the

competitions and the judging of the

men, he obeyed and,

persuaded by the exhortation, defeated his

opponents and with

such glory bound on the olive crown

that among the most

celebrated competitions

his manliness is counted

and his zeal; for these reasons it was

resolved that not only within the knowledge

of those present, nor of those who

attended the stadium at the time,

should the testimony about these things stop,

but also to entrust, through

this decree, him still more
