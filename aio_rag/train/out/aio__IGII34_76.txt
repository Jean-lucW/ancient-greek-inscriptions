Tharrias of Erchia proposed: the tribesmen shall resolve, since Poseidippos of Hestiaia, the treasurer

of the tribe, administered the funds well and justly for the tribesmen and performed the sacrifices on behalf of the prytany members

that had to be sacrificed, to praise him for his excellence and justice towards the prytany members

and crown him with a foliage crown.

Aristophanes of Ikarion proposed: the tribesmen shall resolve to praise, since well and justly

they managed the gathering of the People and the distribution of the tokens and made the gift

to the tribesmen, to praise them for their excellence and justice towards the tribesmen and crown

each of them with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice towards the tribe:

Diodoros son of Philokles of Gargettos, Timokritos son of Timokrates of Ikarion, Tharrias son of Tharriades of Erchia.

Tharrias of Erchia proposed: to praise the religious officials who served as religious officials for the Mysteries at Eleusis

and crown each of them with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice towards the

tribesmen: Poseidippos of Hestiaia, Timokritos of Ikarion, Aristophanes of Ikarion, Chairias of Plotheia,

Kallistratos of Teithras, Pythion of Kydantidai, Eubios of Ankyle, Theomnestos of Phe-

gaia, Theophilos of Myrrhinoutta, Melieus of Ionidai.
