**Part 1**

Gods.

Kephisokles son of Kephisokleides of Probalinthos proposed: the Tetrapolitans shall decide, since Charidemos of Probalinthos fulfilled his office well and justly and made all the sacrifices to the gods and the heroes on

the stated days and at the appropriate times and took care of the other common matters well and justly, the Tetrapolitans shall resolve to praise

Charidemos son of Charidemos of Probalinthos and

crown him with a gold crown of

1000 drachmas for his excellence and justice towards the Tetrapolitans; and the herald shall announce him at the Dionysia in the tragedies, that 'the Tetrapolitans crown Charidemos son of Charidemos of Probalinthos for his excellence

and justice towards the Tetrapolitans'; and he shall also have a seat of honour; and to inscribe this decree on a stone stele and stand it in the Dionysion.
