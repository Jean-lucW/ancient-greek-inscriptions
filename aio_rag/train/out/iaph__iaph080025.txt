[Imperator Caesar?],

[son of the God Julius?],

[consul desig]-

nate for the second and [the third] time,

one of the Triumviri

Reipublicae

Constituendae,

to the magistrates, Council and People of the Plarasans and Aphro-

disians,

greetings.

If you are well, it would be

good; I too am in good health

with the army.

Solon

son of Demetrios, your

envoy,

having taken the greatest care

over the affairs of your

city,

was not only

content with the

arrangements that have been made, but also

urged us

to despatch to you copies,

checked against the public

tablets, of the

judgement and decree

and treaty and law

which have been made for you;

for which I praised

Solon and

welcomed him all the more

and held him among

my acquaintances,

and I have also allotted him

the appropriate privileges,

considering the man

worthy of our

honour; and I share your

pleasure at having

such a citizen.

The copies

of the privileges granted

to you

are written below;

and I wish you

to enter them

in the public

records

kept among you.

Letter of Caesar.
