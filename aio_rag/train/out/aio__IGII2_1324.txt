[Since Stephanos, having been superintendent]

[for the] year [in the]

[archonship of -], has presided over [the]

repair of the [sanctuary as]

was fitting, and sent [also]

the procession worthily of the goddess,

displaying love of honour in all these matters and

having spent in addition from his own resources, and

of the other things which were proper in the

year he took care well and in an orderly

manner; so that the orgeones also

may be seen to render worthy thanks

to those who continually display love of honour,

for good fortune, the orgeones shall decide

to praise Stephanos for his

piety towards Bendis and Deloptes

and the other gods

and his love of honour towards the orgeones,

and to crown him

with a foliage crown, so that for all the

rest of the orgeones too there may be an

incentive, for those who wish to be pious

towards the gods and to display love of honour

towards the orgeones, knowing

that they will receive worthy thanks

from the orgeones; and there shall be granted

to him also a place for a dedication

in the sanctuary wherever it seems

to be fitting; and the secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele

and stand it by the dedication;

and for the making and the inscribing

the treasurer shall allocate the expense

accrued.

In crown: The orgeones

(crown) Stephanos.
