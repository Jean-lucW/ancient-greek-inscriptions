In the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39), at the principal

assembly. The Aixonians decided. Glaukides son of Sosippos of

Aixone proposed: the Aixonians shall resolve, since

Kallikrates son of Glaukon of Aixone and Aristokrates son of Aristophanes of Aixone are good men and show love of honour towards the People

of the Aixonians,

to praise them

for their excellence and justice towards

the People of the Aixonians and crown them with a

gold crown of five hundred drachmas

each; and the money for the crowns

shall be from the administration, from the surplus funds

in the archonship of Theophrastos; and Hegesileos

the demarch and the treasurers shall give them

the money for the crowns; and to announce also at the

Dionysia at the comedies in Aixone in the

theatre, that the People of the Aixonians crowns them

for their excellence and justice towards the People

of the Aixonians and the common affairs of the Aixonians; and the

demarch Hegesileos and the treasurers shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

in the theatre in Aixone.
