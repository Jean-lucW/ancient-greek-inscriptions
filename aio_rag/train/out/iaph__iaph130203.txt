(Made while still) alive.

[The sarcophagus] and the base and the niches constructed in the base itself were

[constructed] and repaired by Mar(cus) Aur(elius) Athenagoras son of Eudamos (the third), son of Philon, for himself

[and for] his [wife] Aurelia Meltine daughter of Dionysios son of Athenagoras son of Eupolemos, and for their children

[...]; after he himself and his wife have been buried in the sarcophagus, there shall be constructed

[by] his children by Meltine, Eudamos and Athenagoras, a cover-slab which shall cover those

[...] buried, and thus on the cover-slab, when they have died, his children by Meltine,

Eudamos and Athenagoras, shall be buried; but I wish no-one else to be buried

in the sarcophagus, but that it be consecrated to the heroes; and in the niches beneath the base of the sarcophagus

shall be buried Aureliai Meltine and Meltine, my daughters; and no-one shall have the authority

to bury anyone else in the sarcophagus other than those whom I have wished, or to exhume those who have been

buried, since he shall be impious and accursed and a tomb-robber and shall pay in addition to the

most sacred treasury 2,500 silver denarii, of which one-third shall belong to the

prosecutor. Of this inscription a copy was deposited in the archive in the

thirteenth stephanephorate of Attalis daughter of Menekrates son of Apphion, in the month of Apellaios.
