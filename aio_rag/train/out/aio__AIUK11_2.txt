**Part 1**

[...]

a twelve-[knobbed (cake)]

Metageitnion: for the goddesses [...] [...]

[...] of the complete rite, a round cake, [twelve]-knobbed, of a choinix, on the fifteenth, wineless.

Boedromion, on the thirteenth, for Nephthys and Osiris

you shall burn a cockerel as an offering, sowing wheat

and barley, pouring a libation of honey-mixture. On the seventeenth, for Demeter (and) Kore, a piglet, without delay. On the eighteenth, the vintage offering for Dionysos and the other gods, without delay.

Pyanopsion: for Apollo and Artemis, on the seventh, a round cake of a choinix, with an upright knob, and a seated one,

twelve-knobbed.

Maimakterion: for Zeus Georgos, on the twentieth, a round cake

of a choinix, with an upright knob, twelve-knobbed,

a raised cake of a choinix with moulding on it,

an offering of all fruits, wineless.

Posideon, on the eighth of the waxing month, a round cake

of a choinix, twelve-knobbed, seated,

for Poseidon Chamaizelos, wineless. On the nineteenth,

for the Winds, a round cake of a choinix, with an upright knob, twelve-knobbed, wineless.

Gamelion: you shall crown the Dionysoi with ivy on the nineteenth.

Anthesterion: priests from the baths.

Elaphebolion, on the fifteenth, for Kronos a round cake,

twelve-knobbed, seated; you shall mould on it an ox of a choinix, without delay. Mounichion, on the second of the waning month, for Herakles and the divine one (?), 2 cockerels, round cakes

of a choinix, twelve-knobbed, with upright knobs,

without delay.
