**Part 1**

[...] as many as are inscribed on the stele or their descendants; and if anyone sacrifices

to the goddess, those of the orgeones who have a share in the sanctuary shall sacrifice free of charge;

and if any private person sacrifices to the goddess, he shall give to the priestess for a suckling animal 1½ ob.

and the skin and a whole right thigh, and for a full-grown animal 3 ob. and the skin and

the thigh in the same way, and for an ox 1 dr. and the skin; and the priestly dues from the female animals shall be given to the priestess, and from the male animals to the priest; and no one shall

sacrifice beside the altar in the sanctuary, or he shall owe 50 drachmas; and in order that the house and

the sanctuary may be repaired, the rent of the house and the water, for however much it is sold, (shall go) to the repair of the sanctuary [and the] house, and it shall be spent on nothing else, until the sanctuary and the house have been repaired, unless the orgeones vote something else;

[...] into the sanctuary; and they shall leave water for the occupant so that he can use it;

and if anyone proposes or puts to the vote contrary to this law, he shall owe 50 drachmas to the

goddess, both [the proposer and] the one who put it to the vote, and he shall have no share in the common affairs; and the superintendents shall inscribe

[him as owing] this money to the goddess on the stele;

and the superintendents and the religious officials shall hold a meeting and gathering in the sanctuary about the common affairs on the second day of each month; and each of the orgeones who have a share

in the sanctuary shall give to the religious officials for the sacrifice 2 drachmas

in Thargelion before the sixteenth, and whoever is resident in Athens and in good health and does not contribute shall owe 2 dr. sacred to the goddess; and so that there may be as many orgeones of the sanctuary as possible, it shall be possible for anyone who wishes, on paying

[...] drachmas, to have a share in the sanctuary and to be inscribed on the stele; and

the orgeones shall scrutinise those being inscribed on the stele and hand over [the names of those approved to the secretary] in Thargelion [...]
