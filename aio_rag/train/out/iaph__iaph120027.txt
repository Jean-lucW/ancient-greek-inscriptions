during his other visit he both honoured and adorned

us and gave varied displays of poems of every kind,

through which he both delighted the elders

and benefited the younger; and at all these things

the People, being pleased, ordered that the appropriate honours

be voted for him. It shall be decided that Gaius Julius

Longianus be made a citizen among us free of charge,

being both a good man and the best poet of

our time, and be honoured with the other rights of citizenship and with the

greatest honours deriving from the laws, and with bronze

statues, which shall be set up both in the other most

notable places of the city and in the precinct of the

Muses and in the gymnasium of the ephebes beside

the old Herodotos; and it shall be resolved also that his

books be publicly dedicated in the libraries

among us, so that in these too the young may be educated

in the same way as in the writings

of the ancients; and so that to the People of our kinsmen the Aphro-

disians our goodwill and zeal towards their citizen

may become clear, it shall be decided also that a copy

of this decree be sent through Julius himself

to the Aphrodisians, sealed with the public seal,

from which they too will learn both our

character, which we continually display towards all educated men

alike, and the honours with which we have honoured the man

as one who excels the others.
