Theoboulos son of [Theoboul]os proposed: for the good

fortune of the [People] of the Eleusinians and

of the Athenians, the Eleusinians [shall decide]:

since [Euthydemos] continues to be well disposed

to the People [of the] Eleusinians and

of the Athenians both privately and publicly, and

having been allotted demarch has served as demarch well and justly

and sacrificed the sacrifice to

Dionysos for the health and preservation

of the demesmen at his own expense, and

has shown love of honour towards the demesmen and

has made the income greater and

[managed] the other affairs of the demesmen well [and]

according to the laws, Euthydemos

shall have, [since this award was also given to]

his ancestors, a seat of honour, for himself [and his descendants],

and [the demarch in office at any]

time shall call him to the [seat of honour or]

shall owe 100 drachmas [sacred to Dionysos];

and to praise [Euthydemos son of Moir]-

okles of Eleusis [for his excellence]

and good will towards [the People of the]

Eleusinians and to crown [him with a]

foliage crown [...]
