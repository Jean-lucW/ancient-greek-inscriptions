(Decree for) [Eudem]os of Plataia.

Uninscribed space

[In the archon]ship of Aristophon (330/29),

in the ninth prytany, of Leontis,

for which Antidoros son of Antin[ous]

of Paiania was secretary. On the

eleventh of Thargelion, the nineteenth

of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee Anti-

phanes of Euonymon was putting to the vote. The

[People] decided. Lykourgos son of Lykophron

of [Bouta]dai proposed: since

[Eudem]os both previously announced

to the People that he would donate for

[the] war, should it be needed, [4000]

drachmas, and has now donated,

for the construction of the stadium

and the Panathenaic theatre,

one thousand yoke-teams, and these

he has sent, all of them, before the Panathe-

naia, as he promised, the People shall

decide: to praise Eudem[os]

son of [Phi]lourgos of Plata[ia] and

crown him with a foliage crown

for his good will towards the

Athenian People; and he shall be

among the benefactors of the

Athenian People, himself and

his descendants; and he shall have

right of ownership of land and a house; and

he shall serve on

military campaigns and pay

capital taxes with the Athenians;

and the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this decree

and stand it on the acropolis; and for

inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall [give 30]

drachmas from the People's fund for expenditure

on decrees.
