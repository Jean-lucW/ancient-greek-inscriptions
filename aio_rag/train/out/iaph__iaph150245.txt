The plot is (the property) of Adrastos son of Glykon son of Glykon son of Leon son of

Hekatomnos Polychronios, which plot Polychronia daughter of Kallikrates conceded to him,

on which plot he constructed a memorial, the sarcophagus set on the plot and the burial niches in it

and the rest in it; in which sarcophagus I buried Barilla my own wife; and I wish also myself

to be laid in the sarcophagus, but no one else; and in the first burial niche, lying below the sarcophagus, I wish

my wife and Polychronios my son to be buried; and in the other burial niche I wish to be laid

Tatianos and Adrastos my children; but no one else either in the sarcophagus or in the burial niches

to be laid; and if my heirs, after I have been laid in the sarcophagus, do not secure the closing-bar,

the goddess Aphrodite shall be my heir; and this the neopoioi in office at the time shall enforce, (and) in this matter

they shall be accountable; [and if] anyone [...] what I have arranged, buries anyone else, he shall be accursed and

shall pay in addition to the most sacred treasury 8 denarii, of which the third [part] shall belong to the prosecutor.
