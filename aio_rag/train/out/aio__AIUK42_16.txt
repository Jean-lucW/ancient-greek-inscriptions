Decree 1 (Honours for sacrifices)

[...] [proposed: concerning what] the superintendent [of the ephebes] reports,

[...] [-os son of -enides of Marathon, about] the sacrifice which he made [with the ephebes in the city hall, the entry-sacrifices to] Hestia and the other [gods for whom it was traditional]

on behalf of the Council and the [People and children and] women and the friends [and allies],

and that in all the offerings [the sacrifices] have turned out [fine and salutary]; for good fortune, [the Council shall decide]:

to accept the good things that occurred [in the sacrifices which he made for the] health and preservation of [the Council and]

[the People] and children and women and the friends; [and to praise] the superintendent [...] [-os]

[...] son of -enides of Marathon and the ephebes [and to crown each] of them [with a foliage crown for] their piety towards [the] gods [and their zeal and love of toil throughout the whole year],

[in order that], these things being brought to pass, the Council [may be seen to be honouring them worthily of the honour they deserve].
