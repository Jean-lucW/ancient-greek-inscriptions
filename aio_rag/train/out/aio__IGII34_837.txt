(Hymns) of Diophantos son of Diophantos of Sphettos

These words I, your loving temple-attendant, say to you,

Asklepios, child of Leto's son:

how shall I come to your golden house,

O blessed, longed-for one,

divine head, not having the feet

with which before I came to the sanctuary,

unless you, kindly, should be willing

to heal me and bring me in again,

so that I may look upon you, my god,

brighter than the earth in springtime?

These things I, Diophantos, pray to you:

save me, blessed one, most mighty,

by healing my evil gout,

by your father, to whom I pray greatly;

for none of the mortals upon the earth

could provide release from such pains.

You alone, divine blessed one, have the power;

for the gods who are pre-eminent over all

gave you, the compassionate one, a great gift

to mortals, a release from pains.

[Thrice]-blessed Paian Asklepios, by your skill

Diophantos (has been) [healed] of an incurable evil sore,

no longer seen crab-footed nor walking as if on

cruel thorns, but sound of foot, as you promised.
