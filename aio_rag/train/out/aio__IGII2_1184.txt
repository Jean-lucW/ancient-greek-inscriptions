And the women officials, both of them jointly,

shall give to the priestess for

the festival and the management

of the Thesmophoria a half-sixth

of barley, a half-sixth of wheat, a half-

sixth of barley meal, a half-sixth of wheat

flour, a half-sixth of dried figs, a chous

of wine, a half-chous of olive oil, two cups

of honey, a choinix of white sesame,

a choinix of black, a choinix

of poppy seed, two fresh cheeses

each weighing not less than a stater,

and two staters of garlic, and a torch

worth not less than two obols, and

4 drachmas of silver; and the women officials

shall give these things; and so that this may

be done on behalf of the deme of the Cholargians

in accordance with what is written for all

time, the officials in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3)

shall stand a stele and

inscribe this decree on a stone

stele in the Pythion; and whatever they spend

they shall account for to the Cholargians.
