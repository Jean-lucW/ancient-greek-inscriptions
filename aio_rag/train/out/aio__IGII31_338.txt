In the archonship of Nikokrates (333/2), in the first prytany, of Aigeis,

for which Archelas son of Chairias of

Pallene was secretary. On the ninth

of Metageitnion, the thirty-ninth of the

prytany. Of the presiding committee Nikias of Themakos

was putting to the vote. The People decided. Chaironides

son of Lysanias of Phlya proposed: concerning what the People

required the Council to formulate and bring

forward about Pytheas, how he shall be honoured

by the People, for good fortune, the People shall decide:

since Pytheas, having been elected over the fountains,

both manages the other matters in his office well

and with love of honour, and has now both built

the new fountain by the sanctuary of Ammon

and constructed the fountain at the sanctuary of Amphiaraos,

and has managed the water-channel and the underground conduits

there, to praise Pytheas

son of Sosidemos of Alopeke, when he has rendered his accounts,

and crown him with a gold crown

of 1000 drachmas for his excellence and justice

in the management of the fountains, so that

the others who are elected from time to time over

the fountains may also each show love of honour towards the

People; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree and that

of the Council on two stone stelai

and stand one

in the sanctuary of Ammon, and one at the sanctuary of

Amphiaraos; and for inscribing the stelai

the treasurer of the People shall give 20 drachmas for each

stele from the People's fund for expenditure
