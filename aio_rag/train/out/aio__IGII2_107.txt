**Part 1**

Gods.

[Of the Myt]ilenaeans.

[Nausig]enes was archon (368/7). Aiantis was in [prytany]. Moschos of Kydathenaion was [secretary]. Aristyllos of Erchia [was chairman].

The Council and the People [decided].

[Dio]ph[antos] proposed: concerning what the envoys who have come from Lesbos say, the Council shall resolve that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the next Assembly shall bring them before the [People], and submit the opinion of the Council to the [People] that it seems good to the Council: since the Mytilenaeans [are good] men with regard to the People of Athens both [now] and in [time past], to praise the People of Mytilene for their excellence [towards the] People of Athens; and they shall have access, [if they need anything], to the Council or the People first after the sacred business.

And [to praise] also Hieroitas because he is a good man with regard to

the People of Athens and that of Mytilene; and the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele [and] stand it on the acropolis; and [he shall] also inscribe on the same stele the decree which the People gave in reply to the envoys

[of the My]tilenaeans who came with [Hie]roitas; and for inscribing the stele the treasurer of the People [shall give] to the secretary of the Council 20 drachmas; and to praise the envoys who were

[sent] to Mytilene and invite them to dinner in

[the city] hall tomorrow; and to invite also the delegates of the Mytilenaeans to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow; and to invite also the delegates of the Methymnaeans

[and A]ntissaeans and Eresians and Pyrrhaeans to [hospitality]

[in the] city hall tomorrow.

Autolykos proposed: in other

respects in accordance with the Council; but to praise the envoys

who were sent to Lesbos, Timonothos and Autolykos and Aristopeithes, and invite them to dinner in the city hall tomorrow.
