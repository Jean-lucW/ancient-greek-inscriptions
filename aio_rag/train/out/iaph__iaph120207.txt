Decision of the Council and the People, proposal of the magistrates and of Hypsikles son of Hy[psikles]

son of Menandros, secretary of the People, and of Menippos son of Teimokles son of Po-

lemarchos, the general in charge of the territory: since Titus Antonius son of Adrastos, of the voting tribe Qui[ri]-

na, Lysimachos Grypos, a man of the first rank and of a family that has provided many great benefits in all respects

to the fatherland, and having himself from his first youth surpassed all in dignity

of character and in the conduct of a virtuous life, and having by his own deeds adorned the [glory] of his family

in the magistracies which he fulfilled and in services and embassies and costly market-overseerships

and contributions and magnanimous liturgies and a most costly high-priesthood,

has now died; and our People, grieved at what has happened, was led to honour

the man also after his death with honours and to crown him with the crown of

virtue, and also to console Antonia Tatia daughter of Neikoteimos, his mother;

the Council and the People shall decide to honour also after his death Antonius Lysimachos, a man

worthy of every honour, with the finest honours, and to crown him for

virtue with a crown, and also to comfort Antonia Tatia daughter of Neikoteimos, his

mother, to bear bravely what has befallen by the will of the divinity. (leaf)
