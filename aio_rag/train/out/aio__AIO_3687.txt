**Part 1**

When Euthippos was demarch.

The Teithrasians decided, Eudikos proposed: in order that the common property may be secure for the demesmen and the Teithrasians may know the existing [resources] and the incoming revenues, the demarch shall inscribe all those who have leased any of the common property in perpetuity.

The Teithrasians decided.
