On the twentieth of Theudaisios.

King Ptolemy and queen

Cleopatra his sister to the Cyrenaeans, greetings.

Of the letters which we have sent to those

appointed over the cities and of the ordinance which we have decided

to publish, we have appended

for you the copies, so that you may be informed.

Since, then, we are eager that none of those

ranked under the kingdom should

be harassed unreasonably, let the purport of the ordinance

be entered also in the judicial

regulation deposited among you, so that in future also

those making the requests,

following it, may do nothing at random.

Farewell. Year 9, Gorpieios 24,

Phamenoth 24.
