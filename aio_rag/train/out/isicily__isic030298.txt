In the archonship of

Artemidoros

son of Eielos and Gnaios

son of Oppios, of Panamos

on the new moon.

Since some of the cities, for the synoikismos

of the city, helped with grain, some both giving

it as a gift to the city, and others both measuring it out, and displ-

ayed great good will towards the people of the En-

tellans, and in place of a shortage of grain brought about

abundance; resolved by the council: that as many of the cities

as helped with grain or money or anything else for the synoikismos

of the city, they are to enjoy good will and common citizenship with

the people of the Entellans for all time, and

are to be invited to the games and to the seat of honour; and the private

individuals who [...] the city with grain for the syn-

oikismos are to be proxenoi of the city of the Entel-

lans, both themselves and their children. Resolved also by the assem-

bly. Of the Petrinoi, the community measured out medimnoi of wheat;

of the Kytattarinoi, the community gave as a gift wheat, bar-

ley; of the Scherinoi, the community gave as a gift wheat,

barley; of the Makellinoi, the community measured out whe-

at, medimnoi. Private individuals: the Petrinoi who measured out (grain)

are these: Theodoros son of Praton Sanneios, wheat;

Aischylos son of Praton Sanneios, wheat; Hera-

kleios son of Herakleidas, wheat; Arimnastos son of Simos;

Sosandros son of Ariston, wheat; Minatos Korouios

the Mamertine, barley. The ar-

chons are to write this decree on a bronze plaque and exhibit it in the

council chamber.
