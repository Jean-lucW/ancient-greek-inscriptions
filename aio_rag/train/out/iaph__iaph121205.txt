The tomb and the [tower?] built on it [...],

whose entrance is in front towards the south [...],

on a cleared site, were constructed by Hermogenes son of Menodoros [...]

-doros son of Demetrios, and Apollonios and Hermogenes and I- [...],

the children of Hermogenes, for themselves and for the [children and?]

descendants and successors to be born of them, no one having any authority [of the]

heirs or successors of theirs to alienate either the tower or

the tomb beneath it, either by way of sale or of cession, since

whoever does anything towards alienation in any way whatsoever, and he who accepts it,

shall be liable for impiety, and each of them shall pay into the imperial Fiscus

ten thousand denarii each, as if by a judgement, of which one third shall belong to the prosecutor; and what

has been done contrary shall be invalid, and none the less the provisions declared

regarding the consecrated things shall remain (in force). Of this inscription a copy was now deposited

in the property-archive in the stephanephorate of Attalos son of Adrastos

son of Neikoteimos, hero, for the second time, in the month Dios.
