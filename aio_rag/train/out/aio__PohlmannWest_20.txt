come, so that you may celebrate with songs your kinsman Phoibos, golden-haired, who over the double-peaked seat of this Parnassian rock, together with the far-famed Delphian maidens, comes to the streams of well-watered Kastalia,

frequenting the oracular hill on the Delphic

peak.

[Behold] the famous great city of Attica, which, through the prayers of arms-bearing Tritonis, dwells in an unravaged land; and on the holy altars Hephaistos burns the thighs of young bulls; and together with it the Arabian smoke spreads up to Olympos; and the lotus-flute, sounding clearly, weaves its song with varied melodies; and the golden sweet-voiced cithara rings out with hymns.
