In the archonship of Philokles (322/1), in the ninth prytany, of Oineis,

for which Euthygenes son of Hephaistodemos of Kephisia

was secretary. On the second of Thargelion,

the twenty-third of the prytany. Assembly.

Of the presiding committee Eualkos of Phaleron was putting to the vote.

The People decided. Diophantos son of Phrasikleides of Myrrhinous

proposed: in other respects in accordance with the Council;

but since Euenor son of Euepios became proxenos

of the Athenian People for his benefaction, and takes care of everything

that the Athenian People instructed him, both individually

and collectively, for good fortune, the People shall resolve:

to praise Euenor son of Euepios and crown him

with a foliage crown for his love of honour [and]

care; and he and his descendants shall have right of ownership [of land and]

a house, keeping away from public [and sacred]

property; and [the Council in office at any time]
