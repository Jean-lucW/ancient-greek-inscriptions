The Council

and the People decided,

and (on the proposal of) Pereitas

son of Antiochos, secretary

of the People,

and of Attalos son of

Menandros, general in charge

of the territory:

since Quintus Oppius son of Quintus, praetor and proconsul of the Romans, has sent (word) that Laodikeia and

he himself are under siege, and the People decided to send help in full force, and that the resident foreigners and the slaves should also march out with them, and chose in

the Assembly a man to lead them; and it is necessary also to despatch ambassadors who will make clear to the

proconsul the policy which our People holds towards the Romans, who are saviours and benefactors, and, if the praetor

orders anything else of the city, to make arrangements so that it may be made clear and carried out; the People shall decide to choose as ambassadors men from among those who are honoured

and who have good faith and are well disposed towards the Romans, who, having arrived before Quintus Oppius the

proconsul, shall make clear to him the policy which our People holds towards him and towards all the Romans, and shall show that not

only did we decide to fight as allies in full force, but we also chose a man to be in charge of the alliance,

Artemidoros the stephanephoros, a man from among those who are honoured

and who have good faith, and who excels in valour in arms;

and they shall make clear to him that our whole People, with women

and children and all its livelihood, is ready to risk itself on behalf of

Quintus and the affairs of the Romans, and that without the

leadership of the Romans we do not even choose to live.
