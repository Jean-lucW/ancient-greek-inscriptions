The Council and the People decided.

Thoukritos son of Alkimachos of Myrrhinous proposed:

since Prytanis, being of good will to the People, and

the generals having often declared that he had also previously given

proof of it, having been summoned

by the People and having given himself without

excuse to the common need of the city, went

abroad ⟦...⟧, taking into account neither

toil nor danger, none

of those that would come, nor caring about any expense,

and having arrived ⟦...⟧ and having spoken

on behalf of the common interests with frank-

ness as if for his own fatherland, making every

effort, he has reported to the People about these

matters, having on no occasion fallen short

in eagerness at all, nor omitting any of the things fitting for the

need of the People; in order therefore that the People may be seen

at every opportunity to be mindful of those who have zealously

rendered services to it, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the forthcoming Assembly shall put

these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise Prytanis son of Astyleides of Karystos

and to crown him with a gold crown according to

the law for his good will and love of honour which he

continues to have for the Council and the

Athenian People, and to announce this

crown at the tragedies of the City Dionysia in the

new competition and at the gymnastic competition of the Panathenaia;

and the generals and

the treasurer of the military fund shall take care of the making of the crown and the

announcement; and it shall be possible for him,

on maintaining his preference, to obtain from the

People also other benefit, whatever he may be deemed worthy of;

and to invite him also to dinner in the city

hall tomorrow; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in the agora; and the

expense accrued for the setting up and the

inscribing of the stele the treasurer

of the military fund shall allocate.
