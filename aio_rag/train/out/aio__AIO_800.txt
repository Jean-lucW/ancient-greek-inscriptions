**Part 1**

Theodotos was archon (387/6). Paramythos son of Philagros of Erchia was secretary.

The People decided. Theodotos was archon. Kekropis was the prytany. Paramythos was secretary. Daiphron was chairman. Poliagros proposed: to praise the People of the Klazomenians because it is keen towards the city of the Athenians both

now and in time past; and concerning what they say, the People shall decide that, provided the Klazomenians pay the twentieth (tax) imposed in the time of

Thrasyboulos, concerning peace and war with those at Chyton, and concerning the hostages from Chyton whom the Klazomenians hold, the [People of the Klaz]omenians shall be sovereign; and it shall not be possible for [any] of the generals [either] to restore the exiles without the [People of the Klazo]menians or to expel any of those who remain; [and concerning a governor] and a garrison, [the People] shall vote [at] once whether it is necessary to establish (them) in Klaz[omenai, or whether] the [People of the Klazo]menians shall have full power concerning these matters, whether it wishes to receive (them) [or not]; [the cities] from which the Klazomenians import corn, [Phokaia and Chi]os [and S]myrna, shall be within the treaty for [them to sail into the harbours]; and the generals [...] [shall take care that] there shall be treaties [...]
