Gods.

Kallistratos son of Termonios the Achaean

proposed: since the cavalry commanders and

the commanders of tribal cavalry contingents in the archonship of Nikias (282/1)

have continued to fulfil

their office according to the laws and

have taken care of the grain rations so that

both the cavalry and the Tarantines might receive them in full,

and they have continued also in all other respects

both individually and collectively to show love of honour

towards both the cavalry and

the Tarantines, for good fortune,

the Tarantines shall decide to

praise the cavalry commanders and

the commanders of tribal cavalry contingents, Nikogenes son of

Arkesas of Euonymon, Alkimachos

son of [Kl]eoboulos of Myrrhinous,

[Dio]nysios son of Pythokritos of Gargettos,

[Me]non son of Hippolochides of Hippotomadai,

Kallidemos son of Kallimachos of Euonymon,

Kalliades son of Aristophon of Steiria,

Lysippos son of Lysippos of Kettos,

Charias son of Charias of Kephale,

Antimachos son of Kleagoras of Acharnai,

Proxenos son of Prokles of Phlya,

Thrasykles son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia,

Aristophon son of Aristoteles of Oinoe,

Python son of Pytharchos of Alopeke,

and to crown each of them with a gold

crown according to the law for their justice

and love of honour

towards both the cavalry and the

corps of the Tarantines, so that also

the other cavalry commanders and commanders of tribal cavalry contingents

who are appointed may show love of honour

towards both the cavalry and the

Tarantines, knowing that they will receive thanks

worthy of their benefactions;

and the secretaries of the cavalry commanders shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and

stand it in the stoa of the Herms.
