[In the archonship of Nikias] of Otryne (266/5), [in the sixth prytany, of -is],

for which Isokrates son of Isokrates [of Alopeke] was

secretary. On the eleventh of Posideon, the twelfth of the

prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Mne-

sistratos son of Sosippos of Kydathenaion was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided. Phytios son of Pamphilos of Thorikos

proposed: since Strombichos, previously serving as a soldier

with Demetrios and having been left behind in the town with Spin-

tharos, when the People took up arms for its freedom

and called on the soldiers too to side

with the city, obeyed the People in the cause of freedom,

and sided with the city in arms, thinking that he should not stand

in the way of what was in the interests of the city but share responsibility

for its preservation, and he joined the People in the siege of the Mouseion

and after affairs had been concluded in favour of the city

he has continued to provide its remaining needs unhesitatingly

and has remained steadfast in his good will for the People, and

when war came he was a good man [with regard to the People and]

[in his efforts] on [its] behalf [he did whatever the]

[general] ordered, and [etc.]
