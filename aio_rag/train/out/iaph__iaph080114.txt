Imperator Caesar ⟦Gaius Messius Quintus T[raia]nus

D[ec]ius⟧ Pius Felix Augustus, holding the tribunician

power for the third time, consul for the second time, designated for the third,

father of the fatherland, proconsul; and ⟦Quintus Herennius Etrusc[us]

Messius Decius⟧, pontifex maximus, holding the tribunician power

for the first time, consul designate, to the Aphrodisians'

magistrates and Council and People, greetings.

It was fitting that you, both on account of the goddess who gives her name to the city and

on account of your kinship and loyalty towards the Romans, should have rejoiced

at the establishment of our reign

and should have offered due sacrifices and prayers. And we too

preserve for you your existing freedom

and all the other rights which you have obtained from the

Emperors before us, being ready to

increase your hopes for the future.

The ambassadors were Aurelii Theodoros and Onesimos.

Farewell.
