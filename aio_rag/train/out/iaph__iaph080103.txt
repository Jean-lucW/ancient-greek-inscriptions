Imperator Caesar Marcus Antonius Gordianus Pius Feli[x Aug]ustus, po[ntif]ex maximus, holding the tri[buni]

cian power for the 6th time, consul for the 2nd time, father of the fatherland, proconsul, to the Magistrates, Council and People of the Aphrod[isians,]

greetings. The resolution of Asia which brought you also into a sh[aring] in the help for those who have suffered misfortune

was not an order, for it is not possible to use an order towards free men, but a good

civic act, establishing you in participation in a humane deed, and of such a kind as you yourselves also perform when in the con-

struction of some building you join in assisting with the restoration for those in need; and concerning the fu-

ture there is no need at all to fear; for to free men, in which (status) you share in the fullest measure, the only law regarding such matters

is that of free will. Aurelius Ktesias and A[e]lius Kallikrates served as ambassadors. Farew[ell.]
