Chairedemos son of Epicharinos of Kolonos proposed: since Hera-

kleitos son of Asklepiades of Athmonon, both previously, when stationed with king

Antigonos, continued saying and doing

on behalf of the People of the Salaminians whatever he understood to be bene-

ficial, and for those of the Salaminians who came privately to the

king sharing in their love of honour so that none of them, so far as was possible,

should go away having achieved nothing, and now, having been appointed by the

king general in charge of the Piraeus and the other places

assigned together with the Piraeus, continues to take much forethought

that no wrongdoing should occur in the country-

side, and when the walls on the island had fallen he shared in taking care

that they should be rebuilt, and when the war took place concerning A-

lexander and pirates were sailing out from Epilimnion

he took every forethought that nothing harmful should occur in

the countryside, conducting his management of these things in accordance

with the policy of the king towards the People; and further, when a person

was seized from the island and carried off to the [enemy],

he ransomed him and punished the wrongdoers, making it clear

[that] he will not abandon to wrongdoers what is in the city and in the country-

[side], and he makes himself useful both collectively and individually to any of the Sal-

aminians who wishes, and he continues to do in other respects what is beneficial to the Salaminians,

[both individually] and collectively; for good fortune, the People of the Sa-

[laminians] shall decide [to praise] Herakleitos son of Asklepiades of Athmonon [...]
