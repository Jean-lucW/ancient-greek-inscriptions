**Part 1**

For good fortune. In the archonship of Phanomachos. In Mounichion; at the Herakleia.

On the following terms the gene reconciled with each other, that of the Sounians and that

from the Seven Tribes, under the chosen conciliators, Antigenes of Semachidai and Kal⟦liteles⟧ of Sounion:

that of the precinct of Herakles there shall be left unused (the area) as far as the altars and beyond the stand as far as the first olive trees; ⟦ ⟧ and the rest of the precinct shall be bounded on the north by the first

wall, on the east (literally, where the sun rises) by where the

boundary markers lie that mark the plots, on the west by the stepping-stones

both those by the sea and the one lying above, and this

precinct shall be common to both gene. And the Salaminioi from Sounion shall construct a threshing-floor at their own expense in the common precinct of the same size as their own, and this threshing-floor shall belong to the Salaminioi from the Seven Tribes. And of the houses, the one bordering on the precinct shall belong to the Old Salaminioi from the Seven Tribes as far as both the outer doors lead from the sea and as far as the boundary markers of the plots mark in a straight line; and the other house, the one towards the east, shall belong to the Old Salaminioi from Sounion as far as the outer doors lead from the sea and as far as the boundary markers mark in a straight line

those of the plots. And the gardens and the well shall be half for each genos, and the salt-works and the agora in Koile shall be common to both gene, and of the plots those towards the east shall belong to the Old Salaminioi from Sounion as the boundary markers lie; and those towards the west shall belong to the Old Salaminioi from the Seven Tribes as the boundary markers lie, and (so shall) the sacred field.
