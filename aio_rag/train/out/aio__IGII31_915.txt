In the archonship of Menekles (267/6), in the eighth prytany, of Pandionis,

for which Theodoros son of Lysitheos

of Trikorynthos was secretary. On the old and new day

of Anthesterion. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

-doros son of Nausimachos of Euonymon was putting to the vote and his fellow

presiding committee members. The People decided. Kallistratos

son of Glaukon of Kropidai proposed: concerning what

the superintendents of the Mysteries report about the

sacrifice which they made at the Mysteries at Agrai,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide that the

presiding committee allotted to preside in the

People at the coming Assembly shall present

them to the People and put the matter on the agenda, and

submit the opinion of the Council to the People,

that it seems good to the Council that the Council and the People should accept

the good things which they say occurred

in the sacrifices which they made for the health and preservation

of the Council and the People and of all others

who are well disposed and friendly to the People; and since

the superintendents of the Mysteries both previously

at the sacrifice of the Great Mysteries took care

of the sacrifice and have now made the sacrifices for

preservation to the Goddesses on behalf of the Council and the People

from their own resources, and have taken care of the other matters

well and with love of honour, to praise the superintendents

of the Mysteries, Philoktemon son of Glaukon

of Kropidai, Demokrates son of Epikrates of Kerameis,

for their piety towards the Goddesses and

their love of honour towards the [Council and the People]; and

the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stele [...]
