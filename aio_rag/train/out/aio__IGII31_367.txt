In the archonship of Antikles (325/4), in the fifth prytany, of Aigeis,

for which Antiphon son of Koroibos of Eleusis was

secretary. On the eleventh. On the thirty-fourth of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee Phylyllos of Eleusis was putting to the vote.

Demosthenes son of Demokles of Lamptrai proposed: since

Herakleides of Salamis continues to show love of honour

towards the Athenian People and to do what

good he can, and previously donated in the

grain shortage 3000 medimnoi of wheat at 5 dr.,

the first of the merchants who sailed in, and again,

when the donations were made, he donated 3000 dr.

for the purchase of grain, and in other respects continues to be well disposed and to show

love of honour towards the People, the People shall decide:

to praise Herakleides son of Charikleides of Salamis

and crown him with a gold crown for his good will

and love of honour towards the Athenian People;

and that he shall be proxenos and benefactor of the

Athenian People, he and his descendants; and that they shall have

the right of ownership of land and a house according to the law, and

shall perform military service and pay

the property-based taxes with the Athenians; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree

and the other praises that have been given to

him on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis; and

for inscribing the stele the treasurer shall give

30 dr. from the People's fund for expenditure

on decrees. uninscribed space

Telemachos son of Theangelos of Acharnai proposed: since

Herakleides of Salamis donated grain to the

People at five drachmas, the first of the merchants who sailed in,

in the archonship of Aristophon (330/29), the People shall resolve

to praise Herakleides son of Charikleides of

Salamis and crown him with a gold crown

for his love of honour towards the Athenian

People; and since, when he was sailing to Athens, he was brought into port by the Herakleians

and his sails were taken from him by them, to elect as ambassador

one man from all Athenians, who shall go

to Herakleia to Dionysios and request him to return

the sails of Herakleides and in future not to wrong

any of those sailing to Athens; and acting in this way he will do what is

just and will not fail to obtain from the People

anything that is just; and the treasurer of the People shall give to the ambassador elected

50 dr. for travelling expenses from the

People's fund for expenditure on decrees. Elected

as ambassador: Thebagenes of Eleusis. uninscribed space

Kephisodotos son of Euarchides of Acharnai proposed: concerning the proposal that the

People instructed the Council to formulate about

Herakleides of Salamis, the Council shall decide:

since Herakleides, having sailed into Athens bringing grain,

donated to the People three thousand medimnoi

at five drachmas each, that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the first Assembly shall present

Herakleides to the People and put the matter on the agenda,

and submit the opinion of the Council to the People,

that it seems good to the Council to praise Herakleides son of

Charikleides of Salamis and crown him with a gold

crown of 500 dr.; and that he shall be permitted

to obtain from the People whatever good he can, so that

others may also show love of honour, knowing that the Council honours and crowns those who

show love of honour. uninscribed space
