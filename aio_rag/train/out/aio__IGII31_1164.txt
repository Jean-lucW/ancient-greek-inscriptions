In the archonship of Diokles (214/3), in the thirteenth prytany, of Hippothontis, for which

Aristophanes son of Stratokles of Keiriadai

was secretary. Decrees of the Council. On the third

of Skirophorion. The third of the prytany.

Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding committee

Phanokles son of Hippomachos of Angele was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and the People decided.

Demokrates son of Sounieus of Kolonos proposed: since

the superintendents of the Mysteries who were elected

for the year in the archonship of Diokles both

made the sacrifices, as many as fell to them in the year,

to Demeter and Kore and the other gods

for whom it was traditional, on behalf of the Council and the People and children

and women, and made also the preliminary sacrifices which are required,

and prepared the cart at their own expense for

the conveying of the sacred things, and the sum allocated to them

for the price of the cart they gave as a contribution to the Council,

and they managed also the driving to the sea

and the reception of Iakchos at Eleusis, and likewise

also the Mysteries at Agrai, which took

place twice in the year because of the celebration of

the Eleusinia, and they also sent to the

Eleusinia a bull as a sacrifice, and they also distributed meat

to the Council of the six hundred and fifty,

and concerning all these things they have submitted

accounts to the auditors and to [the]

Metroon and have rendered their accounts in

the court according to the laws, and all the

other things which were appropriate for the sacrifices

they have spent from their own resources, showing love of honour

towards the Council and the People; in order, therefore,

that there may be an incentive for those who love honour,

knowing that they will receive thanks worthy [of what]

benefactions they confer, for good fortune, the Council shall

decide, that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People,

that it seems good to the Council to praise the

superintendents of the Mysteries in the archonship of [Dio]-

kles, Thrasykles [...]

of Auridai, Niketes son of Niketes of P-,

and crown each [of them with a myrtle]

crown for [their] piety [towards]

the gods and love of honour [towards the Council]

and the People; [and it shall be possible for them also in]

future, if they show love of honour, [to obtain another benefit]

from the [People, whatever they may be deemed worthy]

of; and [the prytany] secretary shall inscribe [this decree]

[on two] stone [stelai and stand one in the court]

of the sanctuary [at Eleusis, and the other ...]
