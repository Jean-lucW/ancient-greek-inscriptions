(Altar) of Zeus Phratrios.

The priest ⟦⟦Theodoros⟧ son of Eupha⟦ntid⟧es⟧ inscribed and set up the stele.

The following priestly dues shall be given to the priest: from the meion, a thigh, a side, an ear, 3 ob. of money; from the koureion, a thigh, a side, an ear, a flat cake of one choinix, a half-chous of wine, 1 dr. of money.

The phrateres decided the following, in

the archonship of Phormion at Athens (396/5), and when Pantakles of Oion was phratriarch.

Hierokles proposed: as many as have not yet been adjudicated in accordance with the law of the Demotionidai, the phrateres shall adjudicate concerning them forthwith, having made a promise by Zeus Phratrios, taking the ballot from the altar. Whoever is judged to have been introduced without being a phrater, the priest and the phratriarch shall erase his name from the register kept among the Demotionidai and from the copy. And the one who introduced the person rejected shall owe a hundred drachmas sacred to Zeus Phratrios; and the priest and the phratriarch shall exact this money, or owe it themselves. And the adjudication

in future shall be in the year after that in which the koureion is sacrificed, on Koureotis day of the Apatouria; and they shall take the ballot from the altar. And if anyone wishes to appeal to the Demotionidai concerning those whom they vote out, it shall be possible for him; and the house of the Dekeleieis shall choose as advocates in their cases five men over thirty years of age, and these

the phratriarch and the priest shall put on oath to advocate what is most just and not

to allow anyone who is not a phrater to be a member of the phratry. Whichever of those who have appealed the Demotionidai vote out, he shall owe a thousand drachmas sacred to Zeus Phratrios, and this money shall be exacted by the priest

of the house of the Dekeleieis, or he shall owe it himself. And it shall be possible also for any other of the phrateres who wishes to exact it for the common fund. These things shall apply from the archonship of Phormion. And the phratriarch shall put to the vote concerning those whom it is necessary to ⟦adjudica⟧te during the year,

each year. And if he does not put it to the vote, he shall owe five hundred drachmas sacred to Zeus

And if any of these things prevents it, wherever the priest gives notice in writing, there they shall bring the meia and the koureia. And he shall give notice on the fifth day before the Dorpia on a whitened board not less than a span in size, wherever the Dekeleieis frequent in the city. And the priest shall inscribe this decree and the priestly dues on a stone stele in front of the altar at Dekeleia at his own expense. Nikodemos proposed: in other respects in accordance ⟦with the previous decrees which exist concerning the introduction of the children and the adjudication. And the three witnesses, who it has been stated are to be provided at the preliminary examination, from his own thiasos members, testifying to what is asked⟧

and swearing by Zeus Phratrios.

And the witnesses shall testify and swear holding the altar. And if there are not so many in number in that thiasos, he shall provide them from the other phrateres. When

the adjudication takes place, the phratriarch shall not give the ballot concerning the children to all the phrateres before the thiasos members of the person being introduced have voted, taking the ballot secretly from the altar. And the phratriarch shall count their ballots in the presence of all the phrateres present at the meeting and announce which way they

have voted. And if, when the thiasos members have voted that he is their phrater, the other phrateres vote him out, the thiasos members shall owe a hundred drachmas sacred to Zeus Phratrios, except those of the

thiasos members who appear as accusers or opponents

in the adjudication. But if

the thiasos members vote him out, and the introducer appeals to the whole body, and the whole body decides that he is a phrater, he shall be enrolled in the common registers. But if the whole body votes him out, he shall owe a hundred drachmas sacred to Zeus Phratrios. And if,

when the thiasos members have voted him out, he does not appeal to the whole body, the rejection by the thiasos members shall be valid. And the thiasos members shall not, with the other phrateres, take the

ballot concerning the children from their own

thiasos. And the priest shall inscribe this decree in addition on the stone stele. Oath of the witnesses at the introduction of the children: 'I testify that the one whom he is introducing is his own son, legitimate, born of a wedded wife; this is true, by Zeus Phratrios; if I keep my oath may many good things be mine, ⟦but if⟧ I break it, the opposite.'
