**Part 1**

[The Council and the People decided.] [...]

[...] was in prytany. Kall- [...] of Alopeke was secretary. Simon [...] was chairman. Astyphilos proposed: concerning

what the Methymnaians say, since the Methymnaians are allies and well disposed to the city of Athens, so that

they may have the alliance also with the other allies of the Athenians, the secretary of the Council shall inscribe them, just as the other allies have been inscribed; and the embassy of the Methymnaians shall swear the same

oath as the other allies also

swore, to the representatives of the allies and to the generals and to the

hipparchs; and the representatives of the allies

and the generals and the hipparchs shall swear to the Methymnaians in the same way; and Aisimos and the representatives in charge of the oaths shall take care that the officials of the Methymnaians swear just as the other allies did;

and to praise the city of the Methymnaians and to invite the ambassadors of the

Methymnaians to hospitality.
