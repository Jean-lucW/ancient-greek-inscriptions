**Part 1**

Famous for wisdom and revealer of the holy nights

of Deo and Kore, you see the pure attendant,

who once, shunning the lawless deed of the Sarmatians,

saved the rites and his life for his fatherland,

and displayed the ceremonies and won glory equal

to wise Eumolpos and most holy Keleos,

and initiated the Ausonian, renowned Antoninus
