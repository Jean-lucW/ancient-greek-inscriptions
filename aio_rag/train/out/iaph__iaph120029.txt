The Council and the People and the Gerous[ia]

honoured with the first honours

Tata daughter of Diodoros son of Diodoros son by

birth of Leon, chaste priestess of Hera for life,

mother of the city, who became

and remained the wife of Attalos son of Pytheas,

stephanephoros, and herself of a first

and illustrious family; having been priestess of the

Augusti for a second time; having provided oil

twice in handfuls from overflowing basins

most lavishly for the greater part also of the

night; having been stephanephoros; having sacrificed

throughout the whole years for the health

of the Augusti; having feasted the People

many times also with banquets for the whole people;

and at the musical and theatrical contests

having herself for the first time brought the foremost

performers in Asia and shown

them to her fatherland, so that for the display of the

performers the neighbouring cities came together

and joined in the festival; a woman

who spared no expense,

generous, adorned with virtue and chastity.
