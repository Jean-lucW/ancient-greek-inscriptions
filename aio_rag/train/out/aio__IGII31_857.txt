In the archonship of Olympiodoros (293/2), when Epikouros

son of Epiteles of Rhamnous was recorder, in the tenth prytany,

of Pandionis. On the old and new day of Mounichion,

the first of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding

committee Nikoboulos son of Nikias of Phrearrhioi was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. Stratokles son of

Euthydemos of Diomeia proposed: since both the ancestors

of Philippides continued to be good men

towards the Athenian People and on all

occasions demonstrating their good will and love of

honour, provided many great services

to the People, and spent much money on the voluntary contributions and trierarchies

and choregic sponsorships and the other liturgies

from their own resources, of which as reminders in

the sanctuaries of the city tripods and the other dedications

have been left behind; and they held fine and

glorious generalships both by land and by

sea; and Philippides himself, valuing most highly

the good will and love of honour towards the People

that he has inherited from his ancestors, has continued

to demonstrate it both in the voluntary contributions and

trierarchies and choregic sponsorships and the other liturgies

and all acts of love of honour; and having been elected

general in charge of the fleet he served as general

well and in the interests of the People; and being king (archon)

he sacrificed all the sacrifices which it was proper for him to sacrifice on behalf of

the city piously and in accordance with ancestral custom,

and he has taken care of everything else in his office which the laws

prescribed for him well and

incorruptibly; and having been chosen competition-master he has put on

all the competitions for the gods well and with love of

honour, and has taken care of all the other superintendencies to which

the People or the Council has elected him

justly; and he has undertaken fine embassies in the interests of

the People; and the same man has shown himself a champion

for the interests of his fatherland on all

occasions; the People shall decide to praise

Philippides son of Philomelos of Paiania for both the

[good order and excellence] of his ancestors and for his love of honour

[towards the People, and to crown] him with a gold

[crown of 300 drachmas]; and to announce the crown

[at the tragedy competition of the City Dionysia] and the Demetrieia;

[and the competition-master] shall take care of the announcement;

and the People shall also stand [a bronze statue] of him

[in the agora]; and he and

[whoever at any time is the eldest] of his descendants shall have meals in the city hall and

[a seat of honour at all the] competitions that the city puts

on; [and the] recorder [shall inscribe this] decree

[on stone stelai and] stand one on the acropolis

[and the other beside the statue]; and it shall be possible for Philippides

[to have inscribed in addition on the stelai] the benefactions

[and voluntary contributions and] trierarchies and the other

[liturgies performed] by his ancestors

[and himself; and the expenditure] for the making of the

[crown and the statue] and the inscribing of the

[stelai shall be allocated by the] administrator.
