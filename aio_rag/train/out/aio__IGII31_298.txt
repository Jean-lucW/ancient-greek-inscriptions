Relief

To Spartokos, Pairisades,

Apollonios, sons of Leukon.

Uninscribed space, perhaps once painted

In the archonship of Themistokles (347/6),

in the eighth prytany, of Aigeis,

for which Lysimachos son of Sosidemos

of Acharnai was secretary. Theophilos

of Halimous was chairman.

Androtion son of Andron of Gargettos proposed: concerning

what Spartokos and Pairisades wrote in their letter

and what the ambassadors who have come from them

report, to reply to them that the People

of Athens praises Spartokos and

Pairisades, because they are good men, and they promise

the People of Athens that they will take care

of the despatch of the grain, just as

their father took care, and that they will serve

eagerly in whatever the People needs; and

the ambassadors shall report to them that, doing these things,

they will not fail to obtain anything from the People

of Athens; and since they give the gifts

to the Athenians which Satyros and Leukon gave,

Spartokos and Pairisades shall have the

gifts which [the People] gave to Satyros and Leukon,

and to crown each of them with a gold crown at the Great

Panathenaia of a thousand drachmas;

and the games-organisers [shall have] the crowns made

in the year before the Great

Panathenaia, according to the decree of the People

previously voted for Leukon, and

to announce that 'The People of Athens crowns

Spartokos and Pairisades the sons of Leukon

for their excellence and good will towards

the People of Athens'; and since they dedicate

the crowns to Athena Polias,

the games-organisers shall dedicate the crowns in the temple,

inscribing on them, 'Spartokos

and Pairisades sons of Leukon dedicated (this)

to Athena, having been crowned by the People

of Athens'; and the treasurer of the People shall give the money

to the games-organisers for the crowns

from the People's fund for expenditure on decrees;

and for the present

the receivers shall hand over the money for the crowns

from the military funds; and

the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it near

that of Satyros and Leukon; and for the

inscribing the treasurer of the People shall give thirty

drachmas; and to praise the ambassadors

Sosis and Theodosios, because they take care of

those who come from Athens to the Bosporos,

and to invite them to hospitality in the city hall

tomorrow; and concerning the money

owed to the sons of Leukon, so that

they may recover it, the presiding committee

allotted to preside in the People [on the]

[eighteenth] shall put it on the agenda first after the sacred business, so that,

having recovered the money, they may not make complaints [against the]

People of Athens; and to give [the]

assistants which Spartokos and Pairis[ades] request;

and the ambassadors shall register the names [of the]

assistants whom they receive with the secretary [of the]

Council; and those whom they register shall be deemed

to have done their duty by doing whatever good [they can]

for the sons of Leukon. P[olyeukt]-

os son of Timokrates of Krioa proposed: [in other respects]

in accordance with Androtion, but to crown [also Apol]-

lonios the son of Leukon from [the same funds]. Uninscribed space
