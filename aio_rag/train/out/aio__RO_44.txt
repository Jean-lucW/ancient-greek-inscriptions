**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Nikophemos (361/0).

Alliance of the Athenians and

the Thessalians for all time.

The Council and the People decided. Leontis was in prytany; Chairion son of Charinautes of Phaleron was secretary; Archippos of Amphitrope was chairman; on the twelfth of the prytany. Exekestides proposed: concerning what the ambassadors of the Thessalians say, the People shall resolve: to accept the alliance, for good fortune, on the terms that the Thessalians offer; and they shall have the alliance with the Athenians for all time; and all the allies of the Athenians shall also be allies of the Thessalians and those

of the Thessalians (allies) of the Athenians; and of the Athenians the generals and the Council and the cavalry commanders and the cavalry shall swear this oath: 'I shall help with all my strength as far as possible if anyone goes against the federation of the Thessalians for war, or overthrows the archon whom the Thessalians chose, or

sets up a tyrant in Thessaly'; and they shall swear in addition the

customary oath; and so that the Thessalians too may swear to the city, the People shall choose five men from all the Athenians, who having arrived in Thessaly shall administer to Agelaos the archon and the polemarchs and

the cavalry commanders and the cavalry and the [hieromnemones]

and the other officials who hold office on behalf of the federation of the Thessalians this oath: 'I shall help with all my strength as far as possible if anyone goes against the city of the Athenians for war or overthrows the People of the Athenians'; and the ambassadors of the Thessalians who are staying at Athens shall also swear in

the Council the same oath; and it shall not be possible to end the war against Alexander, [either] for the Thessalians without the Athenians [or] for the Athenians without the archon and the federation [of the Thessalians]; and to praise Agelaos the archon [and the federation] of the Thessalians, because they did everything well and enthusiastically concerning what the city announced to them; and to praise

[also] the ambassadors of the Thessalians who have come, and to invite them to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow; and the stele with Alexander, the one about the alliance, shall be taken down by the

[treasurers] of the goddess; and to the ambassadors the [treasurer] of the [People] shall give for travelling expenses 20 drachmas each; and this alliance the

secretary of the Council shall inscribe on a [stone] stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and for [inscribing] the stele the treasurer of the People shall give 20 drachmas; and Theaitetos of Erchia also, as one who speaks best and does whatever

good he can for the People of the Athenians and the Thessalians, shall be in the assigned position (?).
