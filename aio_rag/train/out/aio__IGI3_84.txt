The Council and the People decided; Pandionis was in prytany; Aristox-

enos was secretary; Antiochides was chairman; Antiphon (418/7) was archon; Adosio-

s proposed: to fence the sanctuary of Kodros and Neleus and Basile and

to lease out the precinct in accordance with the contract specifications; and the official sellers shall let out the contract for the fencing;

and the king shall lease out the precinct in accordance with

the specifications, and they shall send the boundary-setters to mark out the boundaries of these sanctuaries,

so that they may be as well and as piously as possible; and the money for the fencing

shall come from the precinct; and they shall do these things before this

Council leaves office, or each shall be called to account for one thousand drachmas in accordance with what has been

stated. Adosios proposed: in other respects in accordance with the Council; but the king shall

lease out, and the official sellers, the precinct of Neleus and Basile in

accordance with the specifications for twenty years; and the lessee shall fence the sanc-

tuary of Kodros and Neleus and Basile at his own expense; and whatever

rent the precinct brings in each year,

he shall pay the money in the ninth prytany to the receivers,

and the receivers shall hand it over to the treasurers of the Other Gods

in accordance with the law; and if the king does not do what has been voted, or any oth-

er of those to whom these matters have been assigned, in the prytany of Aigeis,

he shall be called to account for ten thousand drachmas; and the man who has bought the mud shall carry

it away out of the ditch during this Council, having paid the money

to Neleus, as much as he bought it for; and the king shall erase the buyer of

the mud, when he has paid the price; and the lessee of the

precinct, and for how much he leases it, the king shall inscribe opposite on the

wall, and the guarantors, in accordance with the law which is laid down concerning the precincts;

and this decree, so that it may be possible for anyone who wishes to know, having inscribed it

the secretary of the Council shall set it down on a stone stele in the Neleion

beside the stands; and the kolakretai shall give the money for this.

And the king shall lease the precinct of Neleus and Basile on

these terms: the lessee shall fence the sanctuary of Kodros and Neleus

and Basile in accordance with the specifications during the incoming

Council, and shall work the precinct of Neleus and Basile on these terms:

he shall plant olive seedlings, not less than two hundred, and more if he

wishes; and the lessee shall have control of the ditch and of the water from the sanctuary of Zeus,

as much as flows between the Dionysion and the gates by which the initiates drive

out to the sea, and as much as between the public house and

the gates which lead out to the bath of Isthmonikos; and he shall lease it for

twenty years.
