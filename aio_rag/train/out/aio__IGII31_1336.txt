Gods.

In the archonship of Xenokles (168/7), in the twelfth prytany, of Oineis,

for which Sthenedemos son of Asklepiades of Teithras was secretary.

On the old and new day of Skirophorion, the thirty-first of the prytany.

Assembly convened in the theatre in accordance with a decree, which Arist-

of Semachidai proposed. Of the presiding committee Alexidemos son of -

-kos of Koile was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and the [People] decided.

Artemon son of Polymelos of Piraeus proposed: since Diodoros, being a [friend]

of king Eumenes and his brothers and [...]

being especially honoured by them and advanced, [is] well-disposed

collectively to the People, saying and doing on behalf of the city whatever [good]

he can, and individually [always] to those of the citizens who meet [him]

makes himself useful, and to the

ambassadors sent by the People he continues to give assistance

[zeal]ously, concerning which evidence has also been given [for him by]

[a number of people]; so that the People too may be seen to honour [those who]

[display] good will towards it, for good [fortune],

[the Council shall decide] that the presiding committee allotted [to preside at the forth]-

[coming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda [...]]
