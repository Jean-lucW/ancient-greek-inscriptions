Gods.

The Council and the People decided.

Antiochis was in prytany, Euk-

leides was secretary, Hierokles

was chairman, Euktemon was archon (408/7).

Dieitrephes proposed: since Oiniades of Old Skiathos

is a good man

with regard to the city of the

Athenians and keen to do

whatever good he can, and

treats well any of the Athenians who

comes to Skiathos, to praise

him and to inscribe him

as proxenos and benefactor of the Athenians,

and his descendants; and

so that he suffers no harm, the Council in office at any time

and the generals

and the governor in Skiathos,

whoever he may be on each occasion, shall take care of him; and

the secretary of the Council shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and put it on the acropolis;

and to invite him also

to hospitality in the city hall

tomorrow. Antichares proposed: in other

respects in accordance with the Council, but in

the motion to rewrite, instead

[of "of S]kiathos", so that it shall be

[written], "Oiniades of Old

[Skiathos"].
