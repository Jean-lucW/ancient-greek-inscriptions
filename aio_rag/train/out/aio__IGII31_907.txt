Gods.

In the archonship of Pytharatos (271/0), in the second prytany, of Antigonis,

for which Isegoros son of Isokrates

of Kephale was secretary. On the ninth of Metageitnion,

the seventh of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Ai[s]chines

son of Nikomachos of Anaphlystos was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee

members. The Council and the People decided. Euboulos

son of [Lys]idemos of Melite proposed: since the commanders of tribal regiments

of the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) have made all the sacrifices which

they were meant to make with the generals,

from their own resources, on behalf of the Council and the People,

and each took care of his own tribe,

so that they might turn out equipped with arms as well as possible

for the guard duties and the

reserve posts and the inspections, and they continued

also in all other matters to obey

the generals in accordance with the laws;

in order therefore that the Council and the People may be seen

to honour those who provide services to it,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide that the presiding committee

allotted to preside in the

People at the forthcoming Assembly shall put

these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise the commanders of tribal regiments of the archonship of Lysithei[d]es

(272/1) and to crown each of

them with a gold crown according to the law for their excellence

and love of honour towards the Council and

the People of Athens; and they shall also have front-row seats

at all the competitions that the city puts on;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele,

and the names of the commanders of tribal regiments, and stand it in front of

the headquarters; and for the inscribing

the board of administrators shall allocate 10 drachmas.
