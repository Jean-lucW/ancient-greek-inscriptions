If a woman miscarries, if it is distinguishable, they are

polluted as from one who has died, but if it is not

distinguishable, the house itself is polluted as

from childbirth.

Concerning suppliants.

An introduced suppliant: if he is sent against the

house, if (the householder) knows from whom he has come upon him, he shall

name him, proclaiming for three days; but if

he has died in the land or perished somewhere else,

if he knows the name, he shall proclaim him by name, but if

he does not know, (he shall say) 'O person, whether you are man or woman',

having made figurines, male and female,

either of wood or of earth, having received them, he shall set beside

them a portion of everything; and when you have performed the

customary rites, carrying (them) to an unworked forest, he shall fix

the figurines and the portions (in the ground).

A second suppliant, initiated or uninitiated, having

seated himself at the public sanctuary: if a demand is

made, for whatever (amount) the demand is made, so shall he be

initiated; but if no demand is made, he shall sacrifice the fruit of the earth

and a libation every year for ever. And if he neglects it,

afresh twice as much. And if a child leaves off, having forgotten,

and a demand is made on him, whatever is prescribed for him when he consults

the oracle, this he shall pay to the god and

sacrifice, if he knows, to the ancestral (god); if not, he shall

consult (the oracle).
