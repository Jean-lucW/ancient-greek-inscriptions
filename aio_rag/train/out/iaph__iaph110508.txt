(The Council and People honoured) Myon Adrastos son of Peritas the son of Adrastos the son of Dionysios the son of Peritas

the natural son of Adrastos Molon Myon, a man

whose father and ancestors were distinguished, of a first family, and who performed many gymn-

asiarchies and stephanephorates and served in emb-

assies and magistracies and all liturgies, a descendant of men

who contributed to the founding of their country; and he himself from

the age of boyhood lived nobly and virtuously and in a manner appro-

priate to the worth of his family, having pursued education, and

in those first embassies and the priesthood of the God

Nerva in which he showed love of honour, he made himself useful to his country; (honoured) with dedications of painted images

on gilded shields and of busts

and statues in sacred and public places bearing

inscriptions appropriate to the worth of his family.

In the sarcophagus lying on this memorial it shall be permitted for no-one else

to be placed except only Peritas, who constructed the memorial; since whoever places

anyone else shall pay the penalties set out in the will of

Peritas. He is alive.



(The Council and People honoured) Peritas Myon son of Adrastos the son of Dionysios the son of

Peritas the natural son of Adrastos the son of Mo-

lon, a man of a brilliant and distin-

guished family, which served in gymnasiarchies

and stephanephorates and embassies and construc-

tions of works and magistracies and all liturgies; and

he himself from his first youth followed

the dignity of his family, living with love of honour

towards his country, and the magistracies and embassies and advocacies

entrusted to him he carried out fair-

ly and justly and worthily of his ancestors; and

when the eleventh gymnasiarchy and stephanephorate

was being performed, he too, just like Myon his great-grand-

father, splendidly and zealously and worthily of his

ancestors carried out the duties of the gymnasiarchy

and stephanephorate from his own resources, and he was also

priest of the God Titus
