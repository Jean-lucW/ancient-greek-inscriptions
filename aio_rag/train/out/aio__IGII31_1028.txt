In the archonship of Lysanias (234/3), in the eighth prytany, of Oineis,

for which Eumelos son of Empedion of Euonymon

was secretary. On the eighteenth of Anthesterion,

the eighteenth of the prytany. Principal

Assembly. Of the presiding committee Euphronios son of Kleemporos of Aixone was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

Theophilos son of Philotheos of Lamptrai proposed: since

Antidoros, allotted priest of Kalliste for the year

of the archonship of Ekphantos, made all the sacrifices

that occurred in the year on behalf of the Council

and the People well and with love of honour, and dedicated

a stone altar from his own resources, and has rendered

accounts for everything he administered

in the year in accordance with the laws; in order

that the Council and the People may be seen to render thanks

to those who show love of honour towards the sanctuary,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the

presiding committee allotted to preside at

the forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda,

and submit the opinion of the Council to the

People, that it seems good to the Council to praise Antidoros

son of Antidoros of Pergase and crown him with a foliage

crown for his piety towards the gods

and love of honour towards the Council and the People;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in

the sanctuary of Kalliste; and for the inscribing

and making of the stele the administrator

shall allocate the expenditure accrued.

In crown

The Council

(crowns) Antidoros

of Pergase.

In crown

The People

(crowns) Antidoros

of Pergase.
