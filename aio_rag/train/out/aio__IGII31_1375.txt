[The Council] and the People [decided.]

[-] son of [-] of Rhamnous proposed: [since]

[Timarchos son of Timarchos of Sa]lamis is well-disposed [towards the People]

[of the Athenians and] individually to those of the [citizens] who come

[to Salamis] in Cyprus makes himself useful,

[and] those of the citizens [who have come] to Salamis have testified

to him about this; in order therefore that

[all] may know that the People of the Athenians are continually

grateful to those who display love of honour towards them, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise Timarchos son of Timarchos of Salamis and crown

him with a foliage crown for his good will and love of honour

towards the People of the Athenians; and he shall be proxenos

and benefactor of the People of the Athenians, and in the future,

on maintaining the same preference towards the People,

he shall also obtain other benefits, whatever he may be deemed worthy of; and so that there may be a

reminder for him of his good will towards the People, the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree

on two stone stelai, so that one shall be set up

in the precinct of the People and the Graces, and the other

in Salamis in the sanctuary of Athena; and for the inscribing

of the stelai and the setting up the treasurer shall allocate

the expense accrued.

In olive crown

The Council,

the People

(crown) Timarchos

son of Timarchos

of Salamis.
