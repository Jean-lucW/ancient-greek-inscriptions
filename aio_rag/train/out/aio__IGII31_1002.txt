In the archonship of Polyeuktos (250/49), in the second prytany, of Erechtheis,

for which Chairephon son of

Archestratos of Kephale was secretary. On the

eleventh of Metageitnion, the eleventh of the

prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Kleidemos son of Phrynon of Phlya was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council

and the People decided. Demostratos son of Aristophanes

of Paiania proposed: concerning the report which

Aristophanes the son of the priestess of Aglauros makes

about the sacrifices that she made at the initiatory

rituals to Aglauros and Ares and Helios

and the Horai and Apollo and

the other gods for whom it was traditional, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

who preside at the first

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda among the sacred

business, and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council

that the Council and the People accept the good things

that occurred in the sacrifices for the health

and preservation of the Council and the People of

the Athenians and children and women and on behalf

of king Antigonos and queen

Phila and their descendants; and since

the priestess of Aglauros sacrificed the

introductory sacrifices and the appropriate sacrifices,

and also took care of the good order at

the all-night festival, and also adorned the table,

to praise the priestess of Aglauros,

Timokrite daughter of Polynikos of Aphidna,

and crown her with a foliage crown

for her piety towards the gods; and

the prytany secretary shall inscribe the decree

on a stone stele and

stand it in the sanctuary of Aglauros; and for

the inscribing of the stele the board of administrators

shall allocate the expense accrued.

In crown

The Council

The People

(crown) the priestess

Timokrite.
