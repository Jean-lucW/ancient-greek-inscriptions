The Young Men

honoured with the finest

and greatest and first honours

Adrastos Hierax son of Neikotimos the son of

Artemidoros the son of Zenon,

son of the Young Men, a great man, lover of his fatherland

and lover of his fellow citizens and benefactor and

founder through his ancestors

of the People, who was high priest of the

Augusti, twice gymnasiarch

with oil in abundant measures flowing

without interruption, twice stephanephoros,

three times president of games, four times

agoranomos, founder,

many times ambassador [on behalf of]

the fatherland, who was advocate

of public affairs, who has

provided banquets and lavish distributions

from his own funds, both because of

his continuous goodwill towards the fatherland and because of

his disposition of love of good towards the Young Men,

living so as to provide an example of virtue.

The Young Men made the dedication of the statue

from their own funds, under the

supervision of Hermogenes son of Hyp[sikles],

by birth son of Hermes, the secretary [of the]

Young Men; the work was overseen by Pan[philos]

Krokion son of Artemon.
