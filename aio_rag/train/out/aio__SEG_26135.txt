**Part 1**

Kleainetos son of Kleomenes of Melite proposed:

the orgeones shall decide: since they are good men

regarding the collective affairs of the orgeones of Amynos and Asklepios and Dexion,

Kalliades son of Philinos of Piraeus and Lysimachides son of Philinos of Piraeus, to praise them

for their excellence and justice towards the

gods and regarding the collective affairs of the orgeones, and

to crown each of them with a gold crown of 500 drachmas; and they shall also have

exemption from the chous in both the sanctuaries,

both themselves and their descendants; and to give them also for

a sacrifice and a dedication whatever seems good

to the orgeones; and to inscribe this decree on two stone stelai and stand

one in the sanctuary of Dexion and the other

in that of Amynos and Asklepios; and to give

them also for the stelai whatever seems good

to the orgeones, so that the others too may show love of honour regarding the collective affairs of the orgeones, knowing that they [will return] thanks to those who confer any benefit on them.
