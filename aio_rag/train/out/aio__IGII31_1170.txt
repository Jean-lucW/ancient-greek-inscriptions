[In the archonship of -], in the fifth [prytany], of Hippothontis,

for which Archikles son of Charidemos of Erchia was

[secretary]. Decree of the [Council]. On the sixth of Pyanopsion;

the seventh of the prytany. Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding

committee Eubios son of Nikanor of Phaleron was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding

committee members. Agathokles son of Diophanes of Dekeleia proposed: since the Magnesians

on the Maiandros, being kinsmen and friends of the People of the Athenians,

having voted to hold a sacrifice and a competition for a crown, equal-to-

Pythian, musical and equestrian and athletic,

for the founding goddess of their city, Artemis Leukophryene,

have despatched sacred envoys, Apollophanes son of Aischylos, Euboulos

son of Anaxagoras, Lykomedes son of Charisios, who, having come before

the Council and the People, renewed the previously existing

close relationship and friendship between the cities, and having given an account

of the epiphany of the goddess, called upon them with

all zeal and love of honour to accept the competition

for a crown, equal-to-Pythian, which they hold for Artemis

Leukophryene, and to declare their city and territory sacred

and inviolate, just as the god at Delphi also

prophesied to them; in order therefore that the People also may be seen at every

opportunity to honour the divine and to

maintain the close relationship and friendship with the Magnesians, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted for the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

that the People shall accept both now and for the future

the libations and the sacrifices and the competition which the

Magnesians hold for Artemis Leukophryene, for a crown,

equal-to-Pythian, and that their city and territory shall be sacred

and inviolate in accordance with the oracles of the god; and to praise

the People of the Magnesians and crown it

with a foliage crown for its piety; and to praise also

the sacred envoys who are present, Apollophanes, Euboulos,

Lykomedes, and crown each of them with a foliage

crown for their piety towards the goddess and

their love of honour towards their fatherland; and to invite them

to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary; and the treasurer and the board of administrators shall allocate the expenditure

accrued for making and putting up

the stele. uninscribed space
