For good fortune.

Of wheat-bearing Demeter the pre-eminent hiero-

phant,

who won great glory for her piety,

first by birth of Greece of the broad dancing-floors

and of the East,

granddaughter of Isaios, supreme in wisdom,

who indeed of the blameless emperor Hadrian

held the noble teaching in the arts of the Muses;

and daughter of Isaios, himself also greatly praised

and outstanding both in virtues and in self-control;

whom Deo too, rewarding her, to the Blessed

Isles

led, free from every kind of pain;

and she gave her a death sweeter than pleasant

sleep,

and altogether better than that of the Argive youths.

She it was who, Antoninus together with Commodus,

the emperors,

beginning the rites, crowned as initiates.

Her, then, by decree the Council dear to Ares

established (in honour),

and a sacred new statue was completed by

both Eunike, her dear daughter, and her daughter's sons,

the Zoilidai, (honouring) their mother's divine mother,

Kallaischros and Glaukos, glorifying her in a statue,

she whom Demeter too granted to the immortals.
