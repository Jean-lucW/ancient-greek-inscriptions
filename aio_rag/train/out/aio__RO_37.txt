**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Charikleides (363/2) for the Athenians. On the following terms the arbitrators reconciled the Salaminioi from the Seven Tribes and the Salaminioi from Sounion, who agreed with one another that what the arbitrators decided was fair, the arbitrators being Stephanos of Myrrhinous, Kleagoros of Acharnai, Aristogeiton of Myrrhinous, Euthykritos

of Lamptrai, Kephisodotos of Aithalidai: the priesthoods shall be common to both for all time, that of Athena Skiras, and that of Herakles at Porthmos, and that of Eurysakes, and that of Aglauros and Pandrosos and of Kourotrophos; and they shall be allotted jointly from both whenever any of the priestesses or of the priests dies; and those who obtain them by lot shall serve as priests on the same terms as the previous ones served as priests; and the land at the Herakleion at Porthmos and the salt-pan and the agora in Koile they shall divide in two, equal for each, and each shall set up boundary markers of their own share; and they shall sacrifice to the gods and the heroes on these terms: whatever the city provides from the public fund, or the Salaminioi happen to receive from the oschophoroi or from the deipnophoroi, these

they shall both sacrifice jointly and divide the meat raw, half to each; and whatever the Salaminioi used to sacrifice from the rent, they shall sacrifice from their own resources according to

ancestral custom, each contributing half towards all the sacred rites; and they shall give to the priests and the priestesses the perquisites that are written; to the priest of Herakles as priestly dues 30 drachmas; for a sacrificial cake 3 drachmas; of these each shall contribute half; and of the victims of which he begins the sacrifice, of the

common ones, he shall receive, of a flayed one the skin and the leg, of a singed one the leg; and of a bovine nine pieces of meat and the skin;

to the priest of Eurysakes as priestly dues 6 drachmas; for a sacrificial cake on both occasions 7 drachmas; for leg and skin in the Eurysakeion 13 drachmas; of these each shall contribute half; for the hero at the salt-pan he shall receive, of the victims sacrificed, the skin

and the leg; and they shall distribute to the priests and the priestesses in the sanctuaries where each of them serve as priests a portion from each side; and the loaves at the (sanctuary) of Skiras they shall distribute as follows, after removing from all of them those customarily removed according to ancestral custom: to the herald a loaf, to the priestess of Athena a loaf, to the priest of Herakles a loaf, to the priestess of Pandrosos and Aglauros a loaf, to (the priestess) of Kourotrophos and the basket-bearer a loaf, to the rowers a loaf; and of the rest each shall take half; and they shall allot an archon in turn from each side, who shall appoint

the oschophoroi and the deipnophoroi with the priestess and the herald according to ancestral custom; and both shall inscribe these things on a common stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Athena Skiras; and the same man shall be priest for Eurysakes and for the hero

at the salt-pan; and if any of the sanctuaries needs to be repaired, they shall repair it jointly, each contributing half; in the archonship of Charikleides those from the Seven Tribes provided the archon; and all the documents shall be common to both; and the land shall be worked by the man who has leased it until the period for which he leased it expires, paying half the rent to each; and each side in turn shall begin the preliminary sacrifice of the contest; and each shall take half of the meat and of the skins; and the priesthood of the herald shall belong to Thrasykles according to ancestral custom; and they shall be released from all other charges, both private and common, up to the month Boedromion in the archonship of Charikleides.

In the archonship among the Salaminioi of Diphilos son of Diopeithes of Sounion, the following of the Salaminioi from Sounion swore: Diopeithes son of Phasyrkides, Philoneos son of Ameinonikos, Chalkideus son of Andromenes, Chariades son of Charikles, Theophanes

son of Zophanes, Hegias son of Hegesias, Ameinias son of Philinos. In the archonship among the Salaminioi of Antisthenes son of Antigenes of Acharnai, the following from the Seven Tribes swore: Thrasykles son of Thrason of Boutadai, Stratophon son of Straton of Agryle, Melittios

son of Exekestides of Boutadai, Aristarchos son of Demokles of Acharnai,

Arkeon son of Eumelides of Acharnai, Chairestratos son of Pankleides of Epikephisia, Demon son of Demaretos of Agryle.

Archeneos proposed: so that the Salaminioi may always sacrifice the sacrifices to the gods and the heroes according to ancestral custom and

the terms may be fulfilled on which the conciliators reconciled both sides and those chosen swore, the Salaminioi shall resolve that the archon Aristarchos shall write up all the sacrifices and the perquisites of the priests on the stele on which

the reconciliation is, so that the archons from both sides may always know what money each must contribute towards

all the sacrifices from the rent of the land at the Herakleion, and shall stand the stele in the

Eurysakeion. Mounichion. At Porthmos: for Kourotrophos a goat, 10 dr.; for Iolaos a sheep burnt whole, 15 dr.; for Alkmene a sheep,

12 dr.; for Maia a sheep, 12 dr.; for Herakles a bovine, 70 dr.; for the hero at the salt-pan a sheep, 15 dr.; for the hero at Antisara a piglet, 3 dr. 3 ob.; for the hero Epipyrgidios a piglet, 3 dr. 3 ob.; for Ion a sheep, to be sacrificed alternately every other year; wood for the offerings and for those which the city gives from the kyrbeis,

10 dr. On the eighteenth, for Eurysakes a pig, 40 dr.; wood for the offerings and for the other things, 3 dr. Hekatombaion. At the Panathenaia, for Athena

a pig, 40 dr.; wood for the offerings and for the other things, 3 dr. Metageitnion. On the seventh, for Apollo Patroos a pig, 40 dr.; for Leto a piglet,

For Poseidon Hippodromios a pig, 40 dr.; for the hero Phaiax a piglet, 3 dr. 3 ob.; for the hero Teukros a piglet, 3 dr. 3 ob.; for the hero Nauseiros a piglet, 3 dr. [3] ob.;

wood for the offerings and the other things, 3 dr. 3 ob. Pyanopsion. On the sixth, for Theseus a pig, 40 dr.; for the other things, 3 dr. At the Apatouria, for Zeus Phratrios a pig, 40 dr.;

wood for the offerings and the other things, 3 dr. Maimakterion. For Athena Skiras a pregnant sheep, 12 dr.; for Skiros a sheep, 15 dr.; wood for the altar, [3] dr.

Total of what both must spend on all the sacred rites: 530 dr. 3 ob. These they shall sacrifice jointly from the rent of the land at the Herakleion of Sounion, each side contributing money towards all the sacred rites; and if anyone proposes, or an archon puts to the vote, the dissolution of any of these things, or diverts the money anywhere else, he shall be liable to account to the whole genos and to the priests in the same way, and individually liable to prosecution by anyone of the Salaminioi who wishes.
