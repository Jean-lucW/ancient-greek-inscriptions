Kallikrates of Aixone proposed: since the ephebes of Kekropis

in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) are well-disciplined and do

everything that the laws require of them and obey the controller

elected by the People, to praise

them and crown them with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas

for their orderliness and discipline; and to praise also the controller

Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon and crown him with a gold

crown of 1000 drachmas, because he took care well and with love of honour

of the ephebes of the tribe Kekropis; and to inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Kekrops.

Hegemachos son of Chairemon of Perithoidai proposed: since the ephebes

of Kekropis stationed at Eleusis well and with love of honour take

care of what the Council and the People instruct them and

show themselves well-disciplined, to praise them for their orderliness

and discipline and crown each of them with a foliage crown;

and to praise also their controller Adeistos son of Anti-

machos of Athmonon and crown him with a foliage crown when

he has rendered his accounts; and to inscribe this decree on the dedication

which the ephebes of Kekropis are dedicating. uninscribed space

Protias proposed: the demesmen shall resolve, since well and

with love of honour the ephebes of Kekropis take care of the guard of Eleusis,

and their controller Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon,

to praise them and crown each of them with a foliage

crown; and to inscribe this decree on the dedication which

the ephebes of Kekropis in the archonship of Ktesikles

are dedicating. uninscribed space

Euphronios proposed: the demesmen shall resolve, since the ephebes

enrolled in the archonship of Ktesikles are well-disciplined and

do everything that the laws require of them, and the controller

elected by the People declares that they

are obedient and do everything else with love of honour, to praise

them and crown them with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas

for their orderliness and discipline; and to praise also

their controller Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon and crown

him with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas, because he took care well and with love of honour

both of the demesmen and of all the others of the tribe Kekropis;

and to inscribe this decree on the dedication which

the ephebes of Kekropis and their controller are dedicating. uninscribed space
