Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan Parthicus,

grandson of the God Nerva, Trajan Hadrian Augustus,

pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power for the 3rd time,

to the magistrates, Council and People of the Aphrodisians, greetings.

Your freedom and autonomy and the other

privileges granted to you both by the Senate and

by the emperors before me I confirmed earlier.

Having been petitioned by an embassy about the use of

iron and the tax on nails, although

the matter is disputed, since

it is not now for the first time that the tax-collectors have undertaken

to collect it from you, nevertheless, knowing that the city

is in other respects worthy of honour and removed

from the formula of the province, I release it

from the payment and I have written to Cl(audius) Agrippinus

my procurator to instruct the contractor for

the tax in Asia to keep away from your city.
