In the hieromnamonship of

Lucius son of Paccius,

on the twenty-sixth of Eumenideios.

The Assembly decided, as also the Coun-

cil: since the Geloans, both when

previously we lived in this city,

war having broken out

against the Carthaginians, came to the help

of our city with cavalry

and infantry, and became bound by oath

and concluded an alliance,

and similarly also now, since we have been living in the

city, they make it clear

that they are mindful of their friendship

and good will towards us, it shall be de-

cided to set up on a bronze plaque,

writing it in the sanctuary of Hest[i]-

a, the good will and alliance

which exists between us and

the Geloans; and to invite

them to the games and

to sacrifice with them, so that we may make it

clear to future

[generations] that we keep in memory those who to

our city have rendered

services.

(Text relating to) the Geloans.
