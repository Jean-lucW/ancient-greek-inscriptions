The Council and the People buried

and honoured with the greatest

honours Hermias Glykon son of Hermias the son of

Phanias, a man fine

and good, of a first family and

one which contributed to the founding of the city,

gymnasiarch and stephanephoros,

having performed the liturgies

most lavishly and most brilliantly,

and having held all the greatest offices

in the finest way, and having

carried out embassies, both

very many others and to Rome, and

in general having lived as an example of all

virtue; just as he was also honoured for each

of these things through the decrees passed.

The Council and the People buried

and honoured Apphia daughter of Me-

nestheus the son of Eumachos,

wife of Hermias Glykon son of Hermias,

of a first family

and most distinguished and one which

contributed to the founding of the city, and

herself outstanding in prudence and

dignity

and having lived in a manner worthy both of her

ancestors and of her husband,

just as she was often honoured through

decrees.
