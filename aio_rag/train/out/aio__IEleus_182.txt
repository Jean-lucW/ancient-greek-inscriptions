Lysias son of Thesbion of Eleusis proposed:

since Dion, both in previous times

serving as secretary to the treasurers of the grain-purchase fund

and to the board of administrators, continues

to be well disposed to the Athenians stationed

at Eleusis and being of service

both collectively and individually, making

evident the good will which he has towards them, and

now too, serving as secretary to the treasurer of the grain-purchase

fund for the year of the archonship of Menekles (267/6),

has shown great zeal

concerning the distribution of the grain and of the Assembly

payments given for the grain;

for good fortune, the Athenians

stationed at Eleusis shall decide: to praise

[Dion] and crown him with a foliage

[crown] for his good will and love of honour

[towards] them; and he shall

[if he shows love of honour in future,] obtain

[whatever benefit he may be deemed] worthy of; and

[this decree shall be inscribed] on a stone

[stele ...]
