The Rhamnousians and those of the citizens living

at Rhamnous decided. Timokrates son of Epigenes of Oa proposed: since

Menandros, having been appointed trierarch for the year

in the archonship of Niketes, managed both the repair

of the ship well and with love of honour, spending

from his own resources as much as those appointed over these matters

instructed him; and he also provided oil for the young men

so that, taking care of their bodies, they might become

stronger; and he also sacrificed to Zeus Soter and Athena

Soteira for the health and safety and concord of those

who sailed with him, so that, being in concord and kept

safe, they might also in future be useful to the People,

and he entertained (them) with love of honour from his own resources; and he crowned

also the crew on the ship for their love of honour

towards themselves; and he also gave the ship-guard

fees from his own pocket, and having come to Rhamnous

he sacrificed to Nemesis with the general and the religious

officials chosen along with him, and contributed sacrificial victims

and wine; and so that there may be an incentive for the trierarchs appointed

at any time, knowing that they will receive thanks worthy

of their benefactions, for good fortune, the

Athenians who sailed in the open ship shall decide

to praise Menandros son of Teisandros of Eitea and

crown him with a gold crown in accordance with the law

for his excellence and love of honour towards themselves;

and he shall also have exemption from the voyage in future;

and to inscribe the decree on a stone stele and stand it by

the gate; and also to elect three men from themselves immediately who

shall fund what has been decreed, and the expense

accrued they shall account for to the association. These were elected: Timokrates

son of Epigenes of Oa, Archestratos son of Aischines of Erchia,

Diokles son of Dion of Hamaxanteia.

The Athenians

who sailed with him (honour)

Menandros

son of Teisandros

of Eitea.
