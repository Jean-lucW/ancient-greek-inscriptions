will make for the god a dedication as

from the dedications, to the Hero

Physician'; and the men chosen shall inscribe

the names of those who have made dedications in

the sanctuary and the weight on a stone

stele and stand it in the sanctuary; and whatever

they administer, they shall render an account of it

themselves; and also to choose a public slave to act as copying

secretary, so that when these things have been done

the relations of the Council and the People with the gods may be

well and piously conducted; and to sacrifice to the god
