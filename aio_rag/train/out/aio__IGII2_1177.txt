[the demarch [...] shall take care]

[with] the priestess, the [demarch in office at any]

[time], of the Thesmophorion, [that]

[no] one releases consecrated animals or

assembles [thiasoi] or sets up

shrines or performs purifications or

approaches the altars or the megaron

without the priestess,

except when there is the festival of the Thesmophoria

and the Plerosia and at the Kalamaia

and the Skira and if on any other day

the women come together according

to tradition; the Piraeans shall resolve,

if anyone does any of these things contrary to

this, the demarch, having imposed a fine,

shall bring him before the

court, using the laws

which are laid down concerning these things; and concerning

the gathering of wood from the sacred places, if anyone

gathers wood, the ancient laws which are laid down concerning

these things shall be valid. And the boundary-setters

with the demarch shall inscribe this decree

and stand it by the ascent

to the Thesmophorion.
