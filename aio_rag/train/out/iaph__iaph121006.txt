The Council and the People honoured [...]

Iason son of Menodotos grandson of Menandros [...]

Prabreus, a fine and good man, of a

leading and most distinguished family, and one who sustains

the fatherland, having been gymnasiarch [and]

stephanephoros with magnanimity and

distinction, and having been high priest of the Augusti [and]

president of the games twice; [and also] having completed

from his own resources, of the archive, [together?]

with Iulia Paula his wife, of the peristyle

south stoa, from the foundations, the [...]

-ic (part) entire, and the hall in it with door-frames

and book-cases and the workshops beside it,

two of two storeys; and of the east stoa from the [foundations]

eight intercolumniations; and having also transferred [to]

[this] from the old stoa, in accordance with the [decree]

[passed], eight [intercolumniations]; and of the west stoa

one [intercolumniation]; and on the columns [of this stoa]

to have added the capitals on all, [and the architraves]

and friezes and cornices on the columns [to have placed]

on all; and on its columns, five having the [...]

-aioi only, to have placed the remaining

drums, all; and of the north hall [and of the]

exedra having made all the remaining parts in white marble,

with ceilings and the columns in the hall and book-cases

and the [plaster]work and all the rest; and having completed

workshops, together with those in the exedra, [nine]-

teen; and the whole peristyle to have been fitted with beams [and]

tiled and roofed and completed

from his own resources by himself, together [with]

all the doors set up in it.
