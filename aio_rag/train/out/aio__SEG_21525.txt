[Dr]omokles son of Arkesas of Oa proposed: since

the cavalry commanders and the commanders of tribal cavalry contingents in the archonship of Nikias (282/1)

have continued to fulfil

their office according to the laws and, taking care

of the cavalry so that it may be useful to the People,

have also taken care of the enrolment of the

cavalry and appointed in addition one hundred cavalrymen,

so that the cavalry, having been brought up to strength as far as

is possible at present and having become

three hundred, may render service to the country;

and they also took care that there should be commanders of tribal cavalry contingents

for all the tribes and that the law should be repealed

which prevented anyone who wishes from all the cavalry

from serving as commander of a tribal cavalry contingent; and they have also taken care of the

valuations and the scrutinies, and they also carried out

the scrutiny of the bodies (scil. of the horses) according to the

law together with the Council, well and in the interests

both of the People and of the cavalry; and they have also

taken care of the distribution of the grain, so that

the cavalry receive it in good order in each

prytany; and they continue in all other

respects, both individually and collectively, to show love of honour

concerning the cavalry and the People; for good

fortune, the cavalry shall decide to praise

the cavalry commanders Alkimachos son of Kleoboulos

of Myrrhinous and Nikogenes son of Arkesas

of Euonymon, and also to praise the commanders

of tribal cavalry contingents Dionysios of Gargettos, Menon

of Hippotomadai, Kallidemos of Euonymon,

Kalliades of Steiria, Lysippos of Kettos,

Charias of Kephale, Antimachos of Acharnai,

Proxenos of Phlya, Thrasykles of Dekeleia,

Aristophon of Oinoe, Python of Alopeke,

and to crown each of them with a gold

crown according to the law for their excellence and

love of honour towards the cavalry and the People,

so that the others who are appointed from time to time

may also show love of honour in taking care of the cavalry,

knowing that they will receive thanks worthy

of their benefactions; and the secretaries of the cavalry commanders

shall inscribe this decree

on two stone stelai and stand one

by the Poseidonion and the other in

[the] Stoa of the Herms. uninscribed space
