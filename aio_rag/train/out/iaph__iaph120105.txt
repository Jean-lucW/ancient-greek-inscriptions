[The Council and the People and the]

[Gerousia and] the Young Men honoured,

even after his death,

Gaius Iulius Potitianos, son of Adrastos,

a man who loved his fatherland,

who carried out every liturgy and

service for his

fatherland, and was ambassador

on behalf of the fatherland at his own expense on several

occasions to the emperors,

having always done what was advantageous

for the People, with the honours worthy of and

fitting for him, he being

in everything fine and good,

honoured by decrees and

grants of citizenship also by many other

cities.

Julia Antonia his daughter set up the honour (statue)

together with the altar,

as he prescribed.

Similarly the Council and

the People and the Gerousia honoured with the finest

and fitting honours

also Antonia Flaviane daughter of L(ucius) Antonius Flavianus,

priestess

of Arete, the wife of Potitianos,

loving her husband and loving her children,

living with due order, so that she is attested

by all for her prudence.

She set up her own honour (statue)

for herself from her own funds

while still living.
