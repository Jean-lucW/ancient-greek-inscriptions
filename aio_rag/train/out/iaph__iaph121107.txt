The sarcophagus and the base and [the burial niches and everything around them]

were constructed and belong to Tib(erius) [Julius Glykon, as is also]

made clear through the contract drawn up through the [property-archive; in which sar-]

cophagus shall be buried Glykon himself or [whomever he himself wishes or]

ordains; and in the burial niches shall be buried [those whom]

Glykon [wishes to bury] or permits to anyone in writing [or ordains; but]

no [other person] shall have the right to bury anyone either [in the sarcophagus or in the]

burial niches, except those whom Glykon himself while living [wishes to bury; and no]

other person shall have the right to bury anyone or [to remove a body of those]

buried by the wish of Glykon, [neither by decree nor]

by petition to the governor nor [in any other way, nor to]

alienate nor to move the [sarcophagus; since whoever dares]

or permits [any of these things] shall pay to the most [sacred treasury five]

thousand [denarii], of which the third shall belong to the [prosecutor. Of this]

inscription a copy was deposited [also in the property-archive in the]

thirteenth stephanephorate [of Attalis daughter of Menekrates],

in the month Xand[ikos].
