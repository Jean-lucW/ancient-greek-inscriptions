**Part 1**

The Council and the People decided. Antiochis was the prytany; Drakontides was chairman. Diognetos proposed:

the Council and the jurors of the Athenians shall swear the oath as follows: 'I shall not expel the Chalkidians from Chalkis nor shall I make the city uninhabited nor shall I deprive any individual of rights nor punish him with exile nor shall I arrest him nor kill him nor take away the property of anyone without trial without the permission of the People of the Athenians, nor shall I put to the vote anything against an unsummoned party,

neither against the community nor against any individual whatsoever, and when an embassy comes I shall bring it

before the Council and People within ten days when

I am in the prytany, as far as possible. These things I shall guarantee to the Chalkidians if they obey the People of the Athenians.' And an embassy coming from Chalkis shall administer the oath to the Athenians with the commissioners for oaths and list the names of those who have sworn. And the generals shall take care that all take the oath.

The Chalkidians shall swear as follows: 'I shall not revolt from the People of the Athenians by any means or device whatsoever, neither in word nor

in deed, nor shall I obey anyone who revolts, and if anyone revolts I shall denounce him to the Athenians, and I shall pay to the Athenians the tribute which

I persuade the Athenians, and I shall be an ally as best and most just as I can, and I shall help and defend the People of the Athenians, if anyone wrongs the People of

the Athenians, and I shall obey the People of the Athenians.' And all the Chalkidians of age shall swear; whoever does not swear, he shall be without rights and his property shall be confiscated and

a tithe of the property shall be sacred to Zeus Olympios. And an embassy of the Athenians coming to Chalkis shall administer the oath with the commissioners for oaths in Chalkis and list the names of the Chalkidians who have sworn.

Antikles proposed: for the good fortune of the Athenians, the Athenians and Chalkidians shall make the oath just as the People of the Athenians voted for the Eretrians; and the generals shall take care that it happens as quickly as possible.

And the People shall choose five men immediately, who shall go to Chalkis and administer the oath. And concerning the hostages, reply to the Chalkidians that for now the Athenians decide to leave matters according to what has been voted; but when they decide, they shall deliberate and make the settlement, in whatever way seems to be suitable for the Athenians and the Chalkidians. And as for the foreigners in Chalkis, apart from those who, being resident,

do not pay taxes to Athens, and anyone who has been granted exemption from taxes by the People of the Athenians, the others shall pay taxes to Chalkis, just as the other Chalkidians do. And this decree and the

oath shall be inscribed, at Athens by the secretary of the Council on a stone stele and set down on the acropolis at the expense of the Chalkidians, and in Chalkis in the sanctuary of Zeus

Olympios the Council of the Chalkidians shall inscribe it and set it down. These things shall be voted for the Chalkidians. And the sacrifices from the oracles concerning Euboea shall be made as quickly as possible with

Hierokles by three men, whom the Council shall choose from among its own members; and the generals shall join in taking care that the sacrifice is made as quickly as possible and shall provide the money for it.

Archestratos proposed: in other respects as proposed by Antikles; but the punishments for the Chalkidians against their own people shall be in Chalkis just as for the Athenians at Athens, except for exile and death and loss of rights; concerning these there shall be appeal to Athens to the Heliaia of the thesmothetai in accordance with the decree of the People; and concerning the guarding of Euboea the generals shall take care as best they can, so that it shall be as good as possible for the Athenians.

Oath.
