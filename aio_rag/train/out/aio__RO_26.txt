**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Sokratides (374/3).

Law concerning the twelfth-share tax on the grain

of the islands.

Agyrrhios proposed: so that the People may have grain in public ownership, to sell the twelfth-share tax in Lemnos and Imbros and Skyros and the fiftieth on grain; each portion shall be five hundred medimnoi, one hundred of wheat and four hundred of barley; the buyer shall convey the grain at his own risk to Piraeus and shall convey the grain up to the city at his own expense and shall pile up the grain in the Aiakeion; and the city shall provide the Aiakeion roofed and fitted with doors; and the buyer shall weigh out the grain to the city within thirty days,

when he has conveyed it up to the city, at his own expense; and when he conveys it up to the city, the city shall not exact rent from the buyers; the buyer shall weigh out the wheat at a weight of a talent for five hekteis, and the barley he shall weigh out dry at a weight of a talent for the medimnos, clean of darnel, having adjusted the counterweight on the beam, just as the other merchants do; the buyer shall not pay a down payment, but sales tax and herald's fee of twenty drachmas per portion; the buyer shall appoint two creditworthy guarantors per portion, whom the Council shall approve; a partnership shall be (formed from) portions of three thousand medimnoi,

six men; the city shall exact the grain from the partnership, both from one and from all of those who are in the partnership, until it recovers what is due to it; and the People shall choose ten men from all the Athenians in the Assembly, whenever they elect the generals, who shall take care of the grain; and these, having had the grain weighed out to them according to what is written, shall sell it in the agora, whenever the People decides; and it shall not be permitted to put the sale to the vote before the month of Anthesterion; and the People shall fix the price of the wheat and of the barley at which those elected must sell; and those who have bought the twelfth-share tax shall convey the grain before the month of Maimakterion; and those elected by the People shall take care that the grain is conveyed within the time stated; and when those elected have sold the grain, they shall render account

in the Assembly and shall come bringing the money to the People, and the proceeds from the grain shall be military funds; and the receivers shall allocate the down payment from the islands and from the fiftieth, as much as it fetched last year from the two tenth- [...], for the present to go to the administration, and in future not to take away the two tenth- [...]

from the funds paid in.
