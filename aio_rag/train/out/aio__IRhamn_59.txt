[Aphth]onetos [son of Aphthonetos of Rhamnous proposed]:

[since] Apol[lodoros, having been elected general,]

[has] continued [to be well disposed both individually and collectively]

to the People on [every occasion, and]

the People [crowned] him, [rendering]

[appropriate thanks to the] elected [generals],

in return for which both the Council [has many times]

crowned him with gold crowns, [and he continues]

[also] individually, regarding whatever any of the citizens [asks] him,

[to make] himself serviceable;

and now, when the citizens stationed at Rhamnous

wrote concerning a plot which [was]

his own property and wished to buy it so as

to build a sanctuary to Sarapis and

Isis, he did not wish to sell it, but gave it

without payment, regarding as of the greatest importance

both his piety towards the gods and

his good will and love of honour towards

his fellow citizens; so that the Sarapiastai too

may be seen to render thanks to those who display love of honour towards

them, for good fortune,

the association of the Sarapiastai shall decide

to praise and crown with a gold crown

Apollodoros son of Sogenes of Otryne for his

piety towards the gods and his love

of honour towards themselves; and the religious officials whose

turn of duty it is shall also invite him

to the sacrifices; and they shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it before the

entrance of the temple; and they shall choose from among themselves six

men who shall take care of the inscribing

of the decree and the setting up of the

stele; and they shall account for the expense accrued

to the association. These were chosen:

Demokles of Eupyridai, Antiphanes of Oion,

Kleodorides of Rhamnous, Bion of Phrearrhioi,

Aphthonetos of Rhamnous, Philokles of Erchia.

In crown

The association

of the Sarapiastai

(crowns) Apollodoros

son of Sogenes

of Otryne.
