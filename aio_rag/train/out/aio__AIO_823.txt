**Fragment 1**

Gods.

Nikostratos son of Epiteles of Rhamnous proposed: since Epichares, both in the archonship of Lysithe[ides] (272/1)

having been elected cavalry commander and having managed the cavalry well and in accordance with the laws,

was crowned by the Council and the People and by the cavalry; and again

in the archonship of Peithidemos (268/7), when the People elected him general and

assigned him to the coastal countryside, having managed the defence well and with love of honour

he kept the fort safe for the People while there was war,

and brought in the grain [and] the fruit of the trees up to thirty stades,

while the army was [in the] countryside, having stationed secret guards at the look-

[out posts, and] himself [keeping watch alongside] with the soldiers, so that the

[harvesting of the] crops might take place safely for the farmers; and he also protected the vines for as long as

he was [in control] of the countryside; and he also constructed from his own resources a stoa so that, if there is any need,

all may camp beside it and help may be at hand wherever it is needed. And he also built

two guard-posts and established dogs in addition to those existing, himself providing

their food, so that the defence might be greater; and he also made a headquarters, having prepared the sanctuary

of [Aph]r[odite?], so that it may be held in honour and be well and piously (maintained) for the

[demesmen]; and he also brought in 500 medimnoi of wheat and 500 of barley, himself advancing the prices, and distributed them to the citizens and the soldiers at the

established price; and he also made an arrangement concerning those who were taken captive,

that by paying one hundred and twenty drachmas they might be ransomed through the herald and that none of the citizens should be carried off nor the slaves disappear; and he also punished those who guided the pirates into the countryside, having seized and examined them, though they were from the city, as their deeds deserved; and he also made provision for the soldiers from Patroklos
