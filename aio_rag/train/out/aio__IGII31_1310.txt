Decree 1

[In the archonship of Phil]on who was after Menedemos (178/7), in the fourth prytany, of Hippothontis, for which Philisti-

[on son of Phil]istion of Potamos was secretary. On the twenty-second of Pyanopsion, the thirtieth of the pry-

[tany]. Assembly in the theatre. Of the presiding committee Herakleides son of Telemachos of Kerame-

[is] was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. Kalliades son of Pausimachos of Lakiadai proposed: concerning what

the prytany of Hippothontis reports about the sacrifices which they made before the Assemblies to

Apollo Prostaterios and to Artemis Boulaia and to the other gods for whom it is customary,

[for] good fortune, the People shall decide to welcome the good results that have occurred in the sacrifices which they made

for the health and preservation of the Council and the People and the allies; and, since the pry-

tany has made all the sacrifices which are proper in the prytany well and with love of honour, and has also taken

care of the convening of the Council and the People and of everything else which

the laws and the decrees of the People prescribe for it, to praise the prytany of Hippothontis

and to crown it with a gold crown according to the law, for its piety towards the gods and its love

of honour towards the Council and the People of the Athenians; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the prytanikon; and for the inscribing and setting up of the

stele the administrator shall allocate the expense accrued.

Decree 2

In the archonship of Philon who was after Menedemos (178/7), in the fifth prytany, of Akamantis, for which Phili-

stion son of Philistion of Potamos was secretary. On the sixth of Maimakterion, the tenth of the pry-

tany. Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding committee Pytheas son of Pythokles of Acharnai was putting to the vote and his fellow

presiding committee members. The Council decided. Prokles son of Prokles of Thymaitadai proposed: since the prytany of Hippo-

thontis and those who have permanent dining rights, having praised and crowned the treasurer whom

they chose from among themselves, Theodotos son of Theodotos of Koile, report to the Council that he performed all the proper sacrifices [in]

the prytany on behalf of the Council and the People, and also took care of everything else well and with love

of honour, for good fortune, the Council shall decide to praise the treasurer Theodotos son of Theodotos of Koile and

to crown him with a foliage crown; and to praise also the secretary Archestratos son of Phanias of Eleusis

and the priest of the eponymous, Thrasippos son of Kallias of Gargettos, and the secretary of the Council and

the People, Protomachos son of Protomachos of Paiania, and the undersecretary, Demokrates son of Diphilos of Cho-

largos, and the herald of the Council and the People, Eukles son of Eukles of Berenikidai, and the pipe-

player, Kallikrates of Thorikos, and the treasurer of the Council, Kallippos son of Leon of Aixone, and to crown

also each of these with a foliage crown; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele and stand it in the prytanikon; and for the in-

scribing and setting up of the stele the administrator shall allocate the expense accrued.
