Diodoros son of Dazos, Per(-).

The Assembly decided, as also the Council from the sanctuary of Apollo, and

there gave counsel Diogenes son of Kallippos, Sal(-), Philiarchos son of Phalakros, Arch(-),

Hereas son of Di[o]n, Hysg(-), N[iki]ppos son of Philiarchos, Per(-), Apollonios son of Heliodoros, Kra(-),

[H]isteios son of Thrason, Tel(-), Damon son of Damon, Log(-), Artemon son of Damon, Eri(-), Sosipolis

son of Taurion, Log(-), Dion son of Dion, Log(-), Histeios son of Nysas, Hypa(-), Apellas son of Theston, Net(-), Dion

son of Nikokles, Net(-), Hippias son of Damas, Lab(-), Histiodoros son of Euthymos, Hypa(-). Since

Nemenios son of Nemenios, Hysg(-), Daphnis is manifestly well disposed towards the priests

of Apollo and has in fact treated well the community of the priests

of Apollo in every way, both saying and doing what is best and most

advantageous for the priests of Apollo, and both in common and in private acting in a manner worthy

of his own courage and nobility and of the excellence of his ancestors, and it is just

that good men who also display their [goodwill]

should obtain honour and precedence and that an undying memory of them should remain among those who have been well

treated, for which reasons the Assembly of the priests of Apollo shall decide,

as also the councillors, that Nemenios son of Nemenios, Hysg(-), Daphnis shall be a benefactor,

and to set up a bronze statue of him and dedicate it either in the sanctuary of Apollo or in the

basilica, in whichever place he himself wishes, inscribing: 'To all the gods, the community of the priests

of Apollo (dedicated) Nemenios son of Nemenios, Hysg(-), Daphnis the benefactor, on account of his excellence

and goodwill towards it', so that it may be manifest to future generations that the priests

of Apollo are able to bestow thanks and honours on benefactors worthy of their

benefactions; and the Diodoreian treasurers shall pay out the money for the statue and,

having engraved this decree on t[w]o bronze tablets, shall dedicate one in the sanctuary

of Apollo in the pronaos of Zeus, and give the other to him. Votes

calling for Nemenios son of Nemenios, Hysg(-), Daphnis to be a benefactor:

eight hundred and twenty-five, and against, none.
