Gods.

Kallimachos son of Kallikrates proposed: since

Damasias son of Dionysios of Thebes, having

resided at Eleusis, has continued to be orderly

and is kindly disposed towards all

those living in the deme, both

himself and his pupils, and when the Eleusinians

were holding the Dionysia, he was zealous and

showed love of honour towards the gods and

the People of Athens and of the Eleusinians,

so that the Dionysia might be as fine as possible,

and having equipped at his own expense

two choruses, one of boys and the other of

men, he gave them to Demeter and

Kore and Dionysos, the Eleusinians shall

decide: to praise Damasias son of Dionysios

of Thebes for his moderation and his piety

towards the two goddesses and to crown

him with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas;

and the demarch after Gnathis shall announce

him at the Dionysia at Eleusis at the

tragedies, that the deme of the Eleusinians

crowns Damasias son of Dionysios of Thebes

for his moderation and his piety

towards the two goddesses; and he shall have a seat of honour

and freedom from the taxes over which the Eleusinians have authority,

both for himself and his descendants, and if he wishes

to obtain any other benefit from the deme

of the Eleusinians (he may do so), and

the demarch in office at any time shall take care of him in whatever

he needs; and they shall choose immediately someone

who will take care that this

decree is inscribed and stood in the Dionysion;

and for the inscribing the demarch shall give

10 drachmas; and they shall give for a sacrifice

to Damasias 100 drachmas from the common fund.
