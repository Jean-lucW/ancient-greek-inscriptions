**Part 1**

In the archonship of ⟦Ekphantos⟧ Ekphantos (236/5), the Rhamnousians decided, Elpinikos

son of Mnesippos of Rhamnous proposed: since Dikaiarchos, having inherited from his father

friendship towards the Athenian People and the

community of those stationed at Rhamnous, preserves that friend-

ship, and having been appointed with his father Apollonios by

⟦king [Antigonos]⟧ to the defence of the fort, well

and with love of honour managed both the defence of the fort and

those living in it, showing himself well-disciplined, both himself and the

soldiers stationed under his father, and because of

this the Rhamnousians and those of the Athenians living in the fort crowned them both with a gold crown according to

the law; and likewise his father, having been at Eleusis,

was praised and crowned by both the Eleusinians and the other Athenians living in the fort; and again he himself,

having been appointed to Panakton, well and gloriously managed

both the defence of the fort and of the rest of the countryside of Attica; and now, stationed by king Demetrios in the

citadel of the Eretrians, he continues to be well disposed to the Athenian People, both collectively to all and individually to those who have removed their livestock for safety because of the war, keeping them safe and helping in whatever anyone

asks him; and when the general Philokedes came to Eretria he both spoke in his support and one of the citizens

who had been led off to execution he rescued from the prison

and saved, demonstrating the good will which he has towards

the citizens; and he announces that for the rest of time also

in whatever anyone asks him, either the People collectively or any of the citizens individually,

he will render service; and he also gave sacrificial victims for the sacrifice of the Nemesia and of the king from his own resources, when the sacrifices were lapsing because of the war, so that matters relating to

the goddesses may be in good order for the Rhamnousians, for good fortune, the Rhamnousians and the other Athenians and all those living in Rhamnous shall decide: to praise Dikaiarchos son of Apollonios of Thria for the excellence and good will which he continues to have towards ⟦[king] D[emetrios]⟧ and towards the Athenian People and towards the

community of those living at Rhamnous, and crown him with a gold crown according to the law; and the superintendents and the demarch of the Rhamnousians shall inscribe this decree

on two stone stelai, so that there may be a reminder for those wishing

to benefit the community of the Rhamnousians and of those living in the fort, and stand one in the precinct of Dionysos and the other in

the Nemesion; and for the making of the stelai and the inscribing of the decree the treasurer of the Rhamnousians shall allocate

the expenditure and account for it to the demesmen; and also to choose

from among themselves five men who will carry out what has been decreed at once; these were chosen: Elpinikos son of Mnesippos, Lykeas

son of Hierokles, Strombichos son of Kleodorides, Thrasymachos son of Antimachos,

Lysitheos son of Diokles.
