[Ass]embly. Of the presiding committee [was putting to the] vote [...]

[...] son of -osthenes of Xypete [and his fellow presiding committee members. The] People [decided]. Agy[rrhios son of Kallimedon of Kolly]tos proposed: since [previously the ancestors]

of Spartokos [were of service to the People and]

now Spartokos, [having inherited the] connection [with the People], is of service [to the People] collectively and individually to the Athe[nians who come]

to him, in return for which [the Athenian People also made them]

citizens [and honoured] them [with bronze statues] both in the [agora and] in the trading-post [and]

with other grants, [with which it is appro]priate to honour

good men, [and made provision] that, if anyone marches

against the rule [of his ancestors] or that of Spartokos, [to help with all their strength] both by land and

by sea; [and further] Spartokos, when an embassy arrived

[from the Athenians], having heard that the People

had recovered [the town, shared in the] delight at the successes

of the People [and has given a] gift of [grain], fifteen

thousand medimnoi, and announces [also that in the future] he will be of service to the

[Athenian People in whatever way] he can, and [does this, choosing] to maintain the

[good will towards the People] which was handed down

[to him from his ancestors]; so that, therefore, [the People] may be seen

[also to show love of honour] towards those who have remained of good will to

[king] Spartokos son of Eumelos [...]

[...] [and to crown] him with a gold crown [according to]

[the law for his excellence] and good will, which [he continues] to have [towards the] People, and to announce the [crown at the] tragedy [competition] of the Great [Dionysia];

[and] the board of administrators shall manage the making of the crown and the [announcement; and the People shall stand] also a bronze statue of him in the

[agora beside] his ancestors and another in [...]

[...]; [and so] that king Spartokos may also know what has been [decreed] by the People, [the People] shall elect [three] men as ambassadors from all the Athenians, who, [having been elected], shall depart and [deliver] the decree and report the good will which

the People [has towards] him, and shall urge [him to help] the People in whatever way he can; and [to give each] of the ambassadors the [prescribed] travelling expenses; and so that there may also be a memorial of the [connection] and of the grants added for [him to] those already existing, the prytany secretary
