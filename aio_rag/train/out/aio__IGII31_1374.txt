[to those] of the citizens [who come to him], and he happens

[also] to be honoured by Ptol[emy] the general in charge of Cyprus, and he announces

that also in future he will maintain the same preference, and

concerning these things those of the citizens who have been to Cyprus have testified;

in order therefore that all may know that the People of the Athenians

continues to be grateful to those who show love of honour towards it, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted for the

forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise Nikeratos son of Nikeratos of Alexandria and crown

him with a foliage crown for his good will and love of honour

towards the People of the Athenians; and he shall also be proxenos and

benefactor of the People of the Athenians, and if in future he maintains the same

preference towards the People, he may obtain any other benefit

of which he may be worthy; and so that he may also have a reminder of his

good will towards the People, the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the

precinct of Demos and the Graces; and for the inscribing of the

stele and its setting up the treasurer shall allocate the expense
