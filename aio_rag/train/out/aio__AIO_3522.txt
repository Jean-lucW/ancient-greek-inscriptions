(Lease) of the (precinct) in Hermos.

On these terms the demesmen of the section of Archippos and Stesias lease the precinct of the goddess in Hermos, which Hierokles previously farmed, whose neighbour on the north is the estate of Hierophon; and the lessee shall farm on these terms, ploughing the land in alternation, the spaces between the rows with wheat

and barley, and half of the half with pulses, and the rest of the land he shall leave fallow straight away; and the term of the lease for which he shall farm is ten years;

the first archon of the lease is the archon after Lysimachides (339/8); he shall pay the rent at the meeting in the month of Posideon at the assemblies; he shall dig the vines twice each year, in the one year earthing them up at ground level, in the other year digging around them at ground level, and the other cultivated trees in the same way; and he shall stake the vines, the young ones before they sprout, and the old ones whenever he wishes;

the lessee shall live in the house which is on

this precinct and shall hand it over when he leaves the precinct watertight and upright, as he received it; he shall leave behind all the chaff produced on the estate and the manure and all the vine-props which he uses; he shall plant the rows of saplings, not leaving out the fig trees; he shall also plant [twelve] cuttings each year and as many olive trees as the lessors [instruct him to plant] as nursery plants; [wheat] [...]
