**Part 1**

Kephisophon of Paiania

was secretary (403/2).

For the Samians, as many as sided with the People of the Athenians.

The Council and the People decided. Kekropis was in prytany. Polymnis of Euonymon

was secretary. Alexias was archon (405/4). Nikophon of Athmonon was chairman. Proposal of Kleisophos

and his fellow prytany members: to praise the Samian ambassadors, both those who came previously and those now, and the Council and the generals and the other

Samians, because they are good men and keen to do whatever good they can,

and (to praise) what they have done, because they seem to have acted rightly for the Athenians and the Samians; and in return for the good they have done the Athenians and because they now hold them in high regard and

propose good measures, the Council and the People shall decide: the Samians shall be Athenians,

governing themselves in whatever way they themselves wish; and so that this shall be as suitable as possible for both, as they themselves say, when peace comes, then concerning

the other matters they shall deliberate in common. They shall use their own laws,

being autonomous, and do the other things in accordance with the oaths and the agreements, just as

has been agreed between the Athenians and the Samians; and concerning the complaints which arise

against each other they shall give and receive justice in accordance with the existing judicial agreements.

[If] anything necessary arises because of the war, even before (peace), concerning the constitution, as the ambassadors themselves say, they shall deliberate in the light of present circumstances and act

[in whatever way] seems best. Concerning the peace, if it comes about, the same terms shall apply

[as] for the Athenians also to those who now live on Samos; and if it is necessary to wage war, they shall prepare themselves as best they can, acting together with the generals.

[If] the Athenians send an embassy anywhere, those from Samos who are present shall also send with it,

[if] they wish, anyone, and shall give whatever good advice they have. The triremes

[which] are at Samos shall be given to them to use, after they have repaired them as seems good to them; and the names of the trierarchs whose ships these were

[the ambassadors] shall register with the secretary of the Council and the generals, and if [anything] of theirs [is] recorded [anywhere] in the public records as their having received the triremes,

the dockyard superintendents shall [erase all of it] everywhere, and shall [exact] the equipment for the public treasury [as quickly as possible and] compel those who have any of it to return it

[complete. Proposal of Kleisophos and] his fellow prytany members: in other respects in accordance with the Council,

[but the grant shall be valid for those who have] come, just as they themselves request, and

[the officials shall at once] distribute [them] among the tribes in ten parts; and [the generals] shall provide the journey [for the ambassadors] as quickly as possible; and Eumachos and

[all the other Samians who have come with Eumachos] shall be praised as being [good] men

[with regard to the Athenians; and invite] Eumachos to dinner in the city hall

[tomorrow; and the] secretary [of the] Council [shall inscribe what has been decreed] together with the

[generals on a stone stele and] put it on the acropolis, [and the Greek treasurers]

[shall give the money; and it shall be inscribed on Samos] in the same way at their expense.

**Part 2**

[The Council and the People decided. Pand]ionis was in prytany. Agyrrhios of [Kollytos]

[was secretary. Eukleides was archon (403/2).] Kallias of Oa was chairman. Kephisophon [proposed]:

[to praise the Samians because they are] good men with regard to the Athenians, and [all]

[that the People previously] voted, the People of the Athenians for the People of the [Samians, shall be valid].

[The Samians shall send, as they] themselves request, to Sparta whomever [they themselves wish; and since] they ask in addition for the Athenians to cooperate, [to choose in addition]

[ambassadors, and these shall cooperate] with the Samians in whatever good [they can and]

[shall deliberate in common with] them; and the Athenians praise the Ephesians and the Not[ians]

[because they readily received] those of the Samians who were in exile; and the embassy [of the Samians] shall be introduced

[to the People] to transact business if they need anything; and to invite also to

[dinner the embassy] of the Samians in the city hall tomorrow. Kephisophon

[proposed: in other respects] in accordance with the Council; but the People of the Athenians shall resolve that

[what was voted] previously concerning the Samians [shall be valid], as the Council, having made a preliminary resolution,

brought it [to the People]; and to invite the embassy of the Samians to dinner

[in the city hall] tomorrow.

**Part 3**

[The Council] and the People [decided]. Erechtheis was in prytany. Kephisophon of Paiania

[was secretary. Eukl]eides was archon (403/2). Python of Kedoi was chairman. Eu- [proposed]:

[to praise Poses the] Samian because he is a good man with regard to the Athenians, and in return for

[the good he has done the People, the People] shall give him a grant of five hundred drachmas

[for the making of a crown, and the treasurers] shall give the money; and he shall be introduced [to the People and obtain] from the People whatever good he can; and the document

[of the decree] the secretary of the Council [shall hand over to] him immediately;

[and invite the Samians] who have come [to hospitality] in the city hall tomorrow.

[...] [proposed: in other respects] in accordance with the Council; but to praise Poses the

[Samian and his sons, since they are] good [men] with regard to the People of the Athenians;

[and what was previously voted] by the People of the Athenians [shall be valid]; and [the secretary shall] inscribe [the decree on a] stone [stele], and the treasurers shall provide

[the money for the stele; and the People shall give] Poses a grant of a thousand drachmas

[for his excellence towards the Athenians, and from] the thousand drachmas to make a crown [and to inscribe on it that] the People [crowns] him for his manly virtue and

[excellence towards the Athenians; and to praise] also the Samians because they are good men

[with regard to the Athenians; and if they need anything from] the People, the prytany shall introduce them

[to the People, always first after the sacred business]; and [the prytany] shall introduce also the sons of Poses

[to the People at the first] session; and to invite [also to] hospitality

[in the city hall both Poses and his sons] and those of the Samians [who are in town].
