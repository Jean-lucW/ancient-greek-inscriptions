In the archonship of Olbios (275/4), in the twelfth prytany, of Leontis,

for which Kydias son of Timonides of Euonymon was secretary.

The old and new day of Skirophorion, the twenty-ninth

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Aischines son of Antikrates of Phaleron was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council

and People decided. Leon son of Kichesias of Aixone

proposed: since the commanders of tribal regiments of the archonship of Olbios

conducted their office well and according to the laws, [and]

have made as many of the sacrifices as they should have made [with the generals],

all from their own resources, [as they were instructed by(?) the]

People, and each managed [...]

his own regiment [so that they would proceed equipped as well as possible]

with arms [to the inspections and]

the guard-posts, and [they continued also in all]

other respects to obey [the generals in accordance]

with the laws; so, therefore, that [there may be an incentive for those who will hold]

this office [to hold office] with love of honour [and justly, for]

good fortune, the Council shall decide: [that the presiding committee]

allotted to preside [in the People at the coming]

Assembly shall put [these matters] on the agenda [and submit]

the opinion of the Council to [the People, that it seems good to the]

Council to praise the [commanders of tribal regiments of the archonship of Olbios]

and to crown [them with a gold crown according to]

the law for [their] excellence [and manly virtue towards the]

Council and [the Athenian People; and they shall also have]

front row seats [at all the competitions which the city puts on; and]

[the] prytany secretary shall inscribe [this decree]

[on a stone stele and stand it in front of]

[the headquarters; [...] ]
