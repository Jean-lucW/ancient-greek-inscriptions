The city of the Gonneis decided: [to choose]

a receiver of religious delegates in accordance with the decree

which the religious delegates of the Athenians

brought, Sosigenes and Archeptolemos,

and to inscribe the decree on a stone

stele and place it in the sanctuary

of Athena. As receiver of religious delegates Ni-

kaios son of Aristokrates willingly

accepted. Valid. Eukles son of Eukles

of Potamos proposed: since some, the

truce-bearers sent out by the city,

by receiving them as religious delegates,

make manifest their good will and kindness

towards the People,

and it is fitting for the People to fall short in nothing

of what pertains to honour and gratitude

towards those who choose to benefit the

People, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee

allotted to preside

at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put these matters on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council

that those who happen to be receivers of religious delegates

in the cities

designated for the truces

of the Eleusinia and the Panathenaia

and the Mysteries shall from now on be

proxenoi

of the People of the Athenians; and it shall be possible

for them, if they show love of honour

towards the city, to obtain also any other benefit

from the People, of which

they may be deemed worthy; and the

truce-bearers who announce

the Eleusinia and the Pa-

nathenaia and the Mysteries shall also

deliver to the Metroon in their

accounts the names of the receivers of religious delegates

with their fathers' names, whenever they

also report the cities that accepted

the truces; so that,

these things being done, both those who are

[disposed with love of honour] towards the city

may be [made manifest] and the

[People ...] may be seen
