In the archonship of Pelops (165/4), in the twelfth prytany, of Ptolemais,

for which Dionysikles son of Dionysios of Hekale was secretary. On the six-

teenth of Skirophorion, the sixteenth of the prytany. Assembly in the

theatre. Of the presiding committee Rhexion son of Damatrios of Thorai was putting to the vote and his fellow

presiding committee members. The Council and the People decided. Archippos son of Archias of Bate

proposed: concerning what the demarch of the Eleusinians reports about the sacrifices

which he made at the Haloa and the Chloia to Demeter and

Kore and the other gods for whom it was traditional, and he also completed the

sacrifice of the Kalamaia and despatched the procession according to tradition with

the hierophant and the priestesses, spending additionally on all these things no

small sum from his own resources, and he reports that the sacrifices turned out fine and salutary;

for good fortune, the Council shall decide that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the

forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to accept the

good things that occurred in the sacrifices which he made for the health and

preservation of the Council and the People and children and women and the

friends and allies; and to praise the demarch of the Eleusinians, Pam-

philos son of Archon of Eleusis, and crown him with a myrtle crown, as is tra-

ditional, for his piety towards the gods; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in the courtyard of the sanctuary at Eleusis, and for the inscribing

of the stele and its setting up the treasurer of the military funds shall allocate

the expense accrued.
