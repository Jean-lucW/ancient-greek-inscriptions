**Part 1**

Diodoros of Piraeus proposed: since Kallidamas son of Kallimedon of Cholleidai is a good man

towards both the Athenian People and the deme of the Piraeans and does what good he can and has displayed his good will on critical occasions, the Piraeans shall decide to praise Kallidamas and crown him with a foliage crown for his excellence and justice towards the Athenian

People and the deme of the Piraeans; and whenever the Piraeans sacrifice in the common sanctuaries, to assign a portion to Kallidamas also, just as to the other Piraeans, and Kallidamas shall feast together with the Piraeans in all the sanctuaries, except

where it is lawful for the Piraeans themselves to enter but not for anyone else; and he shall be allotted to whatever triakas he himself wishes; and he shall also have a seat of honour in the

theatre, whenever the Piraeans hold the Dionysia, where it is allotted to the Piraeans themselves, and the demarch shall lead him into

the theatre just as the priests and the others to whom the seat of honour has been given by the Piraeans; and he shall pay the same taxes in

the deme as the Piraeans also pay, and the demarch shall not collect from him the tax on landholding by non-demesmen; and the herald shall announce in the theatre at the competition of the tragedies that the Piraeans crown Kallidamas son of Kallimedon of Cholleidai for his excellence and good will towards the Athenian People and the deme of the Piraeans, so that all may know that the Piraeans know how to return worthy thanks to those who show love of honour towards them. And to inscribe this decree on a stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Hestia.
