In the archonship of Polyzelos (367/6), the vendors

Polyeuktos of Lamptrai, Deinias of Erchia, Theaios

of Paiania, Theotimos of Phrearrhioi, Aristogenes of

Iphistiadai, Glaukon of Lakiadai, Kephisokles of

Piraeus, Nikokles of Anaphlystos, for whom Exekestos

of Kothokidai was secretary, sold the following, having received

them from the Eleven, Phaiax of Aphidna and his fellow

officials: on the tenth of Mounichion, Theomnestos

son of Deisitheos of Ionidai registered a house of Theosebes son of

Theophilos of Xypete at Alopeke as public

property, of which the neighbour to the north is the road leading to the Daidaleion

and the Daidaleion, to the south Philippos of

Agryle, Theosebes having been convicted of temple-robbery and not having

awaited the trial, for whatever it is worth more than it is

mortgaged to Smikythos of Teithras for 150 drachmas; summoner

Diogeiton of Alopeke, Philoitios of Ionidai. Kichonides

son of Diogeiton of Gargettos and the association of the phratry members of the

Medontidai lodged a claim that there was owed to himself

and to the phratry members on the house at Alopeke 100

drachmas, which Theomnestos of Ionidai registered as Theosebes'

of Xypete, of which the neighbour to the north is the road leading to the

Daidaleion and the Daidaleion, to the south Philippos

of Agryle, Theophilos of Xypete, the father of Theosebes, having sold to me and to the phratry members

this house. It was decided that it was owed. Isarchos son of Philon of

Xypete claims that there is owed to himself on the

house at Alopeke which Theomnestos son of

Deisitheos of Ionidai registered, since I buried Theophilos, whose the

house was, and the wife of Theophilos, 30 drachmas. It was

decided that it was owed. Aischines of Melite and the association of

orgeones lodged a claim that on the house which Theomnestos

of Ionidai registered there was owed to them

24 drachmas, since we bought this house

from Theophilos for this money on terms of redemption. It was

decided that it was owed. Buyer: Lysanias son of Palathion

of Lakiadai: 575 dr.; of this the down-payment, the fifth

part, the city has, and the sales taxes and the auction fees,

and Smikythos of Teithras the one hundred and

fifty; all together according to the inventory.
