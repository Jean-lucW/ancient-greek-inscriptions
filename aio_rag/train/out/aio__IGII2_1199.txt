Philaios son of Chremes proposed: since the

religious officials allotted to the sanctuary of Hebe

took care justly and with love of honour

of the sacrifice to Hebe and

to the other gods to whom they had to

sacrifice, and have rendered account and audit,

to crown each of them with a foliage

crown, Anticharmos son of Nauson

and Nearchos son of Chairigenes,

Theodotos son of Aischron, Aristokles

son of Kalliphon, for their justice

and love of honour towards the demesmen;

and the demarch of the year after Neaichmos (320/19)

was archon shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary

of Hebe.
