Prayer. Apellis, for the friendship of Eunikos [...] that no one more zeal-

ous than [E]unikos be, nor Phintion, but (that they) praise (him) both willingly and un-

willingly and love (him), for the friendship of Eunikos. I write down the

chorus-sponsors, all of them, for ineffectiveness of both words and deeds, and the

boys from them and the fathers, and for failure both in the con-

test and outside contests, whoever should not leave (them) with me. Kaledias

[I write] down away from Apellis, and all those there, for mesoter-

[...] here. Sosias I write down from the tavern. Alkidas, for the

friendship of [Mel?]anthios, Pyrias, Mysskelos, Damophantos, and the

chorus-sponsor I write down from the boys and the fathers, and the other(s),

[all] who should arrive here, that no one more zeal-

[ous] than Eunikos become, neither among men nor among women; as this lead the [...]

[...] by incantation may they draw honour for Eunikos; (may he) always win everywhere; on lead, for

[the friend]ship of Eunikos, I write.
