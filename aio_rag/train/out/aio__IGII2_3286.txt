To P(ublius) Aelius Hadrianus son of P(ublius), of the tribe Sergia,

consul, member of the board of seven for sacred banquets, member of the Augustal priesthood, legate with praetorian power of the Emperor Nerva Trajan

Caesar Augustus Germanicus Dacicus for Lower Pannonia, praetor and at the same

time legate of the 1st legion Minervia Pia Fidelis in the Dacian war, likewise tribune of the plebs, quaestor of the Emperor

Trajan and companion on the Dacian expedition, twice presented by him with military decorations, tribune of the 2nd legion

Adiutrix Pia Fidelis, likewise of the 5th legion Macedonica, likewise of the 22nd legion Primigenia Pia Fidelis, sevir

of a squadron of Roman knights, prefect of the Latin festival, member of the board of ten for judging lawsuits.

The Areopagos Council and the Council of the Six Hundred and the

People of the Athenians (erected this statue of) their own archon

Hadrian.
