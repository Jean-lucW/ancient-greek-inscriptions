In the priesthood of Hegesipyle.

In the archonship of Euthios (283/2), in the

twelfth prytany, of Aiantis,

for which Nausimenes

son of Nausikydes of Cholargos

was secretary. On the old and new day of

Skirophorion. Kallias son of

Lysimachos of Hermos proposed: so

that the astynomoi who are allotted

each year may take care

of the sanctuary of Aphrodite

Pandemos in accordance with ancestral custom, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the forthcoming

Assembly shall bring forward the

relative of the priestess and put on the agenda

these matters, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that

it seems good to the Council that the astynomoi

who are allotted each year, whenever there is

the procession for Aphrodite Pandemos,

shall provide for the purification

of the sanctuary a dove, and plaster

the altars and pitch the

[roofs] and wash the statues; and they shall
