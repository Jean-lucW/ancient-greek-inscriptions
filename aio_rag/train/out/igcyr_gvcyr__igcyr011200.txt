In the fifteenth year, in the month Loios.

For good fortune. These are the dispositions which king

Ptolemy the younger, (son) of king Ptolemy

and queen Kleopatra, the gods

Epiphaneis, made, of which the copies also

have been sent to Rome. May it be granted to me

with the goodwill of the gods to punish

as they deserve those who organised against me

the unholy plot and chose

to deprive me not only of my kingdom but also

of life; but if anything should befall me

of the lot of mankind before I leave successors

to the kingdom, I bequeath

to the Romans the kingdom which belongs to me,

with whom from the beginning both friendship and

alliance I have genuinely preserved;

and to the same I entrust my affairs

to preserve, entreating them by all the gods

and by their own good reputation, if any

attack either the cities or the territory, to help

in accordance with the friendship and alliance

that has come into being between us towards one another and

justice, with all their strength.

As witnesses of these things I make Zeus

Capitolinus and the Great Gods

and Helios and Apollo the Founder,

in whose (sanctuary) the writings concerning these matters are also consecrated.

For Good Fortune.
