On the sixteenth, the thirty-

third of the prytany. Assembly. Of the

presiding committee Demochares

of Phlya was putting to the vote. The People decided. Demos-

thenes son of Demokles of Lamptrai pro-

posed: since those elected by

the People for the management of

the contest and the other things concerning

the festival of Amphiaraos well and

with love of honour managed the

procession for Amphiaraos and the contest,

the athletic and equestrian, and

the dismounting race and all the other things

concerning the festival which

the People assigned to them, the People shall

decide: to praise those chosen,

Phanodemos son of Diyllos of Thymaitadai,

Lykourgos son of Lykophron of Boutadai,

Demades son of Demeas of Paiania, Sophilos

son of Aristoteles of Phyle, Thrasy-

leon son of Theophon of Acharnai, Epiteles

son of Soinomos of Pergase, Nikeratos

son of Nikias of Kydantidai, Epichares

son of Agonochares of Paiania, Thymochares

son of Phaidros of Sphettos, Kephisophon

son of Lysiphon of Cholargos, for their justice

and love of honour towards

the god and the People of the Athenians,

and to crown them with a gold

crown of 1000 drachmas; and to give

them also for a sacrifice and dedication

100 drachmas; and the money for

the sacrifice the treasurer of the People shall advance;

and in the first lawmakers

there shall be an amendment to the law for the

treasurer; and the treasurer of the People shall also give the thirty

drachmas to those

chosen for the contest, which it is stated

shall be given in the law to the man chosen

for good order; and the secretary

of the Council shall inscribe this decree

and stand it in the sanctuary

of Amphiaraos; and for the inscribing

of the stele the treasurer

of the People shall give 30 drachmas from the

People's fund for expenditure on decrees.
