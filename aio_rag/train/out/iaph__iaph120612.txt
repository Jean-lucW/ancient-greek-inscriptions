Decree. Since

Dionysios son of Papylos the son of Papylos, the priest of Zeus Nineudios, a man

whose father and ancestors were fine and good men and had been active in magistracies and

munificent undertakings, and who himself from his first youth chose a dignified

and virtuous life, and fulfilled the priesthood of the Augusti and the presidency of the

games and embassies and the ephebarchy and the generalship and the secretaryship and all

the other magistracies with dignity and piety and lawfully and justly,

and lives a life adorned with every nobility, as has been attested for him

through very many decrees already, has now been called upon

by the People and, having been chosen, has been elected chief neopoios;

[for which] the People, approving the man's civic-minded zeal in all things,

[when] the elections [were being held], was unanimously moved again to hon-

[our him ...]
