The Council and the People honoured,

also after his death, with the

finest and most fitting

honours Pereitas son of Diogenes

son of Apollonios, Kallimedes,

a virtuous man, who lived

gloriously and conspicuously and

with propriety, having been active in

liturgies and magistracies and

embassies, and having served as neopoios

magnificently and with propriety

and conspicuously, and having done everything

in a manner appropriate

to the standing of his family;

for which the Council and the People acclaimed

that he should be honoured even

after his death.

Mar(cus) Aurelius

Diodoros

Kallimedes, a man

of virtue, who lived

gloriously

and conspicuously

and with propriety, having been

active in

liturgies and

magistracies and

embassies, and having done

everything

in a manner appropriate

to the

standing of his family,

a truly

philosophical man.

The Council and the People

honoured

Tatia daughter of Diogenes son of

Diogenes son of Demetrios

Philemon, prudent

and loving to her husband and loving to her

children, and in every way throughout

her whole life adorned

with dignity and virtue,

who became the wife

of Pereitas son of Diogenes

son of Apollonios Kallimedes,

a man who was active in magistracies

and embassies and liturgies

and who served as neopoios

piously and with love

of honour.
