When Lucius son of Caesius and Philonidas son of Phil[...] (were in office),

on the 4th of Adonios. Resolved

by the assembly as also by the council. Since,

fortune having led the way well, the public affairs

of the Nakonaioi have been set right, and it is advantageous for the future too

to conduct civic life in concord, and envoys of the Egestaioi, having come,

Apellichos son of Aleidas, Attikos son of Piston, Dionysios son of Dekios,

gave counsel on matters of common advantage to all the citizens;

resolved, on the 4th of Adonios, to convene an assembly

of the citizens, and those of the citizens among whom the dispute

has arisen, contending over public affairs, being summoned into

the assembly, are to make a reconciliation with one another, thirty

of each side having been listed in advance; those who have been opponents in

former times shall each list those of the other side; and the

archons, having written the names on lots, separately for each

side, and having cast them into two urns, shall draw by lot one from each

side, and from the rest of the citizens they shall draw in addition three

to join the two, excluding the degrees of kinship which the law

orders to be removed from the courts; and among themselves those

allotted together shall be chosen brothers, living in concord with one another

with all justice and friendship; and when the

sixty lots have all been drawn, and those allotted

with them, they shall allot the remaining citizens,

all of them, in groups of five, not allotting together

kin, as has been written, and among

themselves these too shall be brothers, just as those

previously allotted together among themselves; and the hieromnamones at the sacrifice

shall sacrifice a white goat, and whatever is needed for the sacrifice

the treasurer shall provide; and likewise all the successive magistrates

shall sacrifice each year on this day to the

ancestors and to Homonoia a victim for each, whichever they

approve; and all the citizens shall feast with one another

according to their brotherhoods; and this decree, having engraved it

on a bronze tablet, the archons shall dedicate in the pronaos of Zeus Olympios.
