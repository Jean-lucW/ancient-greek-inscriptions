Decree 2

In the archonship of Demochares (108/7), in the [...] prytany, of [...], for which [...]

son of Dionysodoros of Ankyle [was secretary]. On the [...] of [...], the [elev?]enth of the prytany. [Principal] Assembly [in the theatre. Of the presiding committee - son of Ti]myllos of Eroiadai was putting to the vote and his fellow [presiding committee members. The Council and the People decided. - son of -]

of Melite proposed: since, [having made] an approach [to the Council, the fathers of the maidens]

who have worked the [wool] for Athena [for the robe make clear that] they [have followed all] the decrees of the People [regarding these matters and have done what is right and have taken part in the] procession in accordance with the prescriptions [in the most fine and seemly manner possible, and that they have made] from their own resources a [silver] phiale worth [one hundred drachmas, which they also wish to dedicate]

to Athena as a memorial of their [piety] towards [the goddess, and they request the Council and the]

People to permit the dedication of the [phiale; for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the] presiding committee [allotted] to preside at the forthcoming Assembly shall put [these matters] on the agenda, [and submit] the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council [to permit the dedication of the] phiale which the maidens have made, just as [...]; [and to praise them and crown]

each of them with a foliage crown [for their] piety [towards the gods and their love of honour]

towards the Council and the People; ⟦[...]⟧ and [...] [the] competition director of the Panathenaia, Themistokles [...] [the]

prytany [secretary] shall inscribe on a stone stele the [decree] and the names [of the maidens and set it] up on the acropolis beside the temple of Athena Polias, [so that] [...] the zeal and [industry] which they have shown in these matters [may be easy to follow].
