[... that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the] forthcoming

[Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and]

submit [the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good]

to the Council to praise Eukleides [son of Kleombrotos of Rhodes]

and crown him with a foliage crown [for his good will]

and love of honour towards the People [of the Athenians];

and he shall be proxenos and benefactor of the People;

and he shall be granted right of ownership of land and a house, on his

request, according to the law; and Eukleides shall be permitted, in the

future, on maintaining the same preference, to obtain

other benefits from the People, whatever he may be deemed worthy of;

and in order that there may be a reminder of what has been decreed,

the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the agora;

and for the inscribing and the setting up of the stele

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the

expenditure accrued.
