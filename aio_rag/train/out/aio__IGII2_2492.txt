On the following terms the Aixoneis leased the Phelleis

to Autokles son of Auteas and Auteas son of Autokles for forty

years, for one hundred and fifty-two

drachmas each year, on condition that they may both plant

and (work it) in any other way they wish; and

they shall pay the rent in the month of Hekatombaion,

and if they do not pay, the Aixoneis shall have the right of distraint

both on the crops from the estate and

on all the other property of the one not paying; and it shall not

be possible for the Aixoneis either to sell or to lease it

to anyone else, until the forty years

have expired; and if enemies shut (them) out or destroy

anything, the Aixoneis shall have half of what is produced on the

estate; and when the forty years

have expired, the lessees shall hand over half

of the land fallow and the trees that are on the

estate, and the Aixoneis shall bring in a vine-dresser in the last

five years; the period of the lease begins

for the crop of Demeter with Euboulos as archon (345/4), and for the tree

crop with the archon after Euboulos (344/3); and having inscribed the lease on

stone stelai the treasurers in the demarchy of Demosthenes

shall stand one inside the sanctuary of Hebe,

and the other in the club-house, and (shall set) boundary markers on the estate, no

less than three feet high, two on each side; and if any property

tax is levied on the estate for the city, the Aixoneis

shall pay it, and if the lessees pay it, it shall be

deducted from the rent; and the earth from the excavation

it shall not be possible for anyone to remove except to the estate

itself; and if anyone proposes or puts to the vote anything contrary to these

agreements before the forty years have expired, he shall

be liable to the lessees for damages. Eteokles

son of Skaon of Aixone proposed: since the lessees of the Phelleis,

Autokles and Auteas, agree to the cutting

down of the olive trees for the Aixoneis, to choose men who

with the demarch and the treasurers and the lessee shall

sell the olive trees to the man who offers the most, and of the

money realised, reckoning the interest at a drachma,

to deduct half the interest from the rent and to inscribe

on the stelai the rent as less by so much;

and the Aixoneis shall receive the interest on the money from the price of the

olive trees; and the purchaser shall cut down the olive trees

when Anthias has gathered the crop in the year after Archias

(346/5) was archon, before the ploughing; and shall leave stumps no

less than a palm's breadth high in the trenches around them, so

that the olive trees may become as fine and large as possible

in these years. These were chosen to sell the

olive trees: Eteokles, Nauson, Hagnotheos. uninscribed space
