The Council of the archonship of Pythodotos (343/2) dedicated (this)

to Hephaistos, having been crowned by the People

for its excellence and justice.

Deinostratos son of Deiniades of Ankyle proposed: since the Council of the archonship of Pythodotos, having voted to make a judgement of those speaking in the Council in the ninth prytany

and to honour whoever it deemed to have continued throughout the year speaking and acting best and incorruptibly on behalf of the Council and the Athenian People, judged, having voted

on these matters, the Council, (to be) Phanodemos son of Diyllos of Thymaitadai, the Council shall decide, for the good fortune of the Athenian People and of the Council: to praise Phanodemos son of Diyllos of Thymaitadai

for his excellence and justice towards the Council and the Athenian People and crown him with a gold crown of 500 drachmas; and the money for the

crown shall be from the Council's fund for expenditure on decrees; and those chosen to make the dedication shall also inscribe this decree on the dedication of the Council; and in order that the

People also, knowing what has been voted by the Council concerning Phanodemos, may itself also honour and crown him, if the People decides as the Council did, the presiding committee allotted to preside in

the People at the next Assembly shall put the matter of Phanodemos on the agenda, and the secretary shall read out this decree to the People, and submit the opinion of the Council to the

People, that it seems good to the Council, since Phanodemos son of Diyllos of Thymaitadai has served on the Council well and with love of honour and incorruptibly, speaking and doing what is best on behalf of the Council and

the Athenian People and the allies, to praise him for his excellence and justice towards the Council and the Athenian People [and the allies] and crown

him with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas, when he has rendered his accounts; and the money for the crown shall be from whatever source the People decides; so that [therefore all the others also] may know that

the People and the Council know how to return thanks to those who always speak and do [what is best] on behalf of the Council and the People, [this decree] the prytany

[secretary shall inscribe] on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis, and for inscribing the stele the treasurer of the People shall give 3[0 drachmas from the People's fund for expenditure on] decrees.
