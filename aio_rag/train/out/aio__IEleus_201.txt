Gods.

Thrasyphon son of Hierokleides of Xypete proposed:

since the hierophant Chairetios is continually

well disposed towards the genos both of the Kerykes and of the Eumol-

pidai and speaks and does whatever good

he can on their behalf, and for those travelling abroad

on the truce-bearing missions he continually, with good will,

writes out the announcement, and conducts himself

also in the office of the priesthood decorously,

keeping himself free of reproach; in order

therefore that the genē too may be seen to honour those

who are well disposed and worthy of them, for good

fortune, the Kerykes and Eumolpidai shall decide, to praise

the hierophant Chairetios son of Prophetes of Eleu-

sis and to crown him with a myrtle crown, with which

it is traditional for them, for the good will which he

continually has towards the genē; and to announce this

crown at the Dionysia at the traditional competition

at Eleusis in the theatre, and the announcement

of the crown shall be the responsibility of the archons

appointed from time to time from each of the two genē;

and to inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it at Eleusis in the courtyard of the sanctuary, and

the archons of the genē shall take care of the making of the stele

and its setting up.

In myrtle crown

The Kerykes

and Eumolpidai

(crown) the hierophant

Chairetios

of Eleusis.
