Gods.

At the (sanctuary) of Amphiaraos: from the men's bath-house,

so that the water may not be hindered from flowing by the

torrent-bed, whenever it flows, but the bath-house may be usable

when the god sends winter storms, to make a covered stone

drain, the length beginning from the

paving of the bridge as far as the

descent beside the women's bath-house;

having dug a trench of the length written, and in

width from the retaining wall of the torrent-bed

four feet, and in depth three feet, he will place

stones, those on the floor putting together in a mat pattern,

fitting to one another and steady on their beds,

knocking down each stone

with lead or stone; and having trimmed them on top and

levelled them to the slope so that it may be

free-flowing, he will place on these (stones), having worked

the beds of the stones straight and untwisted and

the joints complete, and he will dress the faces

smooth; and he will place the stones upright on the

one-and-a-half-foot stones, leaving a channel a foot wide;

and he will place them steady on their beds [and]

fitting to one another, bringing together the joints

complete; and having trimmed them smooth on top he will

cover them over with stones, putting them together

fitting to one another and placing them steady on their beds,

nowhere making [joints in line]; and he will pack

all the stones with the earth from the trench; and he will use

stones from the theatre by the

altar, bringing them himself

to the work; and if they are not sufficient, the superintendents

of the work will provide as many as are additionally needed;

and he will undertake the work by four-foot lengths

and hand it over finished within twenty days from the day he

receives the money. He contracted for the four-foot

length at 6 dr. Contractor: Phrynos living in Alopeke. Guarantor:

Telesias son of Tellias of Euonymon.
