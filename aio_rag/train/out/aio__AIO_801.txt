**Part 1**

[...] [to Phanokritos] [...] to [give] for his benefaction, if the People also decides, and to inscribe the benefaction on a stone stele on the acropolis; and to invite him to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow.

Kephalos proposed: in other respects in accordance with the Council; but the secretary of the Council shall inscribe Phanokritos

of Parion as proxenos and benefactor, himself and his descendants, on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis,

since he gave advance notice to the generals about [the]

ships' voyage along the coast, and if the generals had heeded him the enemy triremes would have been

captured; in return for these things he shall have both the proxeny and the benefaction; and to invite him to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow; and the receivers shall apportion the stated money from the funds deposited, once they have apportioned the sums prescribed by the laws.
