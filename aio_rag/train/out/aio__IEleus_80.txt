Timokedes son of Gnathis proposed: since

Smikythion the peripolarch is a good

man concerning the deme of

Eleusis, and both stationed himself

at Eleusis and the soldiers

with him, and acted

with both the generals and the

People so that a sufficient guard might come to

Eleusis, and the other things which he needed

[for the] guarding of Eleusis, the Eleusinians

shall resolve to praise Smikythion

[son of O- of Kephale and]

[crown him with a gold] crown; and

the demarch who is demarch after Isarchos

shall also announce at the Dionysia

at the tragedies that the deme of

Eleusis crowns Smikythion son of

[O- of] Kephale with this

[crown] for his excellence and justice

towards the deme of Eleusis;

and he shall also have a seat of honour,

[and] the demarch [in office at any time]

shall summon him just as also

[the others] to whom a seat of honour has been given;

[and so that] all may also know that

the deme of Eleusis renders thanks

to those who benefit it,

the demarch Isarchos shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele

and stand it wherever seems to be

the finest place.
