Gods.

In the archonship of Kephisodoros (323/2), in the [fifth]

prytany, [of Pandionis], for which Archias son of [Pythodoros of Alopeke]

was secretary. On the [sixteenth] of Posideon, [the twenty-]

[second] of the prytany. [Principal Assembly. Of the]

presiding committee Epameinon [of -] was putting to the vote. [The]

People [decided]. Euphiletos son of Euphiletos [of -]

proposed: since Euphron son of Hadeas of Sikyon [continues to be a good]

man with regard to the Athenian People [and has now come]

from the People of Sikyon and announces [that the city]

is a friend and ally [...]

to the Athenian People, first [of the cities in the]

[Peloponnese], the People shall decide: to praise [Euphron]

son of Hadeas of Sikyon for his excellence [and good will]

towards the Athenian People [and the other allies],

and the grants [previously]

made to him by the [Athenian] People shall be valid; [and so]

that all may know that the [Athenian] People [renders]

favours to its benefactors [worthy of the benefactions]

with which [each] benefits [the People, he shall be Athenian],

himself and his descendants, [and it shall be possible for him to enrol]

in the tribe and deme and phratry that he [wishes and]

of which the laws prescribe; [and to praise the People]

of Sikyon for its excellence and good will [towards the]

Athenian People, and to crown it with a [gold crown]

of 1000 drachmas and to announce it at the competition of the [Great Dionysia];

and to inscribe the decree [on stone]

stelai and stand one on the [acropolis, and the]

other in the agora beside [...]; [and for]

the inscribing of the stelai [the treasurer of the]

People [shall give] 50 dr. from the People's fund for expenditure on

decrees. The following on the proposal of Pamphilos son of Euphiletos: [the prytany]

of Pandionis [shall put the vote] to the People about the citizenship

at the next Assembly, [according to the]

law.
