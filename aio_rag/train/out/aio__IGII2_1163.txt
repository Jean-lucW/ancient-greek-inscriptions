Gods.

Proxenos son of Pylagoras of Acherdous

proposed: since Phyleus, having been allotted priest

of Asklepios in the archonship of Isaios (339/8),

has sacrificed all the sacrifices which

were his concern to sacrifice well and

with love of honour on behalf of the People

of Athens, and is also taking care

of the allotment of the courts

and the other things which both the

laws and the decrees require of him

justly and according to the laws,

and for these reasons both the Council and

the People have praised him; for good

fortune, the Hippothontids shall decide

to praise Phyleus son of Chairias of Eleusis

for his piety towards

the gods and his justice and

love of honour towards the tribesmen

and the People of Athens, and

to crown him with a foliage crown;

and the managers of the tribe shall inscribe this decree

on stone stelai and stand

one in the Asklepieion, the other

in the Hippothontion; and whatever expenditure

is incurred they shall account for it to the tribe.
