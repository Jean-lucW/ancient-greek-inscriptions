**Part 1**

[The Ikarians decided? - proposed: since -aios son of -]igenes of Ikarion sacrificed the sacrifices to all the gods

[and the heroes? [...] - [...] managed] well and with love of honour, and reported that they were fine

[and salutary? [...] - at the revel? of the Ikarians?] (?) good crops have come about throughout the whole territory [...]

[[...], and since] he has rendered an account in the month of Hekatombaion on the tenth

[[...]] he declares a surplus of money, having calculated the revenues and the

expenditures, [- - so that he seems to have held office well] and justly, and handed over to the demarch after him, Thoukydides, the surplus [money - - to praise -]aios and crown him with a gold crown of 1000 drachmas for his excellence and justice towards the demesmen; and to inscribe this decree in the Dionysion; and to announce the crown at the Dionysia at the tragedies.
