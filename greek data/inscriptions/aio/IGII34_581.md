# Funerary monument commemorating victories in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Part 1**

Victories: in the chariot race at the New Isthmia (?).

At the Pythia, at the Nemeia, at the Nemeia,

at the Nemeia, at the Isthmia, at the Isthmia,

at the Panathenaia in the horse race.
