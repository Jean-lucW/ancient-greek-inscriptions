# Dedication of the Aqueduct of Hadrian

## English translation

Latin Text:

Emperor Caesar Titus Aelius Hadrian Antoninus

Augustus Pius, consul 3 times, tribunician power 2 times (139 AD?) , father of the

fatherland, this aqueduct in new Athens, begun by the God Hadrian, his father,

he completed and dedicated.
