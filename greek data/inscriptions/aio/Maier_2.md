# Restoration of the Piraeus walls

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Euboulides (394/3)

Beginning from the sign,

up to the central pillar (?)

of the gates by

the Aphrodision on the right

as one goes out: 790 dr.

Contractor: Demosthenes

of Boeotia for the actual

delivery of the stones.
