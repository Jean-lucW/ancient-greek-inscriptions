# Statue base for the dadouch Sophokles

## English translation

**Part 1**

Ktesikleia, daughter of Apollonios

of Acharnai,

dedicated twice her own husband Sophokles

son of Xenokles of Acharnai, who was

dadouch of Demeter and

Kore.
