# Dedication commemoriating an (ephebic?) gymnasiarchy

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Relief

Γοργίας Λυκίσκου

γυμνασιαρχήσας

ἀνέθηκε.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Gorgias son of Lykiskos,

having been gymnasiarch,

dedicated (this).
