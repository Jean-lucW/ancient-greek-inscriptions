# Honours for a prytany

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Anaxikrates (279/8) , in the ninth prytany,

of -, [for which -es son of Lysistratos of Marathon ] was secretary.

On the [twelfth] of Elaphebolion, the [twenty-first]

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee -s son of Agatho-

of - was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The People

decided. - son of - of - proposed:

concerning what the prytany of Pandionis reports

about the sacrifices which they made before the Assemblies to the gods

to whom it is traditional; and they sacrificed for the Council and

People [...]

Athenian [...]

[...] and the [...] , for good fortune, the

People shall decide: the People shall accept the good things the prytany

declare occurred in the sacrifices which they made for the health

and preservation of the the Council and the People; and to praise

the prytany of Pandionis, and to crown them

with a gold crown [for their excellence and love of honour];

and the secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it [in the prytanikon]; and for the making of

the stele [the board of administrators shall allocate the expenditure].
