# Statue base for a hierophant

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] received splendid gifts

[...] -minded word

[...] Competition-director and eponymous archonship

[...] mystic leader

[...] before revealing the Mysteries to men,

his fatherland honoured him by decree, having adulated him.

His onetime bride [Myrt]ale set up a bronze statue,

a solemn dedication for her godly husband.

She was the daughter of Glaukos, who was the best

of knights, procurator of Cyprus in the sea,

and sister of godlike Glaukos, who himself also

served as hierophant and departed to the immortals.
