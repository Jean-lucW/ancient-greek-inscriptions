# Dedication to Hera

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Ἀρχεστράτη

Εὐριπίδο θυγάτηρ

Παλληνέως

ἀ[νέ]θηκε Ἥρηι γη  
[θ]ομένη.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Archestrate,

daughter of Euripides

of Pallene,

dedicated (this herm) to Hera,

being full of joy.
