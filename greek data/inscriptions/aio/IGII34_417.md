# Dedication by ephebes (Soteria), 144/5 AD (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] in the superintendentship

of Alkidamos,

the ephebes

Themistokles and

Alkidamos sons

of Alkidamos of

Cholleidai dedicated (this)

to Zeus

the Saviour of ephebes

in (the archonship of) Syllas (144/5 AD?) .
