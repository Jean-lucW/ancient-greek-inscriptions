# Honours for Menandros of Pergamon

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Achaios (190/89) , in the - prytany, of -

for which Herakleon son of Nannakos of Eupyridai was secretary.

On the penultimate day (?) of Anthesterion [but by divine reckoning?],

the twenty-seventh of Elaphebolion, the - of the prytany.

Assembly in the Piraeus. Of the presiding committee - son of - of

Ptelea was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and the People decided.

Diochares son of Artemidoros of Berenikidai proposed: since [...]

Menandros of Pergamon, [...]

with king Eumenes, [...]

goodness, is well disposed [...]

to those citizens who come to [Pergamon] [...]

whether on an embassy or individually [...]

[...] [showing] himself honour-loving [...]
