# Dedication by a court president (<i>thesmothetes</i>), 329/8 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

Kleonymos son of Kleemporos of Epieikidai,

who was a court president in the archonship

of Kephisophon (329/8) , dedicated (this)

**Column 1**

In olive crown

The People

**Column 2**

Olive crown (empty)

**Column 3**

In olive crown

The Council

**Face-left b**

**Column 4**

In olive crown

The tribe

**Column 5**

In olive crown

The Council

**Face-right c**

**Column 6**

In olive crown

The demesmen

**Column 7**

In olive crown

His fellow officials.
