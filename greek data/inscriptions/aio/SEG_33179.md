# Ephesos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar [Trajan Hadrian]

Augustus Olympios [Panhellenios, saviour]

(was dedicated by) the mother-city, [first of all and greatest]

of Asia, and twice temple-warden [of the Augusti],

the city of the [Ephesians], for their own [founder and benefactor, through]

the manager Marcus Tigellius [Lupus?],

in the priesthood of Tiberius Claudius [Atticus].
