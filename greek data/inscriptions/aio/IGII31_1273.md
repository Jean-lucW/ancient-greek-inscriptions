# Honours for a man or men from Keos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Euthykritos (189/8) , in the

eighth prytany, of Kekropis, for which Kephalos son of Kephalos

of Kydantidai was secretary. Decrees of the

People. On the nineteenth of Anthesterion, the

thirty-first of the prytany. Assembly

in the Piraeus. Of the presiding committee

Euthymachos son of Ergochares of Kerameis was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and the People decided.

-es son of Nikandros of Paiania proposed: since

-on of Phyle draws to the Council's attention

the benefactions made in the past to the People by

- of Keos and has read to the Council

the awards [that were decreed] for him

[...] on behalf of Epameinon [the People?] of Karthaia

[...] of Charixenos that he be awarded

[...] and of Epameinon himself

[...] for those citizens arriving

in [Karthaia?]; in order therefore that the People may be seen

[...] of benefactors [...]

[...] , the Council shall decide,

that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda and submit

the opinion [...]
