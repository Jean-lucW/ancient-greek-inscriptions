# Citizenship for Bithys of Lysimacheia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

four illegible lines

[...] the People [...]

[...] and the [...]

[...]

[...] assigning them to their regiments [...]

he appoints [...] , and he manages everything else with love of honour

and he is for all Athenians a good man

and of good will to the People, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted

to preside in the People at the next Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise Bithys son of Kleon of Lysimacheia for his excellence

and good will to the Athenian People, and

to crown him with a gold crown according to the law; and that he be

Athenian, both he and his descendants, and

shall enrol in the tribe and deme and phratry that he wishes

according to the law; and the prytany that is

presiding shall put the vote about him, so that there might be

competition among everyone to show love of honour towards the Athenian

People, knowing that they will receive thanks

to the value of their benefactions; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on the acropolis; and for the

inscription of the stele the board of administrators shall allocate

10 drachmas.
