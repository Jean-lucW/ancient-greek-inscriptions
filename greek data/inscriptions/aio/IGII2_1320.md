# Decree of artists of Dionysos honouring officials

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] [and]

[managed] the association of the

artists with the additionally appointed

managers in as honour-loving a manner

as they could, having contributed

from their own resources; for good fortune,

the artists shall decide to praise

the religious officials, Sositheos

the tragic actor and Sophilos the singer and

crown both of them

with an ivy crown for their piety

towards the gods and love of honour

towards the society of artists;

and to inscribe this decree

and their names on a stone

stele and stand it in the dedication of

Poseidippos.

**Column 1**

The artists (crowned)

Sositheos

the tragic actor

**Column 2**

The artists (crowned)

Sophilos

the singer
