# Statue base (?) for Marcus Ulpius Eubiotos

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune,

(this image of) the most brilliant consular and eponymous archon

Marcus Ulpius Eubiotos Leuros of Gargettos (was erected) on account of

all his excellence and his love of honour regarding the fatherland,

both in the grain funds, to which he gave bountifully

two hundred and fifty thousand (denarii), and in the provisions, which

he provided in the great famine, by decree

of the mighty Areopagites, the priest

of Apollo Patroos, Publius Aelius Zenon

of Berenikidai (erected it for) the common benefactor and

his own patron.
