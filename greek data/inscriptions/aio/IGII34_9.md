# Dedicatory inscription for Dionysos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council [in the archonship of -]

[having been crowned by the] People

[for its justice dedicated] to Dionysos

[...] from the revenues,

those who were elected [by the Council] in accordance

with the decree [having taken care of it]:

[...] son of -sios of Azenia

[...] of Acharnai

[...] son of -ratos of Thorai

[...] of Pambotadai.

[...]
