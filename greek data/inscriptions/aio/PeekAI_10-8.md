# Decree of Halai Aixonides

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] ; and [the demarch] shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and

[stand it at Halai in the] sanctuary of Apollo

[Zoster]; and whatever expense may be accrued,

[it shall be accounted for] to the demesmen.
