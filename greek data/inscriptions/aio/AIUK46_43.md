# Grave stele of Agathemeris and Sempronius Niketes

## Greek text

Ἀγαθημερὶς Σ Η (i.e. ζῃ?) Σενπρώνιος

Ἀφροδεισίου ἐκ Κολλυτέων. Νικήτης Κολλυτεύς.

(relief)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Agathemeris SE (i.e. is living?) Sempronius

(daughter) of Aphrodeisios of Kollytos. Niketes of Kollytos.

(relief)
