## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Erastos (163/2) , in the twelfth prytany, of Aiantis , for which

Demetrios son of Xenon of Epikephisia was secretary. Decrees of the Council.

On the twenty-third of Skirophorion, the twenty-third of the prytany.

Council in the Piraeus. Of the presiding committee Zoilos son of Zoilos of Phlya was putting to the vote, and his

fellow presiding committee members. The Council decided. Lykinos son of Nikon of Halimous proposed. Concerning what

the priest of Zeus Soter in the Piraeus reports, and the managers,

about the sacrifices which they have made to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira and

Asklepios and Hygieia and the other gods, having supplied victims

as finely as possible, for good fortune, the Council shall decide to accept the good report
