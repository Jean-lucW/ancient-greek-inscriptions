# List of participants of the first Domitianic <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Emperor Caesar Augustus

Domitian Germanicus Zeus Eleutherios

at Athens, the following came to sacrifice the bull-headed Dodekais:

the herald of Apollo Pythios and priest of Hermes

Patroos of the Kerykes [Philon] son of Philon of Marathon, the religious

official Po[le]m[on son of Philon] of Marathon, the seer

Philemon of Azenia, the [pipe-player] Philomousos of Marathon,

in the priesthood of Apollo Pythios at Athens of Hipparchos son of

[Herodes of Marathon], and at [Delphi] of Euthydamos and Eukleidas.
