# Monument commemorating the foundation and early years of the Asklepieion at Athens

## English translation

**Face-front a**

Telemachos first founded the

sanctuary and the altar to

Asklepios [and to Hygieia],

to the [Asklepiads?]

and [the daughters

of Asklepios?] and [all the other

gods and goddesses?]

[...]

[...] having come up from Zea

at the Great Mysteries

he lodged at the

Eleusinion; and having

sent for assistants (?) from home,

Telemachos brought him here on a

[wagon] in accordance with

[an oracle]; and Hygieia came

with him; and so this sanctuary

was founded all in the

archonship of Astyphilos of

Kydantidai (420/19) . Archeas (419/8) : in

his time the Kerykes disputed

the plot of land and prevented

some things being done.

Antiphon (418/7) : in his time

[...] [Euphemos] (417/6) : in

his time [...]

three lines missing

[...]

established [...] and

constructed [...] Charias (415/4) : in

his time the [enclosure]

from the wooden gate.

Teisandros (414/3) : in his time

the [wooden gates]

and the rest [of the sacred places]

were added to the foundation. Kleokritos (413/2) :

in his time [plantings] were undertaken

and, having adorned it, he established

the entire precinct,

at his own expense. Kallias

of Skambonidai (412/1) : in his

time [...]

[...]

**Face-left b**

11 lines of which only the last three or four letters of each line are preserved (no complete word)
