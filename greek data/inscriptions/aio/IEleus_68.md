# Honorific decree of deme Eleusis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [the demarch?] [...]

[at the Dionysia] in Eleusis [at the]

[tragedies that the deme]

[Eleusis crowns] [...]

[...] [for his excellence and]

[love of honour towards the deme]

[Eleusis, and] he shall have

[a seat of honour and] freedom from taxation in

[the deme] Eleusis for himself

[and his descendants]; and

[the demarch] shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it at

the sanctuary of Demeter.

Crown
