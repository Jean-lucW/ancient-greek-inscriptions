# Dedication by taxiarchs, 356/5 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The taxiarchs dedicated (this) in the archonship of Elpinos (356/5)

having been crowned by the People and the Council

Erechtheis

Eukrates son of Strombichos of Euonymon

Aigeis

Timokrates son of Timochares of Halai

Hippothontis

Timokedes son of Gnathis of Eleusis.
