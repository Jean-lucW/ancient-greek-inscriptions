# Decree of Gargettos honouring Epikydes

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[ [...] the demarch (?)]

[shall inscribe this] decree

on a stone stele and

stand it in the precinct of

Dionysos at Gargettos; and

for inscribing the stele

the treasurer shall allocate

from the common revenues whatever

the cost may be.

The demesmen (crown)

Epikydes

son of Philokydes.
