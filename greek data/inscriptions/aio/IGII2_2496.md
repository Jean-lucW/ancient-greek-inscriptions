# Lease of buildings in Piraeus by [the deme] Kytherros

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune; in the priesthood of Philippides.

On the following terms

Antimachos son of Amphimachos, Pheidostratos

son of Mnesichares, Demaretos son of Leosthenes,

Ktesias son of Ktesiphon, Ktesippos Relief

son of Ktesiphon, Ktesichares son of Ktesiphon,

Ktesias son of Timokrates, Chaireas

son of Mnesichares, the section-members of Kytherros, leased

the workshop in Piraeus and the

dwelling attached to it

and the shed at the dung-heap for all

time to Eukrates son of Exekias of Aphidna for 54 drachmas

each year, free of all taxes, on condition that he pays

30 in Hekatombaion and twenty-

four in Posideon, and carries out the

necessary repairs to the workshop and the dwelling in the first

year; but if he does not pay the rent according to what has been

written or does not carry out the repairs, he shall owe double

and Eukrates shall vacate the workshop without

making an excuse (literally: "saying a word") ; the guarantor that he will do what has been

written within the time specified shall be Exekias of Aphidna;

and the section members of Kytherros shall confirm the lease

for Eukrates and his descendants, and if not, they shall owe

1000 drachmas; and Eukrates shall inscribe this agreement

on a stone stele and stand it by the hero; and if

there should be a property-based tax due or any other charge

of any kind, Eukrates shall pay it according to the valuation of seven minai. Gods.
