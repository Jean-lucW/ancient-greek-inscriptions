# List of ephebes participating in the second Hellenistic <i>Pythais</i> at Delphi, 128/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

God. Good Fortune.

Those who were ephebes in the archonship of Dionysios after Lykiskos in Athens (128/7) , when the priest of

Apollo was Eumelos son of Noumenios of Athens, when the hoplite general was

Theophrastos son of Herakleitos of Acharnai, and in charge of the fleet was Hipparchos son of Timokles

of Piraeus, and in charge of the cavalry was Diokles son of Diokles of Athens, and the tribal cavalry commanders were

Hermon son of Dionysios, but by birth son of Antiphon of Athens, Hagias son of Boulon of Athens, Charikles son of

Theodoros of Athens, Xenokles son of Demetrios of Athens, and (the general) in charge of Piraeus was Pyrrhinos

son of Athenagoras of Kydantidai, (the general) in charge of Eleusis was Epiphanes son of Hippakos of Lamptrai,

the interpreter chosen by the Pythian oracle was Ophelas son of Habron of Bate, and the superintendent was Apollonios

son of Apollonios of Sounion, (the ephebes) having led the Pythais with their teachers,

the trainer Nikon son of Alexis of Berytos, the weapons trainer Sodates son of Soteles

of Soloi, the javelin trainer Nikandros son of Demetrios of Euonymon, the archery trainer Pystilos son of

Nikis of Oe, the secretary Tharrhinos son of Tharrhikos of Lamptrai, the attendant Hieron

son of Herakleides of Anagyrous, the deputy weapons trainer Artemidoros son of Neon

of Tarsos, (the ephebes made) a thank-offering to Apollo.

The Tarantine cavalry commanders were Diogenes son of Aropos (and) Lyson son of Demokrates,

(ephebes) of the Athenians:
