# Dedication by a sculptor and athlete

## English translation

**Part 1**

Sacred votives enhance his glorious reputation among the

Greeks, and this image supplies evidence of his skill;

having won both the horse-race and the competition for art-works

he crowns his sacred Kekropian fatherland.

**Column 1**

at the Ilieia

with a pair of horses

**Column 2**

at the Klaria

in the horse race

**Column 3**

at the Ephesia

in the horse race
