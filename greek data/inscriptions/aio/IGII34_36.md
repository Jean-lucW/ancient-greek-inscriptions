# Dedication by a board of officials of the tribe Aigeis, 313/2 BC ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

The – and their treasurer in the archonship of Theophrastos (313/2?) , having been

crowned by their fellow tribesmen for their excellence and

justice, dedicated (this). Treasurer, Gorgiades son of Mnesikleides of Ikarion ;

–, Androkles son of Andrios of Halai , Philagros

[...] son of Nikokrates of Halai .
