# Athenian dedication at Delphi for Melanopos of Delphi

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Ὁ δῆμ[ος ὁ Ἀθην]αίων ἀνέθ[η]κε[ν τῶί Ἀπόλλωνι]

Μελάνω[π]ον

Ἱέρου

Δελφόν.

Ἐργόφιλος ἐποίησε.

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The People of the Athenians] dedicated [to Apollo]

(this statue of) Melanopos

son of Hieros

of Delphi.

Ergophilos made it.
