# Dedication to the Twelve Gods by the Council of 357/6 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

The Council in the archonship of Agathokles (357/6)

dedicated to the twelve gods, having been crowned

by the Athenian People for its excellence and justice.

**Face-back b**

Pythokritos son of Xenokles

of Koile and Alexandros

son of Theodotos of Pallene

dedicated.
