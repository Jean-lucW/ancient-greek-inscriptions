# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] them [...]

[...] and to obtain any other

benefit that he may be deemed worthy of; and in order that there may also

be a reminder of the gratitude of the People,

the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

[on the acropolis]; and the expense accrued for the

[stele and the inscribing] the treasurer of the

military fund shall defray.

uninscribed

[...] (?)
