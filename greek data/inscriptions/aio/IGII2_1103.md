# Letter from Hadrian banning on-selling

## English translation

[...] measure [...]

and the two obol tax [...] neither [...]

but for the fishermen at Eleusis there a tax-exemption on fish [whenever]

they sell them in the Agora at Eleusis, so that there may be [plenty, and the]

profit from import taxes may reach a great amount, and I want the –

and the on-sellers to stop [...]

or for an indictment of them to be submitted to the herald of the

Areopagos Council, and for him to bring them before the Areopagites, and for them

to evaluate what they (i.e. the indicted men) should suffer or repay. And those who import goods

or who initially buy from them shall sell everything, but, when

yet another buyer of the same goods on-sells them, that increases

the prices. Inscribe this letter on a stele and stand it in Piraeus

in front of the Deigma. Farewell.

uninscribed space

The manager of the city being Tiberius Julius Herodianus of Kollytos.
