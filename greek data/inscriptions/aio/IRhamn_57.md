# Decree of Rhamnous and Athenian residents honouring Sopolis the mercenary leader and his soldiers, late 3rd-early 2nd cent. BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [proposed:? the Rhamnousians]

and the the other citizens

stationed in the fort

shall decide, to praise

the mercenary leader Sopolis

son of Sosistratos of Phokis and crown

him with a gold crown

according to the law, and the

soldiers with him with a

foliage crown, for their discipline

and good will; and the managers

shall inscribe this decree on

a stone stele

and stand it in the sanctuary of

Nemesis.
