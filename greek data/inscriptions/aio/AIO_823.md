## English translation

**Fragment 1**

Gods.

Nikostratos son of Epiteles of Rhamnous proposed: since Epichares when elected

cavalry commander in the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) , after carrying out the cavalry management

well and according to the laws, was crowned both by the Council and the People and by the cavalry; and again

in the archonship of Peithidemos (269/8) when the People elected him general and

assigned him command of the coastal countryside, in carrying out his guarding duties

well and with love of honour he both saved the fort for the People in time of war

and brought in the grain and the arboreal produce for a distance of thirty stades,

with an encampment in the countryside, stationing hidden agents at the lookouts

while he himself took up his post alongside the soldiers, so that the harvest

of the produce might be carried out safely for the farmers; and he [took care of?] the vines as far as he

[controlled] the countryside; and from his own resources he constructed a stoa so that if needed

all might shelter and help might readily be available if necessary; and he has built

a pair of watch-houses and supplied dogs in addition to those already there, donating

their feed himself, so that the guard might be greater; and he has made a headquarters, after constructing

the sanctuary [of Aphrodite?], so that she might be held in honour and be heeded well and in piety by the

[demesmen]; and he brought in 500 medimnoi of wheat and 500 of barley,

himself covering in advance the costs, and he distributed it to the citizens and the soldiers at the

official price; and he arranged on behalf of the existing prisoners

so that in exchange for one hundred and twenty drachmas they were restored to safety through the herald, and

no citizen was taken away and no slaves were liquidated; and he

punished those guiding the raiders into the countryside, after catching and trying them,

being from the city, in a way that they deserved for what they did; and for the soldiers

who arrived from Patroklos to help, he prepared both shelters so that they had sufficient
