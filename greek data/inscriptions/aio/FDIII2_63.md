# List of participants in the fifth Augustan <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

For Good Fortune. In the archonship of Xenagoras at Delphi and of Apolexis

son of Philokrates at Athens, the following held the

bull-first Dodekais: the priest of Apollo Pythios

and Patroos Eukles son of Herodes of Marathon, the interpreter

chosen by the Pythian oracle from among the Eupatridai Polykritos son of Polycharmos of Azenia,

the interpreter elected from among the Eupatridai by the

People Diotimos son of Diodoros of Halai, the herald of the god from the genos

of the Kerykes and priest of Hermes Gorgippos son of Eudemos of Melite,

the hieromnemon Theogenes son of Theogenes but by birth son [of Alexandros]

of Eupyridai, the seer Aristos son of Ammonios of La[mptrai], the pipe-player

Menodoros son of Menodoros of Pambotadai.
