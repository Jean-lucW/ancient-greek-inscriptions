# Abydos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Fragment a**

(This statue of) Emperor

Trajan Hadrian

Olympios

Caesar Augustus (was erected by)

**Fragment b**

the Abydenes

for their own saviour

and founder

through an envoy,

Marcellus Maior.
