# Makedonikos' hymn to Apollo and Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

in cymatium

Makedonikos of Amphipolis

composed this, when the god commanded it.

Hymn the Delian of the good quiver, Zeus’ child, of the silver bow,

with a cheerful spirit, with an auspicious tongue – Ie Paian! –

Place in your hand a suppliant branch of olive and

a glorious shoot [of laurel], Athenian youths – Ie Paian! –

O youths, might a blameless hymn sing of Leto’s son, Hekatos (i.e. Apollo),

famous leader of the Muses [...] – Ie Paian! –

the helper, whom he once begot, the [averter] of diseases and mortal

woe, Asklepios, a cheerful youth – Ie! Oh! Ie Paian! –

On Pelion’s summits, the Centaur taught him, the whole art [...]

that wards off pain from humans, – [Ie Paian!] –

son of Koronis, gentle to men, most venerable spirit, – [Ie Paian!] –

and to him were born youths, Podaleirios and Machaon, for the Greeks [...]

of the lance, – Ie Paian! –

and Iaso, Akeso, and Aigle and Panakeia, (daughters) of Epione – with

very distinguished Hygieia – Ie Paian! –

Greetings, great boon to mortals, most famous spirit – [Ie!] Oh! [Ie Paian!] –

Asklepios, and grant that those hymning your wisdom may flourish forever

in life with most pleasant health, – Ie Paian! –

And may you preserve the Kekropian Attic city, visiting it always, – Ie Paian! –

be kind, blessed one, and draw away loathsome diseases – Ie! Oh! Ie Paian!
