Declaration of the prefect:

Severus (said): The love of honour I also [approve of]

which [he displayed] towards the gods, [and if anyone]

were to dare to disturb any of the consecrated money,

restitution will be made to the Fisc, double [whatever the value was] by the

one who dared to do this, being [guilty] of sacrilege,

and both the hierophant and the dadouch in particular will monitor this

for the purpose that this principal never be despoiled

nor the quantity of the consecrated money reduced by 1 denarius,

it being clear that they have no immunity,

if they should overlook any [misuse] of this (money).

Those who receive this distribution [although not] of Councillor

rank are: Xenion himself: double; [priest] of Daphneph(oros): single (share)
