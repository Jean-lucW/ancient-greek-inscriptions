# Dedication to Apollo at Delphi by Athenian cleruchs on Samos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods, fortune.

In the archonship

of Ktesikles at Athens (334/3)

of Neokles

on Samos,

the Athenian People

on Samos dedicated

a golden crown bestowed

as a prize of valour

to Apollo,

weight

of the gold:

twenty Darics,

a half Daric, a

half obol [...]
