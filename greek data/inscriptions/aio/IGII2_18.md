## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

In the archonship of Euboulides (394/3) . In the prytany of

Pandionis , being the sixth,

for which Platon son of Nikochares of Phlya was

secretary.

The Council decided. Kinesias proposed: concerning what

Androsthenes says, to praise Dionysios the

ruler of Sicily and Leptines the brother

of Dionysios and Thearides the brother

of Dionysios and Polyxenos the [brother-in-law

of Dionysios] [...]
