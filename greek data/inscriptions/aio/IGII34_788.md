# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

(dedicated this statue of) Aristodemos son of Menexenos

of Skambonidai (?)

to Asklepios and Hygieia,

as a thank-offering,

in the priesthood of Phanokles of Kephisia.
