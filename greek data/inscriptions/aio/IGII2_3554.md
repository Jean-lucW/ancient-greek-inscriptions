# Honours for Tertia, arrhephoros

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council and the People (dedicated this statue of)

Tertia daughter of Lucius [...]

having been arrhephoros

to Athena Polias

in the priesthood of Stratokleia.

in crown

The Council

[and the People] (honoured her)

having been

arrhephoros.

in crown

The Council

and the People (honoured her)

having been initiated

from the hearth.

in crown

The Council

and the People (honoured her)

having been basket-bearer

at the

Eleusinia

in crown

(and) having been

basket-bearer

at the

Epidauria.
