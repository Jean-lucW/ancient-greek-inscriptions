# Payments from the treasury of Athena, 411/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Athenians expended in

the archonship of [Mnasil]ochos (first two months of 411/0)

One line uninscribed

The treasurers of the sacred monies

of Athena, Asopodoros

of Kydathenaion

and his colleagues,

for whom Euandros

son of Erithalion of Euonymon

was secretary,

handed over to the Greek treasurers

Antisthenes

of Hermos and his

colleagues, the Council

having voted,

on the twenty-second

of Hekatombaion, from the

monies of Athena

Polias, 27 tal.

≥ 2,074 dr. 4 ob.;

of Athena Nike from

[...] ≥ 640 dr.

[...]
