# Citizenship decree for Straton son of Timon

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

continually [...]

which he has towards [...] , and several of the citizens

have testified for him about this, and it is customary

for the People to give worthy thanks to those who display

love of honour towards it, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People that it seems good to the Council

to praise Straton son of Timon of (?) and crown

him with a gold crown in accordance with the law for his [excellence] and

love of honour towards the People of the Athenians, and to announce

this crown both at the new tragedies of the City Dionysia;

and the generals shall take care of the announcement;

and he shall also be granted citizenship, having been scrutinised

in the court in accordance with the law; and the court presidents

when they have filled the court with five hundred and one

jurors, shall introduce his scrutiny, even if he is not

present; and he shall be enrolled [...]

[...] and it shall be permitted to him also in the future [...]

[...] [to the] Council and People [...]

[...] good, and be honoured [...] ;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it [...]
