# Citizenship for Python

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

trace

[...] within

his power; [in order therefore that there may be incentive among everyone]

to make themselves useful, knowing

that they will be honoured by the People, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside at the next Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the

Council to praise Python son of Demeas of –

and crown him with a gold crown according to

the law for his good will and love of honour for

the Council and People; and he shall be Athenian,

he and his descendants, and it shall be possible for him

to enrol in the tribe and deme and phratry that

he wishes; and the secretary of the People shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and [for the inscription of the]

[stele the board of administrators shall allocate the]

[expenditure accrued ...]
