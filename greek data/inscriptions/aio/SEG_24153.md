# Decree of Teithras honouring Euthippos (the demarch)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Text below follows SEG 24.152

Blepyros proposed: since Euthippos managed

the statue well and justly and made it bigger

than agreed and spent additional money on it,

the Teithrasians shall resolve, to praise Euthippos and crown

him with a foliage crown and remit to him a mina

of money from the deposited money; and to inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it

in the Koreion.
