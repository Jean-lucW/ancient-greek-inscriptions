# Dedication of two crowns in the City Eleusinion by the priestess Lysistrate

## English translation

**Part 1**

Attendant of your secret ceremony, Mistress Deo,

and your daughter’s,

Lysistrate set up this pleasing gift, two crowns, an adornment for your porch, and she does not

spare her possessions, but to the gods she is unstinting to the extent of her ability.
