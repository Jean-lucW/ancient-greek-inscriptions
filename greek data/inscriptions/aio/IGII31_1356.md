# Proxeny decree for Eukleidas of Rhodes

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] at the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People that it seems good

to the Council to praise Eukleidas son of Kleombrotos of Rhodes

and crown him with a foliage crown for his good will

and his love of honour towards the People of the Athenians;

and he shall be proxenos and benefactor of the People;

and he shall be given right of ownership of land and a house, which he has requested

in accordance with the law; and it shall be permitted to Eukleidas also in the

future, on maintaining the same preference, to obtain

other benefits from the People, whatever he may be deemed worthy of;

and in order that there may be a reminder of what has been decreed,

the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the agora;

and for the inscribing and the setting up of the stele

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the expense

accrued.

The Council,

the People (crown)

Eukleidas

son of Kleombrotos

of Rhodes.
