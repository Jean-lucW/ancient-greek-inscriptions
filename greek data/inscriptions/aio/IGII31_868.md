# Citizenship for Philokles, king of the Sidonians

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] so that it is clear to all that the

People honour those who benefit it, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside at the next Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise

Philokles king of the Sidonians and to crown

him with a gold crown according to the law for the good will

which he continues to have for the People; and he shall be

Athenian, and his descendants, and shall enrol in the tribe and

deme and phratry that he wishes; and the prytany

shall put the vote about him at the next Assembly [...]
