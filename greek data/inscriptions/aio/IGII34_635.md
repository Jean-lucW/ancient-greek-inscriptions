# Dedication by the "launderers"

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Having made a vow, the launderers dedicated this to the Nymphs and all the gods.

Zoagoras son of Zokypros, Zokypros son of Zoagoras, Thallos, Leuke,

Sokrates son of Polykrates, Apollophanes son of Euporion, Sosistratos,

Manes, Myrrhine, Sosias, Sosigenes, Midas.
