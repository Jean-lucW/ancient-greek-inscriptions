# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...] he shall be proxenos and benefactor

of the Athenian People, both himself

and his descendants [...]

[...] if he has any need [...]

he shall have right of access to the Council

and the People ; and

should he need anything

from the People, both the Council

in office at any time and the prytany

and the generals shall take care of him; and the

prytany secretary

shall inscribe the proxeny for him on a stone

stele and stand it on the acropolis,

so that others may also know that

the People knows how to give thanks

to its benefactors

[...?]
