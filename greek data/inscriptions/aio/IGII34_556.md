# Building dedication and choregic dedication of Dionysodoros for Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Inscription A

[To Asklepios] and Hygieia and Augustus Caesar ⟦ erasure ⟧

⟦ erasure ⟧, when the archon and priest of Drusus the Consul was Polycharmos, son of Polykritos of Azenia.

[when the Priest for] life was Zenon

son of Leukios of Rhamnous.

Inscription B

Column 1

Dionysodoros was archon, when Dexikles

took me as the prize for victory with a chorus of youths.

Column 1

Archon Dionysodoros dedicated me,

glory of all Eukarpos' comic, tragic, and choral skill,

the dithyrambic tripod, to Asklepios.
