# Lampadedromia dedication, 101/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

-oros son of Xanthias of Themakos, having been elected

general in charge of Rhamnous and the coastal countryside

in the year of the archonship of Medeios (101/0) , dedicated (this) to Zeus Soter

and Athena Soteira and Themis and Nemesis.
