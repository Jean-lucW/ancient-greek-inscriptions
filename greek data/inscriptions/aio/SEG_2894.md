# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Oineis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Since the prytaneis] of Oineis and those with perpetual dining rights in the archonship of -

declare to the Council that the treasurer, whom [they chose]

[from among themselves, Iophon] of Phyle, has made all the sacrifices

[from his own resources and] has taken care of everything else with love of goodness, and

for this they call on the Council to allow them

to crown him with a foliage crown, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide to praise the treasurer of the prytaneis,

[Iophon] of Phyle, and to crown him with a foliage

[crown, with which it is traditional] to crown good

men, and the secretary of the prytany shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and dedicate

it in the Council chamber.
