# Choregic dedication of Quintus Vibius Crispus for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

When Quintus Vibius Crispus of [Marathon was archon and sponsor],

Asklepiades son of Hygeinos of [Anaphlystos was competition-director, Deiogenes [...] ]

was director, Philode[mos] was pipe-player. [ [...] Aiantis was victorious in the men's competition].

[Crispus], three-time consul of Rome, [and as archon was also sponsor],

When Asklepiades [was competition-director for Dionysos],

Deiogenes directed [a chorus of young men, with which] Aiantis

[was victorious], and [Philodemos set a rhythm] for the flutes.
