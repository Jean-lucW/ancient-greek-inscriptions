# Honours for Philetairos, brother of king Eumenes II

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Sonikos (175/4) , in the tenth prytany, of Leontis ,

for which Pausanias son of Bioteles of Perithoidai

was secretary. On the tenth of Mounychion, the eighteenth

of the prytany. Principal Assembly in

the theatre. Of the presiding committee Ergosthenes

son of Euthykles of Probalinthos was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and the People decided.

Archias son of Androkleides of Phegaia proposed: since

Philetairos, the brother of king Eumenes, having

increased the good will which he inherited towards the People
