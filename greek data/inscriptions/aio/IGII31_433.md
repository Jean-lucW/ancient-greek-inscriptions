Sokles and the city shall have the profit for twenty-five

years; and the city shall have the profit in the one

year and Sokles in the other year

in alternation, until the twenty-five years

have elapsed for both; and in the first year

the city shall have it; and each shall bring in the [proceeds]

at their own expense; and the gathering shall be permitted

for Sokles [and the city] from everywhere

that there may be proceeds; and Sokles

shall complete the gathering of the proceeds

by the end of each archon year; and Sokles

shall have the same means of gathering, regarding

sale and valuation and exaction

of property, as there would be

for the city; and when Sokles has started

the working, it shall not be possible

for any [Athenian any longer?] to propose or put to the vote

that Sokles should leave off the working,

or to hinder the working, until

the profit has been reaped [for the prescribed time];
