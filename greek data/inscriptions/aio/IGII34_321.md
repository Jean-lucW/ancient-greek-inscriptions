# Dedication by the Piraeus generals to Hermes Hegemonios, 97/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The generals in charge of Piraeus in

the archonship of Herakleitos (97/6) , having been crowned

by the Council and the People, Eupolemos

son of Zoilos of Paiania, Zenon son of Meniskos

of Erchia, Aristagoras son of Troilos of Piraeus,

dedicated (this) to Hermes Hegemonios.
