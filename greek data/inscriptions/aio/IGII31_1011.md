# Public subscription for the preservation of the city and the protection of the countryside

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Treasurer of the Military Fund

Eurykleides son of Mikion of Kephisia.

In the archonship of Diomedon (248/7) , in the tenth prytany, of [[Demetrias]] ,

for which Phoryskides son of Aristomenes of Leukonoion was secretary.

On the intercalated old and new day of Elaphebolion, the second

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee Kallistratos son of Telesinos

of Erchia was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The People decided.

Theophemos son of Timokles of Marathon proposed: in order that, with the money

supplied, the treasurer can allocate what is needed so

that for the time remaining in the year the harvest from the land may be gathered

in safety, for good fortune, the Council shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted to preside for the coming Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion of the Council that

it seems good to the Council that those citizens and others who reside in the city

who wish to make a contribution for the preservation

of the city and the protection of the countryside should register with the People

or with the Council or through the generals by Mounichion;

that no-one be allowed to contribute more than 200 drachmas

or less than 50; and the contributors shall be praised and honoured,

both collectively and individually, by the People according to what each

of them deserves; and the secretary of the People shall inscribe this

decree and the names of the contributors on a stone stele

and stand it in the Agora in order that the love of honour

of those willing to be benefactors of the People may be seen by all; and

the administrator shall allocate the expenditure accrued

for the stele and the inscription of the names; and this decree, since

it concerns the supply of military funds, shall be entirely for the

protection of the countryside.

The following contributed to the preservation of the city

and the protection of the countryside according to the

decree of the People:
