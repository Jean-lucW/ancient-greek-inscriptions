# Honours for envoys from Priene sent to the Panathenaia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Assembly in the theatre. Of the presiding committee [...] was putting to

the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members [...]

[...] proposed: since the Prienians being friends [and kinsmen of the Athenian]

[People] from ancient times, [...]

[call to mind?] by all means both the other benefactions of the People

towards them, and especially that

the Athenians -settled (?) them after [...]

and now wishing to join in enlarging the honours done to the gods

by the People, have sent [ambassadors]

to the Panathenaia, who [...] the [...]

[...] which there was for Athena Archegetis [and [...] ]

of the city; and those who were present [...]

[...] took care of this also splendidly [...]

and of the Great [Panathenaia] [...]

[to announce] the crown [at the City] Dionysia at the competition

for new tragedies, and the generals shall take care of

the announcement, and to praise also the ambassadors [...]
