# Dedication to Apollo Hypo Makrais

## English translation

**Part 1**

In myrtle crown:

In the archonship

of Alkibiades

son of

Leosthenes

Timo-os son of Daphnos

of -, having been

court president, (dedicated this)

to Apollo Below the

[Heights].
