# Honours for Argaios the doctor

## English translation

**Part 1**

If an old human mind once found anything in a skill,

I say that you perceived all with your ingenious intellect

by judging doctors’ wise teachings and plucking

the superlative material from books with the eye of the soul,

and that you, Argaios, provided the liquid of the refreshing vine

to mortals, thereby warding off paralytic pains.

On account of this, your skill’s lovely glory will never perish,

but will be more brilliant than the stars in heaven.
