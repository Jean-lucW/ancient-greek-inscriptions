# Citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] the People [of the Athenians, and to announce] this [crown]

[at the tragedies of the City Dionysia in the new] competition;

[the generals shall take care of this announcement];

and he shall be granted [citizenship, having been scrutinised in the]

court; [and the court presidents, when they have filled the courts]

[with five hundred and] one [jurors, shall introduce the] scrutiny [of the

enrolment of citizens; and he shall be enrolled in the tribe and] deme

[and phratry that he wishes] [...]
