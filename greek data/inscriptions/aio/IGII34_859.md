# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia by Claudia Agrippina

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Asklepios and Hygieia

as a thank-offering

Claudia Agrippina,

having been saved by their

good will (dedicated this),

in the priesthood for life

of Flavius
