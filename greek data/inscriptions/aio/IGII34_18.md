# Dedication at Delphi by the religious officials who led the Pythais

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People of the Athenians dedicated (this) to Apollo.

The religious officials who led the Pythais:

**Column 1**

Phanodemos son of Diyllos

Boethos son of Nausinikos

Lykourgos son of Lykophron

Demades son of Demeas

Klearchos son of Nausikles

**Column 2**

Glauketes son of Glaukos

Neoptolemos son of Antikles

Kleochares son of Glauketes

Hippokrates son of Aristokrates

Nikeratos son of Nikias .
