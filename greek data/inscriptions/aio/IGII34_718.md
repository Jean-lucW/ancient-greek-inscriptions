# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia by Hegemachos

## English translation

**Part 1**

On abacus

Hegemachos

son of Krataimenes

of Lamptrai

(dedicated this) to Asklepios,

having suffered many terrible things,

having seen a vision, having been saved, he dedicated (this)

in the priesthood of Theophilos,

On shaft

to Asklepios (and) Hygieia.

Eurymedon son of Hegemachos (dedicated it).
