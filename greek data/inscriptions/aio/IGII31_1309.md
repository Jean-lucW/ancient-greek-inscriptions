# Honours for Antiochos (later Antiochos IV?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Philon (178/7) , who came after Menedemos (179/8) , on the

ninth of Pyanopsion, the seventeenth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly in the theatre.

The Council and the People decided.

Aristogeiton son of Poly- of - proposed: since

Antiochos, [son?] of king [Antiochos and brother of?]

[king] Seleukos, having inherited from his forefathers

his good will towards the People,

being a good [man?] in [every way?] [...]

[not only?] [...] [from the beginning?]

[...] but also [...]
