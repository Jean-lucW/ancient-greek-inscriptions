# Decree of tribe Hippothontis honouring Metagenes of Eroiadai

## English translation

**Part 1**

The tribe Hippothontis decided,

Polynikos son of Phokiades of Oion

proposed: since Metagenes

son of Eukleides of Eroiadai well and

justly and incorruptibly

judged that the tribe was victorious at the Thargelia,

concerning [...]
