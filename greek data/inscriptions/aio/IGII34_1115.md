# Dedication to Isis and other Egyptian gods

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Isis, to Sarapis

to Anoubis, to Harpokrates,

Megallis daughter

of Magas of Marathon

on behalf of the daughter

of Demarion and

her sons. On

command, in the

priesthood of Menandros

son of Artemon

of Alopeke, when the

keyholder was

Asopokles of Phlya,

the temple attendant

was Sosikrates of Laodikeia,

the interpreter of

dreams was Dionysos

of Antioch.
