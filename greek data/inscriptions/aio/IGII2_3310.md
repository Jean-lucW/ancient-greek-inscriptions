# Keramos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) the God Hadrian,

their personal benefactor,

(was erected by) the Council and People of the Keramietans

through the works-chairmen and envoys,

Hieron son of Myonides Hermodoros

and Hermodoros son of Apollonides

and Euandros son of Epaphrodeitos,

in the priesthood of Statius Quadratus
