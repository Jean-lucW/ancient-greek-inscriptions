# Dedication by ephebic gymnasiarch, 97/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] as gymnasiarch and overseer

of the torch-race of the ephebes [on Delos]

in the year that Herakleitos

was archon (97/6 BC) after Argeios, and

in the trainership of Staseas son of

Philokles and Philokles son of

Staseas of Kolonai, (dedicated this)

to Hermes, to Herakles,

to Apollo.
