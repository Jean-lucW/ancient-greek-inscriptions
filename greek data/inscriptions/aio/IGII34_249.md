# Dedication by cavalry commanders

## English translation

**Part 1**

The cavalry commanders and commanders of the tribal cavalry contingents dedicated this.

Cavalry commanders:

Antikrates son of Sokrates of Hermos

Patrokles son of Hierokles of Philaidai

Uninscribed line
