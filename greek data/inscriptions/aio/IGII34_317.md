# Lampadedromia dedication

## English translation

**Part 1**

On capital

Xanthippos son of Phantokritos of Sounion,

twice elected general in charge of Rhamnous and the

coastal countryside, dedicated this to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira

and Themis and Nemesis.

On pillar

From the citizens (the victor in the) [Diogeneia]:

Eugeiton son of Demetrios of Eitea.

From the soldiers (the victor in the) Diogeneia:

Phalakrion son of Ktesios of Lamptrai.

From the paroikoi (the victor in the) Ptolemaia:

Sokles son of Lades of Miletos.
