# Funerary Monument of Phanostrate, Midwife and Doctor

## Greek text

**Part 1**

At top of stele

Φανοσ[τράτη patronymic? ]

Μελιτέως.

Labelling figures in relief

Ἀντιφίλη. Φανοστράτη.

Relief

Under relief panel

μαῖα καὶ ἰατρὸς Φανοστράτη ἐνθάδε κεῖται,

[ο]ὐθενὶ λυπηρά, πᾶσιν δὲ θανοῦσα ποθεινή.

## English translation

**Part 1**

At top of stele

Phanostrate [daughter? of –]

of Melite

Labelling figures in relief

Antiphile Phanostrate

Relief

Under relief panel

Midwife and doctor Phanostrate lies here,

she caused pain to no-one and, having died, is missed by all.
