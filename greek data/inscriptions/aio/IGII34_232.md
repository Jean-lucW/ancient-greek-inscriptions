# Dedication to Dionysos (Eleusis)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Moirokles son of Euthydemos

Antitheos son of Kallikles

Timokedes son of Timasios

Antiphanes son of Euxenides

Uninscribed space

dedicated (this) to Dionysos,

having been crowned by the demesmen.
