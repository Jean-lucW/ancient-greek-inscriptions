# Dedication by ephebic gymnasiarch, 165/6 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Sextus (165/6 AD) ,

in the superintendentship

of Herennius Cornelius

of Azenia.

Flavius Proculus,

having been gymnasiarch

in Anthesterion

dedicated the torch,

in the [30th year of the?] trainership

of Abaskantos

son of Eumolpos of

Kephisia.
