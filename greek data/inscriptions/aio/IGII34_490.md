# Choregic dedication of Philomelos for the Thargelia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Front

Philomelos son of Philippides

of Paiania, serving as sponsor,

was victorious for Pandionis (and)

Akamantis in the boys' competition,

⟦Eukles⟧ was director,

Alexippos was pipe-player.

**Part 2**

Rear

Philomelos son of Philippides

of Paiania, serving as sponsor,

was victorious for Pandionis (and)

Akamantis in the boys' competition,

Alexippos was pipe-player,

Eukles was director.
