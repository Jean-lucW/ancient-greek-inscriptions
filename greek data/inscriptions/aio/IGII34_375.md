# Ephebic dedication (Sylleia)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Zenion son of Zenion [of Marathon]

having been victorious in the torch race at the Sylleia

(dedicated this) to Hermes, in the trainership

of Philokles of Kolonai

and the assistant-trainership of -

of Alexandria.
