# Dedications to Asklepios

## English translation

**Face a**

Inscription I (early iii BC)

To Asklepios,

Apollodoros

son of Aristomenes

of Sypalettos (and)

Lysandrides

son of Lysanias

of Pelekes

dedicated this.

uninscribed space

Inscription II (early iii BC)

Kallias

son of Kallias

of Euonymon

dedicated this

to Asklepios.

uninscribed space

Inscription III (early or mid-iii BC)

in the priesthood of

[...]

of Phaleron

**Face b**

Inscription IV (iii-ii BC)

Apollonios (dedicated this).

**Face c**

Inscription V (i AD)

The god having commanded it,

Menandros the servant (dedicated this).
