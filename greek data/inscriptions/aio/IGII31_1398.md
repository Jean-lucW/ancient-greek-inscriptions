# Honours for the prytany of Attalis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] report to the Council

that the treasurer, whom they chose from among themselves, -krates of

Sounion, has made all the proper sacrifices

in the prytany on behalf of the Council and

the People, and has taken care of everything else

well and with love of honour, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide, to praise the treasurer -krates

of Sounion and crown him with a foliage crown, and also to

praise the secretary, - of Atene, and

the priest of the eponymous, - of -, and

the secretary of the Council and the People, Demetrios

of - and the under-secretary, Hegelochos

of - and the herald of the Council and the People,

[Philokles of Trinemeia] and the pipe-player, Kallikrates

of Thorikos and the treasurer of the Council, -mon

of -, and crown each of them with a foliage

crown; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree
