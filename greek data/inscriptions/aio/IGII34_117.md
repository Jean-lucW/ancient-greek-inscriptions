# Dedication by a prytany treasurer, 55/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

having been treasurer of the prytany of [ (name of a tribe) ]

in the year in which Aristoxenos was

archon (55/4) , (dedicated this) on behalf of himself and of his tribe

to Zeus Boulaios and Hestia Boulaia.
