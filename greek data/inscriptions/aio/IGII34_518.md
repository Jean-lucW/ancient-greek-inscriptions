# Dedication by the competition-director, [Androkles?] of Sphettos, 307/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People were the sponsor in the archonship of Anaxikrates (307/6 BC)

Competition-director [Androkles?] son of Xeinis of Sphettos

Victorious poet for tragedies Phanostratos son of Herakleides of Halikarnassos

Victorious actor for tragedies Hieromnemon son of Euanorides of Kydathenaion

Victorious poet for comedies Philemon son of Damon of Diomeia

Victorious actor for comedies Kallippos son of Kallias of Sounion.
