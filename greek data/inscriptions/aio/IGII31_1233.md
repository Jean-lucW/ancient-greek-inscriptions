# Honours for a prytany

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] son of -boulos

of - proposed: concerning what the prytany of -

report about the sacrifices

which they made before the Assemblies to Apollo

Prostaterios and Artemis Boulaia

and the other gods to whom it is traditional, for good

fortune, [the People shall decide] [...]

[...]

[...] and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the prytanikon;

and for [inscribing?] and setting up the stele

the administrator shall allocate the expenditure

accrued.
