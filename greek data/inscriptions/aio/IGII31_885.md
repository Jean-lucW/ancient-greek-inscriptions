# Honours for the cavalry commander Komeas

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of Telokles (280/79) , in the] tenth [prytany, of Akamantis ,

for which - son of - of - was] secretary. [On the nineteenth of]

Elaphebolion, [the twentieth of the prytany]. Principal Assembly.

[Of the presiding committee Pyrgion son of Agatharchos] of Lamptrai [was putting to the vote and his

fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. Philippos son of] Astygenes of Thymaitadai

[proposed: since the Athenians living on Lemnos declare

that Komeas], the cavalry commander [to Lemnos elected by the People],

[has taken care of Lemnos well and with love of honour] according to the

responsibilities of [his office, and declare that he continues on all] occasions

[to show foresight, so that the cities might be in harmony with]

one another [and the citizens amongst themselves and live in peace] both in city

[and country] [...]
