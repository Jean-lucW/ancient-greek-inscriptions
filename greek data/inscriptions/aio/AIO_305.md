**Part 1**

Decree 1 (411?)

[...]

[...]

-ates of Ikarion [was secretary?].

[...] was chairman [...]

[...] and with him p- [...]

[...] of Xypete, Diop- of -, -

[...] of Kephale, Kal- of -.

Hippomenes proposed: for Pythophanes of

-stos, since he is a proxenos and

benefactor of the Athenians and does what good he can

for the city of Athens and that of

-stos, the secretary of the Council now in office

shall inscribe the decree [previously voted]

on a stone stele

and set it down on the acropolis; and as regards

the money belonging to Pythophanes in Athens

or in any other place which the Athenians [rule], and

concerning the ship that he talks about and the

[money?] no-one shall do him wrong; and he shall have

[immunity from seizure] for himself and his money both

coming in and going out. Let this be

decreed, wherever the Athenians [rule], for the entire

household of Pythophanes in the same way; and the generals in

office at any time and the Council

in office at any time shall take care that this comes about;

and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree

on the same stele.
