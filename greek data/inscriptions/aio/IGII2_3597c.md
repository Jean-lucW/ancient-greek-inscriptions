# Statue base for Ti. Claudius Attikos of Marathon

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) the high priest of the

Augusti Tiberius Claudius

Attikos Herodes

of Marathon,

the tribe of Kekropis

dedicated

on account of his goodwill and

beneficence towards

his fatherland.
