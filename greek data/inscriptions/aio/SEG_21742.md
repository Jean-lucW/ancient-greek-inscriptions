# Honours for Tiberius Claudius Novius (47/8 or 51/2 AD)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos Council and the

Council of the Six Hundred

and the People (erected this statue of) Tiberius Claudius

Novius son of Phileinos,

the hoplite general for the

4th time (47/8 or 51/2 AD) and priest of Delian

Apollo for life and competition-director

of the Great Panathenaia

Augusta and Caesarea

Augusta and high priest of Antonia

Augusta, lover of Caesar and

lover of the fatherland, on account of his excellence,

uninscribed space

when the priestess was Junia Megiste

daughter of Zenon of Sounion.

Epagathos son of Aristodemos

of Thria made it.
