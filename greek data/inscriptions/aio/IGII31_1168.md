# Honours for the prytany of Leontis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1

In the archonship of Archelaos (211/0) , in the third prytany,

of Leontis , for which Moschos son of Moschion of Ankyle was secretary.

On the - of Boedromion, the twentieth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Kallistratos son of Kalli- of -

was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members.

The People decided.

Ekphantos [son of Euphanes of Thria] proposed: since the prytany of Leontis

made the sacrifices before the Assemblies to Apollo

Prostaterios and Artemis Boulaia and the other gods to whom it is

traditional, and managed the convening of the Council and the

Assembly, [and everything else which the laws prescribe for them], for good fortune,

the People shall decide, to accept the good things that occurred

in the sacrifices which they made for the health and preservation of the Council

and the People and the women and children, and to praise

the prytany of Leontis and crown it with a gold crown according to

the law for its piety towards the gods and its love of honour

towards the Athenian Council and People; and the prytany

secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it in the prytanikon; and for inscribing the stele

the administrator shall allocate the

expenditure accrued.

**Part 2**

Decree 2

In the archonship of Archelaos (211/0) , in the fourth prytany, of Aiantis ,

for which Moschos son of Moschion of Ankyle was secretary. On the twenty-fourth of

Boedromion, the third of the prytany. Council in the Council chamber, [and from?] the Council chamber in the Eleusinion.

Of the presiding committee Lysikles son of Apollodoros of Kephale was putting to the vote,

and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council decided.

Ekphantos son of Euphanes of Thria proposed: since the prytany of Leontis ,

having praised and crowned the treasurer,

whom they chose from among themselves, Patrokles of Sounion,

and the secretary, Apollophanes of Kettos, report to the Council that they performed

all the proper sacrifices in the prytany on behalf of the Council

and the People, and managed everything else

well and with love of honour, for good fortune, the Council shall decide, to praise

the treasurer, Patrokles son of Kalli- of Sounion, and the secretary,

Apollophanes son of Apollophanes of Kettos, and the treasurer

of the Council, Ekphantos of Thria, and the priest of the eponymous,

Euboulides of Potamos, and the herald of the Council and People,

Eukles of Trinemeia, and the secretary of the Council and the People,

[Ep- son of - of -], and the undersecretary, Lanomos

of Berenikidai; [and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree]

[on a stone stele and stand it in the prytanikon];

and the administrator [shall allocate]

[for and inscribing the stele the expenditure accrued].
