# Choregic dedication of Chares for the Thargelia, 344/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Chares son of Theochares of Angele, serving as sponsor, was victorious,

for Pandionis (and) Akamantis in the boys' competition,

Satyros of Sikyon was pipe-player,

Epikouros of Sikyon was director,

Lykiskos (344/3) was archon.
