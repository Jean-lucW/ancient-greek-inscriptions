# Grave stele of Asklepiodoros and Epikydes of Olynthos

## Greek text

Ἀσσκληπιόδωρος

Θράσωνος Ὀλύνθιος.

Ἐπικύδης Ἀσκληπιοδώρου

Ὀλύνθιος.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Asklepiodoros

(son) of Thrason of Olynthos.

Epikydes (son) of Asklepiodoros

of Olynthos.
