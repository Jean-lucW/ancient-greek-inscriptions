## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Nikostratos (295/4) , in

the ninth prytany, of Demetrias ,

for which Dorotheos son of Aristogenes

of Phaleron was secretary.

On the ninth of Elaphebolion,

the twenty-fifth of

the prytany. Principal

Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Antimachos son of A- of Acharnai was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members
