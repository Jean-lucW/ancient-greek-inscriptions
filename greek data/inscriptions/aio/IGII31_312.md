# Proxeny for Phokinos, Nikandros and Dexi-

## English translation

**Part 1**

Lower part of large A.

Proxeny for Phokinos, Nikandros and Dexi-.

Relief

In the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39) , in the ninth

prytany, of Hippothontis , for which Aspetos son of Demostratos of Kytherros

was secretary. On the eleventh of the prytany. Of the presiding committee

Androkles of Hagnous was putting to the vote. The People

decided. [Demosthene]s son of Demosthenes of Paiania proposed:

[...] People [...]

[...] allies [...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...] they shall be proxenoi of the Athenian People,

themselves and their descendants; and

the Council and the generals shall take care of them so that they come to no harm;

and the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this proxeny

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele the treasurer of the People shall give

twenty drachmas from the People’s fund for

expenditure on decrees; and to invite them to

hospitality in the city hall tomorrow.
