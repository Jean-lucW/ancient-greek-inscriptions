# Choregic dedication of Phrynaios for the Thargelia, 352/1 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Phrynaios son of Phrynichos

of Hybadai, serving as sponsor, was victorious,

for Leontis (and) Aigeis in the boys' competition,

Kommes of Thebes was pipe-player,

Korinos of Opous was director,

Aristodemos (352/1) was archon.
