# Honours for grain purchasers (sitonai)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] for good fortune, the Council shall decide: [that the presiding

committee allotted to preside in] the People at the coming

Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise the

grain purchasers who served in the archonship of Olbios (275/4) and to crown

each of them with a gold crown according to the law for their love of honour

for the People; and they shall have front row seats at

all the competitions that the city puts on, and the director of works in

office at the time shall arrange the seating for them; and to praise

their secretary Chairestratos and crown him with a foliage

crown; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

and the names of the grain purchasers and their secretary on a stone stele

and stand it [...] where [...]

is; and for the making of the stele and the inscribing

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the expenditure accrued.
