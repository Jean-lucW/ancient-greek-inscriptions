# Honours for Claudius (47/8 or 51/2 AD)

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Tiberius Claudius Caesar Augustus

Germanicus Emperor, saviour

of the universe (was erected by) the Council of the Areopagos

and the Council of the Six Hundred and the People,

the hoplite general for the 4th time (47/8 or 51/2 AD) being

[Tiberius] Claudius Novius of Oion.
