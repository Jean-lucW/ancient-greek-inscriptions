# Private Dedication to the Hero Amphilochos (?)

## English translation

[ [...] Logos son of Philokl]es

of Piraeus and Sympherousa daughter of

Hermeias of Marathon and their sons

Herodikos and Olympiodoros and Demetrios,

Hermeias priest for life (dedicated this).
