## English translation

**Part 1**

No. 1

Decree 1 (Soldiers of Kekropis)

[...] proposed: the soldiers shall decide,

[since the taxiarch of the Kekropid] tribe Boularchos son of Aristoboulos

[of Phlya was a good man] concerning the soldiers, to praise

[Boularchos and crown him with a] gold crown of five hundred

[drachmas for his manly virtue and] love of honour towards the soldiers

[...] to obtain from the tribesmen [...]

Decree 2 (Kekropis)

[...] of - proposed: since [Boularchos] [...]

[...] concerning the tribe [...]

[...] the tribesmen shall decide to praise

[Boularchos son of Aristoboulos of Phl]ya and crown him with a [gold] crown

[of - drachmas] for his manly virtue and love of honour,

[...] and the secretary [of the tribe shall - ?] this decree [...]

[...] inscribe it on the dedication as well as

[the decree of the soldiers?].

**Column 1**

[ In crown

The soldiers].

[ In crown

The People?].

**Column 2**

In crown

The Council.

In crown

The tribesmen.

**Part 2**

No. 2

The men of Kekropis who served on campaign in the archonship of Lysimachides (339/8) ,

and their taxiarch, Boularchos son of Aristoboulos of Phlya, to Athena.
