# Honours for Gaius Caesar

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People [of the Athenians (dedicated this statue of) Gaius]

Caesar, [their own]

benefactor [and – and]

saviour [to Apollo?],

[when the priest of] Apollo [for]

[life was Pammenes] son of [Zenon of Marathon].
