# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and stand it

on the acropolis; and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer of the People shall give

20 drachmas from the People’s

fund for expenditure on decrees;

and to praise Arch- and invite

him to hospitality in the city hall

tomorrow.
