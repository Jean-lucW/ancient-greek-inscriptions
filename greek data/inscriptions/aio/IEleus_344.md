# Honours for Polycharmos of Marathon

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) the high priest of Tiberius

Caesar Augustus

and priest of Patroos

Apollo

Polycharmos son of Eukles

of Marathon (was erected by)

Himertos son of Polycharmos

of Marathon, for his own

benefactor,

uninscribed space

when the priestess was Kleo

daughter of Eukles

of Phlya.
