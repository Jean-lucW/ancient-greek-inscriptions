## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods

On these terms the deme of the Prasieis leased

the Theodoreion estate

to Kirrias son of Poseidippos

of Prasiai every year free of tax

and not liable to assessment for the property-based tax

or any other tax for

all time, to himself and his descendants

while they pay the rent; and Kirrias and his

descendants shall pay the rent

to the Prasieis, half

in the month of Metageitnion

[on the sixth?], half

in the month of [Mounichion?]; and it shall not

be possible for the Prasieis

to deprive Kirrias

of the lease while he pays

[] each year, or [the Prasieis?]

[shall be liable] ...
