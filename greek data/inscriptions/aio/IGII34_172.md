# Dedication to Apollo Hypo Makrais

## English translation

**Part 1**

In myrtle crown:

Pole-

march

In label:

Polybios son of Faustus

of Phlya, having been polemarch, in the

year when [Strat?]olaos was archon,

dedicated this

to Apollo Below-the-Heights.
