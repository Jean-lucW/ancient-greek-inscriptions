# Choregic dedication (Unknown festival and Panathenaia)

## English translation

**Part 1**

(Left) Having been victorious with the [cyclical?] chorus. (Right) Having been victorious with the pyrrhichistai, Atarbos son of Ly-, Kephisodoros (366/5 or 323/2) [was archon].
