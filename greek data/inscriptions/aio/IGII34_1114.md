# Dedication to Isis and Anoubis

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Isis, to Anoubis

Nikonymos son of Antiphanes of Oinoe

having been priest in the archonship

of Xenon (133/2) dedicated (this), when

Metrodoros of Chalkedon was temple attendant
