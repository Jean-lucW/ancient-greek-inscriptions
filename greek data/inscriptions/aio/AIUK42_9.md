## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] Concerning the lawful supplication

that it was decided that name [the seer?] made in the Assembly,

the Council shall decide, that the

presiding committe who happen

to preside shall introduce him to the People at the next Assembly,

and submit the opinion of the Council to the People that

it seems good to the Council to praise name the

seer (or name -mantis) [...] and

crown him [...]

[...]
