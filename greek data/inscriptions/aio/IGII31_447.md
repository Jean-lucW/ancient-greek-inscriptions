# Law and decree about the Little Panathenaia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Law

In the archonship of [...]

[...] Aristonikos son of Aristoteles

of Marathon proposed: for the good fortune of the Athenian

People, so that the sacrifice to Athena may be as fine as possible

at the Little Panathenaia and the income as great as possible [for the] [...]

[...] the lawmakers shall decide: [that the official sellers shall let the Nea]

ten days before [...]

for ten years in lots to the [...]

[...] in the year before, or to the one to whom the [...]

[...] tak- guarantors for the lessees [...]

[...] shall sell the two percent tax on the [Nea] [...]

[...] and the prytany shall post an announcement [...]

[...] the lease of the Nea specifically and

the sale [of the two percent tax] on the Nea separately [...]

[...] income be two talents [...]

[...] of the property in the Nea [...]

[...] this shall be for Athena (?) [...]

[...] of the Little Panathenaia [...]

[...] and let the [receivers] allocate it for these things [...]

[...] shall be and the [...]

[...] shall lease according to (?) [...]

[...] and the treasurer [shall] [...]

[...]

Decree

[...]

[...] [so that] [...] piously and

[...] annual

[...] prepared as excellently as possible

for Athena every year on behalf of the Athenian

People and that the other things that are necessary concerning the festival

which is celebrated for the goddess are organised well by the religious officials for all

time, the People shall resolve: in other respects

as proposed by the Council, but the religious officials shall sacrifice the two

-, the one to Athena Hygieia and the one

sacrificed in the [ancient temple] as previously, and

having distributed to the prytany members five portions and to the nine

archons [...] and to the treasurers of the goddess one and to the religious officials

one and to the generals and the infantry commanders

[...] and to those participants in the procession who are Athenians and to the

[basket carriers?] as is customary, they shall apportion the other

meat to the Athenians; and the religious officials, having bought cows with

the forty-one minai from the lease of the Nea

with the cattle-buyers, and having conducted the procession for the goddess,

shall sacrifice all these cows on the great

altar of Athena, but one on the altar of Nike,

having selected it from the most beautiful cows; and having sacrificed to

Athena Polias and Athena Nike all

the cows bought with the forty-one minai,

they shall distribute the meat to the Athenian People in

[the Kerameikos?] as at other meat distributions;

and they shall allocate portions to each deme according

to [the number of participants in the procession?] that each deme provides;

and for the contract costs for the procession and the butchers’ fee and

adornment of the great altar and the other things that [it is fitting

to [...] ] for the festival and an all-night revel,

they shall give 50 drachmas; and the religious officials who organise

the annual Panathenaia are to make the

all-night revel as fine as possible for the goddess and to conduct the procession

at the rising of the sun, punishing anyone who is not obedient to authority

with the penalties in the laws; and the People are to choose

[as ... five?] men from all the Athenians [...]
