# Dedication to Asklepios, Hygieia, and all other gods and goddesses by Caecilius Boulon

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Asklepios and Hygieia

and all the other gods

and goddesses, in accordance with

a dream, Caecilius

named Boulon son of

Moiragenes of Phyle (dedicated this)

uninscribed space

when the temple attendant was Marcus

Curtius Rufus

of Hagnous.
