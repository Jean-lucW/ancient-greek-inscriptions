# Marker of property used as security (Phlya)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Boundary marker of land (put up as security for) the dowry

of Hippokleia daughter of Demochares

of Leukonoion for 1 talent;

to the extent that its value is in excess of that

it is mortgaged to the Kekropidai

and the Lykomidai

and the

Phlyans.
