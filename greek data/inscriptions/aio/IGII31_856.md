# Citizenship for a poet

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] and

the prytany shall put the vote about him

at the next Assembly; and the court presidents shall bring

the scrutiny for him before

the court according to the law; and the inspector

and the trittys commanders shall stand a bronze statue

of him in the theatre of Dionysos and shall allocate the expenditure for this;

and three men shall be chosen from all the

Athenians who will manage the making

of the statue and its setting up so that it might be as good

as possible; and this decree shall be inscribed

on two stone stelai and one shall be placed in the

Dionysion, the other on the acropolis; and for

inscribing the stelai the administratror

shall give 30 ( scil. drachmas) for each from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
