# Dedication by pre-ephebes (<i>mellepheboi</i>), 94/3 BC (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The pre-ephebes of the archonship of Kallias (94/3 BC?) (dedicated this)

to the Muses:

Philetairos son of Philetairos

Sosibios son of Artemidoros of Miletos

Leonides son of Theophrastos of Kephisia

Ammonios son of Dionysios of Amphitrope

Sotaretos son of Euboulides of Marathon

Athenodoros son of Athenodoros of Ankyle

Asklepiades son of Herakleides of Probalinthos

Androkles son of Androkles of Dekeleia.

**Column 1**

The pre-ephebes (honour) their instructor

Dionysios

of Lamptrai.

**Column 2**

His fellow ephebes (honour)

their treasurer

Philetairos.
