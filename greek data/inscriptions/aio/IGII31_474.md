# Proxeny for Ph- son of Admetos of Priene

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the Athenian People, to praise Ph-

son of Admetos of Priene, and crown him with a gold

crown of a thousand drachmas; and he shall also be proxenos

and benefactor of the Athenian People,

both himself and his descendants, and they shall have

right of access to the Council and the People first

after the sacred business, and right of ownership of land and a house, according

to the law; and the Council in Samos shall take care of them,

and the People and the generals

[sent out] from Athens [at any time], so that

they suffer no harm; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree and

stand it on the acropolis; and for inscribing the

stele the treasurer of the People shall give [30] drachmas

from the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees; and to invite the envoys of the Prienians

to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow.

**Column 1**

[ In crown

[...] ]

**Column 2**

In crown

The People.

Ph-.
