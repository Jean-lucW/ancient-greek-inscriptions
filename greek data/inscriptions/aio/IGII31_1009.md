# Honours for the managers of the Mysteries

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Hieron (249/8) , in the sixth prytany,

of Antiochis , for which Phainylos son of Pamphilos of Oe

was secretary. On the thirteenth of intercalated

Posideon, the thirty-second of the prytany.

Assembly. Of the presiding committee Poseidippos son

of Ktesikles of Cholargos was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

Thrasyphon son of Hierokleides of Xypete proposed:

since the managers of the Mysteries elected

for the year of the archonship of Polyeuktos (250/49)

sacrificed as many sacrifices as were due

in the year to Demeter and Kore

and the other gods for whom it is traditional on behalf of

the Council and Athenian People and children

and women and king Antigonos
