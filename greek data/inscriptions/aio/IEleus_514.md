# Catalogue of prytaneis of Antiochis and those with perpetual dining rights (190/1)

## English translation

**Fragment a**

When the greatest Emperor Caesar Mar(cus) Aurelius

Commodus Antoninus Augustus Pius Felix

of Besa was festival-commander, [in the archonship]

of the city of Ju(lius) Hierophant (190/1) , [when the hoplite general was]

Aiolion [of Phlya], month of Boedromion [...] [the prytaneis of the tribe]

of Antiochis, having honoured [themselves and those]

with perpetual dining rights, inscribed them.

Eponymos [Ae(lius) Leu(kios) of Pallene]
