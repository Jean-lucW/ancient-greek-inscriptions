## English translation

**Part 1**

[The ephebes] of the tribe [Hippothontis] in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) [and their controller] [...]

[...] having been crowned by the Council and the [ Eleusis] [...]

[dedicated (this) to Demeter and Kore].

[...] proposed: since [the ephebes] of Hippothontis [...]

took care of [...] and were disciplined and took care of [...]

[while stationed at?] Eleusis and [the controller declares] [...]

[...] the demesmen shall resolve to praise them and crown [...]

for their – towards the deme of Eleusis, and to praise [also their controller] [...]

for his excellence and care [...]

and crown him with a – crown, and to announce it at the competition [...]

of the Dionysia, and the demarch shall invite him [...]

[just as the others to whom the deme?] has awarded a seat of honour [...]
