# Citizenship decree for Klearchos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Hippias (181/0) , in the twelfth prytany, of - ,

for which Theodosios son of Xenophantos of Lamptrai was secretary. On the

twenty-third of Skirophorion, the twenty-fifth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly in the theatre. Of the presiding committee

Drakontides son of Lonikos of Aphidna was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council

and the People decided. Lakrates son of Mentor of Perithoidai proposed: since Klearchos

is well-disposed collectively to the People, and individually

to citizens coming to E- [...]

and evidence of these things has been given by a number of the citizens,

and it is ancestral custom for the People [to honour good men];

for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted to preside

at the forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People that it seems good to the Council

to praise Klearchos son of T of - , and crown him with a foliage

crown for his excellence and his good will towards the People

of the Athenians; and he shall also be granted citizenship, having been scrutinised in

the court in accordance with the law [...]
