# Choregic dedication of Phormisios for the Thargelia, 380/79 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Phormisios son of Menekleides of Thymaitadai,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Hippothontis (and) Aiantis

in the men's competition, Diophon of Athens was director,

Kallikles of Tegea was pipe-player. Pytheas (380/79) was archon.
