# Letter from Hadrian to the artists of Dionysos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan

Parthicus, grandson of the God Nerva,

Trajan Hadrian Augustus, pontifex

maximus, tribunician power - times

consul three times, father of the fatherland, to the

[association] at Athens of the

artists of Dionysos, [sacred victors],

crown-wearers, greetings.
