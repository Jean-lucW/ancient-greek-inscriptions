# Dedication to Hermes by ephebes (Piraeus)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Those who were ephebes in the archonship of Theodorides (127/6)

(dedicated this) to Hermes

Demonikos son of Apollodoros of Aixone

Theomnestos son of Kephisodoros of Paiania

Sosos son of Euarchides of Oe

under the physical trainership of Apollodotos of Halimous.
