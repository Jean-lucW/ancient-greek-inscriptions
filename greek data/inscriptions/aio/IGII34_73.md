# Dedication by a prytany, 226/5 BC ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

The prytany of -tis dedicated (this)

to the hero in the archonship of Theophilos (348/7 or 226/5)

having been crowned by the

Council and the People for its excellence

and justice.
