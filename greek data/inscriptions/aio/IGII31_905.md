# Honours for the managers of Zeus Soter

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) , in the

twelfth prytany, of Kekropis ,

for which Semonides son of Timesias of Sounion

was secretary. On the eleventh

of Skirophorion, the eleventh of the prytany.

Assembly. Of the presiding committee Antidotes

son of Spoudias of Plotheia was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided.

Promenes son of Promenes of Kephale proposed:

since those elected

by the People for the management

of the sacrifice made the sacrifice with the priest to Zeus

Soter and Athena Soteira

well and with love of honour,

and managed the procession

and the strewing of the couch and

the adornment of the table [...]
