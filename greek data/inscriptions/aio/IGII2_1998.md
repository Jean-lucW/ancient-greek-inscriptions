# List of ephebic friends, ca. 75 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

For the good fortune of Caesar’s

victory. In the archonship of Aiolion (ca. 75 AD) ,

the superintendent being Demetrios (son of Demetrios) of Sphettos,

the trainer being Apollonios

son of Tertios of Paiania, the tutors

were Apollonios, Alexandros,

Thraseas, Themison, Elpias, Theodoros,

Dionysios. Eision son of Eision of Paiania,

having honoured his own friends and fellow

ephebes, dedicated (this):
