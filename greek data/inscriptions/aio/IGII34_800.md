# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia by a father

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aphrodisios son of – of Leukonoe,

on behalf of [his son] Eutychides,

dedicated to Asklepios [and Hygieia], as a thank-offering

in the priesthood of Theophilos son of Eudoxos

of Eleusis, this

statuette.
