# Honours for the father of a participant in a procession

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysias (238/7) , in the ninth

prytany, of -, for which Epichares son of Eudemos

of Aphidna was secretary. On the twenty-second

of Elaphebolion, the thirty-second

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

S- [...] was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

- son of Protogenes of - proposed: since

[the priestess] of Basile [...]

and the religious officials who were elected [by

the People declare?] that the [...]

[...] sent in the [procession

of the Awesome goddesses?] his own daughter [...]

[...] well and with love of honour [...]

[...] the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted for the forthcoming Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good

to the Council [...]
