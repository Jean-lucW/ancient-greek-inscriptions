# List of participants in the first Augustan <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

For Good Fortune of the Council and People of Athens, in the archonship of

Antigenes at Delphi and of Architimos at Athens, uninscribed space the following held

the Dodekais: the priest of Apollo Eukles son of Herodes of Marathon,

the interpreter from the Eupatridai appointed by the People Diotimos

son of Diodoros of Halai, uninscribed space the herald of Apollo Gorgippos son of Eudemos of Melite, uninscribed space the seer

Lysias son of Paramonos of Lakiadai.
