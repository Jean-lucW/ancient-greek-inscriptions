# Dedication by the general Kallisthenes of Prospalta at Rhamnous, 253/2 BC (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

A (on cornice)

Kallisthenes son of Kleoboulos of Prospalta, having been elected general in charge of the coast,

and having been crowned by the Council and the People, dedicated (this) to Dionysos Lenaios.

B (on base)

**Column 1**

In crown

The cavalry (crown him)

having been

commander of a tribal cavalry contingent

in the archonship of

Philostratos (260/59)

**Column 2**

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him)

having been commander of a tribal cavalry contingent

in the archonship of

Philostratos (260/59)

**Column 3**

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him)

having been general

in the archonship of

Phanostratos (254/3)

**Column 4**

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him)

having been general

in the archonship of

Pheidostratos (253/2)

**Column 5**

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him) him having been

cavalry commander

in the archonship of

Antimachos (256/5)

**Column 6**

In crown

The cavalry (crown him)

having been

cavalry commander

in the archonship of

Antimachos (256/5) .
