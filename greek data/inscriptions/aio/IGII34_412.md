# Dedication by an ephebe victor at the Hephaistia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Having been victorious

over the (other) ephebes in the torch race

at the Hephaistia,

Dionysios son of Menophilos

of Kephisia

(dedicated this) to Apollo.
