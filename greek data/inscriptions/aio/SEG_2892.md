# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Hippothontis

## English translation

**Fragment a+b**

[ [...] so that, when these things are completed],

it shall be apparent that the Council [takes appropriate]

[forethought] with love of glory, [and the prytany secretary]

[shall inscribe this decree on] a stone stele

and set it up in the Council chamber [and for the inscription],

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate [the expense incurred].
