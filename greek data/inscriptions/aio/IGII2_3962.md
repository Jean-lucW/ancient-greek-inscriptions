# A mother erects a statue of her son

## English translation

**Part 1**

In accordance with the petition

to the most awesome

Council of the 500,

Firmania

Eugamia Serapias,

having made the request, erected (this image of)

Firmanius Aigialos,

her own

offspring,

the temple attendant being Aurelius

Dionysodoros son of

Hermeias of Melite.
