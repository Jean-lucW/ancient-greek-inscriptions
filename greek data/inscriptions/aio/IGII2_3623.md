# Statue base

## English translation

[...] and [having been competition director]

of the Olympics of the League of [Asia, [...] ]

of Asia, of the Smyrnaians [...] ,

priest of the God Hadrian [Panhellenios and]

the harmony of the Greeks [and eponymos of the]

[tribe] of Hippothontis (erected this statue) [on account of their good will]

towards [himself].
