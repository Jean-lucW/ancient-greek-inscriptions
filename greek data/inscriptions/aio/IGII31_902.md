# Honours for the managers of Zeus Soter

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Glaukippos (273/2) , in the twelfth prytany,

of -, for which Euthoinos son of Euthykritos

of Myrrhinous was secretary. On the twenty-first of

Skirophorion, the twenty-third

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

- son of Kephision of Hagnous was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People

decided. Ar- son of - of Gargettos proposed:

since the managers sacrificed all the

sacrifices to Zeus Soter and Athena

Soteira and managed everything else with

the priest well and with love of honour, and managed

the strewing of the couch and the

adornment of the table, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted

to preside in the People shall put the matter

on the agenda and submit the opinion of the

Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise the managers:

- son of - of Halai, Epikydes

son of - of -, -les son of Onomastos

of -, - son of -pos of Athmonon, Ka-

son of - of -, -leon son of Panta-

of -, - son of Geitonides of A-,

- son of -ktetos of Marathon, Pr-

son of - of Aphidna, Charippos son of L-

of -, Dromeas son of Diokles of Erchia,

- son of - of Paiania, Menekles son of Ch-

of -, and to crown each

of them with a foliage crown for their piety

to the gods and love of honour for the

Council and People; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the agora;

and for inscribing the stele the board of administrators

shall allocate 50(?) drachmas.
