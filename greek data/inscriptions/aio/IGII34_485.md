# Choregic dedication of Antisthenes for the Thargelia, 360/59 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Antisthenes son of Antiphates of Kytherros,

serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Pandionis (and) Akamantis in the boys' competition

Eukles was director,

Kleanthes of Sikyon was pipe-player

in the archonship of Kallimedes (360/59) .
