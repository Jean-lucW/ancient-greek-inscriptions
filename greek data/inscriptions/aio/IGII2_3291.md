# Aigina honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar

Trajan Hadrian

Augustus Olympios

and founder (was erected by) the city

of the  
Aigenetans for

their own saviour and

benefactor,

through a manager,

Sosikleides
