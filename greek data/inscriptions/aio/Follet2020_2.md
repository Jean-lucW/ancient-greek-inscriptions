## English translation

**Part 1**

For good relief fortune,

-tes of - proposed:

[...] [...] [...] of Emperors

[...] [...] [...] he provides interpretation:

[...] [...] [...] their piety towards

[...] [...] [...] so, [having come home] from the [embassy] to Rome

[...] [...] [...] and having received [many]

benefactions [and gifts through] the [Emperors'] innate

philanthropy [and foresight (?)], and since Julia Augusta

the [benefactor (?) and] saviour of [Athens],

collaborated [with us] in all matters, [it shall be decided]: in other respects that everything

be done [just as] my collaborator in the [administration],

Elpide[phoros son of Asklepi]ades of Pallene, decreed [during]

my absence, but that [all]

[the] archons each [year] shall sacrifice to Good Fortune

[on the] most sacred day, on which [Julia] Augusta [was] born,

[...] they grant this [...]

[...] [ and shall make the] entry sacrifices to the [saviour of]

[Athens Julia Augusta, as] to Athena Polias, [and]

the hoplite general shall [pray and sacrifice]

[on behalf of] chariots [and armies], and the archon shall [set]

the [goddess] with her [...] , so that [the two goddesses] may be throne-sharers

[and] the fourth day of Thargelion the month [of the]

[Augusta (?) shall be sacred], on which day Athens granted

the old temple to her [as to Polias], and the

polemarch [shall sacrifice] to [the mother] of the camps, [on]

[the] first day of the year according to the Roman calendar,

and the priestess of Athena [Polias] shall begin [these] sacrifices

and shall get the perquisites,

and [also to set up] a golden statue of Julia Augusta in [the]

Parthenon, [and the general shall] complete [the sacred rites] for Good Fortune

[and] the [archons and] all the priests [and the]

genos-members shall participate in the procession, [and the] priestesses and the

queen, wife of the king, shall [sacrifice and] perform the entry sacrifices for their

priesthoods to Athena Polias, and the arrephoroi being released (from service) shall also

[sacrifice] and set up a torch and [sing together]

and dance for her, so that, when these things have been

done, their piety [towards] the saviour of [Athens]

Julia Augusta shall be [manifest], and

the interpretation [regarding these matters] shall be inscribed on a stele

and set up by the altar of the Augusti

[on the] Acropolis.
