# Citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

to praise [...] for his

good will towards the Athenian People, and

love of honour; and he shall be granted citizenship,

having been scrutinised in the court, and be enrolled in the tribe

and deme and phratry that he wishes; and the

court presidents shall introduce his scrutiny,

even if he is not present, as and when they fill the court for the

enrolment of citizens; and the [secretary shall present?] this

decree to the jurors; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it on [the acropolis]; and the board of administrators shall allocate

the expenditure for inscribing and [setting up?] the stele.
