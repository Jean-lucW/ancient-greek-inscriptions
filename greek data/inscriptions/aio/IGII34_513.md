# Choregic dedication from Rhamnous

## English translation

**Part 1**

On abacus

Megakles son of Megak[les] of [Rha]mnous dedicated (this) to Themis, having been crowned by the demesmen

on account of his justice, and having won victories as gymnasiarch in the boys' and in the men's scil. age classes , when Kallisto was priestess,

On base

and as theatrical sponsor in comedy when Pheidostrate was priestess of Nemesis.

Chairestratos son of Chairedemos

of Rhamnous made (this).
