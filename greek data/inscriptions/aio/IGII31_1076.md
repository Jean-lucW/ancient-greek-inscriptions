# Honours for the People of Erythrai

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

submit the opinion of the Council to the People,

that it seems good to the Council to praise the Erythraian

[People] and to crown them with a gold crown according

to the law for the good will that they have for the Athenian

People, and to announce the crown at the

Dionysia at the new competition for tragedies; and the

administrator shall manage the making and

announcement of the crown; and they shall have

front row seats at all competitions that the city puts on;

and to praise the ambassadors who have come

from the [Erythraian] People and to crown

both of them with a - crown [...]
