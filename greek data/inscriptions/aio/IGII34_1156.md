# Dedication to Herakles

## English translation

**Part 1**

On abacus

Timotheos

son of Teisias

(dedicated this) to Herakles.

On shaft

uninscribed space

Sanctuary of Herakles of

the Praxiergidai and of the

[...] listed below for whom there is a share (in this):

-uschon of Alopeke,

Aresandros [...]

[...] of Agryle

Olympiodoros [...]

[...] of Melite

[...]
