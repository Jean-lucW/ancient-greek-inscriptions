# Choregic dedication of all the sponsors for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People was victorious,

Lucius Flavius Flammas

of Kydathenaion was archon.

uninscribed space

All the sponsors and every tribal chorus

erected me, an ornament for the People of Kekrops,

voluntarily abandoning the contest,

so that no one would bear the shame of being deprived of the ivy wreath,

and I distribute as much renown to each,

as I owe him, being a shared (ornament).
