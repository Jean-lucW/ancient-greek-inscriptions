# Dedication by pre-ephebes (<i>mellepheboi</i>)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The pre-ephebes] of the archonship of Philemon

(dedicated this) to the Muses:

-os son of Eubiotos of Phlya

– son of Dionysios of Rhamnous.
