# Honours for the prytany of Aiantis

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Polyeuktos (250/49) , in the twelfth prytany,

of [Aiantis] , for which Chairephon son of Archestratos of Kephale

was secretary. On the [old and new day] of Skirophorion, the twenty-

ninth of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Dionys- son of - of - was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. - son of - of

Thorikos proposed: concerning what the prytany

of Aiantis reports about the sacrifices which they made before the Assemblies

to Apollo Prostaterios and Artemis

Boulaia and the other gods to whom it is traditional,

for good fortune, the People shall decide, to accept the good

things that occurred [...]

uncertain number of lines lost

[...] [the]

priest of the [eponymos ... and]

the herald [of the Council and People Eukles son of Philokles]

of Trinemeia; [and to praise the secretary of the Council]

and People [...] and

the under-secrtetary [...] ;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it in the

prytanikon; and for the inscription of the stele the administrator

shall allocate the expenditure accrued.
