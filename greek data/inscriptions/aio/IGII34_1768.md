# Donation of a house and garden to Asklepios in accordance with an oracle

## English translation

**Part 1**

The god instructed the Athenian People to dedicate

the house of Demon and the garden belonging with it

to Asklepios, and that Demon himself should be his priest.

Uninscribed

The priest, Demon son of Demomeles of Paiania, dedicated

both the house and the garden, as the god commanded,

the Athenian People also having granted that he be priest

of Asklepios in accordance with the oracle.
