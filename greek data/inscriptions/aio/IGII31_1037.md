# Honours for a foreigner

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and he shall have [...]

[...] and right of ownership of land and a house according to the law, as

written in the request; and the court presidents shall bring the scrutiny

for him to the court [when it is first possible]; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on the acropolis; and for the [making?] of the stele

the treasurer [of the military fund] shall allocate the expenditure.
