# Lampadedromia dedication, 106/5 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [having been]

elected general in charge of Rhamnous and

the coastal countryside in the year of the archonship of

Agathokles (106/5) , dedicated (this) to Zeus Soter and Athena

Soteira.
