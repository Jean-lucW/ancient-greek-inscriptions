# Dedication commemorating a priesthood of Pandion

## Greek text

**Part 1**

[ἱερ]εὺς Πανδίονος Ἀντισθένης

Ἀντιφάτους Κυθήρριος ἀνέθηκεν.

οἱ φυλέται.

## English translation

**Part 1**

The priest of Pandion Antisthenes

son of Antiphates of Kytherros dedicated (this).

The tribesmen.
