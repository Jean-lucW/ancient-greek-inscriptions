**Part 1**

Stone A

I

[The glory] of these men’s excellence [shines] always [...]

For, both on foot [and] [...] , they held [...] , so that all Greece might not [see a day] of slavery.

**Part 2**

II

Then, theirs was a [heart stronger than] adamant, when they set their spear before the gates [against the] [...]

by the sea, to burn [...] the town, when by force the Persians were turned back [...]

Stone B

[...] both on foot and [...]

[...] on the island [...] having thrown [...]

Stone C

For a wall in front of [...] of Pallas [...]

but they occupy the most fertile cape of the calf-nourishing mainland, to whom all-flourishing happiness comes.
