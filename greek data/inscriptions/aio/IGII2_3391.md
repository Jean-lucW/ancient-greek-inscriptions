# Dedication of the market inspectors' office (Agoranomion)

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Emperor Titus Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Pius

the Council of the 500 dedicated the market inspectors’ office, when the market inspectors were

Antipatros son of Mousaios [of Alopeke] and Leukios (son of Leukios) of Marathon.
