# Choregic dedication (Panathenaia and City Dionysia)

## English translation

**Face-front a**

[...] Relief [...]

serving as sponsor for the [pyrrhichistai] of the Relief boys

at the Great Relief Panathenaia.

**Face-right b**

[...] ?

of Pallene (sponsor) [at the Dionysia]

in the City [...]

was victorious.

Amymon [of Sikyon was director?].
