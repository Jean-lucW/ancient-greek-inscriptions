# The ephebes honour their trainer, 156/7 or 157/8 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

Having requested permission

from the Areopagites, the

ephebes in the archonship

of Lykomedes (156/7 or 157/8 AD) , through their

superintendent Publius Aelius

Theophilos the incredible

of Sounion, (honoured)

the trainer

for life of the ephebes

Abaskantos son of Eumolpos

of Kephisia.
