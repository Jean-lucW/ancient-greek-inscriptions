# Decree of Halai Aixonides prescribing an audit procedure, 368/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Euthemon proposed: in order that the common resources may be secured

for the demesmen, and that the demarchs and treasurers

may render accounts,

the demesmen shall decide, that the demarchs

and the treasurers shall deposit the account of the receipts

and expenses into the

box each month, since

those of the archonship of Nausigenes (368/7)

are also themselves voluntarily depositing the account each

month; and shall render their accounts

for the last year before the month of Metageitnion,

from the accounts from the box,

and not from other accounts; and to stand a stele

in the agora, having inscribed this

decree (on it); and the demarch shall administer an oath to the

auditor and his deputies in

accordance with the decree inscribed in the agora;

and if [...]

and they do not perform the audit in accordance with

this decree [...]
