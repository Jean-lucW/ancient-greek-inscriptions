# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia at Eleusis by Euphrosynos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Euphrosynos

son of the hierophant,

being temple attendant of Asklepios

and Hygieia

dedicated the

porch and the

building, in the

archonship of

Kallikratides.
