# Honours for the priestess of Aglauros

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Polyeuktos (250/49) , in the second

prytany, of Erechtheis , for which Chairephon

son of Archestratos of Kephale was secretary.

On the eleventh of Metageitnion, the eleventh of the

prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Kleidemos son of Phrynon of Phlya was putting the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People

decided. Demostratos son of Aristophanes

of Paiania proposed: concerning the report

of Aristophanes son of the priestess of Aglauros

about the sacrifices which she made at the initiatory rituals

to Aglauros and Ares and Helios

and the Horai and Apollo and the

other gods for whom it is traditional, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

who are to preside at the next

Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda as an item of the sacred

business and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council that

the Council and People receive the benefits

that occurred in the sacrifices for the health

and preservation of the Council and the Athenian

People and children and women, and on

behalf of king Antigonos and queen

Phila and their descendants; and

since the priestess of Aglauros made the

introductory sacrifices and the sacrifices appropriate to her,

and took care of the good order of the

all-night revel, and adorned the table,

to praise the priestess of Aglauros,

Timokrite daughter of Polynikos of Aphidna

and to crown her with a foliage crown

for her piety towards the gods;

and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe the decree on a stone stele and

stand it in the sanctuary of Aglauros; and for

the inscription of the stele the board of administrators

shall allocate the expenditure accrued.

The Council

the People (crown)

the priestess

Timokrite
