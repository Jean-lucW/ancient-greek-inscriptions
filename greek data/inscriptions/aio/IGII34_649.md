# Dedication to Pankrates by officials of a friendly society (eranistai)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

The treasurer and the managers and

the secretary in the archonship of

Hegemachos (300/299) dedicated (this) to Pankrates

having been crowned by the

friendly society for their excellence and justice

concerning the friendly society.

In olive crown

Treasurer: Mys

Managers:

Dionysodoros of Miletos

Eunostos of Thebes

Demophilos of Herakleia

Dionysios

Secretary:

Konon of Herakleia.
