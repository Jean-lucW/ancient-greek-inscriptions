# Sacrificial regulation for Asklepios and Hygieia in Attica

## English translation

**Part 1**

The precinct of

Asklepios and

Hygieia is sacred.

The farmers and

those dwelling close by shall sacrifice

to the two gods as is customary

and distribute the portions

to the founder and

the officiant.

No taking away

of meat.
