# Dedication of the spoils of victory over the Boeotians and Chalkidians

## English translation

**Part 1**

Base A

[With a painful iron chain], the sons of [the Athenians quenched arrogance, in deeds of war],

[when they tamed the races of the Boeotians and Chalkidians], from whom they dedicated these mares [to Pallas as a tithe (of the spoils)].

Base B

When they tamed [the races of the Boeotians and Chalkidians in deeds of war], the sons of the Athenians,

[with a painful iron chain], quenched [arrogance], from whom they dedicated these mares [to Pallas] as a tithe (of the spoils).
