# Decree of Halai Araphenides honouring <i>choregoi</i>, 341/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Nikomachos (341/0) , in the

demarchy of Phormos, the Halaians decided;

Philokles son of Philokedes

proposed: since the sponsors

sponsored choruses well and with love of honour,

Antimachos son of Archemachos, Soinautes

son of Nausistratides, to

praise them and crown

each of them with a foliage crown,

and they shall have a seat of honour

in the competitions and the

demarch in office shall invite

them to their seat of honour;

and the demarch shall

inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in the

Dionysion.

Soinautes Antimachos
