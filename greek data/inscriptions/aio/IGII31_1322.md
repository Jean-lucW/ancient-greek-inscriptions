# Honours for ephebes

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] continually

[...]

[...] and its erection in the Lyceum shall be a monument to their [love of toil]

and [discipline]; and to inscribe their names with their father’s names and deme and of their

[superintendent] and [trainers]; and to praise also their [superintendent], [...]

[...] , for the good-will and love of honour which he continually shows towards the Council and

the People; and to praise also their trainers, their [javelin trainer], Nikomachos

son of Nikomachos of Aphidna, and their weapons trainer, Persaios son of Symmachos of Kikynna, and their [physical trainer],

[...] of Phlya, and their archery trainer, [...]

[...] , and their artillery officer, Pedieus son of Neandros of Kerameis, and their secretary [...]

[...] of Piraeus; and to crown each of them with a foliage

crown; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele, as well as the names of the ephebes by tribe, and stand it [in the Agora],

and the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the expenditure accrued for the stele and its [erection].
