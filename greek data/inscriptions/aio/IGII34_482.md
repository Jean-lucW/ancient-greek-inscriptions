# Choregic dedication of Automedes for the Thargelia, 363/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Automedes son of Phanokles

of Gargettos, serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Aigeis (and) Leontis in the boys' competition,

Alexippos of Argos was pipe-player,

Polyzelos of Thebes was director

uninscribed space

Charikleides (363/2) was archon.
