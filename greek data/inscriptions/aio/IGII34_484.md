# Choregic dedication of Mnesarchides for the Thargelia, 361/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Oineis (and) Aiantis were victorious in the boys' competition

Mnesarchides son of Mnesikles of Lakiadai

was sponsor, Eukles was director,

Alexippos of Argos was pipe-player,

Nikophemos (361/0) was archon.
