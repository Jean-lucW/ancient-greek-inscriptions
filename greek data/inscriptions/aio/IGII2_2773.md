# Endowment of Flavius Asklepiades

## English translation

**Part 1**

For Good Fortune,

the herald of the [Council of the Areopagos]

and high [priest of the Augusti and the]

Augustan [House, Flavius Asklepiades]

of Diomeia [from his own resources (?) gave]

the most revered [board of the]

Areopagites [all the] gifts

recorded below.

uninscribed space

Each month [...]

[...] of Menogenes [...]

[All] the Areopagites will receive [...]

at the end of the year for a month in the city hall [dining rights (?) [...] each]

(will receive) 6 (denarii), and on the twelfth (of Skirophorion) at the Skira each [(will receive) [...] and on ]

my birthday likewise each (will receive) [...]

each (will receive) 3 (denarii), which I want to be given

at my hero-shrine.

Principal: Testament from Asklepiades [of Diomeia]
