# Honours for Eudemos of Plataia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Of Eudemos of Plataia.

In the archonship of Aristophon (330/29) ,

in the ninth prytany, of Leontis ,

for which Antidoros son of Antinous

of Paiania was secretary.

On the eleventh of Thargelion, the

nineteenth of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee Antiphanes

of Euonymon was putting to the vote. The People

decided. Lykourgos son of Lykophron

of Boutadai proposed: since

Eudemos both announced previously

to the People that he would donate for

the war, should it be needed, [4,00]0

drachmas, and now has donated

for construction of the Panathenaic stadium

and the seating

a thousand yoke of oxen and has sent

all of this before the Panathenaia,

as he promised, the People

shall decide: to praise Eudemos

son of Philourgos of Plataia and crown

him with a foliage crown

for his good will towards the

Athenian People; and he shall be

among the benefactors of the

Athenian People, himself and

his descendants, and he shall have

right of ownership of land and a house, and

he shall perform military

service and pay

capital taxes with the Athenians;

and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree and

stand it on the acropolis; and for

inscribing the stele the treasurer

of the People shall give

drachmas from the People’s

fund for expenditure on

decrees.
