# Honours for commanders of tribal regiments (taxiarchoi)

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Philokrates (276/5) , in the

twelfth prytany, of Demetrias , for which

Hegesippos son of Aristomachos of Melite was

secretary. The old and new day of Skirophorion,

the thirty-second of the prytany.

Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Arketos son of Kallistratos of Marathon was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People

decided. Epicharmos son of Kallistratides

of Kolonai proposed: since the

commanders of tribal regiments of the archonship of Philokrates (276/5)

conducted their office well and according to the

laws [...]
