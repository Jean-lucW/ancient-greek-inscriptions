# Dedication by an ephebic polemarch and victor, 145/6 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

Apollophanes son of Euphemos,

polemarch, having won

the torch race, dedicated (this)

to the gods in the stadium,

in the trainership of Abaskantos.
