# Honours for service connected with Eleusis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] for their piety towards

the gods and love of honour towards the Council

and People, and crown each

of them with a myrtle crown;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele

and stand it [by the Eleusinion?] [...]
