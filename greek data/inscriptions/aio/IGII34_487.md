# Choregic dedication of Diodoros for the Thargelia, 355/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Diodoros son of Satyros of Eroiadai,

serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Hippothontis (and) Kekropis in the boys' competition,

Eukles was director,

Sosistratos of Oreos was pipe-player,

Kallistratos (355/4) was archon.
