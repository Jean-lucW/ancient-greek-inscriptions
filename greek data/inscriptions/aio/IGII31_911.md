# Honours for Kallias of Sphettos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People (honours)

Kallias

son of Thymochares

of Sphettos.

In the archonship of Sosistratos (270/69) , in the sixth prytany,

of Pandionis , for which Athenodoros son of Gorgippos of Acharnai was secretary.

On the eighteenth of Posideon, the twenty-first

of the prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Epichares

son of Pheidostratos of Erchia was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

Euchares son of Euarchos of Konthyle proposed: since Kallias,

when the uprising by the People against those occupying

the city occurred and they expelled the soldiers

from the town, but the fort on the

Mouseion was still occupied and the countryside was in

a state of war with those from the Piraeus, and Demetrios was

on his way to the town from the Peloponnese with his army,

when Kallias learned of the danger facing

the city, he selected a thousand of the soldiers

under his command in Andros, distributed

pay to them, supplied them with provisions and arrived

at the town immediately to help the People, acting in accordance with the

good will of king Ptolemy towards the People, and leading

out the soldiers with him into the countryside he

made every effort to protect the harvesting of the grain,

so that as much grain as possible would be brought into

the city; and when Demetrios arrived, and

surrounded the town with his army and laid siege to it, Kallias, fighting

on behalf of the People and going on the attack with the soldiers

with him, despite being wounded shirked

no danger whatsoever at any time for the sake of

the preservation of the People; and when king Ptolemy

despatched Sostratos to act in the interests

of the city, and Sostratos sent for an embassy

to meet with him at Piraeus with whom he would devise

the terms of the peace for the city with Demetrios, Kallias

complied with the generals and the Council on these matters, and

went as an ambassador on behalf of the People, doing everything in the interests

of the city, and he stayed on in the town with

his soldiers until the peace was concluded, and, after setting sail

to king Ptolemy with the embassies

despatched by the People, he shared in the effort

in every way and worked in the interests of the city;

and when king Ptolemy the younger took up the throne,

Kallias paid a visit to the town, and when

the generals called on him, pointing out the

situation the city was in and beseeching him to hurry

on behalf of the city to king Ptolemy so

that some help might be available as quickly as possible to the town by way of

grain and money, Kallias set sail himself at his own expense

for Cyprus, and meeting with the king there, with love of honour

on behalf of the People he delivered to the People fifty talents

of silver and twenty thousand medimnoi of wheat as a gift

which was measured out on Delos to those despatched by the

People; and when the king first held the Ptolemaia

sacrifice and competitions for his father, the People voted

to send a sacred delegation and thought it right that Kallias should serve

as chief sacred delegate and lead the sacred delegation on behalf of the People,

Kallias complied in these matters with love of honour, refusing

the thirty minai which had been voted to him by the People for heading

the delegation but donating it to the People, while he himself led the

sacred delegation from his own resources well and worthily of the People, and he

managed the sacrifice on behalf of the city and all other matters

that fell to the sacred delegates; and at the time when the People were about to

hold the Panathenaia for Archegetis for the [] time since the

town had been delivered, Kallias held discussions with the king concerning

the equipment which was needed for preparing the robe, and when

the king donated it to the city he made sure that things were as fine

as possible for the goddess and that the embassy members who were elected with him

immediately conveyed the equipment here; and

now he has been stationed in Halikarnassos by the king Ptolemy,

Kallias continues to show love of honour in relation to the

embassies and the sacred delegations despatched by the People to king

Ptolemy, and individually for each of the

citizens who call on him he takes

every care, and for the soldiers who are under his

command, and collectively placing the highest value on what is in the interests

and the good standing of the city; and Kallias never submitted to the

[...] in his fatherland [...]

when the People were overthrown, but abandoned his

very property to be confiscated in the oligarchy rather than do

anything contrary either to the laws or the democracy

of all Athenians; so that all who wish to show love of honour

for the city know that the People always

remembers those who have performed benefactions for it and repays

each with thanks, for good fortune, the Council shall decide: that the

presiding committee allotted to preside at the Assembly

appointed by the law shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to

praise Kallias son of Thymochares of Sphettos for the excellence and

good will which he continues to have for the Athenian People and to

crown him with a gold crown according to the law, and to

announce the crown at the new competition tragedy of the

Great Dionysia; and the board of administrators shall manage

the making of the crown and the announcement; and the People

shall stand a bronze statue of him in the Agora; and he shall

have a front row seat at all the competitions that the city puts

on, and the director of works elected for religious matters

shall organise his front row seat; and the People shall elect

three men from all the Athenians who will manage

the making of the statue and its erection; and

the court presidents shall introduce the scrutiny of his grant

into the court when the days set by the law

have passed; and so that for the rest of time a memorial

of his honour loving acts for the People remains for Kallias,

the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on

a stone stele and stand it next to the statue; and for the inscribing

and the stele the board of administrators shall allocate the

expenditure accrued.
