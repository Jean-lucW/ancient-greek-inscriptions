# List of manumissions in court

## English translation

**Column 1**

[...] was acquitted

in a prosecution by Dionysios

son of - of Cholargos.

⸏

Demetria living in Melite

was acquitted in a prosecution by Echedemos

of Kephisia.

⸏

Chrysis living in Melite,

textile worker, was acquitted

in a prosecution by Hieromnemon

living in Melite.

Agathokles living in Kollytos,

shoe-maker, was acquitted in a prosecution by

Katagogios living in

Melite.

⸏

Eutychos living in Alopeke,

shopkeeper, was acquitted in a prosecution by

Soterides living in Keiriadai,

Diognetos

son of Eualkos of Poros.

⸏

Epikrates living in Skyros

was acquitted in a prosecution by Androkleides

son of Androsthenes

of Acharnai.

Eukrates living in Kerameis

was acquitted in a prosecution by Amphichares

son of Amphichares of

Keiriadai.
