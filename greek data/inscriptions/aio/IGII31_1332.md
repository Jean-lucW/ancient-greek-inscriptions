# Honours for the ephebes of 172/1 and their officers

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Antigenes (171/0) , in the fourth prytany, of Erechtheis ,

for which Sosandros son of Sosikrates of Alopeke

was secretary. On the eleventh of Pyanopsion,

the seventeenth of the prytany. Principal Assembly

in the theatre. Of the presiding committee E [...] enides

son of Echephylos of Acharnai was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and the People decided. Anaxiphon son of Euphragoras of Rhamnous proposed: since the

ephebes who were ephebes in the archonship of Sosigenes (172/1)

continually behaved in a disciplined manner [...] and their [superintendent]

and generals [...]

they took care of the [protection] [...]

likewise the laws and the decrees of the People

and [...] [the processions]

proper to themselves [...]

the Revered Goddesses [...]

the proper [...]

with orderliness [...]

[...] In order therefore that there should always be an incentive for those who are

ephebes to be disciplined and obedient to those appointed by the People

to be in charge as [expert instructors], that the Council

and the People may honour those who perform [beautifully] as ephebes, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted

for the forthcoming Assembly shall place these matters on the agenda,

and submit the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good

to the Council to praise the ephebes of the archonship of Sosigenes (172/1) ,

and crown them with a gold crown in accordance with

the law [...] for their [piety] towards the

gods [...] and to announce the

crown [...]
