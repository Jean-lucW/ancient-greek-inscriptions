# Proxeny for Drakontides and Hegesias of Andros

## English translation

**Fragment a**

Of Drakontides,

Hegesias, Andrians .

In the archonship of Chairondas (338/7) , in the

tenth prytany, of Pandionis ,

for which Philippos son of Antiphemos

of Eiresidai was secretary [...]

**Fragments bc**

**Part 1**

[...] to praise them and

crown each of them

with a foliage crown for their manly virtue

and good will towards the

Athenian People; and they shall be

proxenoi and benefactors

of the Athenian People, both

themselves and their descendants, Drakontides

son of Amphoteros and Hegesias son of Stes-,

and they shall have, if they need

it, access to the

Council and the Athenian

People first after the sacred business;

and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree on

the acropolis on a stone stele;

and for inscribing the stele

the treasurer shall give 20

drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees;

and to invite them to hospitality in the

city hall tomorrow.

**Column 1**

[ In crown

The Council].

**Column 2**

In crown

The People.
