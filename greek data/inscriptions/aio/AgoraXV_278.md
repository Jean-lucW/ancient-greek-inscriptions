# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Hippothontis, 47/6 or 45/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The prytaneis (crown)

the treasurer of the

tribe,

in crown

Diophanes

son of

Apollonios

of

Azenia.

Since the prytaneis of Hippothontis

and those with perpetual dining rights in the archonship of Diokles of Azenia (47/6 or 45/4)

declare to the Council that the treasurer,

whom they chose from among themselves, Diophanes son of

Apollonios of Azenia, has made the sacrifices [from his own resources]

and has taken care of everything else

with love of goodness, and for this they call on the Council

to allow them to crown him with a foliage crown,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide to praise [the]

treasurer of the prytaneis of Hippothontis, [Diophanes]

son of Apollonios of Azenia, and to crown [him with a foliage]

crown, with which it is traditional to crown [good]

men, and the secretary of the prytany shall inscribe [the decree]

on [a stone stele] and dedicate it

in the Council chamber.
