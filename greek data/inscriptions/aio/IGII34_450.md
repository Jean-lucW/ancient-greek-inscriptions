# Choregic dedication for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Hippothontis] was victorious in the men’s competition,

[...] son of [...] -los of Piraeus was sponsor,

[...] of Skarpheia (?) was director,

[...] -s of Sikyon was pipe-player.
