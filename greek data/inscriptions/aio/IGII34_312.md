# Lampadedromia dedication

## English translation

**Part 1**

- of Pithos, having been

elected general in charge of Rhamnous

and the coastal countryside in the year of the archonship of

Demochares (108/7) , dedicated (this) to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira.
