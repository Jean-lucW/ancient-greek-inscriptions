# Decree of Rhamnous honouring the general Diomedes, 301/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Philokrates son of Philokles of Rhamnous proposed:

for good fortune, that the Rhamnousians shall decide,

since Diomedes son of Diodoros of Ptelea,

having been elected general by the Athenian

People, took care of the Rhamnousians

well and [with love of honour], saving

the fort for the Rhamnousians and the Athenian

People, to praise Diomedes son of Diodoros

[and crown him with a gold crown?]
