# Choregic dedication of Apollodoros for the City Dionysia, 332/1 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Oineis was victorious in the boy’s competition,

Apollodoros son of Pasion of Acharnai

was sponsor, Lysiades of Athens was director,

Aristodemos (332/1) was archon.
