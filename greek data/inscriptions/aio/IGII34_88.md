# Dedication by official sellers (<i>poletai</i>), 324/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The official sellers in the archonship of

Hegesias (324/3) dedicated (this):

Erginos son of Theoktitos

of Kydathenaion ,

Antigenes son of Aristomachos

of Xypete .
