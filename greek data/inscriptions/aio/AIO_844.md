## English translation

**Part 1**

Kleochares son of Teisonides of Rhamnous proposed: since

Kleochares son of Kleodorides, [chosen as demarch (?) by the]

demesmen in the archonship of Antipatros (263/2) , [managed it, so that]

both the shares of the produce were [...]

[...] , and showed [worthily his own good will (?)],

and delivered to [ [...] who was?]

stationed in Piraeus [...] of the

demesmen whom Kineas took; and he sacrificed honey cakes

to all the gods and the heroes [...] so that

the relations of the Rhamnousians to the gods might be on a good and pious footing,

for good fortune, the Rhamnousians shall decide: to praise

Kleochares son of Kleodorides of Rhamnous for his [justice]

[and good will] and to crown him with a foliage crown;

and (they) shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele and

stand it [wherever they decide]; and they shall choose now three men from

their own number for the making and setting up of the stele. [The following

were chosen: [...] ]
