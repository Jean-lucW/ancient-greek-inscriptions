# Dedication by a competition-director

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The People were the sponsor. - was archon].

[Competition-director] [...]

**Column 1**

Akamantis was victorious in the boys’ competition

Deinon of Aegina was pipe-player

Klearitos of Arcadia was director

**Column 2**

[ Tribe was victorious in the men’s competition]

[...] [was pipe-player]

[...] [was director]
