# Honours for Dionysios

## English translation

**Fragments ab**

[...]

[...] [in the seventh prytany, of -,

for which [...]

[...] was secretary?]. On the - of Anthesterion (?),

the - of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee

[...] was putting to the vote.

The People decided. Polyeuktos son of Sostratos

of Sphettos proposed: since Dionysios

previously announced to the

People that he would donate three thousand medimnoi,

should it be needed, and [...] in

[the] previous [grain shortage?] [...]

[...]

[...]

**Fragment c**

**Part 2**

[...] and he shall be liable to

military service and pay capital taxes

with the Athenians; and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on

the acropolis; and for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give

30 (?) drachmas from the People's fund

for expenditure on decrees.
