# Honours for two [Rhodians?]

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[since Olympodoros and Did]ymar[chos]

[of Rhodes? are well disposed collectively] to the

People [and individually to those citizens always?]

coming to [Rhodes?] and continue to be

[...] of the Athenians

[...] of the country

[...] in the [...]

[...] of king Ptolemy

[...] them

[...] [sailing?] to Hydrea

[...] [memorials of their ancestors?]

[...] about these things

[...] and preferential access

[...] Olympodoros

[...] more enthusiastic

[...] zeal they have displayed

[...] again [...] the Athenians

[...] that they will be honoured

worthily of their benefactions, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide, that the

presiding committee allotted for the forthcoming Assembly

shall place these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People,

that it seems good to the Council to praise

Olympodoros [son of -] and Didymarchos son of Poly-

[- of Rhodes?], and crown each

of them with a [gold?] crown for their excellence and

[good will] towards the Athenian People;

and they shall be proxenoi and benefactors

[...] according to the

laws; and on their showing love of honour

and [maintaining] the same preference, they shall be permitted

to obtain other benefits from the People,

whatever they may be deemed to be worthy of; and to invite

them to hospitality in the city hall

tomorrow; [and in order that there may] be [a reminder]

for them of the People's gratitude

[...] [shall inscribe this decree] [...]

[...]
