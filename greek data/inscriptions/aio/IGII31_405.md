# Proxeny for a Phaselite

## English translation

**Fragment a**

[...]

of Phaselis [...]

and [...]

concerning the Athenian People,

the People shall decide:

to praise - son of

-ron of Phaselis for his

good will and [love of honour]

concerning the Athenian People;

and he shall be proxenos

and benefactor of the Athenian

People, both himself and

his descendants, and he shall have

[right of ownership] of land and a house.

**Fragment b**

[...] and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer of the People

shall give 30 drachmas

from the People’s fund

for expenditure on

decrees.
