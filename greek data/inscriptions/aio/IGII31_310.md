# Honours for Theoklos of Corinth

## English translation

**Part 1**

Crown

Gods.

In the archonship of Nikomachos (341/0) , in the tenth

prytany, of Leontis , for which Onesippos son of Smikythos of Araphen

was secretary. On the old and new day, the thirty-seventh

of the prytany. Of the presiding committee [...]

was putting to the vote. The Council and the People

decided. - son of - of Phrearrhioi proposed:

[since] Theoklos of Corinth [has continued to take care

of any Athenian] in need in Corinth

and [has ... concerning] the Athenian

People, the Council shall resolve: to praise Theoklos

[of Corinth?] and crown him with a foliage

crown and submit the opinion of the Council to the

People that it seems good to the Council that the presiding committee [...]
