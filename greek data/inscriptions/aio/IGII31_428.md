# Honours for Philomelos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] so that also others who, being

well disposed, show love of honour concerning the

Athenian People, may know that

the People will honour them as each deserves,

the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside at the next

Assembly shall introduce Philomelos

to the People and place his case on the agenda first after

[the sacred business], and submit the opinion of the

Council to the People that it seems good to the Council,

since Philomelos is a good man concerning

the Athenian People and in the [...]

[...] money [...]

[...] having shown [himself useful?] [...]

[...]
