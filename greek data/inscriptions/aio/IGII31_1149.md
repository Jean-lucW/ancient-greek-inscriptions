# Honours for the prytany of Aiantis

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Ergochares (225/4) , [in the – prytany, of Aiantis ],

for which Zoilos son of Diphilos of Alopeke was secretary.

On the eighth of –, the [?]second [of the prytany. Assembly]

in the theatre. Of the presiding committee – son of –

of Phaleron was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. –

son of Menekles of Aphidna proposed: about what the prytany of Aiantis

report concerning the sacrifices which they made before the Assemblies to

Apollo Prostaterios and Artemis Boulaia and the

other gods to whom it is traditional, and managed the convening of the

Council and the People and everything else which the laws

and decrees of the People require of them; to praise the prytany

of Aiantis and to crown it with a gold crown according to the law for its

piety towards the gods and love of honour towards the Athenian Council and

People; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand it in the prytanikon;

and the administrator shall allocate for [inscribing?] the stele

the expenditure accrued.
