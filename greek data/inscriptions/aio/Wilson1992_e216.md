## English translation

**Part 1**

For good [fortune],

on the occasion of the victory of the most divine Emperors [Marcus Aurelius and Lucius Verus]

Augusti Armeniaci, Parthici [Maximi, in the archonship of Sextus]

of Phaleron (165/6 AD) , the superintendent of the ephebes [Lucius Herennius Cornelius, also known as]

Attikos, of Azenia inscribed the names of [those who were ephebes under him, who were]

the first who wore white, because of the [most brilliant benefaction]

of the mighty high priest Tiberius Claudius Herodes [of Marathon.]

The presiding committee asked, who thought [that the ephebes should wear white on the]

day on which the [cohort marches] to Eleusis, and who did

not. No one raised (any objection). Herodes said, [“Ephebes, while I am here,]

you will not lack white cloaks”.

Superintendent of the ephebes: Lucius Cornelius, [also known as Attikos, of Azenia]

Deputy superintendent: Moschos son of Hermeros [...] ,

the trainer being: Abaskantos [son of Eumolpos of Kephisia].
