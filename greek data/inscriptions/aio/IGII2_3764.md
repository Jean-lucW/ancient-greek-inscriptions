# Herm for Aelius Apollonios

## English translation

**Part 1**

This man, born of dadouchs,

and of a sacred mother

who displayed the ceremonies for the two goddesses

by the temple of Deo,

Aelius Apollonios, renowned

superintendent of boys,

was set up by his son of the same name,

who was archon of ephebes.
