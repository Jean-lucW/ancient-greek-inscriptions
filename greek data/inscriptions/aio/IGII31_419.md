# Honours for -kles son of Sotairos of Amphipolis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] of the

prytany. Assembly. [Of the presiding committee]

Mnesikles of was putting to the vote.

The People decided. Demophilos

son of Demophilos of Acharnai proposed: since

-kles son of Sotairos of Amphipolis has both

previously continued to be well disposed towards the Athenian

People, both himself and his ancestors,

and they have paid all the

[capital taxes] which the laws require

of them, and have served on all

the military campaigns with the People

well and with love of honour [...]
