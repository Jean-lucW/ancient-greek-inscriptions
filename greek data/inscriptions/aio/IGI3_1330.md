# Gravestone of Myrrhine, first priestess of Athena Nike

## English translation

**Part 1**

Far-shining memorial

of Kallimachos’

daughter, who first

tended the temple

of Nike.

She had a name

companion to her good repute,

as by divine fortune Myrrhine

she was called

in truth;

she was first

to tend the statue (?)

of Athena Nike,

(chosen) by lot from all,

Myrrhine by good fortune.
