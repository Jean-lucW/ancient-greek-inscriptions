# Dedication by ephebic competition-director

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the superintendentship of Ploutarchos of Acharnai,

Achoristos set up

a stele for the citizens

of wide renown. / His father's

name he bears, from the deme

Eupyridai, /

having also bestowed on the ephebes

extraordinary prizes /

and having alone obtained

the glory of a crown, /

having been Competition-director

of the Hadrianeia.

**Column 1**

in crown

Severeia

in crown

Antoneia

**Column 2**

in crown

Antinoeia

in crown

Commodeia.
