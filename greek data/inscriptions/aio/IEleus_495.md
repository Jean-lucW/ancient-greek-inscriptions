# Apollonia honours Marcus Aurelius

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar

Marcus Aurelius Antoninus

Germanicus Parthicus

Medicus (was erected by) the Apollonians

below Cyrene, through Marcus

Julius Praxis,

Panhellene.
