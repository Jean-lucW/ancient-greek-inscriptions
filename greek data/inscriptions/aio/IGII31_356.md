# Honours for a son of -oiros of Larisa

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Kephisophon (329/8) ,

in the fourth prytany,

of Aigeis , for which Sostratides

son of Ekphantos of Eupyridai was secretary.

On the [old and new day] of Pyanopsion,

the eleventh of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee -ippos

of Oa was putting to the vote. The People decided.

Demades son of Demeas of Paiania

proposed: since -

son of -oiros of Larisa [and]

[his - have?] always [been friends?]

of the Athenian People [...]
