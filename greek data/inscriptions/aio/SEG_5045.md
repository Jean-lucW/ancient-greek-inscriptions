**Part 1**

Decree 1

[...] to the secretary twenty drachmas.

Decree 2

In the archonship of Alkaios (422/1) .

On the nineteenth of the prytany.

The Council and the People decided. Akamantis was in

prytany; Archikles of Halai was secretary; Didymias

was chairman. Alkibiades proposed: to praise Polypeithes

of Siphnos, because he is a good man towards the Athenian

People, both himself and his ancestors,

Kallaischros and Stesileides; and reply to him that

Kallaischros [was honoured by the People?] in return for the good

that he did to the Athenians, and [on behalf of the?] Athenian [People ? he acted

with regard to?] all [the matters of war?] [...]
