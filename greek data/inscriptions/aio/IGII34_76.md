# Dedication by the prytany of Aigeis and four tribal decrees, 341/0 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

The prytany of Aigeis dedicated (this) in the archonship of Nikomachos (341/0)

having been crowned by the Council and the People for its excellence

and justice

**Part 2**

Tharrias of Erchia proposed: it shall be resolved by the tribesmen: since Poseidippos of Hestiaia, treasurer

of the tribe, served well and justly as treasurer for the tribesmen and has offered sacrifices on behalf of the prytany,

such as needed to be offered, to praise him for his excellence and justice regarding the prytany

and to crown him with a foliage crown.

**Face-left b**

**Part 3**

Aristophanes of Ikarion proposed: it shall be resolved by the tribesmen to praise, since they well and justly

managed the convening of the People and the distribution of the tokens and made the donation

to the tribesmen, to praise them for their excellence and justice towards the tribesmen and to crown each of them with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice towards the tribe,

Diodoros son of Philokles of Gargettos, Timokritos son of Timokrates of Ikarion, Tharrias son of Tharriades of Erchia.

Tharrias of Erchia proposed: to praise the religious officials who officiated at the Mysteries at Eleusis

and to crown each of them with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice towards

the tribesmen, Poseidippos of Hestiaia, Timokritos of Ikarion, Aristophanes of Ikarion, Chairias of Plotheia,

Kallistratos of Teithras, Pythion of Kydantidai, Eubios of Ankyle, Theomnestos of

Phegaia, Theophilos of Myrrhinous, Melieus of Ionidai.

**Column 1**

In crown

The People

**Column 2**

In crown

The Council

**Face-right b**

Diodoros of Gargettos proposed: to praise Aristophanes son of Eukleides of Ikarion for his justice towards the tribe Aigeis and to crown him with a foliage crown.
