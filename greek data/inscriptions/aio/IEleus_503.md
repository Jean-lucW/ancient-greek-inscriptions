# Honours for L. Memmius Altar Priest

## English translation

**Part 1**

The city (erected this statue of)

L(ucius) Memmius Altar Priest of Thorikos,

who was descended from Dadouchs and archons

and generals and competition-directors,

who also, along with many other magistracies

and liturgies, served as eponymous

archon (161/2) and hoplite general

and manager of the gymnasiarchy

of the God Hadrian and competition-director three times,

and envoy many times about the

greatest matters, including also about the Gerousia, who performed initiations

in the presence of the God Hadrian,

who initiated the God Lucius Verus

Armeniacus Parthicus and the Emperors

M(arcus) Aurelius Antoninus ⟦and M(arcus) Aurelius⟧

⟦Commodus⟧ Germanici Sarmatici,

who served the two goddesses for 56 years, who was descended

from high priests, who was a lover of his fatherland.
