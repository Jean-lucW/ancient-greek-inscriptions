# Dedication commemorating victories by the kitharode, Nikokles, in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Face-front A**

Nikokles son of Aristokles

In laurel crown (II)

At the Pythia

In laurel crown (III)

At the Pythia

In laurel crown (IV)

At the Pythia

In olive crown (I)

At the Great

Panathenaia

In ivy crown (VIII)

At the Lenaia

in the dithyramb

In laurel crown (V)

At the Pythia

In laurel crown (VI)

At the Pythia

In laurel crown (VII)

At the Pythia

**Face-left B**

In crown (IX)

At the -ieia

In crown (X)

At the Hekatomboia

In pine crown (XI)

At the Isthmia

the first

In crown (XII)

At the Basileia

in Macedonia

**Face-right C**

In oak(?) crown (XIII)

At the Basileia

in Alexandria

In crown (XIV)

At the Helieia

In crown (XV)

At the Basileia

In crown (XVI)

At the Asklepieia
