# Honours for Timosthenes [of Karystos]

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Heliodoros (228/7) , in the eighth prytany,

of Pandionis , for which Charias son of Kallias of Athmonon was

secretary. Decrees of the People. On the old and new day of Anthesterion.

On the twenty-ninth of the prytany. Assembly.

Of the presiding committee Hagnotheos son of Philogenes

of Agryle was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members, Lamprias

of Thorai , Hieron of Anagyrous , Kallimedes of Kydantidai ,

Nikokleides of Phrearrhioi , Diokles of Prospalta ,

Apollodotos of Oe , Kallistratos of Sypalettos ,

Leonides of Kopros , Sophanes of Trikorynthos , [[-]]

of Pallene . Kephisodoros son of Aristodemos of Xypete

proposed: since the laws also ordain, that those whom

the People has honoured for setting up trophies, whether by land or

sea, or for re-establishing liberty,

or for putting their private resources towards the collective preservation ,

or for having been benefactors or good advisors,

with meals in the city hall, that the Council

and the People should take care of them and their descendants, and that the People

should give a dowry for the betrothal of their daughters, as much as

it shall wish, and for the re-establishment of their private property, to each according

to the worth of their benefactions, and the grandfather of Timosthenes,

Timosthenes, was responsible for the People obtaining many great benefits
