# Dedication commemorating a victory in a singing competition

## English translation

**Part 1**

Here I lie as a marker of Alkibiades’ victory.

He set me up as a prize not for his singing but for his excellence

uninscribed space

When Alexandra, daughter of Leon of Cholleidai,

was priestess.
