# Honours for Therson

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

[...] and [...]

[...] [in order, therefore, that there shall be] an incentive for all

to benefit the Athenian People, knowing they will be honoured

[...]

[...] with a [gold]

crown for his piety towards the gods and

the love of honour, which he has towards the Athenian People; and the herald

[...] these

[...] for Therson

[...] [and the People shall stand a bronze] statue of him

[...] ; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

[...] ; [and the treasurer of the military fund]

[shall allocate the money] spent on [these things].
