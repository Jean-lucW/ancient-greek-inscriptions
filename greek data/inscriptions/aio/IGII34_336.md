# Dedication by ephebes of Erechtheis, 333/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The controller Perik- son of - of Anagyrous

The gymnasiarchs of the ephebes of Erechtheis

of the archonship of Nikokrates (333/2) dedicated (this), having won the torch-race,

-andros son of Tim- of Euonymon, Charikles son of Aleximenes of Pergase.
