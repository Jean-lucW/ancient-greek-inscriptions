# Dedication by Xenokles of Sphettos, manager of the Mysteries, 321/0 or 318/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Demeter and Kore

Xenokles son of Xeinis of Sphettos

dedicated (this), having been manager

of the Mysteries.

Aristopeithes son of -nymos of Phyle made it.
