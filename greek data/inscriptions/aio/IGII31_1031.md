# Honours for the ephebes and officials

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

[...] from - because [...]

[...] and following the laws; and to praise

the physical trainer Hermodoros son of Heortios of Acharnai and

the javelin instructor Lysikles son of Antipatros of Sypalettos and

the weapons trainer Nikandros son of Nikandros of Ankyle and

the secretary Hermogenes, because they managed the ephebes

well and [justly?]; and so that there may be a memorial [of their

good will?], the prytany secretary shall inscribe the

names of the ephebes and of their superintendent and of their instructors

on a stone stele and stand it [in the agora . . .

and for the] stele and [its erection? the administrator

shall allocate the expenditure] accrued.
