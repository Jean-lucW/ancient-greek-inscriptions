# Honours for Claudius

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Tiberius Claudius

Caesar Augustus Germanicus, emperor,

(was erected by) the Council of the Areopagos and the Council of the Six Hundred and the People,

when the hoplite general and also the first competition-director

of the Augustan games was Novius son of Philinos of Oion.
