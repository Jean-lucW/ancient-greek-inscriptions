# Dedication by an ephebe

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] , having been overseer of the [torch-race?], in the

year that – was archon

(dedicated this) to Hermes, in the trainership

of Philios son of Philios

of Phrearrhioi, the assistant-trainership

of Apollonides

son of Apollonides of Phrearrhioi.
