# Decree of the orgeones of Bendis (329/8 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Gods.

Philokrates proposed: since Euphyes

and Dexios, having become managers of

the sanctuary in the archonship of Kephisophon (329/8)

managed it well and with love of honour and worthily of

the goddess and the orgeones, the orgeones

shall decide to crown each of Euphyes

and Dexios, for their justice and care,

with a gold crown of 100 drachmas;

and to inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and set it up in the sanctuary

of the goddess.

Two crowns
