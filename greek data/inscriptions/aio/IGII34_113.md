# Dedication by <i>basileus</i>

## English translation

**Part 1**

Miltiades [son of Zoïlos of Marathon]

having been king, dedicated (this).

Deputies:

Dionysios son of Zoïlos of Marathon

Nikomachos son of Apollodoros of Perithoidai.

**Column 1**

In painted crown

The Council

**Column 2**

In painted crown

The People

**Column 3**

In painted crown

The Council.
