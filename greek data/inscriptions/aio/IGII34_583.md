# Dedication commemorating victories in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Part 1**

Diophanes

son of Empedion

victory at the Isthmus.

Two children of Empedion, Athenians, have been victorious,

Diophanes as a youth at the Isthmus in the pankration;

and Progonos (has won) crowns (?). They showed the strength of their hands.
