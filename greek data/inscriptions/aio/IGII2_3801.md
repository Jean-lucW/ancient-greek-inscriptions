# Symmachos honours Aurelius Herakleides

## English translation

In accordance with the decision of the

mighty Council of the

500, (this herm of) Aurelius Herakleides

of Eupyridai, the successor

of the doctrines from Zeno,

(was erected by) Symmachos (son of Symmachos)

of Phlya, for his teacher,

on account of his excellence.
