# Honours for the People of Tenedos and Aratos of Tenedos

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39) . The People decided.

Kekropis was in prytany. On the eighth of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee S- [...] put to the vote.

The secretary was Aspetos son of Demostratos of Kytherros.

Kallikrates son of Charopides of Lamptrai proposed:

concerning what the Tenedians say, to praise the

People of Tenedos for their excellence and good will

towards the Athenian People and the allies,

both in times past [...]

[...] have helped [...]

[...] money [...]

[...] from (?) [...]

[...] and so that the Tenedians

may recover the money [that they have asked for?] [...]

[...] in the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39) [...]

[...] the People for assistance [...]

[...] everything, the People shall decide [...]

[...] after the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39) [...]

the allied levy voted [...]

[...] and for the [regular administration] [...]

[...] in the year after the archonship of Theophrastos (340/39)

they shall have [all?] this conveyed to Tenedos

[...] until [...] have recovered [...]

[...] and during this time it shall not be permitted for

any general or anyone else to exact either money

or anything else, nor shall it be permitted for the []

to make a levy assessment during this time, until the Tenedians

have recovered the money they [have asked for?],

so that also in the future both the allies

and anyone else who is well disposed towards the

Athenian People may know that the Athenian People

takes just care of those allies who act

in the interests of the Athenian People and

the allies; and to praise the People of Tenedos

and crown it with a gold crown

of a thousand drachmas for its excellence and good will

towards the Athenian People and the allies;

and to praise [the representative of Tenedos],

Aratos, and crown him with a foliage crown;

and to praise [...]

representatives [...]
