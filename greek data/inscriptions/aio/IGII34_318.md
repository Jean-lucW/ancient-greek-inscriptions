# Lampadedromia Dedication, 99/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

- son of -lotos of A-, [having been elected]

in charge of Rhamnous and the coast

in the year of the archonship of Prokles (99/8) dedicated this

to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira.
