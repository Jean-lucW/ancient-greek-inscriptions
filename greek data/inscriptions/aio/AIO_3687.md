## English translation

**Part 1**

In the demarchy of Euthippos.

The Teithrasians decided; Eudikos proposed: so that the common property

may be secured for the demesmen and the Teithrasians may know what they possess

and their revenue, the demarch shall inscribe

those common properties which are leased in perpetuity.

The Teithrasians decided; Pandios proposed: since

Xanthippos is a good man concerning the common property

of the Teithrasians, the Teithrasians shall resolve to lease

to Xanthippos the estate at Teithras of which the neighbour to the north

is the estate of Aigeus and the heroon

of Epigonos (?), and to the south the Herakleion and the estate of

the hero [...] (topographical? description of hero) and the precinct of Zeus

for all time both to himself and to the heirs of Xanthippos

on both sides; and it shall be permitted for

Xanthippos and the heirs of Xanthippos to use the estate in whatever

way he may wish, paying 220 drachmas

to the Teithrasians each year; and if the enemy

causes damages to the tenant of the estate, he shall

remit half of what he takes from the land to the Teithrasians,

[...] he shall be relieved of the lease

by the Teithrasians; and to set up a stele in the Koreion

and inscribe on it those who have leased estates

in perpetuity and the rent that each

[pays?]; and anything written previously contrary to this

decree shall be erased from the stele

by three men with the demarch

[...] that the Teithrasians

release the lessees from the leases for all time;

and the Teithrasians shall lease the estates in perpetuity;

and the lessees shall pay to the Teithrasians

[the contributions each] year and

pay the property taxes on these (scil. estates) to the city;

and they shall pay the rents to the demarch

in office, in the month of Elaphebolion
