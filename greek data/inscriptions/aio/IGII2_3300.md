# Miletos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) the greatest emperor

Trajan Hadrian

Olympios

(was erected by) the mother-city of Ionia,

the city of the Milesians,

for their founder and benefactor,

the erection being managed

through the officials around Tiberius Julius

Macer.

The sculptor Aulus Pantulius Gaios

of Ephesos and Miletos made it.
