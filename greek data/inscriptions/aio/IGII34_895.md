# Dedication to Asklepios at Eleusis by his priest

## English translation

**Part 1**

in olive crown

Eisidotos

son of Parrhasias

of Phyle, being

priest, dedicated

this.

below crown

Orthagoras son of Eisidotos

of Phyle, key-bearer.
