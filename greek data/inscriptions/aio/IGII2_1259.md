# Decree of the orgeones [of Amynos and Asklepios and Dexion?]

## English translation

**Part 1**

Theodotos proposed, since the feast-givers

in the archonship of Theophrastos (313/2) , Antikles

son of Memnon and Kleitophon son of D[emophi]los,

took care well and with love of honour

of both the collective things and the sacrifices,

to praise them and crown them

with a foliage crown, so that others

may also act with love of honour regarding the orgeones,
