# Choregic monument of Nikias, 320/19 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Nikias son of Nikodemos of Xypete dedicated (this) having been victorious sponsor for Kekropis in the boys’ competition.

Pantaleon of Sikyon was the pipe-player. Song: The Elpenor of Timotheos. Neaichmos was archon (320/19) .
