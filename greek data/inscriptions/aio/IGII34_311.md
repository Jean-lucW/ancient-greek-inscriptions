# Lampadedromia dedication, 117/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Elpias son of Elpias of Konthyle, having been elected general in charge of Rhamnous

and the coastal countryside in the year of the archonship of Menoites (117/6) ,

dedicated (this) to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira.

**Column 1**

From the citizens Xenokrates

of Konthyle won the torch race

at the Diogeneia.

**Column 2**

From the soldiers

Polemarchos of Marathon

won the torch race at the Diogeneia.

**Column 3**

From the foreigners Demetrios

of Antioch

won the torch race at the Ptolemaia.
