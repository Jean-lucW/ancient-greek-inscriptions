# Choregic monument of Lysikrates, 335/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Lysikrates son of Lysitheides of Kikynna was sponsor,

Akamantis was victorious in the boys’ competition, Theon was pipe-player,

Lysiades of Athens directed, Euainetos was archon (335/4) .
