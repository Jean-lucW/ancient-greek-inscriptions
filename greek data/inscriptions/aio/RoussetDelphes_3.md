**Part 1**

God. For Good Fortune.

[...]

**Column 2**

[...] which is called [...] , but the left

side, as the stream flows should belong to the Delphians until Aigoneia.

From Aigoneia, as the stream flows, through the ravine

at the ridge called Kerdon, until the road

which leads to the holm oak: the right side should belong to the Phlygonians

and the Ambryssians, whereas the left should belong to the Delphians.

From the road, until the holm oak at Katopterion;

from Katopterion straight along

the ridge to the sharp rock. From the rock

straight on to Petrachos. From [Petrachos]

straight on to Parnassus: The area

to the east of this (line) shall belong to the Phlygonians and the

Ambryssians, whereas the parts to the west shall belong to the Delphians.

The stream flowing past Aigoneia shall belong

to all of them in common. If there are any sanctuaries in these

places, they shall continue to function as they have from the beginning.
