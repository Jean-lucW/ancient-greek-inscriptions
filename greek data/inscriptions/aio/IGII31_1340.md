# Citizenship decree for Timonides

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[ [...] and the court presidents, when they have filled]

[the courts to five hundred and one jurors],

shall introduce his scrutiny and present

this decree about him, even if he is not present; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for the inscribing and [setting up] of the stele

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the

expense accrued.

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown)

Timonides

of Mar-.
