# Dedication by the eponymous archon of 394/3 BC, his deputy (<i>paredros</i>) and secretary

## English translation

**Part 1**

Archon:

Euboulides son of Epikleides of Eleusis (394/3)

Deputy:

Diktys son of Epikleides of Eleusis.

Secretary:

Prokles son of Iophon of Kydathenaion.
