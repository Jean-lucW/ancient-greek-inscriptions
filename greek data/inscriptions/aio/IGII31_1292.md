# Honours for Kephisodoros

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Charikles () , in the ninth prytany, of Aigeis, for which

Aischrion son of Euainetos of Rhamnous was secretary. Decrees of the People.

On the thirteenth of Elaphebolion, but according to divine reckoning the eighteenth (?), the twenty-eighth (?)

of the prytany. Principal Assembly in the Piraeus. Of the presiding committee

Antipatros son of Potamon of Lamptrai was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and the People decided.

Sodamos son of Timasitheos of Oion proposed: since Kephisodoros has displayed to the uttermost

his good will towards the People at every opportunity,

engaging in politics for the thirty years cleanly and incorruptibly,

avoiding no labour or danger for the sake of

the interest of the community, and undertaking all other public services

which the people assigned to him, and in particular being

good and reputable as treasurer of the military fund, and in his conduct of the joint treasurership of the grain supply fund,

as third member, both in the year of Apollodoros (203/2) and that

of Proxenides (202/1) , and making laws conducive to

the harmony of all Athenians, and

advising on fair and just means of raising money, and proposing ways for

the People to keep its existing friends firm in their loyalty

and to obtain others in addition, and foreseeing the plots arising

from those outside the country, and being appointed to be in charge of opposing them,

and counselling good alliances in the interests of

the People, and carrying out embassies in respect of the most

important matters relating to the preservation of the cities and the countryside, and bringing in money

and grain and not a few other donations, and proposing

many useful decrees furthering the good repute, effectiveness and

decorum of the People, and maintaining the same

preference all the time, hating what is wicked, and by the consistency

of his deeds and thoughts being particularly responsible,

with the goodwill of the gods, for the People's

retaining its autonomy, and for the greatest good

befalling (?) no small number of the other Greeks, and by all these things

demonstrating that he deserves to receive honour according to the law; and he requests that

the People set up a bronze statue of him in the agora and

another in the Piraeus in the commercial area, and asks for meals for himself in the

city hall and for whoever at any time is the eldest of his descendants, and a seat of honour at all

the competitions that the city puts on, and he has made his request to the

[Council including] an itemisation of his actions;

so, therefore, that the People may be seen to honour good men and

[those who at any time become] enthusiasts for such a preference [may know] that

they will always receive appropriate honour from the Athenian People,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee

allotted to preside at the principal Assembly

shall bring him before the People and place these matters on the agenda,

and submit to the People the opinion of the Council, that it seems good to the

Council to praise Kephisodoros [...] and crown

him with a gold crown according to the law on account of his [excellence and the

good-will] which he continues to show towards the Athenian People, and to proclaim

this crown at the city Dionysia during the new tragedies,

and at the Panathenaia and the Eleusinia and the Ptolemaia during the gymnastic

competitions; and the making of the crown and the announcements

shall be the responsibility of the generals and the treasurer of the military

fund; and they are to place a bronze statue of him in the agora and another

in the Piraeus in the commercial area; and he shall have meals in the city hall

and whoever at any time is his eldest descendant, and a seat of honour at all the

competitions that the city puts on; and the court presidents are to bring forward

his scrutiny, when the days specified in the law [...] have elapsed [...]
