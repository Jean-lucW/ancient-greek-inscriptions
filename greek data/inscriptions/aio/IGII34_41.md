# Choregic dedication by the <i>basileus</i>

## English translation

**Part 1**

Onesippos son of Aitios of Kephisia, the king, dedicated (this).

In the term of office of Onesippos as king, these men who acted as theatrical sponsors were victors:

**Column 1**

Of the comedies:

Sosikrates, bronze merchant, sponsored,

Nikochares directed.

**Column 2**

Of the tragedies:

Stratonikos son of Straton sponsored,

Megakleides directed.
