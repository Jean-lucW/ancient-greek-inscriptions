# Choregic dedication of the People for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

It was a wonderful day when Diogenes [was sponsor],

since on the stage each one [showed] the peak of [his skill],

[a comrade] of the godlike youth, Antinoos.

Then the whole People of the Kekropidai erected me [as a prize,]

[since Praxag]ores held the reins of a [beautiful] chorus,

while Gry[pon] led the men on with the rhythm [of the pipes,]

[and when the herald came] announcing the good news, glory attended those

[whose namesakes were] Attalos and Akamas,

[Antiochos and] Ptolemy, and Hadrian,

[and for this victory of Aias,] I stand here as the prize of honour

uninscribed space

[ [...] with beauty] and splendour he produced this,

[and thus] was not unnoticed by [the lovely] Graces.
