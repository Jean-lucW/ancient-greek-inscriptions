# Honours for a Sidonian and others

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

traces

son of -neides and [...]

of - and Kall- [...]

-mos of Sidon, to praise and crown each

of them with a gold crown according to the law for the good will

and love of honour that they have for the Athenian

People; and they shall have [...]

[...] and shall obtain other benefits from

the Council and People, whatever they may be deemed worthy of;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele [and stand it]

[on the acropolis]; and for the inscription of the stele the

board of administrators shall allocate the expenditure

accrued.
