# Honours for Aemilius Paulus

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People of the Athenians (erected this statue of)

[Lucius] Aemilius Paulus,

son of Paulus, Lepidus,

their own benefactor

and saviour,

when the priest was Pammenes

son of Zenon of Marathon,

when the manager of the island was

-on son of Markos of Philaidai.
