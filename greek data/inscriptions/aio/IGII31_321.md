# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Phrynichos (337/6) , in the

tenth prytany, of Pandionis , for which

Chairestratos son of Ameinias of Acharnai

was secretary. Of the presiding committee

Antiphanes of Euonymon was putting to the vote .

Demades son of Demeas of Paiania proposed: for the good

fortune of the Athenian People, the People

shall decide: [...]
