# Honours for City Guardians

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) , in the

ninth prytany, of -,

for which Semonides son of Timesias of Sounion

was secretary. On the twenty-fourth

of Elaphebolion, the twenty-sixth

of the prytany. Principal

Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Ka- son of - of Epikephisia was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and

People decided. Mnesiergos son of Mnesias

of - proposed: since the city guardians

of the archonship of Glaukippos (273/2)

managed the procession and the

sacrifice to Asklepios and the adornment

of the table and the all-night

revel well and with love of honour, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that

the presiding committee allotted to preside

in the People at the next

Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda

and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the
