# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia by a priest of Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Zenon of Melite ], having become priest, dedicated this to Asklepios and [Hygieia],

when the keyholder was his son Leonides, when the basket-bearer was Soteira daughter of Athenagoras [of Melite].
