# Dedication (in city hall?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Hestia and Apollo and the gods Augusti and the Council

of the Areopagos and the Council of six hundred and

the People, Philoxenos son of Agathokles of Phlya

dedicated (this) from his own resources, made by his father

Agathokles son of Philoxenos of Phlya,

when the hoplite general was Tiberius Claudius

Theogenes of Paiania and

the manager of the city was Tiberius Claudius Oinophilos

of Trikorynthos.
