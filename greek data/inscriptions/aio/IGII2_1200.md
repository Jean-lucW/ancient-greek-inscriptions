# Decree of Aixone honouring the <i>choregoi</i>, Leontios and Glaukon, 317/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Philoktemon son of Chremes

proposed: the demesmen shall decide,

since the choral sponsors in the

archonship of Demogenes (317/6) , Leontios

son of Dion, Glaukon son of Kallikrates,

sponsored choruses for the Aixoneans

well and with love of honour, to praise

them and crown them

with a gold crown of 500 drachmas

each for their love of honour
