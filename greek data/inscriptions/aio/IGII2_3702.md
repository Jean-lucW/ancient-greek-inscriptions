# Statue base for Marcus Ulpius Pupienus Maximus

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Marcus Ulpius Pupienus

Maximus, the

mighty, the son

of the most brilliant

consular, Marcus Ulpius

Eubiotos, who was a colleague

to his father

as one of the court presidents,

(was erected by) one who was deemed worthy

of nurture

and was promoted

by his father to rank:

Ulpius

Elpidephoros, (for) his personal

benefactor.
