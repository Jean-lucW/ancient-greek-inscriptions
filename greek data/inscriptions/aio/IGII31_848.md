# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted to

preside at the coming Assembly shall present -n to

the People and put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise

-n son of Dionysios of - and crown him with a foliage

crown for his excellence and love of honour towards the Council and

the Athenian People; and that he shall be proxenos of the People; and he shall have

the right of ownership of land according to the laws and a house;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis; and for the inscription

the trittys commanders and the inspector shall allocate 30 drachmas

from the People’s fund for expenditure on decrees.
