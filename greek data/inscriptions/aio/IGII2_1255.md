# Decree of the orgeones [of Bendis?] (337/6 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The orgeones decided. Olympiodoros

– proposed. Since the

religious officials in the archonship of Phrynichos (337/6)

managed well and with love of honour

the procession and the meat-

distribution and everything else,

the orgeones shall resolve to

praise them and crown them with a gold

crown of three hundred drachmas,

Antiphanes son of Antisthenes of Kytherros,

Nausiphilos son of Nausinikos of Kephale,

Aristomenes son of Mosch-

of –, for their love of honour towards

the orgeones; and this decree

shall be inscribed on a stone stele

and set up in the sanctuary of [Bendis]

by [the secretary] Thallos.
