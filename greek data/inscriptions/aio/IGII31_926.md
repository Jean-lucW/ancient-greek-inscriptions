# Honours for a prytany

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

three lines preserving a few letters

[...] [and they managed well] and justly [all the]

[sacrifices and] everything else that

[the laws and decrees] prescribed;

[and to praise them and crown them with] a gold

crown [according to the law for their love of honour] and

[care] for the Athenian People;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

[this decree on a stone stele] and stand it

[in front of the Council chamber]; and for the inscription

of the stele [the board of administrators] shall

allocate [...]
