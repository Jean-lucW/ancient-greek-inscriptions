# Honours for a courtier of King Audoleon

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of Diotimos (285/4) , in the twelfth]

[prytany, of Pandionis . On the twenty-fifth]

[of Skirophorion, the twenty-fifth of the]

[prytany.] Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Philomelos son of Philomelos of Halai was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. Simonides

son of Hegemon of Hagnous proposed: since Timo-

[...] is of good will to the Athenian People

both saying and doing whatever good he can

for the city with Audoleon, and is

of service also to Athenians

visiting Audoleon in whatever way they

ask of him; and further he co-operated

most zealously in the delivery of the grain

to ensure it was conveyed to the People

as quickly as possible; so that in future

he might strive in the interests of

the People, for good fortune, the People

shall decide: to praise Timo- [...]

[...] and crown [...]

[...]
