# Decree of Eitea honouring Hippokles son of Demokles, 332/1 BC?

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Relief

Timokrates son of Timokles of Eitea

proposed: since Hippokles

son of Demokles of Eitea [...]

[...]

[...]

the deme and does what good is in his

power for the demesmen both now

and in time past

by both word and deed, that

the demesmen shall decide, to praise

Hippokles son of Demokles of Eitea

and crown him with a gold crown

of 500 drachmas for his [excellence]

and justice towards

the demesmen;

and the treasurer in the archonship of

[Niketes (332/1) ?] shall inscribe this decree

and stand it in the sanctuary

of Basile on a stone stele

and account to the

demesmen for whatever the cost

may be.
