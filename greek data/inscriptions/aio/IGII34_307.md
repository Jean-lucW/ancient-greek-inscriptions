# Dedication by the general Euxitheos of Kephisia at Rhamnous to Demeter and Kore

## English translation

**Part 1**

Euxitheos son of Philoxenides of Kephisia,

having been elected by the People for a second time as general

in charge of the coastal countryside, dedicated (this) to Demeter and Kore.
