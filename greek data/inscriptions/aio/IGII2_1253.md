# Honorific decree of the orgeones of Amynos and Asklepios and Dexion

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

The orgeones decided [...]

son of Hippomachos of Melite proposed, since [...]

-doros and Ant- [...]

have been just men regarding the collective affairs

of the orgeones of Amynos and

Asklepios and Dexion, to praise

them on account of their justice and

to crown each of them with a gold

crown, and to inscribe this decree

in the sanctuary on a stone stele.

uninscribed space
