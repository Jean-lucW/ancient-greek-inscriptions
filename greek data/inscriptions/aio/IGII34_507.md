# Choregic dedication from Anagyrous

## English translation

**Part 1**

With a chorus of sweet laughter, [he] once [was victorious] at the Dionysia,

and [he set up] this gift to the god as memorial of victory,

an ornament for the deme and an object of emulation for his father, garlanded with ivy;

for even before him [he won] the crown-bearing [contest].
