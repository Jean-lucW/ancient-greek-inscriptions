# Dedication by an ephebe, 59/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] having been treasurer (?)

on behalf of his fellow ephebes

in the archonship of Leukios (59/8 BC) (dedicated this)

to Hermes

in the trainership

of Meniskos of Kolonai,

in the assistant-trainership

of Athlos of Kolonai.
