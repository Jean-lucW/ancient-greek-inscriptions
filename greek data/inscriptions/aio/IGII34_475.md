# Choregic dedication of Aristom- for the Thargelia, 384/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aristom- [...] son of [...] of Perithoidai, serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Oineis (and) Pandionis in the men's competition, Philophron son of Philokrates

was director, Oiniades son of Pronomos was pipe-player, Dietrephes (384/3) was archon.
