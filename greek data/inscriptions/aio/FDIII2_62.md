# List of participants in the fourth (or third?) Augustan <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

For Good Fortune of the Council and People of Athens.

In the archonship of Timoleon at Delphi and of

Theophilos son of Diodoros at Athens, the following held the Dodekais:

the priest of Apollo Pythios Eukles son of Herodes,

the interpreter chosen by the Pythian oracle from the Eupatridai Polykritos

son of Polycharmos, the interpreter appointed by the People from the Eupatridai

Diotimos son of Diodoros, the herald of Apollo

and priest of Hermes Patroos of the Kerykes Gorgippos

son of Eudemos, the hieromnemon Herodotos son of Mousaios, the seers Lysias

son of Paramonos, Aristos son of Ammonios, the pipe-player Menodoros

son of Menodoros.
