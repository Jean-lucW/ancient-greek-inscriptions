# Honours for Claudius (41 AD)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council of the Areopagos [and the Council]

of the Six Hundred and the People (erected this statue of) Tiberius

Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus,

emperor, consul designate

for the second time, pontifex maximus, tribunician

power (41 AD) , the dedicator being [...]

son of Diokles of Piraeus, [having been manager]

of the Piraeus twice, [...]

- and to the great gods, the hoplite

general being Dio-

of Besa, and the eponymous [archon of the city being]

[...] Leo-
