# Statue base for Regilla at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

[By decree] of the City of the Delphians, [Claudius]

[Atticus Herodes] the consul (dedicated this statue of) Appia Annia [Regilla]

[Atilia Caucidia Ter]tulla, daughter of Appius Gallus,

[the consul, uninscribed space the husband for his] wife.
