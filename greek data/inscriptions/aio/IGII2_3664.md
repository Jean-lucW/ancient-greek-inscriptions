# Areopagos, Council, and People honour Iason Logismos of Hagnous

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos Council

and the Council of the 600 and the

People dedicated (this herm of) the priest of Hermes Pyletos

and Charidotes and of Cultivated

Earth and of Pothos, the

All-holy Iason son of Zethos,

also called Logismos, of

Hagnous, having been temple attendant

of Asklepios and of

Hygieia, on account of his excellence

and his piety

towards the

gods.

phallus
