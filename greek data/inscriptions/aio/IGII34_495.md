# Choregic dedication of Lysistratos for the Thargelia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Lysistratos] son of Atarbos of Thorikos,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Akamantis (and) Pandionis

[...] -ios was director,

[...] -os was archon.
