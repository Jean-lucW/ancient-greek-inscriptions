# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis; and for

inscribing the stele the treasurer of the People shall give

[] drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees, so that

[other officials?] may also show love of honour, knowing

that the People gives thanks to whose who

show love of honour towards them.
