# Decree bestowing citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] shall be

an Athenian, and his descendants, and

he shall be enrolled in the tribe, deme and

phratry that he wishes, according to the law;

and the prytany shall put the vote

about him to the People at the next

Assembly [...]
