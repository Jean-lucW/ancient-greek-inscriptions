# Honours for friends of the king and of Antipater

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] The People decided. A[rchedikos]

[son of Naukri]tos of Lamptrai proposed:

so that as many as possible of the

friends of the king and of Antipater,

having been honoured by the Athenian

People, may benefit the city

of the Athenians; for good fortune,

the People shall decide [...]
