# Choregic dedication (Acharnai)

## English translation

**Part 1**

A Front Left

Mnesistratos son of Misgon (and)

Diopeithes son of Diodoros were sponsors,

Dikaiogenes was director.

**Part 2**

A Front Right

Mnesimachos son of Mnesistratos (and)

Theotimos son of Diotimos were sponsors,

Ariphron was director,

Polychares son of Komon was director.

**Part 3**

B Right Side

Theotimos son of Diotimos,

Mnesi- [...] [were sponsors],

Thersandros [was director].
