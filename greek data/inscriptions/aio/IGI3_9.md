# Dealings with the Delphian Amphiktyony

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council and the People decided.

[ ] was the prytany. A–

was secretary. Menyllos

was chairman. –ies proposed: [to agree]

on the alliance, [as

the men from] the Pylaia announce,

for [all] the Amphiktyons

who have a share in the [sanctuary],

swearing [to abide by] the

[alliance by] Apollo [and

Leto] and Artemis,

invoking ruin on ourselves

if we transgress.

[A decree shall be made in accordance with?] tradition [concerning]

[all the things which the men from?] the Pylaia

[announce as having been?] decree[d ... ?]
