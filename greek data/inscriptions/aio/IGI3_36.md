# Decree about payment to priestess of Athena Nike

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council and the People decided.

Aigeis held the prytany. Neokleides

was secretary (424/3?) . Hagnodemos

was chairman. Kallias proposed:

for the priestess of Athena Nike

the fifty drachmas

written on the stele,

the payment officers in office

in the month Thargelion

shall pay (them) to the priestess

of Athena Nike [...]
