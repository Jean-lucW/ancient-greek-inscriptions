# List of two ephebic teams, 163/4 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

In shield

For good

fortune

Left of shield

In the

archonship of

Philisteides of Piraeus (163/4 AD)

Right of shield

The superintendent being

Claudius Herakleides

of Melite

Aurelius Demosthenes of Sphettos and Eleutheros (son of Eleutheros) of Kyrteidai inscribed their own teams.

Team of Aurelius Demosthenes, having been gymnasiarch for the month of Thargelion and

competition director of the Hadrianeia and king.

**Column 1**

Eukarpos son of Achoristos of Paiania

Epagathos son of Theodoros of Melite

Ulpius Epagathos of Alopeke

Olympos son of Olympos of Lamptrai

Agathokles son of Athenion of Eitea

Epigenes son of Epigenes of Acharnai

Daphnos son of Hierax of Phaleron

Aphrodeisios son of Alexandros of Ph(aleron)

Agathopous son of Aktios of Kephisia

Arisemos son of Karpodoros of Gargettos

Zosimos son of Karpodoros of Gargettos

Alexis son of Karpodoros of Gargettos

Controllers

Athenion son of Hygeinos of Eitea

Kleon son of Paramonos of Sphettos

Herodes son of Alexis of Skambonidai

Claudius Stephanos

**Column 2**

Crowned:

Aurelius Demosthenes

at the Antinoeia

in the city

for the encomium, at

the Antinoeia in Eleusis

for the wrestling,

at the Germanikeia for the wrestling.

Epagathos at the Antinoeia

for the long race, (and)

at the Hadrianeia (and)

at the Germanikeia.

Achoristos at the Antinoeia

(in the city) for the stadium race,

at (the Antinoeia) in Eleusis for the stadium race.

Eukarpos for poetry at the Antinoeia, the same for the poem at the Germanikeia.

Arisemos for the long race at the Antinoeia at Eleusis.

**Column 3**

Team of Eleutheros son of Eleutheros of Kyrteidai,

having been gymnasiarch in the month of Poseideon

and herald. Epiktetos

Sostratos son of Theodoros of Acharnai

Claudius Leon of Besa

Eutychides son of Prosdokimos of Philaidai

Mystikos son of Satyros of Myrrhinoutta

Eisidotos son of Epigonos of Sphettos

Antonius Priscus of Eiresidai

Dareios of son of Eunomos of Semachidai

Artemidoros son of Eukarpos of Sounion

Hermeias son of Hermeias of Oa

Theotimos the Milesian

Telesphoros the Milesian

Leonax the Milesian

Controllers

Eleutheros son of Eleutheros of Kyrteidai

Epaphrodeitos

Euelpistos son of Syntrophos of Euonymon

Prosdokimos son of Prosdokimos of Philaidai

Below columns

Eleutheros was crowned at the Hadrianeia for the encomium, at the Hadrianeia for wrestling,

for the encomium at the Antinoeia in Eleusis. Sostratos at the Hadrianeia for heraldry,

at the Hadrianeia for the pankration, at the Antinoeia in Eleusis for the pankration.

Epiktetos for the stadium race at the Antinoeia in the City, at the Antinoeia in Eleusis

for the stadium race, at the Hadrianeia for the stadium race in the City.

Mystikos at the Antinoeia in the City for the double stadium race, at the Hadrianeia for the double stadium race,

at the Antinoeia in Eleusis for the double stadium race.

Leon for the wrestling at the Germanikeia. Hermeias at the Antinoeia

in the City, playing the trumpet.

Dionysios
