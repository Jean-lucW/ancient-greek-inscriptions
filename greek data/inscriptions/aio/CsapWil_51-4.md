## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

[...] the treasurer and the demarch

in office shall also give them

for [the sacrifice] each year

20 drachmas from

the money collected

from the theatre; and if the theatre

is [unsold?] the demarch

and the treasurer in office

shall give the specified amount

for the sacrifice from the

[common] budget of the

demesmen; and the secretary [with? on behalf of?] the demesmen

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

in the sanctuary of Athena

Hippia; and the demarch

-enes shall give them

20 drachmas for inscribing the stele

and account for it to the demesmen.
