# Dedications to Amphiaraos, Hygieia, and Asklepios in the Diogeneion

## English translation

**Face a**

To Amphiaraos and Hygieia

Timokles

son of Sokrates of Halai,

having become priest,

dedicated this,

in the archonship of

Euandros

**Face b**

Phirmos (son of Phirmos)

of Gargettos

dedicated this

to Asklepios,

to Amphiaraos.
