# Honours for doctors (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [showing the goodwill]

which [they have had towards the city]

both now and in the [previous time, both]

[themselves and their ancestors], the People

shall decide to praise them on account of their

goodwill and love of honour towards the

People of the Athenians and shall crown

each of them with a golden crown of 1000

drachmai, and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and set it up in the Asklepieion

in the city, and for the inscription

of the stele, the treasurer of the People shall give

50 drachmai from the People’s expenditure for

decrees.

**Column 1**

In crown

The People

In crown

The Council

**Column 2**

In crown

The People

In crown
