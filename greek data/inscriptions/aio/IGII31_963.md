# Citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[... and they]

shall enrol [in the tribe and deme and]

[phratry] that they [wish according to the law; and]

the court presidents [shall submit the]

scrutiny [of the award to the court,]

in order that there may be a [memory (?) of the award] and

it may be seen by all [that the Athenian People]

understands how to give thanks to those who are

its benefactors [...] [worthy of]

the benefactions; and the secretary [of the People]

shall inscribe [this decree on a stone stele]

and stand it on [the acropolis] [...]
