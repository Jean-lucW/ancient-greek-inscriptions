# Decree of (Rhamnous and?) Athenian residents there honouring Archandros of Eleusis, ca. 248 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

and to inscribe this decree on a stone stele and stand it wherever

he may wish; and for the [inscribing and making of the stele]

those chosen shall contribute the expenditure incurred.

uninscribed space

These were chosen for the dedication:

Attalos son of Timaios of Thria, Dromeas son of Teisandros of Eitea,

Strombichos son of Kleodorides of Rhamnous, -kinetos son of Hegesikles of Eitea,

Menandros son of Teisandros of Eitea, -os son of Aischrion of Trikorynthos,

Timotheos son of Theogeiton of Rhamnous, Lysikleides son of Nikostratos of Rhamnous,

Kleochares son of Teisonides of Rhamnous, K[le]o[chares] son of Kleodorides of Rhamnous.

In crown

[The]

community

of those [living]

at Rhamnous (honour)

Archandros

of Eleusis.
