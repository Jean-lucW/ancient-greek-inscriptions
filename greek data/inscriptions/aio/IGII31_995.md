# Honours for a competition director

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1

Muses.

In the archonship of Kallimedes (252/1) , in the ninth prytany, of Aiantis , for which

Kallias son of Kalliades of Plotheia was secretary. On the of Elaphebolion,

the twenty-ninth of the prytany. Assembly in (the theatre) of Dionysos. Of the presiding committee

Charias son of Heniochos of Pelekes was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The People decided. Neoptolemos son of Phileas of Deiradiotai proposed: concerning what

the competition director reports about the sacrifices which he made to Dionysos and the other

gods to whom it is traditional, for good fortune, the People shall decide: to accept

the good things that occurred in the sacrifices which he made for the health and

preservation of the Council and the Athenian People and the children and women

[[and for king Antigonos and queen Phila and their]]

[[descendants]] ; and since the competition director, placing high value

on piety to the gods and displaying the good will and

love of honour that he has for the Athenian People, made all

the traditional sacrifices at the appropriate times well and piously, and

accomplished the pre-games sacrifices during the rites according to tradition, and managed

the Dionysiac competitions and the others well and

with love of honour, to praise the competition director Agathaios son of Autokles of Prospalta

for his piety to the gods and love of honour

for the Council and Athenian People; and when he submits the accounts for his

term according to the law he shall obtain from the People whatever benefit he is deemed

worthy of; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand it in the [sanctuary of Dionysos];

and for the making and erection of the stele the administrator

shall allocate the expenditure accrued.
