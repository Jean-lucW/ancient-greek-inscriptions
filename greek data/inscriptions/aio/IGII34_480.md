# Choregic dedication of Aisios for the Thargelia, 365/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Front

Aisios son of Mnesioboulos of Sphettos,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Akamantis (and)

Pandionis in the boys' competition, Eukles

was director, Eudamiskos was pipe-player,

Chion (365/4) was archon.

**Part 2**

Rear

Aisios son of Mnesioboulos of Sphettos,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Akamantis (and)

Pandionis in the boys' competition, Eukles

was director, Eudamiskos was pipe-player,

Chion (365/4) was archon.
