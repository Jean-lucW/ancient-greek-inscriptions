# Choregic dedication of Euthydemos for the City Dionysia, 342/1 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aigeis was victorious in the boys’ competition,

[Euthydemos] son of Stratokles [of Diomeia was sponsor],

[...] -s was director, [ [...] was pipe-player].
