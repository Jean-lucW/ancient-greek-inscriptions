# Dedication by troops at Rhamnous honouring the general, Phoxias of Perithoidai

## English translation

**Part 1**

The soldiers, the hidden agents and the select associates,

dedicated (this), having crowned the general Phoxias son of Leosthenes

of Perithoidai for his excellence and justice.
