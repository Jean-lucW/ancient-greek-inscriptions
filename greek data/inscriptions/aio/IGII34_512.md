# Choregic dedication from Thorikos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Democharides for comedy,

Speusiades was actor;

Demochares for tragedy,

-odoros was actor;

-ades for comedy,

[...] was actor.

[...] dedicated (this)

[to Dionysos?].
