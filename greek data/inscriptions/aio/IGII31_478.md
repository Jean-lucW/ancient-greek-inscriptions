# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] with a gold crown of [] drachmas, and he shall be

proxenos and benefactor of the Athenian

People, and his descendants; and he shall have

right of ownership of land and a house, according to the law, and

the [...] shall take care of him, and

the Council and the generals, so that

he suffers no harm; and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on the acropolis; and for

inscribing the stele the treasurer of the People

shall give 20 drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
