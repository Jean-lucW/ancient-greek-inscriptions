# Honours for Livia and Augustus

## English translation

**Column 1**

The People (erected this statue of)

Livia Drusilla,

wife of Emperor

Caesar

**Column 2**

The People (erected this statue of)

Emperor Caesar,

son of the God Julius,

their own saviour

and benefactor.
