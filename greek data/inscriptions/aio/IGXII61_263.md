# Dedication to Apollo at Delphi by Athenian cleruchs on Samos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of] -kritos

the Athenian People

on Samos dedicated

a golden crown bestowed

as a prize of valour

to Apollo,

weight

of the gold:

twenty Darics,

an obol, a

half obol, [...]

sacred delegates
