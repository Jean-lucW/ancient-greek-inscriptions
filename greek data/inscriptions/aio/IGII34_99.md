# Dedication by a court president (<i>thesmothetes</i>), 253/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Deinias son of Kephisodotos of Boutadai

having been court president in the archonship of

Pheidostratos (253/2) dedicated (this).

**Column 1**

In olive crown

[The Council]

**Column 1**

In olive crown

The People
