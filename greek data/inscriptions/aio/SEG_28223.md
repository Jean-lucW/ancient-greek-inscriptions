# Statue base for Vedia Io

## English translation

The Council of the Areopagos

erected (this statue of) Vedia

Io daughter of Vedius

Prophantos of Argos,

Panhellene,

who was competition director

of the Nemea,

and of

Claudia Artemo,

her father having

requested

it.
