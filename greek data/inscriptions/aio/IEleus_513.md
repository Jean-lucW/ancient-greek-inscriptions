**Fragment b**

[...] and

having taken part in the Mysteries

in order that through that

I could rightly agree also to be

a Eumolpid, and could also accept

the title of archon,

as you thought appropriate,

in order that the secrets of the ceremony of the

Mysteries may be rendered to the Goddesses

in an even more splendid

and solemn manner,

if they receive some additional support,

also by aid of the archon of the

genos of the Eumolpidai whom you

have elected, and to avoid that I myself,

having been enrolled beforehand

among the Eumolpidai,

might seem now to refuse the service that belongs with

the honour, the fruits of which I enjoyed ahead of

this office.

Be well.
