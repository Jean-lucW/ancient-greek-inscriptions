# Marker of property used as security for a loan (Artemis Kolainis, Myrrhinous)

## Greek text

**Part 1**

ὅρος

χωρίου

καὶ οἰκίας πεπραμένων

ἐπὶ λύσει κοινῶι Μυρρινοσ[ί]ων ἱεροῦ ἀργυ[ρί]ου Ἀρτέμιδο[ς]

Κολαινίδος

## English translation

**Part 1**

Boundary marker

of land

and a house

sold

subject to redemption to the community

of the Myrrhinousians

for the sacred fund

of Artemis

Kolainis.
