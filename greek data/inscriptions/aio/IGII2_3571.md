# Areopagos, Council, and People honour T. Coponius Maximus

## English translation

The most awesome Council of the Areopagites and the Council of the 600 and

Below moulding:

the People (erected this statue of) the priest of the People and the Graces and

competition-director of the Great Caesarea, [Titus Coponius]

Maximus of Hagnous, Stoic successor,

son of the sacred herald of the two goddesses Titus Coponius Maximus

of Hagnous, on account of his excellence, Hermaios (son of Hermaios) of Kolonai

having managed it.

The City (erected this statue).
