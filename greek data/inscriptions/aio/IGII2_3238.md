# Honours for Livia as the goddess Augustan Foresight

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Julia the goddess Augustan Foresight (was erected by)

The Council of the Areopagos and the Council

of the Six Hundred and the People,

Dionysios son of Aulos of Marathon

having dedicated it from his own resources,

when the market-inspectors

were the same Dionysios of Marathon

and Quintus Naevius

Rufus of Melite.
