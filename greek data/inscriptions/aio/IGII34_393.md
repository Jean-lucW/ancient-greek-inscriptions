# Dedication by an (ephebic) Competition-director, 26/5 BC ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

having been Competition-director in the year that

Diotimos of Halai was archon (26/5 BC?) ,

(dedicated this) to Apollo, in the

trainership of Philios son of Philios

of Phrearrhioi [the younger],

in the assistant-trainership

of Apollonides son of Philios

of Phrearrhioi.
