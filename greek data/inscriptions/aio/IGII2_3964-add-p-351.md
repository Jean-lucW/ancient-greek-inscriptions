# Honours for Titus Flavius Glaukias

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos Council

and the Council

of the 500 and the People (erected this statue of)

Titus Flavius G[laukias]

of Acharnai.

Glaukias’ son [bearing great repute from his ancestors],

once his mother Athen[ais’ hope, but now her woe],

[having completed only one year] past seventeen,

fate has taken you, [dearest of youths, into the Dark],

but a great city, [having been allotted the grief of your death],

assigns immortal honours [with this image]

uninscribed space

(Work) of Antiphon.

uninscribed space
