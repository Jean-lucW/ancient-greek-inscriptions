meat

half the price (of meat)

same price as meat

same price as meat

beef, for a foot: 1 obol

for liver and spleen,

per mina: same price as meat

half the price (of meat)

In the archonship

of Pammenes,

Aischylos son of Aischylos of Hermos,

and the stand for the scales

in accordance with the order

of the board (?) according to the law

pork
