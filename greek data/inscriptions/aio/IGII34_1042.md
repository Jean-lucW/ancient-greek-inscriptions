# Building dedicated to [Demeter and the Girl] and Augustan Justice and the People

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The priestess of Demeter and the Girl, Kleo] daughter [of Eukles of Phlya], but by birth of Nikodemos of Hermos

[and [...] ] and priest of Augustan Justice

[dedicated this to Demeter and the Girl and Augustan] Justice and the People.
