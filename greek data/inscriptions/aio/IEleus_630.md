# Statue base of Fulvius Plautianus

## English translation

(This statue of) ⟦…⟧

kinsman of the greatest and

most divine emperors Lucius Septimius

Severus Pius

Pertinax Augustus and Marcus Aurelius

Antoninus and Publius Septimius Geta

[Caesar], the two-time consul Fulvius

⟦[Plautianus], patron and⟧

[benefactor] (was erected by) the City.
