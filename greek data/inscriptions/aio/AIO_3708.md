## English translation

**Part 1**

[- proposed:] the

[demesmen] shall decide: since those of the demesmen

who served among the select troops

were good men

and when the enemy

[...] walls at night

[...] in battle and repelled

them, and killed and captured

[not a few of them alive?], and

having stripped them of their weapons, they brought them up

to the [acropolis], and in other respects they

have continued to be well-disciplined

among the select troops, the demesmen

shall decide to praise and

crown each of them

with a gold crown of 500 drachmas for their

[excellence] and manly virtue

[...]
