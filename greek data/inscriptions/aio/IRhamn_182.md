# Accounts of Nemesis at Rhamnous

## English translation

**Part 1**

Year 1

In the demarchy

of Autokleides,

of the money

of Nemesis,

total of that

with those

owing the

two hundred drachmas:

37,000 drachmas; of the

other money

of Nemesis,

total:

12,729 dr.,

3 obols.

Year 2

In the period of office

of Mnesiptolemos,

total of all the

sacred money:

51,397 dr., 5 ob.

Year 3

In the period of office

of Nausimenes, total of the

sacred money

with those who have the

two hundred drachmas: 37,000 dr.;

of the rest: 11,723 dr.,

2 ob.

Year 4

In the period of office

of Euainetos, total of the

three hundred drachmas:

13,500 dr.; of the whole:

55,712 dr. 1 ob.

Year 5

Under the demarch Demophanes,

total with the religious officials:

5,206 dr. 4 ob.;

of the three hundred drachmas:

14,400 dr.; of the two hundred drachmas:

37,000 dr.;

of the whole: 56,606 dr. 4 ob.
