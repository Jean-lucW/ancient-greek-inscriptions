# Dedication of a statue of Asklepios to Egyptian gods (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Eukarpos son of Dionysios

of Phyle

on command

set up this (statue of)

Asklepios

on behalf of himself and

the priest bearer of the image of Iakchos,

son of Dionysios of Marathon,

when Aemilius

Attikos of Melite was in charge

of the vestments.
