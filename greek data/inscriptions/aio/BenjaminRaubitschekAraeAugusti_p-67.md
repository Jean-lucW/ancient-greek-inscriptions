# Honours for Augustus

## Greek text

**Part 1**

ὁ δῆ[μος]

[Αὐτ]οκράτο[ρα Καίσαρα]

[Θ]εοῦ υἱὸν [Σεβαστόν]

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People

(erected this statue of) Emperor Caesar

son of a god, [Augustus].
