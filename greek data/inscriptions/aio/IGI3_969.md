# Choregic dedication from Anagyrous

## English translation

**Part 1**

Sokrates dedicated (this),

Euripides was director.

**Part 2**

**Subcolumn 1**

Members of the tragic chorus:

Python

Echekles

Menalkes

Philokrates

Echyllos

Meletos

Emporion

**Subcolumn 2**

Amphidemos

Euthydikos

Lysias

Son

Kritodemos

Charias

Phaidon
