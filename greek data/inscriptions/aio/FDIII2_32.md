# Record of the tripod-bearer, Amphikrates, in the fourth Hellenistic <i>Pythais</i> at Delphi, 98/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

For the Good Fortune of the Council and the People of the Athenians.

In the archonship of Mentor at Delphi and in Athens

of Argeios (97/6) , (the one who) took the sacred tripod from Delphi and

carried it away, and led the fire-bearer (was) Amphikrates

son of Epistratos of Athens.
