# Decree of a deme

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[since [...] of the]

and [...]

of the construction [...]

of the appointed demesmen (?) [...]

and has taken care of the common property for the demesmen [...]

and always takes care of those things

for which the demesmen make him responsible

and constantly says and does what is

best in regard both to matters that are sacred and to those that are divinely sanctioned

incorruptibly and declares that

he will repair the rest of the sanctuaries

and will make dedications in the sanctuaries

paying the demesmen in advance

from his own funds on the basis of (i.e. in expectation of reimbursement from)

the contributions which the demesmen

make from the office which each is allotted towards the

construction of the sanctuaries and the dedications that

they may make and for the establishment of

the for the sake of their own health and

the preservation [of the People?] [...]
