# Divine honours for Geta, 209/10 AD

## English translation

[In the archonship] of Flavius Diogenes of Marathon (209/10) , prytany of Pandionis ,

[4th] prytany, for which Rhodon son of Kallistos of Marathon was secretary.

Priest Theophilos was chairman. Of the presiding committee,

Ioulianos son of Kasios was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. 23rd day of the

prytany, month of Poseideon 15th day. A Council was gathered on the

good news of ⟦Emperor Caesar Publius⟧

⟦Septimius Geta Pius Augustus⟧ having been appointed, at which the

[proposal] of the boards was read out through the officials,

the general Alkamenes of Lamptrai having read out the proposal which is inscribed here:

The Council of the Areopagos and the Council of the 500 and the People of

the Athenians, with the officials – the eponymous archon Flavius Diogenes

of Marathon, and the hoplite general and manager of the

gymnasiarchy of the God Hadrian and deputy archon of the most sacred

Attic Panhellenion Marcus Aurelius Alkamenes of Lamptrai, and the

herald of the Council of the Areopagos and competition director of the

games of the Augustan [House?] Tryphon son of Theophilos of Hybadai –

present a proposal according to ancestral custom: since the most sacred and

perfect of all days, the one hoped for by all, through the immortal harmony

of the holy kings, Lucius Septimius Severus Pius Pertinax

Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus Parthicus Maximus and Marcus

Aurelius Antoninus Pius Augustus, has been revealed

to all people [by a shared announcement] from the greatest [kings], (i.e. the day) on which

they received ⟦the most divine Emperor Publius Septimius Geta⟧

⟦Pius Augustus⟧ into an equal partnership in the office of Emperor

by means of a heavenly vote and judgement, establishing

their whole family in the kingship of the Universe,

the Council of the Areopagos and the Council of the 500 and the People of the Athenians

shall decide to sacrifice in every genos and to hold a festival in common and

[in private for the whole] royal family, and also to celebrate the day

as the birthday of the greatest [office], in accordance with their other

greatest (days), just as we have learnt from the holy pronouncement, and to give

a distribution [to the whole People], just as [their] mighty legate

[and propraetor] and curator of our fatherland Gaius

[Licinnius Telemachos] has also made clear in the preceding edict

... piety, through which we will make clear the city's good cheer

and habitual piety towards their Olympian

house.
