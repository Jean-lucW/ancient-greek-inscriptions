# The Artists of Dionysos honour Hadrian

## English translation

[(This statue of) Emperor] Caesar

[Trajan] Hadrian Augustus

[Olympios (?)], New Dionysos (was erected by)

[the itinerant] association

from the whole inhabited world

[of sacred-victor], crown-wearing

[artists and their] fellow

competitors, by means of

–ktes of Tarsos

and Eukrates of Side,

in the archonship of –

Menandros of –
