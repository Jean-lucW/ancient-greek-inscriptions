## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) , on the second of Skirophorion, [at the principal assembly].

-sinos proposed: since [those appointed?] treasurer and joint managers

have managed the sacrifices [for which they were responsible] well and with love of honour and [the other matters]

relating to the society of the thiasotai and [have presented accounts of all their expenses ?],

for good fortune, the thiasotai shall decide to praise the managers

and crown each of them with a foliage crown [for their love of honour and]

justice: Nikias treasurer, -sias manager, [...] [manager], [...]

[Ophelio?]n manager, Stratokles secretary; [and to praise also the priest,?]

-doros; and the treasurer shall assign to them [...] [15? drachmas]

for a dedication; [and the managers, having received the money, shall dedicate in the?]

sanctuary of Bendis [...]

Nikias

Names of other honorands not preserved
