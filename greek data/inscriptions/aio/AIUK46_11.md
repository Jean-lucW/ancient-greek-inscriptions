# Bilingual grave stele of Artemidoros of Sidon

## Greek text

Ἀρτεμίδωρος

Ἡλιοδώρου

Σιδώνιος.

(rosette) (rosette)

MṢBT SKR BḤYM L‘BDTNT BN

‘BDŠMŠ HṢDNY

## English translation

**Part 1**

Artemidoros

(son) of Heliodoros

of Sidon.

(rosette) (rosette)

A stele to the memory among the living of Abdtanit,

son of Abdshamash, the Sidonian.
