# Decree honouring Iatrokles

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis beside the stele of his father;

and also to choose as an envoy

one man from all Athenians, to

report to Iatrokles the good will

which the People has towards him, and

[...] of the remaining money (?)

[...] shall take care of with Iatrokles (?)

[...] so that it may be secured;

and the treasurer of the People shall give

to the elected envoy

20 drachmas travelling expenses,

and also for the stele another 20 drachmas

to the prytany secretary, from

the People’s fund for expenditure on decrees;

and to invite the man who has come from

Iatrokles to hospitality in the

city hall tomorrow; and D[iotimos?]

the general shall take care of the transport of [...] ,

[...] so that [...] as safely as possible [...]

[...]
