# Dedication by a victor at the Theseia (<i>pareutaktoi</i>) and at the Epitaphia

## English translation

**Column 1**

[...]

[...] of Probalinthos

having won the torch-race

of the pareutaktoi

at the Theseia

(dedicated this) to Apollo

in the gymnasiarchy

of Syndromos

son of Kallikratides

of Steiria.

**Column 2**

[...]

[...] of Probalinthos

having won the torch-

race of the men

at the Epitaphia

(dedicated this) to Apollo

in the gymnasiarchy

of Syndromos

son of Kallikratides

of Steiria.
