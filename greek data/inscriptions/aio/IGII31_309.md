# Honours for the Elaiousians

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Nikomachos (341/0) , in the

seventh prytany, of Pandionis .

On the twenty-ninth of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee

Aristomachos of Oion was putting to the vote. Onesippos

of Araphen was secretary.

The People decided. Hippostratos son of

Etearchides of Pallene proposed: what

the People have decreed for the Chersonesians

is to apply also to the Elaiousians,

and the general Chares

is to take care of them in the same

way, so that the Elaiousians may possess

what is theirs and live on a fair and

just basis with the Athenians in

the Chersonese; and to invite the

Elaiousians to dinner in the city hall

tomorrow.
