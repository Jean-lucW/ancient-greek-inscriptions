# Inventory of the Asklepieion (329/8 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gold finger-ring, not weighed, which Xenokrates dedicated, in a case; silver crown which Dion dedicated (it is not

there); Diopeithes dedicated 50 dr. on a tablet; Kallimachos dedicated 40 dr. on a tablet on the wall; Mnesarete dedicated 10 dr.

(It is short 3 dr. He said that Diokles of Myrrhinous must repay these. It is missing another 1 dr. which is with Telesias); Kallisto

dedicated 2 dr. on the lintel.; Aischylides dedicated 1 dr. 3 ob. attached to a ribbon, another 1 dr. on a tablet; small face

in a shrine on the wall; Demostrate dedicated a bronze oenochoe; Hedyle dedicated a bronze oenochoe; Nikias dedicated a shell made of Parian marble;

Enporion dedicated 12 dr. on a tablet; Onasos dedicated 12 dr. on a little tablet (It is short 4 dr. This shortfall was repaid

by Polyxenos on his behalf); wooden incense burner, one cubit high, gold-plated; Mynnion dedicated 5 dr. (the priest Eunikides

of Halai said that these are old); Diodote dedicated 10 dr. and a gold-plated leg; cloak; wooden throne. The following were dedicated in

the priesthood of Diokles, archonship of Theophrastos (340/39) : Silver: miscellaneous 4 dr; Pasilea dedicated 20 dr. in a case on

the wall; Diphilos dedicated a silver face and 10 dr. of silver on a tablet on the wall; Python dedicated

40 dr. on a tablet next to Antigona’s tablet; Hegemon of Athmonon dedicated 60 dr. attached to a ribbon; Klymene dedicated 74 dr. 3 ob.

on a tablet; Nikomachos dedicated 10 dr. on a tablet; Kallias son of Kallippos of Rhamnous dedicated a silver libation bowl, not weighed, in a case, weight

inscribed on it as 250 dr.; bowl that Aristophon of Azenia dedicated, not weighed, weight inscribed on it as 100 dr.; silver bowl,

which Philon dedicated, unweighed, in a case; Telesarchos of Cholargos dedicated a silver cup, not weighed, weight inscribed on it

as 62 dr. 3 ob., small images of Nike on it; little silver mug in a case, which Theano dedicated, not weighed; small silver cup, not weighed, which Phile

dedicated, weight is inscribed as 50 dr.; Oinanthe dedicated 2 dr. attached to a ribbon; Meletos dedicated a silver heart and a small snake

on a tablet. Bronze dedications: Philon of Phaleron dedicated a two-kados vessel; Pamphile dedicated a little tripod; Timoxenos dedicated an mast-head cup; chalcedony stones,

5; a glass finger-ring; glass seals, 5; grey cloak. The following were dedicated in the archonship of Lysimachides (339/8) , priesthood

of Polyxenos : Gold: weight 1 dr.; Silver: miscellaneous 5 dr., 1 ob.; Chresimos dedicated 20 dr.; Philon dedicated a little offering-tray, not weighed,

bound with a golden chain, weight is inscribed as 1 dr. ½ ob.; silver bowl, not weighed, which Theodoros dedicated, next to the

wooden cicada which has been gilded; 2 gold drachmai; Klearete dedicated 20 dr. on a tablet; Phile dedicated 40 dr.

in a case; Aristodoros dedicated 2 dr. in a case; gold eyes, not weighed, on a tablet, which Lysis dedicated; Hegesias

of Sounion dedicated two triobols on the wall; Menippe dedicated a little offering-tray on a tablet, not weighed; gazelle knucklebones,

bound with silver, 10 dr.; 2 strigils bound with a bronze chain; two more – one bronze, the other iron, of which the first is made of brass;

iron finger-ring bound with a bronze chain; Ameino dedicated a gold-plated chalcedony seal, bound with a bronze

chain; iron finger-ring with silver plating; 4 little seals; ingots, one made of glass; mast-head cup, which Kleino

dedicated, bronze; 16 iron finger-rings and a key with five teeth; oenochoe from the hand of the statue

of Polykritos; 2 gilt bronze crowns; composite seal with gold through the middle, which Aristagora dedicated; chalcedony seal glued to a carnelian; onyx seal. In the priesthood of Teisias, and archonship of Chairondas (338/7) : 2 gold-plated eyes;

one tetradrachm; silver relief on a tablet; 2 gold-plated eyes; 3 pairs of women’s sandals;

box with a conical lid; tablet of Parian marble; round box of Parian marble and an aulos-pipe, which Ias dedicated; 15 drachmai in an artificial flower;

5 more which Hermon dedicated; little gold-plated knucklebone bound with a bronze chain; a strigil; oil-flask; 3

iron strigils; leather oil-flask; oil-flask bound with a chain; iron strigil; 2 oil-flasks; iron strigil

a small bronze cupping implement; bronze strigil with an iron handle; 2 iron strigils; iron strigil with a oil-flask,

iron strigil; little glass mug; woollen cushion; round box in a wooden case; a lyre;

round box, of Parian marble, which – dedicated; 4 eyes, of which two are silver and two gold, which Phanis
