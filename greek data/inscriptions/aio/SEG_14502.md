# Honours for Julius Caesar

## English translation

**Part 1**

The people of the Athenians (erected this statue of) Gaius Julius,

son of Gaius, Caesar, pontifex maximus and commander,

and consul for the second time, the

[common] benefactor of the Greeks,

uninscribed space

[when the manager of the island] was Agathostratos son of Dionysios of Pallene.
