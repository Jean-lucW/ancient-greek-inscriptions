# Statue base for Casianus Antiochos Synesios

## Greek text

**Fragment a**

ἀγαθῇ τύχῃ,

Κασιανὸν Ἀντίοχ[ον]

τὸν καὶ Συνέσιον,

τὸν ἐπὶ τοῦ Μουσίο[υ],

Πανελλήνων ἄρχον[τα],

[ὁ Ἄ]ριος πάγος ἡ β[ουλὴ]

καὶ ὁ δῆμος folium

## English translation

For Good Fortune,

(this statue of) Casianus Antiochos

also called Synesios,

in charge of the Mouseion,

archon of the Panhellenes,

(was erected by) the Areopagos, the Council,

and the People.
