# Honours for the priestess of Athena Polias

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] for good fortune, the Council shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted to preside

at the forthcoming Assembly shall put the matter on the

agenda and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council to accept

the good things [that the priestess says? ] occurred in the

sacrifices that she made for the health and preservation of the Council

and the People and children and women

and [king Demetrios] and queen

[Phthia] and their descendants; and since the priestess

[of Athena] took care well and with love of honour

of the adornment of the table according to

tradition and the other things which the laws

and decrees of the People prescribed, and continues

at every opportunity to be honour-loving towards the

goddess, and in the archonship of Alkibiades (237/6) she dedicated

from her own resources a Theran and [...]

and a garment of plaited hair; and contributed to the Praxiergidai

a hundred drachmas for the ancestral sacrifice from

her own resources; so, therefore, that the People

may be seen to be honouring those who rate most highly

piety to the gods, to praise

the priestess of Athena Polias -te

daughter of Polyeuktos of Bate and

crown her with a foliage crown for her piety

towards the goddess; and to praise also her husband

Archestratos son of Euthykrates of Amphitrope

and crown him with a foliage crown

for his piety towards the goddess and love of honour

towards the Council and People; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand it

[on the acropolis] [...]
