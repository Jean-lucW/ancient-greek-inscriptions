# Choregic dedication from Acharnai

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [ sponsor's name? ] having been victorious dedicated (this),

[...] with a chorus and in comedy (?). Chares of Thebes

was pipe-player, Speuseades of Athens [was director].
