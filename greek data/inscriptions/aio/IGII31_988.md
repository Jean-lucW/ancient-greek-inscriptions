# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Antimachos (256/5) , in the fifth prytany,

of Kekropis , for which Chairigenes son of Chairigenes

of Myrrhinous was secretary. On the [old and new day]

of Maimakterion, the thirtieth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee -

son of - of Philaidai was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

- son of -piodoros of Anaphlystos proposed:
