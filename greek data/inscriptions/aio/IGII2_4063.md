# Statue base for Vibullia Alkia

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Vibullia Alkia

wife of Tiberius Claudius Attikos Herodes

of Marathon

the tribe of

Pandionis dedicated

on account of her

excellence and her

goodwill towards

her fatherland.
