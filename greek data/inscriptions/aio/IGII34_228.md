# Dedication (Tetrakomoi), 330/29 BC

## English translation

**Face a**

The Xypeteans were victorious,

Aristophon was archon (330/29) ,

komarchs:

uninscribed space

Philton son of Aischytos,

Pamphilos son of Aischytos,

Peisidamas son of Peisides,

Lykinos son of Lykon.

**Face b**

The competitors (?): Pamphilos son of Aischytos,

Philton son of Aischytos,

Kephisios son of Kephisios,

Philaios son of Philon,

Pytheas son of -.
