# Decree of Rhamnous and Athenian residents honouring Endios of Aithalidai, 256/5 BC?

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] managed well

and with love of honour the guarding of the fort and those

living in it, doing what was beneficial for the

deme and the citizens and others

living at Rhamnous, for good fortune, the Rhamnousians

and those of the citizens living in Rhamnous

shall decide, to praise Endios son of Aischeas of Aithalidai and

crown him with with a foliage crown for his excellence

and justice towards them; and inscribe this

decree on a stone stele [...]
