# Dedication by trierarchs, 357/6 BC ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

The trierarchs dedicated [to Athena, having been crowned]

by the Council and People [in the archonship of Agathokles (357/6) ?]

those who [sailed] to the Hellespont [with Chares?]

Teisippos son of S- of -

Periandros son of Polyaratos of Cholargos

Habronides son of M- of [Kopros]

Polyeuktos son of - of Lamptrai

Aristokles son of - of Eleusis

Cheimeus son of Kikon of Lakiadai

Diokles son of Diopeithes of Angele

Arrheneides son of Charikles of Paiania.
