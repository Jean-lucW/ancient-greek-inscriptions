# Dedication to Asklepios by a group of men

## English translation

**Part 1**

On epistyle

[...] [Th]odorides, Epeuches, Mnesitheos

Antiphilos

in olive crown

Sostratos

son of Epikrates

in olive crown

Epeuches

son of Dieuches

in olive crown

Diakritos

son of Dieuches

in olive crown

Mnesitheos

son of Mnesitheos (dedicated this).
