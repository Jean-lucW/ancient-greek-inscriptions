# Dedication by an ephebic instructor, 333/2-331/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Theophanes son of Hierophon of Rhamnous dedicated (this) to Hermes having been crowned

by the ephebes and their controllers and superintendents.

**Column 1**

In crown

The ephebes

in the archonship of

Nikokrates (333/2)

**Column 2**

In crown

The ephebes

in the archonship of

Niketes (332/1)

**Column 3**

In crown

The ephebes

in the archonship of

Aristophanes (331/0)
