# Honours for Gaius Julius Sabinus

## English translation

**Part 1**

With good fortune,

uninscribed space

in accordance with a memorandum

of the Council

of the Areopagos

(this statue was erected of) Gaius Julius

Sabinus, Platonic

philosopher.
