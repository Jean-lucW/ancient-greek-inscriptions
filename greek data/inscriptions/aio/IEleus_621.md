# Statue base for the hearth-initiate Publia Aelia Herennia

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune.

For the two goddesses,

the from the hearth Publia

Aelia Herennia , daughter of Publius Aelius

Apollonios who served as

eponymous archon and served

as king archon and

served as hoplite

general and took care

of the gymnasiarchy and

was herald of the Council of the

Areopagos; her mother, Publia

Aelia Herennia dedicated her, the descendant of Konon

and Kallimachos, next to her

great-uncle, Publius Aelius Dionysios, the

dadouch.
