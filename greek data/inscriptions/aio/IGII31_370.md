# Decree about colonising expedition to the Adriatic

## English translation

**Column 1**

Decree in accordance with which

Miltiades took over the

triremes and quadriremes

and the triaconters

and their equipment.

Kephisophon son of Lysiphon

of Cholargos proposed:

for the good fortune of the Athenian

People, so that

the decisions of the People

may be carried out as swiftly as possible

concerning the colony

to the Adriatic, the People

shall resolve: that the

supervisors of the shipyards

shall hand over to the trierarchs

the ships and the equipment

in accordance with the decisions of the

People, and the trierarchs

who have been appointed shall

bring their ships up along to the

[jetty] in the month

of Mounichion before the tenth

and present them

prepared for

sailing; and the first to

bring his ship along, let

the People crown him with a gold

crown of 500 drachmas;

and the second with one of 300

drachmas; and the third

with one of 200 drachmas; and

let the herald of the Council announce

the crowns at the competition of the Thargelia,

and the receivers

shall allocate the money

for the crowns, so that the

love of honour of the trierarchs

towards the People may be apparent;

and in order that the

pleas for exemption may also be heard,

the court presidents shall

fill the courts to

two hundred and one for the

general elected

in charge of the symmories, in the

month of Mounichion on the

second and the

fifth, and

the treasurers of the goddess

shall provide the payment

for the courts according to the

law; and in order that the People

may have for all

time their own markets and

grain transport, and by the

establishment of their own naval station

may have protection against

the Etruscans, and Miltiades

the founder and the settlers

may have use of their own

navy, and those Greeks

and barbarians who

sail the sea

may sail [safely?] to

the Athenian [market?]

[bringing grain?] and other things

[...] that

**Column 2**

[...] but if anyone, of whom each of these things

have been required, whether

official or private citizen, does not act in accordance with this

decree, let whoever does not so act owe

ten thousand drachmas

sacred to Athena, and let the

scrutineer and his assistants

compulsorily condemn them,

or owe it themselves;

and the Council of

500 shall supervise the despatch,

punishing any

trierarchs who are disorderly

according to the laws;

and the prytany shall hold

a session of the Council on the jetty

concerning the despatch,

continuously, until the despatch

has taken place; and the People

shall elect as despatchers

ten men from

all the Athenians; and those

elected shall supervise

the despatch,

as required by the Council;

and it shall be permitted

for the Council and the prytany

who have supervised the

despatch to be crowned

by the People with a gold

crown of 1,000 drachmas;

and if this decree

lacks anything in relation to the

despatch, the Council

shall be empowered to vote on it,

so long as they do not undo any of what

has been voted by the People;

and all these things shall be

for the protection of the country.
