# List of officials organizing the second Hellenistic <i>Pythais</i> at Delphi, 128/7 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

In the archonship of Dionysios (128/7) in Athens,

the nine officials who led the Pythais:

Archon: Dionysios son of Demetrios

King: Heliodoros son of Diodotos

Polemarch: Glaukos son of Lysandros

Court Presidents:

Herakleides son of Herakleides

Euktemon son of Kallias

Boukattes son of Lykinos

Mnesitheos son of Hagnias

Pamphilos son of Nikokles

[...] son of [...]

**Face-right b**

Herald of

the Council

of the Areopagos:

Mnasikles

son of Mnasikles.

uninscribed space

hieromnemon:

traces
