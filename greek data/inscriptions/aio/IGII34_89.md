# Dedication by a court president (<i>thesmothetes</i>), 319/8 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

Phili[pp- son of – of –]

who was a court president dedicated this

in the archonship of Apollodoros (319/8) .

**Column 1**

in olive crown:

The Council

**Column 2**

in olive crown:

The People

**Column 3**

in olive crown:

The tribe

**Face-left b**

**Column 4**

in olive crown:

The deme.
