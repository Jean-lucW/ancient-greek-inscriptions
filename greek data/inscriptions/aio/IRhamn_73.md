# Decree of Rhamnous (and Athenian residents?) honouring Theangelos of Diomeia, ca. 300-250 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods

The Rhamnousians decided, Ankylos [son of - of Rhamnous proposed]:

since Theangelos son of Oiax of Diomeia [continues to be well disposed towards the]

[deme] of the Rhamnousians and [...]

[...] of the Rhamnousians [...]

[...] [and]

[crown] Theangelos [son of Oiax of Diomeia with a gold crown, because]

[he managed the guarding of the fort] well and with love of honour [...]

of them [...]

[...] [and inscribe the decree on a stone stele and stand it]

[in the] agora; [and choose three men to be responsible for setting it up]

[These were chosen:] Ariston of Eitea, [ - of - ],

- of Rhamnous. uninscribed space
