# Catalogue of ephebes, 61/2 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

In pediment

For the good fortune of the victory of ⟦[Nero]⟧ Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus (54-68 )

In the archonship and priesthood of Drusus the consul of Thrasyllos (61/2) , when Theogenes was herald,

and when the hoplite general for the eighth time, high priest of Nero Claudius Caesar Germanicus

and Zeus of Freedom of the Greeks, manager of the city for life, priest of Delian Apollo,

manager of holy Delos, High Priest of the House of the Augusti, the best

of the Greeks, and lawgiver was Tiberius Claudius Novius of Oion.

As superintendent, Epiktetos son of Epiktetos of Eupyridai, having been honoured by the People for the beautiful and decorous manner in which he

both lives his own life and directed the conduct and decorousness of the ephebes, and having received

from the People dining rights in the City Hall for life, submitted these ephebes to the Metroon,

the trainer being Ariston son of Aphrodisios of Rhamnous, the leader being Artemon

son of Nikias of Pallene, the secretary being Areion son of Patrophilos of Kephisia, the weapons trainer being

Satyros son of Kleomenes of Phlya; the tutors were Erastos of Besa, Eutychides of Marathon,

Euphemos of Sypalettos, Gaios of Azenia, Demetrios of Sybridai, Zoilos of Phlya.
