# Choregic dedication for the City Dionysia, 327/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Kekropis] was victorious in the boys’ competition

[...] of Aixone was sponsor,

[...] of Opous was director,

[...] of Tegea was pipe-player, Hegemon (327/6) was archon.
