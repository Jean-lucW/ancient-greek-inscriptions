# Altar for Marcus Aurelius and Lucius Verus

## English translation

**Part 1**

For the victory and

health of the

divine and

brother-loving

Emperors

Marcus Aurelius

Antoninus

and Lucius

Aurelius Verus

uninscribed space

Gnaeus Licinius

Attikos of Gargettos having managed it.
