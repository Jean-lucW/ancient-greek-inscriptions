# Honorific decree of the Paraloi

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

The Paraloi decided. Since

Meixigenes continues to be

a good and just man

regarding the Paraloi, the Paraloi

shall decide, to crown

Meixigenes son of Mikon of Cholleidai

for his excellence and justice

towards the Paraloi

with a golden crown of -

drachmas; and that the Paraloi

will also always assign him a portion,

whenever they sacrifice to Paralos;

and to inscribe this decree on

a stone stele and set it up

in the Paralion; and the

managers in office in the

treasurership of Diophantos

are to take care of setting it up; and after they have given

an account of whatever they spend on

the inscription and erection of the stele, when the next

meeting takes place

they will be reimbursed by the Paraloi.
