# Honours for Thibron

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1

In the archonship of [Lykeas] (241/0) , in the - prytany, of Aiantis ,

for which - son of -on of Eleusis was secretary. On the -

of -, the thirteenth of the prytany. Principal

Assembly. Of the presiding committee - son of - of - was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. [...]

- proposed: since Thibron son of Philo-(?) of [...]

[...] to those residing [...]

[...] and of the [...]

[...] he undertook the management [...]

[...] collectively and as each [...]

[...] money for grain purchase [...]

[...] good will for the People [...]

so that there may be incentive for all to provide services to the People,

knowing that they will obtain thanks for their benefactions, for good

fortune, the People shall decide: to praise Thibron [...]

and to crown him with a gold crown according the law for his excellence

and good will for the Athenian People; and it shall be possible for him to

obtain further good from the People.
