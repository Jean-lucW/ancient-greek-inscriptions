# Honours for Tiberius Claudius Nero

## Greek text

**Part 1**

ἡ βο[υλὴ ἡ ἐξ Ἀρείου πά]γου Τε[βέριον Κλαύ]διον Νέρ[ω]να Eὐεργέτην δ[ιὰ] προγό νων τ[οῦ] δήμου

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council of the Areopagus

(erected this statue of) Ti[berius]

[Clau]dius Nero, benefactor

since his ancestors' time

of the People.
