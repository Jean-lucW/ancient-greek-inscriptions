# Honours for Julia Augusta [Artemis] Boulaia

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Julia Augusta [Artemis]

Boulaia, mother of Tiberius

[Caesar] Augustus (was erected by) [the People and]

the Council of the Areopagos.
