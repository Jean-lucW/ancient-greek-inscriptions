# Sacrificial regulation for the Thesmophoria at Cholargos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] sacred

remembrancers. Uninscribed space

Both (female) officials together

shall give to the priestess for

the festival and for managing

the Thesmophoria: a half-sixth

of barley, a half-sixth of wheat, a half-

sixth of barley groats, a half-sixth of

wheatmeal, a half-sixth of figs, a chous

of wine, half a chous of olive-oil, two

cups of honey, a choinix of white sesame,

a choinix of black, a choinix of poppy

seeds, two fresh cheeses (weighing) no

less than a stater each,

and two staters of garlic, and a torch

worth no less than two obols and

4 drachmas of silver. The (female) officials shall

give these things. And in order that this

will take place on behalf of the deme of Cholargos

according to what is written for all

time, those (male) in office in the archonship of

Ktesikles (334/3) shall erect a stele and inscribe this

decree on a stone stele in the Pythion,

and they shall give an account of their

expenditure to the demesmen of Cholargos.
