# Honours for Kalliteles of Kydantidai

## English translation

**Part 1**

Crown

In the archonship of Phrynichos (337/6) , in the

tenth prytany, of Pandionis , for which

Chairestratos son of Ameinias of Acharnai was

secretary. Of the presiding committee

Euthykrates of Aphidna was putting to the vote. The People

and the Council decided. Diophantos

son of Phrasikleides of Myrrhinous proposed: concerning the statement of

Kalliteles and the resolution of the People

that a proposal should be formulated for him, the

Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

shall put his case on the agenda at the next Assembly

and submit the opinion of the

Council to the People that it seems good

to the Council to praise Kalliteles son of

Kalliphanes of Kydantidai and crown [...]
