# Philemon and Pasippos Apollonios honour Hadrian

## English translation

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar

Trajan Hadrian

Olympios, the saviour

of our grandfather

Philemon, (was erected by) Philemon

and Pasippos <also called>

Apollonios

for their own benefactor,

through the  
management of their father

Polyouchos son of Kartidamas.
