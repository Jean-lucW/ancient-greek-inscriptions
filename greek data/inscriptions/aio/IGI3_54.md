# Reaffirmed alliance with Leontinoi

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods. The envoys from Leontinoi

who made the

alliance and the

oath: Timenor son of Agathokles,

Sosis son of Glaukias, Gelon

son of Exekestos; secretary

Theotimos son of Tauriskos.

In the archonship of Apseudes (433/2)

and the Council for which

Kritiades was secretary.

The Council and

the People decided. Akamantis

was in prytany. Charias was

secretary. Timoxenos

was chairman. Kallias

proposed: that there shall be the alliance

between the Athenians and

the Leontinians, and they shall

give and receive the oath.

The Athenians shall swear

as follows: ‘We shall be allies

of the Leontinians for all time,

without deceit and unharmfully.’

The Leontinians shall swear likewise:

‘We shall be allies

of the Athenians for all time,
