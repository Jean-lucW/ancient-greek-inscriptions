# Letter from Septimius Severus and Caracalla to the Panhellenion on the Panhellenia (201 AD)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Emperor Caesar, son of the God Marcus]

[Antoninus Pius Germanicus]

[Sarmaticus, brother of the God Commodus],

[grandson of the God Antoninus Pius, descendant of the God]

[Hadrian, and of the God Trajan Parthicus, and]

[of the God Nerva, Lucius Septimius]

[Severus Pius Pertinax]

[Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus Parthicus]

[Maximus, Pontifex Maximus, tribunician]

[power 10 times, acclaimed emperor 11 times],

[consul twice, designated consul for a third time, father]

[of the fatherland, proconsul, and Emperor]

[Caesar, son of Lucius Septimius] Severus Pius

[Pertinax Augustus Arabicus Adiabenicus]

[Parthicus Maximus, grandson of the God]

[Marcus Antoninus Pius Augustus]

Germanicus Sarmaticus, great-grandson

of the God Antoninus Pius, descendant

of the God Hadrian, and of the God Trajan Parthicus,

and of the God Nerva, Marcus Aurelius

Antoninus Pius Augustus, tribunician

power 4 times, designated consul (July-December 201) ,

proconsul, to the Panhellenion, greetings.

Cocceius Timasarchos, your archon,

made clear to us that some athletes,

having scorned the games, sailed past

Athens, and we renew [...] and
