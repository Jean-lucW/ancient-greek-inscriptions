# Decree of Aixone awarding honours connected with the festival of Hebe, 320/19 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Philaios (?) son of Chremes proposed: since the

religious officials allotted for the sanctuary of

Hebe took care justly and with love of honour

of the sacrifice for Hebe

and the other gods to whom they must

sacrifice, and have submitted a reckoning and accounts,

to crown each of them with a

foliage crown, Anticharmos son of Nauson

and Nearchos (?) son of Chairigenes,

Theodotos son of Aischron, Aristokles

son of Kalliphon, for their justice

and love of honour towards the demesmen;

and this decree shall be inscribed on a

stone stele and set up in the sanctuary

of Hebe by the demarch in office after

the archonship of Neaichmos (320/19) . Uninscribed space

And to praise also the controllers

and crown with a foliage crown

each of them, Kimon, ,

Pythodoros son of Pytheas, and the

herald Charikles, for their love of honour

concerning the all-night rite; and

to praise also the priest of the Herakleidai,

Kallias, and the priestess of Hebe and

Alkmene, and the archon Kallisthenes

son of Nauson and to crown

each of them for their piety and love of

honour towards the gods; and to inscribe

this decree on a stone stele

and stand it in the sanctuary of Hebe.
