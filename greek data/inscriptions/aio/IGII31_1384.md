# Citizenship decree for Hikesios of Ephesos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[in the trust] of king [Eumenes]; and in order therefore that

the People may also be seen to honour those who provide

services to it, for good fortune, the Council shall decide,

that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming

Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise Hikesios son of Metrodoros of Ephesos for his good will towards

the Athenians; and he shall also be granted citizenship, having been scrutinised

in the court in accordance with the law; and the court presidents,

when they next fill the court with five hundred and one

jurors, shall introduce the scrutiny of his enrolment as citizen,

even if he is not present; and when he has been scrutinised he may

be enrolled in the tribe and deme and phratry that he wishes;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis; and the treasurer

of the military fund shall allocate the expense accrued for it.

traces of a crown
