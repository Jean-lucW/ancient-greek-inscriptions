# Dedication commemorating the victories of a herald in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune

Valerius Eklektos, councillor, of Sinope,

and (citizen) of Athens, and councillor of Delphi,

and (citizen) of Elis, and councillor of Sardis

and councillor of Perge,

and councillor of Nikaia and citizen

and councillor of many other cities,

as herald twice victor in the festival circuit, having been

victorious in the sacred world games listed below:

at the Olympia in Pisa 2 times, at the Pythia in Delphi

2 times, at the Nemeia in Argos 3 times, at the Isthmia 4 times,

at the Panathenaia 4 times, at the Panhellenia, at the Shield-games in Argos,

at the Capitoline Games in Rome 3 times, at the Games of Athena Promachos in

Rome 3 times, at the Millennial Games in Rome, for which he was

honoured with a golden staff, first and unique

among the heralds of all time, at the Eusebeia in Puteoli 4 times, at the Sebasta

in Neapolis 4 times, at the Aktia in Nikopolis, at the Olympia in

Epidauros 2 times, at the Herakleia in Thebes 3 times, at the Trophonia

in Lebadeia 2 times, at the Olympia in Beroia 2 times, at the Pythia in

Philippopolis 2 times, at the Kentreiseia in Philippopolis,

at the Sebasta in Byzantium, at the Severan Games in Nikomedia 2 times,

at the Provincial Games of Bithynia in Nikomedia 2 times, at the Severan

Games in Nikaia 3 times, at the Chrysanthina in Sardis, at the Provincial Games of Asia

in Sardis, at the Pythia in Tralleis, at the Olympia in Ephesos 2 times,

at the Barbilleia in Ephesos 4 times, at the Hadrianeia in Ephesos, at the Olympia

in Smyrna 2 times, at the Hadrianeia-Olympia in Smyrna 2 times,

at the Provincial Games of Asia in Smyrna, at the Augusteia in Pergamon,

at the Olympia in Kyzikos, at the Didymeia in Miletos 2 times,

at the Pythia in Hieropolis, at the Aktia in Tyre, at the Sebasmeia

in Damascus 2 times, at the Purple Games in Sidon 2 times,

at the Capitoline Attaleia in Aphrodisias.

(Dedicated) by the voice-trainer Marcus Aurelius Mousaios, also known as

Heortasios, member of the Council of Elders of Sardis, councillor

of Delphi and of Elis and Aphrodisias, general-

secretary, honoured by the Eleians and Delphians

with statues as first and unique in voice-training.
