# Citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the Council shall decide,

that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise

-ntos son of Philostratos of - and crown him

with a foliage crown for his good will and love of honour

towards the People of the Athenians; and he shall be granted

citizenship in accordance with the law; and the court presidents,

when they have filled the court with five hundred and one

jurors, shall introduce the scrutiny

of his enrolment as citizen; and having been scrutinised, he may be

enrolled in the tribe and deme and phratry that he wishes;

and in order that there may be a reminder of the honour

bestowed by the People, the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and the treasurer of the military fund

shall allocate the expense accrued.
