# Sestos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Trajan Hadrian

Olympios Caesar Augustus

(was erected by) the Sestians for their own saviour

and founder, through the envoys

Titus Ragonius Porthmos and

Claudius Abias, the Councillors.
