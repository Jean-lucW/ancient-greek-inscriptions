# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia by a temple attendant (<i>zakoros</i>)

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Asklepios and Hygieia,

Demetrios son of Antiochos

of Sphettos,

having been temple attendant,

having paved the ground

of the gateway, dedicated this,

uninscribed space

under the management

of Coponius Maximus,

the temple attendant being –

son of Dio-

of -
