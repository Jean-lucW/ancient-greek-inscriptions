# Oracle of Hygieia and Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

From an oracle of Hygieia and Asklepios:

And a celestial snake [...] these statues of the gods,

to whom it is also necessary to sacrifice wineless sacrifices

on the fifteenth, during a moonlit night, about [midnight],

send a good taste to the good [gods].
