# Dion honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

In Latin

To Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan

Parthicus, grandson of the God Nerva

Trajan Hadrian Augustus,

pontifex maximus, tribunician power 16 times (132 AD) , consul 3 times, father of the fatherland,

Olympius

the colony Julia Augusta

Diensium (erected this statue), through an envoy,

Gaius Memmius Lycus

In Greek

Of the Dionians
