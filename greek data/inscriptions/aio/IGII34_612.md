# Dedication by an actor and herald commemorating competitive victories

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune

I, Titus Aelius Aurelius Apollonios

of Tarsos and Athens,

comic actor, herald and victor at the major games

including the Capitoline Games, having been victorious in the contest at the

Olympeia, dedicated

this statue.
