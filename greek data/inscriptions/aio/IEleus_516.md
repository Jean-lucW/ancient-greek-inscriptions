# Statue base for the hierophant who saved the sacred objects of the Mysteries

## English translation

**Fragment a**

You are looking at the displayer of solemn nights, also renowned for his wisdom,

the holy attendant of Deo and Kore,

who, evading once the unlawful work of the Sarmatians,

saved rites and life for the fatherland

and displayed the ceremonies and elevated their glory like

wise Eumolpos and very holy Keleos

and initiated the son of Ausonia, the very glorious Antoninus.
