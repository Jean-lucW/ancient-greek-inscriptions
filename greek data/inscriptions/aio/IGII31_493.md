# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the People shall decide: that they shall be

proxenoi and benefactors of the

Athenian People, both themselves

and their descendants; and they shall [also?] have, like

the other proxenoi

and benefactors, [right of ownership [...] ?];

and the Council and the generals

shall take care of them; and

the secretary of the Council shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis; and

for inscribing the stele let the

treasurer of the People give 20 drachmas from

the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees; and to praise them

and invite them to hospitality in the

city hall tomorrow.
