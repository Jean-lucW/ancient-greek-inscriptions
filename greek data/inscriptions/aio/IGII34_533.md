# Dedication by the competition-director, Theophanes of Euonymon, 270/69 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The People were the sponsor. Sosistratos was archon (270/69 BC) .]

[Competition-director Theophanes] son of Dioskourides [of Euonymon].

Tribe was victorious in the boys’ competition

[...] was pipe-player

Eraton of Arcadia was director.
