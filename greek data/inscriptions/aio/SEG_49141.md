**Part 1**

## English translation

**Part 1**

Hagnotheos son of Diodoros proposed:

since the treasurer and the religious officials

in the archonship of Ambrosios (290/89?)

performed their offices well and with love of honour,

and offered all the sacrifices,

and [...] the construction (?)
