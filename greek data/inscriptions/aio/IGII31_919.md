# Citizenship for Strombichos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] thinking that he should not stand in the way

of what was in the interests of the city but share responsibility

for its preservation, and he joined the People in the

siege of the Mouseion, and after affairs had been

concluded in favour of the city he has continued

to provide unstintingly its remaining needs and has remained steadfast

in his good will for the People, and when war came

he was a good man [...] and in his efforts he has served

in every way according to what the general ordered and

proclaims that for the rest of time he will render service

to the Athenian People; so that there may be competition

for all wanting to obtain benefits from the

People in the knowledge that they will be honoured by the People,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

who are allotted to preside in the People in the

forthcoming Assembly, when the days set by the law

have passed, shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the

Council to praise Strombichos and crown him with a gold

crown according to the law for his good will and

love of honour for the People; and he shall be Athenian,

and his descendants, and he shall enrol in the tribe

and deme and phratry that he wishes according to the

law; and the prytany shall put the vote about him

at the next Assembly; and the court presidents

shall bring the scrutiny for him of the award when

they next fill the courts [...]
