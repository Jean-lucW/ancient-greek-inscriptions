# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Olympiodoros for the [second year] (293/2) ,

when Epikouros son of Epiteles of Rhamnous

was recorder, in the eleventh

prytany, of Pandionis , on the old and new day of Mounichion,

the first of the prytany. Principal Assembly

in the theatre. Of the presiding committee Nikoboulos

son of Nikias of Phrearrhioi was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members.
