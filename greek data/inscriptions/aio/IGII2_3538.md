# Honours for Gaius Julius Spartiaticus

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Gaius Julius Spartiaticus,

high priest of the

Gods Augusti and

of the family of the Augusti

from the League of

Achaia for life, the first one

ever (was erected by)

the priest of Poseidon

Erechtheus Gaieochos,

Tiberius Claudius Theogenes

of Paiania for his own

friend.
