# Citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

court presidents, when they have filled the court with five hundred and one jurors,

shall introduce the scrutiny of their enrolment as citizens; and when they have been scrutinised

they may be enrolled in the tribe and deme and phratry that they wish;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the [acropolis?] [...] statues; and the

expense accrued for the inscribing of the stele and the setting up
