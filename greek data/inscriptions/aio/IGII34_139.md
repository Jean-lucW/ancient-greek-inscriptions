# Dedication to Apollo Hypo Makrais

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Apollo [Below the Long Rocks]

**Column 1**

In myrtle crown:

Having been secretary

of the

board (sunedriou), C[laud]ianus

[son of] P(ublius) Rufus (?) (dedicated this)

**Column 2**

In myrtle crown:

Having been polemarch,

Publius

Ventidius

Rufus (dedicated this).
