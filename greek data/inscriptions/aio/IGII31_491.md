# End of a citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] [the prytany shall put]

the vote about him at the next

Assembly; and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele the treasurer shall give

30 drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
