# Honours for the ephebic superintendent, 140/1 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune,

in the archonship of

Claudius Attalos (140/1 AD) ,

the superintendent

of the ephebes, priest of

the God and Goddess, Eirenaios

(son of Eirenaios) of Paiania.

His son,

Dionysios

son of Eirenaios of Paiania,

served as ephebe. The gymnasiarch

of the ephebes

for the year was

Flavius Stratolaos

of Phyle. There were

two hundred and two

ephebes.
