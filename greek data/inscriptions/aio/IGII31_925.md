# Honours for officials responsible for grain and Rhodians who have assisted them

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and to crown

each of them with a foliage crown

when they have submitted their

accounts, for their love of honour and

justice to the People;

and they shall have the rest of the things

which are given to those who manage affairs justly,

and the grant, which

the People have voted for those

[...] grain purchase

[...] ; and to praise

those whom they declare

have been useful to them

-os of Rhodes, Tim-

of Rhodes, Philinos of

Rhodes, -os of Rhodes, Pot-

of Rhodes, and to crown

each of them with a foliage crown

so that all may know

that the People understands how

to thank those who provide services

to it; and the prytany

secretary shall

inscribe this decree

on a stone stele [...]

[...]
