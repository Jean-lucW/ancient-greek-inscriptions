# Treaty with Mytilene, 347/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Themistokles (347/6) . The Council

and the People decided. Aigeis

was in prytany. Lysimachos son of Sosidemos of Acharnai was

secretary. Theophilos of Halimous was chairman.

Stephanos son of Antidorides of Eroiadai

proposed: concerning what the

envoys from Mytilene say, and the treasurer of the Paralos,

and what Phaidros the general has written in his letter,

the People shall decide: that the friendship and

alliance shall exist for the People of

Mytilene towards the Athenian People,

which the cities have arranged among themselves;

and the money from the allied levy allied levy

falling to the Mytileneans (?),
