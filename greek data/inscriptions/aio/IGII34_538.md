# Dedication by the competition-director, Drakontides of Paiania, 175/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The People were the sponsor. Sonikos was archon (175/4 BC) .]

[Competition-director Drakontides son of Drakon] of Paiania.

[ Tribe was victorious in the men’s competition.] [...] was pipe-player

[...] [was director].
