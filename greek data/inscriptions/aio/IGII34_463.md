# Choregic dedication of Euagides for the City Dionysia, 328/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aigeis was victorious in the men’s competition, Euagides son of Ktesias of Philaidai was sponsor,

Lysimachides of Epidamnos was pipe-player, Charilaos of Lokris was director,

Euthykritos (328/7) was archon.
