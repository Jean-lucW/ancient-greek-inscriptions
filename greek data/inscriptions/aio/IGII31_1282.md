# Honours for a courtier of king Seleukos IV

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Theoxenos (187/6) , in the tenth prytany, of

Erechtheis, for which Bioteles son of Leukios of Perithoidai was

secretary. On the eleventh of Mounychion, the thirteenth

of the prytany. Principal Assembly in the theatre.

Of the presiding committee Diognetos son of - of Cholargos

was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and the People decided.

Kteson son of Kallikles of Kollytos proposed: since - [is honoured?]

by king Seleukos and being of great distinction, is

well-disposed towards the People collectively, and individually to those [...]

coming to king Seleukos [...]
