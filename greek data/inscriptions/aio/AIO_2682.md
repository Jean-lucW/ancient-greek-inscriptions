## English translation

**F.delphes ⅲ 2 34**

God. For Good Fortune. In the archonship of Pyrrhos at Delphi, in Athens of

Dionysios after Lykiskos (128/7) , having won among the tribal cavalry commanders, first

of those who escorted the Pythais with the cavalry commander Diokles and his

fellow officials, when Diokles son of Diokles of Athens held the contest:

**Column 1**

crown to the left of the text

From the tribal cavalry commanders;

Double-length race

with a war

horse:

Hagias son of

Boulon of

Athens.

**Column 2**

crown to the left of the text

From the cavalry:

Double-length race with a

war horse: Hagias

son of Boulon

of Athens.

**F.delphes ⅲ 2 42**

From all: straight race with horse:

Straton son of Boulon of Athens.

crown below

**F.delphes ⅲ 2 36**

**Part 1**

Double-length race

with a war horse: Hermon

son of Antiphon.

crown below
