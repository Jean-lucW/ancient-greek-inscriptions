# Statue base for the hearth-initiate Praxagora

## English translation

**Part 1**

O visitors, after the famous temple

of Deo, behold also a parents’

famous daughter, Praxagora, whom

Demostratos of Melite

and Philiste brought into this world,

both being descended of dadouch parents;

but a chorus of children decorates me too;

in front of the other initiates

at the ceremonies,

they have placed a wreath on my hair.
