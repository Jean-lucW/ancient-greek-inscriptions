# Herm in honour of the hierophant who saved the sacred objects of the Mysteries

## English translation

**Part 1**

[I, . . ., took] the mystic treasury of secrets

to Athens in the hateful war.

For this the Kekropidai have crowned me with headbands

and have dedicated me in the [sacred precinct] of the Mother’s (?) everlasting ceremony.
