# Catalogue of ephebes, 255/6 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

35th

Panathenaia (255/6 AD)

Relief of a shield with a bust of Athena, between two Panathenaic victory amphorae

For good fortune,

in the archonship of Lucius Flavius Philostratos

of Steiria (255/6 AD) , when the superintendent was

Gaius Calpurnius Proculus of Hermos,

the team leaders,

having set up the stele, inscribed (the names of) the superintendent,

his fellow magistrates,

the staff of the Diogeneion, and

their fellow ephebes.

Superintendent: Gaius Calpurnius Proculus of Hermos. Deputy

Superintendent: Aurelius Eupraxides son of Euporos of Melite.

Archon of the ephebes: Herennius Ptolemaios of Hermos.
