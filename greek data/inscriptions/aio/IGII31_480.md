# Honours for a Plataian

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] [Of the presiding committee]

-ikos (?) of Thorai was putting to the vote. The People

decided. Demeas son of Demades of Paiania

proposed: since T-

son of [Mo?]schos of Plataia is well disposed

towards the Athenian People and

does whatever good is in his

power on behalf of the city, the People

shall decide: that he shall be an Athenian, himself and

his descendants, and he shall be enrolled

in the tribe and deme and phratry

that he wishes, except those which the laws

prohibit; and the prytany

of Akamantis shall put

the vote about him to the People

in the Assembly; and the

prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand

it on the acropolis [...]
