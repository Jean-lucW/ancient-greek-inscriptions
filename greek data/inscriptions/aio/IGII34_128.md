# Dedication to Apollo Hypo Makrais

## English translation

**Part 1**

With good fortune,

In myrtle crown:

G(aius) Julius

Metrodoros of Marath(on),

having been court president,

dedicated (this) to

Apollo Below the Long Rocks.
