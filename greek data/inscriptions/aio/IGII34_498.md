# Choregic dedication (from Halai Aixonides?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Eu-, serving as theatrical sponsor, was victor in comedy:

Ekphantides directed Attempts.

Thrasyboulos, serving as theatrical sponsor, was victor in comedy:

Kratinos directed Cowherds.

Thrasyboulos, serving as theatrical sponsor, was victor in tragedy:

Timotheos directed Alkmeon, Alphesiboia.

Epichares, serving as theatrical sponsor, was victor in tragedy:

Sophocles directed Telepheia.
