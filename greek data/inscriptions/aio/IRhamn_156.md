# Dedication of the temple at Rhamnous to the Goddess Livia

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People (dedicated this structure)

to the Goddess Livia, when the hoplite

general and priest of the Goddess

Roma and of Augustus Caesar was Demostratos

son of Dionysios of Pallene, and the archon was

- son of Antipatros of Phlya the younger.
