# Choregic dedication of Oineis for the City Dionysia, honouring Philopappos

## English translation

**Part 0**

The tribe Oineis, on account of those who competed ⟦well⟧

in the Dionysiac chorus, (honours) the archon

and competition-director of the Dionysia, Gaius

Julius Antiochos Epiphanes Philopappos

of Besa, on account of his benefaction to them.

Moiragenes was director,

Boulon was sponsor, both sons of Moiragenes of Phyle.

**Column 1**

text

The chairman was:

Menandros (son of Menandros)

of Phyle.

The chorus members were:

Uninscribed space

The pipe-player was: Philetos

son of Meniskos of Kolonos.

**Column 2**

Apollonios of Oe,

Ampheotides of Lakiadai,

Archikles of Lakiadai,

Eunous of Acharnai,

Eraton (son of Eraton) of Phyle,

Metrodoros son of Primos,

Phidias son of Aphrodisios,

**Column 3**

Terpnos son of Gnaios,

Eision son of Boulon,

Philokrates son of Sokrates,

Asklepiades son of Pankrates,

Aphrodisios son of Epigenes,

Patrobios son of Alexandros,

Mousaios son of Boulon,

**Column 4**

Euphrosynos son of Menophanes.

Acharnians:

Demetrios son of Menneas,

Hermias son of Apollonios,

Iakinthos son of Zoilos,

Mousikos son of Kleon,

Menippos (son of Menippos), Apolloni(o)s (son of Apolloni(o)s),

Stephanos son of Euporos,

**Column 5**

Pamphilos son of Karpodoros,

Phaustos son of Glaukias,

From Oe: Apollonios son of Epiktetos,

Epiktetos son of Apollonios.

Thriasians: Epichares son of Xen-

The singing master was

Mousikos.
