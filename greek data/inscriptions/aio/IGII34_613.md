# Dedication commemorating victories in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Part 1**

In agonistic crowns

Crown 1

The Eleutheria

in Plataia

Crown 2

The Herakleia

in Thebes

Crown 3

The Panhellenia

in Athens

Crown 4

The Nemeia

in Argos

Crown 5

The Eleutheria

in Plataia

Crown 6

The Olympeia

in Athens

Crown 7

The Hadrianeia-

Olympia

in Smyrna

Crown 8

The Olympia

in Ephesos

Crown 9

The Olympia

in Smyrna

Crown 10

The Barbilleia

in Ephesos

Crown 11

On shield

and at

the Shield(-Games)

of Argos
