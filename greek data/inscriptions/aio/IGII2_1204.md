# Decree of Lamptrai honouring Philokedes of Acharnai

## English translation

**Part 1**

Kephisodoros proposed:

that the Lamptraians shall resolve,

since Philokedes

is honour-loving

with respect to the sacrifices and

the common affairs in which he has a share

in the deme,

to praise Philokedes

son of Aristarchos of Acharnai

for his love of honour

and grant him

freedom from taxation and distribute

to him meat in the

sacrifices that

the demesmen make at Lamptrai

just as to the Lamptraians,

both for himself and

his descendants [...]
