# Dedication by a manager of the Lykeion

## English translation

**Part 1**

Dionysios son of Dionysodoros

of Kropidai, manager

of the Lykeion, (dedicated this) to Apollo

in the gymnasiarchy of Kallikratides

son of Syndromos of Steiria.
