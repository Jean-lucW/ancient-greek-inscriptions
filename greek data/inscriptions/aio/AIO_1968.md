Decree 1

[...]

[...] [and he] and his descendants shall be exempt

[from the recurrent?] liturgies [under the laws?];

[...] nor any of the managers

[...] any [...] nor

[...] [put to the vote] contrary to this decree;

sacred [to Akamas: and the managers of

decree [and stand it in the] sanctuary of Akamas:

[ name + demotic ], Aristokrates

of Thorikos, [ name + ] demotic .

Decree 2

In the archonship of –.

- son of – of Eiresidai proposed:

[since the superintendent] of ephebes Autolykos

has managed the young men [well and with love of honour],

the tribe Akamantis shall decide to praise

Autolykos son of – of Thorikos for his love of honour

and management of the ephebes

and crown him with a [foliage] crown, when

[he has rendered an account of his management?], for his excellence and

[orderliness; and the] [...]

shall inscribe this decree on the stele in

[the sanctuary of Akamas on which] is written the

[decree] [...] of the archonship of -.
