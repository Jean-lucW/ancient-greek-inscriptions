# Funerary stele for Melisto and Epigenes

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Μελιστὼ Ἡγεστράτου ἐξ Οἴο ⋮ Ἐπιγένης Μικρίωνος Ἐλευσίνιος.

Relief

## English translation

**Part 1**

Melisto daughter of Hegestratos of Oion. Epigenes son of Mikrion of Eleusis.

Relief
