# Honours for T. Flavius Diophantos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos Council

and the Council [of the 500]

and the People (erected this statue of Titus [Flavius]

[Dio?]phantos of Acharnai.

And both an Elysian Hall and [a better land?]

received this son of Glaukias, the second [from a single family],

who excels in Zeus’ choral dancing,

who ran to meet his own [recently deceased] full brother,

but also for him, the city set up this

stone monument of his uncut [hair among the blessed] gods.

uninscribed space

(Work) of Antiphon

uninscribed space

while the temple attendant was Marcus –

of Besa.
