# Honours for Lamian judges

## English translation

**Part 1**

Kalaides son of Kalaides of Xypete proposed: since the

judges elected by the city of the Lamians

for the court proceedings allotted according to the [judicial agreement]

between the Boeotians and Athenians [resolved] some

and decided the rest justly, for good fortune, the People shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted for the coming

Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise the judges for their justice

and to crown each of them with a foliage crown,

Patron son of Kylouthos, Ari- son of -,

Kleisthenes son of Philippos, [...] ,

-odamos [...]
