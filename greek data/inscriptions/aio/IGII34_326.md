# Dedication from Sounion by a general, 219/18 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Theomnestos son of Theomnestos of Xypete,

having been elected by the People general

in charge of the coastal countryside in

the archonship of Menekrates (219/8) , dedicated (this).
