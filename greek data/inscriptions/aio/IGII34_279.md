# Dedication by soldiers at Eleusis for the general, Thrasyboulos of Erchia

## English translation

**Part 1**

The soldiers, having crowned the

general and having been crowned in return, dedicated (this).

In olive crown

General

Thrasyboulos

son of Thrason

of Erchia (crowned them).
