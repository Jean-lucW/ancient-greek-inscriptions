# Dedication by an ephebic <i>pareutaktos</i>, victor in a torch race

## English translation

**Part 1**

-s son of Polyeuktos (?)

of -, having won the ephebic

torch-race of the pareutaktoi,

in the gymnasiarchy

of Apolexis son of Apellikon

of Oion.
