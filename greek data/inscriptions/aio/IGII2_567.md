**Fragment b**

Council [...]

Priene

[...] [since we observed]

[the goodwill which existed on their part] from the beginning [towards the People]

of the Athenians [...]

and [...]

goodwill [...] to praise

also [the ambassadors of the Prienians for their justice]

and [love of honour and to crown each]

of them [with a crown]

[...] the People

of the [Athenians] [...]

Hekatombaion [...] [and they shall be permitted]

to obtain [other benefits]

the People

[...]

People, [and the secretary shall inscribe this decree]

on a stone stele [and stand it on the Acropolis, and for the] inscribing

of the [stele the treasurer of the People shall give]

30 drachmas from [the fund for expenditure on decrees]

of the People, and to invite [the ambassadors of the Prienians to hospitality]

in the city hall [tomorrow, and the director of works shall also assign them]

a seat at the Dionysia.
