# Honours for a man from the Chersonese

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] son of Aristoteles [...]

[...] apparent [...] and [...]

please the Athenian People, and acting in this way

will not fail to obtain [from the People anything that is just];

and the decrees [which the Athenians] and those

[Athenians living in the Chersonese] voted about him shall be valid;

and the generals in office at any time shall take

care of him and the archons in

the Chersonese, so that he suffers no harm; and the secretary

of the Council shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it on the acropolis ( [...] ?)
