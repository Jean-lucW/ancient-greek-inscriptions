# Dedication by Paianistai

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune.

In the archonship of Dionysios son of

Dionysios of Acharnai

the Paianistai of Asklepios

Mounichios, having petitioned

the mighty curator of the most splendid

city of Athens, Gaius Licinnius

Telemachos, as instructed by the god himself

through Aurelius Isidoros the priest, we have appended

[the answer given by the mighty?]

[curator?] [...]
