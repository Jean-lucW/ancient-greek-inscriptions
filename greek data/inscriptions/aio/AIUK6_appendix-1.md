# Funerary stele for Agron and Agron

## Greek text

**Part 1**

**Column 1**

Ἄγρων

Λαοδικεῦ

χρηστὲ

χαῖρε.

**Column 2**

Ἄγρων

Ἄγρωνος

Λαοδικεῦ

χρηστὲ

χαῖρε.

## English translation

**Column 1**

Agron

of Laodikeia:

farewell,

worthy man!

**Column 2**

Agron,

son of Agron,

of Laodikeia:

farewell,

worthy man!
