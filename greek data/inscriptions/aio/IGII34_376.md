# Dedication of a ritual regulation by ephebes, 61/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune, in the archonship

of Theophemos (61/0 BC) ,

Pythagoras and Sosikrates

and Lysandros,

fellow ephebes,

dedicated (this) to Pan and the Nymphs.

These things the god forbids:

to bring in a coloured

or dyed or [...]

traces
