# Treaty with Eretria

## English translation

**Fragment a**

[...]

[...] according to [...] allies

were assessed [...]

and [of the Eretrians the] five hundred

[cavalry and the Council and] all [the other] officials shall swear

to the alliance; [the oath for the Eretrians shall be as follows]:

I shall abide by the alliance [and the -] with the Athenian

People [and the allies justly and]

without deception; and if anyone [should invade Athenian territory],

or the allies [of the Athenians, or would overthrow the]

Athenian People, I shall help [with all the strength]

in my power; and both sides shall swear [the customary oath];

and [...] the Council and all the officials [...]

[...] [shall swear the oath;

and the People shall elect] five envoys [from all the Athenians]

immediately, who [will administer the oath]

to the Eretrians; and the secretary [...]

shall inscribe this decree and stand it [...]
