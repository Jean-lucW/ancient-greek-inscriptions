# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

Polias son of Ph- (?) [...]

-anax son of Apol- [...]

Apollas son of Apol-, -

son of Akamas, Ant- son of -,

Apollodoros [son of -], and crown

each of them with a foliage crown;

and they shall be permitted to obtain from the People

whatever other benefit

they may be deemed worthy of; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer of the People

shall give 30 drachmas from the

People’s fund for expenditure on

decrees.
