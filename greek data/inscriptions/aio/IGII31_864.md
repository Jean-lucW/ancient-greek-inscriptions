# Proxeny for Habron and Matrias

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Of Habron, Matrias.

In the archonship of Diokles (286/5) , in the

eighth prytany, of Leontis , for which Xenophon

son of Nikeas of Halai was secretary.

On the nineteenth of Anthesterion,

the nineteenth of the prytany.

Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Polyeuktos son of Demetrios of -

was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The People decided. Paramythos son of Polystratos

of Paiania proposed: since Habron

of - and Matrias of Nesos [...]

of the People grain [...]

[...] necessity [...]

[...]

[...] for good fortune, the People

shall decide: to praise Habron of

- and Matrias of Nesos

and to crown each of them

with a gold crown according to the law

for the love of honour and good will which

they continue to have for the

Council and the Athenian People;

and they shall be proxenoi of the

People; and they shall have right of ownership of land

according to the laws, and a house; and the secretary of the People

shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele and stand it [...]

[...]
