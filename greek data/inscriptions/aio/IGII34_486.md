# Choregic dedication of Aristion for the Thargelia, 359/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

(The tribes) Hippothontis (and) Kekropis,

Aristion son of Phaullos of Acherdous,

serving as sponsor, was victorious, Eucharistos (359/8) was archon.

Hegemon of Phleious was director,

Alkathous of Sikyon was pipe-player.
