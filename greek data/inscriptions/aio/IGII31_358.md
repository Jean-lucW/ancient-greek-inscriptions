# Honours for Eurylochos of Kydonia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of Euthykritos (328/7) , in the?],

[sixth prytany, of Erechtheis , for which?],

[Pythodelos son of Pythodelos of Hagnous?]

[was secretary. On the?]

[eigh]teenth [of Gamelion, the thirty-first?]

of the prytany. [Of the presiding committee]

Pamphilos of Ph[yle?] was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. Demades son of Demeas of Paiania

proposed: since both previously the ancestors

of Eurylochos and his [father] Akesandros,

being friendly and well disposed

towards the city, made many great benefactions

to the Athenian People,

and now Eurylochos, maintaining his ancestral

good will towards the Athenian

People, continues to be useful,

both collectively and individually, to Athenians

coming to Kydonia,

and having ransomed many Athenians

from Crete, has dispatched them at his

own expense, and was

responsible for their rescue [from] [...]

[...] to their own country [...]

[...]
