# Memorial of Athenian cavalry killed in the Corinthian War (394 BC)

## English translation

**Column 1**

The following cavalrymen died at Corinth:

the phylarch Antiphanes

**Column 2**

Melesias

Theangelos

**Column 3**

Onetorides

Phanes

**Column 4**

Lysitheos

Demokles

**Column 5**

Pandios

Dexileos

**Column 6**

Nikomachos

Endelos

**Column 7**

at Koroneia:

Neokleides
