# Decree of deme Sounion providing for creation of a new agora, donated by Leukios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Theodelos proposed: that

the Sounians shall resolve, for good

fortune, since Leukios

makes a donation for the creation

of an agora for the demesmen,

to choose immediately three

men, who will mark the boundaries of

the agora with

Leukios, not less than

two plethra

by one plethron, so that

there may be a broad space for the Sounians

to gather in, and anyone

else that wishes,

since the present agora

is congested with buildings; and

it shall not be permitted for

the demarch or anyone else

to build within the

boundary markers; and the demarch together

with Leukios shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and stand

it in the agora.
