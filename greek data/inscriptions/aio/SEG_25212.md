# Statue for Tiberius Claudius Iason Magnus

## English translation

**Part 1**

In accordance with a memorandum

of the Areopagos Council,

(this statue of) Tiberius Claudius Iason Magnus, having been archon

of the Panhellenion (was erected); Tiberius Claudius

Iason Magnus (organised it) for his father.
