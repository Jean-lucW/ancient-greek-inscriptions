# Statue base dedicated by Sempronia Atratina and restored by Claudius

## English translation

**Part 1**

Inscription I

[...]

(was erected by) Sempronia Atratina, daughter of [Lucius Sempronius]

Atratinus, Paulla [wife of Lucius Gellius Publicola].

**Part 2**

Inscription II

Tiberius Claudius [Caesar Augustus] Germanicus,

benefactor of the city [freely gave] and

restored (this statue).
