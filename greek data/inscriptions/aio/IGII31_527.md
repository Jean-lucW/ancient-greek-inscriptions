# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and for inscribing the

stele the treasurer of the People shall give -

drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
