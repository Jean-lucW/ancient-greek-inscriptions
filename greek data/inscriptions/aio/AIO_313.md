## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1 (403 - ca. 395?)

[...] the [secretary of the Council]

shall inscribe [this decree and that previously

enacted for Herakleides? and]

put it on the acropolis.

Decree 2 (Council and People, before 403 BC?)

The Council and the People decided. []

was in prytany. S- was secretary.

[] was chairman. -

proposed: the secretary of the Council shall inscribe

Herakleides [the Klazomenian] as

proxenos and benefactor, [as the People]

shall decide, and put it on the acropolis, because he acted

well by the Athenian [embassies] and in

other respects is a good man towards the Athenian

People.

Decree 3 (People, before 403 BC?)

Thoukydides proposed: in other respects

in accordance with the Council, but since the [envoys]

who have come from the King report that

Herakleides has co-operated with them enthusiastically

with regard to the treaty [...] and

any other business which they proposed, Herakleides

shall have right of ownership of land and [], as for the other

proxenoi; and if he is killed anywhere by a violent form of death

there shall be the same punishment in his case

as if an Athenian is killed; and these things

shall apply to the descendants of Herakleides.

Of Herakleides son of G-,

proxenos and benefactor,

the [Klazo]menian.
