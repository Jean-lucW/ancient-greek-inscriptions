# Dedication by a prytany treasurer, 53/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Menodoros son of Ar- of -, having been

treasurer of the prytany of the tribe Akamantis

in the year in which Diodoros was archon (53/2)

(dedicated this) on behalf of the tribe to Zeus

Boulaios and Hestia Boulaia.
