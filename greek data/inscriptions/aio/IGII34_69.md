# Dedication by the prytany of Kekropis, 343/2 BC ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of [Pythod?]otos (343/2) the pry[tany] of Kekropis

[dedicated (this)] to Agathos Daimon having been victorious [over the other tribes?]

according to the People’s judgement and having been crowned [by the Council]

[and the] People for their excellence and just[ice].
