# Alexandria Troas honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

Latin text

To Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan

Parthicus, grandson of the God Nerva,

Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Pontifex Maximus,

tribunician power 16 times (132 AD) , consul 3 times, father

of the fatherland, Olympios, on account of

many benefactions, some private,

some public, which he performed, the

restorer of their colony, the Trojans (erected this statue),

through the envoys Marcus Servilius, Marcus Tutilius

Paulus, and Lucius Vedumnius Aulus

Greek text

Of the Trojans.
