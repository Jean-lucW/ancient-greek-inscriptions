# Dedication by a court president (<i>thesmothetes</i>), 329/8 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

Antikrates son of Lysanias of Probalinthos

who was a court president

in the archonship of Kephisophon (329/8) dedicated (this).

In olive crown

The People

**Column 2**

In olive crown

The Council

**Face-left b**

**Column 3**

In olive crown

The tribesmen

**Face-right c**

In olive crown
