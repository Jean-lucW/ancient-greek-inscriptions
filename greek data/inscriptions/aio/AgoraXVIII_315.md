# Altar for Marcus Aurelius and Lucius Verus

## Greek text

**Part 1**

in corona:

ἀγαθῆι τύχῃ.

νείκην Αὐτοκράτορσι δι[ὰ] σύ<μ>πονίας

φιλαδέλφοις.

## English translation

**Part 1**

in crown

For good fortune,

victory to the brother-loving

Emperors

through co-operation.
