# Honours for Aischron son of Proxenos and another

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1 (ca. 235 BC)

[...]

[...]

eight thousand medimnoi [...] talents

following the [preference] of their ancestors

[having shown] themselves and their [relatives?] to be [enthusiastic], and

[when detailed] for the handing over of the [...] they

[ensured] that they were the first [to take possession of the grain?] and

[hand it over first?] to the Athenian ambassadors, and further

they behaved with love of honour so that the People might take the best possible

advantage [of the delivery]; so, therefore, that the People may be seen to render worthy

[gratitude] to all who wish to be benefactors of the Athenians,

for good fortune, the People shall decide: to praise

Aischron son of Proxenos and - son of -

of [Deiradio?]tai; and to crown them with a foliage crown

for their good will and love of honour towards the Athenian

People; and when, on the coming of peace, the Athenians

deliberate about their citizen benefactors

the presiding committee shall bring forward [an opinion of the Council] also about these men

to the People so that they may be honoured by the People in a manner worthy of the services

they have performed; and the secretary of the People shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it on

the acropolis; and the treasurer shall allocate for the stele

the expenditure accrued.
