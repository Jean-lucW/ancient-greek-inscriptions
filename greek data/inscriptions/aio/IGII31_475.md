# Proxeny for a son of Thyion

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] -os son of Thyion

of - and crown him

with a gold crown of 1,000

drachmas; and he and his descendants

shall be proxenoi and

benefactors of the Athenian

People and shall have [right of ownership]

of land and a house, according to

the law, so that [everyone]

else may show love of honour

concerning the Athenian People,

knowing that they will receive thanks

worthy of their benefactions;

and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on

the acropolis; and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer

of the People shall give [...]
