# Catalogue of ephebes, 87/8 AD ?

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune, in the archonship of the Emperor Caesar, son of the

Divine Vespasian, Domitian Augustus

Germanicus Zeus of Freedom (87/8 AD?) ,

the superintendent Straton (son of Straton) of Epikephisia,

inscribed the tutors under him and the ephebes

under him. The Theseia

having been held, the competition directors were: Straton (son of Straton) of

Epikephisia the Y(ounger) and [Menandros] son of Straton of

Epikephisia and they [won the sea battle] at S[alamis]. The gymnasiarchs were:

Straton (son of Straton) of Epikephisia and
