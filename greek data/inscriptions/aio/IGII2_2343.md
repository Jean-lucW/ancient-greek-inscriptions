# Offering table of a <i>thiasos</i> of Herakles

## English translation

**Part 1**

Around the edge of the table

upper edge

(This is the table of) Simon of  
Kydathenaion,  
priest of Herakles and of an association of  
members of the thiasos:

left edge

Philonides,  
Euthynomos,  
Theophon,

lower edge

Pythaios,  
Lysanias,  
Antitheos,  
Amphitheos,  
Hegemon,  
Archestratos,

right edge

Melanopides,  
Melanopides,

upper edge, upside down

Theophanes,

between the right and middle rectangular hollows

Telestes,

between the right and middle rectangular hollows, upside down

Apollodoros,

between the middle and left hollows

Nausistratos.
