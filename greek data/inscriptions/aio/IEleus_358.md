# Honours for Claudius Asklepiodoros

## English translation

**Part 1**

uninscribed space

(This statue of) the one who was herald and

gymnasiarch twice,

and competition-director of the

Great Caesarea Augusta

and hoplite

general twice,

lover of Caesar and lover of the fatherland,

[Claudius] Asklepiodo[ros]

of Gargettos [was dedicated by] Claudia

[...] for her own father,

to the two goddesses,

[when the priestess] was Kleo

[daughter of Eukles] of Phlya,

[but by birth of Nikodemos]

[of Hermos].
