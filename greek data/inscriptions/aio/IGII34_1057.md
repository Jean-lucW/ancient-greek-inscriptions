# Honours for Neoptolemos of Melite

## English translation

**Part 1**

Neoptolemos

son of Antikles

of Melite

(dedicated this) to Artemis

in the priesthood of Chairylla.

Gods.

Hegesippos son of Hegesias proposed: the demesmen

shall decide: since Neoptolemos son of Antikles

of Melite, speaking and acting to the best of his ability

for the Athenian People

and the demesmen, and as manager of

all the rites and now [...] the affairs of Artemis [...]

[...] and [...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

[...] he is a good man towards the Athenians

and the deme of Melite, for good fortune,

the demesmen shall decide: to praise

Neoptolemos son of Antikles of Melite and

crown him with a gold crown for his excellence

and good will towards the Athenian People

and the demesmen; and the demarch

shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it in the sanctuary of Artemis

[so that others may also] show love of honour

[towards the demesmen].
