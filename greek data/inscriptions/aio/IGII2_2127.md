# List of ephebic team leaders (systremmatarchs)

## English translation

**Part 1**

With good fortune,

in the archonship of Philisteides

(son of Philisteides) of Piraeus, the superintendent being Abaskantos (son of Abaskantos)

of Kephisia, the fellow-ephebes

of Telesphoros, having been team leaders,

having honoured themselves, inscribed

their god and

themselves.

Telesphoros son of Asklepios

Prosdokimos son of Demetrios of Sounion

Archias son of Demetrios of Sounion

Sotas son of Agelianos

Antas son of Epigonos

Panphilos (son of Panphilos) of Berenikidai

Claudius Philonides

Athenodoros (son of Athenodoros)

Sophianos son of Zosimos

Zosimos (son of Zosimos)

Herakleon son of Attikos of Piraeus

Epitynchanon son of Theokritos of Piraeus

Philemon son of Nikostratos of Sphettos

- Popillius Onesikrates
