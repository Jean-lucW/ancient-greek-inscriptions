# Citizenship decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and his good will towards the Athenians, for good fortune, the Council shall decide,

that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming Assembly shall put

these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion of the Council to the People that it seems good

to the Council to praise [...]

for his good will towards the People of the Athenians; and he shall also be granted

citizenship, having been scrutinised in the court in accordance with the law; and the

court presidents shall introduce his scrutiny, when they have filled the court

with five hundred and one jurors; and, having been scrutinised, he shall be enrolled [...]
