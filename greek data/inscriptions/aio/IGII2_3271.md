# Honours for Claudius (42 AD)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[(This statue of) Tiberius Claudius Caesar] Augustus Germanicus, pontifex maximus, tribunician

power, consul twice, proclaimed emperor three times (42 AD) ,

[father of the fatherland] (was erected by) the Council of the Areopagos and the Council of the Six

Hundred and the People, for their saviour and benefactor, with the work

being managed by Tiberius Claudius Novius of Oion, herald of the Areopagos

Council and competition-director and priest of Delian Apollo.
