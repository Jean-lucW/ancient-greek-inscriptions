## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods

In the archonship of - , Stesileos

[...] proposed: since

[ - having been appointed] general of the

[countryside around] Eleusis, took charge

[of the soldiers] in the fort,

[and he considered also] the other things which

[the laws required of him, and] he

[also made provision for] the sacrifices, well

[and loving honour], for good fortune,

[the soldiers] shall decide, [in order that they may show]

[worthy] gratitude, [to praise] and crown

[him with a gold crown], in accordance with

[the law] [...]
