# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] he shall be proxenos

of the Athenian People, and his descendants;

and he shall have the right of ownership of land and a house;

and on maintaining the same [preference] for the People

it shall be possible for him [in the future] to obtain other benefits,

[whatever he may be deemed worthy of] [...]
