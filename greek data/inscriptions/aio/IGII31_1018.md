# Honours for market inspectors (agoranomoi)

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Kydenor (245/4) , in the ninth

prytany, of Erechtheis , for which Polyktemon son of Euktimenos

of Eupyridai was secretary. On the ninth of Elaphebolion,

the seventeenth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Antikles

son of Exekestos of Aixone was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee

members. The Council and People decided.

Kallistratos son of Telesinos of Erchia proposed: since

the market inspectors of the archonship of Philoneos (246/5)

conducted their office according to the laws and decrees

of the Council and People, and managed

the affairs of the market well and justly,

and for these things have rendered their accounts; in order therefore that

that there may be an incentive for all who will hold

this office to perform it according to the laws, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside for the coming Assembly

shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems

good to the Council to praise the [market inspectors]

of the archonship of Philoneos (246/5) [...]
