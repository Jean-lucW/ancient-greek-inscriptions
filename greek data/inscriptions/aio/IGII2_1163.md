# Decree of tribe Hippothontis for the priest of Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Proxenos son of Pylagoras of Acherdous

proposed: since Phyleus on being allotted as

priest of Asklepios for the archonship

of Isaios (284/3) has made

all the sacrifices, as many as befell

him to make, well and with love of honour

on behalf of the Athenian People, and manages

the allotment of the courts

and the rest which the laws

and the decrees prescribe for him

justly and according to the laws,

and because of these things both the Council

and the People have praised him, for good

fortune, the tribesmen of Hippothontis shall decide:

to praise Phyleus son of Chairias of Eleusis

for his piety towards

the gods and justice and

love of honour for the tribe members

and the Athenian People,

and to crown him with a foliage

crown; and the managers of the tribe

shall inscribe this decree

on stone stelai and stand one

in the Asklepieion, one

in the Hippothontion; whatever expenditure

accrues shall be billed to the

tribe.
