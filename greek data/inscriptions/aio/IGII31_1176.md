# Honours for the ephebes of 204/3 and their officers

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of [Apollodoros (203/2) ], in the third prytany, of Aiantis ,

for which [...] [of Oe ] was secretary. On the - of Boedromion,

the - of the prytany. Council in the Panathenaic stadium.

Of the presiding committee Nikostratos son of Menemachos of Acharnai was putting to the vote, and

his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and the People decided.

Sosige[nes] [...] proposed: since the ephebes of the archonship of

Diodotos (204/3) initiated the proper sacrifices from the hearth in the city hall

and accomplished what is inscribed there in accordance with the

policy of the People, and participated in the processions, of the Semnai

and of Iakchos, adhering with exceptional discipline to the traditions,

and lifted (?) the bovines in the sacrifice, and removed to the sanctuary in

Eleusis, in order that everything relating to the gods should be handled piously, and rendered

service also in the Mysteries, as they were instructed, and were continuously

well-disciplined in the gymnasia, and ran all the torch races

and competed in the other competitions well and in an orderly manner,

and made the demonstration in arms at the Epitaphia, and took care of

the guarding of the cities, obeying the generals and

their superintendent, and rendered service also in the Hephaisteion,

and carried through in a well-disciplined manner their training with weapons, with nothing being allocated

to them by the People, and as regards their nautical training they made [...]

[...] the demonstration to the Council in arms pursuant to

the laws and decrees relating to the usage of war

and fitted themselves to become good future contenders on behalf of their fatherland

[...] and attentive, pursuant to what is inscribed

[...] prepared to perform the exit sacrifices in order that

[...] in good order as also their cloaks

and their other clothing; in order therefore that there should always be an incentive for the ephebes,

knowing that those who are obedient to those elected to secure good discipline

have been honoured, for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the

presiding committee allotted for the forthcoming Assembly shall place these matters on the agenda, and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to praise those who were ephebes in the archonship of Diodotos (204/3) , and crown them with a gold crown

for the [good will and discipline] and love of honour which they have continually shown

towards the Council and the People; and to announce the crowns at the City

Dionysia and Panathenaia and Eleusinia at the gymnastic competitions;

and the generals and the treasurer of the military fund shall take care of

the announcement and making of the crowns; and they shall have front seats

in all the competitions which the city puts on; and they shall have permission

to set up a painting wherever they may choose; and to praise also the physical trainer,

Heortios son of Hermodoros of Acharnai, because pursuant to the laws and decrees

[...] and to praise also

[...] the javelin trainer

[...]

[...]

[...] and the secretary [...] and crown

each of them with a foliage crown; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree and their names

on a stone stele and stand it in the Agora; and the treasurer

of the military fund shall give the expenditure accrued.
