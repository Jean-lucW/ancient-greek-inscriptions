# Dedication by managers of tribe Leontis, 357/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The managers in the archonship of Agathokles (357/6)

dedicated (this) to [Leos?], having been

crowned by the tribe Leontis

for their justice.

**Column 1**

Chairimenes

son of Lysanias

of Deiradiotai.

**Column 2**

Philoneos

son of Gnathios

of Leukonoion.

**Column 3**

Pha-

son of Smik-

of Aithalidai.
