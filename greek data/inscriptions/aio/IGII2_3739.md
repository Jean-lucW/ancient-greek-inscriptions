# The ephebes honour their superintendent, 141/2 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

The ephebes in the archonship of

P(ublius) Aelius Phileas of Melite (141/2 AD)

(honoured) their own superintendent

Sosistratos (son of Sosistratos) of Marathon

on account of the good will

which he maintained towards them,

phallus

the trainer being

for life Abaskantos

son of Eumolpos of

Kephisia.
