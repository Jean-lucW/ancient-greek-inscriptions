# Choregic dedication of Hieronymos for the Thargelia, 364/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Front

Hieronymos son of Laches of Hekale,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Leontis (and) Aigeis

in the boys' competition, Eukles was director,

uninscribed space

Timokrates (364/3) was archon.

**Part 2**

Rear

Hieronymos son of Laches of Hekale,

serving as sponsor, was victorious for Leontis (and) Aigeis

in the boys’ competition, Eukles was director,

uninscribed space

Timokrates (364/3) was archon.
