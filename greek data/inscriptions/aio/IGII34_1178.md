# Dedication to the hero Kallistephanos by the cobbler Dionysios and sons

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Dionysios son of -on and

On pillar

his sons dedicated this to the hero

Kallistephanos,

(sc. Dionysios) the cobbler.

uninscribed space

Having seen a divine vision in his sleep,

Dionysios adorns the hero

and the children

of Kallistephanos; now you, in return for these things,

grant wealth

and blessed health.
