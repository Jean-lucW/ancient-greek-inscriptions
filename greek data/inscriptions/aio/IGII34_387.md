# Dedication by an ephebe, 38/7 or 37/6 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

of Acharnai, having been overseer

of the torch-race, in the year that

Menandros was archon (38/7 or 37/6 BC) ,

dedicated this

to the Muses.
