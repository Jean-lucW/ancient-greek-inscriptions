# Decree about a proposal of Theophantos

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the tenth prytany, of Pandionis , for which -

son of -on of Cholleidai was secretary. [...]

On the thirty-second of the prytany. [Of the presiding committee]

Kallias of Phrearrhioi was putting to the vote. The People decided.

Telemachos son of Theangelos of Acharnai proposed: concerning what

Theophantos says, the Council shall resolve: that the

presiding committee allotted to preside

in the People, shall introduce Theophantos

and put the matter on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People that it seems good to the

Council [...]
