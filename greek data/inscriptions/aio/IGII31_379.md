# Proxeny for Apollonides son of Demetrios of Sidon

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] of the presiding committee

Epameinon [of Erchia?] was putting to the vote. -les (?) son of -

of Anagyrous proposed: since the merchants and the

shippers declare that Apollonides son of Demetrios

of Sidon is a good man and well

disposed towards the Athenian People, the People shall decide:

to praise Apollonides son of Demetrios of Sidon

and crown him with a gold crown of

a thousand drachmas for his excellence and good will

towards the Athenian People, and he shall be

proxenos and benefactor of the Athenian People,

himself and his descendants; and he shall have right of ownership of land

and a house according to the law; and the secretary

of the Council shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele the treasurer

of the People shall give 20 drachmas from the

People’s fund for expenditure on decrees.
