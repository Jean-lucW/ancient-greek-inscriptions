# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] to be [...] Athen-;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give 30 drachmas

from the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees, so that everyone may know

that [...] the Athenian People knows

how to give thanks to those who

are honour-loving [...]

[...]
