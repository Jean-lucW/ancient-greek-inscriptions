# Choregic dedication of Lysikles for the City Dionysia, 323/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Lysikles son of Biottos of Oe was sponsor,

the tribe Oineis was victorious in the boys’ competition,

Pamphilos of Hagnous was director, [ [...] was pipe-player]

Kephisodoros (323/2) was archon.
