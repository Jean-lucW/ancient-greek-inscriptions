# Dedication by pre-ephebes (<i>mellepheboi</i>), 95/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The pre-ephebes

of the archonship of Theodotos (95/4 BC) (dedicated this)

to the Muses:

Boulos son of Hegesisthenes of Lousia

Philon son of Theotimos of Semachidai

Eudoxos son of Eudoxos of Eleusis

Demetrios son of Demetrios of Antioch

Zopyros son of Amphikrates of Phlya

Alexandros son of Iakchos of Adana

Sosigenes son of Sosigenes of Probalinthos

Uninscribed

Python son of Python of Athmonon

Theon son of Menelaos of Paiania

Asketos son of Androkles of Dekeleia (honour)

their instructor:

Uninscribed

Artemon

of Themakos.
