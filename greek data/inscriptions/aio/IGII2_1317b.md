# Decree of a thiasos of Bendis on Salamis (249/8 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Hieron (249/8) and the priesthood of Charinos, on the second of the month Skirophorion,

at the regular assembly. The society decided. Rhythmos proposed:

since the managers who were appointed managed

the sacrifices and everything else concerning the sanctuary of Bendis,

the society of the thiasotai shall decide to praise them

and crown each of them; and the treasurer shall assign 15

drachmas. On receiving this let the managers make a dedication

in the sanctuary of Bendis.

Row 1

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Charinos

priest

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Menon

manager

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Nikias

manager

Row 2

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Nikarchos

manager

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Charinos

treasurer

In crown:

The thiasotai (crown)

Stratokles

secretary.
