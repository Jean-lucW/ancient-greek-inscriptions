# Dedication by ephebes

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the trainership of Ariston,

when Dositheos son of Herakleides of Pambotadai

was superintendent,

when Menodoros (son of Menodoros) of Alopeke

was leader,

when Isigenes of Alopeke was weapons trainer,

the joint recipients of crowns

dedicated (this): Metrodoros

son of – , Sozon son of Sozon,

– son of -genes.
