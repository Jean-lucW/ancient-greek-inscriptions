# Dedication by the soldiers at Rhamnous for a general and a patrol commander

## English translation

**Part 1**

The soldiers [dedicated (this)], having crowned the general

[and] the patrol commander for their excellence and [justice].

**Column 1**

In olive crown

General

Deinokrates

son of Kleombrotos

of Acharnai

**Column 2**

In olive crown

Patrol commander

Telesippos

son of Straton

of Kephisia.
