# Decree of Eleusis honouring a Theban

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] praise

[...] of Thebes and

crown him with a [] crown; and he shall have

a seat of honour and freedom from those taxes

over which the Eleusinians have control;

and the treasurer shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in

the theatre of the Eleusinians.
