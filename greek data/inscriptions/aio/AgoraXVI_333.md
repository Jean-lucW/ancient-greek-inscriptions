# Assembly decree accepting the revision of laws by the Council of the Areopagos

**Part 1**

[For the good fortune of the Council and the People of the Athenians. In the archonship of]

[ [...] in the - prytany of - for which - son of Demetrios]

of Ana- [was secretary [...] ]

twenty-sixth [day of the prytany. Assembly in [...] ]

having been transferred from [...] . [Of the presiding committee],

Neon son of Dorotheos of - [was putting to the vote and his fellow members of the presiding committee. The People decided].

Demeas son of Demeas of [...] proposed. [Since the People of the Athenians? ]

in a democracy [...]

[of the magistrates chosen] by lot and by [election? [...] ]

and giving thanks [...]

and the allotted (magistracies) [...]

and some, through bad practices [...]

the pre-selected public advocates [...]

from which it happens that they have achieved a common and [...] ,

being well-disposed in the same way about [...]

it is allowed to all Athenians already to give consideration

to the things written in [the laws?. For good fortune, the People shall decide]

that what has been provisionally or previously legislated [ [...] by the Council of]

the Areopagos shall be valid, [...]

but it shall be allowed for the other Athenians [...]

[to participate in] the allotted magistracies, [and anyone who attempts],

in any way, to undo [...]

[shall be deprived of his civic rights] and accursed, and for whoever [...]
