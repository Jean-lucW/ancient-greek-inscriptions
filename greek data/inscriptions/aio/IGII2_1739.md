# List of sanctuary-guards for 171/2

## English translation

**Part 1**

In upper tabula ansata

With good fortune,

in the year after the

archonship of

Memmius Flaccus (171/2)

the sanctuary-guards (erected this): Eukarpos

son of Dionysios of Phaleron,

Pardalas son of

Eision of Melite,

Philokles son of Boulon

of Phyle for the second time.

uninscribed space

In lower tabula ansata:

with good fortune

uninscribed space

in the archonship of Lucius

Gellius Xenagoras (172/3)

uninscribed space
