## English translation

**Part 1**

The controller [of Leontis in (the archonship of)] Nikokrates (333/2) [and the ephebes], having been crowned

by the Council and People and the tribe for their excellence and self-discipline (dedicated this) to the hero.

**Column 1**

Theodoros son of Theodoros of Leukonoion

proposed: since Philotheos the controller

of the ephebes of the tribe Leontis

reports about the young men

and says that they are well-disciplined

and obedient to the laws

and himself, Leontis shall decide

that the tribe Leontis

shall praise the ephebes of the

archonship of Nikokrates (333/2) and crown

each of them with a gold crown

for their excellence; and to praise also the

controller, Philotheos son of Philokles

of Sounion and crown him with a gold

crown of 1000 drachmas

when he has rendered his accounts for his excellence

towards the tribe and the

ephebes; and to praise also the infantry

commander, Philokles son of

Philotheos of Sounion, and the captains,

Pandaites son of Pasikles of Potamos,

Epikrates son of Peisianax of Sounion,

Kallichares son of Kalliphanes of Sounion,

Nikoxenos son of Nikokles of Cholleidai,

Timokrates son of Timokles of Potamos,

and crown each of them

with a gold crown of five-

hundred drachmas for their excellence and self-

discipline; and to give them also (permission)

to make a dedication in the sanctuary

[of the hero]; and to praise also the instructors

of the tribe, -tes son of

A- of Pallene, -n son of Ar-

of Methone and crown

them with a foliage crown because they took good

care of the ephebes;

and the secretary of the tribe shall

inscribe this decree of the tribe

and stand it in the sanctuary; and the

managers of the tribe

shall give the cost of writing it up; and the

decree shall be written up on the dedication.
