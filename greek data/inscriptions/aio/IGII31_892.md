# Honours for the People of Tenedos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of Philokrates (276/5) , in the second prytany?], of

-tis, for which Hegesippos son of Aristomachos

of Melite was secretary. [On the twenty-fifth of

Metageitnion, the twenty-?]fourth

of the prytany. Principal Assembly.

Of the presiding committee Kalliades son of Ch-

of - was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The People

decided. - son of - of Melite proposed: since

the People of Tenedos in previous

times continued to be well disposed and a friend

of the Athenian People, and provided many

and great services at every

opportunity, and now [...] the People [...]

[...] the sanctuary [...]

at Eleusis piously [...]

wore wreaths [...]

[...]

answer them, that the Athenian People remembers

their [relationship and in earlier] times

has honoured the People of Tenedos

with fine [and worthy] awards, and now will

honour it and will do all that is just and

cordial in accordance with their friendship; and to praise

the ambassadors who have come for their excellence

and good will to the Athenians; and to praise

the People of Tenedos [...]
