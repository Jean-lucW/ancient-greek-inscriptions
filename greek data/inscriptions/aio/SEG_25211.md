# Thessalian League honours Titus Flavius Kyllos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The League of the Thessalians

(erected this statue of) Titus Flavius

Kyllos, having been archon of the

Panhellenes and

competition-director of the

Great Panhellenia

on account of his excellence

and good will both

towards the Panhellenion

and the city

of the Athenians
