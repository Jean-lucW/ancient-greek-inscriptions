# Honours for a friend of king Eumenes II

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Tychandros (196/5) , in the - prytany, of Akamantis,

for which Sosigenes son of Menekrates of Marathon was secretary.

On the penultimate day of Posideon, the - of the prytany.

Principal Assembly in the theatre. Of the presiding committee - son of

Diozotos of Oinoe was putting to the vote, and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and

People decided. Nikostratos son of Philiskos of Thorai proposed: Since [...]

[...] being a friend of king Eumenes, [in earlier times?]

showed goodwill and has continued to be useful collectively to the People

and individually to citizens coming [to Pergamon],

and now that Eumenes [has taken over] rule [from his father?]

[...]
