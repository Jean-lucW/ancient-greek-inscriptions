# Dedication by ephebes of Aiantis, 333/2 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The ephebes of Aiantis of

the archonship of Nikokrates (333/2)

and their controller, Epichares

son of Epigenes of Oinoe, having won the

torch-race, dedicated (this)

to the hero Mounichos.

From Oinoe

Polymedes son of Polyphilos ⟦and Pythodoros⟧

and Pythodoros son of Apollodoros,
