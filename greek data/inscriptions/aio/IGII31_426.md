# Proxeny for -machos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the presiding committee who are

[...]

Assembly shall introduce -machos

to the People and put his case on the agenda, and

submit the opinion of the Council

to the People that it seems good to the Council

that -machos son of -krates of Al-

shall be a proxenos of the Athenian People,

[himself and his descendants?], and

the generals and the Council in office at any time

shall take care of them,

so that they come to no harm;

and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it on

the acropolis; and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer of the People shall give

[] drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
