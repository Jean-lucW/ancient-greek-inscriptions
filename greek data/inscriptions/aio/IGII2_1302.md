# Athenian soldiers at Sounion honour their general, 219/8

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Menekrates (219/18) , Mounichion.

The Athenian soldiers at Sounion decided:

since Theomnestos, having been elected general

for the coast, was blameless in general

and, alongside the existing sanctuaries,

also furnished a temple and precinct of Asklepios,

so that the soldiers at Sounion

may also be seen to do what follows from Theomnestos’

attitude, with good fortune, the association
