# Honours for Augustus Caesar the New Apollo

## English translation

**Part 1**

[(This statue of) Augustus] Caesar, [the New] Apollo,

(was erected by) Pos[eidoni]os son of Deme[trios] of Phlya,

having been competition director among the ephebes

of him.
