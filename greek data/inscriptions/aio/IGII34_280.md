# Dedication by Charias, general in charge of the countryside

## English translation

**Column 1**

In olive crown

The Council

The People (crowned him)

**Column 2**

Charias son of Euthykrates

[of Kydathenaion],

dedicated (this), having been general

in charge of the countryside.
