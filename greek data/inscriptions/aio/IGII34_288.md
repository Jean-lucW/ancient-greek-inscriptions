# Dedication by the soldiers at Rhamnous for a general and a patrol commander

## English translation

**Part 1**

The soldiers crowned

the general and the patrol commander

for their excellence and justice

towards the soldiers

**Column 1**

In crown?

General

Pherekleides

son of Pherekles

of Perithoidai

**Column 2**

In crown?

Patrol commander

Aristomenes

son of Kannonos

of Philaidai.
