# Dedication to Men by a priest of Isis and Sarapis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief depicting a star within a crescent

Priest in charge of vestments of Isis

and Sarapis, Aur(elius) Epaphroditos,

I dedicated this as a thank-offering

to Men of the heavens.
