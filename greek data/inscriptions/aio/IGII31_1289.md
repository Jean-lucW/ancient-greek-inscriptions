Decree 2

In the archonship of Eupolemos (185/4) , in the - prytany, of -

to which Stratonikos son of Stratonikos of Hamaxanteia was secretary.

Decrees of the Council. On the - of -,

the sixth of the prytany, Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding committee

Patr- son of - of - was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. Telephanes son of - of -

proposed: since the prytany of Leontis and those with permanent dining rights

have praised and crowned the treasurer, whom they chose from themselves,

Apollodoros [...] , and declare to the Council

that he has performed all the proper sacrifices on behalf of the

Council and the People and has also taken care of everything else

well and with love of honour, for good fortune, the Council shall decide

to praise the treasurer, Apollodoros [...]
