# Dedication to Amynos, Asklepios, and Hygieia by Persaios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Persaios

son of Theoxenides

of Marathon (dedicated this)

to Amynos and

Asklepios

and Hygieia, in the

priesthood of

Sophokles son of

Philotas of

Sounion, but by birth

son of Dionysodoros

of Deiradiotai.
