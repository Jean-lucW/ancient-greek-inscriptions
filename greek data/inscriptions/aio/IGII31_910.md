# Honours for the prytany of Oineis

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Pytharatos (271/0) , in the twelfth prytany,

of Oineis , for which Isegoros son of Isokrates of Kephale

was secretary; on the first new and old day of

Skirophorion, the thirty-first of the prytany.

Assembly. Of the presiding committee Kephisokles son of -

of - was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

Philippos son of Astygenes of Thymaitadai proposed: concerning the report

of the prytany of Oineis on the sacrifices

they performed before the Assemblies to the gods for whom

it is traditional, for good fortune, the People decided: to

accept the good things they say occurred in the sacrifices

that they made for the health and preservation of the Council

and the People and the others who are well disposed to the

People; and since the prytany performed the appropriate

sacrifices well and with love of honour, and managed

everything else that the laws and

decrees of the People prescribed for them [...]
