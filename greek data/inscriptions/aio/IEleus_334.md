# Honours for Tiberius

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Tiberius Caesar Augustus (was erected by)

[the Council] of the Areopagos and the Council of the Six Hundred and the [People, in the priesthood of Kleo]

daughter [of Eukles] of Phlya, [the dedication] having been managed

by Papius of Marathon, being priest for life.

Of Papius of Marathon.
