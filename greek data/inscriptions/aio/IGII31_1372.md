# Honours for sacred delegates (<i>theoroi</i>) from Miletos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the [...]

[...] in a seemly manner and [...]

[...] their own [...]

[...] towards the People [...]

[...] to make manifest [...] having come

to Eleusis for the Great Mysteries, in accordance with

the initiation granted them by the city,

[...] a bovine for Demeter and Kore [...]

[...] and they gave the [...]

[...] was [...] . In order, therefore, that the Council and the People be

seen to be honouring and showing favour to those [...]

who are of a zealous disposition towards the city, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted

for the forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters

on the agenda, and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council to accept the good things

that they (i.e. the sacred delegates) reported to have happened at the sacrifices,

which they made for the health and preservation of the people of the

Athenians and the children and women [and likewise for]

[the] people of the Milesians and the children and women;

and to praise the chief sacred delegate, Euan[drides son of

Euandrides] and the sacred delegates Demetrios son of L-,

- son of Apatourios, Krates son of Heli[odoros],

[Hermophantos] son of Lichas, Dios son of Eu-,

and to crown each of them [...]
