# Dedication to Hermes Enagonios by a victorious gymnasiarch, 338/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Hermes Enagonios Autosthe-

son of Autosthenides of Xypete dedicated (this)

having been gymnasiarch for the tribe Kekropis

at the Great Panathenaia, having been crowned

by his tribesmen in the archonship of Chairondas (338/7).

Good luck to whoever adorns me!
