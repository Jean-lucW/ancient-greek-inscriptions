# Honours for the People of Kythnos

## English translation

**Fragment a**

[...]

[...] and continue

to be well disposed towards the Athenian

People, and do whatever

good is in their power for the People,

the People shall resolve: to praise

the [Kythnian] People

and crown them with a gold

crown of [] drachmas, and

those [coming] from the

Kyth[nian] People shall have right of access

to the Council and to the

People first [ ];

and to praise [...]

[...] general [...]

-armeno- [...] and

crown [...]

**Fragment b**

**Part 1**

[...] and stand it

on the acropolis, and for inscribing

the stele the treasurer

of the People shall give 50 drachmas from

the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees.

**Column 1**

In crown

The People.

**Column 2**

In crown

[The Council].
