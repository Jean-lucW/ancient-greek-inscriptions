# Statue base for the hierophant Glaukos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Glaukos, having mixed an aged spirit with a body in its prime,

and with beauty something that is stronger, moderation,

displayed to all mankind the light-bringing rites of Deo

for nine years, and in the tenth he went to the immortals.

Verily a beautiful mystery from the blessed ones, that

death is not only no evil for mortals, but that it is good.
