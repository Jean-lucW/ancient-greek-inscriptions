# Honours for P. Aelius Dionysodoros

## English translation

**Part 1**

With good fortune,

In accordance with their decisions, the

most awesome Council

of the 500 (erected this statue) of the

the allotted

priest of the Saviour

Asklepios, Publius Aelius

Dionysodoros of Acharnai

on account of his excellence

and piety towards the

god.
