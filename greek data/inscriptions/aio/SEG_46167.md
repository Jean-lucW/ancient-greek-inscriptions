# Decree of the Tarantine cavalry squadron honouring cavalry commanders

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Kallistratos son of Termonios of Achaia

proposed: since the cavalry commanders and

the tribal commanders of cavalry in the archonship of Nikias (282/1)

have continued to fulfil

their office according to the laws and

have managed the grain so that

the cavalry and Tarantines

are supplied with full rations; and

have continued in all other respects

to show love of honour both collectively and

individually concerning the cavalry and

the Tarantines, for good fortune,

the Tarantines shall decide to

praise the cavalry commanders and

the tribal commanders of cavalry, Nikogenes son

of Arkesas of Euonymon, Alkimachos

son of Kleoboulos of Myrrhinous,

Dionysios son of Pythokritos of Gargettos ,

Menon son of Hippolochides of Hippotomadai ,

Kallidemos son of Kallimachos of Euonymon ,

Kalliades son of Aristophon of Steiria ,

Lysippos son of Lysippos of Kettos ,

Charias son of Charias of Kephale ,

Antimachos son of Kleagoras of Acharnai ,

Proxenos son of Prokles of Phlya ,

Thrasykles son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia ,

Aristophon son of Aristoteles of Oinoe ,

Python son of Pytharchos of Alopeke ,

and to crown each of them with a gold

crown according to the law for their

justice and love of honour

for both the cavalry and the

squadron of Tarantines; and so that

the other cavalry commanders and tribal commanders

who are appointed show love of honour

concerning the cavalry and the

Tarantines, knowing that they will receive

thanks worthy of their benefactions,

the secretaries of the cavalry commanders

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele

and stand it at the stoa of the Herms.
