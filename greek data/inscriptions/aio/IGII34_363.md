# Dedication to Hermes by ephebes (Piraeus)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Those who were ephebes in the archonship of Sosikrates (111/0) (dedicated this)

to Hermes

Diogenes son of Dionysios of Amphitrope

Aischrion son of Parmon of Oe

Apelles son of Apollodoros of Kephisia

Aischylos son of Chares of Paiania

Asklepiades son of Athenodotos of Oinoe

Metrodoros son of Metrodoros of Myrrhinous

Phanias son of Lysikleides of Hybadai

Sotas son of Kreon of Marathon

Neandros son of Sotades of Oe

Thrason son of Euarchides of Kikynna

Under the physical trainership of Neon of Aphidna.
