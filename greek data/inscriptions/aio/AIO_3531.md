## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods

- son of Menedemos of Rhamnous proposed:

since - having been elected by the People general

for the coastal countryside in the archonship of Kimon (288/7 or 236/5 BC)

managed both the rest of the countryside well

[and the forts where?] the People assigned him well and

beneficially [for both the soldiers and?] for the citizens; and also managed

[the fortress of Rhamnous?] so that being under guard

[the fortress] might be kept safe for the People; and also managed both the

[soldiers and the other] citizens at Rhamnous so that

[...] they might obtain the provisions

[...] other things that the People allocated (?) to them

[...] continues both collectively for all and individually

[...] for everyone in need [...]

[...]
