# Citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

for good fortune, the People shall decide: that [...]

[...] shall be Athenian, himself and his descendants,

and be permitted to enrol in the tribe and deme

and phratry that he wishes; and the prytany shall put

the vote about him at the next Assembly;

and the prytany secretary shall

inscribe this decree on a stone stele on

the acropolis; and the administrator shall

allocate the expenditure for the inscribing and [the making]

of the stele from the fund for expenditure on decrees.
