# Dedication of structures in the Asklepieion, 51/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Diokles son of Diokles] of Kephisia , having become priest in the year of the archonship of L[ysia]des (51/0) ,

dedicated [...] the adjacent building and the exedra to Asklepios and Hygieia.
