# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] proxenos and benefactor

of the Athenian People,

both himself and his descendants;

and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis

at the [...] of Kekrops (?);

and for inscribing the

stele the treasurer of the People

shall give [] drachmas

from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
