# Honours for the priest of Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

and performed all the sacrifices in accordance with the decrees,

and has displayed seemly conduct,

fitting for the priesthood, for good

fortune, that the Council shall decide, that the

presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People that it seems good

to the Council to praise the priest of Asklepios

in the city, Me- son of - of -, and

crown him with a foliage crown for the piety

and honour-loving behaviour which he continues to show towards

the gods; and to inscribe the decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Asklepios;

and the treasurer of the military fund

shall allocate the expense accrued for the inscribing

of the stele.
