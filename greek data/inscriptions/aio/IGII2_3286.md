# Athens honours Hadrian

## English translation

Latin Text

To Publius Aelius Hadrian son of Publius of the Sergia tribe,

consul (108 AD) , one of the seven men of the banqueters, Augustal comrade, legate and propraetor of Emperor Nerva Trajan

Caesar Augustus Germanicus Dacicius during Pannonia Inferior, praetor and at the same

time legate of the legion I Minervia pious and loyal for the Dacian War, also tribune of the plebs, quaestor of Emperor

Trajan and member of his staff on the Dacian Expedition, twice awarded military honours by him, tribune of the legion II

Adiutrix pious and loyal, also of the legion V Macedonica, also of the legion XXII Primigenia pious and loyal, one of the six men

commanding a troop of cavalry of the Romans, prefect during the Latin Festival, one of the ten men for judging lawsuits

Greek Text

The Areopagos Council and the Council of the Six Hundred and the

People of the Athenians (erected this statue of) their own archon (112/3) ,

Hadrian.
