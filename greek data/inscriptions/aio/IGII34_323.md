# Dedication by the cavalry to Salamis

## English translation

**Part 1**

The cavalry dedicated this to Salamis.

Cavalry commander

Theogenes son of Theomedes of Eleusis

**Column 1**

Diphilos son of Philotades of Lamptrai

Nikias son of Euktaios of Xypete

Eustrophos son of Eugenides of Piraeus

Nikostratos son of Nikostratos of Cholargos

Eukles son of Lysias of Melite

Charamantides son of Sokles of Euonymon

Nausikles son of Nausigenes of Anagyrous

Philostratos son of Teisandros of Piraeus

Sokrates son of Eudramon of Aphidna

Aristoteles? [...] of Anagyrous

**Column 2**

In crown?

The People (crown)

Theogenes

of Eleusis

Phileas son of Diokles of Elaious

Smikythos son of Sosippos of Aixone

Kleophantos son of Kleophon of Eleusis

Euktaios son of Nikias of Xypete

Kallias son of Kallikrates of Kolonai

Sokles son of Aleximachos of Euonymon

Prokles son of Erxigenes of Kollytos

Eualkides son of Alkimachos of Kerameis

Epikrates son of Diophantos of Piraeus

Kallias son of Soinautes of Kolonai
