# Dedication to the saviour gods and goddesses of the emperors by the city

## English translation

**Face A**

To the saviour gods

of the brother-loving emperors

the city (dedicated this), with Herodes managing it.

**Face B**

To the saviour goddesses

of the brother-loving emperors

the city (dedicated this), with Herodes managing it.
