## English translation

**Part 1**

- son of -mos of Prospalta proposed:

since Philokles and Diodoros, unit commanders

appointed by Diomedes the general

in charge of the countryside in the archonship of Kl[earchos] (301/0) ,

managed well and with love of honour the

guarding of the fort and the general

and the citizen soldiers who were

stationed with them in Rhamnous,

and they continued to take great care,

speaking and acting in their

interests, showing the good will

that they have for them, for good fortune,

the soldiers shall decide: to praise Philokles

son of Prokles of Hekale, Diodoros son of Oinostratos

of Anagyrous, and to crown each

of them with a gold crown according to the law for

their excellence and the love of honour that they are found to have

towards both the general Diomedes

and the soldiers; and those of the soldiers who are chosen

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in Rhamnous in

the sanctuary of Dionysos; and for the inscription

of the stele and its erection the soldiers

shall give the cost ⟦ [...] ⟧
