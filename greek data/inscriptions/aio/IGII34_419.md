# Dedication by an ephebic team, 145/6 AD

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune

[In the archonship] of Flavius Arrianus of Paiania (145/6 AD),

in the superintendentship of Athenaios,

the team dedicated (this):

from their own resources, Apollophanes

son of Euphemos of Sphettos, polemarch of his fellow ephebes

Apollophanes son of Euphemos of Sphettos

Nouios son of Nouios of Athmonon

Hygeinos son of Pistokrates of Marathon

Menekrates son of Telesphoros of Philaidai

Eutychides son of Trophimos of Phegaia

Glykeros son of Timotheos

Karpodoros son of Karpodoros of Sounion

Eucharistos son of Paramonos of Epieikidai

Dionysios son of Pannychos of Kephisia

Lemnios son of Hermeios

Zenobios son of Philippos

Menodoros son of Herakleides

Controller: Euphemos son of Diodoros of Sphettos

Assistant controller: Sympheron son of

Euphemos of Sphettos

In the 10th year of the trainership of Abaskantos.
