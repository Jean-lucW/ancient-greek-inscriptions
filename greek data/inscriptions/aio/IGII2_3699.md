# Statue base for Marcus Ulpius Eubiotos

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune,

(this statue of) Marcus Ulpius

Eubiotos

the most brilliant

consular

and eponymous

archon,

(was erected) by decree

of the most revered

board

and of the whole

City,

(by) Aurelius Hermonax,

the herald

(for) their benefactor.
