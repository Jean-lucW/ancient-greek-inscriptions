# Honours for -das of Cos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the Athenian merchants and the

People in Samos and the others who

happen to be present, Athenians and

others, declare that

-das of Cos takes care of both the [merchants]

and the shippers,

so that the greatest abundance of grain

sails in for the Athenian People,

and that no

Athenian [suffers?] any [harm?],

but [...] is brought [safely?] to port, and in other respects displays

his good will towards any Athenian

who happens to be present, the Council

shall resolve: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside at the next

Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda [...]

[...]
