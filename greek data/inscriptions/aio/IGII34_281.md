# Dedication from Eleusis honouring Demetrios of Phaleron the Younger

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Athenians stationed by the People

in Eleusis and Panakton and at Phyle,

having crowned the general Demetrios son of Phanostratos

of Phaleron, dedicated (this statue of him) to Demeter and Kore.

**Column 1**

row 1

In crown

The Athenians

stationed

at Eleusis.

**Column 2**

row 1

In crown

The Athenians

stationed

at Panakton.

**Column 3**

row 1

In crown

The Athenians

stationed

at Phyle.

**Column 4**

row 1

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him),

having served as

cavalry commander.

**Column 1**

row 2

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him),

having served as

general.

**Column 2**

row 2

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him),

having served as

general.

**Column 3**

row 2

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown him),

having served as

general.

**Column 4**

row 2

In crown

The cavalry (crowns him),

having served as

cavalry commander.

**Part 2**

Sositheos of Athens made it.

On left side

In laurel crown

In the chariot race

at the Great

Panathenaia.

In laurel crown

The

Eleusinians.

On the right side

In laurel crown

In the chariot race

at the Delia.

In laurel crown

In the chariot race

at the Hermaia.
