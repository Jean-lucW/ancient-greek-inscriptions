# Honours for Athenians

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

for good will and

love of honour for the Council and the

Athenian People, and to praise also

- of - and Ameinias of Xypete

and - of [Phlya?] and the ships' officers

and the soldiers with them

who -ed the ship's breastwork

and crown them with a foliage crown;

and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in the court of -

- ; and for inscribing the

stele the inspector and the

trittys commanders shall give 20 drachmas.
