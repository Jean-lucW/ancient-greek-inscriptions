# Choregic dedication for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] was victorious [...]

[...] was sponsor,

[when] there was a struggle between the twelve [Athenian] tribes,

[...] awarded first prize

[...] of - of Berenikidai.

[The victors were] the namesakes [of [...] ], and they sang

[what [...] ] of Laodikea [skilfully set to music].
