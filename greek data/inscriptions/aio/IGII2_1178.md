# Decree of Ikarion honouring demarch and choregoi

## English translation

**Part 1**

Painting?

Kallippos proposed: that the Ikarians shall resolve

to praise Nikon the demarch and

crown him with an ivy crown, and the herald

shall announce that the Ikarians crown

Nikon and the deme of the Ikarians their

demarch, for his fine and just conduct

of the festival for Dionysos and the competition;

and to praise also the choral sponsors

Epikrates and Praxias and crown them with an ivy

crown and announce it as for the

demarch.
