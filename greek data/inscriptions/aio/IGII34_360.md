# Dedication to Hermes by ephebes (Piraeus)

## English translation

**Face-front a**

The ephebe in the archonship of Herakleides (104/3) (dedicated this)

to Hermes

Xenon son of Theodosios of Elaious

uninscribed space

Under the physical trainership of Neon of Aphidna.

**Face b (top)**

[[Parmeniskos son of Theogenes of Phegous

Mimnokles son of So[nautes?] of Eleusis

Zopyr- son of – of –

(dedicated this) to Hermes

under the physical trainership of –]] of Kolonos.
