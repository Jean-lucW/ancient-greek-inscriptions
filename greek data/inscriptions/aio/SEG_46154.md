# Decree of Aixone awarding honours for management of a festival

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] -mos son of Phi-

[...] Kalli-es son of Leaios,

Apollodoros son of Kalliades

took care well and with love of honour

of the festival for

, the demesmen shall decide

to praise them and

crown each of them

with a gold crown of

five hundred drachmas for their

justice and love of honour

towards the demesmen;

and to inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and

stand it [...]
