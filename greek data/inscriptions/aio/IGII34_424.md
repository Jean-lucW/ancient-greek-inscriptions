# Dedication by member of ephebic staff

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aurelius Parrhasios son of Eutychides of Phyle dedicated (this)

when Publius Aelius son of Alkidamos of Cholleidai was general, when

Aelius Chrysos of Sphettos was herald of the Council.
