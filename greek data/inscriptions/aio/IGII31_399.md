# On Euboean affairs

## English translation

The People decided. Hegesippos [son of Hegesias of Sounion proposed]:

so that none of the allies [of the Athenians?], nor anyone

[of the Athenians?], whether foreigner or citizen, shall harm

any of the allies, setting out from anywhere [in Attica] or

the allied cities, the People

shall resolve: concerning those who have invaded

the territory of the Eretrians, that the Council shall formulate

and bring forward a proposal to the People at the next Assembly,

so that they may be punished according to the [laws?]; and if

in future anyone invades Eretria

or any other allied city, anyone of the Athenians
