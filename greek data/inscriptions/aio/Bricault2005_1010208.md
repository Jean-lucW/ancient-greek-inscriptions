# Dedication relating to a basket-bearer of Sarapis and Isis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] (daughter?) of Aphrodeisios of

Marathon, having been basket-bearer

of Sarapis and

Isis, dedicated (this)

in the priesthood

of Pamphilos son

of Antigenes of

Melite.
