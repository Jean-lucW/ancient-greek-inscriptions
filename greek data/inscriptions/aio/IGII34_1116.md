# Dedication to Isis and Dikaiosynē

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

To Isis, to Dikaiosyne

Ktesikles son of Apollodoros

of Halimous dedicated (this)

in the priesthood of Sosos

son of Charmides of Aithalidai

One line erased

when the temple attendant

was Zopyros of Miletos.
