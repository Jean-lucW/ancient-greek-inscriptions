# Herm marking mid-point between Athens and Kephale

## Greek text

**Part 1**

[ἐ]ν μℎέσοι Κεφαλε͂ς τε καὶ ἄστεος ἀγλαὸς ℎερμε͂ς.

[μνε͂μα τόδ' ℎιππάρχο· –⏑⏑ –⏑⏑ –]

## English translation

At the mid-point of Kephale and the city, splendid Hermes.

[This (is) a monument of Hipparchos. Moral maxim .]
