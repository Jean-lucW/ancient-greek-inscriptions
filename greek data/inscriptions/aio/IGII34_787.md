# Dedication to [Asklepios] and Hygieia by a priest

## English translation

**Part 1**

Asopodoros son of Kleomenes

of Phlya , having become priest

in the year of the archonship

of – (dedicated this) [to Asklepios] and Hygieia,

the keyholder being -chos son of Sokrates

of Phlya [...] the basket-bearer being Diodora

daughter of – of Philadai.
