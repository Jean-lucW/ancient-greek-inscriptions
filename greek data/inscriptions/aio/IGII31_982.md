# Honours for a friend of Antigonos Gonatas

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of [Philostratos (260/59) ], in

the fifth prytany, of -,

for which - son of Phanopompos of Potamos

was secretary. On the -

of Posideon. Principal Assembly.

Of the presiding committee An-

son of of Acharnai was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided.

- son of - of - proposed:

concerning what the ambassadors sent to [Nik?]aia say

about Theog-

[...] and declare that he is

of good will to the People and speaks

and does whatever good he can,

and that for the Athenians on the embassy

to the king he was helpful

in the renewal of friendship

with king Antigonos;

in order therefore that the People may be seen

[to maintain its thanks] to the king

[...]
