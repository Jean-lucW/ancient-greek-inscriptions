# Proxeny for Mikalion of Alexandria

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the Athenian People and presents himself

as ever eager to give individually to all

whatever each needs, for good

fortune, the People shall decide: to praise

Mikalion son of Philon of Alexandria

and to crown him with a gold crown

according to the law for his excellence and

good will towards the Athenian People;

that Mikalion and his descendants

shall be proxenoi and benefactors

of the Athenian People; and they shall have

right of ownership of land and a house

according to the law, so that everyone else may know

that the People honours those

displaying good will to it as

each deserves; and the secretary of the People

shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the

acropolis; and for inscribing the

stele the trittys commanders

and the inspector shall give 30 drachmas

from the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees.
