# Ratification of a treaty with the Klazomenians at Daphnous

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council and the People decided. [ ]

was the prytany. Krates was secretary. Epigenes

was chairman. Alkibiades proposed: that the agreement

which the generals made with those living

at Daphnous shall apply to them in accordance with [what has been laid down],

since they have been good men; and the

secretary of the Council shall inscribe on a stone

stele on the acropolis the agreement and

this decree [...]
