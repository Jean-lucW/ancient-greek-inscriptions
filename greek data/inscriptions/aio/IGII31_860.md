# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] to crown them

with a crown of ivy; and they

shall have [equality of taxation and]

the right of ownership of land and a house according

to the law; and the [recorder] shall

inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand

it on the acropolis; and the administrator

[shall give] for the inscription [of the stele].
