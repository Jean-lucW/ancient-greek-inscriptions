# Dedication to Zeus Hypsistos by Julia Asklepiane

## English translation

**Part 1**

Statue of eagle

For Good Fortune,

Julia Asklepiane,

to the god Hypsistos on behalf

of her son Maximus,

set up this thank-offering.

capital
