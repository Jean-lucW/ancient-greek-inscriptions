# Statue base for Marcus Aurelius Prosdektos

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Demeter and the Girl,

the Sacred Gerousia (dedicated this statue of) Marcus Aurelius

bearer of the sacred stone Prosdektos

son of Pistokrates of Kephale,

having served as envoy at his own expense,

and been honoured by the God

Commodus with Roman

citizenship, having been archon of the

genos of the Kerykes, having been archon

of the Sacred Gerousia,

on account of his piety.

Attikos son of Eudoxos of Sphettos made it.
