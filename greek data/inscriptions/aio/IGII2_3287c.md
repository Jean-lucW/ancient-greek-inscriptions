# Athens honours Hadrian

## English translation

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar, son of the God

Trajan Parthicus, grandson of the God Nerva,

Hadrian Augustus (was erected by) the Areopagos

Council and the Council of the 600 and the People,

the tribe Oineis managing it.
