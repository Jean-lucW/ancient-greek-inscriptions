# Dedication to Asklepios and Hygieia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Dedication 1 (front) 91/2 AD

– of Eirenaios of Phyle

dedicated to Asklepios and Hygeia, a votive

on behalf of her children, in the priesthood for life of Gaius Cassius

– of Kollytos, and under the management of

Titus Coponius Maximus of Hagnous,

and in the zakorate of Alexandros son of Alexandros of Oe,

and in the archonship of Quintus Trebellius Rufus of

Lamptrai (91/2) .

Dedication 2 (right side) 2nd cent. AD

Dekmos son of Theomnestos

of Acharnai to Asklepios

and Hygeia.
