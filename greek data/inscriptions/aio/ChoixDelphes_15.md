# Athenian dedication on the Marathon Base at Delphi

## Greek text

**Part 1**

A

⟦Ἀθεναῖοι το͂ι Ἀπόλλονι ἀπὸ Μέδον ἀκροθίνια τε͂ς Μαραθο͂νι μάχες⟧.

**Part 2**

B

Ἀθεναῖοι τ[ο͂]ι Ἀπόλλον[ι ἀπὸ Μέδ]ον ἀκροθίνια τε͂ς Μαραθ[ο͂]νι μν[άχες].

## English translation

**Part 1**

A

⟦The Athenians (dedicated this) to Apollo from the Medes, as best spoils of the battle in Marathon⟧.

**Part 2**

B

The Athenians (dedicated this) to Apollo [from the Medes] as best spoils of the [battle] in Marathon.
