Eukles son of Eukles of Berenikidai, and the pipe-player, Neokles of

Berenikidai, and crown each of them with a foliage crown; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a

stone stele and stand it [in the prytanikon]; and for the inscribing of the

stele and the setting up the administrator shall allocate

the expense accrued.
