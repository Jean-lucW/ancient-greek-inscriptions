# Anthippasia dedication of Pandionis

## English translation

**Part 1**

The commanders of the tribal contingent of cavalry who were victorious in the anthippasia:

Demainetos son of Demeas of Paiania

Demeas son of Demainetos of Paiania

Demosthenes son of Demainetos of Paiania.

Bryaxis made it.
