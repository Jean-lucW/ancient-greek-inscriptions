# Erechtheion building accounts, 409/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The overseers of the temple on the acropolis in which the ancient statue is, Brosynides

of Kephisia, Chariades of Agryle, Diodes of Kephisia, master of the works

Philokles of Acharnai, secretary Etearchos of Kydathenaion,

wrote up these works on the temple in the state in which they took them over, according to the

decree of the People which Epigenes proposed, those that were complete and those half-finished, in

the archonship of Diokles (409/8) , in the first prytany, of Kekropis , under the Council

for which Nikophanes of Marathon was the first secretary.

**Column 1**

We took over these parts of the temple half finished:

at the corner towards the Kekropion:

4 blocks unplaced: length four feet,

width two feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

1 maschaliaia: length four feet,

width three feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

5 blocks for the wall-capital: length four feet,

width three feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

_____

1 corner block: length seven feet,

width four feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

_____

1 moulded stone, unplaced,

corresponding to the wall-capitals: length

ten feet, height one

and a half feet;

_____

2 corresponding to the architrave:

length four feet, width one and

a quarter feet;

1 column capital, unplaced, for the

interior pilaster: length three feet,

width one and a half feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

5 architrave blocks, unplaced: length eight

feet, width two and a

quarter feet, thickness two feet;

3 architrave blocks up in place,

to be finished: length eight feet,

width two feet and a quarter,

thickness two feet.

The course of Eleusinian stone against which will be

the figures begins all of the

remaining work all round, and three blocks were placed

under these overseers.

Of the columns on the wall

by the Pandroseion,

of the 4 columns in position,

_____

one and a half foot was uncut

from the inner face of the floral ornament

of each column.

_____

An eight-foot architrave block

on the wall to the south

needed to have its moulding

added on the inside.

_____

These were unsmoothed and

unchannelled:

the wall towards the south

wind, unsmoothed

except in the porch

by the Kekropion;

the orthostates unsmoothed

on the outside all round

except those in the porch

by the Kekropion;

all the rounded column-bases

unfluted on the top;

all the columns unfluted

except the ones on the wall; the substructure

all around unsmoothed;

of the wall inside the unsmoothed parts

were 8 four-footers of moulded stone;

of that in the Prostomiaion,

12 four-footers;

of the parastas,

7 four-footers;

_____

of that towards the statue,

two four-footers.

_____

In the porch in front of

the doorway,

the altar of the Thyechoos

is unplaced;

_____

the rafters and

cross-pieces of the roof are unplaced.

_____

In the porch at the

Kekropion are needed

_____

3 stones for the roof

over the maidens to be dressed

on the top side: length

thirteen feet, width five

feet;

_____

the rosettes on the architrave

blocks need

to be finished.

Stones completely worked which are on the ground:

11 blocks: length four feet,

width two feet, thickness

one and a half feet;

_____

1 maschalaia: length four feet,

width three feet, thickness

one and a half feet.
