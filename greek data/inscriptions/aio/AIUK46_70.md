# Kioniskos of Mysta

## Greek text

Μύστα Διονυσίου

Μιλησία, Βάτωνος

Θριασίου γυνή.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Mysta (daughter) of Dionysios

of Miletos, wife of Baton

of Thria.
