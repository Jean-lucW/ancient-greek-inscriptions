# Honours for Diocletian

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Emperor Caesar Gaius Valerius Diocletian pious, lucky,

unconquered Augustus, strongest

and above all previous

emperors most pious Augustus,

Lucius Turrianus Gratianus, most brilliant man, corrector

of the province of Achaia, devoted

to his spirit and majesty, (erected this).
