# List of sacred delegates (<i>theoroi</i>) participating in the second Hellenistic <i>Pythais</i> at Delphi, 128/7 BC

## English translation

**Column 1**

For Good Fortune, in the archonship of Pyrrhos at Delphi,

and of Dionysios after Lykiskos in Athens (128/7) ,

the following were dispatched as sacred delegates by the People

of the Athenians to help conduct

the Pythais.

uninscribed space

Demainetos son of Demeas

Eudemos son of Sarapion, by birth son of Gorgippos

-anos son of Gorgippos

Chares son of Chares, the younger

Ptolemaios son of Euboulos

Euboulos son of Euboulos, Dionysios son of Euboulos

Rhadamanthys son of Attinas

Sostratos son of Alexandros

Xenon son of Philanthes

Lysias son of Pyrrhos.

uninscribed space

**Column 2**

Sacred delegates from the Tetrapolis:

Metrodoros son of Kallistratos

Damokritos son of Damokritos

Heniochides son of Euphiletos

from Pyrrhakidai:

Timanax son of Eukles of Phlya.
