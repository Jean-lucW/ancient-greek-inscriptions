# Honours for Tiberius

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Tiberius Caesar the God

Augustus, son of the Gods Augusti was dedicated by

Publius Occius Anteros and Ariston

son of Teimoxenos of Pagai,

just as Ariston (son of Ariston) of Pagai

instructed them in his will.
