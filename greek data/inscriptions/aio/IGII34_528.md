# Dedication commemorating competition-direction and other offices of Glaukon of Aithalidai

## English translation

**Face-front a**

The People were the sponsor, Nikias was archon (282/1 or 266/5)

Competition-director: Glaukon son of Eteokles of Aithalidai.

Leontis was victorious in the men’s competition. Hippokles the Boiotian

was pipe-player. Theodorides the Boiotian directed.

**Face-right b**

In olive crown (I)

in the anthippasia

at the Olympieia.

In olive crown (II)

The People (crowned)

him, when he had been

commander of the tribal cavalry contingent.

In olive crown (III)

in the anthippasia

at the Great

Panathenaia.

Olive crown (IV)

[...]

**Face-left c**

In olive crown (V)

when he had been

hoplite

general.

In ivy crown (VI)

The People (crowned)

him, when he had been

Competition-director.

In olive crown (VII)

when he had been

hoplite

general.

Crown (VIII)

[...]
