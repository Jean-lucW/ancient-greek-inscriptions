# Honours for Herakleitos of Athmonon

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the People [having been about to hold]

[...] the sacrifice and the [ of the] Panathenaia,

he both makes ready the stadium in a seemly way and dedicates

stelai to Athena Nike with

reminders of the deeds of ⟦the king⟧ against the

barbarians for the preservation of the Greeks;

so that the Council and the People may be seen to be maintaining

their gratitude to ⟦the kings⟧, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide to praise Herakleitos

son of Asklepiades of Athmonon and to crown him with

a gold crown for his piety to the gods

and the good will and love of honour which he continues

to have both for ⟦king Antigonos and⟧ the Council

and the Athenian People; and the [competition-masters]

shall proclaim [this] crown at the gymnastic competition

[...] according to the law; and the administrator

shall take care of the making of the crown;

and so that there may be a reminder of his love

of honour, the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a stele; and for the stele

the administrator shall allocate the expenditure

accrued.
