# Honours for a prytany

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

[... and his fellow presiding committee members.]

[The People decided.]

[...] proposed: concerning what

the prytany of - reports about the sacrifices which they made

before the Assemblies to Apollo Prostaterios and

Artemis Boulaia and the other gods to whom

it is traditional, and managed the convening of the Council

and the People and everything else that the laws and decrees

of the People prescribed for them; for good

fortune, the People shall decide to accept the good things that the prytany

report occurred in the sacrifices that they made

for the health and preservation of the Council and the Athenian People,

and to praise the prytany of - and to crown

them with a gold crown according to the law for their piety

towards the gods and love of honour towards the Athenian People

[...]
