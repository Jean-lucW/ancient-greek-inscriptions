# Choregic dedication of Eurymenes for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

**Subcolumn 1**

Oineis

was victorious

in the boys’ competition

**Subcolumn 2**

Eurymenes

son of Meleteon

was sponsor

**Subcolumn 3**

Nikostratos

was director.
