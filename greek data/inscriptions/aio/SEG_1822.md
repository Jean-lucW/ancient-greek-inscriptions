# Honours for the priest of Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Pelops (165/4 or 150/49?) , in the twelfth prytany,

of Ptolemais . Skirophorion, on the sixteenth

of the prytany. Assembly in the theatre.

The Council and People decided. Aischeas son of Theopeithes

of Kephisia proposed. Since the priest of Asklepios

in the city , Protagoras son of Niketes of Pergase, having

made an approach to the Council, has reported

on the sacrifices which he has performed and the fine and salutary rites

for all Athenians and those who dwell in the cities

of the Athenians, and has supervised the

good order of the sanctuary and performed all the sacrifices in accordance with the

decrees, and has displayed seemly conduct,

fitting for the priesthood, for good fortune, the Council shall decide

that the presiding committee allotted to preside at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put these matters on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People that it seems good to the Council

to praise the priest of Asklepios in the city

and crown him with a foliage crown

for the piety and love of honour which he continues to show

towards the gods; and to inscribe the decree on a

stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Asklepios;

and the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the expense

accrued for the inscribing of the stele.

In crown

The Council,

the People (crown)

the priest,

Protagoras

of Pergase.
