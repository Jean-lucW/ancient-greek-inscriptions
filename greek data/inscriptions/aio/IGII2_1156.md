Decree 1 (tribe Kekropis)

Kallikrates of Aixone proposed: since the ephebes of Kekropis

in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) show good discipline and do

everything that the laws require of them, and obey their controller,

who was elected by the People, praise

them and crown them with a gold crown of 500 drachmas

for their good order and discipline; and praise their controller,

Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon, and crown him with a gold

crown of 500 drachmas because he managed well and with love of honour

the ephebes of the Kekropid tribe ; and inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in the sanctuary of Kekrops.

Decree 2 (Council)

Hegemachos son of Chairemon of Perithoidai proposed: since the ephebes

of Kekropis marshalled at Eleusis manage well and with love of honour

the things that the Council and People require of them, and

show themselves well-disciplined, praise them for their good order

and discipline and crown each of them with a foliage crown;

and praise their controller, Adeistos son of

Antimachos of Athmonon, and crown him with a foliage crown when

he has rendered his accounts; and inscribe this decree on the dedication

which the ephebes of Kekropis are dedicating.

Decree 3 (Deme Eleusis)

Protias proposed: the demesmen shall resolve, since the ephebes of Kekropis

and their controller, Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon,

manage the guarding of Eleusis well and with love of honour,

praise them and crown each of them with a foliage crown;

and inscribe this decree on the dedication which

the ephebes of Kekropis of the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3)

are dedicating.

Decree 4 (Deme Athmonon)

Euphronios proposed: the demesmen shall resolve, since the ephebes

enrolled in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) show good discipline and

do everything that the laws require of them, and their

controller, elected by the People, declares that they

are obedient and do everything else with love of honour,

praise them and crown them with a gold crown of 500 drachmas

for their good order and discipline; and praise their

controller, Adeistos son of Antimachos of Athmonon, and crown

him with a gold crown of 500 drachmas, because he managed well and with love of honour

the demesmen and all the others of the Kekropid

tribe ; and inscribe this decree on the dedication which

the ephebes of Kekropis and their controller are dedicating.
