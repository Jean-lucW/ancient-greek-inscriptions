# Choregic dedication of a sponsor for the Thargelia, 354/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] -os son of A- [...]

of Euonymon, serving as sponsor,

was victorious for Erechtheis (and) Antiochis

in the boys' competition, Oiniades son of Pronomos

of Thebes was pipe-player, Antiphilos

of Megara was director,

Diotimos (354/3) was archon.
