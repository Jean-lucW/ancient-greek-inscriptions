# Pompeiopolis in Paphlagonia honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

[(This statue of) Emperor] Caesar Trajan

[Hadrian] Augustus Olympios

(was erected by) the mother-city of  
Paphlagonia,

Pompeiopolis, through the envoys

– son of Le- and

– son of Theopropion –,

in the priesthood of Claudius Attikos.
