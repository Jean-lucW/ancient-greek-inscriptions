# Honours for Charixenos of Aitolia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] sacred recorder [...]

[...] for good fortune, the Council shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted to preside

at the coming Assembly shall put the matter

on the agenda and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council to

praise Charixenos son of Kydrion of Aitolia and

to crown him with a gold crown according to the

law for his excellence and love of honour for

the Athenian People [...]
