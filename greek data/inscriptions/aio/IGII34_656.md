# Dedication to Dionysos by the leader of a thiasos

## English translation

**Part 1**

For you, king, Dionysios founded this temple here,

a fragrant precinct and wooden statues in your image

and everything, having chosen not to pile up domestic wealth and silver

riches, so as to pay you, Bakchos, the customary reverences;

in return for which, O Dionysos, be kind and preserve him, his house

and his descendance, and your entire association.
