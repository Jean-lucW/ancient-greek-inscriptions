# Grave stele of Theophile

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Relief

σῆς ἀρετῆς μνημεῖα,

Θεοφίλη, οὔποτε λήσει,

σώφρων καὶ χρηστὴ καὶ

ἐργάτις πᾶσαν ἔχουσα

ἀρετήν.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Records of your excellence,

Theophile, will never pass unnoticed,

self-controlled and useful and

hard-working, possessing every

excellence.
