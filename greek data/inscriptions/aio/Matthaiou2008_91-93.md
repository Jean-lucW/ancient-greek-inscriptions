Decree of Kollytos

Eukadmides proposed: [...] for all the gods

and heroes [...]

what has been decided [...] to make a sacrifice and an approach

if things turn out well [...] for good fortune

the men of Kollytos shall decide that - and the demarch

shall offer to all the gods and heroes

cakes and liquid meal [...] after the archonship

of Hegemon (327/6) ; and all the revenues of the demesmen

shall be made available for good fortune for the preservation of the Athenian

People, 2000 drachmas; and if any of the demesmen

wish to lend on the security of the common funds

and the revenues [...]

any [...] the [...]

of those who have lent [...] the revenues [...] the

year after the archonship of Hegemon (327/6) ; and the demarch

in office shall [...] to those who have lent
