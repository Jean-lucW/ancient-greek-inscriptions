# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Antiochis

## English translation

**Part 1**

in pediment

[...] (crowns) [the treasurer],

[Marcus Fulvius] -os (son of -os)

moulding

[Since, having made an approach], the prytaneis of

[Antiochis declare] that Marcus Fulvius

[-os (son of -os) of Besa, the treasurer], has given forethought

[for money from his own resources] and has also taken care of the [shared]

[benefits to the Council and] the prytaneis

[of Antiochis themselves, displaying] all enthusiasm,

[and for this they call (on the Council)] to allow them to make

the dedication [of a painted image of him] in the Asklepieion,

[bearing this inscription: "The] prytaneis of Antiochis

[in the archonship of - and those with perpetual dining rights

[dedicated (this image of)] their own treasurer, [Marcus Fulvius -os (son of -os) of Besa],

[on account of his friendship and enthusiasm and] forethought for money,"

[for good fortune], the Council shall decide to praise

[Marcus Fulvius -os (son of -os)] of Besa for his [enthusiasm] regarding the

[people of] Besa's [deme and for] his skill regarding the

[rest of the prytaneis and] to crown him with a foliage

[crown, with which it is traditional] to crown good

[men, and also to allow] the prytaneis

and those with perpetual dining rights to make] the [dedication of the image] of him

[in the Asklepieion], so that, when these things are done

[and this] witness [exists], those who even privately

[...] shall became providers [...]
