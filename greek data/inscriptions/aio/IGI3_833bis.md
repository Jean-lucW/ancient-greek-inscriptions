# Verse dedication by a [poet] commemorating a victory with a men's chorus

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] in Athens with a men's chorus

[...] of (his?) wisdom dedicated this marker

having fulfilled a vow. With the most choruses elsewhere

among the tribes of men he has been victorious, he says, (in competition) for a tripod.
