## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods. Fortune.

Of her (i.e. the goddess Nemesis) in Hermos.

On the following terms the demesmen of the section

of Archippos and Stesias lease the precinct of

the goddess in Hermos, which Hierokles previously

farmed, which the estate of Hierophon neighbours to the north;

and the lessee shall farm as follows,

tilling the land in alternate years, (planting) the space between the rows with wheat

and barley, and half of the half with pulses,

and the remaining land he will immediately leave fallow; and the

term of the lease for which he will farm will be ten years;

and the lease begins in the next archonship after the archonship

of Lysimachides (339/8 BC) ; and he will pay the rent

to the [meeting] in the month of Posideon

on the day of the Assemblies; and he will dig the vines twice

each year, in one year raising them up

at the base, in the other digging them out

around the base; and he will treat the other cultivated trees in the same

way; and he will stake the vines, the new ones before they

develop, the old ones whenever he wishes;

and the lessee will inhabit the house on this

precinct and will hand it over when he

departs from the precinct, roofed and in good condition, as he

took it over; he will leave all the chaff that is produced

on the estate and the dung and

all the stakes which he may use;

and he will plant the plants in rows, not omitting the

fig trees; and he will plant twelve new plants each

year, and will plant as many olives

as the lessors require of him; [...] [wheat?] [...]
