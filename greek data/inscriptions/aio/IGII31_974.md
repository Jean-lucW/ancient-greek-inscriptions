# Citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that [the presiding committee allotted]

to preside shall bring him before the People

and submit the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems

good to the Council to praise [...]

of - and to crown him with a gold crown according to the law

for his love of honour and the good will that he continues to have for the

Athenian People; and he shall be Athenian, and

his descendants, and shall enrol in the tribe and deme and phratry

that he wishes except those the laws forbid; and the

prytany shall put the vote at the next Assembly;

and the court presidents shall submit to the court for him

the scrutiny of his citizenship according to the law;

and in order that there may be a memorial [of the award given]

[by the] People, [the secretary of the] People shall inscribe [this decree]

[on a stone stele] [...]
