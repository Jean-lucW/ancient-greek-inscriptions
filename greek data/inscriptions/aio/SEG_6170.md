# Choregic dedication of a sponsor for the Thargelia

## Greek text

**Part 1**

[...] [...] ος Μνεσάρχο Ἀχαρνεύς

[ἐχορέγε] Ο̣ἰνείδι Κεκροπίδι παίδον

[...] [...] Ἰθαιγένος ἐδίδασκε

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] -os son of Mnesarchos of Acharnai

[was sponsor] for Oineis (and) Kekropis in the boys’ competition

[...] son of Ithaigenes was director.
