# Choregic dedication of Megakleides for the Thargelia, 349/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Megakleides son of Megakleides

of Eleusis, serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Hippothontis (and) Kekropis in the boys’ competition.

uninscribed space

Kallistratos of Tegea was pipe-player,

Pheidias of Opous was director,

Kallimachos (349/8) was archon.
