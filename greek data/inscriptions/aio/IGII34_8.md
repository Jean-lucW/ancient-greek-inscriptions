# Dedication of the Council to Aphrodite Hegemone

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council in the archonship of Dionysios (191/0?) dedicated

to Aphrodite Hegemone of the People and to the Graces,

when Mikion son of Eurykleides of Kephisia was priest,

when Theoboulos son of Theophanes of Piraeus was general in charge of equipment.
