# Funerary stele of Chairestrate, attendant of the Mother

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Chairestrate]

[wife of Men]ekr[ates]

of Ikarion.

Two rosettes

An attendant of the Mother Begetter-of-all

and awesome reverend woman lies in this tomb,

Chairestrate, whom her husband loved

while she was alive and mourned when she died.

But she left the light fortunate, having lived to see her children’s children.

Relief
