# Honours for the archon Euthios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Nikias (282/1) , in the seventh

prytany, of Oineis , for which Theophilos son of Theodotos

of Acharnai was secretary. On the ninth of

Gamelion, the twenty-third

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Oinokrates son of Oinobios of Eleusis was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The

People decided. Agyrrhios son of Kallimedon

of Kollytos proposed: since Euthios when he was

archon (283/2) performed the sacrifices to the

gods according to tradition and managed

the procession for Dionysos with love of honour and

did everything else concerning his office

justly and and in obedience to the laws and

decrees of the Council and People,

and because of this previously the People

praised him and crowned him in

the Assembly in the theatre of Dionysos; so that

it might be apparent to everyone that the People, now

and in the future, will honour those

serving in their offices justly and according

to the laws, for good fortune, the People

shall decide: to praise Euthios son of Antiphon

of Teithras for the love of honour and good will

which he continues to have for the People,

and to crown him with a gold crown

according to the law; and he shall be able

to obtain other benefits from the People when

he is judged to be worthy, when the Piraeus

and the city are united; and to praise

his assistants Meidogenes

son of Meidon of Athmonon , Sokrates son

of Sodamos of Paiania for their justice

and love of honour, and to crown each

of them with a gold crown according to the law;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in front of the synedrion;

and for inscribing the stele

the board of administrators shall allocate 10

drachmas.

In crown

The People
