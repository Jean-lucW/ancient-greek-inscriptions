# Choregic monument of Thrasyllos, 320/19 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Thrasyllos son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia dedicated (this)

having been victorious sponsor in the men’s competition for the tribe Hippothontis

Neaichmos was archon (320/19)

**Column 1**

Euios of Chalkis was pipe-player

**Column 2**

Karkidamos was director
