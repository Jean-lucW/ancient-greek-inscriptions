# 

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe

this decree

on a stone stele and

stand it on the acropolis; and

for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give

20 drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure

on decrees.

In crown

The People.
