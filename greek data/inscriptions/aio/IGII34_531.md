# Dedication by the competition-director, Thrasykles son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia, 271/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Block A (Base of left foot of tripod)

The People were the sponsor. Pytharatos was archon (271/0 BC) .

Competition-director Thrasykles son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia

Hippothontis was victorious in the boys’ competition

Theon of Thebes was pipe-player

Pronomos of Thebes was director

**Part 2**

Block B (Base of right foot of tripod)

The People were the sponsor. Pytharatos was archon (271/0 BC) .

Competition-director Thrasykles son of Thrasyllos of Dekeleia

Pandionis was victorious in the mens’ competition

Nikokles of Ambracia was pipe-player

Lysippos of Arcadia was director
