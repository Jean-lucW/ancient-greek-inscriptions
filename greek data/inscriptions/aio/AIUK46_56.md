# Lekythos of Pamphilos and Archippe

## Greek text

Πάμφιλος Μειξιάδου ⋮ Ἀρχίππη

Αἰγιλιεύς. ⋮ Μειξιάδου.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Pamphilos (son) of Meixiades ⋮ Archippe

of Aigilia ⋮ (daughter) of Meixiades.
