# Decree of Halimous honouring Charisandros

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

Theophilos proposed: since

Charisandros managed

the sacrifices well

and with love of honour on behalf of the

demarch Ischyrias,

the demesmen having chosen him to manage

whatever religious rites

the demesmen

prescribed, the Halimousians shall

resolve to praise

Charisandros son of Charisiades

and crown him

with a gold crown

of five hundred drachmas

for his excellence and

his justice towards the

demesmen; and the demarch

Kybernis shall inscribe

the decree in the common

records and on a

stone stele and stand

it in the Dionysion.
