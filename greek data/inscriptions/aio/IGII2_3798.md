# Areopagos, Council, and People honour the temple attendant Sozon (101/2)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos Council

and the Council of 600

and the People (erected this herm of) Sozon

son of Ladikos of Sounion,

doctor, having been temple attendant

of Asklepios and Hygieia

in the archonship of

Stratolaos (101/2) , the keyholder being

Sozon (son of Sozon) of Sounion,

uninscribed space

the manager being Coponius

Maximus, sacred

herald.

phallus
