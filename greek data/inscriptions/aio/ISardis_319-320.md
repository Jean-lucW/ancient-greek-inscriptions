## English translation

**Part 1**

**Side a**

(Sardis VII 2, 319)

[...]

to the established (?) Hadrianeion [...]

and congratulating [you on your] prosperity

[resulting from this] man and these things we [...] ,

praying that all those who come from the same place to be resident

here prove to be of this sort,

we pray to the ancestral gods that

you may be well.

**Side b**

(Sardis VII 2, 320)

[...]

let it be left.” The presiding officer

put the question, “Is it decided that a letter should be written to the

the most mighty Sardians about the

most noteworthy Polybios?” (?)

“What do you think?” “Let a memorandum be passed!”

Having been called in, Polybios said,

“I consider no other blessing

more magnificent than my present one,

men of the Areopagos. I have the good fortune

to stand before you and to speak and

to enjoy the testimonial from you,

which even the gods thought highly of,

when they received it.” They shouted out,

“You are worthy!”
