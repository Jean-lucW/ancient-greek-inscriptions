# Dedication by an ephebic gymnasiarch

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship

of Theon (95/6 AD) ,

under the superintendent

Dionysios, Markos (son of Markos)

of Anaphlystos

having

been

gymnasiarch

(dedicated this).

Relief
