# Decree relating to Sestos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] -oxen- [...]

[...] Athen- [...]

[...] the [...]

the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis within ten

days; and the archon shall stand it in Sestos

in the agora; and the Council shall take care of them

so that they may now [...] and as

safely as possible; and to praise them for their manly virtue

towards the Athenian People

and invite them to dinner in the city hall

tomorrow.
