# Decree renewing friendship with the Milesians

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the [Milesian?] People [...]

[...] Aristaichmos [...]

[...] and submit the opinion of the Council to the People

that it seems in good to it to renew the existing [relations and]

friendship [towards the Milesian People],

and the citizenship, for Andrios [...]

Maiandrios, residing with us; and the wall-builders

[and the master of the works] shall take care of the stele

and let out the contract for inscribing the decree [...]
