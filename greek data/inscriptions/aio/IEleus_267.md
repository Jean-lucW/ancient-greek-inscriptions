# Honours for a basket-bearer (and hearth initiate?)

## English translation

**Column 1**

in crown

[The Council],

[the People]

[(crown) her]

[having been basket-bearer]

[...]

[...]

in crown

The Council,

the People

(crown) her,

having been basket-bearer

to Asklepios.

**Column 2**

in crown

The Council,

the People

(crown) her,

having been basket-bearer

to the Mother of the gods.

in crown

The Council,

the People

(crown) her,

having been basket-bearer

to Aphrodite

of Alopeke.

**Part 3**

when the priestess was

Ameinokleia daughter of Phil[anthes of Phyle].

crown
