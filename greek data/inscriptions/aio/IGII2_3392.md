# Honours for Antoninus Pius

## English translation

**Part 1**

[To the best] Emperor Caesar Titus

Aelius Hadrianus Antoninus Pius, Father

of the Fatherland (this statue was dedicated by) the priest for life of [– Lucius]

Cornelius Attikos, also called [Menestheus]

[of Azenia, in the archonship of - ]
