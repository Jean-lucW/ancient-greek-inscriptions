# Statue base for the hierophant who saved the sacred objects of the Mysteries

## English translation

**Part 1**

You gaze upon this high-shining monument of Deo’s companion

who in wisdom gained infinite glory.

He displayed the ceremonies and the all-night rites to the initiates,

pouring forth the charming voice of Eumolpos,

and he did not flee from the onslaught of the hostile, but saved

undefiled the secret objects for the Kekropidai.

Ah, blessed is he, whom the People have crowned, honouring him.
