# Pale of Kephallonia honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar Trajan

Hadrian Augustus

Olympios

(was erected by) the city of the Palians on Kephallenia,

free and autonomous,

through the manager Arnophilos

the son of Agathokles.
