# Choregic dedication of Apollodoros for the Thargelia, 362/1 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Apollodoros son of Thrasyllos of Leukonoion,

serving as sponsor, was victorious

for Leontis (and) Aigeis in the boys' competition,

Kleanthes of Sikyon was pipe-player,

Eukles was director,

uninscribed space

Molon (362/1) was archon.
