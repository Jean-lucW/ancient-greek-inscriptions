# Honours for Philippides of Paiania

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Olympiodoros (293/2) , when Epikouros

son of Epiteles of Rhamnous was recorder, in the tenth

prytany, of Pandionis , on the old and new day of Mounichion,

the first of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding

committee Nikoboulos son of Nikias of Phrearrhioi

was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The People decided. Stratokles

son of Euthydemos of Diomeia proposed: since the ancestors

of Philippides were ever good men to

the Athenian People and at all opportunities

proved their good will and

love of honour, and they provided many and great services

for the People, and they spent much money on the voluntary contributions

and trierarchies and theatrical sponsorships and the other liturgies

from their own resources, to which tripods and

other dedications in the sanctuaries of the city still stand

as memorials; and they performed many fine

and glorious generalships both on land and sea;

and Philippides himself, rating highly

in his turn the duty arising from his ancestors’ service to

the People, has ever proven his good will and

love of honour in public subscriptions and

trierarchies and theatrical sponsorships and all the other liturgies

and honour-loving acts; and when he was elected

general in charge of the fleet he served

well and in the interests of the People; and as king

he has sacrificed all the sacrifices, as many as were his concern,

on behalf of the city piously and according to ancestral custom;

and all the other duties which the laws require of him

in his office he has taken care of well and

incorruptibly; and chosen as competition director he has put on

all the competitions for the gods well and with love of honour,

and all the other duties to which the

People or the Council have elected him he has taken care of

justly; and he has served on successful embassies in the

interests of the People; and he has offered himself

as a champion for the interests of the fatherland

at every opportunity, the People shall decide: to praise

Philippides son of Philomelos of Paiania both

for his [ancestors' and his own?] excellence and love of honour

towards the People, and to crown him with a gold crown

[...] , and to announce the crown

at the city Dionysia and the Demetrieia at the tragedy

competition; and the [] shall take care

of the announcement; and the People shall stand a

[bronze statue of him in the Agora]; and he and the eldest

of his descendants at the time shall have dining rights in the city hall

and a front row seat at all competitions which the city puts on;

and the recorder shall inscribe this decree

on stone stelai and stand one on the acropolis,

the other next to his statue; and Philippides shall be permitted

to inscribe in addition on the stelai the benefactions,

[public subscriptions], trierarchies and the other

liturgies performed by his ancestors

and by himself; and the administrator shall allocate

the expenditure for the making of the crown and the statue and

the inscription of the stelai.
