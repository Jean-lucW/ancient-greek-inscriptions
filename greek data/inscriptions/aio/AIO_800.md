## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Theodotos was archon (387/6) . Paramythos son of Philagros of Erchia was secretary.

The People decided. Theodotos was archon. Kekropis was in prytany.

Paramythos was secretary. Daiphron was chairman. Poliagros

proposed: to praise the People of Klazomenai

because they are enthusiastic towards the city of Athens both

now and in time past. Concerning what they say, the

People shall decide that, if the Klazomenians pay the

five percent tax of the time of Thrasyboulos, then as regards a treaty or no treaty

with those at Chyton, and the hostages whom the Klazomenians have

from those at Chyton, the People of Klazomenai shall have

the authority to decide, and it shall not be permitted for any of the generals either

to reinstate the exiles without the consent of the People of Klazomenai

or to expel any of those who remain; and concerning a governor

and a garrison the People shall vote immediately

whether they ought to install them in Klazomenai or whether

the People of Klazomenai should independently determine these matters,

as to whether they wish to receive them, or not; and as regards the cities

from which the Klazomenians import grain, [Phokaia? and

Chios? and] Smyrna, it shall be within the treaty for them [to sail into?]

[their harbours?]; and the generals [...]
