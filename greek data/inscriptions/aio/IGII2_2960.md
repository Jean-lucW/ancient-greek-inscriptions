# Statue base for the leader of the friendly society (archeranistes) of the Asklepiastai

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Antiochos son of Menandros

of Melite,

leader of the friendly society

of the Asklepiastai

(was erected by) his own father and

his mother Hagne daughter of Neikon

of Marathon

with the association

of the Asklepiastai.
