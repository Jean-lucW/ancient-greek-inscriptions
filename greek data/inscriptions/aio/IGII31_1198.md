# Honours for a prytany

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

to Apollo Prostaterios and Artemis

Boulaia and the other gods to whom

it is traditional, for good fortune, the People shall decide,

to accept the good things that occurred in the

sacrifices which they made for the health and preservation of the

Council and the People; and since the prytany

[of -] made all the sacrifices that were

proper in the prytany well and with love of honour,

and managed the convening of the

Council and the Assembly and everything else which

the laws and decrees of the People

require of them; to praise the prytany [...]

[...]
