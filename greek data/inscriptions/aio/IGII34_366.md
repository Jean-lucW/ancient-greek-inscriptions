# Victory dedication to Hermes from the Heroia

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The prizes?] from the Heroia

– son of Gorgis of Philaidai

having won the torch-race (dedicated them) to Hermes,

in the competition-directorship

of [Nikodem?]os (and) Archon.
