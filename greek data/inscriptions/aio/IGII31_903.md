# Honours for the priest of Zeus Soter

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysitheides (272/1) , in the first prytany,

of -, for which Semonides son of Timesias of Sounion

was secretary. On the eleventh of Hekatombaion,

the eleventh of the prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding

committee T- son of - of - was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People decided.

Promenes son of Promenes of Kephale proposed: concerning

what the priest of Zeus Soter reports about the

sacrifices which he made for the health of the Council to Zeus

Soter and Athena Soteira, for good

fortune, the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee

allotted to preside in the People at

the next Assembly shall introduce the priest

of Zeus Soter to the People as an item of the sacred business and

shall put it on the agenda, and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council that the Council and People

accept the good things which the priest reports

occurred in the sacrifices which he made

for the health and preservation of the Council and the People;

and since the priest made the entry sacrifices

for the preservation of the Council and the People well

and with love of honour, to praise the priest of Zeus

Soter - son of - of Kerameis, and crown

him with a foliage crown for his piety

to the gods and love of honour towards

the Council and People; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it [by] the stoa of Zeus;
