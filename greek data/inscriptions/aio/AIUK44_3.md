# Inventory of the Pronaos, 426/5-423/2 BC and 414/3-411/0 BC

## English translation

**Face a**

**Part 1**

(426/5)

With good [fortune]!

[The four sets of officials who] gave their accounts from Panathenaia to Panathenaia [handed over the following]

[ [...] and his fellow officials], to whom Euboulos son of [Phil]ogeiton of Acharnai was secretary (Board of 427/6) ,

[to the treasurers Androkles of Phlya and] his fellow officials, to whom Kephisophon son of Kephisodoros of Hermos was secretary (Board of 426/5) ;

[the treasurers, to whom Kephisophon son of Kephis]odoros of Hermos was secretary, handed over to the treasurers

[of the goddess Phokiades of Oion and his fellow officials], to whom Lysistratos son of Morychides of Pallene was secretary (Board of 425/4) :

[in the pronaos: a golden libation bowl from which] lustrations are made, unweighed; 121 silver libation bowls, weight [of them]

[2 talents 432 drachmas; 3 silver drinking horns, weight] of them 528 drachmas; 5 silver drinking cups, weight of them 167 drachmas;

[silver lamp, weight of this 38 drachmas]; 7 silver libation bowls, weight of them 700 drachmas; [gold] crown

[weight of it 33 drachmas 3 obols; 2 silver libation bowls], weight of them [200 drachmas]; 4 silver libation bowls, weight of them [329 drachmas];

[drinking cup of Chalkidian silver], weight of it 40 drachmas

[The following were the year's additions: 7 silver libation bowls], weight of them 920 drachmas.
