# Cypriot League honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar

Trajan Hadrian Augustus

Olympios the saviour

and benefactor of the  
Cypriots

(was erected by) their League, through the envoys

Gaius Julius Rufus of  
Paphos

and Kleagenes son of Kleagenes

of  
Salamis, most victorious,

in the priesthood of Claudius Attikos.
