# Honours for Julia Augusta

## English translation

**Part 1**

[(This statue of) Julia August]a (was erected by) the Council [of the Areopagos and the Council of the Six Hundred and the People, during]

[the priesthood of Kleo] the daughter of Eukles [of Phlya, but by birth of Nikodemos of Hermos],

[the dedication having been managed by [...] ]

[ [...] Pr]axag[oras of -, being priest for life],

[ [...] of Praxagoras (?) [...] ]
