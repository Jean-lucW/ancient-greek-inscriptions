# Dedication by a friendly society (eranistai)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[To He]rak[les] [...] , for their

good luck, the friendly society dedicated

(this). They were: Kadous,

Manes, Kallias,

Attas, Artemidoros,

Maes, Sosias,

Sangarios, Hermaios,

Tibeios, Hermos.
