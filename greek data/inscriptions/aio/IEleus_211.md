# Decree of Athenian soldiers stationed at Eleusis, Panakton and Phyle and <i>hypaithroi</i> honouring the general, Demainetos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

The Athenians stationed at Eleusis and Panakton and

Phyle and those in the open country decided: since Demainetos continually

shows good will towards the People, and carried out whatever duties the People elected him to

well and reputably, and often served as ambassador to the Aitolian league,

and likewise to king Philippos,

so that the People might maintain friendship and peace with both,

and the city, not being disturbed by one of them, might be restored to

its original state of well-being; he did not shrink from any toil or danger,

but in word and deed did what was advantageous

for his fatherland; for this, the People often praised him

and crowned him with a gold crown, giving him a worthy reward

for his services to the People; and when he was elected general

in charge of the countryside around Eleusis for the first time, in the archonship of Chairephon (218/7) ,

he carried out his command well and in a manner worthy of himself,

in accordance with the laws and decrees, and he displayed all foresight

and care in ensuring that no wrong was done either in

the countryside or in the garrisons; and just so again,

when he was elected for the second time, for the same territory, in the

archonship of Diokles (214/3) , he continually outdid himself in his

management of the guard and in his love of honour towards those under his command;

and when he was elected general

in charge of the countryside around Eleusis for the third time, in the archonship of Aischron (210/09) ,

he took care of the protection of the countryside and of the garrisons,

as on the previous occasions; and when

the gathering at the Great Eleusinia occurred in the years

when he was general, he sacrificed to the goddesses with the

the people at Eleusis, on behalf of the preservation of the People; and he took care

of the festival of the Mysteries during each period that he was general, so that

it could be performed in all safety; and

towards the citizens under his command he showed himself

in every respect to be fair and incorruptible;

and he took care of the distribution of salaries, provided food

willingly so that they could have it as cheaply as possible,

and advanced money for clothing every year,

always ensuring the orderliness of those under his command;

and he spent not a little of his own means on these things,

being of service to all collectively and considerate to each individually;

in order therefore that the Athenians stationed in

the garrisons may be seen to honour those who

act with love of reputation towards the People and with love of honour towards themselves,

for good fortune, the citizens stationed

at Eleusis and Panakton and Phyle and those in the open country shall

praise Demainetos son of Hermokles of Athmonon and

crown him with a gold crown in accordance with the law and

with a bronze statue, and to place it in the courtyard of the sanctuary;

and to announce the crown at the ancestral contest of the Haloa;

responsible for making the statue and the announcement

shall be the secretary and the treasurer and those who

are appointed.

The following men were appointed for the erection

of the statue:

Kleitophon of Iphistiadai, Xenephon of Berenikidai

- of Paiania, Nikandros of Tyrmeidai.

The Athenians

stationed at

Eleusis and those in the open country

(crown) Demainetos

son of Hermokles of Athmonon,

who was their

general three times.
