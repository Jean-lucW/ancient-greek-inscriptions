# Tribal honours for the prytany of Aigeis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the (archonship of) Isaios (284/3) . On the fifth of Elaphebolion.

Nikokrates son of Archemachos of Phegaia

proposed: since the councillors of the tribe

Aigeis in the archonship of Diotimos (285/4) deliberated on the Council

and took their turn in the prytany

allotted to them well and justly, and for this

the Council and People crowned them

with a gold crown according to the law,

for good fortune, the tribe shall decide: to praise

the councillors of the tribe Aigeis of the

archonship of Diotimos (285/4) and to crown

them with a gold crown according to the law

for their love of honour for the tribe; and the

managers who will be managing [the tribe]

shall give them for a sacrifice

[...] drachmas for [...]

[...] as much [...]

[...]

[...] dedication [...]
