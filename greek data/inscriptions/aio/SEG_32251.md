# Honours for Nero (Parthenon inscription)

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council of the Areopagos and the Council of the 600 and the People of the Athenians (honour) the greatest emperor, Nero Caesar Claudius Augustus

Germanicus, son of a god, when the hoplite general for the eighth time (61/2) , who was also the manager of the city and the lawgiver, was

Tiberius Claudius Novius son of Philinos, when the priestess was Paullina, daughter of Capito.
