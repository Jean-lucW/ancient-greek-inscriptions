# Honours for the priest of Zeus Soter

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[... that it seems good to]

[the Council that the Council and People accept the good]

things that the priest reports have occurred in the

sacrifices he made for the health and preservation of the

Council and the People and the others who are well disposed

towards the People; and since the priest made the entry sacrifices

[...] to Zeus Soter and Athena

Soteira [...] from his own resources, to praise

the priest of Zeus Soter, Smikros son of Philinos

of - and crown him with a foliage crown for his

piety to the gods and love of honour

towards the Council and People; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it by the stoa of

Zeus; and for the inscription of the stele the

board of administrators shall allocate the set amount.

**Column 1**

In crown

The Council

**Column 2**

In crown

The People
