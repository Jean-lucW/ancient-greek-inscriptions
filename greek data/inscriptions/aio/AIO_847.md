**Face-front a**

sacred enclosure by the [Delphini?]on, a full-grown victim, to be sold.

_ An oath victim is to be provided for the audits.

Boedromion: the Proerosia; for Zeus Polieus,

a select sheep, a select piglet; at Automenai (?),

a bought piglet, burnt whole; the priest is to

provide lunch for the attendant; for Kephalos,
