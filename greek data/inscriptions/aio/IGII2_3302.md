# Pompeiopolis in Cilicia honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan Parthicus,

grandson of the God Nerva, Trajan Hadrian

Augustus, father of the fatherland (was erected by) the city

of the Pompeiopolitans of Cilicia, through the envoys Julius

Barchios Pedios
