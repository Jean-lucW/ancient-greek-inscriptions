## English translation

**Fragment a+c+d+f+g+i+j**

For good fortune

of the most divine Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus,

Since the city of the Thyateirans, [feeling gratitude for the benefactions of the]

greatest Emperor, Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, [saviour]

and [benefactor], father of the fatherland, Pontifex Maximus, consul, [through]

which [cities and] peoples of all Greece [have come into this most solemn]

Panhellenion, [wishes] to inscribe on the Acropolis [in Athens] everything which

it achieved [with Mettius Modestus' support?] and the gifts of the

greatest of all kings ever, Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian

Olympios Panhellenios Zeus, for all of which the Council and People of the Thyateirans

decided, for good fortune, that this decree shall be inscribed on a stone stele and stood

on the Acropolis, [so that it may be] very clear to all the Greeks, how much (the city) has got

from the greatest king, [because simultaneously] the king benefited all of the Greeks in common,

having gathered that council together from them, as a common source of honour,

in the most brilliant city of the Athenians, which is the benefactress that [gives]

the fruit of the Mysteries to all alike, that is the [most awesome] Panhellenion, through what he decreed

[the Romans] agreeing [through a decision] of the Senate, and individually [he gave] the peoples and cities [a share] of this

[most honourable council], and it is also clear that [the king increases and benefits]

our [city] conspicuously and often, [...] even the

[...] he benefitted [...] taking on behalf of [...]

[...] from the [...] [the control (?)] of the waters

[...] from [...] according to the - of all

[...]
