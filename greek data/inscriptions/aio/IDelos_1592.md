# Honours for Julia the Elder

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People of the Athenians and the inhabitants

of the island (dedicated this statue of) Julia, daughter

of Caesar Augustus,

wife of Marcus Agrippa,

to Apollo, Artemis, Leto,

uninscribed space

when the priest of Apollo was Pammenes

son of Zenon of Marathon.
