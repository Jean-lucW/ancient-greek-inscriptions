# Funerary monument of Pythion of Megara

## English translation

**Part 1**

This is the memorial lying on the tomb of an excellent man, Pythion

from Megara, having slain seven men and broken off

seven spears in their bodies, he chose excellence, bringing renown to his father

among the People. This man, who saved three tribes

of Athenians, bringing them from Pegai through Boeotia to Athens,

having brought renown to Andokides with two thousand . Having

harmed no man on earth, he went down to Hades deemed blessed

for all to see. These are the tribes: Pandionis , Kekropis , Antiochis .
