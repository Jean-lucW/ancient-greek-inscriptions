# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[... and he shall be proxenos of the People, and]

his descendants, and he and his descendants shall have

right of ownership of land not exceeding in value - and a house,

500 drachmas; and the court presidents shall bring the scrutiny

of the award for him to the court when the days

required by law have passed; and so that there may be a [reminder]

of the award granted to him by the People, the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis,

and for the inscription and [making?] of the stele

the administrator shall allocate the expenditure

accrued.

In crown

The [Council, the People.]
