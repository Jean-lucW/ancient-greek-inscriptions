# Honours for Amyntor son of Demetrios

## English translation

**Part 1**

[In the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) ],

[in the ]

[prytany, of Akamantis , for which]

[Mnesiphilos son of Mneson]

[of Phaleron was secretary].

[On the twenty-fifth of ].

Assembly. Of the

[presiding committee] D[emokr-]

of Paiania was putting to the vote.

The People decided. Demades

son of Demeas of Paiania proposed:

to praise Amyntor,

since he shows good will

towards the Athenians;

and Amyntor son of Demetrios shall be an

Athenian, himself and

his descendants, and it shall be permitted

for him to be enrolled in the tribe

and deme and phratry

that he wishes, those which the laws

stipulate; and the

prytany secretary

shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing

the stele [...]
