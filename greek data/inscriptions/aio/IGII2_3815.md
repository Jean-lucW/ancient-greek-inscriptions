# Honours for Chariton the temple attendant (<i>zakoros</i>)

## English translation

**Part 1**

In accordance with a petition to the

Council of the 500, (this herm was erected of) Chariton

son of Neikias of Marathon, having been

temple attendant of Asklepios

and Hygieia in the year of the

archonship of Pompeius

Alexandros.
