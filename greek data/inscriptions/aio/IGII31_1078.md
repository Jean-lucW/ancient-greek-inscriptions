# Citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] which he continues to have

for the Athenian People and [...] ; and that he

shall be an Athenian, and his descendants,

and the award granted to him by the Athenian People [shall be]

[valid]; and he shall be permitted to enrol in

the tribe and deme and phratry that he wishes according

to the law; and the prytany shall put the vote

to the People about his citizenship at the next Assembly

that is held, according to [the law about]

[citizenship]. So, therefore, that there might be a reminder

for him of the grant awarded by the People, the

secretary of the People shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis, and

the administrator shall allocate the expenditure

accrued for the inscription and the stele.
