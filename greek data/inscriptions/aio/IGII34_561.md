# Victory dedication by a sponsor and director

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] having competed in

the men's circular choruses for the tribe of

Kekropis, serving as sponsor and as director himself,

and having directed

a new tragedy at the Great Panathenaia, and having won

all three, (he dedicated this) to Athena.
