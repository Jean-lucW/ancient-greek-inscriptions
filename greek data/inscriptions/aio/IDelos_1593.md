# Honours for Agrippa Postumus

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council of the Areopagos

and the People (erected this statue of)

Marcus Agrippa

son of Marcus

Agrippa, their own

benefactor

and saviour,

when the priest of Apollo

for life was Pammenes son of

Zenon of Marathon.
