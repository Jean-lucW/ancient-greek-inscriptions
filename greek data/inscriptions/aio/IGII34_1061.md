# Dedication by Ambrosios and Diopeithes to Artemis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Ambrosios and Diopeithes dedicated me ⟦at public expense?⟧ to Artemis,

a memorial of their ⟦excellence⟧ for those to come for all time.
