# Citizenship

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[... and]

enrol in the tribe and deme and phratry that he

wishes; and the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the forthcoming Assembly

shall put the vote about him to the People;

and the secretary of [...] shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it on

the acropolis; and for the inscription of the stele

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate

the cost from the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees.
