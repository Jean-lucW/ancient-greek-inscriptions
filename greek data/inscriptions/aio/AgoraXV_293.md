# Honours for the prytany and prytany treasurer of Pandionis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Apolexis son of Philokrates of Oion proposed it. Since,

having made an approach to the Council, the prytaneis

of Pandionis in the archonship of Demeas of Azenia

declare that the treasurer of the prytaneis,

whom they chose, Philon son of Hegelochos of Paiania,

having undertaken ⟦ [...] ⟧the treasurership for the second time, has

made the sacrifices to the gods, which were traditional, on behalf of the

prytaneis and the Council and the People and children

[and] women and friends and allies, and that, having received good omens

in all the victims, he conducted himself magnificently

[and] worthily of his goodwill towards the prytaneis, and for this the prytaneis

call on the Council to make the fitting

forethought, for good fortune, the Council shall decide to praise

the prytaneis of Pandionis for their piety towards the gods

and also to praise Philon son of Hegelochos of Paiania, for having

submitted to their treasurership for the second time and for having

paid for the sacrifices to the gods, to whom it was traditional (to do so), and to crown

him with a foliage crown, with which it is traditional to crown

good men, so that, when these things are completed, it may be apparent that

the Council takes the appropriate forethought.
