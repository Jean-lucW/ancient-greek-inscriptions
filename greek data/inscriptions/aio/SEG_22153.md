# Honours for Claudius Apollo Patroos

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Tiberius Claudius Caesar [Augustus]

Germanicus Emperor [Apollo]

Patroos (was erected by) his priest [and high priest]

for life and hoplite general

for the third time, Dionysodoros son of So[phokles]

of Sounion, for his own benefactor and his whole

house's saviour and benefactor.

Euboulides (son of Euboulides) of Piraeus made it.
