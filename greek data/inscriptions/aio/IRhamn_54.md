# Decree of (Rhamnous and?) Athenian residents there honouring [a general?], ca. 225-175 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] [and crown]

him with a gold crown

for his excellence and justice

towards themselves; and invite him

to the common sacrifices to Nemesis which the religious officials

in office at any one time put on;

and inscribe this

decree on a stone stele and stand

it wherever he wishes; and for the

setting up of the stele [...]
