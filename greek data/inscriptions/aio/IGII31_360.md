# Dedication by the Council and decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

The following contributed to the dedication which the Council

made in the archonship of Euthykritos (328/7) .

Councillors:

**Column 1**

Philostratos of Acharnai

Euthykrates of Aphidna

Chairestratos of Rhamnous

Oulias of Steiria

Phalanthos of Kerameis

Eukrates of Lamptrai

Lykourgos of Melite

Kallisthenes of Trinemeia

Euetion of Sphettos

Empedos of Oe

Theokrines of Hybadai

Philokrates of Aixone

Protokles of Kephisia

Boulis of Thorai

**Column 2**

Demetrios of Aphidna

Ameinias of Agryle

Antidotos of Sypalettos

Theodoros of Pallene

Epigethes of Eroiadai

Nikandros of Marathon

Lysitheos of Euonymon

Treasurer:

Sotiades of Acharnai

Secretar[ies]:

Sokrates of Paionidai

Pythodelos of Hagnous.

**Column 2**

Aristeides of Hermos

Pheidippos of M[yrrhinous?]

Aristion- of -.

**Part 2**

Kallisthenes son of Charmylos (?) of Trinemeia proposed:

since Euthykrates of Aphidna and Philostratos

of Acharnai and Chairestratos of Rhamnous continue

always to show love of honour towards the Council

and took joint responsibility for the Council’s dedication

in the Amphiaraion well and with love of honour,

the Council shall decide: to praise Euthykrates

son of Drakontides of Aphidna, Philostratos son of Philinos

of Acharnai, Chairestratos son of Chairedemos of Rhamnous,

and crown each of them with a gold

crown of 500 dr. when they have rendered

their accounts, so that others may also show love of honour towards

the Council, knowing that the Council will give to each of them

thanks worthy of their benefactions;

and the secretary of the Council shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it

in the sanctuary of Amphiaraos.
