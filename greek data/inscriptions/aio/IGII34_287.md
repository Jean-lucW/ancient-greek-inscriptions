# Dedication by the soldiers at Rhamnous for a general and a patrol commander

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The soldiers] dedicated (this), [having crowned the general]

[and the] patrol commander [...] [for their excellence and]

justice towards them.

**Column 1**

In crown

General

Blepyros

son of -eon

of Erchia.

**Column 2**

In crown

Patrol commander

Daites

son of Hestiodoros

of Ikarion.
