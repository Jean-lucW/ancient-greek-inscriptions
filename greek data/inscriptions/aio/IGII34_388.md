# Dedication by an ephebe victor at the Theseia, 37/6 or 36/5 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Ptolemaios son of Ptolemaios

of Athmonon, having won the torch-race

at the Theseia in the archonship of

Kallikratides, (dedicated this) to Hermes, in the

trainership of [Meniskos?] of Kolonai,

in the assistant-trainership of

Dioskourides of Kolonai (and)

Herakleitos of Athmonon.
