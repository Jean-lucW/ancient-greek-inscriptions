# Choregic dedication from Aigilia

## English translation

**Part 1**

Timosthenes son of Meixonides

Meixonides son of Timosthenes

Kleostratos son of Timosthenes

having been victorious as sponsors dedicated

to Dionysos the statue and the altar.
