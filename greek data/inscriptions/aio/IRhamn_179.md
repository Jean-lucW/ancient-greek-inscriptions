# Council decree on the cult of Agdistis at Rhamnous

## English translation

**Part 1**

To the gods. Servants: Supporter of [Agdistis]:

Zenon (son of Zenon) of Antioch. Foster-father [...] :

Nikias (son of Nikias) of Karystos. Ankon-bearer(s?):

Chroto and Stratonike. Adamma : [...]

daughter of Dionysios of Miletos; Ankon-bearer and [...]

relief

from the Metroon, with good fortune. In the archonship of Pammenes,

fourth day of Mounichion. Xenophon of Thria proposed: since

Zenon (son of Zenon) of Antioch, having made an approach (to the Council),

demonstrates that he has served in the sanctuary at Rhamnous

of Agdistis, and also in addition he

has been consecrated (as priest) to these gods for a very long time, but now,

being oppressed by some people, he is prevented from his duty,

and therefore he calls on the Council, which looks after

piety towards the gods, to take proper

care of him. For good fortune, the Council

shall decide that Zenon (son of Zenon) of Antioch is allowed

to serve the gods who are at Rhamnous,

just as from the beginning, being stopped by no one

[...]
