# Anemourion honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar

Trajan Hadrian

Augustus Olympios,

father of the fatherland

the saviour of the universe

(was erected by) the Council and People

of the  
Anemourians of  
Cilicia,

through the envoys

Publius Aelius Kaiolos (?)

and Gaios son of Alexandros.
