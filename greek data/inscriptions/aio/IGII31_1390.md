# Citizenship decree for sacred delegates (<i>theoroi</i>) from Miletos

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] City Dionysia at the tragedies [...]

[...] for their good will and love of honour

towards it; and praise the chief sacred delegate, Herm[ophantos son of Lichas]

and the sacred delegates, -on son of Charidemos, - son of - ,

Hieron son of Simas, - son of Iason, Ekphantos son of -, and crown

each of them with a gold crown for their piety

towards the gods and their excellence and love of honour towards the People

of the Athenians and their own fatherland, and announce the crowns

in the city at the Dionysia, at the new competition of the tragedies, and at

Miletos, at the tragedies of the Dionysia; and they shall also be granted citizenship,

and the court presidents shall bring forward the scrutiny,

even if they are not present; and they shall be permitted to be enrolled in the tribe and

deme and phratry that they wish; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree [on two stone stelai

and stand the one at Athen?]s, [and the other at Miletos ] [...]

[...]
