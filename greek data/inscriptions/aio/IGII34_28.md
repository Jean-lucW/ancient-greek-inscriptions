# Dedication by the treasurers of Athena, 349/8 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The treasurers of the goddess in the archonship of

Kallimachos (349/8) , having completed their term of office, dedicated

(this), having been crowned by the Council

and the People on account of their excellence

and justice.

Ameinias son of Philippos of Agryle , Platon son of Isotimos of Iphistiadai ,

[Mnesago]ras son of Mnesilochos of Halai , Deinon son of Deinias of Acharnai ,

Tachykles son of Phormion of Myrrhinous , Exekestides son of Exekias of Xypete ,

Hegesias son of Heg[ [...] ]ias of Sounion , Mormias son of Euboulos of Oinoe ;

secretary, Pistides of Thorai .
