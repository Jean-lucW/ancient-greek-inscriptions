# Dedication to Apollo Hypo Makrais

## English translation

**Part 1**

To Apollo Below the Heights, Eraton

dedicated (this), having been secretary

**Column 1**

In myrtle crown:

In the archonship

of

Trebellius

Rufus

of Lamptrai

**Column 2**

In myrtle crown:

Having been

secretary

of the board,

Eraton

son of Antigonos

of Besa (dedicated this)

**Column 3**

In myrtle crown:

Court

president

Eirenaios

son of Sotas

of

Berenikidai.
