# Koropissos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Trajan

Hadrian Caesar

Augustus Olympios

(was erected by) the Council and

People of the Koropisseans,

mother-city of

the Kietans, through the envoys

Gaius Ninnius

and Athenion

son of Clemens, also called

Lykiskos, in the priesthood of Claudius

Attikos.
