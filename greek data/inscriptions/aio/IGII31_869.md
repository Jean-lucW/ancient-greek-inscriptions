## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Diotimos (285/4) , in the

third prytany, of Kekropis ,

for which Lysistratos son of Aristomachos

of Paiania was secretary.

On the ninth of Boedromion,

the ninth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee

[...] was putting to the vote [...]
