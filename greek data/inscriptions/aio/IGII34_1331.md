# Dedication to the Mother of the Gods, Sarapis and Isis (Rhamnous)

## English translation

**Part 1**

To the Mother of the Gods, Sarapis,

and Isis, Delphis dedicated (this)

in the priesthood of Hegesias

of Rhamnous.
