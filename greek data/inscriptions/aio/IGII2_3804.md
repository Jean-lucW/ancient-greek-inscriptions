# Statue for a teacher from his students

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gaios and Mauros (erected this statue of)

their teacher

also on behalf of their

fellow disciples, having requested it

from the

Areopagos Council,

while the temple attendant was Statius the fire-bearer

from the Acropolis, of Cholleidai.
