# Honours for Euenor of Akarnania

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Gods.

Decree 1

In the archonship of Phrynichos (337/6) , in the

tenth prytany, of Pandionis . Chairestratos

of Acharnai was secretary. On the

old and new day of Skirophorion, the thirty-fifth

of the prytany. [Of the presiding committee]

Euthykrates of Aphidna was putting to the vote. Diophantos son of Phrasikleides

of Myrrhinous proposed: [since Euenor

of Argos?] [...]

[...]

[...] report (?) [...]

[...]

[...]

[...]

were in need [...]

the Council shall resolve: to bring Euenor before

the People and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council,

since Euenor of Akarnania is zealous

concerning the Athenian People and does

what good is in his power, he shall be proxenos

and benefactor of the Athenian People, both

himself and his descendants, and it shall be for him

as for other benefactors; and the secretary of the Council

shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give 20 drachmas from

the People’s fund for expenditure on

decrees.

Decree 2

In the archonship of Philokles (322/1) , in the ninth prytany,

of Oeneis , for which Euthygenes son of Hephaistodemos of Kephisia

was secretary; on the second of Thargelion,

the twenty-third of the prytany. Assembly.

Of the presiding committee Eualkos of Phaleron was putting to the vote.

The People decided. Diophantos son of Phrasikleides

of Myrrhinous proposed: in other respects as proposed by the Council,

but since Euenor son of Euepios became proxenos

of the Athenian People through benefaction

and takes care of everything that the Athenian People has required of him, both individually

and collectively, for good fortune, the People

shall resolve: to praise Euenor son of Euepios and

crown him with a foliage crown for his love of honour and his

professional care; and he and his descendants shall have right of ownership right of ownership of land and

a house, except for [common and

sacred] property; and the Council in office at any

time and the generals, whoever they may be at any time, shall take care of him;
