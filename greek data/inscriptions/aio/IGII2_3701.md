# Statue base for Marcus Ulpius Flavius Teisamenos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The city (erected this statue of) Marcus Ulpius Flavius

Teisamenos the mighty,

the court president, the son

of the benefactor Marcus Ulpius Eubiotos

the eponymous archon,

on account of his fairness and his

good will regarding the fatherland.
