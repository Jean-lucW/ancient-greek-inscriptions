# Honours for a Publius Herennius

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The Council of the Areopagos]

[and the Council of] the [five hundred]

(erected this statue of) the polemarch

[and competition director] of the Great

- and herald

of the Areopagos

Publius Herennius

[ - of Hermos]
