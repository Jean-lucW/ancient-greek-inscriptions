# Honours for the prytany of Kekropis

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 2

In the archonship of Euthykritos (189/8) , in the ninth prytany, of Aiantis,

for which Kephalos son of Kephalos of Kydantidai was secretary.

On the twenty-seventh of Anthesterion, the eighth of the

prytany. Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding committee

- son of - of Probalinthos was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

Xenokrates son of - of - proposed: since the prytany of Kekropis

and those who have permanent dining rights have praised and crowned

Philinos of Pithos, the treasurer whom they chose from themselves,

and have declared to the Council that he has made all the sacrifices which are proper in the

prytany on behalf of the Council and the People, and has taken care

of everything else well and with love of honour, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide to praise the treasurer, Philinos of Pithos, and

the secretary, - of Aixone, and to crown each of them

with a foliage crown and to praise also the priest of the eponymous,

- of -, and the secretary of the Council

and the People, - of -, and the under-secretary

- of -, and the herald of the Council and the People,

Eukles of Berenikidai and the pipe-player, Neokles of Berenikidai;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it in [the prytanikon] [...]
