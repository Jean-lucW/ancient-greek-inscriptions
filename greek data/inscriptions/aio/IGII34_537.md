# Dedication by the competition-director, Praxiteles (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

If anyone of previous generations performed sacrifices to Hermes Enagonios

Gifts of this sort are also appropriate for Nike

Whom as companion to Bromios in the renowned competitions of the theatrical artists

Praxiteles set up under twin tripods.
