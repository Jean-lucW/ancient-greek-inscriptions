# Dedication by the prytany of Antiochis, 334/3 BC

## English translation

**Column 1**

Olive crown

**Column 2**

The prytany of Antiochis in the archonship of Ktesikles (334/3) (dedicated this)

having been crowned by the Council and the People

for its excellence and justice

**Column 3**

Olive crown
