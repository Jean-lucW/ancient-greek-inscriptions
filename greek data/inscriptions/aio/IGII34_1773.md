# Sacrificial regulation for the cult of Asklepios and associated deities at Piraeus

## English translation

**Face-front A**

Gods.

Make preliminary sacrifices according to this:

for Maleatas, three round

cakes; for Apollo, three round

cakes; for Hermes, three round cakes;

for Iaso, three round cakes;

for Akeso, three round cakes;

for Panakeia, three round cakes;

for the Dogs, three round cakes;

for the Hunters with Dogs, three round cakes.

**Part 1**

Euthydemos

of Eleusis,

priest of Asklepios,

erected the stelai

by the altars,

on which (stelai) he first depicted

the round cakes that are required to be

preliminarily sacrificed.

**Face-left B**

For Helios,

a propitiatory cake,

a honeycomb.

For

Mnemosyne,

a propitiatory cake,

a honey-

comb.

Three

wineless

altars.

**Face C (top)**

Three

wineless

altars.

**Face-back D**

Wineless.
