# Dedication for the generals by staff

## English translation

**Part 1**

Leader [...]

Damon son of Phi- [...]

secretary [...]

Philokrates son of Philis-

allotted under-secretary

Pausias,

allotted herald Philo-

and those stationed at the generals' headquarters

and the man in charge of the sacrifices Telephanes [...]

having crowned the generals

who were in office in the archonship

of E-, dedicated this.
