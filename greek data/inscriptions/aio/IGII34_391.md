# Dedication by an ephebe, 35/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Andronikos son of Sostron

of Pelekes, having been overseer

of the torch-race in the year

that Theopeithes was

archon (35/4 BC) , (dedicated this) to Hermes,

in the trainership of

Philios son of Diokles

and Philios son of Philios of

Phrearrhioi, in the assistant-

trainership of

Apollonides (son of Apollonides) of

Phrearrhioi and

Dionysios son of Philios

of Phrearrhioi.
