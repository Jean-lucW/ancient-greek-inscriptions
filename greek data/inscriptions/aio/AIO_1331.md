# Dedication to Athena

## Greek text

**Part 1**

Ϙο[⏕ μ’ ἀνέθε][κε Διὸς γλαυϙ]όπιδι ϙόρει.

## English translation

**Part 1**

Ko- [dedicated me]

[to the bright]-eyed maiden, [daughter of Zeus].
