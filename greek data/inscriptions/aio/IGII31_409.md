## English translation

**Part 1**

three lines erased

[...] and the secretary

of the Council shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and

set it down on the acropolis; and

for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give 30

drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees.
