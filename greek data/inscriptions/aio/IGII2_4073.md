# Statue of Claudia Athenais erected by her father’s student

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Areopagos

Council

having decreed it,

(this statue of) Marcia

Athenais, daughter of Claudius Herodes,

(was erected by) Flavius Macer

for his friend and teacher’s

daughter,

the temple attendant being Eudemos

son of Hermeias of

Gargettos, the deputy temple attendant being

Euangellos

son of Demetrios of Gargettos.
