# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] so that

he suffers no harm; and to inscribe this decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele the

treasurer of the People shall give 20 drachmas from the

People’s fund for expenditure on decrees.

**Column 1**

[ In crown

The Council].

**Column 2**

In crown

The People.
