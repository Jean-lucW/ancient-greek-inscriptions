# Honours for a woman

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) – daughter of –

of Elaious,

wife of Publius Aelius Orfitius [Burri]anus

(was erected) in accordance with a

memorandum of the Areopagites

by her father

on account of her excellence.
