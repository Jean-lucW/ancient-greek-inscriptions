# Choregic dedication for the City Dionysia, 375/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Oineis was victorious,

[...] -oros son of Melesias of Oe was sponsor,

[...] -ikles was pipe-player, Diophon was director,

Hippodamas (375/4) was archon.
