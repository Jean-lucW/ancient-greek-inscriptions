## English translation

**Part 1**

**Fragment a+b**

[Paian and dance] for the God, which Athenaios composed:

**Column 1**

[Listen], fair-armed daughters of far-thundering [Zeus], who received as your share

densely wooded [Helikon]! Come to celebrate with odes

your golden-haired brother Phoibos, who will come

up the double-peaked seat of this Parnassian rock

together with the far-renowned Delphian

maidens to the springs of well-watered Kastalis

ascending the Delphian cliff,

his fortune-telling hill.

[See there] famous Attica with its great city,

which by the benevolence of weapon-bearing Tritonis

inhabits unshakable ground, and on holy altars,

Hephaistos burns the thighs of young bulls.

At once, now, Arabian scent is streaming up to Olympos.

The shrill sounding flute pours forth a song

in varying melodies and the golden, sweet-sounding

cithara sings with hymns.

The entire swarm of [artists], which received Attica as its share,

**Column 2**

altogether [praises] great [Zeus’] famous child.

[For, to you he gave] this snow-capped hill

where you prophesy [immortal and unerring sayings] to all mortals:

How you conquered the fortune-telling tripod,

[which the giant] snake was guarding, when you removed Gaia’s child,

its glittering, twisting [body, until the beast, letting out thick]

hisses, roughly [expired all the same].

[Just as] the [barbarian] Ares of the Gauls, [which] impiously

passed [over this land, perished in the wet streams of snow].

But hurray! Descendant [...]

[...] warlike offspring [...]
