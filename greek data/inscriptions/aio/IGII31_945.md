# Honours for Aristomenes of Paiania

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1 (Council)

[...]

fragmentary line

[...] , for good fortune,

the Council shall decide: to

praise Aristomenes son of

Artist- of Paiania for the

excellence [and justice] which

[he continued to display towards] the

[Council and People]

[...]

Decree 2 (Council and People)

[...]

[...] the presiding committee

allotted to preside

at the next

Assembly shall put the matter

on the agenda and submit the

opinion of the Council

to the People that it seems good

to the Council to praise Aristomenes

son of Arist- of

Paiania for the excellence and

justice which he continued

to display towards the Council

and the People and to crown

him with a [gold] crown

according to the law; in order that

there may be [incentive] for all

citizens to offer themselves [...]

[...] according to the

laws [...]

[...]
