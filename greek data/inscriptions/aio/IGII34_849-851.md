The duties of a doctor are the following: first he must take up the Paionian arts

and heal his own mind before giving assistance to anyone,

and he must not examine or touch anyone in a manner contrary to divine laws and the oath.

He should cure with professional skill and practice, and not be disarmed

by young women and lovely wives, when he touches them as a helper

and his breast becomes warm with desire [far from a true] doctor.

Therefore, I declare to the godly minded and the pure

[...] and [...] healers

and such as to give assistance to all like a brother,

nor would he conceal envy in his mind or increase his pretensions.
