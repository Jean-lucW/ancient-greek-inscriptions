# Dedication by the Conveners of the People, 351/0 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Conveners of the People

in the archonship of Theellos (351/0)

dedicated (this),

having been crowned

by the Council and the

People for their

justice.

in crown to the left: The People

in crown to the right: The Council
