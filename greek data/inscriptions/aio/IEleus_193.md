# The Eleusinians and Athenian soldiers at Eleusis honour King Antigonos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Ameinokles son of Tachyllos of Kydathenaion

proposed: for good fortune, the Eleusinians

and the Athenians stationed at Eleusis

shall decide: since Antigonos

the king on arriving in Eleusis

well and with love of honour [...]
