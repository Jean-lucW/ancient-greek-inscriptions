# Sebastopolis in Pontos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian

Augustus Olympios (was erected by) the Council and People

of the Sebastopolitans in Pontos

for their own benefactor in the first Olympiad (132/3 AD),

through the envoys Capito

Scipio and Gaius -mius.
