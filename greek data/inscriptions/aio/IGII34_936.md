# Dedication to the Hero Amphilochos by a family

## English translation

**Part 1**

Logos son of Philokles

of Piraeus

and Sympherousa

daughter of Hermias of Marathon

and their sons

Herodikos, Olympiodoros,

Demetrios,

Hermeias, priest for life

of the hero

doctor,

Amphilochos (dedicated this).
