# Honours for the priest of Kalliste

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysanias (234/3) , in the eighth prytany,

of Oineis , for which Eumelos son of Empedion of Euonymon

was secretary. On the eighteenth of Anthesterion,

the eighteenth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee Euphronios son of

Kleemporos of Aixone was putting the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

Theophilos son of Philotheos of Lamptrai proposed: since

Antidoros, having been allotted priest of Kalliste for the year

of Ekphantos (235/4) performed all the sacrifices

that occurred in the year on behalf of the Council

and People well and with love of honour, and

dedicated a stone altar from his own resources, and has rendered

accounts for everything on which he has expended money

in the year in accordance with the laws; so that

the Council and People should be seen to render

thanks to those who display love of honour with respect to the

sanctuary, for good fortune, the Council shall decide: that the

presiding committee allotted to preside at

the forthcoming Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda

and submit the opinion of the Council to the

People, that it seems good to the Council to praise Antidoros

son of Antidoros of Pergase and crown him with a foliage

crown for his piety towards the gods

and his love of honour towards the Council and People;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it in

the sanctuary of Kalliste; and for the inscription

of the stele and its making the administrator shall

allocate the expenditure accrued.
