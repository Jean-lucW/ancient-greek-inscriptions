# Funerary altar of Valeria Fortunata

## English translation

**Part 1**

Latin text:

To the underworld spirits,

for Valeria Fortunata,

his well-deserving companion,

Antiochus, house-slave

of our Caesar, set this up.

Greek text:

For Valeria Fortunata,

his companion,

Antiochos, slave of Caesar,

had this made.
