## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Lysistratos (369/8) . In the tenth

prytany, of Erechtheis , for which Exekestos

son of Paionides of Azenia was secretary.

Of the presiding committee Euangelos of -

was putting to the vote. [The Council and the People decided.]

**Column 1**

Crown

**Column 2**

Crown

**Part 2**

Pandios proposed: concerning what the ambassadors who

have come from Dionysios say, the Council

shall decide: concerning the letter which

Dionysios sent about the building

of the temple and the Peace, the allies

shall present a decision to the People, whatever,

on consideration of the matter, seems best

to them. And the presiding committee shall introduce the

ambassadors to the People at the next Assembly,

inviting the allies also,

and shall put their [statement] on the agenda,

and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council,

to praise Dionysios the ruler of

Sicily and the sons of Dionysios,

Dionysios and Hermokritos, because they are good

men with regard to the People of Athens

and the allies, and they support the

King's Peace, which the Athenians and

Spartans and the other Greeks

made. And to Dionysios send the

crown which the People voted him; and

crown the sons of Dionysios, each with

a gold crown of a thousand drachmas,

for their manly virtue [and friendship];

and Dionysios and his sons shall be Athenians,

themselves and their descendants, and shall be of whichever

tribe and deme and phratry they wish.

The prytany of Erechtheis shall

put to the Assembly the vote

about their citizenship; and they shall

have access to the Council and the

People first after the sacred business. The

generals and the prytany shall arrange

that these things happen. The

secretary of the Council shall inscribe this

decree [...]
