# Dedication to Hermes by ephebes (Piraeus)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Those who were ephebes in the archonship of Agathokles (106/5) (dedicated this)

to Hermes

Olympiodoros son of Satyrion of Erchia

Timesianax son of Timesianax of Epieikidai

Aphrodisios son of Eudoxos of Marathon

Philostratos son of Philostratos of Kolonai

Asklepiades son of Pasion of Halai

Euphris son of Patron of Probalinthos

Aristagoras son of Aristagoras of Piraeus

Neon son of Neon of Euonymon

under the physical trainership of Neon of Aphidna.
