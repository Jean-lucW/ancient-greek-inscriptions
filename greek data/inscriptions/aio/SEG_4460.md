# Decree of a thiasos of Bendis on Salamis (harbour) (244/3 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Lysiades (244/3) on the second of Skirophorion,

at the principal assembly. Batrachos proposed: since the managers and the secretary

have managed the sacrifices to the gods, according to what is traditional, and all

the other matters which the laws require of them, for good fortune the thiasotai

shall decide to praise them and crown each of them

with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice;

and the treasurer shall assign for the crown 15 drachmas, and on

receiving the money [they shall dedicate in the sanctuary of Bendis].
