# Grave stele of the family of Adrastos of Kios

## English translation

**Part 1**

Adrastos son of Ariston of Kios.

Theano daughter of Euaristos of Kios.

Philothea daughter of Adrastos of Kios.

Kallis daughter of Adrastos of Kios.

Hermogenes son of Adrastos of Kios.

Poa daughter of Euaristos of Kios.

Aristonike, Damon of Mylasa.

Plangon daughter of Lakleides of Aigina.

Nanous son of Dios of Herakleia.

Boidas son of Dios of Herakleia.
