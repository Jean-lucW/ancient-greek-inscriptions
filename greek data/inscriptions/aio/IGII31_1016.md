# Honours for ephebes and officials

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Kydenor (245/4) , in the [fourth] prytany, of [[- -]] ,

for which Polyktemon son of Euktimenos of Eupyridai was secretary.

On the [sixteenth of Pyanopsion, the six]teenth of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee - son of - of - was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided.

- son of - of - proposed: since the ephebes who served

in the archonship of Philoneos (246/5) maintained through the year

[...] and to the

fragmentary line

uncertain number of lines lost

[...] for good fortune,

the Council shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted to preside

in the People shall put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise the

ephebes who served in the archonship of Philoneos (246/5) and their

superintendent - son of Philokles of Euonymon and to crown

them with a gold crown according to the law for their [good order] and the

love of honour that they continued to have for the People; and to

praise the physical trainer Hermodoros son of Heortios of Acharnai

and the javelin instructor Lysikles son of Antipatros of Sypalettos and the

weapons trainer Charisandros son of Charisiades of Halimous and the

archery instructor Aristodemos and the secretary Herakleides of Kephisia and

to crown each of them with a foliage crown; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree and the names of the ephebes

on a stone stele [in the Agora]; the administrator shall allocate the

expenditure accrued.
