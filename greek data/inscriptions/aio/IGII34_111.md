# Dedication by <i>basileus</i>

## English translation

**Part 1**

Euxenos son of Euxenos of [Thorai?]

having been king, dedicated (this).

Deputies:

Gniphonides son of Semion [of -]

Timokrates son of Timo- [of -].

**Column 1**

in olive crown

The Council

**Column 2**

In olive crown

The People

[ In olive crown

The Tribe]
