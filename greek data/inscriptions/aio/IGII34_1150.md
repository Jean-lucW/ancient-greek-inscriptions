# Dedication to the Eileithyiai by a grandmother

## English translation

**Part 1**

Publius Aelius Aristokles of Pallene

son of Aelius Attalos of Pallene and

of Aelia Pythias daughter of Aristokles

of Potamos. Ma daughter of Eraton

of Phaleron (dedicated a statue of him) , her daughter's son,

to (the) Eileithyiai, when Isidora was priestess.
