# List of participants in the third (or fourth?) Augustan <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

For Good Fortune of the Council and People of Athens. In the archonship of Kleon

son of Nikias at Delphi and of Nikostratos son of Nikostratos at Athens, the following held the

Dodekais: the priest of Apollo Pythios Eukles son of Herodes, the interpreter

[chosen by the Pythian oracle] from the Eupatridai Polykritos son of Polycharmos, the interpreter [appointed]

[by the People] from the Eupatridai Diotimos son of Diodoros, the herald of Apollo

and priest of Hermes Patroos of the Kerykes Gorgippos son of Eudemos, the hieromnemon

Epainos son of Euphronios of Phaleron, the seers Lysias son of Paramonos, Aristos son of A[mmonios],

the pipe-player Menodoros son of Menodoros.
