# Equality of taxation for a man of Pergamon

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Antimachos (256/5) , in the tenth

prytany, of Hippothontis , for which Chairigenes son of Chairigenes

of Myrrhinous was secretary. On the eleventh

of Mounichion, the twenty-first of the prytany.

Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee - son of

- of Pallene was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

The Council and People decided. S- son of

-enes of Probalinthos proposed: since -ias

continues to be of good will to the Athenian People, and now

on the occasion of the public subscriptions has contributed to the People

for the protection of the city - medimnoi of barley

and 200 drachmas of silver; and he declares

that in the future he will be useful to the People

as far as he is able, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide: that the presiding committee allotted to preside

in the People, when the days set by law have

passed, shall put the matter on the agenda at the next

Assembly and submit the opinion of the Council

to the People, that it seems good to the Council to praise

-ias son of Akrot-os of Pergamon for his good will

towards the Council and Athenian People, and

equality of taxation shall be granted to him and his descendants,

the right of ownership of a house to the value of -; and the

[court presidents] shall submit for him the scrutiny of the grant

to the court [when they fill the courts];

and to inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it on the [acropolis]; and for the

[setting up] the administrator shall allocate the

expenditure accrued.
