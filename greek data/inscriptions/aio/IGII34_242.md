# Dedication by Acharnai to Ares and Augustus in the Athenian Agora

## English translation

**Part 1**

[For good fortune. When the priest] of Ares was Apollophanes,

[son of -, the temple attendants? were] Theoxenos son of Demetrios

[and - son] of Apollonios, the eponymous

[archon of the city was ], the community of Acharnai (dedicated this) for

[their benefactions to it?] as a thank-offering to Ares and Augustus

[Caesar?] Uninscribed space

-os son of Diognetos of Acharnai made it.
