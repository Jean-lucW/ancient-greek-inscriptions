# Dedication by the soldiers at Rhamnous for a general, patrol commander, and manager, 338/7 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

The soldiers dedicated (this), having crowned [the general]

[and] the patrol commander for their excellence [and justice]

and the manager, in the archonship of Chairondas (338/7)
