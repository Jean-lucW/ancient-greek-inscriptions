# Honours for the prytany of Erechtheis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] crown each of them with a foliage

crown; and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone a stele and stand

it in the prytanikon; and the administrator

shall allocate for inscribing the stele the expenditure

accrued.
