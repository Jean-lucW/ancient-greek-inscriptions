## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] [Ar]ch[e]la[os] (?)

The Council and the People decided. Akamantis

was the prytany; Phelleus was the secretary;

[Antigenes (407/6) was the archon?]; Sibyrtios was the chairman;

- proposed: for the making of [ships]

[...] the generals with Pe[rikles]

[...] from the current

[...] to [the shipwrights?]; and whatever they lend

the [trireme-makers? shall pay] back to them;

and the [generals shall dispatch those detailed?]

to sail in order to [fill the ships]

as quickly as possible; and if not, they shall be brought

before the court on a charge of -; and the [generals are to

bring actions concerning] anyone refusing

to depart; and [the Council] shall take care of

the bringing of whatever ships [the shipwrights]

send from Macedonia, in order that

[they may send them as quickly as possible] to Athens

and [fill them] and [the army] may be brought

to [Ionia?] and [keep] the best guard;

and if anyone fails to act in accordance with these stipulations, he shall owe

- drachmas, sacred to Athena;

and to the [first to come] and bring

a ship, a [prize shall be given] as may be

[decided by the People; and since Arche]la[os] both now

and in former times is a good

man to the Athenians and received

those Athenians who sailed out and

sent off [...] to the [...] and

[...] the camp,

and [...] [wood] and oars and

other good things, as much as they asked from him,

to praise [Archelaos because he] is a good man

and keen to do whatever good he can,

and in return for his benefactions to the city

[and the People of] Athens, to write up

[him and his sons] as proxenoi and

benefactors on a stone stele on the acropolis, and

[...]
