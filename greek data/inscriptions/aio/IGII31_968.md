[...]

[to crown [...] for excellence]

and good will [for the Athenian People];

and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe [this decree on a stone stele and stand]

it in the [...] ; for the inscription

of the stele [the board of administrators] shall allocate

the expenditure accrued.
