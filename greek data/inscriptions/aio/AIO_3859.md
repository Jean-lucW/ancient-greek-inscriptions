# Grave monument of Telekles and altar of Augustus

## English translation

**Face A (IG II2 12764)**

[...]

those commanding [...] the tools of wisdom,

and from the Academy, Telekles, [your fame]

was not unproclaimed by the strong Kekropians,

[and now, here], a youth, Seleukos, has placed you beneath the earth,

[weeping], and a good man paid good thanks.

**Face B (IG II2 3224/5) **

(Altar) of Emperor

Caesar, son of

the God, Augustus.
