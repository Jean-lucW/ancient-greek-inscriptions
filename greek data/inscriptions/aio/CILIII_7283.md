# Caesarea Antiochia honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

Latin text

[To Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan]

[Parthicus, grandson of the God Nerva],

[Trajan] Hadrian [Augustus, pontifex maximus],

tribunician power 16 times (132 AD) , consul 3 times, father of the fatherland, [Olympios],

Caesarea Antiochia [(dedicated this statue) on account of the many]

benefactions, some [private, some]

[public, which he performed for the colony],

[through the envoys [...] ]
