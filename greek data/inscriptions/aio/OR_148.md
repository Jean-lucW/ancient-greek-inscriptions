## English translation

**Part 1**

The Athenians spent the following on Corcyra. In the

archonship of Apseudes (433/2) and the Council for which Kritiades

son of Phaeinos of Teithras was first secretary,

the treasurers of the sacred funds of Athena,

- of Kerameis and his fellow officials, to whom

Krates son of Naupon of Lamptrai was secretary,

handed over to the

Corcyra, Lakedaimonios of Lakiadai,

Proteas of Aixone, Diotimos of Euonymon,

in the first prytany, of [Aiantis ],

(when) thirteen days (of the prytany)

had elapsed, [2?]6 talents.

In the archonship of Apseudes (433/2) and the Council

for which Kritiades son of Phaeinos of Teithras was first

secretary, the treasurers of the sacred funds

of Athena, Pronapes of Erchia and his fellow officials,

to whom Euthias son of Aischron of Anaphlystos

was secretary, handed over to the

to Corcyra, Glaukon

[of Kerameis, Metagenes?] of Koile, Drakontides

[of Thorai], in the [first] prytany,

of Aiantis , on the last day

of the prytany, [50?] talents.
