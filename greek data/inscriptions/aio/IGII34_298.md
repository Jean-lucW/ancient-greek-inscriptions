# Dedication [by the general Nikomachos] of Paiania to Aphrodite Hegemone, 211/0 BC (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[Nikomachos son of Ainiades of] Paiania,

[having been elected by the] People

[general in charge of the coastal] countryside,

[dedicated (this) to Aphrodite] Hegemone.
