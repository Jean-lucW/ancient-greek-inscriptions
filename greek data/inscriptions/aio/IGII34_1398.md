# Dedication of the Panathenaic ship and a statue of Athena

## English translation

The most solemn

competition-director of the

Great Panathenaia

and priest panages

Herennius Dexippos

son of Ptolemaios

of Hermos

the prow-ornament for the city

from the Panathenaic

ship and the

statue of the

goddess
