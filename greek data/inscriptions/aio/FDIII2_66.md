# List of participants of the second Domitianic <i>Dodekais</i> at Delphi

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of - at Delphi,

when the priests of Apollo

Pythios were G(aius) M(emmius) Euthydamos and

Eukleidas son of Astoxenos,

in the month Bathoos, and at Athens

when the priest of Pythios

and high priest was Ti(berius) Cl(audius)

Hipparchos of Marathon, and the priest

was -naios [son of - ]

[...]

For Good Fortune, in the year after the

archonship of Rufus

at Athens, in the month

of Boedromion, the following, having been sent

to Delphi

in accordance with the oracle

of the god by the Athenians

for the bull-headed

Dodekais-sacrifice,

sacrificed to the god according to the

ancestral customs: The priest of

Hermes Patroos of the Kerykes

and herald of Apollo

Pythios Philon son of Philon of Marathon,

the religious official Philon son of Polemon

of Marathon, the religious official of the

Kerykes and Euneidai

Polemon son of Philon of

Marathon, the seer Philemon,

the pipe-player

Philomousos.
