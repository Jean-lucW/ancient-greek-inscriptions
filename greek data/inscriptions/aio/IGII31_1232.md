# Honours for the prytany of Erechtheis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and managed

everything else well and with love of honour, for good fortune,

the Council shall decide, to praise the treasurer,

Hegesias of Euonymon, and to praise also the secretary, Sopatros of Lamptrai,

and to praise also the priest, and the herald of the Council and

People, Eukles of Berenikidai, and the secretary of the Council

and People, Dionysios son of - of Anakaia, and the

undersecretary, Timokles son of Me- of Kikynna; and the prytany

secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it in the prytanikon; and the administrator

shall allocate for [?] and inscribing the stele the expenditure

accrued.
