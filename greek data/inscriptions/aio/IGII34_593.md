# Dedication commemorating victories in Panhellenic festivals

## English translation

**Face-front A**

**Column 1**

In olive crown

The crown-bearers

**Column 2**

In olive crown

The tribesmen

**Face-right B**

**Column 3**

In parsley or celery crown

At the Isthmia

**Column 4**

In olive crown

At the Amphiereia

**Column 5**

In olive crown
