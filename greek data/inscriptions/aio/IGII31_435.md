# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] proxenos

and benefactor of the Athenian

People, himself and his descendants;

and he shall have right of ownership of land

and a house; and the generals and

the Council in office at any time

shall take care of him, so that

he suffers no harm; and he shall have

right of access to the

Council and the People first after

[the sacred business]; and the prytany secretary

shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele

and stand it on the acropolis;

and for inscribing the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give 30

drachmas from the People’s fund

for expenditure on decrees; and to invite

[...] to hospitality in the

[city hall] tomorrow.

**Column 1**

[ In crown

The People].

**Column 2**

In crown

The Council.
