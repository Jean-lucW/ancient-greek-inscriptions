# Statue base for Syeris, diakonos of Lysimache

## English translation

**Part 1**

Sye[ris ...]

S-

assistant

of Lysimache.

This image of

me in the sanctuary shows my form

clearly. My deeds and [spirit]

now live (?), clear to all.

An awesome fate

brought me to the most beautiful temple

of holy Pallas,

where I performed this not unglorious

labour for the goddess.

Uninscribed space

Nikomachos made it.
