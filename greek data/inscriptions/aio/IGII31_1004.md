# Honours for Xenokrates of Chios

secretary. On the [sixteenth] of Pyanopsion, the sixteenth of

the prytany. Principal Assembly. Of the presiding committee -

son of - of - was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members.

[asks of him, and] coming in contact [with many of the citizens]
