# Honours for the archon and assistants

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

the board of administrators [...]

[...] prescribed by law; and to praise his

assistants Thra- son of - of - and

Ktesiphon son of Ktesi- of - and to crown

each of them with a [foliage] crown; and

the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it in front

of the [Stoa of] Z[eus]; for the inscription of the

stele [the board of administrators] shall allocate the

expenditure.
