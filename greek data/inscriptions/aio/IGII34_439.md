# Choregic dedication of Meneteles for the City Dionysia

## English translation

**Part 1**

II

Kleidemos son of Meidon of Plotheia dedicated

(a statue of) [...] son of Autokrates of Daidalidai.

**Part 2**

I

Erechtheis was victorious in the men’s competition,

Meneteles son of Menes of Anagyrous was sponsor,

Aratos of Argos was pipe-player.

**Part 3**

II

Nikomachos made (this).
