# Thasos honours Hadrian

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Emperor

Hadrian Augustus

Olympios

(was erected by) the  
Thasians,

through the envoy and

artist, Xenophantos

son of Chares,

in the priesthood of Claudius Attikos.
