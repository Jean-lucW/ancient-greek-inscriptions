# Honours for a priest of Apollo Zoster

## Greek text

**Part 1**

ἡ βουλή, ὁ δῆμος

τὸν ἱερέα

Εὐκλῆν Εὐκλέους

Ἁλαιέα

≡

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council, the (crown)

the priest

Eukles son of Eukles

of Halai.

≡
