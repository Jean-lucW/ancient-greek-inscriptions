# Dedication to Dionysos by the Tetrapolis

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Four Cities dedicated this

to Dionysos.

Lysanias son of Kallias of

Trikorynthos was archon. Religious officials:

Phanodoros of Marathon

Melanopos of Trikorynthos

Ph[ry?]nokles of Oinoe

Antikrates of Probalinthos.
