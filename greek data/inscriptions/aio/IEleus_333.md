# Honours for Menandros, priest of the Senate, People, and the Graces

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) the priest of the Senate [of the Romans]

and the People and the Graces,

having served as eponymous archon,

on lower moulding

Menandros son of Asklepiodoros

of Gargettos (was erected by [...] ).
