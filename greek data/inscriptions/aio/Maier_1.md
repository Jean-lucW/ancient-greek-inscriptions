# Restoration of the Piraeus walls

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of Diophantos (395/4) ,

in the month of Skirophorion

for day labour.

For yoke-teams

bringing the stones,

payment: 160 dr.

For iron tools,

payment: 53 dr.
