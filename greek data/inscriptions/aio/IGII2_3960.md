# Herm for Glaukos

## English translation

**Part 1**

The Council of 500 having voted,

Telete daughter of Glaukos

of Kephisia, having served as

arrephoros, (set this up to honour) her own

son, Glaukos son of Memnon

of Anaphlystos, at the request

of her husband, Claudius Attikos.
