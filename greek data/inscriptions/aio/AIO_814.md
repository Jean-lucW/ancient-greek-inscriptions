## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] This

decree the [prytany] secretary shall inscribe on the

acropolis; the treasurer shall give the money for the stele

from the People's fund for expenditure on decrees;

[and to send ambassadors to Karystos to] receive the oaths

[from the Karystians; and there shall swear] for them the generals [and the taxiarchs and the] Council. Praise the People

of Karystos and the ambassadors of the Karystians and their

representative, and invite them to hospitality in the city hall

tomorrow. Praise also Menon the general

and the ambassadors sent to Karystos, and invite them

to dinner in the city hall tomorrow; and the treasurer

of the People shall pay them travel expenses of 20 drachmas from the

People's fund for expenditure on decrees; and the treasurer

of the People shall pay also to the ambassadors who have served on

embassies to Eretria and Chalkis and Hestiaia 20 drachmas

each. And to those who were ambassadors regarding the alliance

the treasurer of the People shall pay 10 drachmas each.

These swore: the Council of the archonship of Agathokles (357/6) ; the

generals, [[Chabrias of Aixone]] , Cha[], Iphikrates of Rhamnous,

Menon of Potamos, Philochares of Rhamnous,

Exekestides of Thorikos, Alkimachos of Anagyrous,

Diokles of Alopeke.
