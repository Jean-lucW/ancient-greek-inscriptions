# Honours for a son of Eupor-

## English translation

**Part 1**

In the archonship of -, in the

- prytany, of A-, for which

[...] was secretary.

On the twenty-seventh [...]

the - of the prytany.

[Of the presiding committee]

[...] was putting to the vote. The Council

and People decided. Lykourgos

son of Lykophron of Boutadai proposed: concerning what -s

says, the Council shall decide:

that the [presiding committee] allotted to preside

at the next Assembly shall bring

him before the People and

put his case on the agenda, and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good

to the Council, since -s son of Eupor- [...]

[...]
