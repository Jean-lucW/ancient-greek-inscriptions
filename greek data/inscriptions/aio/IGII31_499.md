# Honorific decree

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] inscribe [...] decree

on a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and for the inscribing the treasurer of the People shall give

30 drachmas from the People’s fund for expenditure

on decrees; [and to praise?] [...] ;

and to invite Hegel- to - in [the city hall]

tomorrow.
