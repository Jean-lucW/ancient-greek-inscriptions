# Honours for Lucius Vibullius Athenaios

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Athenaios son of Theophilos

of Paiania was dedicated by his father

Lucius Vibullius

Theophilos of

Paiania,

in accordance with a

memorandum of the

Areopagites and a

petition to the

Council of the [500]

and to the People.
