# Decree of a thiasos of Bendis on Salamis (251/0 BC)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Thersilochos (251/0) , on the second of Skirophorion,

at the principal assembly. Nikias proposed: since the managers

appointed by the society have managed well and with love of honour

the sacrifices to the gods and all

other matters concerning the society and have submitted accounts

of their expenses, for good fortune the thiasotai shall decide,

to praise them and crown each of them

with a foliage crown for their excellence and justice:

Rhythmos manager vac. Onesimos manager vac. Ophelion

manager vac. treasurer Dokimos vac. secretary Stratokles

of Eleusis vac. priest.
