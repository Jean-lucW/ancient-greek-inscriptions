# Funerary stele for Aischines, Epiphile and their family

## English translation

**Part 1**

Aischines son of Euethion.

Epiphile daughter of Eukles.

Phile daughter of Anaxilas.

Isthmodoros

son of Aischines.

Mika

daughter of Aischines.
