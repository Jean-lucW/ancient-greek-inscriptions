# Dedication by a competition-director

## English translation

**Part 1**

[The People were the sponsor. - was archon].

[Competition-director –]as son of Philinos of Lakiadai

**Column 1**

[ Tribe was victorious in the boys’ competition]

[...] [was pipe-player]

[...] [was director]

**Column 2**

Erechtheis was victorious in the men’s competition

[...] [was pipe-player]

[...] [was director]
