# Decree of Epikephisia honouring those chosen to prosecute Neokles

## English translation

**Part 1**

The demesmen decided. Pythodoros

son of Philokles of Epikephisia

proposed: since those chosen

by the demesmen to be prosecutors

of Neokles managed

the case well and justly

and convicted him in the

jury court, the demesmen

shall decide to praise

them for their [excellence] and

justice [...]
