# Honours for Nikostratos

## English translation

**Part 1**

Gods.

In the archonship of Euainetos (335/4) , in the

tenth prytany, of Antiochis ,

for which Proxenos son of Pylagoras of

Acherdous was secretary.

On the eighteenth of Skirophorion,

the twenty-third of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee

-os of Lamptrai was putting to the vote. Alkimachos

son of - of Myrrhinoutta proposed:

concerning the proposal that the People required

the Council to formulate and bring forward

about Nikostratos, for him

to be honoured, to the Assembly

[in the tenth prytany?], the Council shall decide:

that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the next

Assembly shall put these matters

on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People that

it seems good to the Council [...]
