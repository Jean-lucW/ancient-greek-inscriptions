# Dedication by victor in equestrian disciplines

## English translation

**Fragments ab**

**Part 1**

[...] his holy crowns

from the chariot race,

likewise those of Deo and Kore from

the Eleusinia.

**Column 1**

In crown

In the chariot

on the curved

course

**Column 2**

In crown

In the discus

uninscribed space

**Column 3**

[ Crown ]

**Column 4**

Crown

**Fragment c**

[...] -ios (son of?) Menodotos [...]

[...] they made it.
