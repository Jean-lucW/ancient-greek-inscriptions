# Letter from Hadrian to the Athenians

## English translation

**Part 1**

Emperor Caesar, son of the God Trajan Parthicus,

grandson of the God Nerva, Trajan Hadrian Augustus,

pontifex maximus, tribunician power [1]3 times , consul [3] times (129 AD) ,

father of the fatherland, to the Areopagos Council and the

[Council of the 500 and the People of the] Athenians

[...] into Attica [...]
