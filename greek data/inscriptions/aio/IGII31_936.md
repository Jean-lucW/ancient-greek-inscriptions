# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] letter trace

[...] [since the People voted concerning

- that the Council after consideration] should make a proposal

to the People, the Council shall decide: to bring

- before the People [at the first opportunity]; and he shall

have the right of ownership of land and a house in Athens, and

he shall have access to the Council immediately after the

sacred business if he needs it; and the secretary of the People shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it on

[the acropolis]; and for the inscription and the setting up

[the board of administrators] shall allocate 20 drachmas.

In crown

The People.
