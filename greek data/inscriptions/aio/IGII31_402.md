# Honours for Kephisophon of Paiania

## English translation

**Face-front a**

[...]

[...] the chairman of the [Council?] in

the [tenth?] prytany, whoever is deemed to have given

the best advice to the Council and the

Athenian People, and the Council resolved to crown him

with a gold crown of 500 drachmas

for his manly virtue and excellence towards the Council and the People, and

having voted on the matter, the Council decided

that Kephisophon had given the best advice

to the Council and the People and had taken care of everything,

well and justly, that the laws command and

the People required the Council to do, the

Council shall resolve: to praise [Kephisophon son of Kallibios]

of Paiania for his manly virtue and excellence towards

the Council and the People [...]

[...]

**Face-left b**

Crown
