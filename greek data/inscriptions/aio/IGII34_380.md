# Dedication by an ephebic gymnasiarch, 55/4 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Dionysios son of Polymnetos of –

having been gymnasiarch in the

year that Aristoxenos was archon (55/4 BC) , (dedicated this)

to Hermes, in the trainership of Philios

son of Diokles of Phrearrhioi, in the

assistant-trainership of Theodoros son of –

of Melite.
