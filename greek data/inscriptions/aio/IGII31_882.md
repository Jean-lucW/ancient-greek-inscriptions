# Honours for commanders of tribal regiments (taxiarchoi)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Archon Ourias. In the second prytany,

of Aiantis , for which Euxenos son of Kallias of Aixone

was secretary. On the penultimate day

of Metageitnion, the twenty-eighth

of the prytany. Assembly. Of the presiding committee

Diokles son of Ischyrias of Ptelea was putting to the vote

and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People

decided. Leon son of Kichesias of Aixone

proposed: concerning what the commanders of tribal regiments who were despatched

to the Basileia say about the

sacrifice which they made, for good fortune, the Council

shall decide that the presiding committee allotted to

preside in the People at the next

Assembly shall present them and

put the matter on the agenda and submit the opinion

of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council

to accept the good things which they say occurred

in the sacrifices for the health and preservation

of the Council and the People; and to praise

the commanders of tribal regiments who were despatched

to Boeotia, Deinostratos son of Deinides

of Cholleidai , Eukleides son of Lysistratos of

Piraeus , Autias son of Autokles of Acharnai ,

Habron son of Epigenes of Halai , Aristodemos

son of Aristophon of Steiria , Theophantos son of

Nikostratos of Gargettos , for their love of honour for

the Council and People; and the prytany

secretary shall inscribe this decree

on a stone stele

and stand it before the headquarters; and for

inscribing the stele the board of

administrators shall allocate 10 drachmas.
