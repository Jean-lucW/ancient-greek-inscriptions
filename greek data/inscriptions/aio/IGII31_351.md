# Honours for Rheboulas, son of the king of the Odrysai, Seuthes

## English translation

**Part 1**

Relief

Rheboulas son of Seuthes, brother of Kotys, of Angele.

Gods.

In the archonship of Aristophanes (331/0) ,

in the tenth prytany, of Kekropis .

On the tenth of Skirophorion,

the [six]teenth of the prytany.

Of the presiding committee Dorotheos

of Halai was putting to the vote.

The Council and the People decided. Nothippos son of Lysias

of Diomeia proposed: concerning what the People [instructed the Council?]

[in the tenth?] prytany [...]
