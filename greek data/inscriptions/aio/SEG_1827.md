# Honours for the priest of Asklepios

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

Since Dion son of Dion [...]

the priest of Asklepios [...]

having undertaken the priesthood in the year of [Dionysios’? (112/1) ] archonship,

and sacrificed the entry-sacrifices [...]

well and piously, and [made a bovine sacrifice] for - and

Asklepios and Hygieia at the Asklepieia and the Epidauria and

the Heroa, supplying [victims] in the finest way,

and contributed to the all-night revels of these festivals; and having conducted auspicious sacrifices on behalf of the Council

and the People and the children and women, and in everything

reported that the [rites] had turned out fine and salutary; and he strewed

the couches [...] in each of the sacrifices

splendidly and [...] ; and made

his son [...] [keyholder]

of the god [...]

and took responsibility for the orderliness of the sanctuary in compliance

with the laws, having deposited [...] in the priesthood

about - drachmas; in order, therefore, that the Council and People may also be seen

to honour and render fitting thanks to those who are

pious towards the gods and generously disposed towards the People,

and that other priests may also be zealous in the same ways,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide, that the presiding committee allotted

to preside at the forthcoming Assembly shall put these matters on the agenda,

and submit the opinion of the Council to the People that it seems good

to the Council to accept all the good reports

of the sacrifices performed for the health and preservation of the Council

and the People [...] and to praise

the priest, - son of - of - , for the piety

which he continues to show towards the gods and his love of honour towards

the People, and crown him with a foliage crown; and to give

him any other benefit of which he may be worthy; and the

prytany secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone stele

and stand it in the sanctuary of Asklepios; and for the inscribing and the stele

the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the

expense accrued.
