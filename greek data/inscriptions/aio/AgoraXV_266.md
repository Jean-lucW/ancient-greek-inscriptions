# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Aigeis

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune of the Council and [the People of the Athenians],

in the archonship of Oinophilos, [in the -th prytany] of [ tribe ],

for which Taranteinos son of Neikias of Aigilia [was the secretary [...] ].

On the eighth day of [month, the -th of the prytany. Council in the]

[Council chamber]. Of the presiding committee [...] [was putting to the vote]

[and his] fellow presiding committee members. [The Council] decided [...]

[...] proposed: since the prytaneis of A[igeis and those with

perpetual dining rights in the] archonship of [Oinophilos], having praised [and crowned him],

[declare to the Council that the] treasurer, whom [they chose from among]

[themselves, [...] ] of Plotheia, [has made] the [proper] sacrifices

in the prytany from his own resources [on behalf of the]

[Council and the People] and children and women [...]
