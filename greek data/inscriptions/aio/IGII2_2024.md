# List of ephebic friends, 111/2 AD

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

For good fortune, victory of Caesar,

Marcus Annius Thrasyllos of Cholleidai, son of

Marcus Annius Pythodoros of Cholleidai,

having been an ephebe in the year of

the archonship of Publius Aelius Hadrianus (112/3 AD)

when the superintendent was

Dositheos son of Herakleides of Pambotadai,

having served as gymnasiarch and skenarch,

inscribed his partner

and fellow ephebe Titus Flavius

Philathenaios and his other fellow

ephebes,

as they served as gymnasiarchs:

Boedromion: Titus Flavius Philathenaios of Eupyridai,

Pyanopsion: Marcus Annius Thrasyllos of Cholleidai,

Maimakterion: Titus Flavius Synekdemos of Marathon,

Poseideon: Phaidrias son of Sosandros of Elaious,
