# Honours for the prytany treasurer of Pandionis

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] of Paiania [...]

[...] many times and [...]

[...] admitted [...]

[...] for good fortune, the Council shall decide to praise [the]

[treasurer of the prytaneis, Sar]ap[i]on of Paiania, and to crown him

[with a foliage crown] and to permit the prytaneis

[and those with perpetual dining rights] to make a dedication of a painted image of him

[on a gilt shield] in the Stoa of Attalos, bearing [this] inscription:

"[The prytaneis] of Pandionis and those with perpetual dining rights in the archonship of D-

dedicated (this image of) their own treasurer," and [the secretary]

of the prytany shall inscribe this [decree] on a stone stele [and]

[stand it in the Council chamber]
