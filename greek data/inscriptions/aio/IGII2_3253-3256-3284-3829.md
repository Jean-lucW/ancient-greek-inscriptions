# Pandaites and Pasikles Monument / Honours for Augustus and his heirs

## English translation

**Face a**

**Column 1**

Lysippe, daughter

of Alkibiades of Cholleidai,

wife of Pandaites

uninscribed space

Pandaites son of Pasikles of Potamos, Pasikles son of Myron of Potamos dedicated it.

Sthennis made it.

**Column 2**

Myron

son of Pasikles

of Potamos

Sthennis made it.

**Column 3**

Pasikles

son of Myron

of Potamos

Leochares made it.

**Column 4**

Timostrate daughter of

Pandaites of Prospalta,

wife of Pasikles

Leochares made it

**Column 5**

Aristomache daughter of

Pasikles of Potamos,

wife of Echekles

[ - made it]

**Face b**

**Column 1 (IG II2 3256)**

The People (erected this statue of)

Drusus.

**Column 2 (IG II2 3254) **

The People (erected this statue of)

Tiberius Caesar.

**Column 3 (IG II2 3253) **

The People (erected this statue of)

Augustus Caesar.

**Column 4 (IG II2 3255) **

The People (erected this statue of)

Germanicus Caesar.

**Column 5 (IG II2 3284) **

(This statue of) Emperor Caesar Nerva Trajan

Augustus Germanicus Dacicus the

God, son of the God, the invincible (was erected by) the Council of the

Areopagos and the Council of the 600 and the People of the

Athenians, for their own benefactor and the saviour

of the world.
