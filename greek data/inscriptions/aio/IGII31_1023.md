# Honours for the grain protectors (sitophylakes)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Decree 1 (Council)

Gods.

In the archonship of Athenodoros (239/8) , in the twelfth prytany, [[of Antigonis]] ,

for which Arketos son of Archias of Hamaxanteia was secretary. On the

twenty-third of Skirophorion, the twenty-third of the prytany.

Council in the Council chamber. Of the presiding committee -nes son of Philostratos

of Pelekes was putting to the vote and his fellow presiding committee members. The Council decided.

Diokles son of Aristophilos of Erchia proposed: since the grain protectors

of the archonship of Athenodoros (239/8) performed the sacrifices pertaining

to their year on behalf of the Council and People and king

Demetrios and queen Phila and his descendants;

and they also managed everything else well and with love of honour which

the laws and decrees of the People prescribed for them;

and they made themselves useful to the treasurer of the Panathenaia Agathaios

and to the competition director Homeros and to the treasurer of the

Council Amphichares in whatever way they asked them; in order therefore that there be

incentive for the officials who are allotted at the time to carry out their office well and justly,

for good fortune, the Council shall decide: to praise the grain protectors

of the archonship of Athenodoros (239/8) Athenodoros of Erchia ,

Archinos of Acharnai , Euthydemos of Melite , Agatharchos of Lamptrai ,

Demokles of Anaphlystos , and the allotted secretary Ergokles of

Kropidai , for their love of honour and justice to the Council

and the Athenian People; and the prytany secretary shall

inscribe the praise for these men on a stone stele and

stand it before the synedrion; and for the inscription of the stele the

administrator shall allocate the expenditure accrued.

Decree 2 (Council and People)

In the archonship of Lysias (238/7) , in the twelfth prytany, of Pandionis ,

for which Epichares son of Eudemos of Aphidna was secretary. On the ninth

of Skirophorion, the eleventh of the prytany. Assembly.

Of the presiding committee Demophanes son of Epizelos of Halai was putting to the vote and

his fellow presiding committee members. The Council and People decided. - son of

-demos of Anaphlystos proposed: since the grain protectors of the archonship

of Athenodoros (239/8) both performed the sacrifices pertaining to their

year on behalf of the Council and the People and king Demetrios

and queen Phthia and [his] descendants, and also managed

the rest well and with love of honour which the

laws and decrees of the People prescribed for them; - sufficient grain

for the festival of the Panathenaia [...]

achieved; and they made themselves useful in the ways they asked them

to the treasurer of the Panathenaia Agathaios of Prospalta

and to the competition director Homeros of E- [...]

elected from the People, and to the treasurer of the Council

Amphichares of Eiresidai, and for these things they were praised by the Council

of six hundred; and they brought their [records] before the auditors

and rendered their accounts; in order therefore that there be [incentive] for

the officials who are allotted at the time to carry out their office well and justly, knowing that

they will be honoured by the People, the presiding committee allotted

for the coming Assembly shall put the matter on the agenda and submit

the opinion of the Council to the People, that it seems good to the Council to

praise the grain protectors of the archonship of Athenodoros (239/8) Athenodoros

of Erchia , Archinos of Acharnai , Agatharchos of Lamptrai ,

Demokles of Anaphlystos , Euthydemos of Melite , and the allotted

secretary Ergokles of Kropidai and to crown them with a [gold]

crown according to the law for their love of honour and justice

to the Council and the Athenian People; and the prytany

secretary shall inscribe this decree on a stone

stele and stand it before the synedrion; and for the inscription of the

stele the treasurer of the military fund shall allocate the expenditure accrued.
