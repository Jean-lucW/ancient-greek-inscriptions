# Honours for the Pellanians

## English translation

**Fragment a**

Decree 2

[...]

[...] was secretary (344/3?) .

[...] of the prytany.

[Of the presiding committee presiding committee] Hippoch-

[...] was putting to the vote. - son of Oinobios of

Rhamnous proposed: concerning what the envoys

of the Pellanians have reported, the People

shall resolve: as regards the decree which Aristo- [...]

proposed about the Pellanians

[last year?], the secretary

of the Council shall inscribe it on

a stone stele and stand it on the acropolis;

and the treasurer of the People shall give

for the inscribing [30?] drachmas

from the People’s fund for

expenditure on decrees [...]
