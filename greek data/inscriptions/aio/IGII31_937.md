# Proxeny

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

traces

[...] what is

in the interests of the Athenian People, and

to crown them with a gold crown

according to the law for their excellence and

manly virtue towards the Athenian

People; and they shall have

for the rest of time [so long as they display]

their good will to the People [...]
