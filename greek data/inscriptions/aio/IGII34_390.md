# Dedication by an ephebe, 36/5 or 34/3 BC?

## English translation

**Part 1**

Apollonios son of Menneas

of Azenia, having won the

torch-race, (dedicated this) to Hermes in

the archonship of Asklepiodoros;

in the trainership

of Meniskos

of Kolonai, in the

assistant-trainership of

Dioskourides of Kolonai

(and) Herakleitos of Athmonon.
