# Dedication of the Council in honour of three councillors, 280/79 BC

## English translation

**Face-front a**

**Part 1**

The Council in the archonship of Telokles (280/79) dedicated.

The People crowned these men,

having judged them to have served on the Council best:

**Column 1**

In crown

Philippos

son of Astygenes

of Thymaitadai

**Column 2**

In crown

[Himeraios]

son of Bo[on]

of Eitea

**Column 3**

In crown

Nikokrates

son of Archemachos

of Phegaia

**Column 4**

In crown

The People (crowned)

the Council

**Part 2**

when Aristeides of Lamptrai was general.
