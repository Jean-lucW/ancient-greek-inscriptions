# Decree of Aixone honouring officials (<i>syndikoi</i>?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...]

Gla[uk-?] [...]

-sthenes [...]

-ides son of Ka- (?) [...]

son of Philotheros [...]

those accompanying [...]

-tes son of Antich[ar-], -

son of Kallias, Go[rg-] son of -,

Leophilos son of Eudikos, the demarch

in office shall invite them

to their seat of honour, and

they shall have all the same grants

as have been given in accordance with the laws

to the legal representatives with

Laches, in order that others

may also show love of honour, knowing

that they will receive thanks

from the demesmen; and Philotheros

the demarch shall inscribe this

decree on a stone stele

and stand it in the theatre.

Crown
