# Honours for Eris of Byzantium and other Byzantines

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] the privileges

[...] Athenians worth-

[...] and [...]

[...] advantageously and [...]

for good fortune, the People shall decide, to praise

Eris son of Kothon of Byzantium and crown him with a gold

crown for the good will and love of honour which he has

towards the Athenians; and he shall also be proxenos

and benefactor of the People, and right of ownership shall be given

to him of land and a house according to the laws;

and to praise also those who were trierarchs with him,

Menophanes son of Asklepiades, Hekataios son of Kolchos, and the

treasurer, Glaukos son of Lysias, Byzantines, and crown

each of them with a foliage crown for their good will

towards the People; and to invite Eris

and Menophanes and Hekataios and the treasurer to

dinner in the city hall to hospitality tomorrow;

and on their striving in future in an honour-loving way to display

more good will towards the Athenians, it shall be possible for them

to obtain greater benefits from the People,

whatever they may be deemed worthy of;

and the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree on a stone stele and stand it

on the acropolis; and the treasurers of the [military fund?]

shall take care of the making and setting up of

the stele and allocate the expenditure accrued,

so that, these things being brought to pass, there may be an incentive

for all to benefit the People [...]

thanks [worthy] [...] [honour?]
