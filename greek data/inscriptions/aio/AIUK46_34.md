# Funerary naiskos of Metagenes

## Greek text

Μεταγένης Ἐπιγένους Φιλουμενὴ Τηλοκλέους

Κυδαθηναιεύς. Κυδαθηναιέως.

(relief)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Metagenes (son) of Epigenes Philoumene (daughter) of Telokles

of Kydathenaion. of Kydathenaion.

(relief)
