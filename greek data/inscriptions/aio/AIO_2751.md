## English translation

**Part 1**

[(This statue of) the most brilliant]

[consular and]

[eponymous] archon

[Marcus Ulpius] Eubiotos

[Leuros] of Gargettos (was erected) on account of

all his [excellence] and his

love [of honour regarding the]

fatherland, both in the

grain funds,

[to which] he gave [bountifully]

two hundred and fifty thousand (denarii)

[and] in the provisions, which

he provided in [the great] famine,

by decree of the

[mighty] Areopagites

[the priest of Apollo Patr]o[os]

[Publius Aelius]

[Zenon of Berenikidai (erected it for)],

[the common benefactor and]

[his own patron]
