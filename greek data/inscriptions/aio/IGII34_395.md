# Dedication by an ephebe victor at the Epitaphia, 17/6 BC (?)

## English translation

**Part 1**

Antiochos son of Phaidrias of Phlya,

having won the torch-race at the

Epitaphia in the year that Apolexis

son of Philokrates of Oion

was archon (17/16?) ,

(dedicated this to) Hermes, in the trainership

of Philios son of Philios the younger

of Phrearrhioi and of Apollonides of Phrearrhioi, in the

assistant-trainership of Demetrios

son of Leukios of Halai.
