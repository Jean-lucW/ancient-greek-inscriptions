# Honours for Prienian Ambassadors

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...] to stand it

on the Acropolis, and for the inscribing of the stele

the treasurer of the People shall give 30 drachmas from the

People’s fund for expenditure on decrees. And to praise the

ambassadors who have come from the Prienian People and

to invite them to hospitality in the city hall tomorrow.

In crown:

The Council (honoured him). The People (honoured him).
