# Honours for commanders of the cavalry (hipparchs) and of the tribal contingents of cavalry (phylarchs)

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] and [...]

[...] the Council and People; and to announce this

crown at the competition of the Great Dionysia; the

board of administrators shall manage the making of the crown

and the announcement; and they shall have a front

row seat at all competitions that the city puts on; and

the prytany secretary shall inscribe

this decree and the names of the commanders of the cavalry and of the

tribal contingents of cavalry on a stone stele and stand it by the Herms; and for the

inscription of the stele the board of administrators shall allocate

10 drachmas.

**Column 1**

Cavalry commander

Diokles of Thria

Commanders of tribal contingents

Praxikydes of Aphidna

-les of Kephisia

Glaukon of Aithalidai

-os of Erchia

-ron of Oe

-anes of Athmonon

**Column 2**

Cavalry commander

Philion of Phrearrhioi

Commanders of tribal contingents

Sosistratos of Sounion

Theomedes of Eleusis

Agathaios of Prospalta

Hagnias from Oion

Leon of Pallene

Sosigenes of Myrrhinous

**Part 2**

The People.
