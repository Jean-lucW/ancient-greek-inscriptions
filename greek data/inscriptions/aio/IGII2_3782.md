# Honours for Argaios the doctor

## English translation

**Part 1**

(This statue of) Argaios son of Argaios of Plotheia

was dedicated by Batakes son of Batakes of Piraeus,

having been tended by him.

Demetrios son of Philon of Ptelea made it.
