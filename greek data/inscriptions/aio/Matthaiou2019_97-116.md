son of [Seuthes] and Amadokos son of Medodokos and Kersebleptes

son of Kotys; and if one of the cities that [...]

is liable to pay tribute to Berisades and Amadokos

and Kersebleptes and also [...] to the Athenians,

Berisades and Amadokos

in charge of the force shall exact it

paying the traditional tribute to Berisades and Amadokos

and Kersebleptes and to the Athenians

being allies of the Athenians as they swore,

and of Berisades and Amadokos and Kersebleptes; and if

any of the cities defects from the Athenians, Berisades
