# Decree of Rhamnous and resident Athenians honouring the trierarch Menandros of Eitea, 224/3 BC

## English translation

**Part 1**

Damaged relief depicting a ship above a moulding inscribed with the archon date (epi ...)

The Rhamnousians and those citizens living at Rhamnous

decided. Timokrates son of Epigenes of Oe proposed: since

Menandros having been appointed trierarch for the year

of Niketes' archonship (224/3) managed

the equipping of the vessel well and with love of honour spending

from his own resources as much as those responsible for these things required of him;

and he also provided oil for the youths

in order that, by taking care of their bodies, they might become stronger;

and he sacrificed also to Zeus Soter and Athena

Soteira for the health and preservation and harmony of

those sailing with him, so that being of one mind and

kept safe they might also later be useful to the People;

and he entertained with love of honour from his own resources; and crowned

the officers on the vessel for their love of honour

towards themselves; and donated the cost of guarding the vessel

from his own resources and when visiting Rhamnous

sacrificed to Nemesis with the general and the

religious officials chosen to accompany him; ⟦ [...] ⟧ and also donated sacrificial animals

and wine; and so that there may be an incentive for those appointed

trierarchs at any time, knowing that they will obtain thanks worthy

of their benefactions, for good fortune, those Athenians

sailing together in the undecked vessel shall decide

to praise Menandros son of Teisandros of Eitea and

crown him with a gold crown according to the law

for his excellence ⟦ [...] ⟧ and love of honour towards themselves;

and he shall also have freedom from tax on sailing for the future

and to inscribe the decree on a stone stele and stand it by

the gate; and to choose forthwith three men from themselves to

implement what has been decreed; and the expenditure incurred

shall be accounted for to the community. These were chosen:

Timokrates son of Epigenes of Oe; Archestratos son of Aischines of Erchia;

Diokles son of Dion of Hamaxanteia.

In crown

The Athenians

who sailed with him (honoured)

Menandros

son of Teisandros

of Eitea.
