# Dedication by ephebic victors

## English translation

**Part 1**

Those who won

the torch-race

Asklepiades

son of Eukarpos of Athmonon ,

Ladikos son of Sozon

of Sounion ,

Sozon (son of Sozon) of Sounion ,

Kallimachos son of Dionysios,

Rufus, Agathon (son of Agathon),

Sotas, Dionysios,

Zosimos, ⟨S⟩ynphas (?),

Phoibos, Attikos,

Zosimas, Sotes.
