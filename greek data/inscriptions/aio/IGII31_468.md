# Honours for Apses and Hieron of Tyre

## English translation

**Part 1**

[...]

[...] from Carthage [...]

[...] brought [...] [cheaper]

than [what was available?] from Italy,

and for the future announces

that he will bring grain to Athens and will do

whatever good is in his power for the Athenians,

both himself and his father,

to praise Apses son of Hieron of Tyre and

Hieron son of Apses of Tyre for their excellence and

good will towards the Athenian People,

and crown both of them

with a gold crown; and they shall be

proxenoi and benefactors of the Athenian

People, themselves and their descendants,

and shall have right of ownership of land and a house,

according to the law; and the

secretary [...] shall inscribe this decree [...]

[...]
