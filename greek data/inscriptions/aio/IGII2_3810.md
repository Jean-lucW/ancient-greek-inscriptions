# Honours for Titus Pompeius Dionysios

## English translation

**Part 1**

The City (erected this statue of)

Titus Pompeius Dionysios

of Paiania, one of those from the Mouseion,

philosopher, having been king archon,

on account of his piety towards the gods and

his good will towards his

fatherland.
