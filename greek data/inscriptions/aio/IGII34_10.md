# Dedicatory inscription on the temple of Roma and Augustus

## English translation

**Part 1**

The People (dedicated this temple) to the Goddess Roma and ⟦Augustus Soter⟧Augustus Caesar, when the hoplite general

was Pammenes son of Zenon of Marathon, priest of the Goddess

Roma and Augustus Soter on the Acropolis, when the priestess of Athena

Polias was Megiste daughter of Asklepiades of Halai,

in the archonship of Areios son of Dorion of Paiania.
