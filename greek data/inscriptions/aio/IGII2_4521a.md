# Honours for Aurelius Zosimos the temple attendant (zakoros)

## English translation

**Part 1**

For good fortune,

in accordance with the petition

to the most awesome

Council of the 500, (this herm was erected of) Aurelius

Zosimos son of Euhemeros

of Eroiadai, having been temple attendant

of the Saviour Asklepios

brilliantly,

his mother having paid for it,

priestess of Ge Olympia

Aurelia Zosime daughter of Zosimos

of Hermos.

phallus
