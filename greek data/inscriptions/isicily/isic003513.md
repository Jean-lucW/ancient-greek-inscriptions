# Funerary inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic003513)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) · Χ(θονίοις) ·  
Ἀβιανία · Σαβεν[α]  
ἔζησεν · ἔτη  
· λε ·

### English translation

To the spirits of the underworld. Abiana Sabeina, lived 35 years.
