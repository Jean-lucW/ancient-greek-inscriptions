### English translation

When ??? was in office...--archos son of Hagelochos sixth (phratry)-second (tribe) bought a house, a portico...in the district of Herakles, and the courtyards from the inner room...and the well and the private mill and the doorways...for five talents from Dikaiagora the daughter of Antallos.
