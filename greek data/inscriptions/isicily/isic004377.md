# Epitaph for Likinis Isauros (I.Sicily ISic004377)

## Ancient Greek text

Λικίνις Ἰσαυ  
ρος ζῶν ὢν  
ἐποίει ἑαυτῷ  
καὶ τοῖς ἰδίοις

### English translation

Likinis Isauros made this while still living for himself and his family
