# Epitaph for Katylleinos (I.Sicily ISic003224)

## Ancient Greek text

❦ Κατυλλεῖνος ❦  
ἒζη[σε]ν ἔτη  
ιγ ❦ ἐποίησεν  
Εὐοδία μνήμης  
χάριν ❦ ⸙

### English translation

Katylleinos lived for 13 years. Eudoia made (this) for his memory
