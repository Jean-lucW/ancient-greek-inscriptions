# Honorific inscription from Haluntium, modern San Marco d'Alunzio (I.Sicily ISic001192)

## Ancient Greek text

οἱ ἀλειφόμε[νοι]  
Ἀγάθαρχον  
Ἡρακλείου

### English translation

The annointed (honour) Agatharchos, son of Herakleios.
