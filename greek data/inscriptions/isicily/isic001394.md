# A dedication to Apollo (I.Sicily ISic001394)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀπόλλωνι  
Ἡράκ⟨λ⟩ειος  
Ἀριστοφύλου  
δεκυρεύσας  
ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

To Apollo. Herakleios, son of Aristophylos (set this up), elected to the senate (or boule?), at his own expense.
