# Funerary inscription (I.Sicily ISic001281)

## Ancient Greek text

[Θε]οις χθο[νίοις]  
Ἀγαθη χ[αῖρε]  
[ἔ]ζησεν [ἔτη ...]

### English translation

To the Gods of the Underworld. Agathe, farewell! She lived for … years.
