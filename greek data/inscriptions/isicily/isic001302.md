# Epitaph for Ioulia Galene (I.Sicily ISic001302)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) ❦ κ(ατακθονίοις)  
Ἰουλία  
Γαλήνη  
ἡ φιλόστολος  
ἔζησε · ἔτη  
λγ

### English translation

To the subterranean deities. Ioulia Galene, 'philostolos', lived for 33 years.
