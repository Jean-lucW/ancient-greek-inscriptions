# Epitaph for Sabeina (I.Sicily ISic003243)

## Ancient Greek text

Λόλλις · Εὐτύχης  
Σαβείνᾳ · ἰδίᾳ ·  
συνβίῳ · ζῶν ἐποίη  
σε ·  
μνήμης χάριν ·  
ἔζησεν ἔτη λε

### English translation

Lollis Eutyches made (this) when he was still alive for his wife Sabeina in her memory. She lived for 35 years.
