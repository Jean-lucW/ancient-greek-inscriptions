# Funerary inscription for Kornelios Agathemeros (I.Sicily ISic001308)

## Ancient Greek text

❦ Θ(εοῖς) Κ(αταχθονίοις) ❦  
Κορνήλιος · Ἀγα  
θήμερος · ἐζη·  
Εὐφροσύνη · ἰδίῳ συν  
βίῳ μνήμης χάριν

### English translation

To the gods of the underworld. Kornelios Agathemeros lived 38 years. Euphrosyne (set this up) as a memorial for her own partner in life.
