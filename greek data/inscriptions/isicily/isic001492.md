# Epitaph for Aisaris Sattaras (I.Sicily ISic001492)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) · Κ(αταχθονίοις) ·  
Αἴσαρις · Σατ  
τάρας · ἔζη  
σεν · ἔτη  
· ν·β ·

### English translation

To the underworld deities, Aisaris Sataras lived for 52 years
