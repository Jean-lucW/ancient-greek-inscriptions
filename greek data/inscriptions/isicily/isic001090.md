# Dedication inscription from Selinus, modern Selinunte (I.Sicily ISic001090)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρκεσὼι  
Αἰσχύλου  
Ἥραι εὐχάν

### English translation

Arkesoi, daughter of Aischylos, (fulfilled her) vow to Hera
