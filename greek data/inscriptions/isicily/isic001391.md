# Greek inscription recording enjoyment of a spring (I.Sicily ISic001391)

## Ancient Greek text

Κελαδιανὸς, Λάλος, Ῥοῦφος  
Φησεῖνος, Εὐσέβης  
Παυλεῖνος εὐφράνθησαν

### English translation

Keladianos, Lalos, Rouphos, Pheseinos, Eusebes, Pauleinos enjoyed themselves
