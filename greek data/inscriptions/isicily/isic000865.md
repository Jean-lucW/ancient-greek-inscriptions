# Funerary inscription from Syracusae, modern Siracusa (I.Sicily ISic000865)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰσπὴς ἔζη  
σεν ἔτη ιβ  
μῆνας δ  
ἡμέρας  
δ

### English translation

Ispes lived for 12 years, 4 months and 4 days
