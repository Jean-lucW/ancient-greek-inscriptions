# Inscription recording architect of the Bouleuterion (I.Sicily ISic003505)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄσκλαπος Διοδώρου Ταυρέας  
ἐπιστάτας  
Βίβακος Τιττέλου Ποσθαῖος  
ἀρχιτεκτόνησε

### English translation

Asklapos Taureas, son of Diodorus, was the overseer. Bibakos Posthaios, son of Tittelos, was the architect.
