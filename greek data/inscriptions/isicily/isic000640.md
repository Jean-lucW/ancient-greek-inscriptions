# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic000640)

## Ancient Greek text

Πουβλειλί  
α ❦ Ἀγάθιν ❦  
χρηστά · χαῖρε ·  
ἔζησεν ἔτη · λγ ·

### English translation

Farewell worthy Publilia Agathin! She lived for 33 years.
