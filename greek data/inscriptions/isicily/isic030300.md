# Entella 8 = A2, decree of Entella recording good will and isopoliteia with the people of Enna (I.Sicily ISic030300)

ντων Ἀρτ

πίου Πανάμου

### English translation

In the archonship of Artemidoros son of Eielos and Gnaios son of Oppios, on the new moon of Panamos. Since the Ennaioi have always been well-disposed towards us, both when we were in our own land and when we were expelled from it and were wandering, encouraging us and receiving us in their city and territory; resolved by the council and the assembly that they are to enjoy good will and common citizenship with the people of Entella for all time. The archons are to write this decree on a bronze plaque and exhibit it in the council.
