# Funerary stele for Hegesippos (I.Sicily ISic002104)

## Ancient Greek text

Ͱε̄γέ̄σιπός ἐ̄μ  
ι τὺ Βύλιος

### English translation

I am Hegesipos, (son) of Bulis
