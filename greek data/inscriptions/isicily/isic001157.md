# Funerary inscription from Thermae Himeraeae, modern Termini Imerese (I.Sicily ISic001157)

## Ancient Greek text

θεοῖς κατ[αχ]  
θονίοις  
ΜΗΚΑΛΙ Σουλ  
πίκιος Ἀνί  
κητος ἔζη  
σεν ἔτη

### English translation

To the gods of the Underworld. ... Soulpikios Aniketos lived 40 years.
