### English translation

When Aristion son of Histieios was hierapolos, Sosis son of Nymphodoros was annual magistrate: decrees of the council of the second semester, on the 30th of Karneios, Hippokles son of Hippokles presiding: regarding a crown for the gymnasiarch. It was resolved by the assembly, as also by the council: since the gymnasiarch elected for this current year, Herakleidas son of Zopyros, has taken great care both for the ephebes and the younger men and the others who disrobe to use the gymnasium, and has accomplished all other matters concerning the gymnasium excellently, behaving in a manner worthy of himself and of the excellence of his ancestors, it is therefore fitting to crown in the assembly the gymnasiarch Herakleidas son of Zopyros with an olive wreath, on account of his care and zeal concerning the gymnasium; and that this decree is to be carved on a stele and set up in the gymnasium; and that the Thirty pay out a sum for the preparation of the stele, according to what the council has determined.  

The people of the Geloioi praises and crowns the gymnasiarch Herakleidas son of Zopyros with a crown of olive on account of his care and zeal concerning the gymnasium.  

The ephebes who have been crowned are: Asklapiadas son of Asklapiadas; Artemon son of Euthymos; Antallos son of Antallos; Polyxenos son of Agatharchos; Protarchos son of Protarchos; Geloios son of Gorgylos, Aristion son of Nymphodoros; Sosipolis son of Isidoros; NIkaros son of Pyrrommis; Apollonios son of Satrros; Zopyros son of Herakleidas, Char(-?)
