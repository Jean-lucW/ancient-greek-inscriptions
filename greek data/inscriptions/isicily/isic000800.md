# Honorific for [---] Lapiron Sal., son of Apollodoros (I.Sicily ISic000800)

## Ancient Greek text

θεοῖς π[ᾶ]σι  
[ὁ δᾶμ]ος τῶν Ἁλαισίνων  
[...]Ἀπολλοδώρου Σαλ  
Λαπίρωνα  
[εὐνοίας]καὶ εὐεργεσίας ἕνεκ(εν)  
τᾶς εἰς αὑτόν

### English translation

The people of the Halaesini (dedicate) to all the gods (a statue of) [-?-] Lapiron Sal., son of Apollodoros, on account of the good will and beneficence which he has shown towards them.
