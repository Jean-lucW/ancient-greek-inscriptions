# Epitaph for Merkouriales (I.Sicily ISic003246)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) Κ(αταχθονίοις)  
Μερκουριάλης  
[ἔζ]ησεν · ἔτη  
μβ ·  
[γυν]ὴ · ἔποισε

### English translation

To the underworld deities. Merkouriales lived for 42 years. His wife made (this).
