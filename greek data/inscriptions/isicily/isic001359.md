# Funerary inscription for Kallistos and Eukarpia (I.Sicily ISic001359)

## Ancient Greek text

Κάλλιστος καὶ Εὐ  
καρπία οἱ φιλόστορ  
γοι ζῶντες ἑαυτοῖς  
ἐποίησαν καὶ τοῖς  
ἰδίοις · εἰρήνη πᾶσι

### English translation

Kallistos and Eukarpia, who, loving one another tenderly, made this while living for themselves and their family. Peace to all.
