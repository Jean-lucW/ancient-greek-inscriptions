# Fragmentary funerary inscription of Marcus Vipsanius Phoebus (I.Sicily ISic003263)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ (εοῖς) Κ (αταχθονίοις)  
Μ (ᾶρκος) Βειψάνιος  
Φοῖβος ἔζησ (εν) ἔτη  
κϛ πατὴρ καὶ μή  
τηρ υἱῷ εὐσεβεῖ

### English translation

To the gods of the underworld. Markos Beipsanios Phoibos (= Marcus Vipsanius Phoebus) lived for 26 years. His father and mother (set this up) for their devoted son.
