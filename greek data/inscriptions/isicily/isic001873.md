# Funerary inscription for Kopria (I.Sicily ISic001873)

## Ancient Greek text

Κοπρία  
χρηστὰ ❦ καὶ  
ἄμεμπτε  
χαῖρε ❦ ἔζη  
σεν ἔτη λ

### English translation

Kopria, worthy and blameless, farewell. She lived 30 years.
