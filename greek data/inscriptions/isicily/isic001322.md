# Epitaph for Saboukis Kallimorphos (I.Sicily ISic001322)

## Ancient Greek text

Σαβοῦκις  
Καλλίμορφος  
ἔζησεν · ἔτη ·  
· λβ · Σαβούκις ·  
Ζωίλος · πάτρων ·  
ἐποίησε ·

### English translation

Saboukis Kallimorphos, lived for 32 years. Saboukis Zoilos, his patron (made this).
