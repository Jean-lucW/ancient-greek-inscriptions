# Funerary inscription from modern Castiglione di Ragusa (I.Sicily ISic003474)

## Ancient Greek text

Το͂ι Πυτίκα  
Πυρίνο̄ι  
ἐποίε̄σε  
Σϙύλος

### English translation

To the (son) of Pytikas Pyrinos, Skylos made it.
