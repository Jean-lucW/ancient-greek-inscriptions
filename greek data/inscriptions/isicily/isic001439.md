# Metrical epitaph for Kaprogonon (I.Sicily ISic001439)

## Ancient Greek text

τᾶς hαγία θ  
υγατρός εἰμι  
Καπρογόνο̄

### English translation

I am (the stele) of Kaprogonon, daughter of Hagias
