# Epitaph of Agathopous (I.Sicily ISic001282)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) ❦ Κ(αταχθονίοι) ς  
Ἀγαθόπου χρη  
στὲ ❦ χαῖρε  
ἔζη[σας ἔτη ...]

### English translation

To the underworld spirits. Agathopous, worthy man, farewell! You lived ---.
