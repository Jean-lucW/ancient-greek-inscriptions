# Mosaic depicting wrestlers, with artist's signature (I.Sicily ISic001904)

## Ancient Greek text

[ἐπ] ἀγαθῷ Νεικίας Διονυσίου δοῦλος · ἐποίει ·

### English translation

Verna, Afer. To good fortune! Neikias, slave of Dionysios made (this).
