# Dedication inscription from Drepanum, modern Trapani (I.Sicily ISic001103)

## Ancient Greek text

[Γ(αίον) Ἀσίννιον N]  
εικόμ[αχον Ἰ]  
ουλιανὸ[ν]  
ὕπατον  
Εὐτυχίω[ν]  
ἐπίτροπο[ς]

### English translation

To Caius Asinius Nicomachus Iulianus, consul, by Eutychion, procurator.
