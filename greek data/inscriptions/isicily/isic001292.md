# Epitaph for Grapte’s husband (I.Sicily ISic001292)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]νᾳ ἄν  
δρὶ ζήσαντι  
ἔτη με · Γρά  
πτη σύμβιο[ς]  
ἐποίησ[εν]

### English translation

To the husband [---] who lived for 45 years, his wife Grapte set this up.
