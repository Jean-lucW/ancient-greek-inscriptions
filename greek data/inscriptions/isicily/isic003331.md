# The Syracusans honour King Gelon, son of Hieron II (I.Sicily ISic003331)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος τῶν Σ υρακοσίων  
βασιλέα Γέλωνα βασιλέος Ἱέρωνος  
Διὶ Ἑλλανίωι

### English translation

The people of the Syracusans (dedicate/honour) King Gelon, son of King Hieron to Zeus Hellanios
