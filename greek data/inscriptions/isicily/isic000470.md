# Bilingual sign advertising engraving of inscriptions (I.Sicily ISic000470)

## Ancient Greek text

στῆλαι  
ἐνθάδε  
τυποῦνται καὶ  
χαράσσονται  
ναοῖς ἱεροῖς  
σὺν ἐνεργείαις  
δημοσίαις

### English translation

Stelai are set out and engraved here for sacred temples together with public works / inscriptions are set out and engraved here for sacred temples together with public works
