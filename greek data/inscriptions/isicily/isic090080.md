# Dedication to an association of landowners (I.Sicily ISic090080)

## Ancient Greek text

Τῆι συνόδωι τῶν συνγεούχων  
Πτολεμαῖος Ἀλεξάνδρου  
Αἰακιδεύς. ( ἔτους) κ.

### English translation

To the association of fellow landowners, Ptolemaios the son of Alexandros, of the deme Aiakideus, (dedicated this), year 20.
