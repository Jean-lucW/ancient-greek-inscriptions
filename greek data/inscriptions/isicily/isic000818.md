# Funerary inscription from Netum (I.Sicily ISic000818)

## Ancient Greek text

Εἰφιόνισα  
χρηστὰ καὶ  
ἄμεμπτε  
χαῖρε  
ἔζησε ἔτη  
γ

### English translation

Eiphionisa, worthy and blameless, farewell. She lived three years.
