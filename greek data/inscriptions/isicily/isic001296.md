# Funerary inscription from Syracusae, modern Siracusa (I.Sicily ISic001296)

## Ancient Greek text

μνησθοῖ  
Ἐνκάρπιν  
χρηστὴ καὶ  
ἄμεμπτος  
ἔζησεν ἔτη  
ν

### English translation

May Enkarpin be remembered, honest and blameless. Lived for 50 years.
