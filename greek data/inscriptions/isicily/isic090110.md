# Dedication of a Kleopatreion by the garrison of Schedia (I.Sicily ISic090110)

## Ancient Greek text

θεῶν Φιλομητόρων Σωτή-  
ρων οἱ ἀποτεταγμένοι  
ἐπὶ Σχεδίᾳ στρατιῶται ὧν  
ἡγεμὼν καὶ χιλίαρχος Σωσίπατρος  
τὸ Κλεοπατρεῖον.

### English translation

In favour of Queen Kleopatra and King Ptolemy, [or: King Ptolemy also called Alexander and Queen Kleopatra (or: Berenike his sister) the gods Philometores Soteres, the soldiers stationed in Schedia, whose commander is the chiliarch Sosipatros, the Kleopatreion.
