# Epitaph for Eukarpia (I.Sicily ISic001853)

## Ancient Greek text

Εὐκα[ρπί]  
α χρηστ[ά χα]  
ῖρε ἔζησ[ας]  
ἔτη [...]  
θ

### English translation

Farewell, worthy Eukarpia, you lived 8 years.
