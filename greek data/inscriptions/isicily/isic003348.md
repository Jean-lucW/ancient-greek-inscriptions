# Tyndaris honours Antimachos as Euergetes (I.Sicily ISic003348)

## Ancient Greek text

Ὁ δᾶμος  
Ἀντίμαχον  
Φίλωνος  
[ε]ὐεργέταν

### English translation

The people (honours) Antimachos son of Philo, benefactor
