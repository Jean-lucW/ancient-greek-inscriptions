# Funerary epitaph for Stateios Eutyches (I.Sicily ISic001264)

## Ancient Greek text

Στάτειος  
Εὐτύχης  
ἔζησε ἀμέμ  
πτως ἔτη · ιϛ  
μῆνες γ  
ἡμέρας · ζ

### English translation

Stateios Eutyches, lived blamelessly for 16 years, 3 months, 7 days.
