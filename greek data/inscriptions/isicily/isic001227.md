# Dedication inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic001227)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀσκληπιῷ  
καὶ Ὑγείᾳ  
σωτῆρσιν  
πολιούχοις.

### English translation

To Asklepios and Hygeia, saviours and protectors.
