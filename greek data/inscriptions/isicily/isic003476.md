# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic003476)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ε]ὐπραξ[ία] Ἡρακλε[ί]  
δαι Ἡρα[κ]λείδα  
ἐποίη[σε]

### English translation

Eupraxia made (this) for Herakleidas son of Herakleidas
