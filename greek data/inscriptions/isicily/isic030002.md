# Part of a Syracusan decree mentioning the gamoroi (I.Sicily ISic030002)

## Ancient Greek text

[θεός τύχα ἀγαθά οἱ Συρακόσι]οι ἐψαφίσαν  
[το ... καὶ ἀτ]έλειαν καὶ ἔν  
[πασιν γᾶς καὶ οἰκίας ...] αἴ κα γαμόρο̄ν  
[ἐσγόνοις εἶμεν καὶ τᾶν πασᾶν ἀ]ρχᾶν πεδεῖμ  
[εν αὐτοῖς πλάν ... καὶ] hιπάρχου καὶ  
[...]

### English translation

God, (grant) good luck. The Syracusans have decreed [in favour of ... and immunity] from taxes and the right [to the possession of land and house ...] in case they are descendants of gamoroi and (have decreed) that they have the right to participate in all public offices [with the exception of (that of) ... and] (that of) the hipparchus and [---]
