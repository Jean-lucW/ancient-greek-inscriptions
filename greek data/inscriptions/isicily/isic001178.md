# Halaesa honours Gaius Vergilius Balbus, proquaestor (I.Sicily ISic001178)

## Ancient Greek text

Γάιον Οὐεργίλιον Γαίου υἱὸν Βάλβον  
ἀντιταμίαν ⟨ὁ⟩ δᾶμος τῶν Ἀλαισίνων  
εὐνοίας ἕνεκεν

### English translation

The people of the Halaesini (honour) Gaius Vergilius Balbus, son of Gaius, proquaestor, on account of his good will
