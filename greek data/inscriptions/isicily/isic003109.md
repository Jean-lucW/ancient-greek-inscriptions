# Dedication inscription from Catina, modern Catania (I.Sicily ISic003109)

## Ancient Greek text

Δάματρι καὶ [Κ]όραι  
[...]ων καὶ ἁ γυνὰ αὐτοῦ Ἀρισ[... κ]αὶ τὰ τέκνα ἀ[νέθηνκαν?]

### English translation

To Demeter and Kore/...and his wife Aris...and their children set this up (?)
