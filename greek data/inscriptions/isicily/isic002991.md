# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic002991)

## Ancient Greek text

[Θ(εοῖς)] Χ(θονίοις)  
[... χ]ρησ  
τά χαῖρε ❦  
ἔζησες ❦  
ἔτη ❦ κ ❦

### English translation

To the underworld spirits. [---] a good woman, farewell! You lived for 20 years.
