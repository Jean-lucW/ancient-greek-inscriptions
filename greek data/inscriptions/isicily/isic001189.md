# Haluntium honours the son of Timandros (I.Sicily ISic001189)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος  
Βίωτον Τιμάνδρο[υ]  
εὐνοίας ἕνεκεν

### English translation

The people (honours) Biotos son of Timandros, for the sake of goodwill
