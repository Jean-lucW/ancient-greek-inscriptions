# Honorific inscription from Segesta, modern Segesta (I.Sicily ISic001107)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος τῶν Ἐγεσταίων Φάλα[κρον]  
[Δ]ιοδώρου Ἐρύσσιον ἀρετᾶς ἕνε[κα]

### English translation

The people of the Segestans (honours/dedicates) Phalakros Eyrssios, son of Diodoros, on account of his virtue.
