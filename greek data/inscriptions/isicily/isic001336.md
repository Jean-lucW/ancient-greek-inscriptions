# Funerary inscription (I.Sicily ISic001336)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ΕΝΕ[...]ΙΝΕΣ[...ἐκ τ]  
[ῶν] ἰδίων [ἐποίησεν]  
[τό]πον [ἑ]α[υτῷ]  
[καί τοῖς ἰδίος]

### English translation

… built it at his own expense for himself and his children.
