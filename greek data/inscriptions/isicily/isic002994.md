# Dedication by a bronze-worker (I.Sicily ISic002994)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ χαλκοτύπος  
Μένας μ’ἀ  
νέθηκεν  
εὐχάν

### English translation

Menas the bronzeworker dedicated me, as a votive offering
