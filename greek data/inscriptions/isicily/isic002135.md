# Funerary epitaph for Besson (I.Sicily ISic002135)

## Ancient Greek text

Βέσσων  
[χρ]ηστὸς καὶ  
ἄμεμπτος  
ἔζησεν  
ἔτη η [...]  
[ἡ]μέρας ζ

### English translation

Besson, worthy and blameless, lived 8 years, [x months?], 7 days.
