# The people of Tauromenion honour Nymphodoros son of Eukleides (I.Sicily ISic003124)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος τῶν Ταυρομενιτᾶν  
Νυμφόδωρον Εὐκλείδα Αρεθ  
εὐνοίας ἕνεκεν

### English translation

The people of the Tauromenitani honour Nymphodoros, son of Eukleidas, Areth., for the sake of good will.
