# Dedication inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic002816)

## Ancient Greek text

[στενις καλινις σ]ταττιηις  
[μαρασ πομπτιες] νιυμσδιηις  
[μεδδειξ ο]υ[π]σενς  
[εινειμ τωϝτo] μαμερτινο  
[αππελλο]υνηι σακορο

### English translation

SStenis Kalinis (son) of Stattis (and) Maras Pompiis (son) of Niumdis, meddices, and the Mamertine people built (this). Sacred to Apollo.  

[Latinised] Stenius Calinius (son) of Stattius (and) Maras Pompius (son) of Niumidius, meddices, and the Mamertine people built (this). Sacred to Apollo.
