# Honorific inscription for Marcus son of Publius (I.Sicily ISic003351)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος [τῶν Ἁλαισίνων]  
Μάρκον [...]  
Ποπλίου υἱὸν [...]  
εὐνοίας ἕ[νεκεν καὶ]  
εὐεργε [σίας τᾶς]  
ἐς α[ὐτον]

### English translation

The people of the Halaesini (honour) Marcus [-?-] son of Publius [-?-], on account of his goodwill and beneficence towards them
