# Funerary inscription from Selinus, modern Selinunte (I.Sicily ISic001466)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστογείτο̄ ε̄̓  
μὶ ⁝ το͂ Ἀρκαδίονος  
hὸς hυπὸ Μοτύ  
ϝαι ⁝ ἀπέθανε

### English translation

I am (the stele) of Aristogeitos, son of Arkadion, who died below (the walls of) Motya
