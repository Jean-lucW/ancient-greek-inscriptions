# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic004364)

## Ancient Greek text

θ(εοῖς) · κ(αταχθονίοις) ·  
Σόλων  
χρηστὲ ·  
χαῖρε · ἔζη  
σες ἔτη  
· ιη ·

### English translation

To the spirits of the underworld. Solon, a good man, farewell. You lived 18 years.
