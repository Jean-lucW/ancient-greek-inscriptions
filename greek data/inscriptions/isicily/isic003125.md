# The people of Tauromenion honour Nymphodoros son of Philistion (I.Sicily ISic003125)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμ[ο]ς τῶν Ταυρομενιτᾶν  
Νυμφόδωρον Φιλιστίωνος Σπαρ  
εὐνοίας καὶ εὐεργεσίας ἕνεκεν  
τᾶς εἰς αὑτὸν θεοῖς πᾶσι

### English translation

The people of the Tauromenitani (honour) Nymphodoros son of Philistion, Spar., for the sake of good will and generosity which (he has shown) towards them. (Dedicated) to all the gods.
