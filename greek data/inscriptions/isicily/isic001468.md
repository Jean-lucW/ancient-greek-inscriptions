# Epitaph of Agasias, son of Karias (I.Sicily ISic001468)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀγασία ε̄̓μ  
ὶ τὸ σᾶμα τ  
ο͂ Καρία οἴ  
μοι

### English translation

I am the marker of Agaisas, son of Karias, Alas!
