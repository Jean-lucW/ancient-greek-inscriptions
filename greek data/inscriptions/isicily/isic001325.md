# Epitaph for Sosia Elate (I.Sicily ISic001325)

## Ancient Greek text

Σοσίᾳ Ἐλάτῃ  
γλυκυτάτῃ  
Ἀφροδείσιος  
σύμβιος

### English translation

To the sweetest Sosia Elate, Aphrodeisios, her husband.
