# ISic030319 (I.Sicily ISic030319)

## Ancient Greek text

Φαίκων ἀπὸ Κεντοριπίνων ἀνέθηκε πάσι θεοίς

### English translation

Phaikon dedicated (this belt taken) from the (men) of Kentoripe to all the gods.
