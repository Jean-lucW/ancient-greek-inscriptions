# Epitaph of Iason and Theudora (I.Sicily ISic001556)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰάσων Ἀρταφᾶντος  
ἔζησεν ἀμέμπτω  
ς ἕτη ο  
Θευδώρα  
ἔζησεν ἀμέμπτω  
ς ἕτη ξ

### English translation

Iason son of Artaphan lived blamelessly for 70 years; Theudora lived blamelessly for 60 years.
