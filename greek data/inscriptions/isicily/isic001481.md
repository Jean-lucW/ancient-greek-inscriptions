# Funerary inscription from Dianae Fons, modern Comiso (I.Sicily ISic001481)

## Ancient Greek text

τεῖδε Χοροὶ Κα[τ]  
ελὸς κεῖ⟨ν⟩ται θα[ν]  
άτοιο λαχόντε  
ς ἀνφοτέρος δ  
ὲ καλὸς ͱυιὸς ἔ  
θαπσε φίλος

### English translation

Here lie Choro and Katelos, having come to death. Both of them, in fine fashion, their beloved son has buried.
