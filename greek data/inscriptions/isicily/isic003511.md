# Epitaph for Klodia Romana (I.Sicily ISic003511)

## Ancient Greek text

❦ Θ(εοῖς) Δ(αίμοσιν) ❦  
Κλωδία  
Ῥωμάνα  
ἔζησε ἔ  
τη λε  
❦ ❦

### English translation

To the divine spirits. Klodia Romana lived 35 years.
