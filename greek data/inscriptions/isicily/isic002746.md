# Artist's signature inscription from Segesta, modern Segesta (I.Sicily ISic002746)

## Ancient Greek text

[Δ]ιονύσιος [Ἡρ]ακλ[είδου]  
[Ἀλεξ]ανδρεὺς ἐ[ποίει]

### English translation

Dionysios, son of Herakleides, the Alexandrian, made this
