# Funerary inscription for Herais (I.Sicily ISic001170)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] ων  
[...] ου  
[...] λε  
[χαῖ]ρε  

Ἡραΐ  
Ἡρύλου  
θυγάτηρ  
χαῖρε

### English translation

[Right column]: Herais, daughter of Herylos, farewell.
