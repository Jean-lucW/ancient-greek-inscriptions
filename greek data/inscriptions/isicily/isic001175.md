# Halaesa honours Diogenes Lapiron, son of Diogenes (I.Sicily ISic001175)

## Ancient Greek text

[θ]εοῖς πᾶσι  
[ὁ] δᾶμος τῶν Ἀλαισίνων  
[Δι]ογένην Διογένεος  
Λαπίρωνα  
[εὐ]εργεσίας ἕνεκεν

### English translation

The people of the Halaesini (dedicate) to all the gods (a statue of) Diogenes Lapiron son of Diogenes on account of his beneficence.
