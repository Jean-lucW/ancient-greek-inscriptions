# Votive dedication of the ruler of Gela, reinscribed by Polyzalos (I.Sicily ISic004437)

## Ancient Greek text

[Π]ολύζαλός μ’ ἀνέθηκ[ε]  
[... τ]ὸν ἄεξ’ εὐο̄́νυμ’ Ἄπολλ[ον]

### English translation

Earlier version: “[...] he, being ruler of Gela, dedicated. [...] Give him glory, noble Apollo.”  

Later version: “Polyzalos dedicated me. [...] Give him glory, noble Apollo.” (Adornato)
