# Dedication to Damater(?) (I.Sicily ISic003009)

## Ancient Greek text

Δάμ[ατρι καὶ Κόραι]  
βασιλ [έα Ἱερώνυμον]  
βασι [λέος Γέλωνος]  
[Συρακόσιοι]

### English translation

The Syracusans dedicate (this statue of) King Hieronymos, son of King Gelon, to Damater and Kore
