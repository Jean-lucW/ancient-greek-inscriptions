# Fragmentary metrical epitaph (I.Sicily ISic000651)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] αὐτῷ τύμβο[ν ...] τῷδε τάφῳ  
[... ἐκ] διαθήκων  
[... τὸ στέφο]ς ἀμφέθετο  
[... δό]σις υἱ[ῷ]  
[...]

### English translation

--- for him a grave --- / --- in this tomb / --- in accordance with his dipositions / --- he put round himself a crown / --- a gift to the son.
