# Statue base for Hieron II of Syracuse (I.Sicily ISic000823)

## Ancient Greek text

Βασιλέος ἁγε [μόνος]  
̒Ιέρωνος Ἱεροκλέος  
Συρακόσιοι θεοῖς πᾶσι

### English translation

The Syracusans (dedicate this statue) of the king and hegemon Hieron, son of Hierokles, to all the gods
