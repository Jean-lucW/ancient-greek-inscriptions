# The koinon Kinakon honours Marcus Iunius Felix (I.Sicily ISic002988)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(άρκον) Ἰούν(ιον) Φήλι[κα]  
βουλευτ(ὴν) Λιλυ[βα]  
ειτῶν φιλοτει[μη]  
σάμενον περἰ τὴ[ν]  
πατρίδα τὸ κοινὸ[ν]  
Κινακων ἐκ τῶν  
ἰδίων πάτρω[νι]  
ἀξίῳ  
δ δ β

### English translation

The koinon Kinakon, at its own expense, (honours) Markos Iounios Phelix, councillor of the Lilybaetanans, who has been zealous for his fatherland. For a worthy patron, by decree of the councillors and council(?).
