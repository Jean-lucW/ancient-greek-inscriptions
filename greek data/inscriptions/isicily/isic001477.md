# Dedication inscription from Netum (I.Sicily ISic001477)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀγέμαχος Πολυάρχου  
Ἐλώρωι καὶ Τιμάσσαι

### English translation

Agemachos son of Polymarchos (dedicated this) to Eloros and Timassa
