# Building inscription from Soluntum, modern Solunto (I.Sicily ISic003067)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἄν]ταλλος Ἀσκλάπου Ὀ[ρνιχᾶ]ς  
[...]  
[σ]τρῶσ[ιν] ἐκ τοῦ ἰδ[ίου]

### English translation

Antallo Ornichas, son of Asklapos .... (laid) the pavement at his own expense
