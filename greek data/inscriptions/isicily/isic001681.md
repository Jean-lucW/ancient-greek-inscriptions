# Honorific inscription from Segesta, modern Segesta (I.Sicily ISic001681)

## Ancient Greek text

[ὁ δᾶμος] τῶν Ἐγεστα[ίων]  
[... Ἀ]ρτέμωνος Ν[...]  
[πρεσ]βύτερον ἀρετᾶ[ς ἕνεκα]

### English translation

The people of the Segestans (dedicate this statue of) [---] N[---] the elder, son of Artemon, on account of his excellence
