# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic001400)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεοῖς κατα  
γαιδίοις ❦  
Κορνελία ❦  
χρηστὴ χαῖ  
ρε ἔζησες ❦  
❦ ἔτη κβ

### English translation

To the gods who dwell beneath. Farewell kind Cornelia. You lived for 22 years
