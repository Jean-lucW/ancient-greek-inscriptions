# Epitaph for Threptylos (I.Sicily ISic001300)

## Ancient Greek text

• Θ(εοῖς) [Κ(ατακθονίοις)]  
Θρεπτύλο[ς ἔζησεν]  
μῆνας [...]  
• Εὐφροσύνη • μήτηρ

### English translation

To the underworld spirits. Threptylos lived for [---] months. His mother Euphrosyne (made it).
