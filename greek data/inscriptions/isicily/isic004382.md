# Epitaph erected by Sossis for his wife Rufina Marcia (I.Sicily ISic004382)

## Ancient Greek text

Ῥουφείνα Μαρκία  
ζήσασα ἔτη λε  
Σόσσις ἀνήρ ἐπόη  
σε ἰδίᾳ γυναικί εὐσεβεσ  
τάτη μνημεῖον

### English translation

Rufina Marcia having lived for 35 years, her husband Sossis made this memorial for his own most pious wife
