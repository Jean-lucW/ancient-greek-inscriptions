# Funerary inscription for Basil (I.Sicily ISic002992)

## Ancient Greek text

[Θ(εοῖς)] Χ(θονίοις)  
[...] Βασι  
[... χαῖ]ρε ἔζη  
[σες ἔτ]η · κ·ε · καὶ  
[ἔνθα κ]ατοικεῖς  
[εἰς τ]οὺς αἰῶ  
[νε]ς ❦

### English translation

To the spirts of the underworld. Farewell ... Basil... ! You lived for 25 years and you'll live here forever.
