# Building dedication for the Temple of Apollo (I.Sicily ISic000822)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλεομ [...]ε̄ς ∶ ἐποίε̄σε το̄̓πέλο̄νι ∶ ͱο Κνιδιε[ί]δα ∶ τἐντε[λ]ε͂ στύλεια ∶ κα[λὰ] ϝέργ[α]

### English translation

Kleo[...]es, the son of Knidieidas, built the entire colonnades for Apollo. Successful works!
