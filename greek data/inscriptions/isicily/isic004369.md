# Inscription recording an architect called Proxenos (I.Sicily ISic004369)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρχιτέκτω ν  
Πρόξενος  
Λωιΐσκου  
Θ ε ρμίτας

### English translation

Proxenos, son of Loiïskos of Thermae Himeraeae, architect
