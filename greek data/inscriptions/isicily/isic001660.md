# Lilybaeum honours Diognetos Megas as euergetes (I.Sicily ISic001660)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος  
τῶν Λιλυβαιιτᾶν  
Διόγνητον  
Δαματρίου Μήγα[ν]  
εὐεργέταν

### English translation

The people of the Lilybaitani honour Diognetos Megas, son of Damatrios as benefactor
