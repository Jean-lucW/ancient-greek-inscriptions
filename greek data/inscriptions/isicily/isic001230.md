# Dedication inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic001230)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀσκληπιῶι  
και Ὑγείᾳ  
σωτῆρσιν  
πολιούχοις.

### English translation

To Asclepius and Hygeia, saviours and protectors.
