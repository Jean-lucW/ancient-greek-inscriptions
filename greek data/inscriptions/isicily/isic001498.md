# Epitaph of Marcus Antonius Skorpus, from Corinth (I.Sicily ISic001498)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(άρκος) Ἀντώνιος  
Σκόρπος Κορίν  
θιος ἐνθάδε κίτ  
αι ἐτῶν κε χαίρετε  
οἱ προσιόντες  
❦

### English translation

Marcus Antonius Skorpus the Korinthian lies here, 25 years old. Farewell, those present.
