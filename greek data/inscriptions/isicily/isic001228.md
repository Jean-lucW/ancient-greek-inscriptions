# Honorific inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic001228)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰλίῳ Ἀδριανῷ  
Ἀντωνείνωι  
Σεβαστῷ Εὐσεβεῖ  
π(ατρὶ) π(ατρίδος)

### English translation

To Aelius Adrianus Antoninus Augustus Pius, father of the country.
