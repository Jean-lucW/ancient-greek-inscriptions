# Inscribed funerary urn of Flavianos (I.Sicily ISic003236)

## Ancient Greek text

Φλαβιανὸς · χρησ  
τὸς · καὶ ἄμεν(πτος) · ἔζη(σεν) ·  
ἔτη · λζ · μῆν(ας) · γ ·  
ἡμέ(ρας) · ι ·

### English translation

Phlabianos (Flavianus), worthy and blameless, lived for 37 years, 3 months, 10 days.
