# Apollonia(?) honours Andron son of Thrasios (I.Sicily ISic001181)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος  
Ἄνδρωνα Θρασίου Λαβ  
εὐεργεσίας ἕνεκεν  
θεοῖς πᾶσι

### English translation

The people (dedicate) Andron, son of Thrasios, Lab. on account of his good will to all the gods.
