# Funerary inscription from Messana, modern Messina (I.Sicily ISic003528)

## Ancient Greek text

Σέλλιος Πρα  
κτικὸς καὶ Λικ  
ινία Κλείτη ζῶ  
ντες ἑαυτοῖς  
ἐποίησαν ❦

### English translation

Sellios Praktikos and Likinia Kleite made this for themselves while living.
