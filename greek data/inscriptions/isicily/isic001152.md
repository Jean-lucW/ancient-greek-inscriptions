# Funerary inscription from Thermae Himeraeae, modern Termini Imerese (I.Sicily ISic001152)

## Ancient Greek text

Θε(οῖς) · Κα(ταχθονίοις)  
Πονπωνία  
Μάξιμα  
ἔζ(ησεν) · ἔτ(η) · μ.  
Κλ(αύδιος) Μέλισσος  
τῇ ἰδίᾳ  
συ[μ]βίῳ

### English translation

To the gods of the Underworld. Pomponia Maxima lived 40 years. Klaudios Melissos (made this) for his life partner.
