# Epitaph for Euphrainon (I.Sicily ISic001399)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) Κ(ατα) χ(θονίοις) ❦  
Εὐφραίνων  
χαῖρε ❦  
χρηστέ ἔζησας  
ἔτη · ζ · μῆ(νας) γ

### English translation

To the underworld gods. Euphrainon farewell, worthy one you lived 7 years, 3 months.
