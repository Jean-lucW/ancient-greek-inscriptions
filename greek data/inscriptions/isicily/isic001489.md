# Dedication to Hermes (I.Sicily ISic001489)

## Ancient Greek text

Φάλακρος ⁝ Θευδώρου  
Ἑρμᾶι ⁝ ἀνέθηκε

### English translation

Phalakros son of Theudoros dedicated (this statue) to Hermes
