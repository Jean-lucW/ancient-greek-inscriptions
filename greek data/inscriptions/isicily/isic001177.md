# Soldiers serving at Eryx honour their commander (I.Sicily ISic001177)

## Ancient Greek text

θεοῖς πᾶσι  
οἱ στρατ ⟨ευσ⟩ άμενοι  
ἐν Ἔ⟨ρυκ⟩ι  
[...]  
[...]  
Ἡράκλεον Διοδώρου  
Κα [...]

### English translation

The men who served as soldiers in Eryx [-----] (dedicate) to all the gods (a statue of) Herakleios son of Diodorus Ka[---], who was military tribune in Eryx, on account of his goodwill.
