# Funerary inscription for Mousa (I.Sicily ISic001872)

## Ancient Greek text

Μοῦσα  
χρηστὰ ❦ καὶ  
ἄμεμπτε  
χαῖρε ἔζη  
σε μῆνες  
ι · ἡμέρας κε

### English translation

Mousa, worthy and blameless, farewell! She lived 10 months, 25 days.
