# Funerary inscription from Megara Hyblaea, modern Priolo Gargallo (I.Sicily ISic003372)

## Ancient Greek text

Εὐτυχής χρηστὸς καὶ ἂμ  
εμπτος χαῖρε ἔζησε ἔτη λ

### English translation

Eutyches, worthy and blameless, farewell! He lived 30 years.>
