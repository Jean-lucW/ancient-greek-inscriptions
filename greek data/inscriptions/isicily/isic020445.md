# ISic020445 (I.Sicily ISic020445)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστοκλείας ε̄̓μὶ τᾶς καλᾶς καλὰ | ͱαύτα δ᾽ε̄̓μὰ Πίθαϙος αἰτε̄́σας ἔχει  

ΑΗ

### English translation

I am Aristocleias the beautiful's beautiful (lekythos), and she is mine; Pithacos after demanding her owns her.
