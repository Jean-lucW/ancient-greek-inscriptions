# Honorary inscription from a gymnasium (I.Sicily ISic001280)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] Νεμηνίου [...] Ὄρθωνος Ν[... καὶ οἱ? ἀλειφό?]μενοι ΕΡ[...]Κ ΑΣ[...]

### English translation

(name) son of Nemenios --- (name) son of Orthon --- and the attendants of the gymnasium ---.
