# Funerary inscription for Kailia Heorte (I.Sicily ISic003223)

## Ancient Greek text

Δαίμοσι  
χθονίοις  
Καιλία · Ἑορτὴ ·  
ἐβίωσε · ἔτη · ο  
Βλάστη · θυγάτηρ  
μητρὶ · εὐσεβεσ  
τάτῃ · ἔποισε

### English translation

To the underworld spirits. Kailia Heorte lived for 70 years. Blaste, her daughter, made this for a devoted mother.
