# Dedication by Marcus Aimilius Rho[--] (I.Sicily ISic000770)

## Ancient Greek text

θεοῖς πᾶσι  
Μ(άρκος) · Αἰμίλιος · Ῥό[δων]  
Κίπου · υἱὸς · ὑπ[ὲρ τῆς]  
ἀγορανο μ [ίας]  
ἀνέθ[ηκεν]  
ἐκ τῶ[ν ἰδίων]

### English translation

Markos Aimilios Rhodon, son of Kipos dedicated (this) to all the gods at his own expense in commemoration of his aedileship.
