# A koinon of priests honours [-?-] Lapiron, son of Diogenes (I.Sicily ISic001176)

## Ancient Greek text

[...τὸ κοι]νὸν τῶν ἱερέω[ν ... Διογ]ένεος Λαπίρων[α ...]  
[...εὐνοίας? ἕνεκ]εν καὶ εὐεργεσία[ς ...]

### English translation

The koinon of the priests (honours) [-?-] Lapiron, son of Diogenes, on account of his good works and good will.
