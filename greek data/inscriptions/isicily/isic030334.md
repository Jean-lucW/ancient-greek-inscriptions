# Dedication of Pantares at Olympia (I.Sicily ISic030334)

## Ancient Greek text

Παντάρε̄ς μ’ ἀνέθε̄κ[ε] Μενεκράτιος Διὸ[ς ἆθλον?] το͂ Γελοαίο̄

### English translation

Pantares, son of Menekrates the Geloan, dedicated me as a prize of Zeus.  

Pantares, the son of Menekrates, from the renowned plain of Gela, dedicated me, after having won by horse the contests of Zeus. [Alternative translation by Fouquet following Ebert's edition]
