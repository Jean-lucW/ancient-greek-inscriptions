# Dedication inscription from modern Montagna di Balate (I.Sicily ISic002744)

## Ancient Greek text

Νικόμαχος  
hαγεσία  
Γελλίας  
Ἀντισθένεος  
Λυκύμνιοι

### English translation

Nichomachos son of Hagesias, Gellias son of Antisthenes, Lykymnioi (dedicated this)
