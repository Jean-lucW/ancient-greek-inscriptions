# The municipium of the Haluntini honours Gnaeus Pollienus (I.Sicily ISic001190)

## Ancient Greek text

Τὸ μουνικίπιον τῶ[ν]  
Ἀλοντίνων Γναῖον  
Πολλιηνὸν ΠΟΛΛΕΑ[...]  
υἱὸν εὐεργ[έ]των ἀ[π]ό  
γονον εὐνο[ίας] ἕνεκεν

### English translation

The municipium of the Haluntini (honours) Gnaios Pollienos son of [?], descendant of benefactors, for the sake of goodwill
