# Dedication inscription from modern Montagna di Balate (I.Sicily ISic002743)

## Ancient Greek text

τοὶ Χιθαῖοι  
ἀνέθηκαν  
καὶ Πολέμαρχος  
καὶ Γελλίας

### English translation

The Chithaioi dedicated (this), both Polemarchos and Gellias
