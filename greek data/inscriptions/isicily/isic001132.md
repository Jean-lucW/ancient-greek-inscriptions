# Dedication to Aphrodite by the city magistrates (I.Sicily ISic001132)

## Ancient Greek text

Δωρόθεος Λυσ[...]  
Ἄνταλλος Νύμφ[ωνος]  
ἀγορανομήσαν[τες]  
Ἀφροδίται  
[[ἐκ τοῦ δ[ήμ]ο[υ]]]  
γραμματεὺς  
Ἀπολλόδωρος Φαλάκρου

### English translation

Dorotheos Lys[----], Antallos son of Nymphon having served as agoranomoi, (dedicated this) to Aphrodite, at public expense. The secretary was Apollodoros son of Phalakros
