# Inscription recording works undertaken by the agoranomos Herakleios (I.Sicily ISic002727)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἡράκλειος Διονυσίου  
Πετρεῖνος ἀγορανομέων  
ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

Herakleios Petreinos, son of Dionysios, while serving as agoranomos, at his own expense.
