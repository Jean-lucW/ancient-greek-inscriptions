# Epitaph for Daphne Lykeia (I.Sicily ISic004378)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ(εοῖς) Κ(αταχθονίοις)  
Δάφνη Λύ  
κεια ἔζη  
σεν ἔτη  
λγ

### English translation

To the shades of the underworld. Daphne Lykeia lived 33 years.
