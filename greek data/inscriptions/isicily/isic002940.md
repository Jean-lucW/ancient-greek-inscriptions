# Honorific inscription from Segesta, modern Segesta (I.Sicily ISic002940)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀρτε]μιδώρα Νύμφωνος τὸν αὐτάστα πατέρα  
[Ἀρτέ]μωνα Ἀλείδα γυμνασιαρχήσαντα  
[ἀνέθ]ηκε κατὰ διαθήκαν

### English translation

Artemidora wife of Nymphon dedicated (this statue of) her own father, Artemon son of Aleidas, gymnasiarch, according to the terms of the will.
