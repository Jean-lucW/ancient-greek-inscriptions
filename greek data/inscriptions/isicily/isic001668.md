# A tessera hospitalis recording xenia between Imulch and Lyson (I.Sicily ISic001668)

## Ancient Greek text

Ιμυλχ [Ιμι]λχωνος  
Ινιβαλος Χλωρὸς ξενίαν  
ἐποήσατο πρὸς Λύσων  
Διογνήτου καὶ τῶν ἐγγόνων

### English translation

Imulch, son of Imilchon, Inibal(os) Chloros established guest-friendship with Lyson son of Diognetos and their descendants
