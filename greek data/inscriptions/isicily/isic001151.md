# Funerary inscription for Opetreia Letois (I.Sicily ISic001151)

## Ancient Greek text

Ὀπετρηία Λητώι Μου  
σαίου γυνήι χαῖρε χρησ  
τὴ καὶ εὐσεβής

### English translation

Opetreia Letois, wife of Mousaios, worthy and pious, farewell.
