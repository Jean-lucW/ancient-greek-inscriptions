# Dedication inscription from Segesta, modern Segesta (I.Sicily ISic001679)

## Ancient Greek text

Διὶ ὁ δᾶμ[ος τῶν Ἐγεσταίων]  
Ἡράκλειον Νύμφωνος Πε[...]  
ἀρετᾶς ἕνεκα

### English translation

To Zeus. The people of the Segestans (have dedicated this statue of) Herakleios Pe[---] son of Nymphon, on account of his excellence
