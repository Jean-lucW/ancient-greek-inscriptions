# Funerary inscription from Catina, modern Catania (I.Sicily ISic001307)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλωδία Βαλεντεῖνα  
χαῖρε ἔζη(σεν) ἔ(τη)  
ἐποίησε ἰδίᾳ γυναι  
κὶ Ἰγνᾶτις Κάρικος  
μνή(μης) χάριν

### English translation

Sacred to the spirits of the underworld. Klodia Balenteina, farewell. She lived 40 years. Ignatis Karikos made this for his own wife, for the sake of her memory.
