# Funerary inscription (I.Sicily ISic001289)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀφρο]δισίας καὶ  
[Θε]υδᾶ καὶ Μαρύλλας  
εἰς μνείαν γονέων  
τέκνα ἐποίησαν

### English translation

(Property of) Aphrodisia, Theudas, Marylla. The sons built (i.e. this) in memory of their parents.
