# Epitaph for Glykera (I.Sicily ISic001397)

## Ancient Greek text

Γλυκέρ  
α χρηστ  
ὰ χαῖρε  
ἔζησες  
ἔτη · νγ ❦

### English translation

Glykera, worthy one, farewell! you lived 53 years.
