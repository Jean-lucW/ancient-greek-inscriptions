# Entella 6 = C1, renewal of friendship between the Entellinoi and the Assorinoi (I.Sicily ISic030299)

### English translation

When Lucius the son of Paccius was hieromnamon, on the 26th day of Eumenideios. Resolved by the Assembly as also by the council. Since in former times there has existed common friendship between us and the Assorinoi, and similarly now too when we have resettled the city they perform services according to their powers, resolved that there remain with the Assorinoi for the people of Entella the common citizenship originally existing and friendship and goodwill and that they be invited to the games. The archons are to write this decree on a bronze plaque and exhibit it in the temple of Hestia, so that there may be a memorial for future generations of the friendship and common citizenship which exists with the Assorinoi. (Text relating to the) Assorinoi.
