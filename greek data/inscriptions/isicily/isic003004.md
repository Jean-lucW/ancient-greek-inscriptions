# Votive dedication of Artemidoros (I.Sicily ISic003004)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρτεμίδωρος  
Κλήμεντος · δ(οῦλος) · κατ' ὄναρ

### English translation

Artemidoros, son of Clemens, slave, (dedicated this) on account of a dream.
