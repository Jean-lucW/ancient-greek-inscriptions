# Funerary inscription from Centuripae, modern Centuripe (I.Sicily ISic004365)

## Ancient Greek text

· θ(εοῖς) · κ(αταχθονίοις) ·  
Σεκοῦνδα ·  
χρηστὰ χαῖ  
ρε · ἔζησες  
ἔτη οε ·

### English translation

To the spirits of the underworld. Sekounda, a good lady, farewell. You lived 75 years.
