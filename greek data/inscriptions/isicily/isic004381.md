# Fragmentary Greek epitaph (I.Sicily ISic004381)

## Ancient Greek text

[Θ(εοῖς)] · Κα(ταχθονίοις)  
[...]γνητος  
ἔζησεν  
ἔτη · μ ·

### English translation

To the shades of the underworld. [--]gnetos lived 40 years
