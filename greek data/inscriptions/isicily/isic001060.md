# Dedication by the Hieronian youths in the gymnasium (I.Sicily ISic001060)

## Ancient Greek text

ἐπὶ γυμνασιάρχω[ν]  
Ἀριστίωνος – [το]ῦ Ἀγαθ [...]  
Φιλιστίωνος – τοῦ Ἐπικράτ[εος]  
νεανίσκ[οι Ἱερ]ώνειο[ι]

### English translation

When Aristion son of Agath[---] and Philistion son of Epikrates were gymnasiarchs, the neaniskoi Hieroneioi (dedicated this).
