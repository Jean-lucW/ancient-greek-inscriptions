# Epitaph for the foundling Epaphrus (I.Sicily ISic003522)

## Ancient Greek text

Κορνήλιος  
Μάγνος · Ἀ  
παμεὺς · ἰδί  
ῳ · θρεπτῷ  
Ἐπαφρύδι  
μ(νήμης) · χ(άριν)

### English translation

Cornelius Magnus of Apamea set this up for his adopted foundling, Epaphrus, for the sake of memory.
