# Statue base in honour of Olympis Mestos (I.Sicily ISic001259)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δᾶμος τῶν Ταυρομενιτᾶν  
Ὄλυμπιν Ὀλύμπιος Μεστὸν  
νικάσαντα Πύθια κέλητι  
τελείωι

### English translation

The people of the Tauromenitani (dedicate/honour) Olympis Mestos son of Olympis, having been victorious at the Pythian games in the riding contest
