# Private honours for a grandson (IGCyr2 108900)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀκέσανδρον Θεοχρήστω  
[Ἀ]κέσανδρος Τιμάρχω  
τὸν ἄμναμμον

### English translation

Akesandros son of Timarchos (dedicated the statue of) Akesandros son of Theochrestos his grandson.
