# Dedication and list of ephebes (IGCyr2 126700)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

[καὶ τῶν πρ]εσβυτ[έρων]  
[...] Ιγισα[ντος]  
[τριακ]άτι[οι]  
[... Ν]έω[νος]  

[...]

### English translation

[... as well as] (the gymnasiarch) for seniors [So-and-so] son of Igisan, (were honored by the) triakatioi: [So-and-so] son of Neon, [...].
