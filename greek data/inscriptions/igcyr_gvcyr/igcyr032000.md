# Dedication to the Eumenides and to the Heroes (IGCyr2 032000)

## Ancient Greek text

Δημητρίου ἴδιος,  
Εὐμενίδων, Ἡρώων.

### English translation

(Altar) of the Eumenides and of the Heroes, the own property of Demetrios.
