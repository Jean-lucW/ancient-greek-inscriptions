# Private honours (?) (IGCyr2 103600)

## Ancient Greek text

Σθένων[α ...]  
Πρᾶξις Κ[...],  
Σθένων Κ[...], ,  
Ἀριστόκριτ[ος ...]

### English translation

(The statue of) Sthenon (son of So-and-so) (was dedicated by) Praxis son of K[...], Sthenon son of K[...] and Aristokritos [son of So-and-so].
