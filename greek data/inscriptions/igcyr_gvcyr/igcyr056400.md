# Epitaphs (IGCyr2 056400)

## Ancient Greek text

Δράκων Ποσειδίππω,  
[Ἁ]γεμόνα Δράκοντος.

### English translation

Drakon son of Poseidippos. Hagemona daughter of Drakon.
