# Private honours and artist's signature (IGCyr2 021000)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  
[...]  

Ἄριστις Θευδώρω  
ἱαριτεύων  

[... Θευδ]ώρω ἀνέθηκε.  

Ἀγάθω[ν Ἀγα?]θοκλεῦς ἐπόησεν.

### English translation

i. [So-and-so son of So-and-so ...],  

Aristis son of Theudoros, priest.  

[So-and-so son of Theu]doros dedicated (the monument).  

ii. Made by Agathon son of[Aga?]thocles.
