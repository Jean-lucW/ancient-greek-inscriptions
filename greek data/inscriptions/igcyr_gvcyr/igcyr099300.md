# Dedication on occasion of the telesphoria of Artemis (IGCyr2 099300)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]νις Ιγισαντος [...]  
Ἀρταμίτια τελεσ[φορέων? ἀνέθηκε?]

### English translation

[...]nis son of Igisan dedicated (the statue) of [So-and-so son of So-and-so] while celebrating the telesphoria of the Artamitia.
