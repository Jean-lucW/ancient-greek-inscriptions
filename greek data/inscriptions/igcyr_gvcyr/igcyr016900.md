# Dedication to Asklepios (IGCyr2 016900)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεμίσων Ἀρίστιος  
Ἀσκλα[πι]ῶι ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Themison son of Aristis dedicated to Askle[pi]os.
