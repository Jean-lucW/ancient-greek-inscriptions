# Dedication (IGCyr2 020800)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐπὶ Μελανίππω·  
ἀπὸ σκυλακίων,  
ἀπ’ ὀρνίχων.

### English translation

Being (priest) Melanippos, thanks to the revenues from puppies and birds.
