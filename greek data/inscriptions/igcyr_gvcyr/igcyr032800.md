# Owner's mark (IGCyr2 032800)

## Ancient Greek text

[Κλε]υγένευ[ς]  
[Σ]π[ο]νδάρχω.

### English translation

(Altar) belonging to Kleugenes son of Spondarchos (or :to Kleugenes and Spondarchos).
