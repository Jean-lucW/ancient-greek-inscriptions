# Tithe on occasion of telesphoria (IGCyr2 104600)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

[Χαριείδης?]  
[...]  

[Ἀριστοξ?]ήνα  
[Χαριείδε?]υς  

Ἀριστοβώλα  
Στάσιος  

Ἁγησίδ[αμος]  
Χαριείδ[ευς]  

[?]Χαριείδης [... ...ια τελε]σφορήσας δε[κάταν ἀνέθηκε]

### English translation

[So-and-so, son of So-and-so].  

[Charieides? son of So-and-so].  

[Aristo?]xena daughter of [Charieid]es.  

Aristobola daughter of Stasis.  

Hagesidamos daughter of Charieides.  

[Charieides, son of So-and-so?], after having achieved the telesphoria [of the Karneia/Artamitia], [dedicated] (this monument) as a tithe.
