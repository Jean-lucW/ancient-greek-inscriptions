# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 104400)

## Ancient Greek text

[...μ]αχος ⋮ [...]  
[Ἀπόλλ]ωνι ⋮ δ[εκάταν]

### English translation

[...]machos [son of so-and-so] (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
