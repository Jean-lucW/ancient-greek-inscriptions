# Dedications to the Eumenides, the Curete and Melichios (IGCyr2 064900)

## Ancient Greek text

Λύσιος  
Εὐμενίδων. Κωρὴς. Μηλίχιος.

### English translation

(Altar belonging to) Lysis. (Altar) of the Eumenides. Curete. Melichios.
