# Dedication to the Theoi Apotropaioi (IGCyr2 114900)

## Ancient Greek text

Δαμόφιλος Δαμοφίλω  
Ἀπόλλωνι {δ} Διὶ  
Ἀθηνᾶι  
Ἀποτροπαίοις

### English translation

Damophilos son of Damophilos (dedicated this monument) to Apollo, Zeus and Athena, (gods) Apotropaioi.
