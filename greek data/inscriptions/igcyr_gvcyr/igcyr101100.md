# Dedication to Athena (IGCyr2 101100)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεύδωρος  
Ἡρακλείτω  
[Ἀ]θαν[αί]αι

### English translation

Theudoros son of Herakleitos (dedicated this monument) to Athena.
