# Dedication on occasion of telesphoria (IGCyr2 016500)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] Ἀθανόδωρος Ἀσμένω  
[Καρνῆια? τελεσφ]ορέων ἀνέθηκε .

### English translation

(The statue of) [so-and-so, son of so-and-so] was dedicated by Athanodoros son of Asmenos, while celebrating the [telesph]oria [of Karneia?].
