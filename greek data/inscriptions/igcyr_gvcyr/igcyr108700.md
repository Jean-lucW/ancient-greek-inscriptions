# Dedication by a priest (IGCyr2 108700)

## Ancient Greek text

[Εὐ?]άνθης  
[Εὔ]φριος  
[ἱαρ]ιτεύων

### English translation

Euanthes son of Euphris (dedicated this monument) while being priest.
