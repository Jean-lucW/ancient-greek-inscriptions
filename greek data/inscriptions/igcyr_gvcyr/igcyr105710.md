# Private honours for Ptolemy VIII Euergetes II (IGCyr2 105710)

## Ancient Greek text

[Βασιλέα Πτολεμαῖον θ]εὸν Ε[[ὐ]]ε[ργέτην ... τῶν] π[[ρώ]]τ[ων φίλων ...]

### English translation

(The statue of) [king Ptolemy] God Euergetes [... was dedicated by So-and-so] of the First Friends [...].
