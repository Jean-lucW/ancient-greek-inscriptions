# Dedication of tithe to Apollo. (IGCyr2 137100)

## Ancient Greek text

Σῶσις Βιβίου  
Ἀπόλλωνι  
δεκάταν

### English translation

Sosis son of Bibios (or Bibias?) (dedicated) to Apollo as a tithe.
