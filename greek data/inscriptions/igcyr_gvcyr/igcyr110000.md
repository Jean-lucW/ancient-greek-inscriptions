# Dedication of a fountain (IGCyr2 110000)

## Ancient Greek text

Φι[λοθά]λη[ς] Ἰάσονος ἱαριτεύ[ω ν ἀνέθηκε]

### English translation

Philothales son of Iason [dedicated?] while being priest.
