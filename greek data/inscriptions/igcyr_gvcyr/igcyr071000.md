# Tithe (IGCyr2 071000)

## Ancient Greek text

Εὐγένης [Ἀρίμμα?]ντος δεκάταν  
τῶι Ἀπόλλωνι

### English translation

Eugenes son of [Arimmas?] (dedicated) the tithe to Apollo.
