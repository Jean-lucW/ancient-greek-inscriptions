### English translation

[Gods. Priest of Apollo So-and-so, damiergoi So-and-so, So-and-so, So-and-so.]. The crops were estimated: [barley, so much; wheat, so much]; pulse, [so much; cultivated hay, so much]; wild, [so much; straw, one net], 6 drachmas; grapes: early, under guarantee, [so much]; black 4 drachmas; without guarantee, early, [so much]; black [so much]; raisins, 4 obols; [olives (or oil), so much]. Total income: 380 mines 52 drachmas. [Expense]: [... at least] 251 mines 44 drachmes 2 obols. [Remainder]: [... at least] 7 drachmes 3 obols. Allowance for the damiergoi: 3 mines.
