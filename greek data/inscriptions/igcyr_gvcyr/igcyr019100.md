# Dedication to Apollo and Artemis (IGCyr2 019100)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]λη Πολυκλε[ῦς]  
[...κ]λεια Ποσείδι[ος?]  
[Ἀπόλλ]ωνι, Ἀρτάμιτ[ι]  
[ἀ]νέθηκε.

### English translation

(This statue of) [...]kles son of Polykles was dedicated to Apollo and Artemis by [...]kleia daughter of Poseidis.
