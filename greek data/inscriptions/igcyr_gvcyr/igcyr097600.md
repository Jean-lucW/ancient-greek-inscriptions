# Heading of an official document, possibly a letter (IGCyr2 097600)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἱαρεὺς Α[...]  
ὀγδόαι φθ[ίνοντος ...]  
Ἐπ[ιστολὰ? ...]  
[Ἀ]ρατομε[νης? ...]

### English translation

Priest (So-and-so son of So-and-so), the 8th of the waning month [...].  

Letter [...].  

Aratome[nes ...].
