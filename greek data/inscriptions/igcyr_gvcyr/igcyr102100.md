# Private honours (IGCyr2 102100)

## Ancient Greek text

[Λ]ῦσις  
[Πο]λυβώλω.  
Θεύμανδ[ρος ἐπ]οίησεν

### English translation

Lysis son of Polybolos. Theumandros was the artist.
