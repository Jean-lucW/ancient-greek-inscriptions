# Dedication to Demeter and Kore (IGCyr2 067500)

## Ancient Greek text

[Διο]νύσι[ο ...]  
Δάματ[ρι καὶ? Κόραι? ἀνέθηκε?].

### English translation

Dionysios [...] to Demeter [and to Kore dedicated?].
