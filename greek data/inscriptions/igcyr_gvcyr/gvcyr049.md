# Tithe of Aiglanor for Apollo (GVCyr2 049)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰγλάνōρ μ’ ἀνέθε̄κε  
hὀντιπάτρō δεκάταν.

### English translation

Aiglanor, the son of Antipatros, dedicated me as a tithe.
