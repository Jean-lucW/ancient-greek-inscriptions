# Dedication (IGCyr2 098000)

## Ancient Greek text

Μνάσαρχος Θεοχρήστω  
ἱαριτέυων ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Mnasarchos son of Theοchrestos dedicated (this monument) while being priest.
