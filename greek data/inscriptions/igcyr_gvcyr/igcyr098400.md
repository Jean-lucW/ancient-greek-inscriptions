# Dedication (IGCyr2 098400)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]α Ἀθαναία[ι ...]  
[...καὶ?] τὰ διαφράγ[ματα]  
[... ἱαριτε]ύων ἀνέ[θηκε]

### English translation

[So-and-so] son of [...]as dedicated to Athena [... and] the barrier [...] while being priest.
