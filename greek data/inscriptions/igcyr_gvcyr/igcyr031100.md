# Dedication to Zeus Melichios and to the Eumenides (IGCyr2 031100)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ζ]ε̄νὶ Με̄[λιχίōι],  
[Εὐμε](νίσι)  
[Εὐμ](ενίσι) [Δάμας].

### English translation

To Zeus Melichios and to the Eumenides. To the Eumenides Damas (dedicated?).
