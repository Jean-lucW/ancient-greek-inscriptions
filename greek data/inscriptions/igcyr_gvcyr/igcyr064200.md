# Probably private honours and artist's signature (IGCyr2 064200)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ρητος ἱεριτεύων Ἀπόλλων[ος]  
[...] Ἀριστομένους Ἀθηναῖος ἐποίησε

### English translation

[...] (son of) [...]res, while being priest of Apollo (dedicated this monument).  

Made by [So-and-so], son of Aristomenes, an Athenian.
