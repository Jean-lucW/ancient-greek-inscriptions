# Dedication (IGCyr2 022100)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ς Ἀπόλλωνος, Ἀφροδίτας·  
[... ἱαρ]ιτεύων τὸς βωμὸς ἐπέγραψε.

### English translation

(Altar of?) [...]; (altar of) [...]; (altar )of Apollo; (altar) of Aphrodite.  

[So and so], while priest, had (the mentions) inscribed on the altars.
