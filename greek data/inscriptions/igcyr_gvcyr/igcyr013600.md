# Accounts of the damiergoi (IGCyr2 013600)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Λοιπὸν ...] Ζ >  
[...]. [Πα]ρόρ  
[εγμα δαμιεργοῖς μ(ναῖ)] – – –

### English translation

[... Remainder]: [...] 1 drachma 1 obol. Allowance [for the damiergoi] 3 mines.
