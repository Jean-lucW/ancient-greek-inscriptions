# Dedication to Zeus Soter and other gods (IGCyr2 100000)

## Ancient Greek text

Ζηνὶ Σωτ[ῆρι καὶ θε]οῖς [... παῖ]σιν [Κυρα]ναῖο[ι] ἀ[νέ]θηκαν

### English translation

(a) [...] Cyrene. Apollo. [...] (Such-and-such deity). Libya. [...]  

(b) To Zeus Soter [and] to all the gods [...] the Cyrenaeans dedicated (this monument).
