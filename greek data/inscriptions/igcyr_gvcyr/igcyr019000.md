# Tithe and artist's signature (IGCyr2 019000)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Ε]ὐαγγελίδα  
[Ἀπό]λλω[νι] δεκάταν.  
[...]η[...]ς : ἠργάσσατο.

### English translation

[So and so son] of Euangelidas (dedicated) to Apollo as a tithe. Made by [so and so].
