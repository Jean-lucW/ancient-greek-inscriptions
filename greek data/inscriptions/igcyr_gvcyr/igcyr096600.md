# Dedication, possibly to Hestia (IGCyr2 096600)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰσχύ[λος ...]  
ἱαροθ[υτ... ...]  
Ὶστίαι [ἀνέθηκε?]  
τὸν Ἀπόλ[λωνα?].

### English translation

Aischylos acting as a hiarothytas [dedicated] to Histia (the image of?) Apollo.
