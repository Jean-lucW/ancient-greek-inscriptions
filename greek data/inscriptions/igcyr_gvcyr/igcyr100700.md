# Dedication to Hermes and Heracles (IGCyr2 100700)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]υσανίου  
[γυμνασι]αρχήσας  
[ἱαρὰς? ξ]ύστρας  
[ὀγδ]οήκοντα  
[Ἑρμᾶ]ι Ἡρακλε[ῖ]

### English translation

(So-and-so) son of [...]ysanias, after having been gymnasiarch, (dedicated) [as sacred] eighty strigils to [Hermes] and Heracles.
