# Accounts of the damiergoi (IGCyr2 012300)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] καλλιστῆια τῶ[ι ... ...] 𐅠 𐅠 : ἐξάρχοις [... Ἀ]θαναίας ἱαρέα[ι τροφά? ...] μ(ναῖ) : [...] – – – – Χ 𐅠 𐅠 𐅠 > [...] Χ Χ [...]

### English translation

[... for such and such person], award of excellence [...] 8 drachmas; for the exarchoi [...]; for the priestess of Athena, food [...]; [...] 4 mines 33 drachmas; [...] 40 drachmas [...].
