# Dedication (IGCyr2 127800)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] Ἐλαιίτα ἱ[αριτεύων?]  
[... ἀ]νέθηκ [ε ...]

### English translation

[So and so] (son of) Elaiitas [being priest?] dedicated [...].
