# Dedication (IGCyr2 020900)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐπ’ Εὐμηλίδα·  
ὀρνίχων·  
[σκ]υλακίων.

### English translation

Being (priest) Eumelidas, thanks to the revenues from puppies and birds.
