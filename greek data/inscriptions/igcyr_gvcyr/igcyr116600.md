# Dedication to Demeter (IGCyr2 116600)

## Ancient Greek text

Δάματρι θ[...]  
εὐχὰν Κλευ[...]  
Αἰγλάνο[ρος ἀνέθηκε?]

### English translation

Kleu[...] (daughter or son) of Aiglanor [dedicated?] (this monument) to Demeter Th[...] ex voto.
