# Dedication by generals (IGCyr2 108600)

## Ancient Greek text

[...ό]ξηνος Νικι[...], [...], [...]  
[Αἰγ]λάνορος, Κλευ[...], [...],  
[σ]τραταγέντε[ς ...]

### English translation

[...]oxenos son of Niki[...], [So-and-so son of So-and-so, So-and-so] son of Aiglanor, Kleu[... son of So-and-so, So-and-so son of So-and-so], while being strategoi, [dedicated?] (this monument) [to such and such deity?].
