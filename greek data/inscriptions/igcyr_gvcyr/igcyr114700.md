# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 114700)

## Ancient Greek text

Πραξικράτης  
Ἐπαμείνονος  
Ἀπόλλωνι  
δεκάταν

### English translation

Praxikrates son of Epameinon (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
