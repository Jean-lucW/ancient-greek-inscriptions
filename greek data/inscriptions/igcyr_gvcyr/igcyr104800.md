# Private honours for Theudoros (IGCyr2 104800)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεύδωρον Εὐμηλίδα  
Ἀγλωτίμα Ἀριστίππω  
ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

(Statue of) Theudoros son of Eumelidas dedicated by Aglotima daughter of Aristippos.
