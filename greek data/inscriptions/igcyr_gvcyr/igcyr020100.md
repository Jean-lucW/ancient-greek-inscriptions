# Dedication to the gods of the agora (IGCyr2 020100)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] τ[οῖ]ς θεοῖς τ[οῖς] ἐ[ν] τᾶι ἀ[γορ]ᾶι ἀν[έθηκε].

### English translation

(So-and-so son of So-and-so) dedicated to the gods of the agora.
