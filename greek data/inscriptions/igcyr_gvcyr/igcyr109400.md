# Sacred regulations (?) (IGCyr2 109400)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]μη[... ἄλ]λη δὲ πο[... πρί?]ασθαι βάβα  
[λ...]· [αἰ] δέ κα πωλῆι  
[...]ς ἐξ ἀλλοτρί  
[ων ...]α οἱ τένται ἀλ  
[λ ...]σω ατιμακ  
[...]ωρια.

### English translation

[...] and elsewhere [...] to buy secular objects [...]; and if one sells [...] from someone else's belongings [... if] other (properties) belong him [...] the value/valueless [...].
