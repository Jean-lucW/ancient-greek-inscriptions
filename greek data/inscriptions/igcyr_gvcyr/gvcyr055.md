# Caption for a relief and acclamation (GVCyr2 055)

## Ancient Greek text

καθαρὸν δίκαιον πισστὸν ἀλαθὲς  
πνεῦμα θεοῦ  
σωτῆρος Ἀπόλλωνος

### English translation

Pure, righteous, trustworthy, true, the breath of saviour god Apollo.  

(J.M. Reynolds' unpublished translation)
