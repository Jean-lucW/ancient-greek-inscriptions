# Dedication to the Dioscuri (IGCyr2 110100)

## Ancient Greek text

τοῖς Διοσϙόροις Μ[...]σαρχος ἀνέθεκ[ε ...]

### English translation

[...]sarchos dedicated (this cup) to the Dioscuri.  

[...]sarchos ha dedicato (questa coppa) ai Dioscuri.
