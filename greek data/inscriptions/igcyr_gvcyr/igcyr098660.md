# Tithe (IGCyr2 098660)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρχενομίδα[ς]  
Πολιά[ν]θευς  
δ[ε]κά[τα]ν.

### English translation

Archenomidas son of Polianthes (dedicated this monument) as a tithe.
