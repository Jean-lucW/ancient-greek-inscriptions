# Unclear and composite sacred text with owners' mark (IGCyr2 032900)

## Ancient Greek text

[[Ἡρώω[ν],  
Ζ[η]ν[ό]ς.  
Ε[ὐ]μέ[νιδ]ε[ς]]]  

Ἅδε προβώμιος  
Λύσιος [...]  
τὸ ἄντρον{ι}  
καὶ Ἀμμων[ί]ω  
καὶ Ο[...]  
καὶ Αγ[...]

### English translation

a) [[(Cult area) of the Heroes, of Zeus. Eumenides.]]  

b) [...] before the altar. Belonging to Lysis (is) this cave as well as to Ammonios, to O[...] and to Ag[...].
