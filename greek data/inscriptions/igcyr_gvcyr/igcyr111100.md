# Dedication (IGCyr2 111100)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλητόμαχος  
Πραξιάδα  
ἱαριτεύων  
ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Kletomachos son of Praxiadas dedicated (this monument) while being priest.
