# Dedication to Hermes and Heracles implying a gymnasiarchos (?) (IGCyr2 065300)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀρ]ίμμαν Ἀρι[...]  
[γ]υμνασιαρχ[...]ης μείζονος [...]ηνσυνωστη[...]γηνσαν[...] Ἑρμεῖ Ἡρ[ακλεῑ]

### English translation

Arimmas son of Ari[...] gymnasiarch [...] bigger [...] to Hermes, to Heracles.
