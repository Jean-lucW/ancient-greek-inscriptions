# Dedication to Ammon and artist's signature (IGCyr2 108400)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἵππαρχος Ναύσιος  
Ἄμμωνι ἀνέθηκε  

[...]ίωνος ἔργον

### English translation

Hipparchos son of Nausis dedicated (this monument) to Ammon. Work of [...]ion.
