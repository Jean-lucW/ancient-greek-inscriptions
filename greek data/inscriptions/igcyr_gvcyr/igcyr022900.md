# Probably private honours and artist's signature (IGCyr2 022900)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Φιλο?]κώμω  
[...]ων·  
[... ἐπ]οίησε.

### English translation

[So-and-so son of Philo]komos (erected this monument) while fulfilling (such and such office).  

Made by [So-and-so son of So-and-so].
