# Tithe (IGCyr2 095800)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ὑ]πέρβιος Ἀκεσάνδρω  
Ἀπόλλωνι δεκάταν.

### English translation

Hyperbios son of Akesandros (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
