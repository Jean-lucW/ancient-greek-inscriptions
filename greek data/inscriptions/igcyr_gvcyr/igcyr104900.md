# Private honours for Charitimos (IGCyr2 104900)

## Ancient Greek text

[Χαρ]ίτιμον Κλητο[μάχω]  
[Κλ]ητόμ[α]χος Χαριτ[ίμω]  
[ἀνέ]θη[κε]

### English translation

(Statue of) Charitimos son of Kletomachos dedicated by Kletomachos son of Charitimos.
