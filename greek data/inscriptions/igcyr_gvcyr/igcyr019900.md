# Dedication to the gods of the agora (IGCyr2 019900)

## Ancient Greek text

Πραξιτέλης Ἰσχεμά[χω]  
Νέμεα  
[τ]οῖς θεοῖς τοῖς ἐν τᾶι ἀγορᾶι ἀνέθηκ[ε].

### English translation

Praxiteles son of Ischemachos, (winner at the) Nemean games, dedicated (this monument) to the gods of the agora.
