# Dedication to Dionysos (IGCyr2 113800)

## Ancient Greek text

Κάλλι[ς? ...]  
κατὰ πρό[σταγμα τῶ]  
Διο[νύσω?]

### English translation

Kallis [son of So-and-so] (dedicated this monument) as prescribed by Dio[nysos?].
