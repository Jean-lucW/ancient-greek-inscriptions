# Funerary verse-inscription for Phileso (GVCyr2 059)

## Ancient Greek text

Νεκρὸς ἠμὶ κόπρος  
κόπρος δὲ γῆ· Γῆ  
δ' ἐστὶ θεός· εἴ τι  
θεόν Γῆ, κα[ὶ] θεὸς δ' ἐστὶ  
νεκρός.  

[χαῖρ]ε Φιλησὼ Ἰλ  
αρίωνος 𐅹(ἐτῶν) ζ  
[...]

### English translation

I, a corpse, am dirt. But dirt is earth. But earth is god. If Earth is something divine, then a corpse is god too. (transl. F. Favi).  

Farewell Phileso, daughter of Ilarion, aged seven.
