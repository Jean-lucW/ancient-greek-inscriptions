### English translation

[...], under priest Ka[...]es son of Mnasias. [The crops] were estimated: barley, [so much]; wheat, 3 drachmas; pulse, [so much]; raisins, 1 drachma 2 obols 1/2; figs [so much]; olives [so much]; oil [so much]; [...].
