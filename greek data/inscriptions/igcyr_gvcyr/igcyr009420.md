# Accounts of the damiergoi (IGCyr2 009420)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ς : Ἀ[πόλλωνο]ς [ἱαρι  
τεύοντος] : Ἱαρ[οκλεῦς? ...]·  
[Καρποὶ] ἐτιμ[άθεν : πράταν ἑξά  
μηνον : σ]πυρ[οὶ ἀν : ... : ...]

### English translation

[...], being priest of Apollo Hiaro[kles son of So-and-so]. [The crops] have been estimated. [First semester]: [whe]at, [per unit, so much]; [...].
