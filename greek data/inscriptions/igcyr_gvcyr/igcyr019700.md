# Dedication to the nymph Cyrene (IGCyr2 019700)

## Ancient Greek text

Καλλίμαχος  
Ὀλυμπιοδώρω  
Κυράναι ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Kallimachos son of Olympiodoros dedicated to Cyrene.
