# Tithe for Apollo and private honours (IGCyr2 006700)

## Ancient Greek text

Πολιάνθης : ᾿Ανάξιος  
τὸμ πατέρα Ἄναξιν  
Ζευξιμάχω τῶι : ᾿Απόλλωνι  
δεκάταν : ἀνέθηκε

### English translation

Polianthes, son of Anaxis dedicated as a tithe to Apollo (the statue) of his father Anaxis son of Zeuximachos.
