# Alphabet (IGCyr2 000700)

## Ancient Greek text

α β γ δ ε ϝ ζ η θ ι κ λ [μ ν ο π ϻ ϙ ρ τ υ φ χ]

### English translation

Alpha, beta, gamma, delta, epsilon, digamma, zeta, heta, theta, iota, kappa, lambda, [mu, nu, omicron, pi, san, qoppa, rho, tau, upsilon, phi, chi].
