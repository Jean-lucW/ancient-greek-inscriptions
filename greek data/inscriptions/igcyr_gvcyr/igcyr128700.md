# Dedication or private honours (IGCyr2 128700)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ος  
[...]άτω  
[ἀνέ]θηκ[ε]

### English translation

[...]os son of [...]atos dedicated.  

(or) (The statue of)[So-and-so son of ...] was dedicated by [So-and-so] son of [...]atos [to Apollo].
