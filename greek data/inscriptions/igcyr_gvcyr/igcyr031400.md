# Dedication (IGCyr2 031400)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστ[...]  
τῶ Ζεύ[ξ]ιος  

Εὐμενί(δων)  

Ζηνός

### English translation

Belonging to Arist[...] son of Zeuxis.  

(Altar) of the Eumenides.  

(Altar) of Zeus
