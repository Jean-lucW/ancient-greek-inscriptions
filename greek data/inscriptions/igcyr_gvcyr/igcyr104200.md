# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 104200)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]άνθης Κλευδάμω  
[Ἀπ]όλλωνι δεκάταν.

### English translation

[...]anthes son of Kleudamos (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
