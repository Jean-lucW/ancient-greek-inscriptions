# Private honours with artist's signature (IGCyr2 065800)

## Ancient Greek text

[Θεοκ]ρίσιος Ἀριστίππω Θεοκρίσιος Παραιβά[τα].  
[...]άτης Μιλήσιος ἐποίησε. [[[...]]]

### English translation

Theokrisios son of Aristippos, Theokrisios son of Paraibatas.  

Made by [...]ates of Miletus [[[...]]].
