# Dedication of the Propylaeum (IGCyr2 098200)

## Ancient Greek text

Πραξιάδας Εὐ[κλείδα]  
ἱαριτεύων ἀν[έθηκε].

### English translation

Praxiadas son of Eukleidas dedicated (this monument) while being priest.
