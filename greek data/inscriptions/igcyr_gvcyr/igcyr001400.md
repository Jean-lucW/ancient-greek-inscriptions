# Dedication to Zeus Eri(nymenos?) and Hermes Dolios (IGCyr2 001400)

## Ancient Greek text

το͂ Ζε̄(νός)  
το͂ Ἐρι(νυμένō?)  

το͂ hερμᾶ  
το͂ Δολίō

### English translation

a: (Altar) of Zeus Eri(nymenos?).  

b: (Altar) of Hermes Dolios.
