# Epitaph (IGCyr2 007000)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀλκάνορος ἁ ὀπὰ ἰδία  
καὶ τάφο[ς καὶ] σορός

### English translation

This is Alkanor's own recess, his burial place and his casket.
