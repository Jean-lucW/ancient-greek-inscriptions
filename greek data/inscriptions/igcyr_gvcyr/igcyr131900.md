# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 131900)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Δαμ]ασάνο[ρος]  
[υἱ]ὸς τῶ[ι Ἀπόλλωνι] δεκάταν  
Τέλω[να Δαμ]ασάνορο[ς]

### English translation

[So-and-so] son of Damasanor (dedicated to Apollo) as a tithe (the statue of) Telon son of Damasanor.
