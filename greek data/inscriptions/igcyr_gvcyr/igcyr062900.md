# Private honours for Ptolemy II Philadelphus by Ariston (IGCyr2 062900)

## Ancient Greek text

[Βασιλῆ Πτο]λε[μ]αῖον Σωτήρων  
[Ἀρ]ίστων Λυσιφάνευς  
ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

(The statue of) [king] Ptolemy son of the Soteres was dedicated by Ariston son of Lysiphanes.
