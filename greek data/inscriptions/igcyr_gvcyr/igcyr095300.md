# Dedication to Apollo (IGCyr2 095300)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ...φ]άνευς  
[ἱαριτεύ]ων Ἀπόλλωνι  
[ἀν]έθηκε.

### English translation

(So-and-so son of) [...]phanes dedicated (this monument) to Apollo while being priest.
