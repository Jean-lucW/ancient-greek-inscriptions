# Sign with sacred regulation (IGCyr2 100300)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἱαρὸν Ἑκάτ[ας]·  
οὐ ποτιφέ[ρεται]  
λίβανo[ς] .

### English translation

Sacred area of Hekate. Incense is not brought in.
