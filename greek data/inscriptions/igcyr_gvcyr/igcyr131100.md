# Private honours (IGCyr2 131100)

## Ancient Greek text

[Δαμάτ]ριον Κ[...]ος Δα[...]λάνορος [τὸν]  
[ἀδ]ελφ[ὸν]  
[ἀ]νέθ[ηκε]

### English translation

(The statue of) Damatrios son of K[...], his brother, was dedicated by Da[...] son of [...]lanor.
