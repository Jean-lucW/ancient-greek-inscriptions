# Epitaph of Archo (GVCyr2 004)

## Ancient Greek text

Mνῆμα ἀρετῆς τόδε ἔθηκε  
Φίλων Ἀρχοῖ συνομεύνωι  
[...]αι [...]ωι.

### English translation

Philon laid down this (monument) for his wife Archo as a record of her virtue [...].
