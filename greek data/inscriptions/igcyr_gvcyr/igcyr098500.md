# Dedication of the Strategeion (IGCyr2 098500)

## Ancient Greek text

στραταγοὶ  

Ἀριστοφάνης  
Παραιβάτα  

Ἄριστις  
Βακαλος  

Αὐτόβιος  
Ἁνιόχω  

[...]  

[...]  

τῶι Ἀπόλλωνι δεκάταν ἀπὸ τῶν πολεμίων ἀνέθηκαν

### English translation

The strategoi Aristophanes son of Paraibatas, Aristis son of Bakal, Autobios son of Haniochos,[So-and-so son of So-and-so] and [So-and-so son of So-and-so] dedicated (this momument) to Apollo as a tithe taken from the ennemies.
