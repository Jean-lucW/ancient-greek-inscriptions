# Dedication by a king Ptolemy probably to Zeus Soter (IGCyr2 064500)

## Ancient Greek text

[Βασιλεὺς Πτολε]μαῖος  
[Σωτὴρ Ζη?]νὶ Σ[ω]τῆρι  
[τὸν?] οἶκ[ον]

### English translation

[King] Ptolemy [Soter?] (dedicated) to [Zeus?] Soter [this?] building.
