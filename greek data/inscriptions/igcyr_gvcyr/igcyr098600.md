# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 098600)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀγ]άθιππος Αὐτοκλεῦς  
[δεκ]άταν τῶι Ἀπόλ[λ]ωνι

### English translation

Agathippos son of Autokles (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
