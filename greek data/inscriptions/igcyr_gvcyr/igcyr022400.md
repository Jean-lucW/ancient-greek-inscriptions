# Dedication of a portal (IGCyr2 022400)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  
[...]  
[...]α  
[...]μω  

Στᾶσις Δάμιος,  
Φίλων Αννικεριος,  
Νικόδαμος Δαμώνακτος,  

[στραταγέντες? τὰ]ν ὀπὰν ἀνέθηκαν.

### English translation

[... three names with fathers' names],  

[So-and-so son of So-and-so], [So-and-so] son of [...]as, [So-and-so] son of [...]mos,  

Stasis, son of Damis, Philon son of Annikeris, Nikodamos son of Damonax,  

[...] dedicated the portal [while being strategoi?].
