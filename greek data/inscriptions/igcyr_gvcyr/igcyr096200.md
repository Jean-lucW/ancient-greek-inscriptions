# Dedication to the Muses and the Nymphs (IGCyr2 096200)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰσχίνης Σωσάρχου, Καλλίκλεια Ξούθου,  
Βασὼ Αἰσχίνου Μούσαις καὶ Νύμφαις  
ἀνέθηκαν.

### English translation

Aischines son of Sosarchos, Kallikleia daughter of Xouthos and Baso daughter of Aischines dedicated (this monument) to the Muses and to the Nymphs.
