# Private honours for Ptolemy VIII Euergetes II (IGCyr2 063700)

## Ancient Greek text

Ὑπὲρ βασιλ[έως Πτολεμαίου]  
θ[εο]ῦ Εὐεργ[έτου τὴν ...]  
καὶ τὰ συ[νκύροντα καὶ τὰ ἐν]  
αὐτῆι π[άντα ...]  
.

### English translation

For the prosperity of king Ptolemy, God Euergetes, the [...] and its annexes and all that it contains [were dedicated by ...].
