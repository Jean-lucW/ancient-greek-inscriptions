# Dedication to Apollo of the 'bench of Elaiitas' (IGCyr2 080500)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐ[λα]ιίτας [Θευδώρ?]ω τῶι [Ἀπόλλω]νι ἱαρ[ι]τεύων ἀ[ν]έθηκ[ε].

### English translation

Elaiitas son of [Theudor?]os being priest dedicated to Apollo.
