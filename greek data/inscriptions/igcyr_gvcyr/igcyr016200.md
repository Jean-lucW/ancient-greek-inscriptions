# Honorary inscription for Kallimachos on occasion of the telesphoria (IGCyr2 016200)

## Ancient Greek text

[Καλ]λίμαχον Λυ[σιμ]ά[χω]  
[Λ]υσίμαχος Λύσι[ος]  
[Ἀ]ρταμίτια τελεσφορήσ[ας]  
ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

(The statue of) Kallimachos son of Lysimachos was dedicated by Lysimachos son of Lysis, after having accomplished the telesphoria of Artemis' feasts.
