# Pederastic acclamation (IGCyr2 004310)

## Ancient Greek text

Σίταρχ{σ}ος κύσθος, ψωλά  

αρ  
α  

αρ

### English translation

a) Sitarchos, a vagina, a penis.  

b) and c) not usefully translatable.
