# Honours for the priestess Aristokleia (IGCyr2 019300)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστόκλε[ιαν ...]  
ἱαριτε[ύοισαν]  
Γαλέστας [...]  
ἀνέ[θηκε].

### English translation

(The statue of)Aristokleia [daughter of So and so], who was priestess, was dedicated by Galestas [son of So and so].
