# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 095500)

## Ancient Greek text

Μεγαλοκλῆς ⋮ Ὑψοκλεῦς Ὑψοκλῆς  
Μεγαλοκλεῦς ⋮᾿ Ἀπόλλωνι δεκάταν.

### English translation

Megalokles son of Hypsokles and Hypsokles son of Megalokles (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
