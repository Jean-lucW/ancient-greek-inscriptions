# Honorary inscription for Aristoteles on occasion of the telesphoria (IGCyr2 016100)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστοτέλη Ἀριστ[οτέλους?]  
Καρνεῖα τελεσφορήσα[ντα]  
[...]α Ἀρχέτω.

### English translation

(The statue of) Aristoteles son of Arist[oteles?], after his accomplishing the telesphoria of Karneia, (was dedicated by) [So-and-so] daughter of Archetos.
