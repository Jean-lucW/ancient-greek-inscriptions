# Dedication to Demeter (IGCyr2 067400)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστοπάτρα Θαλίννω  
τᾶι Δάματρι ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Aristopatra daughter of Thalinnos dedicated to Demeter.
