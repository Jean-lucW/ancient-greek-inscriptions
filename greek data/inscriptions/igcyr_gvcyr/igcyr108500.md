# Dedication on occasion of a telesphoria (IGCyr2 108500)

## Ancient Greek text

[Τε]λεσ[φορέ]ντες τῶι Ἀ[...νι]  

[...]  

Ἰάσων  
Χαριτίμω  

Πρατομ[ήδης]  
Λυσ[...]  
[...]

### English translation

During their telesphoria (this monument was dedicated) to A[...]n by [So-and-so son of So-and-so], Iason son of Charitimos, Pratomedes son of Lys[...], [So-and-so son of So-and-so].
