# Epitaph (IGCyr2 086400)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστοτέλης  
Σώσιος ἱαρεὺς  
Ἀπόλλωνος· μηθέ  
να ἐντίθη .

### English translation

Aristoteles son of Sosis, priest of Apollo. Don't bury anyone else here.
