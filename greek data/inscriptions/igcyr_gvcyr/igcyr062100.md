### English translation

[To Zeus Lykaios the? ...] dug and set upon [...] and the [neopoiai?] made the [...]. [Under] Thrason, Damon son of Epigenes ⟨son of?⟩ Stratomachos, Iason son of Anchistratos, Kall[ippos?] son of Lagoras, [...].
