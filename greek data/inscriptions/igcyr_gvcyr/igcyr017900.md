# Dedication to Apollo by Philon (IGCyr2 017900)

## Ancient Greek text

Φ[ί]λων Αννικεριο[ς]  
τὸ[ν β]ωμὸν ἀνέθηκε τὸν λύγδ[ινο]ν

### English translation

Philon son of Annikeris dedicated the marble altar.
