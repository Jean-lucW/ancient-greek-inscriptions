# Honours or epitaph (?) (GVCyr2 003)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀριστι[...]  

μναμῆιον τα [...]  
πάσαις νικη [...]  
ἦμεν καὶ τόδ[ε ...]  
σαμῆιον εὐτυ[χίας ...]

### English translation

Aristi[...].  

Memorial of [...] victory/having winned in all the [...] that this be also [...] sign of his success [...].
