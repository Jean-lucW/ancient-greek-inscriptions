# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 098900)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]μος Αὐτοβώλω  
[Ἀπόλλ]ωνι δεκάταν

### English translation

[...]mos son of Autobolos (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
