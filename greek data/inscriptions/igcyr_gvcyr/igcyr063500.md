# Honours for a Ptolemaic officer (IGCyr2 063500)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ἀρ]ετᾶς ἕνεκα  
[καὶ εὐνοίας τᾶς ἐς βασιλῆ Π]τολεμαῖον  
[καὶ βασίλισσαν Κλευπάτραν ...]  
.

### English translation

(The statue of) [...] (was dedicated by) [...] on account of his valour [and his goodwilling towards king] Ptolemy [and queen Cleopatra ...].
