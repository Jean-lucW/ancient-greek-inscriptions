# Private honours and tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 099000)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ⋮] Πολυάρχω  
[Ἀπόλλ]ωνι ⋮ δεκάτ[αν]  
[...]ον ⋮ Χαίνυος

### English translation

(So-and-so) son of Polyarchos (dedicated) to Apollo as a tithe (the statue of) [...]os son of Chainys.
