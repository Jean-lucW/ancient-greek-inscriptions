# Private honours (IGCyr2 016300)

## Ancient Greek text

Φαίνιππος  
Ἀκέστα  

[...]  

Ἀκευσιλέα Ἀκε[... ἀνέθηκε].

### English translation

Phainippos son of Akestas.  

[So-and-so son of So-and-so].  

Akeusilea daughter of Ake[... dedicated] (the statues).
