# Dedication (to Artemis?) (IGCyr2 020400)

## Ancient Greek text

Κριτολᾶ Ἀλέξιος συ[ν]ιαριτεύοισα Ἀλεξάνδρωι

### English translation

Kritola daughter of Alexis, while being priestess with Alexandros (dedicated).
