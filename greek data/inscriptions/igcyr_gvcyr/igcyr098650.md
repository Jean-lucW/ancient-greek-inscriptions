# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 098650)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀπόλλωνι δεκάταν  
Ἀντίπατρος Ἀρτέμονο[ς]

### English translation

Antipatros son of Artemon (dedicated this monument) to Apollo as a tithe.
