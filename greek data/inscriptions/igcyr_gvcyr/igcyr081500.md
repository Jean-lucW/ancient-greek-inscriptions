# Epitaph of Hermaios son of Agonis (IGCyr2 081500)

## Ancient Greek text

Ηερμαίō τō Ἀγό̄νιιο[ς]  
τόδε σαμ{ει}ε͂ιον ἁλιῆος  
Χαίρōν

### English translation

This is the tomb of Hermaios son of Agonis, a fischerman, (built by) Chairon.
