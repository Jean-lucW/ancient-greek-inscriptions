[Ἐφ᾿ ἱαρεῦς ...]ίω

[... ...ω]νος

[... ...ω]νος  
[...]λω  
[...]κλεῦς

### English translation

[Under priest So-and-so] son of [...]ios, [So-and-so] son of [...]chos, [So-and-so] son of [...]on, [So-and-so] son of [...]doros, [So-and-so] son of [...]on, [So-and-so] son of [...]los, [So-and-so] son of [...]kles, [So-and-so] son of Chairadas, [So-and-so] son of Agatharchos, [So-and-so] son of Ephaistion (dedicated the image) [of Apollo] Nomios.  

[...] the very beautiful statue [...] the guardians of the laws.
