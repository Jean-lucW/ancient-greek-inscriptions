# Tithe for Apollo (IGCyr2 104500)

## Ancient Greek text

Κυδιλέα ⋮ Θευτίμω  
ἁ τῶ Παιᾶνος ⋮ Ἀπόλ  
λωνι δεκάταν ⋮ ἀν  
έθηκε

### English translation

Kydilea daughter of Theutimos wife of Paean dedicated to Apollo as a tithe.
