# Dedication of an exedra as a tithe (IGCyr2 008900)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ης Ἱππoκλεῦς  
Ἀγασικλῆς Φιλοκλεῦς  
Ἀπόλλωνι δεκάταν  

Τιμα[...]  
Ἀγαμ[...]

### English translation

A. [So-and-so] son of Hippokles and Agasikles son of Philokles dedicated to Apollo as a tithe.  

B. Tim[...] son of Aga[...].
