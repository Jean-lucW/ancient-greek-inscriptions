# Honorary inscription for Cleopatra III and another member of the royal family (IGCyr2 015400)

## Ancient Greek text

Βασίλισσαν Κλευπάτραν  
Θεὰν Εὐεργέτιν  

[...]  

Κυρα[ναῖοι]

### English translation

(The statues) of queen Cleopatra, Goddess Evergetis and of [...] (were dedicated by) the Cyrenaeans.
