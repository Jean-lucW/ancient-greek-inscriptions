# Dedication to all gods and honours for a Cyrenaean citizen (IGCyr2 033900)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]νους Κυρηναῖον,  
[συγγενῆ καὶ σ]τρατηγὸν τῶν κατὰ  
[Λιβύην τόπω]ν Ἵππις Ἵππιος τῶν  
[... θε]οῖς [π]ᾶσιν ἀνέθηκεν.

### English translation

(The statue of) [so and so, son of ...]nes, a Cyrenaean, [Akin] (to the king), general of the [places throughout Libya], was dedicated to all the gods by Hippis son of Hippis, a member of the [...].
