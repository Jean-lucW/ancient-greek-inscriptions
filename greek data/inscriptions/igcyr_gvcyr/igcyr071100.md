# Private honours and dedication to Apollo (IGCyr2 071100)

## Ancient Greek text

Χαρμί[ππα ...]  
τ[ὸν ἑαυ]τᾶς ἄνδρα  
Ἴα[σι]ν Λυσανία Ἀπόλλωνι

### English translation

Charmi[ppa] daughter of [...] (dedicated the statue of) her husband Iasis son of Lysanias to Apollo.
