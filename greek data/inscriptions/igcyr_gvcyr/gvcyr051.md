# Funerary invocation (GVCyr2 051)

## Ancient Greek text

Τείμιέ μοι ❧  
χαίροις ❧ καὶ  
❧ εἰν Ἀΐδαο ❧  
δόμοισιν.

### English translation

Timios, dear to me, might you be happy even in Hades' houses!
