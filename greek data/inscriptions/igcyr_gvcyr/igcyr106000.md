# Private honours (IGCyr2 106000)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀφεν[οκλῆ]  
Ἀστυ[κράτευς]  
Ἀφενο[κλῆς...]

### English translation

(The statue of) Aphenokles son of Astykrates (was dedicated) by Aphenokles [son of ...].
