# Epitaph (IGCyr2 008100)

## Ancient Greek text

Βαρκαῖος  
Ἀστυκλεῦς  
Μνάστα  
Κρίνιος

### English translation

Barkaios son of Astykles. Mnasta daughter of Krinis.
