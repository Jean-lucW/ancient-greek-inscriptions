# Dedication (IGCyr2 077700)

## Ancient Greek text

Πατροφίλα Βακαλος  
εὐξαμένα ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Patrophila daughter of Bakal, having made a vow, dedicated.  

Patrophila figlia di Bakal, a séguito di un voto, ha dedicato.
