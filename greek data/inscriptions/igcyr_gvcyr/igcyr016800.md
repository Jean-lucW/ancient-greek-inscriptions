### English translation

[...] the priest [...], except in Apollo's sanctuary during the fixed holidays in honour of the god and whenever the civic body meets ⟨under⟩ a portico or near Lysis' (statue) or whenever assembled magistrates are eating in the prytaneion. Neither the city, nor an officer on duty, nor a private person, should provide a vase to anyone, nor take any outside nor receive any. Be it forbidden to anyone to use one by whatever trick, deceit or pretext. (10) If anyone should act against those rules, he will be guilty of sacrilege and should endure the worst calamities that hit impious men.  

There are [12] drinking vases: amongst which one, weighing [...] drachmas; (15) another, of [...] drachmas; another, of [...] drachmas; another, of [...] drachmas; another, of [...] drachmas; another, of [...] drachmas; (20) another, of [... drachmas]; another of [... drachmas];; another, of [...] drachmas; another, of [... drachmas]; another, of [... drachmas]; (25) another of [... drachmas].
