# Dedication (IGCyr2 097700)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἁγεμόνα Φιλοθάλευς ἱαριτεύοισα ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Hagemona daughter of Philothales, while being priestess dedicated (this monument).
