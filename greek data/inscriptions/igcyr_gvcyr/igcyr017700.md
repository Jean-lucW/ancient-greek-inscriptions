# Dedication of a tithe to Apollo (IGCyr2 017700)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ψ]άφων  
Ἐρμοκρέοντος  
δεκάταν ἀνέθηκε.

### English translation

Psaphon son of Hermokreon dedicated as a tithe.
