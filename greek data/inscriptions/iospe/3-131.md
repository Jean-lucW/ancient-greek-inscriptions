# Building inscription relating to fish market (IOSPE 3.131)

## Ancient Greek text

[ἀγ]αθῆι τύχηι  
[...]νης Διογένους  
[ἀγορα]νομήσας ἐκ τῶν  
[ἰδίων τ]ὴν ὀψόπολιν  
[ἐπιμελη?]τεύοντος Διο  
[... Φ]ιλαδέλφου

### English translation

[With go]od fortune. [- - -], (son of) Diogenes, having served as agoranomos, (built), from [personal funds], a fishmarket. Overseer of works was Dio[- - -], (son of) Philadelphos.
