# Epitaph of Apollonia (IOSPE 3.282)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀπολλωνία  
Ἡροδότου,  
Διονυσίου  
γυνά

### English translation

Apollonia, (daughter of) Herodotos, wife of Dionysios.
