# Dedication of Delios (IOSPE 3.111)

## Ancient Greek text

Δήλιος Ἀπολλᾶ  
Παρθένωι  
καθ’ ἐνύπνιον

### English translation

Delios, (son of) Apollas, (dedicated) to Parthenos, in accordance with a dream.
