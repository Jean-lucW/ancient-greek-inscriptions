# Epitaph of Timoxena (IOSPE 3.287)

## Ancient Greek text

Τιμ[ο]ξένα  
Ἀπολλοφάνειος,  
Ἀπολλωνίου  
γυνά.

### English translation

Timoxena, (daughter of) Apollophanes, wife of Apollonios.
