# Epitaph of Kalleas and Hedytea (IOSPE 3.364)

## Ancient Greek text

Καλλέας Γαίου καὶ ἡ γ[υνὴ αὐτοῦ]  
Ἡδυτέα Δημητρίου θυγά[τηρ]· [χαίρετε].

### English translation

Kalleas, (son of) Gaius, and his wife Hedytea, daughter of Demetrios, greeting.
