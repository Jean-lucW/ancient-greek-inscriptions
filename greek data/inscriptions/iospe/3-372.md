# Epitaph of Soterichos and Koncha (IOSPE 3.372)

## Ancient Greek text

Σωτήριχος θεᾶς Παρθ  
ένου ζήσας ἔτη π καὶ  
ἡ σύμβιος αὐτοῦ Κόνχα  
ζήσασα ἔτη

### English translation

Soterichos (priest?) of Goddess Parthenos, having lived 80 years, and his wife Koncha, having lived ... years.
