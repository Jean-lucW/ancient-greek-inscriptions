# Epitaph of Gazourios (IOSPE 3.336)

## Ancient Greek text

Γαζούριος Μητροδώρου πρωταρ  
χοντεύων ἐτῶν ν,  
χαῖρε.

### English translation

Gazourios, (son of) Metrodoros, first archon for 15 years, greeting.
