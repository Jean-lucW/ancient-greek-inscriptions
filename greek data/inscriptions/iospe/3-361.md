# Epitaph of Hermes (IOSPE 3.361)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἑρμῆς Ἀρίσ[τω]  
νος ζήσας  
ἔτη με

### English translation

Hermes, (son of) Ariston, having lived 35 years.
