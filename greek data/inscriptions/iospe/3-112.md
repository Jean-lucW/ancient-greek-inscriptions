# Dedication to Egyptian gods (IOSPE 3.112)

## Ancient Greek text

Χάρμιππος Πρυτάνιος  
κατ’ ἐπίταγμα  
Σαράπι , Ἴσι , Ἀνούβι.

### English translation

Charmippos, (son of) Prytanis, (dedicated), in accordance with divine command, to Sarapis, Isis, Anubis.
