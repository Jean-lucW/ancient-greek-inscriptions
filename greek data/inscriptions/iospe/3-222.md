# Epitaph of Aristo (IOSPE 3.222)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρίστω θυγάτηρ  
Ἁγνοκρίτου, γὺνα  
Ἡροφάντου

### English translation

Aristo, daughter of Agnokritos, wife of Herophantos.
