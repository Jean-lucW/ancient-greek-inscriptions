# Epitaph of Bassianos (IOSPE 3.359)

## Ancient Greek text

Βασσιανός Ἀθ  
ηναίου ζήσας  
ἔτη ΙϚ, χἓρε, (παῖ?).

### English translation

Bassianos, (son of) Athenaios, having lived 16 years, greeting you, [son?].
