# Epitaph of Damatria (IOSPE 3.302)

## Ancient Greek text

Δαματρία  
Τίμωνος,  
Ἀντιπάτρου  
γυνά.

### English translation

Damatria, (daughter of) Timon, wife of Antipatros.
