# Dedication to Egyptian gods (IOSPE 1.17)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ς Κρατίνου Σαράπιδι, ῎Ισιδι,  
[...] θεοῖς συν⟨ν⟩άοις χαριστήριον.

### English translation

[- - -], son of Kratinos, to Sarapis, Isis [- - -], the gods sharing a temple, a thanks offering.
