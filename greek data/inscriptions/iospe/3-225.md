# Epitaph of Archeso (IOSPE 3.225)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρχεσώ  
Λυσάνδρου,  
Εὐθύνου  
γυνά.

### English translation

Archeso, (daughter of) Lysandros, wife of Euthynos.
