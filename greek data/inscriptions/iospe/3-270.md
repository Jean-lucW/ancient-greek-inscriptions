# Epitaph of Marko (IOSPE 3.270)

## Ancient Greek text

Μαρκὼ  
Ἀριστοκλέος,  
Ἀπολλᾶ γυνά

### English translation

Marko, (daughter of) Aristokles, wife of Apollas.
