# Epitaph of Philokrates (IOSPE 3.374)

## Ancient Greek text

Φιλοκράτης  
Φαρνακίωνος  
ἐτῶν οε χε͂ρε

### English translation

Philokrates, (son of) Pharnakion, 75 years old, greeting.
