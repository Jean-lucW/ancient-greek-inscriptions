# Dedication to Zeus Atabyrios (IOSPE 3.594)

## Ancient Greek text

Διὶ Ἀταβυρίωι Ποσίδεος Ποσιδέου  
χαριστήριον.

### English translation

Posideos, (son of) Posideos, (dedicated) to Zeus Atabyrios, a thanks offering.
