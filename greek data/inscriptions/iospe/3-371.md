# Epitaph of Syrion and Sopolis (IOSPE 3.371)

## Ancient Greek text

Συρίων Συρί  
ωνος, χαῖρε.  

Σώπολις  
Συρίωνος, χαῖρε.

### English translation

Syrion, (son of) Syrion, greeting.  

Sopolis, (son of) Syrion, greeting.
