# Epitaph of Geminios (IOSPE 3.411)

## Ancient Greek text

Γεμίνιος Γεμινίου Οὐάλεντος  
υἱός ἐτῶν γ

### English translation

Geminios, son of Geminios Valens, 3 years old.
