# Family epitaph (IOSPE 3.365)

## Ancient Greek text

Κιλία Ἀπολλωνίου,  
γυνὴ δὲ Ἀπελλᾶ.  

Κυράθων Ἀπελλ[ᾶ].  
Ἀπελλᾶ Κυράθω[νος],  
χαῖρε.

### English translation

a. Kilia, (daughter of) Apollonios, wife of Apellas.  

b. Kyrathon, (son of) Apellas. Apellas, (son of) Kyrathon, greeting.
