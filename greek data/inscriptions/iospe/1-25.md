# Building inscription (IOSPE 1.25)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ὑπ]ὲρ τῆς τ[οῦ αὐτοκράτορος ...] Ἀμαστ[ρ ...]  
Τυραν[... πλ]ῆθος ἱκ[ανόν ...]

### English translation

For the [well-being or health vel sim of the emperor - - -] Amast[ris - - - the city] of Tyran[oi - - -] su[fficient qua]ntity [- - -]
