# Epitaph of Severas (IOSPE 3.368)

## Ancient Greek text

Σεβηρᾶς Λίλλω  
νος ὁ καὶ Λίλλις, ζή  
{ση}σας ἔτη τριάκοντα · ζ ·

### English translation

Severas, son of Lillon, also known as Lillis, having lived 37 years.
