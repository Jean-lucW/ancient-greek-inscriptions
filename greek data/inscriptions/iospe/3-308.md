# Epitaph of Nana (IOSPE 3.308)

## Ancient Greek text

Νάνα Δα[μοκ]  
λεῖος Φιλ[ίπ]  
που γυν[ά].

### English translation

Nana, (daughter of) Damokles, wife of Philippos.
