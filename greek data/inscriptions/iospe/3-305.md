# Epitaph of Kleo (IOSPE 3.305)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλεώ  
[Ν]ικαγόρα,  
[Νι]κασιτίμου  
γυνά

### English translation

Kleo, (daughter of) Nikagoras, wife of Nikasitimos.
