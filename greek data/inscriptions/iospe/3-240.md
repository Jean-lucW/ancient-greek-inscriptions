# Epitaph of Polykasta (IOSPE 3.240)

## Ancient Greek text

Πολυκάστα  
Ἱπποκράτειος,  
Δελφοῦ γυνά

### English translation

Polykasta, (daughter of) Hippokrates, wife of Delphos.
