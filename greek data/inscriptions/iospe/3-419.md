# Epitaph of Pontikos (IOSPE 3.419)

## Ancient Greek text

Ποντικὸς  
Νεμερί  
ου Ἡρα  
κλεώτ  
ης, ἐ  
τῶν ξ

### English translation

Pontikos, (son of) Nemerios, citizen of Herakleia, 60 years old.
