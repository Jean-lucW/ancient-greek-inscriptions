# Epitaph of a couple (IOSPE 3.249)

## Ancient Greek text

Εὐξίθεος  
Χαιρομένεος  

Ἱερὼ Κρονίου  
Εὐξιθέου γυνά

### English translation

Euxitheos, (son of) Chairomenes.  

Hiero, (daughter of) Kronios, wife of Euxitheos.
