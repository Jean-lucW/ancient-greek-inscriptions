# Dedication of Phormion (IOSPE 3.116)

## Ancient Greek text

Φορμίων Ἥριος [ἱερεὺς] ἐὼγ καὶ βα[σι]  
[λεύων ... ἀν]έθη[κεν]

### English translation

Phormion, (son of) Herios, serving as priest and king, [---] dedicated.
