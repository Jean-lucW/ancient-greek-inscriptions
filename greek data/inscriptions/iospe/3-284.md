# Epitaph of Soteris (IOSPE 3.284)

## Ancient Greek text

Σωτηρὶς  
Ἀ⟨λ⟩κίμου  
γυνά,  
Ἱέρωνος  
θυγάτηρ

### English translation

Soteris, wife of Alkimos, daughter of Hieron.
