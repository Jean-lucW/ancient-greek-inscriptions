# Honorific inscription for anonymous (IOSPE 1.30)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

[ὑπὸ Τυρανῶν]  

[ὑπὸ ...]  

ὑπὸ Ὀλβιοπολιτῶν  

ὑ[πὸ ...]  

ὑπὸ Κυζικηνῶν  

ὑπὸ Ῥοδίων

### English translation

[- - - crowned with golden crowns by citizens of Tyras?, by citizens of - - -], by citizens of Olbia, [by citizens of - - -], by citizens of Cyzicus, by citizens of Rhodes.
