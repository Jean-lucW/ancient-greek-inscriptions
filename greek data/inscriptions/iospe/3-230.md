# Epitaph of Damatria (IOSPE 3.230)

## Ancient Greek text

Δαματρία  
Θεοφάνειος,  
Ἡρακλείου  
γυνά

### English translation

Damatria, (daughter of) Pheophanes, wife of Herakleios.
