# Dedication of altar to Nemesis (IOSPE 3.113)

## Ancient Greek text

ἀγαθῇ τύ  
χῃ  
θεᾷ Νεμέ  
σει Βασιλε  
ίδης Καλοῦ

### English translation

Good fortune. Basilides, (son of Kalous), (dedicated) to Goddess Nemesis.
