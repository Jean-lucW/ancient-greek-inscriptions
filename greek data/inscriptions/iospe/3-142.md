# Building inscription of Chrysous (IOSPE 3.142)

## Ancient Greek text

Χρυσοῦς Φαρ  
νάκου φιλοτει  
μησάμενος  
εἰς τοὺς κείο  
νας δηνάρια φ

### English translation

Chrysous, (son of) Pharnakes, (dedicated), having donated 500 denarii for the columns.
