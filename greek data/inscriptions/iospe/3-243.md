# Epitaph of Eirana (IOSPE 3.243)

## Ancient Greek text

Εἰράνα  
Ἡρονείκο[υ],  
Διονυσίο[υ]  
γυνά.

### English translation

Eirana, (daughter of) Heroneikos, wife of Dionysios.
