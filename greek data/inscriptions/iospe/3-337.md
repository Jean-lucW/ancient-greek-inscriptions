# Epitaph of Gazourios and Daiskos (IOSPE 3.337)

## Ancient Greek text

Γαζούριος Μητροδώρου  
ἐτῶν λα, χαῖρε.  

Δαίσκος Μητροδώρου  
ἐτῶν κε χαῖρε.

### English translation

Gazourios, (son of) Metrodoros, 31 years old, greeting.  

Daiskos, (son of Metrodoros), 25 years old, greeting.
