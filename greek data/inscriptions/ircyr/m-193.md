# Dedication to Asklepios (IRCyr2020 M.193)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀϲκληπ]ιῶι · Βαλαγρείτηι · Ἰητρῶι ·  
[... Λο]ύκουλλοϲ · ἐπίτροποϲ · τοῦ Ϲεβαϲτοῦ  
[...]ν ὑγείαϲ · καὶ εἰϲ ὑπόμνηϲιν · τῆϲ πρὸϲ  
[ἑαυτὸν?] φιλίαϲ · δράκοντα · ἀργύρεον ❦

### English translation

[To Asklep]ios of Balagrae, Doctor, [?] Lucullus, procurator of the Emperor, [?in thanks for] health and as a memorial of friendship [towards himself?] (dedicated) a silver serpent.  

[To Asklep]ios of Balagrae, Iatros, [Lo]ukoullos, procurator of the Emperor, [?in thanks for] health and in memory of kindness [towards himself?] (dedicated) a silver serpent.
