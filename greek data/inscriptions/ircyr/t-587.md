# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.587)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Ἁθὺρ  
[ι Ἀ]λύπα  
[τ]ος Βαχ  
[ί]δος (ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 7, Hathyr 10, Alypatos (son) of Bachis, aged 20.
