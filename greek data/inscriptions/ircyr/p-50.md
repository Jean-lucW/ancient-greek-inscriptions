# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.50)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) γ  
Κόϲμοϲ  
δημοϲιω  
νικὸϲ (ἐτῶν) κ  
β

### English translation

Year 3, Kosmos, slave of the publicani, aged 22.
