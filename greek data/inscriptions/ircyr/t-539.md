# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.539)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ  
Μεσορὴ  
ι Φιλοξή  
να Ἰοάν  
ουϲ (ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 3, Mesore 10th, Philoxeta, daughter of . . . mines, aged 4.  

Year 3, Mesore 10, Philoxena (daughter) of Ioanes, aged 30.
