# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.537)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Παῦ  
νι ιη  
Ἰοά  
νης  
Ἰοάνους  
(ἐτῶν) θ

### English translation

Year 7, Payni 18, Ioanes (son) of Ioanes, aged 9.
