# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.108)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) οβ Μεχεὶρ κη  
Κλευπάτρα  
Θευδότου  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 72, Mecheir 28: Kleupatra (daughter) of Theodotos, aged 30.
