# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.456)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Ἁ[θ]ὺρ  
ηι Ἁγ[ε]μ  
ονα Λυϲα  
νία (ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 10, Hathyr 18, Hagemona daughter of Lysanias, aged 35.  

Year 10, Hathyr 18, Hagemona (daughter) of Lysanias, aged 35.
