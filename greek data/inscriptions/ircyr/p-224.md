# Military funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.224)

## Ancient Greek text

[...ἔπαρχος] ἴλης Φρυγῶν ἑβδόμη[ς ...]

### English translation

. . .] and president at the games, commander of the cohort B[. . . commander] of the seventh Phrygian squadron [. . .
