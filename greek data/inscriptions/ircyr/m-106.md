# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.106)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰαδινχαν Ἡρακλίδα ἐτιῶν ο  
Ἀρατους Ἰαδινχαντος ἐτιῶν λζ

### English translation

Iadinchan (son) of Heraklidas, aged 50; Aratous (child) of Iadinchan, aged 37
