# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.358)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρη Φαρμου  
θὶ κ Κάλλις  
Φίλωνος  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 108, Pharmouthi 20, Kallis (son) of Philon, aged 35.
