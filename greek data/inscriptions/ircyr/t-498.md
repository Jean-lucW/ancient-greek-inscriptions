# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.498)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ  
Τυβὶ ϛ  
Δημᾶϲ  
Πρώρω

### English translation

Year 7, Tybi 6, Demas son of Prorus.  

Year 7, Tybi 6, Demas (son) of Proros.
