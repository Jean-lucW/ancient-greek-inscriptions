# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.355)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) [...] Χοιά  
χι [...] Π(όπλιος) Σήδι  
ος Σκευᾶς  
(ἐτῶν) ξ

### English translation

Year ? Choiak ?, P(oplios) Sedios Skeuas, [P(ublius) Sedius Scaeva], aged 60.
