# Funerary inscription, Tomb S.74 (IRCyr2020 C.519)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀλθαιμένευϲ  
τῶ Πρώρω  

Πρώρω  
τῶ Ἀλθαιμένευϲ

### English translation

(i) : (Tomb) of Althaimenes the (son) of Proros. (ii) : (Tomb) of Proros the (son) of Althaimenes
