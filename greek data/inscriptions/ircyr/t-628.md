# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.628)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Φαωφ  
ὶ ε Φλακ  
κία Μα  
γίου  
(ἐτῶν) κε

### English translation

Year 13, Phaophi 5, Phlakkia [Flaccia] (daughter) of Magios, aged 25.
