# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.244)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Φλάβιος  
Σταλακκίου  
ἐτῶν · μ

### English translation

Year 12, Flabios (son) of Stalakkios, aged 40.
