# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.691)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιζ Φαμεν  
[ὼ]τ ζ Ἀνφίων  
Ἀνφίωνος  
(ἐτῶν) ν  
σεν

### English translation

Year 17, Phamenoth 7, Anphion (son) of Anphion, aged 50.
