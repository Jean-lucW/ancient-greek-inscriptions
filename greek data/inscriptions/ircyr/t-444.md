# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.444)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Ἐπει  
φὶ ιε  
Κριτολᾶ  
Λυσία  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 13, Epeiph 15, Kritola (daughter) of Lysias, aged 35.
