# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 C.732)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Ϲίμω]  
[νοϲ] (ἐτῶν) ιϛ  
[Δωσί]θεοϲ  
[Ϲίμ]ωνοϲ  
[(ἐτῶν) ιγ]

### English translation

. . . -] (son) of Simon, aged 16. Dositheos (son) of Simon, aged 13.
