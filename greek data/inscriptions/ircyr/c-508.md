# Funerary inscription, Tomb W.85 (IRCyr2020 C.508)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ  
Θεύδωροϲ  
Τιμάρχω  

Πρᾶξιϲ  
Τιμάρχω  
(ἐτῶν) κδ

### English translation

Year 4. Theudoros (son) of Timarchos.  

Praxis (son) of Timarchos aged 24.
