# Ephebic inscription. (IRCyr2020 T.720)

## Ancient Greek text

Κάρποϲ [Εὐφ]  
ροϲύνω  

Φιλό  
δημοϲ  
Βύζα

### English translation

(i) Karpos (son?) of Euphrosynos.  

(ii) Philodemos (son) of Byzas.
