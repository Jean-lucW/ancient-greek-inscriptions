# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.272)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Φαρμο[υ]  
θὶ α Σέκον  
δος Καλλι  
κράτους  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 6, Pharmouthi 1, Sekondos [Secundus] (son) of Kallikrates, aged 20.
