# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.499)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Φαρμο[υ]  
θὶ ϛκ Εὐφ  
ράνωρ Π  
ρώρου [(ἐτῶν) ...]

### English translation

Year 10, Pharmouthi 26, Euphranor (son) of P[rorus?], aged ?  

Year 10, Pharmouthi 26, Euphranor (son) of Proros, [aged . . .]
