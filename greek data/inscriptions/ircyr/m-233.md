# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.233)

## Ancient Greek text

[?] Πόπλιος  
Ὀκτάβιος Νίγερ  
καλὸς κἀγαθός  
Μάρκωι Ὀκταβίω[ι]  

υἱὸς (ἐτῶν) κϛ

### English translation

Publius Octavius Niger, handsome and good, son of Marcus Octavius, aged 26  

P(oplios) Oktabios Niger [P(ublius) Octavius Niger], handsome and good, son of Marcus Octavius, aged 26
