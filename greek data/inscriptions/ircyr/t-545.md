# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.545)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) η Ἁθὺρ ζ Του  
βίας Ἀριστο  
κράτους (ἐτῶν) κ  
(ἔτους) η Θώθυ ηκ  
Ἀριστοκράτη  
ς Ἀριστοκρ  
άτους (ἐτῶν) δ

### English translation

Year 8, Athur 7th, Toubias, son of Aristocrates, aged 20. Year 8, Thoth 28th, Aristocrates, son of Aristocrates, aged 4.  

Year 8, Hathyr 7, Toubias (son) of Aristokrates, aged 20. Year 8, Thoth 28, Aristokrates (son) of Aristokrates, aged 4.
