# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 A.56)

## Ancient Greek text

Μυρὼ Πτ  
ολεμαίο  
υ ανυνη  
ὡϲ ἐτῶν  
ιγ

### English translation

Myro (daughter) of Ptolemaios, aged approximately 13
