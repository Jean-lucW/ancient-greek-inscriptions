# Prayer to the Highest God (IRCyr2020 P.317)

## Ancient Greek text

Λ(ούκιος) Ἀντώνιοϲ  
θεῷ ὑψίϲτῳ  
εὐχήν

### English translation

L(oukios) Antonios [L(ucius) Antonius], to the Highest God, a prayer
