### English translation

Year 28, in the priesthood of Epinikos (son) of Sotechnos, while Timonaktos (son) of Nikathlos, Demetrios (son) of Neikon, and Simos (son) of Xenon were Directors of Ephebes, while Theodoros (son) of Nikaios, Dexikrates (son) of Polykles, Timouchos (son) of Philokomos were gymnasiarchs, Demetrios (son) of Zenodotos was assistant: the ephebes under them (were):  

(b.i) : Arsakes (son) of Iason; Demetrios (son) of Demetrios; Theodoros (son) of Philostratos; Sosis (son) of Philon; Andrikos (son) of Andrikos; Timotheos (son) of Onasion; Timainetos (son) of Philon; Leschis (son) of Ammonios; Leukippos (son) of Akeson; Epinikos (son) of Epinikos; Kyrenaios (son) of Kallisthenes; Hyperbassas (son) of Ial; Itthalamon (son) of Apellas; Serapion (son) of Loinikos; Karneades (son) of Pithakos; [. . .] (son) of Timon; [. . .] (son) of Antigenes; [. . .] (son) of Theochrestos; [. . .]. (b.ii) : Potamon (son) of Potamon; Nikon (son) of Theogenes; Eraton (son) of Stratis; Ammonmios (son) of Philamon; Ammonios (son) of Aldamon; Theoxenos (son) of Aristarchos; Anyssan (son) of Ial; Aisagys (son) of Dachis; Eutychos (son) of Broson; Metrodoros (son) of Metrodoros; Agatharchos (son) of Da[. . .]; Nikathlos (son) of Nikathlos; Astyanax (son) of Aristonaos; Diogenes (son) of Diogenes; Serapion (son) of Serapion; Damokritos (son) of Damokritos; Dorotheos (son) of Sostratos; Ammonios (son) of Pratomedes; Kleogenes (son) of Pisikes; Arimman (son) of Dorion; Sosistratos (son) of Hyper[bassas].
