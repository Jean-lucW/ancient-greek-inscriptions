# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.201)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) η  
Ἀμμώ  
νιοϲ  
Λύκου  

(ἔτους) ι  

Ἀρίμμ[ας]  

Μαίων

### English translation

(i) Year 8. Ammonios (son) of Lykos. (ii) Year 10. (iii) Arimmas. (iv) Maion.
