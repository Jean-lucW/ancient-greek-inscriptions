# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 C.429)

## Ancient Greek text

[Αὐ]τοκράτω[ρ]  
[Κα]ῖσαρ Οὐεσ  
[π]ασιανός  
[ἀ]ρχιερεὺς μέ  
[γισ]τος δημαρχ[ι]  
[κῆς] ἐξουσίας τ[ὸ]  
[γ Αὐ]τοκράτωρ  
[τὸ] η πατὴρ πα[τ]  
ρίδος ὕπατος  
[τὸ] γ δεδειγμέ  
[νος] τὸ δ διὰ Κ(οΐντου)  
[Π]ακονίου Ἀγρι[π]  
[π]είνου ἰδίου [π]  
[ρ]εσβευτοῦ Δ(ήμῳ) Ῥ(ωμαίων)  
[τὸ] Π[τυ]λυμαῖο[ν]  
[ἀπ]εκ[ατ]έστησε[ν]

### English translation

a: (Latin) Emperor Caesar Vespasian Augustus, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power for the third time, acclaimed imperator for the eighth time, father of the fatherland, consul three times, designated for a fourth time, through his personal envoy Q(uintus) Paconius Agrippinus restored the [Ptolem]aion to the Roman People.  

b: (Greek) Emperor Caesar Vespasian, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power for the third time, acclaimed imperator for the eighth time, father of the fatherland, consul three times, designated for a fourth time, through his personal envoy Q(uintus) Paconius Agrippinus restored the Ptolemaion to the Roman People.
