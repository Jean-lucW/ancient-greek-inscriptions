# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 C.649)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) πγ  
Κλευπάτρα  
Ἀλεξικράτου  
(ἐτῶν) με

### English translation

Year 83, Kleupatra (daughter) of Alexikrates, aged 45
