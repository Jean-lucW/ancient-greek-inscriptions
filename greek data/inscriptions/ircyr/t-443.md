# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.443)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Παχὼν  
γ  
Σάρρα Τελε  
σιστράτου  
(ἐτῶν) ϙη

### English translation

Year 12, Pachon 3, Sarra (daughter) of Telesistratos, aged 98.
