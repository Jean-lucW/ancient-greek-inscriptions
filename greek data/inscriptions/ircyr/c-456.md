# Dedication of a statue by Claudia Venusta (IRCyr2020 C.456)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλ(αυδία)· Βενόστα · Κλ(αυδίου)· Καρτισθένους  
Μελίορος · θυγάτηρ  
Διονύσον ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

Claudia Venusta, daughter of Claudius Kartisthenes Melior, gave the statue of Dionysos at her own expense.  

Kl(audia) Benosta [Cl(audia) Venusta], daughter of Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Kartisthenes Melior, (gave the statue of) Dionysos at her own expense.
