# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.663)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιϛ Φαωφὶ ηκ  
Ἰουλία Λω  
πύου (ἐτῶν) ιζ

### English translation

Year 16, Phaophi 28, Julia daughter of Lopyos (?), aged 17.  

Year 16, Phaophi 28, Ioulia [Julia] (daughter) of Lopyos, aged 17.
