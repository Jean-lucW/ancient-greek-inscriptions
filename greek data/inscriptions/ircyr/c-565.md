# Funerary inscriptions, Tomb N.66 (IRCyr2020 C.565)

## Ancient Greek text

Φιλω ιε  
ραϲ (ἐτῶν) η  

Ἀγαθόκλεα  
(ἐτῶν) κε

### English translation

(a) : Philon . . . aged 8. (b) : Agathokles, aged 25
