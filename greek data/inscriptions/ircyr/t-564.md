# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.564)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Φαρμ  
ουθὶ κ Ἰου  
λία Νικα  
ίου (ἐτῶν) κ  
γ

### English translation

Year 11, (month) Pharmouthi 8th, Julia, daughter of Nicaios, aged 20.  

Year 11, Pharmouthi 20, Ioulia [Julia] (daughter) of Nikaios, aged 20 (or 23).
