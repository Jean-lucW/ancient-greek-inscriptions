# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.73)

## Ancient Greek text

[Κλα]υδία Κράτεια  
αὑτῆι καὶ τοῖϲ ἰδίοιϲ

### English translation

Κlaudia Krateia (made this) for herself and her own people.
