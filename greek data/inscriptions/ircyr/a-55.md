# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 A.55)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) {ο} ημ Φα[μ](ενὼτ) : κγ  
Βερενείκα  
δημοϲιωνῶν  
(ἐτῶν) ε

### English translation

Year 78, Phamenoth 23: Berenika (slave) of the (Roman) company of tax-collectors, aged 5.
