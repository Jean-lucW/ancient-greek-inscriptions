# Captions for wall-paintings, Tomb N.22 (IRCyr2020 C.557)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἐ]πίκτητοϲ  
[ὁπλ]όμαχοϲ  

ἀλλ’ἐψόφηκεν ἡ θύρα·  
προϲέρχεται ὁ πατήρ  

κρούϲ[ομαι] αὐτόϲ

### English translation

1: Epiktetos, fighter in armour. 2: ??. 3, i: But the door has sounded; the father is approaching. ii: I shall knock myself
