# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.391)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Μεχὶρ  
ηκ Θευδ[ώ]  
ρα Ἀρτ  
έμω  
[ν]ος [(ἐτῶν)] ζ

### English translation

Year 9, Mecheir 28, Theudora (daughter) of Artemon, aged 7
