# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.512)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Ἁθὺρ  
ζκ Ἄρισ  
τις Δωσι  
θέου (ἐτῶν) ξ

### English translation

Year 13, Hathyr 27, Aristis son of Dositheos, aged 60.  

Year 13, Hathyr 27, Aristis (son) of Dositheos, aged 60.
