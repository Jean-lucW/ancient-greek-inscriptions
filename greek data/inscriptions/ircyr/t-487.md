# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.487)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους)] ιϛ Θώθ[υ ...]  
[Ἀ]πολλ[ώνι]  
[ο]ς Λύκου  
[(ἐτῶν) ιε]

### English translation

Year 16, Thoth ?, Apollonios (son) of Lykos, aged 15.
