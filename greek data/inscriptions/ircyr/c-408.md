# Fragmentary building inscription (IRCyr2020 C.408)

## Ancient Greek text

[Κλαυ?]δία  
[...]αναν  
[... Λ]όνγου  
[...]ολον

[?Klau]dia [. . .] of Longos, [. . . ?gave] the panelling [. . . ?the work was completed by] the heirs, with the flooring.
