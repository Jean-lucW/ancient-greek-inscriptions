# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.258)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Φαρμουθὶ δ  
Πραξαγόρα [Ἰ]ωάνουϲ  
(ἐτῶν) ιγ

### English translation

Year 12, Pharmouthi 4, Praxagora, (daughter) of ?Joanes, aged 13.
