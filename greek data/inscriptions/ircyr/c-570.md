# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.171 (IRCyr2020 C.570)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰάϲων [[Μενάρηϲ]]  

Μνάϲαρχος Ἴασις  

Κληνάτα Ἀριϲτοτέλευϲ  
ἱάρεια Ἥρας  

Κυδιμάχα θευχρήστω  
ἱάρεια  

Πλήρης

### English translation

i. Iason ?Menares. Mnasarchos ?(son) of Iasis.  

ii. Klenata (daughter) of Aristoteles, priestess of Hera.  

iii. Kυdimacha (daughter) of Theochrestos, priestess.  

iv. (The tomb is) full.
