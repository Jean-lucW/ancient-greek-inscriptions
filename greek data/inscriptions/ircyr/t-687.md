# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.687)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ια Ἁθὺρ  
εκ Θε  
[υκ]λῆϲ  
[Θε]υδ  
ώρου  
(ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Year 11, Hathyr 25, Theukles (son) of Theudoros, aged 15.
