# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 C.430)

## Ancient Greek text

Αὐτοκράτωρ Καῖσαρ  
Οὐεσπασιανὸς ἀρχιε  
ρεὺς μέγιστος δημ  
αρχικῆς ἐξουσίας τὸ δ Αὐτο  
[κρ]άτω[ρ] τὸ η πα  
[τὴρ πα]τρίδος ὕ  
[πατο]ς δ διὰ Κ(οΐντου) Πα  
[κ]ωνίου Ἀγριππεί  
νου ἰδίου πρεσ  
βευτοῦ Δ(ήμῳ) Ῥωμαίων  
ἀποκατέ σ  
τησεν

### English translation

a: (Latin) Emperor Caesar Vespasian Augustus, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power for the fourth time, acclaimed imperator for the eighth time, father of the fatherland, consul four times, through his personal envoy Q(uintus) Paconius Agrippinus restored (this land) to the Roman People.  

b: (Greek) Emperor Caesar Vespasian Augustus, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power for the fourth time, acclaimed imperator for the eighth time, father of the fatherland, consul four times, through his personal envoy Q(uintus) Paconius Agrippinus restored (this land) to the Roman People.
