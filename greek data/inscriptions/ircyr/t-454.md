# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.454)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Φαωφὶ ιε  
Ἀκύλας  
Ἁμαι  
σίννα  
(ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Year 9, Phaophi 15, Akylas [Aquila] (son) of Amaisinnas, aged 15.
