# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.426)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) αι Ἁθὺ[ρ]  
κϛ Ἀντίγονο[ς]  
Ἀριστάρχου  
(ἐτῶν) πγ

### English translation

Year 11, Hathyr 26, Antigonos (son) of Aristarchos, aged 83.
