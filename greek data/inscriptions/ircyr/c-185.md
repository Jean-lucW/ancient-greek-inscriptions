# Funerary inscription for a soldier (IRCyr2020 C.185)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ἐνθ]άδε  
[κεῖμαι ἐ]τῶν κθ  
[ἐϲτρ]ατευσά  
[μην] ἔτη ιϛ

### English translation

. . . I ?name lie] here, aged 29; I served (as a soldier) for 16 years.
