### English translation

(i) . Year 22. Loukios [Lucius] (son) of Lusanias.  

(ii) . Year 5. Markos [Marcus] (son) of Theoteimos.  

(iii) . Year 25. Maker [Macer] (son) of Klemens [Clemens].
