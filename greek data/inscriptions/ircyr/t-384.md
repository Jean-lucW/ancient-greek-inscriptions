# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.384)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐπι⟨φὶ⟩ ια  
Ἀρτάφα  
ντοϲ ἐτ  
ῶν ιε  
Ἀντιγόν  
α Νικίου  
Χοάχι  
ε ⟨(ἐτῶν) ⟩ ξϛ

### English translation

Epeiph 11 (tomb) of Artaphan, aged 15; Antigona daughter of Nicias, Choiak 5, aged 66 (or: year 66)  

Epeiph 11 (Tomb) of Artaphan, aged 15; Antigona (daughter) of Nikias, Choiak 5, aged 66 (or, Year 66)
