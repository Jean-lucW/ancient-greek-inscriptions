# Funerary text (IRCyr2020 M.69)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κ Με  
χεὶρ κ  
Νεϲϲη  
νίϲ Ἀπο  
λλωνί  
ου (ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 20, Mecheir 20. Nessenis (daughter) of Apollonios, aged 35.
