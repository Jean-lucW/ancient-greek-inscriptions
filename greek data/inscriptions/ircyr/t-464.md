# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.464)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Φαω  
φὶ ε Εὐ  
φράνωρ  
Ἀνδροσθέ  
νους (ἐτῶν) ιβ

### English translation

Year 9, Phaophi 5, Euphranor (son) of Androsthenes, aged 12.
