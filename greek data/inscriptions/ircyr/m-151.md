# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.151)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρλζ  
Ἁθὺρ γ Ϲώ  
[δαμοϲ]  
Ἀριϲτω  
δάμω  
(ἐτῶν) κε

### English translation

Year 137, 3rd of the month of Hathyr: So[damos] (son) of Aristodamos (died) aged 25 years.
