# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.432)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Φαμεν  
[ὼθ] κγ  
[...]μ[...] Δ  
ωσιθέου  
(ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Year 11, Phamenoth 23, [. . . (child)] of Dositheos, aged 15.
