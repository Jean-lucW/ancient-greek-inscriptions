# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.410)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Ἐπιφὶ δ Εὐ  
πρόϲυνοϲ  
ἔπληϲεν  
ἔτη ν καὶ ἀ  
πέ[θ]ανε

### English translation

Year 2, Epeiph 4, Euprosynos; he completed 50 years and died.  

Year 2, Epeiph 4, Euprosynos completed 50 years and died.
