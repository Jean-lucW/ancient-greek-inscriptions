### English translation

(Latin) : Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, great-great grandson of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, consul; by the action of Lucius Acilius Strabo, his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.  

(Greek) : Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius Caesar Augustus and of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, high priest [pontifex maximus], holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, consul s; by the action of Loukios Akilios Strabon [Lucius Acilius Strabo], his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.
