# Building inscriptions from the temple of Zeus (IRCyr2020 C.419)

## Ancient Greek text

[... κ]αὶ ἀφ[ιε]ρώσαντος τῶ κρα τί[στ]ω ἀνθυπάτω Κλ(αυδίω)  
Ἀττάλω

### English translation

(a) : . . . proconsul [?dedicated] to Jupiter Augustus . . . the city [of the Cyrenaeans . . .  

(b) : . . . and dedicated the most excellent proconsul Claudius Attalos.
