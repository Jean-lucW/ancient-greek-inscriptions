# Funerary inscription for Pyrrus son of Longus (IRCyr2020 T.266)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους)? Ἁ]θὺρ ζ  
Π[ύ]ρρος  
[Λ]όνγου  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year_ Hathyr 7, Pyrros (son) of Longos [Longus] aged 20.
