# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.375)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Χοι  
ὰχ ιε Εἰ  
ρήνη  
Θεοδό  
του  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 7, Choiak 15, Eirene daughter of Theodotos, aged 35.  

Year 7, Choiak 15, Eirene (daughter) of Theodotos, aged 35.
