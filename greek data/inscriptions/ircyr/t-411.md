# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.411)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Θώθυ  
ζ Μά  
κερ Ἰρή  
νας (ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 3, Thoth 7, Maker [Macer] (son) of Irena, aged 20.
