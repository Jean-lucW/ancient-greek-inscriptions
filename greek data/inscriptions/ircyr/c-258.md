# Building dedication for a column (IRCyr2020 C.258)

## Ancient Greek text

Τ(ῖτος) Φάβ[ιος ... τὸν κείονα]  
[ἐκ τ]ῶν ἰδίων.

### English translation

T(itos) Phab[ios [T(itus) Fab[ius] . . . (gave) the column from] his own resources.
