# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.450)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Τυβὶ  
βι Βιλλιῆ  
νος Ἰούδ  
ας (ἐτῶν) γ

### English translation

Year 9, Tybi 12, Billienus Judas, aged 3.  

Year 9, Tybi 12, Billienos [Billienus] Judas, aged 3.
