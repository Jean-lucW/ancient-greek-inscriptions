# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.150)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρλη  
Καλλιβί  
α Τιμιϲ  
(ἐτῶν) μ ε

### English translation

Year 138: Kallibia (daughter) of Timis, aged 45.
