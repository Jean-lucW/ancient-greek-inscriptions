### English translation

Year 133: Ti(berios) Klaudios [Ti(berius) Claudius] Attalos son of Ti(berios) Klaudios [Ti(berius) Claudius] Klearchos, priest of Apollo.  

Year 134: T(itos) Phlabios [T(itus) Flavius] Eukleidas son of T(itos) Phlabios [T(itus) Flavius] Ba[tt]os, priest of Apollo.  

Year 135: Ti(berios) Klaudios [Tiberius Claudius] Philoxenos Antonianos.  

Year 142: P(oplios) Sestios Pollio [P(ublius) Sestius Pollio] son of Caios Sestios Phloros, grandson of M(arkos) Antonios Phlamma [M(arcus) Antonius Flamma], priest of Apollo, senator and quaestor of Rome, curule aedile, praetorian legatus propraetore of Crete and Cyrene.  

Year 137, also [10th year [of Trajan]]: C(aios) Postomios Optatos (son) of [?] Postumios Optatos, [priest] of Apollo in a good year.  

Year 138, [also 11th [of Trajan]]: [R]utilio[s . . .
