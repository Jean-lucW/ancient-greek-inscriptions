# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.660)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ει Μεϲορὴ  
βι Ῥοῦφοϲ  
Λουκίου  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 15, Mesore 12, Rouphos [Rufus] (son) of Loukios [Lucius], aged 35.
