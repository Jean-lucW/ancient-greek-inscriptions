# Dedication of a water basin (IRCyr2020 C.243)

## Ancient Greek text

[Τ]ι(βέριος) Κλ(αύδιος) Ἰάσων Μάγνος ἱε  
ρώμενος ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

Ti(berios) Kl(audios) [Ti(berius) Cl(audius)] Jason Magnos, while he was priest, (dedicated this) from his own funds.
