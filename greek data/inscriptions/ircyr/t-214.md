# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.214)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰαλ[...]άν Εὐ  
[κ]λίδα [...]  

Δάχι[ς]  
Νικάσιος  
Λυσανίας  

Ἀτθυ[μάλλας]

### English translation

(i) Aialan (son) of Euklidas. (ii) Dachis (son) of Nikasis. Lysanias. (iii) Atthumallas
