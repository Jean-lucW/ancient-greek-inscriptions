# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.301)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρξγ  
Ἀρράτα Μα  
γνου  
Ἀρέτα (ἐτῶν) κδ

### English translation

In the year 163, Arrata (daughter) of Magnus. Areta aged 24.
