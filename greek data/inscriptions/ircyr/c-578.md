# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.213 (IRCyr2020 C.578)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐπαφροδείτου τοῦ Λουκίου τόποϲ ἀρχεφήβου  
ὁ καλούμενοϲ Μυρόπνουϲ

### English translation

The place of Epaphrodeitus son of Lucius, the Archephebe, which is called myrrh-breathing.  

Place of Epaphrodeitos son of Loukios [Lucius], chief ephebe, he who was called Myropnous [fragrant].
