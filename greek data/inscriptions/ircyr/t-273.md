# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.273)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Φαρμ[ου]  
θὶ ι Εἰρήν[α]  
Μηνᾶ  
(ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 3, Pharmouthi 10 (April 5) , Eirena (daughter) of Mena, aged 50.
