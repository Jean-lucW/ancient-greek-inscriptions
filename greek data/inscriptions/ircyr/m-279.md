# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.279)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρμβ Φιλίππ  
α Ἀριϲτοκράτ  
ου (ἐτῶν) με

### English translation

Year 142: Philippa (daughter) of Aristokrates, aged 45.
