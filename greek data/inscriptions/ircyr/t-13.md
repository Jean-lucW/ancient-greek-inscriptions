# Anonymous dedication (IRCyr2020 T.13)

## Ancient Greek text

κατὰ ἐπιφανεί αν  
θε ο[ῦ]  
ἐπὶ ἱερέ[ως] ἐπωνύμου  
Ἀντωνίου Ἅγνου  
νέου

### English translation

According to the appearance of (the) God, in the time of the eponymous priest Antonius Hagnos the young(er)
