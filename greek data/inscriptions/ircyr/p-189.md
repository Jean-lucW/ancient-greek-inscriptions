# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 P.189)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιε Λύκος Νικασίωνος  

(ἔτους) ιη Ἔρως Ἔρωτος  

Ὑπ[...]  

(σύγκοιτοι)

### English translation

(i) Year 15. Lykos (son) of Nikasion  

(ii) Year 18. Eros (son) of Eros  

(iii) Not translatable  

(iv) Bed-fellow/s
