# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.303)

## Ancient Greek text

Μελ  
α[νία]ϲ  
Ζω[ϲί]  
μο[υ]  
(ἐτῶν) μθ  
[Χ]οιάχι ε  
(ἔτους) ϛ

### English translation

Melanias (son) of Zosimos, aged 49, Choiak 5, year 6
