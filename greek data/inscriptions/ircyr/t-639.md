# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.639)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Ἁθὺρ  
Μ[η]τρο  
φά[νη]ς Λευ  
κίππου  
(ἐτῶν) ξε

### English translation

Year 13, Hathyr . . . ?, . . . son of Leukippos, aged 65.  

Year 13, Hathyr ?, Metrophanes (son) of Leukippos, aged 65.
