# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.117)

## Ancient Greek text

Θέων Εὐγ[...]  

Αἰγλά(νωρ?)  

Ἄ[ν]ύσσαν  

[...] Αρτε[μ...]  

Εὔαρχος  

Εὐγέ[νης]

### English translation

(i) Theon (son) of Eug-. (ii) Aiglanor. (iii) Anussan. (iv) Artem-. (v) Euarchos. (vi) Eugenes
