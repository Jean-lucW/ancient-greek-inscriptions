# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.646)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Παῦνι  
θ Τρύφαι  
να Φιλίπ  
που (ἐτῶν) οε

### English translation

Year 12, Payni 9, Tryphaina (daughter) of Philippos, aged 75.
