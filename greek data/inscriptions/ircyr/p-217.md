# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.217)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Χοιάχι  
ϛι Δόρκα  
Ἰωϲήπω  
[(ἐτῶν)] λγ

### English translation

Year 10, Choiak 16: Dorka (daughter) of Iosepos, aged 33.
