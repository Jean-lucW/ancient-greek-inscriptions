# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.536)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ  
Ἁθὺρ ιζ  
Μάρθα  
Ἰοάνους  
(ἐτῶν) πε

### English translation

Year 3, Athur 17th, Martha, daughter of Iohannes, aged 8.  

Year 3, Hathyr 17, Martha (daughter) of Ioanes, aged 85.
