# Honours for a benefactor (IRCyr2020 T.6)

## Ancient Greek text

Μάσκυκα Ἀγαιμένευς  
Πτολεμαέα ἀρετᾶς ἕνεκα  
καὶ εὐνοίας τᾶς ἐς τὰν πόλιν  
Ἀρσινοές

### English translation

Maskyx (son) of Agaimenes, citizen of Ptolemais: for his virtue and his goodwill towards their city, the citizens of Arsinoe (honoured him).
