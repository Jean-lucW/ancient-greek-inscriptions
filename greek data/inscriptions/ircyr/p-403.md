# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.403)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κ  
Μεχὶρ ιϛ  
Ἀπολλών  
ιος Ἰναθα  
(ἐτῶν) πϛ

### English translation

Year 20, Mecheir 16: Apollonios (son) of Inathas aged 86.
