# Funerary inscriptions (IRCyr2020 M.152)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρκβ τοῦ  
ι Κλευπά  
τρα Τίμι  
οϲ κε  

Κλευπά  
τρα Θευ  
[χ]ρήτου  
(ἐτῶν) ξε

### English translation

Year 122, the 10th [of the emperor Domitian]: Kleupatra (daughter) of Timis, aged 25. Kleupatra (daughter) of Theuchrestos, aged 65.
