# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.236 (IRCyr2020 C.589)

## Ancient Greek text

Στράτω  
ν Εὐπό  
λιδος  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Straton (daughter) of Eupolis, aged 30.
