# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.445)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κα Φαωφὶ  
ια Λυσίας  
Εὐφράνορος  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 21, Phaophi 11, Lysias (son) of Euphranor, aged 30.
