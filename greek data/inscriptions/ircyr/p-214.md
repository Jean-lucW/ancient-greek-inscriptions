# Acclamations serving as captions for frescoes (IRCyr2020 P.214)

## Ancient Greek text

καιρόϲ  

[...]  

χηιϲει[...]  

τῶι Εὐτύχῳ καὶ Φιλότᾳ [καὶ] Ῥούφῳ [εὐτυ?]χῖτε

### English translation

(a) . . .] kairos  

(b) To/for/by good fortune  

(c) For Eutychos and Philotas and Rufus - may you have good luck.
