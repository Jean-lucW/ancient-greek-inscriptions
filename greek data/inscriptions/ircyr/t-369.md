# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.369)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ  
Ἁθὺρ  
κβ  
Ἀντίπα  
τροϲ  
Νικηφό[ρω]  
(ἐτῶν)  
λϛ

### English translation

Year 7, Hathyr 22, Antipatros (son) of Nikephoros, aged 36.
