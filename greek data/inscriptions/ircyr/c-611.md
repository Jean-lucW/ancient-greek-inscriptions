# Funerary inscription for Mnaso (IRCyr2020 C.611)

## Ancient Greek text

Μνασὼ Σίμωνος Δωροθέου [...]  
(ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Mneso (son) of Simon (by adoption) of Dorotheo (by nature), aged 15  

Mnaso (daughter) of Simon Dorotheos, aged 15.
