# Acclamation (IRCyr2020 C.312)

## Ancient Greek text

Σωσιβίῳ Ῥούφῳ  
νέῳ  
τῷ ἀληθῶς  
καλλιέτει  
εὐτυχῶς  

[...]  

κοιρο (ἐτῶν)  
επάρω  

[...]  

Θεοδώρῳ  
τῷ ἀληθῶϲ καλλιέτει  
εὐτυχῶϲ

### English translation

(a) For Sosibios Rouphos [Sosibius Rufus] the young(er), truly (priest) in a good year, with good fortune!  

(b) : (Not usefully translatable)  

(c) For Theodoros, truly (priest) in a good year, with good fortune!
