### English translation

(Latin) : Nero Claudius son of Claudius, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of [Tiberius Caesar] Augustus, great-great grandson of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, accalimed imperator, by (the action of) Lucius Acilius Strabo his personal envoy, restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.  

(Greek) : Nero Claudius son of Claudius, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of [Tiberius Caesar] Augustus, and great-great grandson of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, acclaimed imperator, by (the action of) Lucius Acilius Strabo his personal envoy, restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.
