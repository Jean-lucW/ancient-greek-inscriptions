# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.436)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρζ Θώθυ  
ιγ Διόδοτος  
Διοδότω  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 107, Thoth 13, Diodotos (son) of Diodotos, aged 35.
