# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.551)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Τυβὶ ιδ  
Βάσος  
Ἀρτεμᾶ  
[(ἐτῶν)] μϛ

### English translation

Year 1, Tubi 14th, Basos son of Artemas, ?  

Year 4, Tybi 14, Basos [Bassus] (son) of Artemas, aged 46.
