# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.694)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Φαωφὶ  
θκ Δωσι  
[θε]ία Φίλω  
νος (ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 10, Phaophi 29, Dositheia (daughter) of Philon, aged 20.
