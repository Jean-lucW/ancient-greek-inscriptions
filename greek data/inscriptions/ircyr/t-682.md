# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.682)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Φαω  
φὶ βι Ἀρ  
σινόα Εὐ  
δαίμο  
νος (ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Year 11, Phaophi 12, Arsinoa (daughter) of Eudaimon, aged 15.
