# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.404)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κ Μεχὶρ  
α Γάϊ  
ος Ἰου  
λίου  
(ἐτῶν) β

### English translation

Year 20, Mecheir 1, Gaios [Gaius] (son) of Ioulios [Julius], aged 2.
