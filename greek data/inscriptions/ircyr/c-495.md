# Dedication of a statue of Demeter and a shrine (IRCyr2020 C.495)

## Ancient Greek text

[Κλ(αυδία) Βε]νόστα Κλ(αυδίου) · Καρτισ[θ]ένο[υ]ς  
Μελίορος · θυγάτηρ ·  
[τ]ὰν μεγάλαν Δάματραν ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων καὶ τὸν  
ναὸν

### English translation

Claudia Venusta, daughter of Claudius Kartisthenes Melior, (gave) the great Demeter from her own funds, and the temple.  

Kl(audia) Benosta [Cl(audia) Venusta], daughter of Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Kartisthenes Melior, (gave) the great Demeter from her own funds, and the temple.
