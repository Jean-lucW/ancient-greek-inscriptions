# Dedication of an image of Augustus (IRCyr2020 C.125)

## Ancient Greek text

[Αὐτοκράτορα Καίσαρα θ]εῶ υἱὸν τετρακ[αι]  
[δεκάκις Αὐτ]οκράτορα  
[Ἑρμῖ ?] Ἡρακλῖ  

[...]

### English translation

[Emperor Caesar], son of a divinity, for the fourt[eenth time [acclaimed]] Imperator, [so-and-so [dedicated his statue]] to Hermes and Herakles [. . ..
