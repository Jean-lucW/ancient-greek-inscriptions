# Funerary> inscription (IRCyr2020 T.563)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρ Ἁθὺρ ι  
Νίκων

### English translation

Athur ? Nicon son of Nicaios, aged 80  

Year 100, Hathyr 10, Nikon (son) of Neikaios, aged 6.
