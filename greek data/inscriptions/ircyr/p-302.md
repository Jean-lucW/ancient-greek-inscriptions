# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.302)

## Ancient Greek text

Τ(ιβέριοϲ) Κ[λαύ]διοϲ  
[...]α[...]οϲ  
ἐπ[οί]ηϲεν  
ἑα[υτ]ῷ καὶ  
τοῖ[ϲ ἰ]δίοιϲ

### English translation

T(iberios) Klaudios [T(iberius) Claudius] -os made (this) for himself and his family.
