# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.704)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Με  
χὶρ δκ  
Φ[ί]λω  
ν Στρά  
των  
ος (ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 11, Mecheir 24, Philon (son) of Straton, aged 50.
