# Dedication to Iatros and Iaso (IRCyr2020 M.190)

## Ancient Greek text

Μεθύϲι,  
Μινέρβι,  
Ἰατρῶι Ἰαϲο͂ι,  
θεοῖϲ ἐπηκόοιϲ,  
Κλ(αύδιοϲ) Λύκοϲ εὐχὴν  
ἀνέθηκα  
❦

### English translation

For Methysis, Minerva, Iatros, Iaso, gods who listen, I Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Lykos dedicated (this in fulfilment of) a vow
