# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.417)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Ἁθὺρ  
κε [Ἀ]ρϲι  
νόα Μ  
άρκω ἔζη  
ϲεν ἔτη  
κ

### English translation

Year 11, Hathyr 25, Arsinoa (daughter) of Markos [Marcus], lived 20 years.
