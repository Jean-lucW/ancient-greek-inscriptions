### English translation

[Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson] of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius Caesar [Augustus] and of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, high priest [pontifex maximus], holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, [consul]; by the action of Loukios Akilios Strabon [Lucius Acilius Strabo], his personal envoy [he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons].
