# Honours for a wife (IRCyr2020 C.230)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Πα]υσίλαν Βάκαλος  
Θαλίαρχος Ἁνιόχω  
τὰν ἑαυτοῦ γυναῖκα  
ἀνέθηκε

### English translation

Pausila (daughter) of Bakal: Thaliarchos (son) of Aniochos dedicated (the statue of) his own wife.
