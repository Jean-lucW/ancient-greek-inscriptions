# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.574)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Ἁθὺ[ρ ...]  
Λυϲίμαχ  
οϲ  
Θευδότ  
ου  
(ἐτῶν) ζ

### English translation

Year 5, Hathyr ?, Lysimachos (son) of Theudotos, aged 7.
