# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.495)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Με  
χὶρ κ Εἰ  
ρήνα  
Ἀριστέα  
(ἐτῶν) ε

### English translation

Year 11, Mecheir 20, Eirena (daughter) of Aristes, aged 5.
