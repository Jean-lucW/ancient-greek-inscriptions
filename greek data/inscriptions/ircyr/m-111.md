# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.111)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Μεχε[ὶρ ... Πρ?]  
όκλα Πρ[όκλου?]  
(ἐτῶν) ιδ [?]

### English translation

Year 11, Mecheir [?]: Pr]okla (daughter) of Pr[oklos?], aged 14.
