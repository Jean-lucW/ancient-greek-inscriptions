# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.389)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιδ  
Τερτία  
Ἰωσῆ  
τος (ἐτῶν) μ  
Ἰωϲῆϲ (ἐτῶν)  
κε

### English translation

Year 14, Tertia (daughter) of Ioses, aged 40; Ioses, aged 25
