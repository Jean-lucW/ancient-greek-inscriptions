# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.350)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρθ Φαωφὶ  
ιε Πατρο  
φίλα Νει  
κασίωνος  
(ἐτῶν) κγ

### English translation

Year 109, Phaophi 15, Patrophila (daughter) of Neikasion aged 23.
