# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.693)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρα Μεσ  
ορὴ θκ  
Καλλικλ  
[ε]ία Γ[...]  
ος (ἐτῶν) πα

### English translation

Year 101, Mesore 29, Kallikleia (daughter) of G..., aged 81.
