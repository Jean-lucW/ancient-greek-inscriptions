# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.540)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Τυβὶ θ Τρυ  
φαίνα Ἰοά  
νους (ἐτῶν) ι  
(ἔτους) [...] Ἐπαγομέ  
νων [...] Ἰουδί  
ων Ἰοάνους  
(ἐτῶν) ιγ

### English translation

Year 7, Tubi 17th, Truphainato(s) son of A . . . aged . . . . Year . . ., . . . nois, daughter (?) of . . . onios, aged 13.  

Year 7, Tybi 9, Tryphaina (daughter) of Ioanes, aged 10. Year 7, intercalary day ?, Ioudion (son) of Ioanes, aged 13.
