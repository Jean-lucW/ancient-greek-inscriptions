# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.513)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Μεχὶρ ε  
Ἀφροδισία  
Ἀλεξᾶ (ἐτῶν) ε

### English translation

Year 10, Mecheir 5, Aphrodisia (daughter) of Alexas, aged 5.
