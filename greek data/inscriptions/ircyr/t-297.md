# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.297)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ςι Φαωφὶ[...]  
Ζήνων Ὀ  
νησίμω  
(ἐτῶν) ιη

### English translation

Year 16, Phaophi ? Zenon (son) of Onesimos, aged 18.
