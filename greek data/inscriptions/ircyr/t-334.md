# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.334)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ(αίου) Ἰουλίου  

Ἀϲκλάπω  
τῶ καὶ Λό  
πω

### English translation

i: (Tomb) of G(aios Ioulios[G(aius) Julius]. ii: (Tomb) of Asklapos, also called Loupos [Lupus].
