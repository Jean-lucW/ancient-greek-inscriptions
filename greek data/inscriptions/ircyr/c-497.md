# Dedication of a statue of Parthenos and a shrine (IRCyr2020 C.497)

## Ancient Greek text

[Κ]λ(αυδία) · Βενόστα · Κλ(αυδίου) · Κ[αρ]  
τισθένους θυγάτηρ · τὰ[ν]  
Παρθένον · ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων  
καὶ τὸν ναὸν ❦

### English translation

Claudia Venusta, daughter of Claudius Kartisthenes, gave the Parthenos from her own funds, and the temple.  

Kl(audia) Benosta [Cl(audia) Venusta], daughter of Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Kartisthenes (gave) the Parthenos from her own funds, and the temple.
