# Ephebic dedication and fragmentary list (IRCyr2020 P.348)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] ἐφ’ἱερέως Ἀριστοτέ[λευς ...]  
[ἐφη]βαρχούντων Αγ[...]ΑΠ[...] γυμ[νασιαρχούντων ...]ορ[...]ανδρου [...]μμων[ος ...]ππου  
Ἑρμι Ἡρακλι [...]

### English translation

[. . .] In the priesthood of Aristoteles [/(son) of . . .], while Ag[- . . . and . . .] were Directors of Ephebes [. . . while . . .] were Directors of the Gymnasium [. . .] to Hermes and Herakles
