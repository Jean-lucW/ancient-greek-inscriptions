# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.484)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Χοιά[χι]  
[...] Μ(ᾶρκος) Λυ  
κιά νιος  
Νίλων  
(ἐτῶν) π

### English translation

Year 4, Choiak ?, M(arkos) Lukianos [M(arcus) Lucianus] Nilon, aged 80.
