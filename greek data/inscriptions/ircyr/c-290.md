# Dedication of a statue of Apollo (IRCyr2020 C.290)

## Ancient Greek text

Τ(ιβέριος) Κλαύδι[ο]ς Ἀριστο  
μένης Μάγνος ὁ καὶ  
Περικλῆς ἱαρειτεύ  
ων ἐκ [πρ]οσόδ[ων τῶ]  
[Ἀ]π[όλλωνος]

### English translation

Ti(berios) Klaudios [Ti(berius) Claudius] Aristomenes Magnos Klaudios [Magnus], also called Perikles, serving as priest, (dedicated this) from the income of [Apollo].
