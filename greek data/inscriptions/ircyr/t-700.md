# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.700)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Τυβὶ δι  
Ἀγεμόνα  
Τύδευς  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 13, Tybi 14, Hagemona (daughter) of Tydes, aged 20.
