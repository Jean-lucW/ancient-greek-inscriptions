# Name on a funerary casket (IRCyr2020 B.53)

## Ancient Greek text

ἐπ’ Ἀριϲτοδάμω  
Ἀκεϲοῦϲ τᾶϲ Θεάρω

### English translation

(In the priesthood of) Aristodamos, Akesous (daughter) of Thearos.
