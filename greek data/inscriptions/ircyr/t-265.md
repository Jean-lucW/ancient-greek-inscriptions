# Funerary inscription for Arimmas son of Mogon (IRCyr2020 T.265)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϙ Παχὼ  
ν ε Ἀρίμ  
μας Μό  
γωνος  
(ἐτῶν) ιϛ

### English translation

Year 90, Pachon 5, Αrimmas (son) of Mogon, aged 16.
