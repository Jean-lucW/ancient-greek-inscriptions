# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.253)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Νείκα[νδρ]  
οϲ Νεικ[α]  
γόρα [...]  
(ἐτῶν) μ [...]  
(ἔτους) ϛ Ϲάρρα Νει[κάν]  
δρου Χοιάχι α  
(ἐτῶν) ιζ πλήρηϲ  
ἀρετῆϲ

### English translation

Year 3, Nicandros son of Nicagoras [. . .] aged 40. year 6, Sara, daughter of Nicandros, Choiaki 1, aged 17, full of virtue.  

Year 3, Neikandros (son) of Neikagoras [. . .] aged 40.  

Year 6, Sarra, (daughter) of Neikandros, Choiak 1, aged 17, full of virtue.
