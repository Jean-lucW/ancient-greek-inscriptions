# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.66 (IRCyr2020 C.564)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰωσῆς Κρίσ  
που (ἐτῶν) δ  
Κόϊντος Κο  
ίντου (ἐτῶν) ιε  
Λύκα Γαίου  
(ἐτῶν) νη  
Σάρρα προσ  
ήλυτος  
(ἐτῶν) ιη

### English translation

Joses (son) of Krispos [Crispus], aged 4; Kointos [Quintus] (son) of Kointos [Quintus], aged 15; Lyka (daughter) of Gaios [Gaius], aged 58; Sarra, proselyte, aged 18.
