# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.590)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) α Θώ  
θυ βκ  
Εὐδαίμ  
ων Βάσ  
σω (ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 1, Thoth 22, Eudaimon (son) of Bassos [Bassus] aged 20.
