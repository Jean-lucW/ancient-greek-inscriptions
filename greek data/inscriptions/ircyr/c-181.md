# Fragmentary building inscription (IRCyr2020 C.181)

## Ancient Greek text

[]  
[...]λισια τὸν ναὸν ἐκ [θεμελίω]ν κατεσκεύασεν σὺν  
[...] καὶ τὸν [...]πον ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων  

[]  
[...]ταν[...]αστω[... ...]και[... ...]

### English translation

(a-b) ?Emperor Commodus (name erased) . . . -]lisia built the temple with . . . and the [?] at his/their own expense.  

c and d are not translatable.
