# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 C.618)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρμδ  
Παοῖ  
νι β  
Νεικάριν  
Εὐμένευς (ἐτῶν) ιη

### English translation

Year 144, Payni 2, Neikarin (daughter) of Eumnenes, aged 18.
