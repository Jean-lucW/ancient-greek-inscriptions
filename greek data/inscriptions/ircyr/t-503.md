# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.503)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) βκ Μάριν  
Εὐφράνο  
ρος (ἐτῶν) μ

### English translation

Year 22 (or ?120) , Marin (daughter) of Euphranor, aged 40.
