### English translation

(a) M(arkos) Aurelios [M(arcus) Aurelius] Euphranor, priest of Apollo the Founder, made (the floor) from the money of the god.  

(b) For ever, the koinon of the Six Cities!  

(c) (Not usefully translatable)
