# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.401)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) η Βαινήα  
ς Ἀρτεμι  
δ⟨ώ⟩ρου (ἐτῶν) π

### English translation

Year 8, Baineas (?) (son) of Artemidoros, aged 80.
