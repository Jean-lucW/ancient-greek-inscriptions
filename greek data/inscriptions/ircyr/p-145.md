# Honours for M. Aurelius Flavianus (IRCyr2020 P.145)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(ᾶρκον) Αὐρήλιον  
Φλαβιανὸν  
Βαλερία Μαρ  
κιανὴ τὸν  
γλυκύτατον  
υἱὸν  
ψ(ηφίσματι) β(ουλῆς)

### English translation

M(arkos) Aurelius Phlabianos [M(arcus Aurelius Flavianus]: Baleria Markiane [Valeria Marciana] (honoured) her dearest son. By Decree of the Council.
