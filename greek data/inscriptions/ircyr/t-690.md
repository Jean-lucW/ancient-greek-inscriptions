# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.690)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Φα{υ}ωφ[ὶ]  
δ Ἁνίοχος  
Τυδεύς  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 10, Phaophi 4, Aniochos (son) of Tydes, aged 20.
