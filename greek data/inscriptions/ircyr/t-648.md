# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.648)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δι Ἁθὺρ  
βι Πρῖμος  
Κασσί  
ου (ἐτῶν) λγ

### English translation

Year 14, Hathyr 12, Primos [Primus] (son) of Kassios [Cassius], aged 20.
