# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 C.545)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους)  

[...] Κάρνι  
οϲ (ἐτῶν) ιη  
Τιμάσα  
Μεμέω  
(ἐτῶν) η

### English translation

Year ?...(child) of Karnis, aged 18. Timasa (daughter) of Memeos, aged 8.
