# Building inscription for cisterns (IRCyr2020 C.166)

## Ancient Greek text

⟨Ναι⟩υιανὸς ὁ κρ(άτιστος)  
θεμελίων κατεσκεύασεν καὶ ἀφιέρωσεν μετὰ Σειλίου❦  
Πλαυτίου Ἀτεριανοῦ τοῦ κρατίστου ταμί⟨ου⟩

### English translation

(Latin) By the authority and generosity of the best and greatest emperors Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus, victor in Armenia, and Lucius Aurelius Verus, victor in Armenia, greatest victor in Parthia, cisterns [were built], from public funds.  

(Greek) -ouianus the most excellent . . . of foundations constructed and consecrated, with Silius Plautius Haterianus, the most excellent quaestor.
