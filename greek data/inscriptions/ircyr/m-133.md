# Funerary inscriptions (IRCyr2020 M.133)

## Ancient Greek text

Φιλοῦς Εὐφράν  
οροϲ (ἐτῶν) ξ  

Γάϊοϲ  
Γαίου (ἐτῶν) λε  

Είραϲ  
Γαίου (ἐτῶν) ζ

### English translation

(i) : Philous (daughter) of Euphranor aged 60; (ii) : Gaios [Gaius] (son) of Gaios [Gaius], aged 35; (iii) : Eiras (son) of Gaios [Gaius] aged 7.
