# Dedication of a statue of Kore and a shrine (IRCyr2020 C.496)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλ(αυδία) · Βενόστα · Κλ(αυδίου) · Καρτισ  
·θένους · Μελίορος  
θυγάτηρ  
τὰν Κόραν ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων  
καὶ τὸν ναὸν

### English translation

Claudia Venusta, daughter of Claudius Kartisthenes Melior, gave the Kore from her own funds, and the temple.  

Kl(audia) Benosta [Cl(audia) Venusta], daughter of Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Kartisthenes Melior (gave) the Kore from her own funds, and the temple.
