# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.655)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Φαωφὶ κγ  
Λ(ούκιοϲ) Ἀουιάνιοϲ  
Μάξιμοϲ  
(ἐτῶν) κγ

### English translation

Year 6, Phaophi 23, L(oukios) Aouianios Maximos, [L(ucius) Avianius Maximus], aged 23.
