# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 C.544)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε  
Ἐπιφὶ γκ  
Ἰάϲων  
Διονυϲί  
ω (ἐτῶν) λϛ

### English translation

Year 5, Epiphi 23: Jason (son) of Dionysios, aged 36.
