# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.699)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Χοιά  
χι ζ [Ε]ύ  
τύχης  
Ἁλιάδα  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 11, Choiak 7, Eutyches (son) of Aliadas, aged 35.
