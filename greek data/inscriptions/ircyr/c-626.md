# Funerary inscription, Tomb S.147 (IRCyr2020 C.626)

## Ancient Greek text

ὑγίαινε καλ[ὲ] κἀ[γα]θὲ  
Ἀπ[ο]λ[λώνιε?] (ἐτῶν) λη

### English translation

Be well, noble and good Apoll[onios?], aged 38.
