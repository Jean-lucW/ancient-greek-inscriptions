# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.252)

## Ancient Greek text

Λύκα Θαίω  
[(ἐτῶν)] λα  
Γαΐω Γαΐω  
(ἐτῶν) με

### English translation

Luka (daughter) of Thaios, aged 31. (Tomb) of Gaios [Gaius] (son) of Gaios [Gaius], aged 45.
