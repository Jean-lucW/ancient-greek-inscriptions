# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.488)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Ἐπιφὶ βι  
Κάνδιδος  
Ἑρενν⟨ί⟩ου  
(ἐτῶν) ιθ

### English translation

Year 5 (?), Epiphi 12th, Candidus, son of Herennus, aged 15.  

Year 6, Epeiph 12, Kandidos [Candidus] (son) of Herennios [Herrenius], aged 19.
