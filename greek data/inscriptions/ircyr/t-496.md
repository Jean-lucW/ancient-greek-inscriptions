# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.496)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιθ Ἁθὺρ  
κη Δωσί  
θεος Ἀρισ  
τεύς (ἐτῶν) ιδ

### English translation

Year 19, Hathyr 28, Dositheos (son) of Aristes, aged 14.
