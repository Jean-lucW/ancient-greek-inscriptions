# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.11 (IRCyr2020 C.554)

## Ancient Greek text

Λεύκιος Οὐίβιος Λευκίου (ἀπελεύθερος)  
Γάτταβος  
Λεύκιος Nουμίσιος Μαρίω Λ(ευκίου) (ἀπελεύθερος)  
ἀδελφὸς ἐποίησε[ν]

### English translation

(Latin) : For L(ucius) Vibius Gattabus, freedman of L(ucius). L(ucius) Numisius Mario, freedman of L(ucius), his brother, was responsible for the construction [of the tomb].  

(Greek) : Leukios Ouibios Gattabos, freedman of Leukios. Leukios Numisios Mario, freedman of L(eukios), his brother, was responsible for the construction [of the tomb].
