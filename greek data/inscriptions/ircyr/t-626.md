# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.626)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Φαρμου  
θὶ ακ Ἰσιδί  
κα Ἰούδα (ἐτῶν) θ

### English translation

Year 11, Pharmouthi 21, Isidika (daughter) of Judas, aged 9.
