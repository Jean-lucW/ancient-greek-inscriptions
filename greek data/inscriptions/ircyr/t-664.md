# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.664)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους)  
ια Χοιὰκ ι  
Μαρία  
Ἰουλίου  
(ἐτῶν) κβ

### English translation

Year 11, Choiak 10, Maria (daughter) of Ioulios [Julius], aged 22.
