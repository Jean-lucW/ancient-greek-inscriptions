# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.550)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Παχὼν  
κ Μάριν  
Κασσίω  
(ἐτῶν) ο

### English translation

Year 20, Pachon 20th, Marin son of Cassios, aged 70.  

Year 2, Pachon 20, Marin (daughter) of Kassios [Cassius], aged 70.
