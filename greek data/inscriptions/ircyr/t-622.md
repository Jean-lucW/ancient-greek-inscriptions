# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.622)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Με  
σορὴ  
ιθ Ἁγε  
μόνα  
Εὐκλίδ  
α (ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 2, Mesore 19, Hagemona (daughter) of Euklidas, aged 30.
