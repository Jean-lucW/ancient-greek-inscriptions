# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.623)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ι  

[...] ΜΕ  
[... Π]έλεια  
[Κρίτ]ωνοϲ  
[...] (ἐτῶν) ιε  
[...] Λυϲ  
[ανία?]ϲ  
[Κ]ρίτωνο  
[ϲ] (ἐτῶν) ζ

### English translation

Year 10, . . . ? Peleia daughter of Criton . . .?, aged 15. . . . ? Lysanias son of Criton, aged 7.  

Year 10, [. . .] Peleia (daughter) of Kriton [. . .] aged 15. [. . .] Lysanias (son) of Kriton, aged 7.
