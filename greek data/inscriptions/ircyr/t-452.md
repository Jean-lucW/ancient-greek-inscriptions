# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.452)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους) ε] Μεσ[ορὴ]  
ϛ Ἀνδροσ  
θένης  
Ἀνδροσ  
θένους  
(ἐτῶν) ξζ

### English translation

Year 5, Mesore 6, Androsthenes son of Androsthenes, aged 67.  

Year 5, Mesore 6, Androsthenes (son) of Androsthenes, aged 67.
