# Dedication of a statue of Dionysos (IRCyr2020 C.308)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ποσει]δώνιος Ποσειδωνίω  
[ἱερατ]εύων Διόνυσον  
[χ]αριδώταν

### English translation

Posidonios (son) of Posidonios,serving as priest, (gave the statue of) Dionysos, giver of joy.
