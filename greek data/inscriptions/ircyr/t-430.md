# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.430)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Πα[οινὶ...]  
Νικο δίϲ  
τα Διοδό  
τω ἐ[τ]ῶν [...]

### English translation

Year 5, Payni ?, Nikodista [daughter] of Diodotos, aged --.
