# Military presence inscription of a Syrian (IRCyr2020 M.5)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) · πβ · Μεχεὶρ · ζ · ἥκω  
Μαγίων Ἀρτεμ[ι]δώρου κεντυρίων  
Ἀπαμεὺϲ τῶν ἀπὸ Ϲυρίαϲ

### English translation

Year 82, Mecheir 7, I came here, Magion (son) of Artemidoros, centurion, an Apamean with the men from Syria
