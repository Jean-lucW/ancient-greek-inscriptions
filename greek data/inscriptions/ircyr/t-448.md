# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.448)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ιε Παῦνι [...]  
Γ(αίοϲ) Ἰολιοϲ Ἀρ[ε]τᾶ  
ϲ Γ(αίου) Ἰολίου Κ[αν]  
δίδου υἱὸϲ [(ἐτῶν) ...]

### English translation

Year 15, Payn ?, C. Julius Aretas son of C. Julius Candidus, aged ?  

Year 15, Payn ?, G(aios) Iolios [G(aius) Julius] Aretas (son) of G(aios) Iolios Kandidos [G(aius Julius Candidus], aged ?
