# Posthumous honours for Kudimacha (IRCyr2020 C.397)

## Ancient Greek text

Δάματρι καὶ Κόραι ἡρωίδα  
Κυδιμάχαν Κλεάρχω  
ἀρετᾶς ἕνεκα Κυραναῖοι

### English translation

To Demeter and Kore, the Cyrenaeans (dedicated the statue of) the Heroine, Kudimacha (daughter) of Klearchos, because of her virtue.
