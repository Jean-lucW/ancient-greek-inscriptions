# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.271)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) α Λύκα  
Θευδώρω (ἐτῶν) ε  
μηνῶν ι

### English translation

Year 1, Lyka (daughter) of Theudoros, aged 5 years 10 months.
