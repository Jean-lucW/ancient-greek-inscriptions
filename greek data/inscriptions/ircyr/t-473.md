# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.473)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους) ... Φαρ]  
[μου]θὶ  
[η Ἁρπ]α  
[λιν] Μα  
κέδονος  
(ἐτῶν) κε

### English translation

[Year ---, Phar]mouthi 8, Harpalin (daughter) of Makedon aged 28.
