# Military funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.215)

## Ancient Greek text

Λ(ούκιος) · Φούφιος ·  
[Σέ]κονδος  
ὀετράνος · λεγ(ιῶνος)  
α Εἰταλικῆς  
ἐτῶν · ξ

### English translation

Lucius Fufius Secundus, veteran of Legion Prima Italica, aged 60.  

L(oukios) Phouphios Sekondos [L(ucius) Fufius Secundus], veteran of Legion Prima Italica, aged 60.
