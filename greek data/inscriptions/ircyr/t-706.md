# Imperial building inscription by Hadrian (IRCyr2020 T.706)

## Ancient Greek text

[Α]ὐτοκράτωρ Καῖσαρ θ[εοῦ Τραιανοῦ Παρθικοῦ]  
[υἱ]ός, θεο[ῦ Νέρβα υἱωνό]ς, Τραιανὸ[ς] Ἀδριανὸς Σεβαστός,  
ἀρχιερεὺ[ς μέγιστος δημα]ρχικῆς ἐ[ξ]ουσίας ❦❦ τὸ ζ  
[ὕ]πατ[ος τὸ γ τῇ πόλει?] ❦ ἔδωκεν

### English translation

(Latin) : [Emperor Caesar son of deified Trajan, victor in Parthia, grandson of deified Nerva, Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Chief Priest], holding tribunician [power for the seventh time,] consul for the third time [?gave to the city]  

(Greek) : Emperor Caesar son of [deified Trajan, victor in Parthia, grandson] of deified [Nerva], Trajan Hadrian Augustus, Chief Priest, holding tribunician power for the seventh time, consul [for the third time] gave [?to the city]
