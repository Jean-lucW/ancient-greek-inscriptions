# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.620)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιε Θώθυ  
κβ Πελήα  
Κρίτωνος  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 15, Thoth 22, Pelea (daughter) of Simon, aged 20.
