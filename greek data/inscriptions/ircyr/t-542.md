# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.542)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Τυβὶ  
δι Δωϲᾶϲ  
Ἀριϲτοκρά  
τουϲ (ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 11, Tubi . . . th, Didosas, son of Aristocrates, aged 35.  

Year 11, Tybi 14, Dosas (son) of Aristokrates, aged 35.
