### English translation

(a) [With Good Fortune. Year ?]also year 1 of the [Emperors] Caesars M(arcus) Aurelius Antoninus and L(ucius) Aurelius Verus, Augusti, when the priest of Apollo was Poplios Agarenos Sekondos. The ephebarch and triankatiarch was G(aios) Balerios Euphrates Phretensios [G(aius) Valerius Euphrates Fretensius]; he generals, acting as gymnasirachs and dekadarchs were G(aios) Sedios Antoneinos [G(aius) Sedius Antoninus] and L(oukios) Nobios [L(ucius) Novius] Iatrodoros; the akademarch was T(itos) Phlabios Phlabianos [T(itus) Flavius Flavianus]. The ephebes were:
