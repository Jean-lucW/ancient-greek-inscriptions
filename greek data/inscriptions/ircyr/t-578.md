# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.578)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιη Θώθυ  
κ Ἀριστί  
[ω]ν Ἰούδα  
(ἐτῶν) θ

### English translation

Year 18, Thoth 20, Aristion (son) of Ioudas, aged 9.
