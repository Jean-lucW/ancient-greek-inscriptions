### English translation

(a) : [?Year ? of Emperor Caesar Marcus Aurelius Anto]ninus [Augustus] victor in Armenia, victor in Parthia, victor in Media, great victor in Germania, in the priesthood of Apollo of M(arkos) Klaudios [M(arcus) Claudius] Karneades, under the regulation of Numisius Marcellianus the most excellent proconsul; the ephebarchs being L(oukios) Anthestios [L(ucius) Anthestius] Polybios, the young(er) and L(oukios) Anthestios [L(ucius) Anthestius] Archibios; the generals being Sex(tos) Balerios Biktor [Sex(tus) Valerius Victor] and Satorneilos [Satornilus] (son) of Ammonios; the gymasiarchs being Sex(tos) Anthestios Balerianos [Sex(tus) Anthestius Valerianus] and Sex(tos) Balerios Phortounatos [Sex(tus) Valerius Fortunatus].  

(b, column I) : Sex(tos) Balerios Phortounatos [Sex(tus) Valerius Fortunatus], L(oukios) Domitios Satris [L(ucius) Domitius Satris], L(oukios) Pontios Sextilianos [L(ucius) Pontius Sextilianus], L(oukios) Balerios Markos [L(ucius) Valerius Marcus] , A(ulos) Stalakkios [A(ulus) Stalaccius] Aphrodeisios, L(oukios) Balerios Preiskos [L(ucius) Valerius Priscus], G(aios) Kassios [G(aius) Cassius] Antiochos, G(aios) Pompeios Hilaros [G(aius) Pompeius Hilarus], L(oukios) Ioulios Rouphos [L(ucius) Julius Rufus], G(aios) Phlabios [G(aius) Flavius] Iatrodoros, M(arkos) [M(arcus)] Lukios Asklepiades, Ioulios Pouplios Sekoundos [Julius Publius Secundus], Petronios Aurelius [Petronius Aurelius] Leon, L(oukios) Beibios Keler [L(ucius) Vivius Celer], T(itos) Phlabios [T(itus) Flavius] Athenodoros, G(aios) Ioulios Loukios [G(aius) Julius Lucius] , Ancharenos Serenos [Ancharenus Serenus], L(oukios) Ioulios [L(ucius) Julius] Euphrosynos, L(oukios) Mousidios [L(ucius) Musidius] Serapion, M(arkos) Eppios Loukios [M(arcus) Eppius Lucius], M(arkos) Antonios [M(arcus) Antonius] Isokrates, P(oplios) Kornelios [P(ublius) Cornelius] Diodoros, Nemesios Gaios [Nemesius Gaius], Rouphos Stalakkios Proklos [Rufus Stalaccius Proclus]  

(b, column II) : L(oukios) Ailios Preimos [L(ucius) Aelius Primus], L(oukios) Nobios Sekoundos [L(ucius) Novius Secundus], Ailios [Aelius] Tyrannos, Ti(berios) Klaudios Satorneilos [Ti(berius) Claudius Satornilus], Heliodoros (son) of Heliodoros, Bathyllos (son) of Longos, Hippias (son) of Hippias, Aristomedes (son) of Gabios, Jason (son) of Jason, Loukis (son) of Krispos [Crispus], Zosimos (son) of Loukios [Lucius], Rouphos [Rufus] (son) of Kallippos, Ammonios (son) of Ammonios, Polianthes (son) of Polianthes, Kuranais (son) of Demetrios, Theodoros (son) of Rouphos [Rufus], Aniochos (son) of Heliodoros, Ammonis (son) of Lysippos, Ptolmaios (son) of Ptolemaios, Iatrodoros (son) of Autolykos, Euphras (son) of Euphras, Oktabis [Octavius] (son) of Preimos [Primus], Loukis [Lucius] (son) of Loukios [Lucius], Herakles (son) of Itthannuros, Markos [Marcus] (son) of Euphranor, Epikles (son) of Ioulios, Kuranais (son) of Theulogos, Heliodoros (son) of Lysippos, Loukis (son) of Alexandros, Rouphos [Rufus] (son) of Rouphos [Rufus].  

(c) : the paidonomoi and overseers, according to the ordinance of Numisius Marcellianus the most excellent proconsul, being Ioulios [Julius] Kallietes, and Ailios [Aelius] Epaphrodeitos.
