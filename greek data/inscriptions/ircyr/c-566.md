# Funerary inscription, Tomb N.66 (IRCyr2020 C.566)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρκα ια  
Τυβὶ θι  
Ἰθάκη  
Νικαϲίω  
(ἐτῶν) ογ

### English translation

Year 121, also (regnal year) 11, Tybi 19, Ithake (daughter) of Nikasios, aged 73.
