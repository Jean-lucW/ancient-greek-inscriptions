# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.549)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε  
Φαμενὼ[θ]  
βκ  
Δαμίων  
Φιλοκρά  
τευς  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 5, Phameno(th . . .) 22nd, Damon, son of Philocrates, aged 30.  

Year 5, Phamenoth 22, Damion (son) of Philokrates, aged 30.
