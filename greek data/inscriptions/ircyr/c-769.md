# Dedication to Apollo (IRCyr2020 C.769)

## Ancient Greek text

Θ[ε]ύχρηστο[ς τοῦ δεῖνα]  
ἱαριτεύων κα[...]  
ἀ ν έ [θηκεν]

### English translation

Theuchrestos [(son) of ?], serving as priest [. . .] dedicated.
