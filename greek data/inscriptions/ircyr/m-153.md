# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 M.153)

## Ancient Greek text

Νέρων Κλ[αύδι]  
[ος] θεοῦ Κλ[αυδίου]  
υἱὸς Γερμ[ανικοῦ]  
[Κ]αίσαρος υἱ[ωνὸς]  

[...]  

διὰ Λ(ουκίου) Ἀκιλίου  
Στράβωνος το[ῦ]  
ἰδίου πρεσβευτο[ῦ]  
ὅρους διακατε  
χομένους ὑπὸ [ἰ]  
διωτῶν Δήμῳ  
[Ῥ]ωμαίων ἀπο  
[κ]ατέστησεν

### English translation

(Latin) : Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus [Caesar], great grandson of Tiberius Caesar Augustus, [great-great grandson] of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, [pontifex maximus], holding tribunician power, [acclaimed imperator, consul]; by the action of Lucius Acilius Strabo, his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.  

(Greek) : Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, [great grandson of Tiberius Caesar Augustus and of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, high priest [pontifex maximus], holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, consul]; by the action of Loukios Akilios Strabon [Lucius Acilius Strabo], his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.
