# Dedication of statues (IRCyr2020 P.99)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(ᾶρκοϲ) Οὔλπιοϲ Κομίνιοϲ ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων τῇ πατρίδι

### English translation

M(arkos) Oulpios Kominios [M(arcus) Ulpius Cominius] from his private means for the Fatherland.
