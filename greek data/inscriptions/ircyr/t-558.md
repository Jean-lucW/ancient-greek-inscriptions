# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.558)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρε Χοιάχι κθ  
Δωϲ⟨ι⟩θέα Πολυ  
άρχω (ἐτῶν) ϛ

### English translation

Year 105, Choiak 29th (possibly 19th), Dosithea daughter of Polyarchos, aged 9 (possibly 5)  

Year 105, Choiach 29, Dosithea (daughter) of Polyarchos, aged 6.
