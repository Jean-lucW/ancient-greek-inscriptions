# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.641)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ργ Φα  
μενὼ⟨θ⟩ ζ  
Θαλλ[ο]ῦς  
Ἀριστοκ  
[ρ]άτους  
(ἐτῶν) ϛ

### English translation

Year 103, Phamenoth 7, Thallous (daughter) of Aristokrates, aged 6.
