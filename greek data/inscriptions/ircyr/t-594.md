# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.594)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Τυβὶ  
ε Λούκι  
ος Λούκι  
ου (ἐτῶν) ξ

### English translation

Year 2, Tybi 5, Loukios [Lucius] (son) of Loukios [Lucius], aged 60.
