# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.423)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ(αῖοϲ) Ἰούλιο[ϲ]  
Μάγνοϲ  
ἐποίηϲεν

### English translation

G(aios) Ioulios Magnos [G(aius) Julius Magnus] made (this tomb).
