# Funerary inscription for Theudas and Jason, sons of Theudas (IRCyr2020 T.267)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ[...]  
Θευδὰϲ Θευ  
δᾶ (ἐτῶν) δ καὶ Ἰ[ά]σ  
ων Θευ[δ]  
ᾶ (ἐτῶν) γ

### English translation

Year (?4 or -4) , Theudas (son) of Theudas aged 4, and Jason (son) of Theudas, aged 3.
