# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.661)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Φαωφὶ εκ  
Σεκόνδα Φ  
αβίου τοῦ  
Ἀρίστου  
(ἐτῶν) ιβ

### English translation

Year 2, Phaophi 25, Sekonda [Seconda] (daughter) of Phabios [Fabius] (son) of Aristos, aged 12.
