# i: Dedication of a statue; ii: sculptor's signature (IRCyr2020 P.176)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(ᾶρκοϲ) Οὔλπιοϲ Κομίνιοϲ  
ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων τῇ πατρίδι  

Ἀσκλεπιάδηϲ  
Ἀθηναῖοϲ ἐποίε

### English translation

a: M(arkos) Oulpios Kominios [M(arcus) Ulpius Cominius] from his private means for the Fatherland.  

b. Asklepiades, Athenian, made it.
