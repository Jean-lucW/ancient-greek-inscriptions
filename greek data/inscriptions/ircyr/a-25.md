# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 A.25)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

[δι]ὰ Λο[υκίου]  
Ἀκιλίο[υ] Στρά[βωνος]  
τοῦ ἰδί ου πρ[εσβ]  
[ε]υτοῦ [ὅ]ρο[υς δια]  
κατεχομ[ένους]  
[ὑ]πὸ ἰδιωτῶ[ν]  
[Δήμῳ Ῥ]ωμαί[ων ἀπο]  
[κα]τέ[στησεν]

### English translation

[Presumably the emperor Nero] restored. (End of Latin text) By the action of Loukios Akilios Strabon [Lucius Acilius Strabo], his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.
