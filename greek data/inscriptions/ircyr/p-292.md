# Funerary inscriptions (IRCyr2020 P.292)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) νε Τερτιὰϲ  
Λαϲθέν[ο]υϲ  
Παῦνι δ  

[...]ια  
[... Πρ]όκλα  
[...] (ἐτῶν) οε

### English translation

i: Year 55, Payni 4, Tertias (daughter) of Lasthenes.  

ii: . . .] Prokla [. . .], aged 75.
