# Funerary inscriptions (IRCyr2020 C.613)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ὀ]κταβία  
[Ἀφρ]οδεισίου  
θυγάτηρ  
Κάτυλλα  
(ἐτῶν) ιβ  

[Λ]ούκιος Ὀκτάουιος  
Ἀφροδεισίου υἱὸς  
Κάμαρς (ἐτῶν) κδ  

Πόπλιος Ὀκ  
τάϊος Φαύστου  
υἱὸς Νάστας  
(ἐτῶν) ιη

### English translation

Octavia, daughter of Aphrodisius, Catulla, aged 12, who was buried. Octavia, daughter of Aphrodisius, aged 12. [L.] Octavius, son of Publius (of the tribe of Publilia), Camars, aged 24, who was buried. [L]ucius Octavius, son of Aphrodisius, Camars, aged 24. Publius Octavius, son of Faustus, Nestes aged 18.  

(Latin) : Octavia Catulla, daughter of Aphrodisius, aged 12, lies here. (Greek) : Oktabia Katulla, daughter of Aphrodisios, aged 12.  

(Latin) : [Lucius O]ctavius Camars, son of Publius, of the tribe Publilia, aged 24, lies here. (Greek) : [L]oukios Oktaouios Kamars [Lucius Octavius Camars], son of Aphrodisios, aged 24.  

Poplios Oktaios [Publius Octavius] Nastas, son of Phaustos [Faustus], aged 18.
