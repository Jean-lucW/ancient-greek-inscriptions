# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.265)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Παίλιγνοϲ  
βετράνοϲ  
ἐτελεύτη  
ϲε ἐτῶν ν

### English translation

Year 10, Pailignos, a veteran ended his life, aged 50
