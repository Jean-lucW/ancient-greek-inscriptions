# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.437)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρζ Θώθυ γ  
Τειμοκρ[άτης ...]  
Διοδό[τω]  
(ἐτῶν) δ

### English translation

Year 107, Thoth 3, Teimokrates (son) of Diodotos, aged 4.
