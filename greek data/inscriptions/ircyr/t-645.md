# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.645)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Ἁθὺρ  
η Εὐφρο  
σόνα Ἀ  
φροδισια  
νοῦ  
(ἐτῶν) μη

### English translation

Year 5, Hathyr 8, Euphrosona (daughter) of Aphrodisianos, aged 48.
