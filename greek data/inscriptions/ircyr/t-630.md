# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.630)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κ Μεϲορὴ  
β Ἰο⟨υ⟩δὰϲ Ἰοϲ  
ήπω (ἐτῶν) ρ

### English translation

Year 20, Mesore 2, Judas (son) of Iosepos, aged 100.
