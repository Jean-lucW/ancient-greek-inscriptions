# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.279)

## Ancient Greek text

Λ(ούκιος) Πετρώνιος  
Ἐπαφρόδιτο ς  
ἑαυτῶ καὶ τοῖ ς ἰδίοις

### English translation

L(oukios) Petronios [L(ucius) Petronius] Epaphroditos, for himself and his family.
