# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.544)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Φαωφὶ ϛι  
Ἀριϲτοκράτηϲ  
Ἀρίμμα (ἐτῶν) ξγ

### English translation

Year 7, Phaophi ?th, Aristocrates, son of Arimmas, aged 63.  

Year 7, Phaophi 16, Aristokrates (son) of Arimmas, aged 63.
