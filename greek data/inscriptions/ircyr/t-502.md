# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.502)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Τυβὶ κ  
Καλλικλῆς  
Νικαίου (ἐτῶν) γκ

### English translation

Year 2, Tubi 20th, Callimede, daughter of Nic . . .  

Year 2, Tybi 20, Kallikles (son) of Nikaios, aged 23.
