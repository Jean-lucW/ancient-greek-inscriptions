# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.480)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Φαρμο  
υθὶ ει Ἀρι  
στοκράτ  
ης Μενε  
λάου (ἐτῶν) ξ

### English translation

Year 6, Pharmouthi 15, Aristokles (son) of Menelaos, aged 60.
