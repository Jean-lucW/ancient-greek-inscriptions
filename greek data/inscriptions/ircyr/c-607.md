# Funerary inscriptions (IRCyr2020 C.607)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρκ Παχὼν ε  
Ῥοῦφος Λ(ουκίου) Ἑλβίου  
Ῥούφου υἱὸς  
γραμματεὺς  
πόλεος Κυρήνης  
ἐνθάδε ἐτάφη (ἐτῶν) πϛ

### English translation

Year 12, Pachon 5: Rouphos [Rufus], son of L(oukios) Helbios Rouphos [L(ucius) Helvius Rufus], Secretary of the city of Cyrene, was buried here, aged 76.  

Year 122, Payni 27: K(ointos) Ponpeios Pollion [Q(uintus) Pompeius Pollio], aged 62.  

Year 123, Mecheir 24: Klaudia Rouphilla [Claudia Rufilla], aged 36.  

Year 133, Thoth 27: P(oplios) Maikilios Sekondos [P(ublius) Maecilius Secundus], aged 14.  

Year 130, Phaophi 4: G(aios) Stalakkios Pollion [G(aius) Stalaccius Pollio], aged 8.  

Kl(audia) [Cl(audia)] Aphrodeisia, aged 16.  

Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Demetrios, aged 20.
