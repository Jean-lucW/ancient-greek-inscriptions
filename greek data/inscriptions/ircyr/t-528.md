# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.528)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Μ  
εϲορ  
ὴ τρί  
τη Ϲω  
γένη  
ϲ Ἰωϲή  
πω εἰ  
κοϲιτ  
έϲερα  
[ἐτῶν]

### English translation

Year 2, Mesori 8th, Tri . . . te, daughter of Sogen(es) . . .  

Year 2, Mesore 3, Sogenes (son) of Iosepos, [aged] 24.
