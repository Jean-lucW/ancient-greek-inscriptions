# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.517)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) πθ Ἐπειφὶ [...]  
Θεόδοτος  
Πτολεμαίου  
(ἐτῶν) π  
(ἔτους) δ Ἁθὺ[ρ ...]  
Καλλικλεία  
Σωγένους  
(ἐτῶν) ελ

### English translation

Year ? . . . ., Theodotos son of Ptolemaios, aged 40.  

Year 4, Athur, P . . ., Pheid(i)as, Philip (. . . ), sons (?) of Sogenes.  

Year 89, Epeiph ?, Theodotos (son) of Ptolemaios, aged 40.  

Year 4, Hathyr ?, Kalliklea (daughter) of Sogenes, aged 35.
