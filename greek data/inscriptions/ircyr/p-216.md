# Gladiator memorial (IRCyr2020 P.216)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἱππομέδων  

Ἱππομέδων ὁ πρεὶν  
Καρπόφοροϲ οὗ πυ(γμαὶ) θ  
πυ(γμαὶ) θ

### English translation

(a) Hippomedon  

(b) Hippomedon, who was formerly Karpophoros, who had nine fights. Nine fights.
