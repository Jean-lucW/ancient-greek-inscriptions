# Dedication of an image of Artemis (IRCyr2020 C.220)

## Ancient Greek text

Τὴν Ἄρτε  
μιν ἐκ τῶν  
ἀναθημάτω[ν]  
τῶν τεθέν  
τω⟨ν?⟩ ⟨αὐτῇ?⟩ ⟨Νου?⟩  
μί⟨σιος?⟩

### English translation

The statue of Artemis, from the dedications which have been made [to her, Nu]mi[sius . . .
