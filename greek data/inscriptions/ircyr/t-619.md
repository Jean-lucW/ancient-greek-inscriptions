# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.619)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) δ Φ[αρ]  
μουθὶ ε  
Κρίτων  
Ϲίμων  
οϲ (ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 4, Pharmouthi 5, Kriton (son) of Simon, aged 50.
