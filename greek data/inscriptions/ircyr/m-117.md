# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.117)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) τιε ἐφ’ἱερέ  
ωϲ Φίλω  
νοϲ το͂ Ἀγ⟨α⟩  
θίνο Αἰγλά  
νωρ Ἀπο  
[λλ]οδώρο  
[(ἐτῶν)] μ

### English translation

Year 315: in the priesthood of Philon the (son) of Agathinos, Aiglanor (son) of Apollodoros, aged 40
