# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.428)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ [Ἐ]πικλ  
ῆϲ Φίλωνο  
ϲ  
(ἐτῶν) ν Πα  
οῖνι δ

### English translation

Year 7, Epikles (son) of Philon, aged 50, Payni 4
