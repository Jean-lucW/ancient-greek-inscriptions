# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.460)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Θώθυ α  
Ἀνδροϲθέ  
νης [...]ο  
Φιλαργύρο[υ]  
(ἐτῶν) κγ

### English translation

Year 11, Thothy 1, Androsthenes ?son of Philargyros, aged 23.  

Year 11, Thoth 1, Androsthenes [. . (son?)] of Philargyros, aged 23.
