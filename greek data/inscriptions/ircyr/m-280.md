# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.280)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρμε Ἀριστοκ  
λεία Ἁγησισ  
τράτω (ἐτῶν) ιδ

### English translation

Year 145: Aristokleia (daughter) of Hagesistratos, aged fourteen.
