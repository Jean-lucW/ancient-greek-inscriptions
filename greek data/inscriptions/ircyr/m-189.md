# Dedication to Iatros and Iaso (IRCyr2020 M.189)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰατρῶι Ἰαϲο͂ι θεοῖϲ ϲωτῆρϲι  
Μ(ᾶρκοϲ) Ἀντώνιοϲ Ϲεκοῦνδοϲ ἀνέθηκε

### English translation

Dedicated to Iatros and Iaso, saviour gods, by Marcus Antonius Secundus.
