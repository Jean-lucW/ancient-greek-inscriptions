# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.524)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Μεσορ  
ὴ θι  
Θεύδω  
ρος Πτυλ  
μα[ί]ου  
(ἐτῶν) λγ

### English translation

Year 10, Mesore 19, Theudoros son of Ptulmaios, aged 33.  

Year 10, Mesore 19, Theudoros (son) of Ptulmaios, aged 33.
