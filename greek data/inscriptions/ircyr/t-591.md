# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.591)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Τυβὶ γ  
Βάχις  
Καλαμέ  
ρας (ἐτῶν) κδ

### English translation

Year 5, Tybi 3, Bachis (son) of Kalamera, aged 24.
