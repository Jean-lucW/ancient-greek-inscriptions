# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.696)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Χοιάχι  
[...]α Ματ[ί]η  
νος Θύω  
νος (ἐτῶν) νζ

### English translation

Year 5, Choiak 11 (or 21) , Matienos [Matienus] (son) of Thuon, aged 57.
