# Military presence inscription (IRCyr2020 M.18)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ο Θεο[...]ιας Ἀρι[σ]τοδή[μ]ω  
κεντυρ[ίω]ν [...]ησλυακος  
ἥκω

### English translation

Year 70, Theo[..]as (son) of Aristodemos, a centurion [. . . ), Ι came here.
