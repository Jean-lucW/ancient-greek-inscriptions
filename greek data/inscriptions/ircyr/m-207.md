# Dedication (IRCyr2020 M.207)

## Ancient Greek text

Τι(βέριοϲ) Κλαύδιοϲ Ἴϲτροϲ τοῖϲ θεοῖϲ  
ἀπέδωκε τὴν θυϲίαν.

### English translation

Ti(berios) Klaudios [Ti(berius) Claudius] Istros gave the sacrifice for the gods.
