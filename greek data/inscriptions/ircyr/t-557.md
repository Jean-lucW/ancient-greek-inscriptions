# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.557)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δρ Τυβὶ α  
Θευδώρα  
Γεμέλω  
(ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 4, Tubbi 30th, Theodora, daughter of Gemelos, aged 50.  

Year 104, Tybi 1, Theudora (daughter) of Gemelos [Gemellus], aged 50.
