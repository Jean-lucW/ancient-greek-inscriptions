# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.371)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Ἐπα  
γομέν  
ων ε Ἀ  
ρίμανθ  
ος Καλ  
λισθέν  
ους (ἐτῶν) θ

### English translation

Year 4, intercalary day 5, Arimanthos son of Callisthenes, aged 9.  

Year 4, intercalary day 5, Arimanthos (son) of Kallisthenes, aged 9.
