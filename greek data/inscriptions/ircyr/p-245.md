# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.245)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Λύκα Ϲταλακ  
κίου (ἐτῶν) με

### English translation

Year 12, Lyka (daughter) of Stalakkios, aged 45.
