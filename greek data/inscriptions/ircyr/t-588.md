# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.588)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Ἁθὺρ  
ιβ Διδύ  
μη Κλυ  
ους (ἐτῶν) λη

### English translation

Year 7, Hathyr 12, Didyme (daughter) of Klyes, aged 38.
