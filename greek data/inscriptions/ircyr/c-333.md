# Restoration inscription (IRCyr2020 C.333)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(ᾶρκος) Ἀντώνιος Μ(άρκῳ) Ἀν[τω]νίωι τοῦ Φλάμμαι Γεμέλλου υἱὸς  
Μάξιμος ἱαρεύς

### English translation

M(arkos) Antonios Maximos[M(arcus) Antonius Maximus], son of M(arkos) Antonios Gemellos [M(arcus) Antonius Gemellus] (son) of Flamma [Flamma], priest
