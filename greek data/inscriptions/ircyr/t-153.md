# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.153)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἐμισαθι[ς]  

Μάχ  
αρος  

μδρ  

Λῦσις  

Ἴασμις  

Θαρσίπολις  
Ἰτθαννυ  
ρας (φίλος)

### English translation

(i) Emisathis. (ii) Macharos. (iii) (monogram, undeciphered) (iv) Lysis. (v) Iasmis. (vi) Tharsipolis, Itthannyras, friends.
