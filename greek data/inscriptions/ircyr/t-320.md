# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.320)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) α Χοιάχι  
δκ Καλ  
λοῦς Εὐ  
[φρ]άνορος  
(ἐτῶν) α [κ]αὶ μη  
νῶν ε

### English translation

Year 1, Choiak 24, Kallous (daughter) of Euphranor, aged 1 year, 5 months.
