# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.230)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Βόλχαϲ  
ἑαυτῷ  
καὶ τοῖϲ  
ἰδίοιϲ

### English translation

Year 3. Bolchas, for himself and his family.
