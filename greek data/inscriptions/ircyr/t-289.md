# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.289)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) [...]β Θώθυ  
α Νύων Ἐ  
παγάθω  
(ἐτῶν) β

### English translation

Year -2, Thoth 1, Nuon (son) of Epagathos, aged 2.
