# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.349)

## Ancient Greek text

Καλλίνικοϲ ἔτη ιε  
Τιμ[ῶ]χοϲ ἐτῶν ι

### English translation

Kallinikos, aged 15. Timochos aged 10
