# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.595)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Φαμε  
νὼτ ι Ἀλ  
εξᾶϲ Γ  
αίω  
(ἐτῶν) μϛ

### English translation

Year 10, Phamenoth 10, Alexas (son) of Gaios [Gaius], aged 46.
