# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.478)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Μεσο  
ρὴ δ Ἀνδρ  
οσθένης  
Λύσιος (ἐτῶν) ξη

### English translation

Year 6, Mesore 4, Androsthenes (son) of Lysis, aged 68.
