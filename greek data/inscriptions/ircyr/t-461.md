# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.461)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) α Χοι  
ὰκ ι Κα  
λαμέρ  
α Νικία  
(ἐτῶν) ιη

### English translation

Year 1, Choiak 10, Kalamera (daughter) of Nikias, aged 18.
