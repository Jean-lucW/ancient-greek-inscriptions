# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.462)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ριγ  
Μεσορὴ  
β  
Εὐφράν  
ωρ Εὐμ  
μάχου (ἐτῶν) κϛ

### English translation

Year 113, Mesore 2, Euphranor (son) of Eummachos, aged 26.
