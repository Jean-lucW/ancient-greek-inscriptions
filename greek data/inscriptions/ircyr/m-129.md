# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.129)

## Ancient Greek text

Καικιλία Κοιθῶ ἐτῶν μ  
ἔτους δεκάτου

### English translation

Kaikilia (daughter) of Koithas, aged 40. Year ten.
