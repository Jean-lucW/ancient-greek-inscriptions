# Dedication to Iatros and Iaso (IRCyr2020 C.756)

## Ancient Greek text

[τῶ? Ἀπόλλω?]νος ἱεριτεύων  
[...]ρ Φιλίννα  
[Ἰατρῶι κα]ὶ Ἰασῶι  
[τὸν? βωμὸν? ἱ]δρύσατο

### English translation

While being priest of Apollo [. . . -]r (son) of Philinnas established [?the altar] for Iatros and Iaso.
