# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.476)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) η [Ἐπιφὶ]  
γ Εὐ[τέρ]  
πη Θ[ευδό]  
του (ἐτῶν) κβ

### English translation

Year 8, Epeiph 3, Euterpe (daughter) of Theudotos, aged 22.
