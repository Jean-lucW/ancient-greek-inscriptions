# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.121)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἴσχις  
Διδυμ[...]  

Ἰάϲων  

Ἀρχίβιος  

Χυλδαῖος  

Τιμοκράτη  
[ς]

### English translation

(i) Ischis (son) of Didum[os]. (ii) Iason. (iii) Archibios. (iv) Chuldaios. (v) Timokrates.
