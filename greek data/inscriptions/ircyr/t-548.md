# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.548)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρα Φαρμ  
οθὶ ιη  
Μεγόι Ἰάλ  
μιοϲ (ἐτῶν) αι

### English translation

Year 11, Pharmuthi 18th, ?, ?, aged 40.  

Year 101, Pharmouthi 18, Megoi (daughter) of Ialmis, aged 11.
