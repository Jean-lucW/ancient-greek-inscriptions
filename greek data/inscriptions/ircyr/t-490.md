# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.490)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιδ Ἄριϲ  
τιϲ Δω  
ϲιθέου  
(ἐτῶν) νε

### English translation

Year 14, Aristis (daughter) of Dositheos, aged 55.
