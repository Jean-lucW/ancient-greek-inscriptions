# Building inscription for Spring of Kura (IRCyr2020 C.316)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Διονύσιος Σωτᾶ  
ἱερειτεύων τὰν κράναν  
ἐπεσκεύασε

### English translation

Year 13. Dionysios (son) of Sotas, serving as priest, restored the spring.
