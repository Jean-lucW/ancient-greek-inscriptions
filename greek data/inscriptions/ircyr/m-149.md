# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.149)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρμα Θώ  
θυ ζ Νικα  
ϲὼ Παν  
χάρου (ἐτῶν)  
κη

### English translation

Year 141, Thoth 7: Nikaso (daughter) of Panchares, aged 28.
