# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.326)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ϛ Παχ  
ὼν η Γρ  
άνιοϲ  
Ἀντων  
ίου (ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 6, Pachon 8, Granios [Granius] (son) of Antonios [Antonius], aged 30
