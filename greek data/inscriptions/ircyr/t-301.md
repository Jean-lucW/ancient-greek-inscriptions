# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.301)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ [Χοι]άκι κ  
Μάριος Ἀ  
ριστοκλ  
[έους] (ἐτῶν) κα

### English translation

Year 6, Choiak 20, Marios [Marius] (son) of Aristokles, aged 21.
