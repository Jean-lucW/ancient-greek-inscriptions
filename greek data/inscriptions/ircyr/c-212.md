# Honours for Arsinoa daughter of Zenion (IRCyr2020 C.212)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρσινόαν Ζηνίωνος  
Κυραναῖοι.

### English translation

Arsinoa (daughter) of Zenion: the Cyrenaeans (erected the statue of her)
