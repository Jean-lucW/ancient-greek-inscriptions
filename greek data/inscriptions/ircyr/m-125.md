### English translation

(a) : [Nero Claudius, son of deified Claudius, grandson of] Ge[rmanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius] Caesar Augustus and of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, high priest [pontifex maximus], holding tribunician power [acclaimed imperator, consul]; (b) by the action of Loukios Akilios Strabon [Lucius Acilius Strabo], his personal envoy, he restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.
