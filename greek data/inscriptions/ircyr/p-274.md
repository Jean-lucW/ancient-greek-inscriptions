# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.274)

## Ancient Greek text

Εἰρήνα  
Μάρκ[ο]υ  
ἐτελ[εύ]  
τηϲε  
ἐτῶ[ν ...]  
ἑξή[κον]  
τ[α]

### English translation

Eirena (daughter) of Markos [Marcus] died, aged 60.
