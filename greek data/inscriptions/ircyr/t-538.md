# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.538)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιε Φαρ  
μουθὶ η  
Φίλιππο  
[ς] Φιλίππου  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 15, Pharmuthi 8th, Philippos, son of Philippos, aged 20  

Year 15, Pharmouthi 8, Philippos (son) of Philippos, aged 20.
