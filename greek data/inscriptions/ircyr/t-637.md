# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.637)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους)] κε Μεχ  
[ὶρ] ζ Ἁγ  
[εμόν]α Ασ  
[...]ου  
(ἐτῶν) ιδ

Year 25, Mechir 7, Hagemona daughter of . . . ?, aged 14.
