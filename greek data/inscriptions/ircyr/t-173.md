# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.173)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀρίσταρχος  

[...]πευγ[...]  

(ἔτους) μθ Ἀφθάν  

Δαμοκλῆς ἔθ[ο]  
ς Ἀγαθοκλ[εῖ]  

Τηλο[κ]λῆ[ς ...]νασ[...]

### English translation

Aristarchos. (ii) (Not usefully translatable) . (iii) Year 49, Aphthan. (iv) Damokles, customary companion of Agathokles. (v) Telokles. (vi) (Not usefully translatable) .
