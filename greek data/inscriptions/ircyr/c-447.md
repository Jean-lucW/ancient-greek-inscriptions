# Fragmentary honours (IRCyr2020 C.447)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]άρχω  
[... χάριν? κ]αὶ τᾶς ποτὶ  
[τὰς θεὰς εὐ]σεβέας  
[Κυρ]άναιοι

### English translation

. (son or daughter?) of [. . . -]archos [for their ?virtue] and their piety towards [the goddesses (?gods)], (set up by) the Cyrenaeans.
