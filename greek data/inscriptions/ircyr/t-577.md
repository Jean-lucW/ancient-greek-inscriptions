# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.577)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Τυβὶ  
ζκ [...]  
Λεαίνα  
Ἀρίμμα  
(ἐτῶν) ιϛ

### English translation

Year 3, Tybi 27, Leaina (daughter) of Arimmas, aged 16.
