# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.518)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρϛ Μεσο  
ρὴ ι  
Πτολεμαῖ  
ος Θεοδό  
του (ἐτῶν) π

### English translation

Year 2, . . . . , Ptolemaios, son of Theodotus, aged 40.  

Year 106, Mesore 10, Ptolemaios (son) of Theodotos, aged 80.
