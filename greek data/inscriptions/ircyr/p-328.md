# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 P.328)

## Ancient Greek text

Καπίτ(ων)  

Σ έκον[δ]  
ος Φλαβ[ί]  
ου ❦

### English translation

(i) Kapiton [Capito]. (ii) Sekondos [Secundus] (son) of Phlabios [Flavius]
