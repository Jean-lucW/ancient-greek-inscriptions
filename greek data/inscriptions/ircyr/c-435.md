# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 C.435)

## Ancient Greek text

[Νέρων] Κλαύδιος  
Θεοῦ Κλαυδί  
oυ υἱὸς Γερ  
μανικοῦ Καίσαρος  
ὑωνὸς Τι(βερίου) Καίσαρος  
Σεβαστοῦ καὶ θεοῦ  
Σεβαστοῦ ἔγγο  
νος

### English translation

(Latin) : . . .] restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.  

(Greek) : [Nero] Claudius son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius Caesar Augustus and of deified Augustus, [. . .
