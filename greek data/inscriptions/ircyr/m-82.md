# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.82)

## Ancient Greek text

ἔτουϲ ι Ἐπιφὶ κε  
Ϲεκόνδα Ἀντιόχ  
ω (ἐτῶν) ιβ

### English translation

Year 1, Epeiph 25, Sekonda [Secunda] (daughter) of Antiochos, aged 12.
