# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.246)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιϛ Χοιάχι κβ Πόπλιοϲ Ϲαβεί  
νω  
ἐτῶν  
κβ

### English translation

Year 16, Choiak 22 , Poplios [Publius] (son) of Sabeinos [Sabinus], aged 22.
