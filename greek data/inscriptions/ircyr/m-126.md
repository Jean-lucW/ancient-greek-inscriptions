# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.126)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεύδυτ[οϲ ...]χρ[...]  
καὶ Δειθ[... Ἡ]ράκω[νοϲ]  
ἐπύϲεν ἑατῦϲ [καὶ τῦϲ] ἑ  
ατῶν [...]

### English translation

Theudotos [. . . -chr- . . . and Deith[- (son/s) of] Herakon set this up for themselves and their families.
