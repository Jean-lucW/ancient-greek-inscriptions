# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.479)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Χοιά  
χι κα Ἀσ  
πασία Λ  
ουκίου  
(ἐτῶν) αι

### English translation

Year 11, Choiak 21, Aspasia (daughter) of Loukios [Lucius], aged 11.
