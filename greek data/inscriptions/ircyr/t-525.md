# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.525)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κα Μεσορὴ κδ  
Σωγένης  
Δωσιθέου  
(ἐτῶν) ξδ

### English translation

(30) Phamenoth 24th, (S)ogenes, son of Dositheos, aged 64.  

(35) . . .. , Mesori . . . , Sogenes, son of Dositheos, aged 64.  

Year 21, Mesore 24, Sogenes (son) of Dositheos, aged 64.
