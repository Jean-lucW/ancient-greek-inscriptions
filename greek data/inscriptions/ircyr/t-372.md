# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.372)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Φαω  
φὶ δ Ἀρ  
ταις Κα  
λ λισθ  
ένους  
(ἐτῶν) η

### English translation

Year 9, Phaophi 4, Artais (?) daughter of Callisthenes, aged 8.  

Year 9, Phaophi 4, Artais (daughter) of Kallisthenes, aged 8.
