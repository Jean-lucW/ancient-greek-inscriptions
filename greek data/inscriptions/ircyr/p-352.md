# Ephebic honours (IRCyr2020 P.352)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια  
Πεντίας  
[Ἀ]γαθοκλέους  
[?] φίλος Γ(άιον)❦  
Πάκκιον [Π]αιλι  
γνὁ[ν]

### English translation

Year 11, Pentias, (son) of Agathokles, (his) friend (honoured?) Gaios Pakkios Paelignos [Gaius Paccius Paelignus].
