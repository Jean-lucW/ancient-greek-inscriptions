# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.276)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ργι Παῦ  
νι δ  
Σελανία  
Δημητ[ρ]  
ίω (ἐτῶν) κγ

### English translation

Year 113, Payni 4, Selania (daughter) of Demetrios, aged 23.
