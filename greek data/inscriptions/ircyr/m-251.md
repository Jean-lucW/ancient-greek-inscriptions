# Boundary marker (IRCyr2020 M.251)

## Ancient Greek text

Νέρων Κλαύδιος θεοῦ  
Κλαυδίου υἱὸς Γερ[μ]α[νι]  
κοῦ Καίσαρος υἱωνὸς Τι(βερίου)

### English translation

(Latin) : Nero Claudius son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great-great grandson of deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, pontifex maximus, holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, consul, , by (the action of) Lucius Acilius Strabo his personal envoy, restored to the Roman People the boundaries [of the estates] which had been occupied by private persons.  

(Greek) : Nero Claudius son of deified Claudius, grandson of Germanicus Caesar, great grandson of Tiberius Caesar and deified Augustus, Caesar Augustus Germanicus, high priest, holding tribunician power, acclaimed imperator, consul, by (the action of) [Lucius Acilius] Strabo [his personal] envoy, [restored to the] Roman People [the boundaries of the estates] which had been occupied [by private persons].
