# Honours for Timareta (IRCyr2020 C.284)

## Ancient Greek text

Τειμαρέταν Κ[αρνήδα ...] ιρ[...]  
Φλά[μ]μα ἱαριτε[ύων] τὰ[ν ...]

### English translation

Teimareta (daughter) of Karnedas [. . .] Phlamma, serving as priest, (set up the statue) [. . .]
