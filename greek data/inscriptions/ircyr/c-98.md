### English translation

Year 90 and year 91, in the priesthood of Istros and the priesthood of Asklapos, the (sons) of Philiskos. The nomophylakes who held office together, Lysis (son) of P(ublius) Quintilius Apollos, Elazar (son) of Jason, Gemellos (son) of Alopex, Damis (son) of Arimmas, Thrasymachos (son) of Lysimachos, [. . .] (son) of Aiglanor, [. . .] (son) of Hyratthis, [. . .] (son) of Teimonax, [. . . -] (son) of Agathonemos.
