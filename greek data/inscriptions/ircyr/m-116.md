# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.116)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ Χο  
ιάχι κδ  
Ἅβρολ  
α Λουκ  
ίω (ἐτῶν) ξ

### English translation

Year 12, Choiak 24: Habrola (daughter) of Loukios [Lucius], aged 60
