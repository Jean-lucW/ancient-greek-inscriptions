# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.435)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρζ Θώθυ γ  
Ἄριστις  
Ἀριστοδ  
[ά]μω (ἐτῶν) κε

### English translation

Year 107, Thoth 3, Aristis (child) of Aristodamos, aged 25.
