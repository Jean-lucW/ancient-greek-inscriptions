# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.568)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Μεϲορὴ  
δι Ἀρ[ϲ]ινόα  
Παυ[ϲ]ανί  
α (ἐτῶν) μ

### English translation

Year 4, Mesore 14, Arsinoa (daughter) of Pausanias, aged 40.
