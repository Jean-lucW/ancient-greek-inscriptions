# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.343)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Πα  
ῦνι ιε  
Διόδοτ  
ος Ὑράτ  
θιος  
(ἐτῶν) μδ

### English translation

Year 11, Payni 15, Diodotos (son) of Hyratthis, aged 44.
