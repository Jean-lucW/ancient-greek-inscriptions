# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.575)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ηϙ Φοα  
φὶ ε Μᾶρ  
κος Ἀρί  
[μ]μα (ἐτῶν) ϛ

### English translation

Year 98, Phaophi 5, Markos [Marcus] (son) of Arimmas, aged 6.
