# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.390)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) κθ Με[χὶ]ρ ι λ[... Βι]  
λλιηνω (ἐτῶν) β [...]

### English translation

Year 29, Mecheir 10, L- (son) of Billienos [Billienus], aged 2, 12 or a multiple of 10 + 2
