# Honours for Claudia Euporiane (IRCyr2020 P.146)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλ(αυδίαν) Εὐποριανὴν  
τὴν καὶ  
Κλαυδιανὴν  
Μ(ᾶρκος) Ἀντώνιος  
Φλαβιανὸς  
τὴν φιλότεκνο[ν]  
σύνβ[ιον]

### English translation

Kl(audia) [Cl(audia)] Euporiana, also known as Klaudiana: M(arkos) Antonios Phlabianos [M(arcus) Antonius Flavianus] (honoured) his child-loving wife.
