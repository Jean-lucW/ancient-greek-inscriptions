# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.465)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Θώθυ  
ζι Διονυ  
σία Ἰά  
σονος  
(ἐτῶν) λβ

### English translation

Year 11, Thoth 17, Dionysia (daughter) of Jason, aged 32.
