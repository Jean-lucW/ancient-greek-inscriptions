# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.127)

## Ancient Greek text

Φίλιπποϲ Ἡράκωνοϲ  
καὶ Εὐφρώ[...]  
ἐπύηϲαϲ  
ἡατοῦϲ

### English translation

Philippos (son) of Herakon, and Euphro [. . .] set this up for themselves.
