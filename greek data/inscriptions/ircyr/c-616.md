# Funerary inscription for Ktesikles (IRCyr2020 C.616)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἔ]τουϲ ι[...]  
Κτηϲικλ  
ῆϲ Κτηϲικ  
λεῦϲ  
ἐτῶν ο

### English translation

Ctesicle (son) of Ctesicle, aged 50  

Year ?16. Ktesikles (son) of Ktesikles, aged 70
