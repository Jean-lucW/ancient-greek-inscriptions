# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.531)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ριβ μην  
ὶ Μεχεὶρ ε  
Ϲεραφίω  
ν Θευδώ  
ρω (ἐτῶν) ιγ

### English translation

Year 112, month of Mecheir 5th, Serpion son of Theodoros, ?.  

Year 112, Mecheir 5, Seraphion (son) of Theudoros, aged 13.
