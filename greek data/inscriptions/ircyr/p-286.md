# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.286)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰουλία Πρόκλα  
ἐπόηϲεν ἑαυτῇ  
καὶ τοῖϲ αὑτῆϲ

### English translation

Ιουlia Proκla made this for herself and her family.
