# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.527)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Θώθ  
[υ] γ Ἁ  
γηϲιτ⟨ί⟩μ  
α Πτυλᾶ  
(ἐτῶν) οα

### English translation

Year 3, Thoth 3, ?, daughter of Ptulas, aged 71.  

Year 3, Thoth 3, Hagesitima (daughter) of Ptylas, aged 71.
