# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.546)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Ἐπι  
φὶ γ Ἀρί  
μμαϲ  
Ἀριϲτο  
κράτους  
(ἐτῶν) ο

### English translation

Year 2, Epiphi 3rd (possibly Epiph 13th), Arimmas, son of Aristocrates, aged 9.  

Year 2, Epeiph 3, Arimmas (son) of Aristokrates, aged 70.
