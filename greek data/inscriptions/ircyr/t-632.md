# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.632)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Μεχὶ[ρ]  
ηι Ζεῦξις  
Ἀμύντα  
(ἐτῶν) μ

### English translation

Year 5, Mecheir 18, Zeuxis (son) of Amyntas, aged 40.
