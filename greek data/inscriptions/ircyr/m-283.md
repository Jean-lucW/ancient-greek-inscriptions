# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.283)

## Ancient Greek text

[...άν]  
δροϲ Λουκίου  
ἐτῶν ἐτελεύ  
τηϲε δέκα ὀκτ  
ώ

### English translation

[...]-andros (son) of Loukios [Lucius] died, aged 18.
