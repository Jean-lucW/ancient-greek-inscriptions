# Dedication (IRCyr2020 P.309)

## Ancient Greek text

Μᾶρκος Αὐρήλι  
ος Γουνθας σό  
σαντι σωθ[εὶς]  
ἀνέθηκα

### English translation

Marcus Aurelius Gounthassos, in exchange for being saved, I set (this) up  

I, Markos Aurelios Gounthas [Marcus Aurelius], having been rescued, dedicated (this) to Him who saved me.
