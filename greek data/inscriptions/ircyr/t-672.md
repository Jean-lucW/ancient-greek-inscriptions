# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.672)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) β Μεϲορὴ  
κζ Πολλία  
Φιλοῦϲ (ἐτῶν) ιε

### English translation

Year 2, Mesore 27, Pollia Philous, aged 15.
