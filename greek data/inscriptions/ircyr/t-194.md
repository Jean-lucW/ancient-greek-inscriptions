# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.194)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ιθ Εὐβ  
ουλίδα  

(ἔτουϲ) κα  
Ὁνωρ[ᾶ]  
τοϲ Ὁν  
ωρ[ά]του

### English translation

i: Year 19, of Euboulidas. ii: Year 21. Onoratos [Honoratus] (son) of Onoratos [Honoratus].
