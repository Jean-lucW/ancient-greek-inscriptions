# Dedication of a statue of Dionysos and a shrine (IRCyr2020 C.498)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλ(αυδία) · Βενόστα · Κλ(αυδίου) · Καρτισθένου[ς]  
Μελίορος θυγάτηρ  
Διόνυσον ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων  
σὺν τῷ ναῷ❦

### English translation

Claudia Venusta, daughter of Claudius Kartisthenes Melior, gave the Dionysos from her own funds, with the temple.  

Kl(audia) Benosta [Cl(audia) Venusta], daughter of Kl(audios) [Cl(audius)] Kartisthenes Melior (gave the) Dionysos from her own funds, with the temple.
