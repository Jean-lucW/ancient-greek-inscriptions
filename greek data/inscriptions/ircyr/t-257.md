# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.257)

## Ancient Greek text

Τ(ίτου) Ϲεϲτίου Θεοδώρου  
Λούπου βωμός. Κλ(αυδία) Φαυ  
στῖνα ἡ μήτηρ ἀνέθηκ[ε]  
(ἐτῶν) κθ

### English translation

Altar of T(itos) Sestios Loupos [T(itus) Sestius Lupus] (son) of Theodoros. Kl(audia) Phaustina [Claudia Faustina] , his mother, dedicated (it). (He was) aged 29.
