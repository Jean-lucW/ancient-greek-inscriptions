# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.543)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Ἁθὺρ  
β Ἀρίμμ  
ας Ἰούδα  
(ἐτῶν) κα

### English translation

Year 11, Athur 2nd, Arimmas, son of Judah, aged 21  

Year 11, Hathyr 2, Arimmas (son) of Ioudas, aged 21.
