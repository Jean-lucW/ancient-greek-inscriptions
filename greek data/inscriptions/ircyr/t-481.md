# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.481)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιγ Παῦ  
νι ε Κ⟨λ⟩ευ  
πάτρα Θ  
ευγίτον  
ο[ς] (ἐτῶν) οε

### English translation

Year 13, Payni 5, Kleupatra (daughter) of Theugiton, aged 75.
