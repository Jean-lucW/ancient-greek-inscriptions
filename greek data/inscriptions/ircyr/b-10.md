# Invocation (IRCyr2020 B.10)

## Ancient Greek text

[εὐ]τυχῶς Κοσμ[ι]  
κῷ καὶ τοῖς σὺν  
αὐτῷ

### English translation

With good fortune for Kosmikos and those with him.
