# Funerary inscription for Astynphidas (IRCyr2020 M.77)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ηι  
Ἀϲτυνφί  
δαϲ ἐτῶν  
οε

### English translation

Year 18, Astynphilas (or Astynphidas) , aged 75 years.
