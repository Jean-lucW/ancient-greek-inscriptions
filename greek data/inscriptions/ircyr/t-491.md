# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.491)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Τυ  
βὶ κ Σε  
κόνδα  
Ἔρωτος  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 3, Tubi 2nd, Sephoris, daughter of Abeos, aged 35  

Year 3, Tybi 20, Sekonda [Secunda] (daughter) of Eros, aged 35.
