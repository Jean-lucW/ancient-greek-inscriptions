# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.128)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ(άιον) Κλ(αύδιον) Οὐαλερια  
νὸν Νεμωνι  
[α]νόν ὁ πα  
τὴρ Γ(άιος) Κλ(αύδιος) Ἀκύ  
λαϲ Νίκαιοϲ

### English translation

G(aios) Kl(audios) Oualerianos Nemonianos [G(aius) Cl(audius) Valerianus Nemonianus]: his father G(aios) Kl(audios) Akulas [G(aius) Cl(audius) Aquila] Nikaios (set this up)
