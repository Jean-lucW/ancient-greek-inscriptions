# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.382)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) λδ  
[Χοιὰ]χ η Βι  
[λλι]ήναϲ Ἱπ  
[π]ονίκαϲ  
(ἐτῶν) μβ

### English translation

Year 34, Choiak 8, (tomb) of Billiena Hipponica, aged 42.  

Year 34, Choiak 8, (tomb) of Billiena Hipponika, aged 42.
