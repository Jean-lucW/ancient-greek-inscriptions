# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.523)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Μεχεὶρ [...]  
Ἁγεμόνα  
Ϲωγένευϲ  
(ἐτῶν) λγ

### English translation

Year 1?, . . . . . ? of Sogenes, aged 33.  

Year 10, Mecheir ?, Hagemona (daughter) of Sogenes, aged 33.
