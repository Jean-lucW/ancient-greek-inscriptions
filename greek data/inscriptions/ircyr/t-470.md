# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.470)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Θώθυ  
κ Ἁγεμό  
ν[α] Ῥού  
[φου] (ἐτῶν) ιϛ

### English translation

Year 2, Thoth 20, Hagemona (daughter) of Rouphos [Rufus], aged 16.
