# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.585)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Θώθυ  
βκ Νεί  
κων Ἀ  
πολλω  
νίου (ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 3, Thoth 22, Neikon (son) of Apollonios, aged 30.
