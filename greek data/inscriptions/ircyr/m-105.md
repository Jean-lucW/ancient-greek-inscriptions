# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.105)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ  
Ἁθὺρ ιη  
Θεύδωρ  
ος Παραι  
βάτα (ἐτῶν) νε

### English translation

Year 12, Hathyr 18: Theudoros (son) of Paraibatas, aged 55.
