# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.415)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Μάκε  
ρ Μάρκω  
ἔζησεν ἔ  
τη λβ

### English translation

Year 9, Maker [Macer] (son) of Markos [Marcus], lived 32 (years).
