# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.374)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ [Ἐ]π[α]γομέ  
νων β Ἱππο  
νίκα Θεοδ[ό]  
του [(ἐτῶν)] κα

### English translation

Year 4, intercalary day 2, Hipponika daughter of Theodotos, aged 21.  

Year 4, intercalary day 2, Hipponika (daughter) of Theodotos, aged 21.
