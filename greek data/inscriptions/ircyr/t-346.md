# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.346)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Λονγε[ῖ]  
νοϲ Πτυ  
λεμαίου  
(ἐτῶν) μϛ  
Φαωφὶ β

### English translation

Year 4, Longinos [Longinus] (son) of Ptolemaios, aged 46. Phaophi 2.
