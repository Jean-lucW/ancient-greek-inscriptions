# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.534)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιδ Τίμ  
αρχος  
Μαγαυ  
νω (ἐτῶν) δ

### English translation

Year 11, Timarchos son of Mat(t)ania, aged 4.  

Year 14, Timarchos (son) of Ma[?], aged 4.
