# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.420)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) δ Ἰο[ύ]  
λιοϲ Μ  
άρκω  
Χοιάχι κϛ

### English translation

Year 4, Ioulios [Julius] (son) of Markos [Marcus], Choiak 26.
