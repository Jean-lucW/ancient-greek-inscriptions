# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.552)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) η Μεσο  
ρὴ ⟨η⟩ Γναῖος  
Βάσσιος  
Ἀρτε  
μᾶς (ἐτῶν) ηι

### English translation

Year 8, Mesore (8th or 3rd) Gnaios son of Artios (?), aged ?.  

Year 8, Mesore 8, Gnaiοs Bassios [Gnaeus Bassius] Artemas, aged 18.
