# Funerary monument. (IRCyr2020 C.772)

## Ancient Greek text

Σωτάδα τὸ σαμᾷον καὶ τὸ δει  
πνιϲτήρυον  
ΑΞΙΟΝ

### English translation

Of Sotadas, the tomb and the dining area.
