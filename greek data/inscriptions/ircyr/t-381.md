# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.381)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ιη Φαμενὼ⟨θ⟩ [...]  
Νικᾶϲ Νικία  
ἐτῶν πε

### English translation

Year 18, Phamenoth (?), Nicas of Nicias, aged 85.  

Year 18, Phamenoth, Nikas (son) of Nikias, aged 85.
