# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.634)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Μεσορὴ  
κα Τελεσὼ  
Μελίτωνος  
(ἐτῶν) λβ

### English translation

Year 5, Mesore 21, Teleso daughter of Meliton, aged 32.  

Year 5, Mesore 21, Teleso (daughter) of Meliton, aged 32.
