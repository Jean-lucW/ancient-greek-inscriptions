# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 M.175)

## Ancient Greek text

(Ἔτουϲ) ρ, ἐπὶ ἱαρέ  
ωϲ Μαξίμω,  
Χυάκι ϛκ  
Λύων Ἀν  
δροκλεῦϲ  
(ἐτῶν) ακ

### English translation

Year ?100, in the priesthood of Maximus, Choiak 26, ?Lyon (son) of Androkles, aged 21.
