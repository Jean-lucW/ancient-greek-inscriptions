# Fragmentary acclamation (IRCyr2020 C.710)

## Ancient Greek text

εὐτυχῶϲ  
Κλ(αυδίῳ) Ῥού[φῳ] κα[ὶ]  
τοῖϲ θ[...]ενοι[ς ...]  
τῶ ε[...]νω νι[...] ἐ[κ]  
θεμελίων

### English translation

With good fortune for Kl(audios) Rouphos [Cl(audius) Rufus] and the [. . .] from the foundations
