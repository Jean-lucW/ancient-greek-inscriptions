# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.277)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϙη Φαωφὶ κϛ  
Τρυφή[νία]  
Κ[α]λλιμάχου  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 98, Phaophi 26, Truphenia (daughter) of Kallimachos, aged 20.
