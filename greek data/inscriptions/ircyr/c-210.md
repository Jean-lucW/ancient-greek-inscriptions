# Dedication by Anaxo (IRCyr2020 C.210)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀναξὼ Εὐφρά  
νορος ἐξ ὑπ[ο]  
σχέσεως Εὐ  
φράνορος τοῦ  
Νεικοδάμου  
πατρός

### English translation

Anaxo (daughter) of Euphranor (dedicated this) according to the promise of Euphranor (son) of Neikodamos, her father
