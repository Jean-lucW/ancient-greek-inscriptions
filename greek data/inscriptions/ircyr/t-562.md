# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.562)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρια Θώθ  
υ λ Ἐξαι  
νέτα Ἀρ  
ίμμα  
(ἐτῶν) λε

### English translation

Year 111, Thoth 30th, Exaintea, daughter of Arimmas, aged 35.  

Year 111, Thoth 30, Exaineta (daughter) of Arimmas, aged 35.
