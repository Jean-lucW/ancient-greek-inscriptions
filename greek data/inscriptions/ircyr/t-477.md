# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.477)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ι Τυβὶ α  
Μ(ᾶρκος) Ῥέμμι  
ος Φιλωνᾶς  
(ἐτῶν) κη

### English translation

Year 13, Tybi 1, M(arkos) Remmios [M(arcus) Remmius] Philonas, aged 28.
