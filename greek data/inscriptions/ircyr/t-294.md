# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.294)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϙα Θώθυ  
ι Λούκιος  
Βιλλιῆνος  
Ἀνί  
κητος  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 91, Thoth 10, Loukios Billienos [Lucius Billienus] Aniketos, aged 30.
