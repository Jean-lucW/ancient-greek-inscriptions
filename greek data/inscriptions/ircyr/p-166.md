# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.166)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ(αΐωι) Ἰουλίωι Ἐπινείκωι ἥρωι ἐπήκοωι  
Γ(άϊοϲ) Ἰούλιοϲ Παυϲανίαϲ καὶ Κοκκεΐα  
❦ Θάλεια ❦  
❦ οἱ γονεῖϲ ❦

### English translation

For G(aios) Ioulios [G(aius) Julius] Epineikos, hero, who hears our prayers, G(aios) Ioulios [G(aius) Julius] Pausanias and Kokkeia [Cocceia] Thaleia his parents (dedicated this).
