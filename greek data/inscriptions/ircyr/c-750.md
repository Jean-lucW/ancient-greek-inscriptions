### English translation

(a) : For Caius, Julius Karo, son of Caius, of the tribe Voltinia, fron the province Narbonensis, military tribune of the Third Cyrenaican legion, prefect of the Second Mounted Cohort of Astyrians, granted a crown for the Brittanic war, a mural crown, a camp crown, a golden pure spear; the centurions and soldiers of the Third Cyrenaican legion and the Twenty-second legion, deployed in the province of the Cyrenaeans, (put this up) because of their affection.  

(b) : ii. Leon (son) of Megon. iii. Oualerios [son of Oualerios son] of ?Stadieus  

(c) : (not usefully translatable) .
