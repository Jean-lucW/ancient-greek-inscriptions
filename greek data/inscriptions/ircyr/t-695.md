# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.695)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρ Ἁθὺρ κ  
Ἀλ[έ]ξανδ[ρ]  
ος Λυσιμ  
[άχ]ου (ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 100, Hathyr 20, Alexandros (son) of Lysimachos, aged 20.
