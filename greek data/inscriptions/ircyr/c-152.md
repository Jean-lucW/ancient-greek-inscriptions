### English translation

(a) : Good Fortune. Year 254, which is also year 3 of Emperor Caesar M(arcus) Aurelius Severus Alexander, Pius, Fortunate, Augustus; the priest of Apollo the founder was M(arkos) Antonios [M(arcus) Antonius] Aristippos the young(er); the ephebarchs M(arkos) Ioulios [M(arcus) Julius] Demetrios and Anthestios Maximos [Anthestius Maximus], and the general P(oplios) Kassios [P(ublius) Cassius] Zotikos and the gymnasiarchs Phoulbeinios Kointos [Fulvinius Quintus] and Aurelios [Aurelius] Apollos. Ephebes:  

Ti(berios) Ai(lios) [Ti(berius) Ae(lius)] Serapion, secretary; Ku(intos) Ai(lios) [Qu(intus) Ae(lius)] Hermes, secretary.  

(b.i) : Ba(lerios) Markos [Va(lerius) Marcus]; Aur(elios) Phaustos [Aur(elius) Faustus]; Kai(kilios) [Cae(cilius)] Augouris; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Onesimos; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Demetrios; Phl(abios) [Fl(avius)] Alexandros; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Serapodoros; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Iason; Sos(sios) [Sos(sius)] Telesarchos; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Aristas; Phoul(bios) Rouphos [Ful(vius) Rufus]; Aur(elios) Agrippeinos [Aur(elius) Agrippinus]; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Anussan; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Thoas; Se(stios) Pakonis [Se(stius) Paconius]; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Iason; Kai(kilios) Roupheinos [Cae(cilius) Rufinus]; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Iambaris; Ak(ilios) Blandos [Ac(ilius) Blandus]; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Asklepas; Aur(elios) Pollion [Aur(elius) Pollio]; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Paraibates; M(arkos) Kl(audios) [M(arcus) Cl(audius)] Aristas; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Teimarchos; Aur(elios) [Aur(elius)] Hippias; Ail(ios) Markellos [Ael(ius) Marcellus]; Tib(erios) Kl(audios) Leon [Tib(erius) Cl(audius) Leo]; Phl(abios) Gais [Fl(avius) Gaius]; Kask(ellios) [Casc(ellius)] Attikos.
