# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.492)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) γ Με  
σορὴ κϛ  
Ξένων  
Μενε  
λάω (ἐτῶν) ια

### English translation

Year 3, Mesore 26, Xenon (son) of Menelaos, aged 11.
