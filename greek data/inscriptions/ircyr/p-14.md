# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 P.14)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιβ  

(ἔτους) η  

(ἔτους) ιβ Ἀμμώνιος Ἑρμαίου

### English translation

i. Year 12.  

ii. Year 8.  

iii. Year 12: Ammonios (son) of Hermaios.
