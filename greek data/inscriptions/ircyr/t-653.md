# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.653)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ια Τυβὶ  
ςκ Μητ  
ροφάνη  
ς Ἀγαθο  
κλέος  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 11,Tybi 26, Metrophanes (son) of Agathokles, aged 20.
