# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.589)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) β Φαωφὶ  
γκ Φυλὶς  
Βάσσω  
(ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 2, Phaophi 23, Phylis (daughter) of Bassos [Bassus], aged 50.
