# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.485)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Θώθυ  
λ Σεκόν  
δα Μίδα  
(ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 5, Thoth 30, Sekonda [Secunda] (daughter), of Midas, aged 30.
