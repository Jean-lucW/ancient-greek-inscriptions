# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.522)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Με  
σ[ορὴ ...]  
Σω[γένης?]  
Πτ[ολε]μ  
αίου (ἐτῶν) ο

### English translation

Year 4, Mesore ?, Sogenes (?) son of Ptolemaios, aged 70.  

Year 4, Mesore ?, So[genes?] (son) of Ptolemaios, aged 70.
