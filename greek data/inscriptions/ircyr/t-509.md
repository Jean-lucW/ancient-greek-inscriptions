# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.509)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιϛ Θώθυ  
ε  
Εὔπραξις  
Φίλωνος  
(ἐτῶν) η

### English translation

Year 16, Thoth 5, Eupraxis, (child) of Philon, aged 8.
