# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.547)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρι  
Φαρμουθὶ  
γι  
Φιλοκράτηϲ  
Ἕρμωνοϲ  
(ἐτῶν) λη

### English translation

Year 110, Pharmuthi ?, Philocrates, son of Hermon, aged 38.  

Year 110 , Pharmouthi 13, Philokrates (son) of Hermon, aged 38.
