# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.631)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ε Ἁθὺρ  
θκ Φιλοῦς  
Θεογίτο  
νος (ἐτῶν) ν

### English translation

Year 5, Hathyr 29, Philous (daughter) of Theogiton, aged 50.
