# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.442)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϛ Πα  
χὼν δ  
Πρωτί  
ων Λυ  
σία (ἐτῶν) δ

### English translation

Year 6, Pachon 4, Protion son of Lysias, aged 4.  

Year 6, Pachon 4, Protion (son) of Lysias, aged 4.
