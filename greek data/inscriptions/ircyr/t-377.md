# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.377)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ρια Θώθυ  
ιθ Βιλλι  
ήνα Ἱπ  
πονίκα  
(ἐτῶν) μ

### English translation

Year 111, Thoth 19, Billiena Hipponika, aged 40.
