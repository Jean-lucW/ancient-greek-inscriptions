# Dedication of a statue of Aphrodite by nomophylakes (IRCyr2020 C.97)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Δι]ονυσίω  
[...]ος Σωπάτρω  
[Ἰά]σων Λυσανί[α]  
Δημῆτρις Κλεάρχ[ω]  
Κράτης Τελεσί[α]  
τὰν Ἀφροδε[ίταν]  
ἀνέθηκ[αν]

### English translation

[...] (son) of Dionysios, [...] (son) of Sopatros, Jason (son) of Lysanias, Demetris (son) of Klearchos, Krates (son) of Telesias, set up the (image of) Aphrodite.
