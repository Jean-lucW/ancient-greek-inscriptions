# Building dedication for a column (IRCyr2020 C.255)

## Ancient Greek text

ϲ[...]  
Ἰάϲ[ων ...]  
Λεοκ[ρ]άτι  
ϲ τὸν κείονα  
ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

. . .] Jas[on . . .] Leokrates (gave) the column from his own resources.
