# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.571)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δ Π[αῦ]ν ηκ  
[...]ας Σι  
λίω (ἐτῶν) ια  
(ἔτους) ρι Εὐφρο  
[σύ]να Σιλί  
ου (ἐτῶν) ιζ Πα  
ῦ[ν] θι.

### English translation

Year 4, Paun 28, [. . ? . .]as son of Silius, aged 11; Year 110, Euphrosyna daughter of Silius, aged 19.  

Year 4, Paun 28, [. .-]as (son) of Silios [Silius], aged 11. Year 110, Euphrosyna (daughter) of Silios [Silius], aged 19.
