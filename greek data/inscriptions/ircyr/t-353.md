# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.353)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ζ Φαμε  
νὼτ ι Ἀρίσ  
τας Κρίσ  
που (ἐτῶν) λ

### English translation

Year 7, Phamenoth 10, Aristas (son) of Krispos [Crispus], aged 30.
