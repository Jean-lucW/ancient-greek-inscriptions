# Ephebic names (IRCyr2020 T.60)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) ιζ Γ(άιοϲ) Ὀφίλλιοϲ Λόποϲ  

(ἔτουϲ) ε  
Χαιρεϲι  
λαϲ Λάω  
[ν]ο[ϲ]

### English translation

(i) . Year 17. G(aios) Ophellios Lopos [G(aius) Ofellius Lupus]  

(ii) . Year 5. Chairesilas (son) of Laon.
