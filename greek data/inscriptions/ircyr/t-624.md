# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.624)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Με  
χὶρ κγ  
Κράτε[ι]  
α Θευχρ  
[ήστου]  
[(ἐτῶν)] η. Μεχὶρ  
λ Γάϊ  
[ος]  
[Γα]ΐου  
(ἐτῶν) κ

### English translation

Year 9, Mechir 23, Crateia daughter of Theuchrestos, aged 8; Mechir 30, Gaius son of Gaius, aged 20.  

Year 9, Mecheir 23, Krateia (daughter) of Theuchr[estos], aged 8; Mecheir 30, Gaios [Gaius] (son) of Gaios [Gaius], aged 20.
