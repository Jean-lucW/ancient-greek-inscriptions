# Honours for Demetria (IRCyr2020 C.459)

## Ancient Greek text

Δημητρία  
Ἀμμωνίω · (ἐτῶν) · ι  
Ἀρρία · τὰν  
θυγατέρα

### English translation

Demetria (daughter) of Ammonios, aged 10. Arria (gave the statue of) her daughter.
