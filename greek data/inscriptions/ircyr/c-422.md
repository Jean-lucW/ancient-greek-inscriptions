# Dedication to Zeus Olympios (IRCyr2020 C.422)

## Ancient Greek text

Διὶ Ὀλυμπίῳ  
θεῷ ἐπηκόῳ  
Αὐρήλιοϲ  
Ῥοῦφοϲ ἀρχι  
τέκτων εὐ  
χὴν ἀπέδωκα

### English translation

For Olympian Zeus, the God who listens, Aurelios Rouphos [Aurelius Rufus] , architect, gave (the fulfilment of) his vow
