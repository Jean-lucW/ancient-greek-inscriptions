# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.516)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ϙε Ἐπαγο  
μένων γ  
Θεόδοτος  
Σωγένους  
(ἐτῶν) γ

### English translation

Year 115, Epagomenon 3rd, Theodotos son of Sogenes, aged 3.  

Year 95, 3rd of the intercalary days, Theodotos (son) of Sogenes, aged 3.
