# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.486)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) [Φαμε?]  
νὸ[θ? ...] Σέ  
κ[ονδο]ϲ  
Ϲεκ[ό]νδ  
αϲ (ἐτῶν) ιϛ

### English translation

Year ? [Phame]no[th ?], Sekondos [Secundus] (son) of Sekonda [Secunda], aged 16.
