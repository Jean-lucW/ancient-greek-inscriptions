# Dedication of a statue (IRCyr2020 P.98)

## Ancient Greek text

Μ(ᾶρκος) Οὔλπιοϲ Κομίνιοϲ ἐκ τῶν  
ἰδίων τῇ πατ[ρί]δι τὸ ἄγαλμα [...]

### English translation

M(arkos) Oulpios Kominios [M(arcus) Ulpius Cominius] from his private means (set up) the statue for the Fatherland.
