# Funerary inscriptionr (IRCyr2020 T.638)

## Ancient Greek text

[(ἔτους) ... Φα]μενὼ[θ] ε Εὐφ  
[ράνωρ?] Εὐφ[ρ]ανορ

### English translation

Year ?, Phamenoth 5, Euphranor (?) son of Euphranor, aged 28.  

Year ?, Phamenoth 5, Euph[ranor] (son) of Euphranor, aged 28.
