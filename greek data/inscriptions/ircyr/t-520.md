# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.520)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) θ Ἐπαγο  
μένων [...]  
Μάρα Πτ  
ολεμαί  
ου (ἐτῶν) κβ

### English translation

Year 9, Epagomen ?, Mara, daughter of Ptolemaios, aged 22.  

Year 9, ? of the intercalary days, Mara (daughter) of Ptolemaios, aged 22.
