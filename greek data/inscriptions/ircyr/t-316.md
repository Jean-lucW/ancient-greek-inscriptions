# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.316)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) ιε Ἁθὺρ  
ηι Θαλ  
α[σσὶς] Λ[υ]  
σ[α]ν[ί]α  
(ἐτῶν) λϛ

### English translation

Year 15, Hathyr 18, Thalassis (daughter) of Lysanias, aged 36.
