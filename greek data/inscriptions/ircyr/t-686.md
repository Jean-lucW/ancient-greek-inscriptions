# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.686)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) δι Θώθ  
υ γ  
Μαρία  
Ἀπολλω  
νίω (ἐτῶν) ϛ

### English translation

Year 14, Thoth 3, Maria (daughter) of Apollonios, aged 6.
