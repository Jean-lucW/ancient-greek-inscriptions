# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 T.357)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτους) η Χοιὰ  
χ ια Ϲάρ  
ρα Ϲκευ  
ᾶ (ἐτῶν) ιζ

### English translation

Year 8, Choiak 11, Sara (daughter) of Skeuas [Scaeva], aged 17
