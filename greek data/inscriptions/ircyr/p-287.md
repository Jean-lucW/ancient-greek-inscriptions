# Funerary inscription (IRCyr2020 P.287)

## Ancient Greek text

(ἔτουϲ) π [...]τω[...]  
Ἀπολλώνιοϲ  
εὖ ἔζησα (ἐτῶν) ν  
Θώθυ κ

### English translation

Year 80+ .... I, Apollonios, lived well (and died), aged 50, on Thoth 20.
