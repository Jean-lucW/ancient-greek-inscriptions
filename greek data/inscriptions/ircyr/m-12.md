# Presence inscription of a Syrian (IRCyr2020 M.12)

## Ancient Greek text

[Οὐ]αλέριοϲ  
[...δ]ώρου  
Ϲύροϲ αιι[...]  
ἥκω (ἔτους) π[...]

### English translation

Valerius (son) of -doros, a Syrian, A[-]: I came here. Year 80 (or 80+: 49-59 CE) .
