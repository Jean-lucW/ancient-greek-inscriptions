# Maresha, 180 CE - 299 CE. Altar. Dedicatory. (IIP mari0005)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἀγαθῇ τύχῃ]  
Ὑπὲρ σω  
τηρίας  
Κομόδ(ου)  
τοῦ κυρ(ίου)  
θεῷ Ἡλι  
οπολε[ίτ]ῃ  
Ἑλκ[ίας]

### English translation

With good luck: For the salvation (of) Commodus the Lord, to the god (Zeus) Heliopolites, Elk[ias?] (has dedicated)
