# Mt. Carmel, 150-250 CE. Marble statue. Dedicatory (IIP mtca0001)

## Ancient Greek text

Διὶ Ἡλιοπολείτῃ Καρμέλῳ  
Γ·(αῖος) Ἰούλ·(ιος) Εὐτύχας  
κόλ>(ων) Καισάρευς

### English translation

(Dedicated) to the Heliopolitan Zeus (god of) Carmel (by) Gaius Iulius Eutychas colonist (of) Caesarea.
