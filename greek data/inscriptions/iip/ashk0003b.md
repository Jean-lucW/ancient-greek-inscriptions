# Ashkelon, 284 CE - 305 CE. Column. Milestone. (IIP ashk0003b)

## Ancient Greek text

ἀπὸ Ἀσκ(άλωνος)  
μί(λια) γ

### English translation

To the Emperors Caesars, brothers Gaius Valerius Diocletian and Marcus Aurelius Valerius Maximianus, pious, fortunate and invincible Augusti. From Ashqelon, three miles.
