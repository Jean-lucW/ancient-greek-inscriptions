# Saura, 81 CE. Dedicatory. (IIP saur0002)

## Ancient Greek text

L(ἔτους) κη᾿ β  
ασ(ιλέως) Ἀγρ(ίππα),  
Αὐσος  
Αἰου, δε  
κουρί(ων).

### English translation

Year 28 of king Agrippa, Ausus son of Aius, a decurion.
