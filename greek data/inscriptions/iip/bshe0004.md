# Scythopolis-Beth Shean, 141/2 CE. Votive inscription on an altar found in the Roman basilica. (IIP bshe0004)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀγαθῇ τύχῃ.  
Θεῷ Διονύσῳ  
κτίστῃ τῷ κυ  
ρίῳ Ϲέλευκος  
Ἀρίστωνος χα  
ριστήριον. Ἒτει εσʹ.

### English translation

With good fortune. Seleucus son of Ariston (dedicated this) as a thanks offering to the god Dionysus, the lord founder. Year 205.
