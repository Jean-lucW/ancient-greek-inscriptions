Θεοδόσιος  
Ἀρτέμιδι

Theodosius (dedicated) to Artemis (in payment of) a vow. (to Artemis?), Theodosios (dedicated in payment of) a vow. Theodosius (dedicated) to Artemis (in payment of) a vow.
