# Caesarea. 2nd-3rd century, CE. Garland sarcophagus. (IIP caes0149)

## Ancient Greek text

θάρσι Μηνόφιλα ἀσύνκριτε,  
οὐδεὶς ἀθάνατος, ταῦτα·  
Ἀλῦπις τῇ ἀγαπητῇ συμβίῳ,  
μ(νήμης) χ(άριν).

### English translation

Take cheer, incomparable Menophila . No one is deathless; such is life. Alypis to his beloved wife, for the sake of her memory.
