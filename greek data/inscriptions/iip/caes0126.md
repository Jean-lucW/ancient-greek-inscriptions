# Caesarea, First to third century CE. Column. Dedicatory (Votive) (IIP caes0126)

## Ancient Greek text

Κ(οΐντος) Καικίλλιος [...]ους  
κωμῳδὸς [πρω]το  
λόγος Ἀντιοχε[ὺς]  
τῶν πρὸς Δάφνην  
εὐξάμενος ἀνέθη(κεν).

### English translation

Kointos Kaikillios --ous of Antioch-by-Daphne, a comic actor of leading roles, dedicated this in fulfillment of a vow.
