# Kafr Harib, 63 BCE - 640 CE. Tombstone. Funerary. (IIP kafr0005)

## Ancient Greek text

[θάρσι]  
Ζήνω  
ν Εὐμ[έ]  
νους  
ἐτ(ῶν) οʹα´

### English translation

[Be of good courage,] Zenon, son of Eumenes! Seventy-one years old.
