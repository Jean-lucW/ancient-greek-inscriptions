# Zoora, 2nd Century CE to 3rd Century CE. Tombstone. Funerary (Epitaph) (IIP zoor0387)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰσμεήλῃ Ἀβδαρέτου, Ζωΐλος  
Ἀλέβου, συμβίῳ, εὐνοίας χάριν.

### English translation

Zoilos, (son) of Alevos, (made this tomb) for his wife Ishmeele, (daughter) of Abdaretas, out of affection (for her).
