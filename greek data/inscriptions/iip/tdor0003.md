# Tel Dor, 138-137 BCE. Sling bullet. Label. (IIP tdor0003)

## Ancient Greek text

Τρύφωνο(ς)  
νίκη  
ε  
Δω(ριτῶν) π(όλεως). Ῥοῦ  
γεῦσαι

### English translation

For the victory  
of Tryphon  
Dor. Year 5.  
Of the city of Dorians. Have  
a taste of sumac.
