# Golan. Ulatha (Hule) Valley. 293-305 CE. Boundary stone. (IIP ulat0016)

## Ancient Greek text

Λὶθον διορ  
ίζοντα ἀγρ  
οὺς κω(μῶν) Ωσεας  
κὲ Περισης

### English translation

The stone which divides the lands of the villages Ωσεα and Περιση.
