# Scythopolis-Beth Shean, 139/40 CE. Votive inscription on a limestone base. (IIP bshe0003)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἔτους γσʹ  
Λούκιος Οὐάριος Κυ  
ρείνᾳ Πρόκλος Δὶ  
Ἀκρα[ί]ῳ [Σ]ω[τ]ῆρι  
μετ᾽ εὐχαριστίας  
ἀνέθηκε[ν].

### English translation

Year 203. Lucius Varius of the (tribe) Quirina dedicated (this) to Zeus Akraios, the saviour, in thanksgiving.
