# Maresha. Fifth to second centuries BCE. Altar. Dedicatory (IIP mare0206)

## Ancient Greek text

Δημῆς  
Δαμ[ο]κ[λεῦ]ς  
τ᾽ υἱὸς  
παρὰ τῇ  
Ἰσίδι

### English translation
