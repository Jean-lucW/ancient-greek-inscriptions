# Mt Hermon, 3rd century CE. Post. Warning inscription. (IIP mher0001)

## Ancient Greek text

Κατὰ κέ  
λευσιν  
θεοῦ με  
γίστου κ[αὶ]  
{β} ἁγίου  
οἱ ὀμνύον  
τες ἐντεύ  
θεν

### English translation

According to the command of the great and holy God, those who take an oath [proceed] from here.
