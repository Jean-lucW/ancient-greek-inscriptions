# Avdat, 23 July 241 CE. Tombstone. Epitaph. (IIP odob0012)

## Ancient Greek text

Αὐρ(ηλία) Μούλχη  
Ἀβδομανχου τοῦ  
καὶ Ἀμλαίφου ζήσα  
σα ἔτη πα᾿ ἐτελευτη  
σεν δ᾿ Ἄβ ἔτους ΡΛϛ

### English translation

Aurelia Moulche (daughter) of Abdomanchos, known also by the name of Amlaifos, lived 81 years, died on the 4th of Ab of the year 136.
