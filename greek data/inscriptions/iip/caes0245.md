# Caesarea, Third century CE. Plaque. Funerary. (IIP caes0245)

## Ancient Greek text

Αὐρ(ήλιον) Ἑρμογέ  
νυν Γλύκων  
ὁ κὲ Παῦλος  
τὸν ἴδιον σύντρο  
φον,  
μν(ήμης) χά(ριν).

### English translation

Glykon, also called Paul, to Aurelios Hermogenes , his own foster brother, for the sake of his memory.
