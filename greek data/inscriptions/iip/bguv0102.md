# Beit Guvrin, 1st-2nd century BCE. Cave Wall. Graffito. (IIP bguv0102)

## Ancient Greek text

Φιλῖνος ὁ νέαξ  
ἐνθάδε ἐπύγι{ζ}  
ζεν Παπίαν τὸν  
τοῦ Κρατερ⟨ο⟩ῦ  
προγόνον

### English translation

Here Philinos the youth buggered Papias, Krateros' stepson.
