# Caesarea. Late first to third century, CE. Block of kurkar. Dedication. (IIP caes0124)

## Ancient Greek text

Οὐίκτορ εὐξά  
μενος ἀνέ  
θηκεν κὲ ἠρ  
γάσετο τῷ Δι(ὶ)  
Δολιχηνῷ.

### English translation

Viktor, in fulfillment of a vow, deciated and built this altar to Zeus (Jupiter) Dolichenos.
