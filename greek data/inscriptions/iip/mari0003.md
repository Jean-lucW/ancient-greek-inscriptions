# Maresha, 312 BCE - 63 BCE. Tablet. Prayer. (IIP mari0003)

## Ancient Greek text

[Σκόπα]ς Κράτωνος  
[Ἀπόλλ]ωνι εὐχήν

### English translation

[Skopa]s, son of Kraton, to Apollo [addresses his] prayer.
