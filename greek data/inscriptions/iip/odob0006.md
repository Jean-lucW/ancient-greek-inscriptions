# Avdat, Late third century CE. Column drum. Dedicatory. (IIP odob0006)

## Ancient Greek text

Ῥαίσος Ἀβδάλγου  
εὐχαριστῶν  
ἀνέγειρεν τὸν στέγο⟨ν⟩

### English translation

Raisos (son) of Abdalgos has built the roof as thanksgiving.
