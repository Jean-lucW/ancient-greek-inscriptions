# Hebron, Roman period. Votive plaque. Dedication. (IIP hebr0020)

## Ancient Greek text

Διὶ ἁγίῳ{ω}  
Βερενίκη εὐχ  
αριστοῦσα  
ἀνέθηκα

### English translation

I, Berenice, gratefully dedicated (this) to Holy Zeus.
