# Khirbet Za'aquqa, 330-250 BCE. Wall. Graffito. (IIP kzaa0009)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀθηνίου τῆς Δημοφίλ  
ου  
τοῦ πατρὸς  
πάντων  
Δημοφίλου πατρὸς πάντων  
θυγατρός

### English translation

Of Athenion daughter of Demophilus. Of the father of all of them. Of Demophelus father of all of them. Daughter.
