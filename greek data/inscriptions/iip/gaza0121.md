# Gaza, 229-230 CE. Column. Dedicatory. (IIP gaza0121)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀμμών[ι]ος  
Δομεστικο⟨ῦ⟩  
ὑπὲρ Δομε  
στικοῦ υ  
ἱοῦ ἀνέθη  
κεν L(ἔτους) ϙσ

### English translation

Ammonius son of Domesticus dedicated (this column) on behalf of his son, Domesticus. Year 290.
