# Golan. Ulatha (Hule) Valley. 293-305 CE. Boundary stone. (IIP ulat0017)

## Ancient Greek text

Λὶθος  
διορίζων  
ὅρια  
κω(μῶν) Γαλα  
νιας καὶ ὅ(ρια)  
Ῥαμης

### English translation

The stone which divides the lands of the villages of Galania and Rama.
