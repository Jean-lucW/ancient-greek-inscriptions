# Caesarea, First to second century CE. Plaque. Funerary. (IIP caes0159)

## Ancient Greek text

Τ(ίτε) Φ(λάουιε) Ἱερώνυμε  
χρηστὲ καὶ ἄλυπε  
χαῖρε ἐτῶν κβ’.

### English translation

Farewell, Titos Flavios Hieronymos , twenty-two years old, a man who was upright and harmed no one.
