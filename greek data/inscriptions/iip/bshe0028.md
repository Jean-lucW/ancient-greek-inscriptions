# Scythopolis-Beth Shean, 144-5 CE. Octagonal Altar. Votive (IIP bshe0028)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀγαθῇ  
Τύχῇ.  
Διὶ Ἀκραίῳ  
Θεογενὴ  
Τοβίου  
ἀνέθη  
κεν.

### English translation

With good fortune. Theogene (daughter) of Tobias dedicated (this altar) to Zeus Akraios.
