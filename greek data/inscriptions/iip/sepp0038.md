# Sepphoris, 3rd CE. Stone lintel. Funerary inscription. (IIP sepp0038)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ(άιος) Ιούλιος Παῦλος οὐ  
τρανὸ[ς] Iουλ⟨ίᾳ?⟩ Πα[ύ?]λ[ᾳ?...]  
[...]ΟΝ[...]ΠΑΝ[...]

### English translation

Gaius Iulius Paulus, veteran, [prepared a tomb for] Iulia Paula[?] or Iulius Paulus[?]
