# Upper Galilee. Cadasa (Tel Qedesh), 189/190 CE. Pedestal. Dedicatory. (IIP kede0004)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  
εὔς Ἀντικέρασ  
oς Κράτηρος Ἀ  
ήλιας Ἀγαθοκ[λῆ]  
ς συμβίου καὶ Ἰο[...]  
πτης θυγατρὸ[ς]  
[τ]ὸν ἀνδριάντα σὺν τῇ βάσει  
τῷ ειτ ἔτει

### English translation

(Blessed be) Antikerasos the good, Krateros, Aelias, Agathokles with his wife and Io[..]ptes his daughter (they erected) the statue with the pedestal in the year 315.
