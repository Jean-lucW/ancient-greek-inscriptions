# Avdat, Late Third century CE. Building stone. Dedicatory. (IIP odob0010)

## Ancient Greek text

[Εὐσε]βείᾳ καὶ προνοίᾳ Αὐσ  
οέβδου τοῦ Ἐράσου  
ἐγένετο ἡ πλάκω(σις)  
Ἀφροδείτης ΕΤΙϛ  
ἰδίᾳ Ἀβδαισέ(ου)  
καὶ Ἀμέου οἰκοδ(όμου)

### English translation

For the sake of piety and by the initiative of Ausoebdos (son) of Erasos was made by the facing of marble of (the hosue of?) Aphrodite ETIS (?), built by Abdaisei and Ameiou the architect, of their own resources.
