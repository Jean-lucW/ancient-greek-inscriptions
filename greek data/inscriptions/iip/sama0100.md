# Samaria, Second-third century CE. Marble fragment. Invocation. (IIP sama0100)

## Ancient Greek text

Εἷς Θεός  
ὁ πάντων  
δεσπότης  
μεγάλη Κὀρη  
ἡ ἀνείκητος

### English translation

One god, the ruler of all, great Kore, the invincible
