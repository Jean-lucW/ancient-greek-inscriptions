# Hermon Mons (Har Senaim). Mid-second century CE to fourth century CE. Fragment of a hard limestone altar. (IIP sena0001)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] καὶ Γάλεσος καὶ Μοβογέος  
[ἀνέθηκ]αν ἐκ τῶν εἰδίων τὸν βωμ  
[ὸν Ἀφα?]ρεὺς κατόχου ὄντ[ος]  
[τοῦ θε]οῦ ἐγένετο ὁ βωμ[ὸς]

### English translation

[-------] and Galesos and Mobogeos [dedicat]ed this alt[ar] at their own (expense). [Apha?]reus bei[ng] a 'temple-recluse (under a vow)', (when) the alt[ar] was made [to the (local) go]d.
