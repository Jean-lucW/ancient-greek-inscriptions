# Avdat, Late Third century CE. Building stone. Dedicatory. (IIP odob0008)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ζε]ῦ Ὄδοβα  
[μνησ]θῇ Ἄμμου  
[...]ο ὁ οἰκο  
[δόμος ...] ΝΠΗΡΟΔΟΝ  
[...]ΣΒΥ  
[...] Χαρέστου  
[...] Γαράμου

### English translation

Zeus Odoba. Remember Ammos … the builder … (son) of Chaestos … (son) of Garamos.
