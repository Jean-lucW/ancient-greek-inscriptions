# Maresha, late 3rd-early 2nd century BCE. Ostracon. Fine Document. (IIP mare0508)

## Ancient Greek text

Ζημιοῖ τὸ  
κοινὸν Κοσα  
δάρος Ῥοδωνα  
ἀρ(γυρίου) ⊦(δραχμαῖς) μ᾿

### English translation

The Koinon of Kosadar fines Rhodon 40 silver drachmae.
