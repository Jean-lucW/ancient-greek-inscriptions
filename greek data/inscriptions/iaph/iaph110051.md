# Dedication by Tiberius Claudius Apollonios Aurelianos (IAph2007 11.51)

## Ancient Greek text

Τι(βέριος) Κλ·(αύδιος) Ἀπολλ[ώ]  
νιος Αὐρηλια  
νὸς ἀρχιε  
ρεὺς καὶ ἱε  
ρεὺς διὰ βίου  
θεοῦ Διονύ  
σου ❦

### English translation

Tiberius Claudius Apollonios Aurelianos high priest and priest for life of the god Dionysos (set this up).
