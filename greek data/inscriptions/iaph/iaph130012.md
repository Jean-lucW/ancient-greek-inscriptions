# Honours for Epigonos son of Glykon (IAph2007 13.12)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ βουλὴ καὶ ὁ  
δῆμος ἐτεί  
μησεν Ἐπί  
γονον Γλύκ  
κωνος τὸν  
βούλαρχον

### English translation

The Council and the People honoured Epigonos, (son) of Glykon, the leader of the Council.
