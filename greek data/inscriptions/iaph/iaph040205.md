# Honours for the emperor Nerva (IAph2007 4.205)

## Ancient Greek text

Αὐτοκράτορα  
Νέρβαν ❦ Καίσα  
ρα Σεβαστὸν  
ὁ Δῆμος ἐκ τῶν  
Διογένους · τοῦ  
Μενάνδρου  
τοῦ Διογένους  
νεωτέρου

### English translation

The People (set up the statue of) Imperator Nerva Caesar Augustus from the bequest of Diogenes the Younger, son of Menandros, son of Diogenes.  

Emperor Nerva Caesar Augustus; the People (erected his statue) from the (funds) of Diogenes the younger, son of Menandros, son of Diogenes.
