# Funerary inscription for Aimilia Meltine wife of Alexandros (IAph2007 14.4)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ σορός ἐστὶν Αἰμιλίας Μελτίνης τῆς Ἀλεξάνδρου το[...]  
ζ [ῆ]

### English translation

The sarcophagus belongs to Aimilia Meltine (wife) of Alexandros son of [...]  

She lives.
