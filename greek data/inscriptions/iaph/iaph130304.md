# Gladiator memorial for Podenemos (IAph2007 13.304)

## Ancient Greek text

Ποδηνέμος πάλου γ ὑπ[ὸ]  
Ἑρμᾶ πάλου δ

### English translation

Podenemos of brigade 3 by Hermas of brigade 4.
