# Dedication to Zeus Nineudios (IAph2007 2.115)

## Ancient Greek text

Μελέαγρ[ος ?]  
χαλκεύς Δι[ὶ Νινευ]  
δίῳ εὐχήν[?]

### English translation

Meleagros, the smith, to Zeus Nineudios in fulfillment of a vow.
