# Funerary inscription for Heraklide, Thyodora, and Meltine (IAph2007 15.244)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

να τοῦ Ἀπ [...]  
Ἡρακ⟨λ⟩ίδη κα[ὶ ...]

[...] ?-na (daughter? wife?) of Ap- [...] Heraklide and [...] Thyodora and [...] Meltine, daughters [...] of Thyodora [...]. Farewell
