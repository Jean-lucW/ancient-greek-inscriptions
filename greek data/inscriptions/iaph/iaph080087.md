# Honours for Kandidianos, circuit-victor (IAph2007 8.87)

## Ancient Greek text

Κανδιδιανὸν Ἀ{τ}κτεονίκην  
περιοδονίκην ἡ πατρίϲ

### English translation

The fatherland (honours) Kandidianos, victor at Actia, circuit-victor.  

The fatherland (honors) Candidianus, victor at Actia, circuit victor.
