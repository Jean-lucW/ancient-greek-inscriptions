# Funerary fragment from Portico East of Theatre (IAph2007 8.506)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ὧν τὸ τ]ρίτον ἔστε τοῦ ἐ⟨κ⟩δικη[σαντος ...] ὧν τὸ τρίτον ἔστε το[ῦ ἐκδικήσαντος ...]

### English translation

.a. . .] of which the third part will belong to the prosecutor [. . .]  

.b. . .] of which the third part will belong [to the prosecutor . . .]
