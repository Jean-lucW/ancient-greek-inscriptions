# Building Dedication for the neoi (IAph2007 2.507)

## Ancient Greek text

[Φά?]νης Παμφίλου γυμνασιαρχήσας  
τὸγ κίονα τοῖς νέοις

### English translation

[Dioge?]nes son of Pamphilos, former gymnasiarch (set up) the column for the Young Men.  

[Pha?]nes son of Pamphilus, former gymnasiarch (set up) the column for the Young Men.
