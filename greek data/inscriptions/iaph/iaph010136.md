# Panel fragments from temple (IAph2007 1.136)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]απ[...]τουι[...]  

[...]κδ[...]η [...]υν[...]  

[...]ννα[... ἱ]ερεια [... γ]υνή[...]

### English translation

b. line 3, might give part of ΣΥΝ- or ΓΥΝΗ.  

c. line 2, gives the word for "priestess", for "priesthood", or for "offering"; line 3 that for "wife".
