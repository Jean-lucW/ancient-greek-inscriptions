# Statue Dedication to Hadrian (IAph2007 4.204)

## Ancient Greek text

Αὐτοκράτο  
ρα Καίσαρα  
θεοῦ Τραια  
νοῦ Παρθι  
κοῦ υἱὸν θεοῦ  
Νέρουα υἱω  
νὸν Τραια  
νον Ἁδρια  
νὸν Σεβασ  
τὸν ὁ Δῆμος

### English translation

The People (set up the statue of) Imperator Caesar Hadrianus Augustus, son of Divus Traianus Parthicus, grandson of Divus Nerva.  

Emperor Caesar Trajan Hadrian Augustus, son of deified Trajan the conqueror of the Parthians, grandson of deified Nerva. The People (erected the statue).
