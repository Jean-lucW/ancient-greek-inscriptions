# Honours for Aphrodite ancestress of the Augusti (IAph2007 9.34)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀφροδίτην  
Προμήτορα  
θεῶν  
Σεβαστῶν

### English translation

Aphrodite, Foremother (Prometor) of the theoi Sebastoi  

(Statue of) Aphrodite, the first mother of the gods, the Augusti.
