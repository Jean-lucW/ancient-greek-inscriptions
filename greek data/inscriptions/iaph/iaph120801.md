# Funerary inscription for Aba (IAph2007 12.801)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄβα Ἀτραπάτου  
γυνὴ δὲ Ἀθηνα  
γόρου τοῦ Μηνο  
δότου Κασταίου  
χαῖρε

### English translation

Aba, (daughter of) Atrapatos, and wife of Athenagoras, son of Menodotos, also called Kastaios. Farewell
