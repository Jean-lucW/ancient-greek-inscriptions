# Supervisor's text (IAph2007 16.180)

## Ancient Greek text

ἐπεμελήϲατο Ἴθαρο[ϲ]  
Ἰθάρου τοῦ Μεν⟨ίππου⟩

### English translation

Itharos son of Itharos son of Men⟨ippos⟩ oversaw (the work)
