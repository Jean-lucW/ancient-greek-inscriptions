# Statue Dedication to the Emperor (IAph2007 12.902)

## Ancient Greek text

Δία Πατρῷον  
Σεβαστὸν Καίσαρα

### English translation

'Zeus Patroos Sebastos Kaisar' or 'Ancestral Zeus Augustus Caesar'  

(Statue of) Zeus of our Ancestors, Augustus Caesar.
