# Regulation formula from funerary text (IAph2007 12.1009)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

[τῆς ἐπιγραφῆς ἀπετέθη ἀντίγραφον εἰς]  
τὸ χρεοφ[υλάκιον ἐπὶ στεφανηφόρου ...]  
τὸ ζ μη(νὸς) ζ

### English translation

[... A copy of the inscription was deposited in] the property-archive when [...] was stephanephorus for the seventh time, in the seventh month.
