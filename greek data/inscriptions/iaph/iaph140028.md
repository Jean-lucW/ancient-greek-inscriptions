### English translation

[Resolved by the Council and the People; the proposal of the Strategoi [and ...] of Peritos, son of Mindron, [and . . . the Secre]tary of the People: Since Artemidoros, son of Dionysio]s, grandson of Demetrios, [descended from ancestors who were ...] and distinguished, and especially [... having] chosen a well-ordered [life ... and] filled [with every] virtue; [and whereas] he [has now . . .] passed away; and the People [sharing in? the gri]ef of his parents [......] toward the deceased [...]; it was unanimously decided to honor [him with the] fitting honors. It has been decreed by the Council and the] People that Artemidoros, the aforementioned be praised and honoured. [Let there be set up for him] both a painted portrait on a gilded shield and a stele of white stone [...] in a public place, upon which [it shall be insc]ribed: "The Council and the People honored Artemidoros, son of Dionysios, grandson of Demetrios, a man noble and good, who lived in all things as an example of virtue."
