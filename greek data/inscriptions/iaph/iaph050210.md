# Dedication of a caryatid by Julia Paula, with sculptor's signature (IAph2007 5.210)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἰουλία Γαί  
ου Ἀπολλο  
δότου θυ  
γάτηρ Παῦ  
λα ἀρχιέρει  
α ἀνθηφό  
ρος  

Ἀνδρόνει  
κος ἐποί  
ει

### English translation

(i) : Julia Paula, daughter of Gaius Apollodotos, high priestess, flower-bearer (dedicated this).  

(ii) : Andronikos made (the statue)  

Julia Paula, daughter of Gaius Apollodotos, high priestess (and) flower-bearer (anthephoros), (set up the statue). Andronikos made (it).
