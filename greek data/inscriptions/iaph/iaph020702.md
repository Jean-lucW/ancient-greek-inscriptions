# Sculptor's signature of Apollonios Aster (IAph2007 2.702)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀπωλλώ  
νιος Χρυσίπ  
που Ἀστὴρ  
ἐποίησεν

### English translation

Apollonios Aster, son of Chrysippos, made (this)
