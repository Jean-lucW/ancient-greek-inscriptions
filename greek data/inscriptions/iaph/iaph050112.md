# Votive offering to Asklepios by Amias (IAph2007 5.112)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀμια  
ϲ θεῷ Ἀϲ  
κληπίῳ εὐ  
χήν

### English translation

Amias to the god Asklepios, in fulfilment of a vow.  

Amias, to the god Asklepios (in payment of) a vow
