# Votive offering to Zeus (IAph2007 8.207)

## Ancient Greek text

Κόρυνβος [...]  
θεῶι εὐχή[ν ...]

### English translation

Korynbos to [- - the] god in fulfilment of a vow.  

Κorynbοs to [the] god [who listens?] (in payment of) a vow.
