# Dedication of a caryatid by Attalis (IAph2007 5.7)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀτταλὶς Μη  
νοδότου  
τοῦ Νεικομά  
χου ἀρχιερέ  
ως ἱέρεια Ἀρε  
τῆς τῷ Δήμῳ

### English translation

Attalis daugher of Menodotos the son of Nikomachos, high priest, priestess of Arete, to the People.  

Attalis, daughter of Menodotos son of high priest Nikomachos, priestess of Arete, to the demos.
