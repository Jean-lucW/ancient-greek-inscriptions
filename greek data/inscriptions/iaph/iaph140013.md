# Honours for Tiberius Claudius Capitolinus (IAph2007 14.13)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ πατρίς  
Τιβέριον  
Κλαύδιον  
Τιβερίου  
[Κ]λαυδίου  
[Σ]μαράγδου  
[υἱ]ὸν Καπι  
τωλεῖνον

### English translation

The fatherland (set up a statue of) Tiberius Claudius Kapitoleinos son of Tiberius Claudius Smaragdos.
