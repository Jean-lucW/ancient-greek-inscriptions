# Votive offering to an unnamed God (IAph2007 13.617)

## Ancient Greek text

Πολυχρόνιοϲ ὁ τῆϲ [...]  
ειεριαϲ γανβρὸϲ εὐ⟨ξ⟩ά[...]  
τῷ θεῷ εἰϲ τὸ ἁγίαϲμ[α ... τοῦ?]  
το Φλ·(άουιοϲ) Ἔρ·(ωϲ?) ἐποίηϲα [...]

### English translation

Polychronios the brother-in-law of the [feminine ...] of the priestess, having made a vow [...] to the god with a view to the sanctification of [...] Flavius ?Er(os) I made this.
