# Fragmentary honours (IAph2007 15.2)

## Ancient Greek text

[...τειμαῖ?]ς πάσαις[...]χον Μενε[...] ἄνδρα κα[λὸν καὶ ἀγαθὸν?...]  
[...]σωτῆρα [τῆς πατρίδος? ... ...]

### English translation

Mentions of all (honours); -chos (?son of) Mene(krates?); a man who was noble (and good?), a saviour (of his country?)
