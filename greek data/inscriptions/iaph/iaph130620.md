### English translation

. . .] those buried in it, since whoever acts in any way contrary to these [provisions] is to be (considered) impious and accursed and a tomb-breaker, and in addition to pay to the most sacred Treasury [at Rome] 2800 denarii of which a third is to belong to the prosecutor. And I wish that in the burial places of the tomb (heroon) Antonius Hermes, my brother, be buried in the upper one, and in the lower one Epigonos and Polychronia ⟨and?⟩ her children, whom I have brought up. A copy of this document was deposited also in the archives a copy, in the sixth stephanephorate of Titus Flavius [?Chair]eas, in the month Gorpiaios.
