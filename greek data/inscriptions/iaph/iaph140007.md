# Funerary inscription for Adrastos and Rhodine (IAph2007 14.7)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄδραϲτοϲ  
καὶ Ῥοδίνη  
Ἀδράϲτου  
τοῦ οἱοῦ αὐ  

τῶν μνεί  
αϲ χάριν

### English translation

Adrastos and Rhodine, in memory of their son, Adrastos
