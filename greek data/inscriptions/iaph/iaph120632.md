# Funerary fragment (IAph2007 12.632)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ σορὸς καὶ αἱ ὑποκείμεναι ἰσῶσται τέσσαρες κα  
τεσκευασμέναι εἰσὶν ὑπὸ Αὐρηλ[ί- ...]

### English translation

The sarcophagus and the four burial niches lying below were prepared by Aurel[ius or Aureliai or just possibly the Aurelii . . .]
