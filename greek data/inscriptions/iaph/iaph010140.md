# Funerary formula from sarcophagus (IAph2007 1.140)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]νους ὁ ἀνὴρ α[ὐτ]ῆς οὐδεὶς δὲ ἕξει ἐξουσίαν ἕτερον οὐδένα ἐνθάψ[αι ...]

### English translation

[... nes] her husband; and no one shall have the right to bury anyone else therein [...]
