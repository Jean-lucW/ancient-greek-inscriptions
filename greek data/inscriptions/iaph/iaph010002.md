# Building Dedication for Aphrodite by C. Julius Zoilos (IAph2007 1.2)

## Ancient Greek text

Γ[[άϊος]] [[Ἰούλιος]] [[Ζω[ΐ]λ]]ος [[ὁ]] ἱερ[[εὺς]] [[θεοῦ Ἀφροδείτη[ς]]]  
σ[[ωτὴρ]] [[καὶ]] [[εὐεργέτη]]ς τῆς πατ[[ρίδος]] [[τὸν ναόν Ἀφροδείτῃ]]

### English translation

C. Julius Zoilos, priest of the god Aphrodite, saviour and benefactor of his country (gave?) the temple to Aphrodite.  

C. Julius Zoilos, priest of the god Aphrodite, saviour and benefactor of his country (gave?) the sanctuary to Aphrodite.  

C. Julius Zoilos, priest of the god Aphrodite [. . ? . .] saviour and benefactor of his country (built, defined the boundary of?) the sanctuary of Aphrodite.
