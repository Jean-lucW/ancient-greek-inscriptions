# Honours for Julia Augusta/Livia as Hera (IAph2007 9.39)

## Ancient Greek text

[Ἰο]υλίαν Σεβαστὴ[ν]  
Σεβαστοῦ θυγατέ[ρα]  
Ἥραν

### English translation

Julia Sebaste Hera, daughter of Augustus  

(Statue of) Julia Augusta, daughter of Augustus, Hera
