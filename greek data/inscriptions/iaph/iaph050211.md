# Dedication of a caryatid by Claudia Apphia Chaeremonis. (IAph2007 5.211)

## Ancient Greek text

❦ Κλαυδία  
Ἀκυλείνου  
θυγάτηρ Ἀπ  
φία Χαιρημο  
νὶς ἀρχιέρεια

### English translation

Claudia Apphia Chaeremonis, daughter of Aquilinus, high priestess (dedicated this)  

Claudia Apphia Chairemonis, daughter of Aquilinus, high priestess, (set up the statue).
