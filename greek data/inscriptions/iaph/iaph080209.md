# Votive offering to Kore Plyarei by Diogenes and Tatias (IAph2007 8.209)

## Ancient Greek text

Διωγένηϲ  
μετὰ Τατιάϲ  
Κούρῃ Πλυαρει  
εὐχήν

### English translation

Diogenes together with Tatia to the Virgin of Plyara in fulfilment of a vow.  

Diogenes with Tatias to the Koure of Plyara.
