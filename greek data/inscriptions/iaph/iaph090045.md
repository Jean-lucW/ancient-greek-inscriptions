# Building restoration of the Sebasteion propylon by Eusebes and Menandros (IAph2007 9.45)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀφροδίτηι θεῷ Σεβαστοῖς τῶι Δήμωι  
τὸ πρόπυλον καὶ τὰς ἐν αὐτῶι τιμὰς  

[Εὐσ]εβὴς Φιλόπατρις καὶ Μένανδρος  
[οἱ Μεν]άνδρου τοῦ Εὐνίκου καὶ [Ἀ]πφίας Μ[ενάν]δρου

### English translation

To Aphrodite, (the) Theoi Sebastoi, (the) Demos, the Propylon and the honours [timas, that is honorific statues] on it. Eusebes Philopatris and Menandros the sons of Menandros son of Eunikos, and Apphias daughter of Menandros (set them up)  

For Aphrodite, the gods Augusti, the People: the propylon and the honorific statues on it, Eusebes [entitled] lover of his country and Menandros the sons of Menandros son of Eunikos, and Apphias daughter of Menandros (set it up)
