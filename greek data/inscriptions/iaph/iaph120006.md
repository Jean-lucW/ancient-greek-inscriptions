# Funerary inscription for anonymous (IAph2007 12.6)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ βωμὸς καὶ ⟨αἱ⟩ ἐν αὐτῷ εἰσῶσται καὶ [ἡ]  
ἐπικειμένη σορὸς κα[ὶ] ὁ ὑποκειμέ[νος...]

### English translation

The platform and the burial niches in it and the sarcophagus lying on it and the [?tomb] below [. . .
