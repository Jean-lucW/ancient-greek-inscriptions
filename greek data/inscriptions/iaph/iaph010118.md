# Honours for anonymous, overseen by Marcus Aurelius Zenas (IAph2007 1.118)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  
ὲπιμελησ[αμένου τῆς ἀναστασέως?]  
Μ·(άρκου) Αὐρ(ηλίου) Ζην[ᾶ υἱοῦ Ζηνᾶ τοῦ Διονυσίου]  
τοῦ Απολλ[ωνίου ...]υ νεοποίο[υ ...]  
[συντ]ρόφου α[ὐτοῦ?...]

### English translation

Marcus Aurelius Zenas [son of Zenas the son of Dionysios] the son of Apollonios undertook responsibility [for the erection of the monument?], ?he was a (?the chief) temple-builder [...]? his foster-brother.
