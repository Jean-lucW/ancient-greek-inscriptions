# Funerary inscription for Dionysios descendant of Diogenianos (IAph2007 13.106)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ ϲορόϲ ἐστιν κὲ ὁ τό[ποϲ? ...]  
Διονυ  
ϲίου  
❦ δ ❦  
Διογε  
νια  
νοῦ

### English translation

The sarcophagus and the plot [...] belong to Dionysios, fourth [son of Dionysios, son of Dionysios, son of Dionysios] son of Diogenianos.
