# Dedication of a caryatid by Flavia Attalis Aeliane. (IAph2007 5.212)

## Ancient Greek text

ὑπὲρ Φλαβίας  
Ἀτταλίδος  
Αἰλιανῆς τῆς  
ἑαυτοῦ γυ  
ναικος Τίτος  
Φλάβιος Ἀθη  
ναγόρας Ἀγα  
θος τὴν καρυ  
ατιν τῷ Δήμῳ

### English translation

On behalf of Flavia Attalis Aeliane his wife, Titus Flavius Athenagoras Agathos (set up) the caryatid to the People.  

On behalf of Fl. Attalis Aeliana his wife, T. Fl. Athenagoras Agathos (set up) the caryatid (ten karuatin) to the demos.
