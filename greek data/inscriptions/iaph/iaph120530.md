# Honours for Diogenes and Attalos (IAph2007 12.530)

## Ancient Greek text

Διογένην  
Ἄτταλος  
τὸν ἀδελ  
φόν  

Ἄτταλον  
Διογένης  
τὸν ἀδελ  
φόν

### English translation

(i) . Attalos (set up a statue of) Diogenes his brother  

(ii) . Diogenes (set up a statue of) Attalos his brother  

(i) . Attalus [erects a statue of] Diogenes, his brother
