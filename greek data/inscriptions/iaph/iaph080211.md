# Statue Dedication of Hygeia (IAph2007 8.211)

## Ancient Greek text

[τ]ὴν Ὑγήαν Καίσα[ρι Σεβ]  
[α]στῶι καὶ τῶι Δήμ[ωι]  
[Μ]ολοσσὸς Χρυσά[ο]  
[ρ]ος ὁ ἱερεὺς τῆς Ὑγ[ήας]

### English translation

The (statue of) Hygeia [Health] for Caesar Augustus and the People: Molossos son of Chrysaor, the priest of Hygeia (set it up).
