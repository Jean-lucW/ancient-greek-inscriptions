# Funerary inscription for [Marcus Aurelius] Sophronios, son of Artemidoros (IAph2007 15.341)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ ϲορὸϲ καὶ ὁ τόποϲ καθ'οὗ [ἐπικεῖται ἐϲτι ...]  
Ϲωφρονίου τοῦ Ἀρτεμ[ιδώρου τοῦ? ...]  
τοῦ κα [...]

### English translation

The sarcophagus and the place in which [it stands belongs to ?Marcus Aurelius] Sophronios, son of Artem[idoros son of .. ? ..] son of Ka[...]
