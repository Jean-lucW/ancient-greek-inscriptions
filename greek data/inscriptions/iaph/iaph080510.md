# Honours, perhaps funerary, for Artemidoros from his brothers (IAph2007 8.510)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄτταλος καὶ Διομέδων  
οἱ Ποτάμωνος Ἀρτεμιδώρον  
τὸν ἀδελφόν

### English translation

Attalos and Diomedon the sons of Potamon (honoured) Artemidoros, their brother
