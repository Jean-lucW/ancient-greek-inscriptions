# Funerary inscription for Marcus Aurelius high-priest (IAph2007 11.308)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]συνησ Μάρ(κου) Αὐρ(ηλίου) [...]ς ἀρχιερέως τοῦ ἀνδρὸς τῆς  
[...] Ζηνωνίδος

### English translation

[...]of Mar(cus) Aur(elius) [...] high priest, the husband of [. . .] Zenonis.
