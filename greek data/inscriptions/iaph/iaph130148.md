# Funerary inscription for anonymous (IAph2007 13.148)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

τῆ[ς σοροῦ]  
κήδεται ἡ ἱ  
ερωτάτη βου  
λὴ ὡς ὑπο  
γέγραπται

### English translation

- - -] The most sacred Council has the care of the sarcophagus as has been written below.
