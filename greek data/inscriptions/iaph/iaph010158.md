### English translation

. . .] holding office as first archon [. . .] descendant of high priests and stephanephoroi, men who were leaders in the city and tested [had proved their worth] in the very highest magistracies and liturgies; he having lived in an orderly way and by his distinction and proper behaviour added to the glory of the reputation of his family; having left to the sacred Council for eternal distributions 5,000 denarii in the old issue. The erection of the statue was cared for by Ulpius Claudius Aurelius Menippos Aetius his brother, and the Ulpii Claudii Aurelii Zenas Phosphorios and Menippos Aetius and Attalis, his brother's children, as he specified.
