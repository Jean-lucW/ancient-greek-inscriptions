# Funerary inscription for Tatiane (IAph2007 1.145)

## Ancient Greek text

[... εἰς? ἣν? σορὸν? κηδευθήσεται?]  
αὐτὴ Τατίαν[η? ... ἢ ἐκθά]  
ψαι ἀπὸ αὐ[της? ...]

### English translation

[... in which sarcophagus] Tatiane herself [shall be buried ... but no one else shall have the right to bury anyone in it or to remove any body] from it [...].
