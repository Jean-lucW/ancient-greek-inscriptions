# Funerary inscription for Marcus Aurelius Glykon and Alexandros (IAph2007 13.406)

## Ancient Greek text

Ζῶϲιν  
αὑτή ἐϲτιν ἡ  
ϲορὸϲ καὶ τὸ ὑ  
ποκάτω μνῆ  
μα Μάρκου Αὐρ  
ηλίου Γλύκω  
νοϲ ἀγαλματο  
γλύφου καὶ Ἀλε  
ξάνδρου πιμε  
νταρίου ❦

### English translation

They are (still) living. This is the sarcophagus, and the memorial monument below it, of Marcus Aurelius Glykon, sculptor, and Alexandros, dealer in paints (and/or unguents).
