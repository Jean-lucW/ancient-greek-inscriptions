# Honours for L. Ant. Cl. Dometeinos Diogenes (IAph2007 2.17)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ πατρίς  
Λ(ούκιον) Ἀντ(ώνιον) Κλ(αύδιον) Δο  
μετεῖνον  
Διογένην  
τὸν νομοθέ  
την πατέρα  
καὶ πάππον  
συνκλητι  
κῶν  

τῆς ἀναστάσε  
ως τοῦ ἀνδριάν  
τος προνοησα  
μένου Τιβ(ερίου) Κλ(αυδίου)  
Κτησίου πρεσβυ  
τέρου ποιησαμέ  
νου δὲ καὶ τὸν  
βωμὸν αὐτῷ καὶ  
τὰ λοιπὰ παρ' ἑ  
αυτοῦ .

### English translation

His fatherland (honoured) L(ucius) Ant(onius) Cl(audius) Dometeinos Diogenes, law-giver, father and grandfather of senatorials. Tib(erius) Cl(audius) Ktesias the elder supervised the erection of the statue and himself had the altar made for him, and the other features beside, himself.  

The fatherland (honors) Lucius Antonius Claudius Dometeinus Diogenes, the law-giver, father and grandfather of Roman senators. The setting-up of the statue was seen to by Tiberius Claudius Ktesias the Elder, who also made the base "altar" (bomon: lit.) for it. together with what remained, at his own expense.  

His country (honours) Lucius Antonius Claudius Dometinus Diogenes, legislator, father and grandfather of Roman Senators. The erection of the statue was arranged by Tiberius Claudius Ctesias the elder, who also had the altar and other adjuncts made at his own expense.
