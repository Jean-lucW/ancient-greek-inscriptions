# Honours for son of Attalos the son of Athenagoras (IAph2007 2.114)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Ἀτ]  
τάλου τοῦ Ἀ  
θηναγόρου  
ἐξ οὗ ὁ Νηρεὺς  
διετάξατο  
ἀργυρίου ❦

### English translation

[e.g The authorities put up a statue of x] son of Attalos the son of Athenagoras from the money which Nereus bequeathed.
