# Building Dedication to Demos (IAph2007 12.405)

## Ancient Greek text

Ὑψικλῆς Ἀδράστου τὸ ἐπιστύλιον  
καὶ τὸν ἐπ' αὐτοῦ κόσμον τῶι Δήμωι

### English translation

Hypsikles son of Adrastos, the epistyle and the decoration on it for the People.
