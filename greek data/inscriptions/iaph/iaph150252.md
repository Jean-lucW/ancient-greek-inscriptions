# Funerary fragment (IAph2007 15.252)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]υ ὁ πάππος αὐτοῦ καὶ μ[...]Αὐ ρ(ηλία) Κωμικὴ Ἑρμείου ἡ μ[ητὴρ αὐτοῦ ...]

### English translation

[...] his grandfather and M[... and] Aur(elia) Komike (daughter of) Hermeios, his m[other ...]
