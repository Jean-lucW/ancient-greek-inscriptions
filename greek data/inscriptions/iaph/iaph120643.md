# Honours for a son of Gallienus (IAph2007 12.643)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

τὸν ἐπ[ι]φα[νέστα]  
τoν Καίσα[ρα]  

ἡ λαμπροτάτη Ἀ  
φροδεισιέων πό  
λις

### English translation

[—] the most illustrius Caesar, the most splendid city of the Aphrodisieis.  

[...] the most renowned Caesar; the most splendid city of the Aphrodisians (put up his statue).
