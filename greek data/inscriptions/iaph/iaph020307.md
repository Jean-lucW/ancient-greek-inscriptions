# Letter from a Roman official to Aphrodisias (IAph2007 2.307)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]εἰσπράσσει[... εἰ]ς τὴν οἰκείαν κομιδ[ήν ...]εται καὶ τοῦ τελών[ου ... κ]ωλύσει εὐτυχεῖτ[ε ?]

### English translation

. . .] ? he exacts [. . ? . .] ? with a view to personal supply [. . ? . .] and of the tax-collector [. . ? . .] he will prevent [. . ? . .]. Farewell
