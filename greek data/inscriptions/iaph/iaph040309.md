# Honours for T. Oppius Aelianus Asklepiodotos (IAph2007 4.309)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ πατρὶς  
Ἀγαθῇ Τύχῃ  
Τ(ίτον) Ὄππ(ιον) Αἰλιανὸν  
Ἀσκληπιόδοτον  
τὸν λαμπρότατον  
ὑπατικὸν ἡγεμόνα  
Καρίας καὶ Φρυγίας  
ἀνθύπατον καὶ ἐπα  
νορθωτήν Ἀσίας κτί  
στην καὶ σωτῆρα καὶ  
τῆς ἑαυτοῦ πατρίδος  
Τιβ(έριος) Κλ(αύδιος) Μαρκιανὸς ὁ  
πρῶτος ἄρχων.

### English translation

The fatherland, with good fortune, (set up the statue of) T(itus) Opp(ius) Aelianus Asklepiodotos, the most splendid consular, governor of Karia and Phrygia, proconsul and corrector of Asia, builder and saviour also of his own homeland; Tib(erius) Cl(audius) Markianos the first archon (set this up/was in charge).  

The Homeland, With Good Fortune, (set up the statue of) T(itus) Opp(ius) Aelianus Asklepiodotos, the most splendid consular, governor of Caria and Phrygia, proconsul and corrector of Asia, founder and saviour also of his own homeland; Tib(erius) Cl(audius) Markianos the first archon (was in charge).
