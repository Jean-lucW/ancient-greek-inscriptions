# Funerary inscription for Menekles son of Dionysios son of Epainetos (IAph2007 8.504)

## Ancient Greek text

τὸν πλάταν  
κατεσκεύασεν  
Μενεκλῆς Διο  
νυσίου τοῦ Ἐπαι  
νέτου τοῦ Ἀττά  
λου ἑαυτῷ καὶ γυ  
ναικὶ καὶ τέκνοις  
καὶ θρεπτοῖς ·  
Ζῶσιν ❦

### English translation

The platform (for the tomb) was prepared by Menekles son of Dionysios the son of Epainetos who was the son of Attalos, for himself, his wife, his children and those he has fostered. They are still alive.
