# Funerary inscriptions for Tatis and Herodotos (mother and child) (IAph2007 13.502)

## Ancient Greek text

Τατὶ Περιγένου γυ  
νὴ δὲ Ἀπολλωνίου  

Ἡρόδοτε Ἀπολλ  
ωνίου  

χαίρετε

### English translation

Tatis, daughter of Perigenes and wife of Apollonios; Herodotos son of Apollonios.  

Farewell
