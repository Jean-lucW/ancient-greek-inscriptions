# i: Dedication of a caryatid; ii: Sculptor's signature (IAph2007 4.129)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλαυδία  
Μελιτίνη γυ  
νὴ Τιβερίου  
Κλαυδίου ✳  
Ἀττάλου  
Ἀγαθείνου  
τῷ Δήμῳ  

Ἀνδρόνεικος  
Μουσαίου  
ἐποίει

### English translation

(i) : Claudia Melitine, wife of Tiberius Claudius Attalos Agatheinos, to the People.  

(ii) : Andronikos son of Musaios made (the statue)  

(i) : Klaudia Melitine, wife of Tiberius Attalos Agatheinos (set up the statue) to the demos. Andronikos son of Mousaios made (it).
