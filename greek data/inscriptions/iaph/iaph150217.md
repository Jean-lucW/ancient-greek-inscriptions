# Votive offering to Kore by Alexandros (IAph2007 15.217)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀλέξανδρο[ς]  
ὑπηκόῳ Κόρῃ [εὐ]  
χην ἀνέθηκα

### English translation

Alexandros, to Kore who listens: I dedicated (this in payment of ) my vow.
