# Building restoration of the Sebasteion, by Eusebes and Menandros (IAph2007 9.46)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀφρο[δί]τηι θεῷ Σ[εβ]α[στ]οῖς · τῶι Δήμωι · τὸ πρόπυλον · καὶ τὰς ἐν αὐτῷ τιμὰς [· ?]  
Εὐσεβὴς Φιλό[π]ατρις καὶ Μένανδρος οἱ Μενάνδρου τοῦ Εὐνίκου καὶ [Ἀπφίας Μ]ενάνδρου γ[υν]ὴ Εὐσεβοῦς

### English translation

To Aphrodite, (the) Theoi Sebastoi, (the) Demos, the Propylon and the timas [honorific statues] on it. Eusebes Philopatris and Menandros the sons of Menandros son of Eunikos, and Apphias daughter of Menandros (set them up)  

For Aphrodite, the gods Augusti, the People: the propylon and the honorific statues on it, Eusebes [entitled] lover of his country and Menandros the sons of Menandros son of Eunikos, and Apphias daughter of Menandros, wife of Eusebes (set it up)
