### English translation

i: Sallustia Frontina, daughter of Sallustius Rufus, Roman senator, sister of Sallustius Titillianus, Roman senator, mother of Flavius Athenagoras, Roman senator, wife of Flavius Athenagoras Agathos, procurator of the Emperor. Milon III, [son of Milon son of Milon] son of Hermias son of Milon (set up the image)  

ii: Titus Sallustius Flavius Athenagoras, Roman senator, son of Flavius Athenagoras, procurator of the Emperor. Milon III, [son of Milon son of Milon] son of Hermias son of Milon (set up the image)  

iii: Titus Flavius Athenagoras Agathos, procurator of the Emperor, son of Flavius Mithridates, father of Titus Sallustius Titus Flavius Athenagoras, Roman senator, grandfather of Titus Flavius Athenagoras Claudianus, father of Flavia Apphia high priestess of Asia. Milon III, [son of Milon son of Milon] son of Hermias son of Milon (set up the statue of) his own friend and benefactor.
