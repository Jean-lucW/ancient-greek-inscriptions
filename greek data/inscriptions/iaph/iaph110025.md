# Building inscription of Melition son of Pythion (IAph2007 11.25)

## Ancient Greek text

[[[...]]] καὶ Μελίτιον Πυθίωνος  
[[[...]]]ν καὶ τ[...]ὰ ἐν αὐτοῖς πάντα  
[... ἐκ τ]ῶν ἰδίων

### English translation

. . .]] and Melition daughter of Pythion [made the . . .] and everything in them [... at] their own expense [...]
