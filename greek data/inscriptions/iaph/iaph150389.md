### English translation

[Antonius Carminius Popillius?] Agelaos, chief temple-builder of the temple-builders of the most illustrious goddess Aphrodite, nephew of senatorial families, who provided (funds?) for 2 distributions. [Tiberius Claudius] Julius Candidus Hēgemoneus, son of an Asiarch and chief priest, [relative?] of senatorial families, (honoured him?) as he promised.
