### English translation

a: [The Council and the People by a decision] and a [decree} honoured [Marcus] Aurelius [Zen]on, son of Publius Celtius Zenon, the son of Dionysios, with the [finest] and greatest honours - a man of the well-born among us, one whose life style has been and is seemly and worthy of respect to the point of being a demonstration of virtue; and on becoming [?a town] councillor [?he instituted a fund for distributions of money . . .  

b: The appointed day for the lottery is the 20th day of the eighth month.
