# Building Dedication for Gaius Caesar and Concordia (IAph2007 11.228)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] καὶ τῷ Δήμωι καὶ [[[...]]]  
[...] Ὁμονοίας καὶ Γαίου Καίσαρος  
[... καὶ?] τὰ ἐν αὐτῶι ἀνέθηκαν

### English translation

. . .] and for the People and [. . . (?temple/altar)] of Homonoia and of Gaius Caesar [. . .] and the things [?decorations, statues] on it, they set up.
