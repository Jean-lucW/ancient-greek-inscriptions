# Posthomous honours by Artemeis for her brother Bacchios (IAph2007 12.403)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄρτεμεις Ποπλίου  
Βάκχιον Ποπλίου τὸν  
ἀδελφὸν κατὰ τὴν διαθήκην

### English translation

Artemeis daughter of Poplios set up (the image of) Bacchios son of Poplios her brother in accordance with his will.  

Artemeis daughter of Poplios set up (a tomb for?) Bacchios son of Poplios her brother in accordance with his will.
