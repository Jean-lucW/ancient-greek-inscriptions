# Funerary inscription for Attalos (IAph2007 13.204)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἄτταλοϲ Ἐπαγά  
θου τοῦ ἀδ  
ελφοῦ μνεία[ϲ]  
χάριν ἐπόηϲε  
τὸ μνη  
μόριν

### English translation

Attalos son of Epagathos made the memorial of his brother for remembrance.
