# Votive Dedication by children of Hermias (IAph2007 13.119)

## Ancient Greek text

θεοῖϲ ἐπηκόοιϲ  

Εὐάνθηϲ κ⟨α⟩ὶ  
Φιλήτη οἱ Ἑρμί  
ου ὑπὲρ τῶν ἰ  
δίων εὐχήν

### English translation

To the gods who listen: Euanthes and Philete, children of Hermias (paid) the vow for their family.
