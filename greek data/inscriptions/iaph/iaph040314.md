### English translation

-- the erection (of the statue) was taken care of by Ulpius Zenon, also called Zenas, the deputy chief neopoios of the third period neopoiia of Poseidonios, in accordance with the wish of Claudius Apollonios Markianos and Claudia Seleukeia Tibereine, high priestess of Asia and of the fatherland, the priests of the goddess,and in accordance with what also the council and the people approved, as well as by the neopoioi Marcus Aurelius Zenobios V Epaphroditos, Marcus Aurelius Krateros V Athenagoras, Marcus Aurelius Antonius Menestheus, son of Menandros, and Marcus Aurelius Epigonos, son of Epigonos, grandson of Zenon.  

[The Council and] the People honoured [[name unknown]], responsibility for the erection (of the monument) being undertaken by Ulpius Zenon, also called Zenas, standing in as chief temple-builder [neopoios] in the third period of office of Poseidonios, in accordance with the wishes of Claudius Apollonius Marcianus and Claudia Seleuceia Tiberine, high priestess of Asia and of her native city, both being priests of the goddess (Aphrodite), according to the confirmation by the Council and the People, and when the temple-builders [neopoioi] were M. Aurelius Zenobios Epaphroditos V [son of Zenobios.son of Zenobios, son of Zenobios, son of Zenobios], and M. Aurelius Krateros Athenagoras V [son of Krateros, son of Krateros, son of Krateros, son of Krateros], and M. Aurelius Antonius Menestheus son of Menandros, and M. Aurelius Epigonos II [son of Epigonos] son of Zenon.
