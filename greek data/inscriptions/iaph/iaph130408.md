# Funerary inscription for Dioskurides (IAph2007 13.408)

## Ancient Greek text

τὸ μνημεῖον  
Διοσκυρίδου  
τοῦ Διοσκυρίδου  
τοῦ Διοσκυρίδου  
τοῦ Τειμαίου  
Ζῇ

### English translation

The memorial of Dioskurides son of Dioskurides son of Dioskurides son of Teimaios. He lives.
