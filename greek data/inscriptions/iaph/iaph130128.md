# Funerary fragment (IAph2007 13.128)

## Ancient Greek text

[... τεθήσε?]  
τε αὐτὸς κ[...]  
μος καὶ οὓς ἂ[ν βουλήθη ...]

### English translation

[. . .? will be placed] himself [. . .-]mos and whomever [he may wish . . .
