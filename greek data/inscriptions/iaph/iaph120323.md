### English translation

Claudia Antonia Tatiana, e(gregia) f(emina), cousin of the Claudii Diogenes and Attalos, senatorials, on all occasions following family tradition in being a benefactress of the city. Ti(berius) Cl(audius) Kapitoleinos undertook the charge of erecting the statue.  

Claudia Antonia Tatiana, the most excellent cousin of the Claudii, Diogenes and Attalus, of senatorial rank, who in all things according to the pattern of her ancestors hath approved herself benevolently disposed towards the city: The erection of the statue having been confided to Tiberius Claudius Capetolinus  

Claudia Antonia Tatiana, the excellent cousin of Claudius Diogenes and Attaluss, the Senators [?], being a distinguished benefactress to the city, as were her ancestors. Ti. CI. Attalus, the son of Diogenes [?], superintended the erection of the statue.
