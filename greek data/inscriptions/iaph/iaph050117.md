# Votive offering to Asklepios (IAph2007 5.117)

## Ancient Greek text

εὐεπηκόῳ Ἀ  
σκληπίῳ Εὐγενί  
α εὐχὴν ἀνέθηκεν

### English translation

To Asklepios who listens to prayers well: Eugenia dedicated (this) in fulfilment of a vow  

To Asklepios who listens well: Eugenia dedicated (in payment of) a vow
