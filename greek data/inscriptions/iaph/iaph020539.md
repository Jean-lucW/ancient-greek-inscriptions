# Funerary for Neikephoros by his parents (IAph2007 2.539)

## Ancient Greek text

Νεικήφοροϲ [καὶ ...]  
οἱ γονεῖϲ Νεικη[φόρου τοῦ]  
τέκνου μνίαϲ [χάριν ...]

### English translation

Neikephoros [and ...], his parents, (set this up) in memory of their child Neike[phoros].
