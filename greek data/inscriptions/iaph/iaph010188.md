# Funerary formula from sarcophagus fragment (IAph2007 1.188)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ἑτερος δὲ οὐδεὶς ἕξει ἐξουσίαν ἐν]θάψαι τινα ἢ ἐκθάψαι ἐπὶ ἀποτε[ίσει ... ὧν τ]ὸ τρίτον ἔστω τοῦ ἐγ[δικήσαντος ...]

### English translation

[... no-one else shall have the right] to bury anyone (in the sarcophagus) or to remove anyone (from it) since (if he does) he will pay [...] of which the third part is to belong to the prosecutor [...]
