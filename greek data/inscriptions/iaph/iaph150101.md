# Votive to Hekate Euantetos (IAph2007 15.101)

## Ancient Greek text

Ζωτικὸϲ  
Εὐαντήτ  
ῳ εὐχήν

### English translation

Zotikos pays his vow to the deity who is kindly to meet [Hekate].
