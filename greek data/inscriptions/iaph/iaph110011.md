# Funerary inscription for Menandros (IAph2007 11.11)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ πλάταϲ ἐϲτὶν  
Μενάνδρου τρὶϲ  
τοῦ Ἀρκαθίου Ἑκά  
τωνοϲ Κοΐνθου καὶ  
κληρονόμων αὐτοῦ

### English translation

The plot belongs to Menandros Kointhos III [son of Menandros son of Menandros] son of Akathios Hekaton, and his heirs
