# Honours for daughter of Hephaistion (IAph2007 1.159)

[...]

### English translation

[So and so honoured Aurelia Flavia Messoulia Diogeneia] flower-bearer, high priestess, daughter of Flavius Hephaistion and Moessouleia Satorneila, high priests, wife of Aemilius Hypsikles, high priest, knight, priest for life of Helios.
