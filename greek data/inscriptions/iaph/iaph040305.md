# Funerary inscription for Polykrates (IAph2007 4.305)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ πλάταϲ ἐϲ  
τιν Πολυκρά  
τουϲ δ τοῦ Ἀ  
[πολ]λωνίου  

[...] ο  

[...]

### English translation

The platform belongs to Polykrates, son of Polykrates, grandson of Polykrates, great grandson of Polykrates the son of Apollonios [...]  

The area belongs to Polykrates IV [son of Polykrates son of Polykrates son of Polykrates] son of A[pol]lonios [...]  

The area belongs to Polykrates (son of Polykrates son of Polykrates son of Polykrates) son of A[pol]lonios [...]
