# Dedication of a building to Zeus (IAph2007 1.511)

## Ancient Greek text

[Δι]ὶ Σπαλωξίωι καὶ Διὶ Πατρῴωι Εὐσεβὴ[ς]  
[Μ]ενάνδρου Φιλόπατρις, ὁ ἱερεὺς αὐτῶ[ν]

### English translation

To Zeus Spaloxios and Zeus Patroos, Eusebes Philopatris, son of Menandros, their priest (dedicated this).
