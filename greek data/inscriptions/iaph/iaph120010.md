# i: Title for images of Troilos and Achilles; ii: Sculptor's signature (IAph2007 12.10)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δῆμος  
τὸν Τρωΐλον · καὶ [τὸν ἵπ]  
πον καὶ τὸν Ἀχιλλέα [ἀνέστησεν]  

Ἀν[δ]ρονικ

### English translation

(i) : The Demos set up the Troilos and the horse and the Achilles.  

(ii) : Andronikos (or) Of, or belonging to, Andronikos  

(i) : The People (set up the statue of) Troilos and the horse and Achilles.
