# Building Dedication to Demos (IAph2007 13.503)

## Ancient Greek text

Μένανδρος Ἀττάλου [τὰ ἐπιστύλια]  
καὶ τὸν ἐπ'αὐτῶν [κόσμον τῶι Δήμωι]

### English translation

Menandros son of Attalos, [the epistyles] and the [decoration] on them [for the People].
