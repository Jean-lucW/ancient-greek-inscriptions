# Honours for Dionysios son of Artemidoros (IAph2007 12.307)

## Ancient Greek text

[ἡ βουλὴ καὶ ὁ δῆμος]  
[ἐ]τειμησεν ταῖς καλλ[ίσ]  
ταις τειμαῖς Διονύ  
σιον Ἀρτεμιδώρο[υ]  
τοῦ Μενίππου το[ῦ]  
Διονυσίου τοῦ Δη  
μητρίου ζήσαντα  
κοσμίως καὶ πρὸς [[[...]]]  
ὑπόδειγμα ἀρετῆς

### English translation

The Council and the People honoured with the finest honours Dionysios son of Artemidoros son of Menippos son of Dionysios the son of Demetrios, who has lived a well-ordered life and one with a view to a demonstration of virtue.  

. . . . honoured with the fairest [?] honours Dionysius, son of Artemidorus, the son of Menippus, the son of Dionysius, the son of Demetrius, living decently and as a pattern of virtue.
