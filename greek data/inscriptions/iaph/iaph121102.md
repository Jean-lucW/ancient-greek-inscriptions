# Honours for Sokrates son of Theophrastos (IAph2007 12.1102)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ βουλὴ καὶ ὁ δῆμος ἐτείμησεν  
Σωκράτην Θεοφράστου ἄνδρα  
γενόμενον καλὸν καὶ ἀγαθ[όν]

### English translation

The Council and the People honoured Sokrates son of Theophrastos, who was a fine and good man.  

The Council and the People honoured Sokrates son of Theophrastos  

The Senate and the People honoured Socrates, the son of Theophrastus, who had been an honourable and good man.
