# Funerary for Attalos son of Menippos (IAph2007 11.8)

## Ancient Greek text

[...] ἡ σορὸς σὺν τῷ βωμῷ  
[...] Ἀττάλου τοῦ Μενίππου  
[...] τοῦ Ἀθηναγόρου ζῇ

### English translation

The tomb with the base [is the property] of Attalos son of Menippos [? . . .] son of Athenagoras. He lives.
