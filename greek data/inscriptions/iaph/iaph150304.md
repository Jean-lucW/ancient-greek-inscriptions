# Funerary formula fragment, second to third cent. (IAph2007 15.304)

## Ancient Greek text

[... ἕτερος? δὲ? οὐδεὶς? ἕ]ξει ἐξουσί[αν ἐνθάψαι τινὰ ἐπεὶ ὁ τούτων τι]  
[ποιήσας ἔσται ἀσεβὴς κ]αὶ ἐπάρατος [καὶ τυμβωρύχος ...]

### English translation

[No-one else] shall have the right [to bury in it or to remove a body from it since anyone who does so shall be considered sacrilegious] and accursed [and a tomb breaker and shall pay ...]
