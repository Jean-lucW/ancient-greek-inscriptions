# Votive by Dionysios on behalf of Ammia (IAph2007 11.3)

## Ancient Greek text

Διονύϲιοϲ  
ὑπὲρ Ἀμ  
μίαϲ ❦  
εὐχήν

### English translation

Dionysios on behalf of Ammia, in fulfilment of a vow.  

Dionysios on behalf of Ammia (in payment of) a vow.
