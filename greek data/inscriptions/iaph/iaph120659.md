# Verse epitaph of Adrastos (IAph2007 12.659)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ιω[...]οϲ κα τύμβιοϲ ἐνθάδε [...] κληθεὶϲ ἐν ϲτάδιοιϲ καὶ ἐν μερόπεϲιν·  
Ἄδραϲτοϲ

### English translation

[--- the deceased lies] here as occupant of the tomb, who has been acclaimed [as a good/worthy man] in the stadia and among the mortals. Adrastos
