# Funerary inscription for Athiktos (IAph2007 12.1004)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀθίκτῳ ἥρωι  
ἡ γυνὴ καὶ τὰ  
τέκνα μνή  
μηϲ χάριν  
❦

### English translation

To Athiktos, hero, his wife and his children, in memory.
