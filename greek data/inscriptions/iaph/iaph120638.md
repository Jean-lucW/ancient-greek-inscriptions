# Dedication to Hypnos by high-priest and priest of Asklepios (IAph2007 12.638)

## Ancient Greek text

[τῷ] Ὓπνῳ  
[Σε]πτίμιος  
Αὐ(ρήλιος) · Φλ(άουιος) ·  
Οὐενίδιος  
Ὑψικλῆς  
Εὔφρων  
ἀρχ[ι]ερεὺς  
ὁ ἱε[ρεὺ]ς τοῦ  
Ἀσ[κληπ]ίου

### English translation

For Hypnos: Septimius Aurelius Flavius Venidius Hypsikles Euphron, high-priest and priest of Asklepios (set this monument up).
