### English translation

?,] wrestler, having won the Aphrodeisiea Adonea; Tiberius Claudius Aurelius Mucianos Apollonios Beroneikianos, the most distinguished, vice-neopoios for Tiberius Claudius Apollonios Beroneikianos Akasson, high-priest, his own grandfather, in the first cycle of the chief-neopoioate funded from the bequest of Apollonios, set up (the statue) from his own resources
