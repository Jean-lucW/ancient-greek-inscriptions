# Votive to Asklepios (IAph2007 4.113)

## Ancient Greek text

θεῷ  
Ἀϲκληπ[ίῳ]  
[ε]ὐχή[ν]

### English translation

To the god Asklepios in fulfillment of a vow.  

To the god Asklepios (in payment of) a vow.
