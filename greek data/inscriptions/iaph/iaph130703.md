### English translation

(A) : . . . no one else shall have authorization] to either bury or remove anyone, because he shall pay as a fine to the most sacred treasury 10,000 denarii, one third of which shall belong to the vindicator.  

(B) : Of Achilleus Dromax (“the dromedary”), chief secretary.
