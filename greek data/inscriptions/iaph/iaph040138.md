# Dedication of caryatid (IAph2007 4.138)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰλία  
Στατειλία  
Στρατονεί  
κη τῷ Δή  
μῳ

### English translation

Aelia Stateilia Stratoneike, to the People  

Aelia Stateilia Stratonike (set up the statue) [to the demos/patris].
