# Funerary fragment (IAph2007 11.37)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]  

ΕΙΣ ὑποκείσονται [...]  
τῇ θεᾷ Ἀφροδείτῃ Ι[... τοῦτο δὲ ἐκδικήσουσιν οἱ κατὰ?]  
καιρὸν νεοποιοὶ ν[...]  
θης ἐσ[τι]ν ὁ Τατιανὸ[ς ...]

### English translation

. . .] will lie below [? penalty to be paid] to the goddess Aphrodite [? which will be exacted by] the neopoioi then in office [...] is Tatianos [. . .  

. . .] will lie below[? penalty to be paid] to the goddess Aphrodite [? under the guidance of] the neopoioi then in office [...] is Tatianos [. . .
