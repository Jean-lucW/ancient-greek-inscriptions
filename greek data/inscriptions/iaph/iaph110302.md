# Building Dedication to Demos by Eupolemos son of Artemidoros (IAph2007 11.302)

## Ancient Greek text

Εὐπόλεμος  
Ἀρτεμιδώρου  
τὸν πύργον  
τῶι Δήμωι

### English translation

Eupolemos son of Artemidoros, the tower, for the People.
