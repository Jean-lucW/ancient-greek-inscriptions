# Funerary inscription for Ti. Claudius Achilleus (IAph2007 13.303)

## Ancient Greek text

Ζῶϲιν  
ὁ πλάταϲ ἐϲτιν  
Τι(βερίου) Κλ(αυδίου) Ἀχιλλέ  
ωϲ

### English translation

They are living. The platform belongs to Ti(berius) Cl(audius) Achilleus ?[. . .
