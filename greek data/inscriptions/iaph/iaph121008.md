# Heading for honours for anonymous (IAph2007 12.1008)

## Ancient Greek text

ἡ βουλὴ · καὶ · ὁ δῆμος  
καὶ · ἡ γερουσία · καὶ οἱ  
[νέοι ἐτείμησαν ...]

### English translation

The Council and the People and the Gerousia and the [Young Men honoured ...]
