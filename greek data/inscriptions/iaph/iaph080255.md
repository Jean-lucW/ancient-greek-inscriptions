# Place inscription for Aelius Benouseinos (IAph2007 8.255)

## Ancient Greek text

Αἰλ(ίου)  
Βενουϲί  
νου κρα(τίϲτου)

### English translation

(Place) of Ael(ius) Benuseinos, most distinguished.
