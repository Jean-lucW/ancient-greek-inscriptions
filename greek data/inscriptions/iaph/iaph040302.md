# Sculptor's signature: Menodotos (IAph2007 4.302)

## Ancient Greek text

[Μηνόδοτος Νικο]μάχου τοῦ Μηνοδότ[ου]  
ἐποίει [... ?]

### English translation

Menodotos, son of [Niko]machos, grandson of Menodotos, made (this).  

Menodotos, son of [... ?-]machos, the son of Menodotos, made (this).  

Menodotos, son of [?-]machos, grandson of Menodotos, made (this).
