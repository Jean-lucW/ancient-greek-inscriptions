# Votive offering to a goddess (Aphrodite) (IAph2007 10.103)

## Ancient Greek text

Θεᾷ ἐπηκόῳ  
Εὔδαμοϲ  
εὐχὴν

### English translation

To the goddess who listens to prayers, Eudamos in fulfilment of a vow  

To the goddess who hears, Eudamos (pays his) vow
