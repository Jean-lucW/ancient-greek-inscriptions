# Votive offering to divinity (IAph2007 8.712)

## Ancient Greek text

[Εὐ]πρέπηϲ Θε[ῷ]  
[ἐπηκόῳ?] εὐχή[ν]

### English translation

[Eu]prepes to the god [?who listens] (in payment of) a vow.
