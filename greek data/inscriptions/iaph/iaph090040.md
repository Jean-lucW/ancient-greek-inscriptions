# Honours for Atia mother of Augustus (IAph2007 9.40)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀτίαν Βάλβου μητέρα  
Σεβαστοῦ Καίσαρος

### English translation

Atia (daughter of) Balbus, mother of Augustus Caesar  

(They set up the statue of)) Atia daughter of Balbus, mother of Augustus Caesar.
