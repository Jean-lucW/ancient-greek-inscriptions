# Honours for Ulpia Carminia Claudiana (IAph2007 12.1020)

## Ancient Greek text

[... Οὐλ(πίαν) Καρ(μινίαν)]  
Κλαυδιανὴν θυγα  
τέρα πόλεως μητέ  
ρα συνκλητικῶν · Ἀ  
σίας ἀρχιέρειαν θυ  
γατέρα · Μ·(άρκου) Οὐλ·(πίου) Καρ·(μινίου)  
Κλαυδιανοῦ · Ἀσίας  
ἀρχιερέως · καὶ · ἀρχιε  
ρέως καὶ Οὐ·λ·(πίας) Κλ·(αυδίας) Καρ·(μινίας)  
Προκλῆς θυγατρὸς  
πόλεως · Ἀσίας ἀρχιε  
ρείας δὶς ἀρχιερείας  
ἱερέων διὰ βίου · τῆς  
θεοῦ Ἀφροδείτης  
προγόνων Ἀσίας ἀρ  
χιερέων · καὶ ἀρχιερέ  
ων ἀνεψίαν συνκλη  
τικῶν καὶ ὑπατικῶν  

ἐπιμεληθέντος  
Μ(άρκου) Αὐρ(ηλίου) Ζηνοβίου τετράκις  
τοῦ Ζηνοβίου τοῦ Ἀρτεμι  
δώρου Ἐπαφροδείτου ἱε  
ρεως διὰ βίου Θεῶν Ἐρώτων

### English translation

...? [honoured? Ulpia Carminia]] Claudiane, daughter of the city, mother of senators, high priestess of Asia, daughter of M(arcus) Ulp(ius) Car(minius) Claudianus, high priest of Asia and high priest (of the city) and of Ulp(ia) Cl(audia) Car(minia) Prokle, daughter of the city, high priestess of Asia twice, high priestess (of the city), (both of them) priests for life of the goddess Aphrodite, descended from high priests of Asia and high priests (of the city), cousin of senatorials and consulars. M(arcus) Aur(elius) Zenobios IV, [son of Zenobios, son of Zenobios, son of Zenobios] son of Zenobios Epaphroditos son of Artemidoros, priest for life of the divine Erotes, oversaw provision (of the monument).  

...? [honoured? Ulpia Carminia]] Claudiane, daughter of the city, mother of the senators M(arcus) Ulp(ius) Car(minius) Claudianus, high priest of Asia, high priest (of the city) and Ulp(ia) Cl(audia) Car(minia) Prokle, daughter of the city, high priestess of Asia twice, high priestess (of the city), (both of them) priests for life of the goddess Aphrodite, descended from high priests of Asia and high priests (of the city), cousin of senatorials and consulars. M(arcus) Aur(elius) Zenobios, fourth of the name from Zenobios son of Artemidoros, son of Epaphroditos, priest for life of the divine Loves, oversaw provision (of the monument).
