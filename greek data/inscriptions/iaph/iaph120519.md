# Honours for Myon Menandros (IAph2007 12.519)

## Ancient Greek text

ὁ δῆμος  
Μύωνα Ἀγελάου  
φύσει δὲ Εὐσέβους  
Μένανδρον

### English translation

The People honoured Myon Menandros, (adoptive) son of Agelaos, by birth son of Eusebes.  

The People [honoured] Myo Menander, the son of Agelaus, but by birth son of Eusebes.
