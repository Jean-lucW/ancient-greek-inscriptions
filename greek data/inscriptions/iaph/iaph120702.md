# Building inscription by Hermolaos (IAph2007 12.702)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἑρμόλαος πορφυ  
ρᾶς τὸ στεγνὸν  
ἐποίει

### English translation

Hermolaos, purple-dyer, built the shelter
