# Funerary fragment (IAph2007 15.8)

## Ancient Greek text

[...]ΝΕ[... οὔ]τε ἐνθά[ψαι ...]ΙΩ οὔτε Λ[... πο]ιήσας ἔσται [ἀσεβὴς ... Ἀφρο]δείτῃ ἀργ[υρίου ... πε]ντακοσ[ι...]  
[...]ΥΛΙ[...]

### English translation

No useful translation possible: fragments from prohibition of burial, a curse on anyone who does so, and a penalty - payable in silver - to Aphrodite.
