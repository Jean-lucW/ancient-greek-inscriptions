# Dedication to Zeus Nineudios (IAph2007 11.519)

## Ancient Greek text

Μελιτίνη ὑπὲρ {Ε} Ε[ὐστό?]χίου  
τοῦ υἱοῦ Διὶ Νινε[υδίῳ κατ᾿ εὐ]χήν

### English translation

Melitine for her son -chios, to Zeus Nineudios, in fulfilment of a vow
