# Commemoration of ephebes (IAph2007 11.116)

### English translation

(A) : Artemid[oros?] commemorated his fellow ephebes --. (B) : Dionysios [----] commemorated [--]. (C) : The association of friends who served together as ephebes, Zenon, Adrastos, Epiktetos, Papias, and Apathopous, commemorated the good ephebarchos Neikostratos Herakles, honouring him.
