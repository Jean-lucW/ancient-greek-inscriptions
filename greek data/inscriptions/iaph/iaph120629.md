# Funerary relief for anonymous, emporiarch (IAph2007 12.629)

## Ancient Greek text

[[[...]]]  
ἐνπο  
ριαρχηϲ

νουργῶ  
ν

### English translation

[So-and-so], market-supervisor, greets the association of linen-workers and the passers-by.
