# Dedication to Aphrodite and the People (IAph2007 1.305)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀπολλώ[[ν[ι]ο[ς]]] [[[Ἀρτεμι?]]]  
δώρου τοῦ [[[...]]]  
ἀστυνομῆσας [[[Ἀ]φροδείτ[ηι]]]  
καὶ τῶι Δήμωι

### English translation

Apollonios [son of Artemi]doros having been astynomos, to Aphrodite and the People.
