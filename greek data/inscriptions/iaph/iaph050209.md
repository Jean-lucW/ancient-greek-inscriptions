# Dedication of a caryatid by Claudia Seleukeia (IAph2007 5.209)

## Ancient Greek text

Κλ(αυδία) · Σελεύ  
κεια · Ἀπολ  
λωνίου θυ  
γάτηρ τῷ  
Δήμῳ

### English translation

Cl(audia), daughter of Apollonios, to the People.  

Claudia Seleukeia, daughter of Apollonios (set up the statue) to the demos
