# Honours for Agrippina (IAph2007 9.37)

## Ancient Greek text

Ἀγριππῖναν  
Μάρκου Ἀγρίππ[α]  
γυναῖκα δὲ Γε[ρ]  
μανικοῦ Καίσα  
ρος μητέρα Γαΐ  
ου Αὐτοκράτο  
ρος Σεβαστοῦ ✳  
Καίσαρος

### English translation

Agrippina, daughter of Marcus Agrippa, wife of Germanicus Caesar, mother of Gaius, Imperator Augustus Caesar.  

(They set up the statue of) Agrippina (daughter of) Marcus Agrippa and wife of Germanicus Caesar, mother of Gaius, Imperator Augustus Caesar.
